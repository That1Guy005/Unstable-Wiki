# About

These notes cover the scripted Minecraft series **Unstable Universe** (the Unstable SMP), told from the POVs of
ParrotX2, Wemmbu, SpokeIsHere and FlameFrags. This is a fan reference and isn't affiliated with the creators.

## Sources

- **Text.** The recaps, bios and histories are written in my own words from the community recaps and pages on the
  [Unstable Universe Wiki](https://unstable-universe-mc.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_Order), whose text is licensed
  [CC BY-SA](https://www.fandom.com/licensing). Quotes are kept to short phrases.
- **Thumbnails.** Episode thumbnails are loaded directly from YouTube (`i.ytimg.com`) and link to the video on the
  creator's channel. They aren't stored in this site. © their respective creators.
- **Images.** Character skins and place screenshots are loaded directly from the wiki (`static.wikia.nocookie.net`),
  each with a link to its file page for credit and licensing. They aren't stored in this site either.
- **Fanart.** Each character's fanart is loaded from the artist's own Tumblr post (or, for Theobald, the wiki) and
  links back to it. The art belongs to the artists. If you publish this site, it's polite to ask them first, and to
  remove any piece an artist asks you to take down.
- **Cosplay.** Cosplay photos are loaded from the cosplayer's own public Tumblr post and link back to it. Only photos
  where the cosplayer's face is hidden or mostly covered (mask, box head, shot from behind) are used. They show real people, so ask the
  cosplayers before you publish this site, and remove any photo someone asks you to take down.
- **Item pictures.** Mechanics without a wiki image show the item's picture from the
  [Minecraft Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki/) (game assets © Mojang). Events, arcs and a few other pages show the
  thumbnail of the episode where they start.
- **Private videos.** Five side videos are now private on YouTube. Their thumbnails are copies saved by the
  [Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/).

## Cosplay photos

12 characters have a cosplay photo. The others have an empty **Cosplay** slot in their gallery. To fill one:

1. Put the photo in `docs/assets/cosplay/` (create the folder), e.g. `docs/assets/cosplay/wemmbu.jpg`, or use a
   direct image link.
2. Open the character's page in `docs/characters/` and find the comment starting with `<!-- COSPLAY`.
3. Replace the camera icon line and the "No cosplay photo yet" note with the image and credit lines shown in that comment.

Cosplay photos show real people, so only use photos you have the cosplayer's permission for, and credit them.
