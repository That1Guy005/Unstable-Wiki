The finale of Season 1, told from three POVs. [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) and [Wifies](../characters/wifies.md) flee to the End and then the Far Lands, [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](../characters/mapicc.md) infiltrate [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), and [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) trains for the war. The arc ends with the Mafia's stasis chambers destroyed and its downfall.
