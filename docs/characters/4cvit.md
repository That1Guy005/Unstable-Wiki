A skilled technical player and merchant of [Redstone Town](../places/redstone-town.md), [Leow0ok](leow0ok.md)'s friend, who sells or builds redstone gear such as cannon parts for BAT, [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) and others.

## Profile

- Status: alive, captured by Cindercrest.
- Roles: founder of [Redstone Town](../places/redstone-town.md), later mayor of Steampunk City.
- Known for: redstone, parkour and pearl cannons.

## Story so far

- He joins [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md) and runs Redstone Town, a hub for redstone players.
- He tips BAT off about the Director's buyer (MrCube6), and builds Jamato an escape cannon.
- He refuses Wemmbu access to his Far Lands pearl cannon, calling LAW, until Parrot asks; later his cannon takes Wemmbu's group to the Far Lands for Saparata.

## Appears in

### Season 1

- [I Broke into Minecraft's Most Secure Prison](../episodes/season-1/007-i-broke-into-minecrafts-most-secure-prison.md) (ep. 7, ParrotX2, 9 June 2024)
- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): 4CVIT returns, warns the civilization is sure to be found and destroyed, and says Parrot needs to stay put.

### Season 2

- [I Got Hunted by a Minecraft Redstone Genius](../episodes/season-2/031-i-got-hunted-by-a-minecraft-redstone-genius.md) (ep. 31, ParrotX2, 9 March 2025): They follow MrCube's rails to Redstone Town, where 4CVIT is helping him hide the loot in an underground vault he says will explode if anyone steps on the wrong plate.
- [I Got Hunted by a Minecraft Hacker](../episodes/season-2/040-i-got-hunted-by-a-minecraft-hacker.md) (ep. 40, ParrotX2, 4 May 2025): At [Redstone Town](../places/redstone-town.md), 4CVIT won't hand over materials without payment, until Leo, an old friend, arrives.
- [Hunting a Minecraft Player Who Doesn't Exist](../episodes/season-2/045-hunting-a-minecraft-player-who-doesnt-exist.md) (ep. 45, ParrotX2, 31 May 2025): At Redstone Town, 4CVIT won't list his bulk buyers for privacy reasons, until Parrot bribes him with a shulker of the zombies' netherite. 4C remembers an invisible buyer in armour like the Director's.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Farlands](../episodes/season-2/053-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-farlands.md) (ep. 53, Wemmbu, 8 July 2025): Nobody knows where he went, until 4CVIT admits Sharpness commissioned a pearl cannon. 4C takes them to it, and it sends them to the edge of the Far Lands.
- [I Got REVENGE on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/055-i-got-revenge-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 55, FlameFrags, 16 July 2025): 4CVIT tells them Wemmbu is hunting Sharpness in the Far Lands.
- [I Got Hunted By the Minecraft LAW](../episodes/season-2/095-i-got-hunted-by-the-minecraft-law.md) (ep. 95, Wemmbu, 9 December 2025): Out of prison, [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) finds Flame gone and every door closed: 4CVIT snitches, LAW pours into the End, and [MinuteTech](minutetech.md) sends him to the one player who can help, [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md).
- [I Became a Minecraft Killer](../episodes/season-2/107-i-became-a-minecraft-killer.md) (ep. 107, Wemmbu, 25 January 2026): After the debate, Wemmbu stasises to where Egg and 4CVIT wait with a pearl cannon to the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md).

### Season 2+

- [I Became a Minecraft Combat Master](../episodes/season-2-plus/117-i-became-a-minecraft-combat-master.md) (ep. 117, FlameFrags, 27 February 2026): At [Redstone Town](../places/redstone-town.md), Flame trades diamond sets and a netherite sword to 4CVIT for two redstone kits.
- [100 Days as King of the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2-plus/118-100-days-as-king-of-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 118, ParrotX2, 28 February 2026): 4CVIT won't help build an automatic chicken farm because his town would be upset, so Parrot and Theo spend days trying alone while 4C watches.
- [Unstable SMP: Civil War](../episodes/season-2-plus/133-unstable-smp-civil-war.md) (ep. 133, ParrotX2, 16 May 2026): He wants 4CVIT to build a cannon there; 4C lacks time and materials.
- [How I Saved the World's Largest Minecraft Server](../episodes/season-2-plus/154-how-i-saved-the-worlds-largest-minecraft-server.md) (ep. 154, Wemmbu, 19 September 2026): 4CVIT's cannon takes them to the Far Lands entrance as NULL arrives; they can't stop NULL entering.
