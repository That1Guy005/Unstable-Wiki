The opportunistic king of [The Warriors](../factions/the-warriors.md), who believes in winning through power and tricks rather than skill. He beats [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md) twice by cheating until Flame exposes him, and later helps LAW with redstone in the Dimensional War.

## Profile

- Status: alive; king of [The Warriors](../factions/the-warriors.md).

## Personality

Antaggg values victory over honour, winning with arrow cannons, rigged arenas and swapped fighters. He doesn't care what anyone thinks, only about his own power. He's charismatic and manipulative, indoctrinating TheG0dofWar's old civilization and inflating his own legend, and his boasting tips into overconfidence.

## Story so far

- He exiles and later kills TheG0dofWar, runs a rigged tournament, and attacks Canary, until Flame beats him in a fair duel and his Warriors disband.
- He sends Warriors to Terminus to make sure Flame dies, then trades redstone help to LAW for twenty imprisoned Warriors (ep. 160).

## Appears in

- [I Got Trained By a Minecraft Combat Master](../episodes/season-2-plus/144-i-got-trained-by-a-minecraft-combat-master.md) (ep. 144, FlameFrags, 3 July 2026): Antaggg flees and claims victory; Flame vows revenge.
- [Minecraft's Best Players Simulate PVP Tournament](../episodes/season-2-plus/145-minecrafts-best-players-simulate-pvp-tournament.md) (ep. 145, FlameFrags, 21 July 2026): Flame wins the tournament bracket but loses the final to Antaggg's cheating.
- [I Exposed Minecraft's "Strongest" Player](../episodes/season-2-plus/149-i-exposed-minecrafts-strongest-player.md) (ep. 149, FlameFrags, 14 August 2026): Antaggg is beaten and flees; Canary survives and the Warriors defect.
- [Escaping Terminus: The Deadliest Minecraft Prison](../episodes/season-2-plus/157-escaping-terminus-the-deadliest-minecraft-prison.md) (ep. 157, FlameFrags, 6 October 2026): Antaggg wants to find the prison to make sure Flame dies; Alix49 objects and dies as he leaves.
- [10,000 VS 10,000 Player Minecraft War](../episodes/season-2-plus/160-10-000-vs-10-000-player-minecraft-war.md) (ep. 160, FlameFrags, 31 October 2026): He makes an unpopular deal with Antaggg's [Warriors](../factions/the-warriors.md), frees [LettuceK](lettucek.md) from Cindercrest's stasis, chooses a friend over a vault, crushes Cindercrest's invasion, and rescues [NotSkrilley](notskrilley.md) from Terminus.
