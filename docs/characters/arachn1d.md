A former election candidate who founds the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md), proclaims himself the rightful king of the server, holds [Eggchan](eggchan.md) hostage to control [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md), and allies with [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md) while planning to betray it. He is eventually dethroned.

## Profile

- Status: deceased, killed by Yungyx (ep. 150).
- Roles: election candidate and major antagonist of the purge; founder and king of the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md); main antagonist of Wemmbu's Kingdoms saga.

## Personality

Arachn1d is an autocrat, ruthless and power-hungry. Unlike Saparata or Parrot, he has no vision for his kingdom beyond securing his own place as the "true" king. He refuses to accept defeat, and after losing the election he built a vast underground kingdom to continue. He exploits his subjects and despises the weak, purging them. In the end he admitted he should have built his kingdom on intelligence, not strength.

## Relationships

- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): his weapon, then his conqueror.
- [Eggchan](eggchan.md): his hostage.
- [Yungyx](yungyx.md): his follower and killer.
- [Saparata](saparata.md): his civil-war ally, whom he planned to betray.

## Story so far

- [Election Arc](../arcs/election-arc.md): in the purge he hunts candidates and traps Parrot and Horace in a TNT cave.
- He founds the Kingdom of the Caves, takes Egg hostage, uses Wemmbu as his weapon in the civil war while secretly plotting against Cindercrest, and holds the purge ([The Purge of the Caves](../events/the-purge-of-the-caves.md)).
- Ep. 150: duelled and humiliated by Wemmbu, his thorns machine fails and Yungy kills him.
