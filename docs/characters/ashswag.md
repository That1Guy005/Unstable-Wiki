A seemingly harmless loner who turns out to be the leader of [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) and the main antagonist of Season 1. He is the one behind the server lag and the returning duped items, and he warns Spoke to "choose the right thing".

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: leader of [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), main antagonist of Season 1 with ClownPierce.

## Personality

Ash is power-hungry and controlling, determined to rule the server through the Mafia. He likes to mock those who try to stop him, and speaks in grand, flowery, philosophical language, sometimes with religious imagery. With Squiddo, or when mocking serious opponents, he can be playful and theatrical. He's a master manipulator, open about using fear as control, and he once tricked Spoke into trusting him.

## Relationships

- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md): "Fish Bucket", his protégé and the Mafia's destroyer, whom he now calls his equal.
- [TheRealSquiddo](therealsquiddo.md): one of the few people he opens up to.
- [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md) and [FerreMC](ferremc.md): his allies in the Mafia.

## Story so far

- [Invisible Mafia Arc](../arcs/invisible-mafia-arc.md): he befriends Spoke and Mapicc while his invisible players wreck farms, builds the Mafia, and falls when Spoke destroys it from inside ([Fall of the Invisible Mafia](../events/fall-of-the-invisible-mafia.md)).
- He hides at Point Nemo with Squiddo, until Spoke finds it.
- Ep. 155: fishing in the first-generation city, he tells Spoke that they're the same and that Spoke should stop denying what he has become.

## Appears in

### Season 1

- [I Bankrupted Minecraft's Richest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/011-i-bankrupted-minecrafts-richest-civilization.md) (ep. 11, SpokeIsHere, 19 July 2024): A Mafia member is killed by void; Ash is revealed as a manipulator, and the Invisible Mafia's war begins.
- [I Survived Minecraft's Most Corrupt City](../episodes/season-1/018-i-survived-minecrafts-most-corrupt-city.md) (ep. 18, ParrotX2, 2 October 2024): Parrot gets his spyglass back; Ash offers the trio places in the Mafia.
- [I Got Hunted by the Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/019-i-got-hunted-by-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 19, SpokeIsHere, 5 October 2024): Minute explains how Ashswag turned it into an army, and Spoke builds a resistance team.
- [I Infiltrated The Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/022-i-infiltrated-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 22, SpokeIsHere, 18 November 2024): Spoke's cover is blown to Ash, and MinuteTech turns out to have been the diamond-trim watching them.
- [Unstable SMP: The First War](../episodes/season-1/024-unstable-smp-the-first-war.md) (ep. 24, ParrotX2, 7 December 2024): Deanthebean9 is killed by Parrot, set up by Ash.
- [Unstable SMP: Doomsday](../episodes/season-1/025-unstable-smp-doomsday.md) (ep. 25, ParrotX2, 21 January 2025): Wifies dies, apparently, at Ash's hand (later shown to be faked).
- [The End of the Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/027-the-end-of-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 27, SpokeIsHere, 30 January 2025): Ash is spared and leaves, with a warning about Spoke's future.
