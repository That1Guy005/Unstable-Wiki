The flippant leader of the [Treasure Hunters](../factions/treasure-hunters.md), who enjoys making life harder for everyone else, often making a fool of himself in the process. He later runs as a candidate in the election for king.

## Profile

- Status: alive; leader of the [Treasure Hunters](../factions/treasure-hunters.md) and an election candidate.

## Personality

Boosfer is immature and flippant, loves wrecking other players' plans, and makes a fool of himself in serious moments. He's egotistical and condescending, runs bandit crews to leech wealth, throws money around because he can always get more, and bullies his loyal sidekicks Kierrrr and Its_Dev_.

## Story so far

- He hires Wemmbu to destroy Rekrap2's valley and is robbed by him, Parrot and Wifies.
- He loses a rigged election for the End Civilization, and chunk-bans himself running away.
- He runs a minecart shell-game scam until Parrot and Theobald ruin it.
- He hunts treasure in [The Great Sea](../places/the-great-sea.md), and is the only one who knows Wemmbu faked his death; his void trap drops them both into [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md).

### Season 1

- [Why I Destroyed the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/010-why-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 10, Wemmbu, 8 July 2024): At a jungle office, Kierrrr (no helmet) and Its_Dev_ (no boots), "a fashion statement", lead them to Boosfer, who hires them to grief a target.
- [It's Impossible to Find this Player](../episodes/season-1/012-its-impossible-to-find-this-player.md) (ep. 12, ParrotX2, 22 July 2024): At a sky island, "game masters" [Jaden_MAN](jaden-man.md) and [Spongs](spongs.md) set Parrot and [Wifies](wifies.md) seven quests to recover each missing friend's prized possession, while Boosfer's [Treasure Hunters](../factions/treasure-hunters.md) try to steal them.
- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): Their stalker [Deanthebean9](deanthebean9.md), a Mafia member, becomes their friend, and [Horace Altman](horace-altman.md) builds them a city, but Boosfer, spies and [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md)'s bombers find it anyway, and Parrot ends up lighting the fuse himself.
