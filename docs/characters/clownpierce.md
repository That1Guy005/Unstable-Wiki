The server's most feared player, a ruthless Nether trade businessman who goes to extreme lengths to get what he wants. He is hired to assassinate [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md), imprisons half the cast in [Proton](../places/proton.md), is allied with [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) before its fall, and returns in Season 2+ as the leader of the Nether Kingdom.

## Profile

- Status: alive; King of the [Nether Kingdom](../factions/nether-kingdom.md) (as of ep. 161).
- Roles: main antagonist of Season 1 (with [Ashswag](ashswag.md)) and of the Dimensional War; ally of [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) before its fall; Nether trade magnate.
- Known for: theatrical games, prisons and scavenger hunts, and destroying the trial vaults so the remaining maces became the server's last.

## Personality

Clown is ruthless and loves a show: grand entrances, rigged games, social experiments and hunts built to torment his targets. The fear he spreads has made him enemies who form whole alliances just to kill him. As King of the Nether he's turned acquisitive. He controls the dimension's resources, kills or imprisons trespassers, and extends his economic reach through trade deals, blackmail and prisons. He will trade, and even opens the Nether for festivals, as long as no one challenges his control. That overconfidence cost him against NULL. He's patient and calculating too: he claims his goal was always to rule the whole server, and he weakened the Overworld from inside before invading.

## Relationships

- [FerreMC](ferremc.md): his long-time partner and second.
- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): the target of his Season 1 manhunt and his Nether prison, and the one who rallied the Overworld against him.
- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): whom he imprisoned with [PrinceZam](princezam.md) in [Proton](../places/proton.md) and his escape room.
- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md): rival emperor; their Overworld truce lasted only until both sides broke it.
- [Saparata](saparata.md): the king he tricked and then attacked.

## Story so far

- Season 1: hired by [PrinceZam](princezam.md) to kill Parrot, he hunts him from the circus onwards, traps Parrot's friends and Wemmbu in [Proton](../places/proton.md), builds the escape room with Wato1876, and backs the Mafia until the finale.
- Season 2: he claims the Nether, builds a vault (raided by Parrot and Theobald, see [Raid on Clown's Nether Vault](../events/raid-on-clowns-nether-vault.md)), and hunts anyone who enters.
- Season 2+: he sells TNT and resources to the Overworld's factions, imprisons Parrot in his Nether mining prison and plans an invasion in six weeks, pays the [Capitol City Police Force](../factions/capitol-city-police-force.md) to frame Parrot, fights NULL for its capital, and invades the Overworld, taking Highwater City, [LAW City](../places/law-city.md) and [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) before being driven back at [Newtopia](../places/newtopia.md).
