The masked NULL commander, whose name is shown only as a blank. He secretly helps [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md) win [Purgatory](../places/purgatory.md), then serves as his second-in-command in [NULL](../factions/null.md) and the [End Empire](../places/end-empire.md), building the machines behind Spoke's war.

## Profile

- Status: alive; commander of [NULL](../factions/null.md).
- Known for: building NULL's machines, from wither cannons and the shulker tracker to the [Orbital Automatic Pearl Launching Contraption](../mechanics/orbital-automatic-pearl-launching-contraption.md) and the [Orbital Mob Cannon](../mechanics/orbital-mob-cannon.md).

## Personality

The Commander believes NULL should be led by the strongest, which is why he betrayed JamatoP for Spoke. He's practical and often the voice of caution, begging Spoke to go home in the Far Lands and counting NULL's death messages, but loyal enough to obey. He has a dry, odd sense of humour: he gave Spoke a "friend" with a horrible voice changer.

## Story so far

- [Purgatory Arc](../arcs/purgatory-arc.md): he approaches Spoke under the arena and hands him NULL.
- He builds Spoke's machines from the Underworld weapons book, follows him through the Far Lands campaign, and helps him retake NULL's capital from the [Nether Kingdom](../factions/nether-kingdom.md).
