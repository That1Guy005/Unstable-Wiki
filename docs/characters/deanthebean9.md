An unskilled but good-natured player sent by [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) to kill [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and [Wifies](wifies.md). He switches sides instead and becomes a founding member of [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md).

## Profile

- Status: deceased, killed by Parrot in a setup by Ashswag.

## Personality

Dean is portrayed as easy-going and not very bright or skilled.

## Story so far

- An invisible follower Parrot and Wifies unmask and let into [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md).
- Caught robbing Reddoons, he's forced into the Mining City with them.
- He joins the Mafia, and dies at Parrot's hand when Ash sets them up.

## Appears in

- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): Dean joins Parrot and Wifies for good.
- [I Survived Minecraft's Most Corrupt City](../episodes/season-1/018-i-survived-minecrafts-most-corrupt-city.md) (ep. 18, ParrotX2, 2 October 2024): Caught robbing [Reddoons](reddoons.md)' vault, [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md), [Wifies](wifies.md) and Deanthebean9 are forced to mine in the Mining City under [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), with an impossible quota.
- [The Search for Minecraft's Far Lands](../episodes/season-1/021-the-search-for-minecrafts-far-lands.md) (ep. 21, ParrotX2, 4 November 2024): Dean confesses and leaves; Wifies burns the compass, and the friendship cracks.
- [I Infiltrated The Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/022-i-infiltrated-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 22, SpokeIsHere, 18 November 2024): In the chase after Parrot, [Wifies](wifies.md) and Deanthebean9, it turns out Spoke and Mapicc were the hunters chasing Parrot towards the Far Lands.
- [Unstable SMP: The First War](../episodes/season-1/024-unstable-smp-the-first-war.md) (ep. 24, ParrotX2, 7 December 2024): Deanthebean9 is killed by Parrot, set up by Ash.
