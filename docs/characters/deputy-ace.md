[LettuceK](lettucek.md)'s right-hand man in [LAW](../factions/law.md): serious and loyal, but not blindly obedient. He quietly lets [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) escape the cannon island and objects to killing election candidates.

## Profile

- Status: deceased.
- Roles: LettuceK's right hand in [LAW](../factions/law.md); secondary antagonist of the [Kings Arc](../arcs/kings-arc.md).

## Personality

Ace is serious and loyal, but not blindly. He comes to separate authority from power and justice from orders, opposing Lettuce's plans to orbital-strike the Great Sea, execute Wemmbu and assassinate Parrot. He chose to die standing rather than live kneeling.

## Relationships

- [LettuceK](lettucek.md): his leader, whom he defied.
- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md): whom he saved.

## Story so far

- He hunts the exiled Flame and arrests Lomedy.
- He hesitates to fire LAW's sea cannon and lets Wemmbu go.
- Ordered to kill Parrot and Theo, he kills the officers sent with him, arms them and sabotages the prison so they can escape, at the cost of his life.

## Appears in

### Season 2

- [100 Players VS Minecraft Combat Master](../episodes/season-2/080-100-players-vs-minecraft-combat-master.md) (ep. 80, FlameFrags, 18 October 2025): Lettuce introduces his right-hand man, Deputy Ace.
- [Exposing a Player Who Faked His Death](../episodes/season-2/083-exposing-a-player-who-faked-his-death.md) (ep. 83, FlameFrags, 27 October 2025): He makes a fair point: the whole server loves him, so killing him would cost them access to [Merchant City](../places/merchant-city.md), [Redstone Town](../places/redstone-town.md), [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) and more, and Deputy Ace would just replace him.
- [2 VS 1000 Player Minecraft War](../episodes/season-2/085-2-vs-1000-player-minecraft-war.md) (ep. 85, FlameFrags, 1 November 2025): At LAW's headquarters, Deputy Ace asks why Lettuce marched a thousand players into a meat grinder.
- [I Got Hunted on the World's Largest Minecraft Server](../episodes/season-2/092-i-got-hunted-on-the-worlds-largest-minecraft-server.md) (ep. 92, FlameFrags, 29 November 2025): Flame beats Deputy Ace but loses the fight for the farm.
- [I Got Hunted By the Minecraft LAW](../episodes/season-2/095-i-got-hunted-by-the-minecraft-law.md) (ep. 95, Wemmbu, 9 December 2025): Jaden's pirates rescue Wemmbu and Egg; Deputy Ace escapes.
- [I Joined a PIRATE CIVILIZATION in Minecraft](../episodes/season-2/097-i-joined-a-pirate-civilization-in-minecraft.md) (ep. 97, Wemmbu, 14 December 2025): On the main island, Lettuce tells Deputy Ace and The_Oroboros they have everything to build the cannon.
- [My Best Friend Died on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/098-my-best-friend-died-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 98, SpokeIsHere, 19 December 2025): At the gates, Deputy Ace waits with lawmen and guards.
- [I Ran for King on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/099-i-ran-for-king-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 99, ParrotX2, 24 December 2025): Dispensers randomise the pairings and Deputy Ace reads them out: Parrot against Lettuce.
- [Unstable SMP: The Battle for The Great Sea](../episodes/season-2/101-unstable-smp-the-battle-for-the-great-sea.md) (ep. 101, Wemmbu, 28 December 2025): Wemmbu and Egg abandon Pirate City; Deputy Ace quietly lets them go.
- [2 Minecraft Pros VS 1000 Hunters](../episodes/season-2/106-2-minecraft-pros-vs-1000-hunters.md) (ep. 106, ParrotX2, 24 January 2026): When Deputy Ace questions it, Lettuce asks if he's defending them, and Ace apologises.
- [I Became King of the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/112-i-became-king-of-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 112, ParrotX2, 10 February 2026): Deputy Ace is killed by Lettuce after freeing Parrot and Theo.

### Season 2+

- [I Got Hunted By the World's Deadliest Minecraft Player](../episodes/season-2-plus/142-i-got-hunted-by-the-worlds-deadliest-minecraft-player.md) (ep. 142, FlameFrags, 19 June 2026): [SargeLAW](sargelaw.md) says LAW is short on resources, but has stashes from Lettuce's era that only Lettuce and Deputy Ace knew; Flame should ask Lettuce, who Sarge didn't know had been freed.
