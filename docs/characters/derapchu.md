A loyal and funny player who starts out working for Spoke and later joins the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md), usually at [JumperWho](jumperwho.md)'s side. He is the second lead in Parrot's "I Got Hunted by a Minecraft Redstone Genius".

## Profile

- Status: unknown, rumoured to have left the server.
- Roles: former Spoke henchman, Wonder guard and [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) member.

## Personality

Derapchu is sincere and funny, loyal to his cause and to JumperWho, and impatient with people who disagree. Behind the clumsiness is insecurity: used to being called dumb and to needing rescue, he apologises even when no one blames him. That pushes him to do better, and he risked his life to save Parrot from MrCube6.

## Relationships

- [JumperWho](jumperwho.md): always on her team.
- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): who freed him and whom he later saved.

## Story so far

- A Wonder guard who helps Parrot build a counter-cannon, then Spoke's guard, chunk-banned in an ocean prison.
- Freed by Parrot and Rekrap2, he joins the prison-break team that frees Fantst, Jumper and Wifies.
- With BAT he's trapped on MrCube's pressure plate and pursued by TruOriginal.
