[Wemmbu](wemmbu.md)'s best friend and partner, and the comic relief of Wemmbu's POV. Egg is laid-back and not much of a fighter, but he is one of the few people Wemmbu fully trusts, and he is at Wemmbu's side through almost every arc, from building a civilization to being held hostage in the Kingdoms saga.

## Profile

- Status: alive (as of ep. 154).
- Roles: [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md)'s best friend and the namesake of [Eggchan's Civilization](../places/eggchans-civilization.md).
- Known for: being the calm, clever half of the duo, and for being kidnapped, hunted and stasised by nearly everyone in the Kingdoms Arc.

## Personality

Egg is composed and lighthearted, the comic relief who matches Wemmbu's humour at a lower volume. He's often the brains of the pair, and would happily fish for the rest of his time on the server. He's inexperienced next to Wemmbu and not much of a fighter, but he'll put himself in danger for others: he took the arrow that sent him to the End instead of Wemmbu, and argued to protect the Ice Mountain Civilization rather than use its people. He started out a pacifist, convinced wars only breed more wars, but has come round to the idea that sometimes death is the only answer.

## Relationships

- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): his best friend and protector, one of the few people he fully trusts.
- [Jaden_MAN](jaden-man.md): an enemy who became a friend.
- [Arachn1d](arachn1d.md): the king who held his pearls and used him as leverage over Wemmbu.
- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): Wemmbu's rival, who wouldn't take Egg away from Wemmbu in ep. 150.

## Story so far

- Season 1: he partners with Wemmbu in the Zam war and is held in [Proton](../places/proton.md) and by Clown.
- Season 2: he founds the Eggchan Empire, survives exile to the Pirate Islands, and travels the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md) with Wemmbu, then gets pushed through the End portal.
- [Kingdoms Arc](../arcs/kingdoms-arc.md): taken hostage by Arachn1d's alliance, hunted by the [Wraiths](../factions/wraiths.md), and trapped by dozens of [stasis pearls](../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md), he's freed in ep. 150.
- [The Interdimensional War](../arcs/the-interdimensional-war.md): he goes with Wemmbu to the End, the Far Lands and finally [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md).
