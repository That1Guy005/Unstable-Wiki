A former LAWman and later [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md)' newest teammate, who stays with Flame through Antaggg's tournament and the war against [The Warriors](../factions/the-warriors.md).

## Profile

- Status: alive; deuteragonist of the [Warriors Arc](../arcs/warriors-arc.md) and FlameFrags' teammate.

## Story so far

- He appears across the seasons: attacking Wemmbu's Civilization, guarding Proton, mining in Capital City, and in the Far Lands Civilization.
- Second-in-command of LAW until he left over their beliefs, replaced by Deputy Ace.
- He joins Canary's fight against Antaggg, befriends Flame, exposes Antaggg's cheating, and returns to Spawn with him.
