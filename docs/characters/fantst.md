First seen as a hired guard of one of [The Wonders](../places/the-wonders.md), Fantst teams up with [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) to try to stop Spoke's orbital cannon, then turns up repeatedly as one of Spoke's crew and later on various other sides.

## Profile

- Status: alive.

## Personality

Fantst switches sides easily and survives by running: from Wemmbu, from prisons, even into a chunk ban of his own making.

## Relationships

- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): an on-off enemy who once freed him.
- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and [Rekrap2](rekrap2.md): who freed him from an ice prison.

## Story so far

- A Wonder guard, then a member of Spoke's team, chunk-banned by Wemmbu and Zam.
- Freed by Parrot, he chunk-bans himself again; Wemmbu frees him into [Wemmbu's Civilization](../places/wemmbus-civilization.md).
- He joins [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md) and the [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md), both destroyed by the Mafia.
- He takes over and blows up the [Ice Mountain Civilization](../places/ice-mountain-civilization.md), schemes with Baablu to duplicate ender dragons, and hunts Wemmbu with [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md).
- He ends up in LAW's prison with Wemmbu, Zam and Baablu.

## Appears in

### Season 1

- [How to Destroy the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/001-how-to-destroy-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 1, SpokeIsHere, 21 April 2024): [Roshambogames](roshambogames.md), [JumperWho](jumperwho.md), [Derapchu](derapchu.md) and Fantst are paid in duped items to guard the Wonders and keep quiet.
- [This Button Destroys the World](../episodes/season-1/002-this-button-destroys-the-world.md) (ep. 2, ParrotX2, 27 April 2024): The Crone is blown up; its guard Fantst saw a ball of TNT appear.
- [It's Impossible to Catch this Player](../episodes/season-1/006-its-impossible-to-catch-this-player.md) (ep. 6, Wemmbu, 25 May 2024): Chunk banned: TheRealSquiddo, RealReeon, Derapchu, JumperWho, Arcn, Only_A_Squid, Sharooh, Sharkilz_, Fantst, and finally Spoke.
- [I Broke into Minecraft's Most Secure Prison](../episodes/season-1/007-i-broke-into-minecrafts-most-secure-prison.md) (ep. 7, ParrotX2, 9 June 2024): Freed: Derapchu, Fantst (who goes back in), two players who refuse to join, JumperWho, and finally Wifies and Spoke.
- [I Created Minecraft's Greatest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/008-i-created-minecrafts-greatest-civilization.md) (ep. 8, Wemmbu, 15 June 2024): Wemmbu lets Parrot go; Parrot suggests freeing Fantst.
- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): New members Fantst and [LettuceK](lettucek.md) join; Fantst warns of invisible players.
- [Unstable SMP: The First War](../episodes/season-1/024-unstable-smp-the-first-war.md) (ep. 24, ParrotX2, 7 December 2024)

### Season 2

- [How I DESTROYED the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/043-how-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 43, Wemmbu, 28 May 2025): homunculus1031 is killed; Fantst escapes.
- [How I SAVED the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/047-how-i-saved-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 47, Wemmbu, 6 June 2025): Fantst and Baablu's plan is beaten; both escape after losing all their totems.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Fartherlands](../episodes/season-2/054-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-fartherlands.md) (ep. 54, Wemmbu, 15 July 2025): The Avengers are FlameFrags, [Spongs](spongs.md), Willsion, [Jaden_MAN](jaden-man.md), [WyLL](wyll.md), Fantst and Baablu, all after Wemmbu's ban.
- [I Got REVENGE on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/055-i-got-revenge-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 55, FlameFrags, 16 July 2025): Flame wanted to handle it alone, but Jaden insists on a team of Wemmbu's former enemies: [WyLL](wyll.md), Willsion, [Spongs](spongs.md), Fantst and Baablu.
- [This Player Stole My Identity](../episodes/season-2/079-this-player-stole-my-identity.md) (ep. 79, Wemmbu, 14 October 2025): Wemmbu and Fantst are chatting in a [Merchant City](../places/merchant-city.md) restaurant when [LettuceK](lettucek.md) and LAW accuse the invisible player of stealing from Capital City.
- [I Became a Prisoner on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/090-i-became-a-prisoner-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 90, Wemmbu, 22 November 2025): In the [LAW prison](../places/law-city.md), [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) is stripped, put in a prisoner skin and guarded by [Loppezz](loppezz.md), alongside old enemies Fantst and Baablu.
- [Escaping Prison on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/093-escaping-prison-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 93, Wemmbu, 6 December 2025): Wemmbu, Zam, Fantst and Baablu escape the LAW prison (see [The LAW Prison](../events/the-law-prison.md)).
