[ClownPierce](clownpierce.md)'s coach and right-hand man, and the second-highest-ranking member of [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md). He later serves the Nether Kingdom.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: ClownPierce's teammate and the Mafia's second-highest member, leader of its diamond tier.

## Story so far

- He hunts Parrot with Clown, takes Wifies hostage, and destroys Parrot's jungle base.
- Wemmbu and Eggchan blow up his tower.
- He guards Clown's prison, burns ManePear's tree, and gives Wemmbu a mace to save his life.
- In ep. 161 he attacks [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) with Clown's army.

## Appears in

### Season 1

- [I Got Hunted by Minecraft's Deadliest Players](../episodes/season-1/009-i-got-hunted-by-minecrafts-deadliest-players.md) (ep. 9, ParrotX2, 30 June 2024): While solving the vault's puzzles (falling through a shulker into layer one, finding an evacuation port), they hear FerreMC, Clown and Mugm_ inside.
- [Why I Destroyed the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/010-why-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 10, Wemmbu, 8 July 2024): The jobs take them into Spoke's base, FerreMC's tower and the [Treasure Hunters](../factions/treasure-hunters.md)' office, and lead to an alliance with [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and [Wifies](wifies.md) that ends with an [Orbital Strike Cannon](../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) heist and a declaration of war.
- [How I Escaped Minecraft's Largest Prison](../episodes/season-1/014-how-i-escaped-minecrafts-largest-prison.md) (ep. 14, Wemmbu, 17 August 2024): With [Eggchan](eggchan.md) and, later, [Kenadian](kenadian.md), Wemmbu gears up, survives Clown's "juggernaut" FerreMC, an orbital bombardment and Zam's mood swings, and builds a [Pearl Cannon](../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) to shoot himself over the walls and chunk bans.
- [Unstable SMP: The Mafia](../episodes/season-1/026-unstable-smp-the-mafia.md) (ep. 26, Wemmbu, 21 January 2025): [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md) and FerreMC burn down ManePear's treehouse, and [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) vows to kill Clown.

### Season 2

- [Unstable SMP: The Eggchan Empire](../episodes/season-2/030-unstable-smp-the-eggchan-empire.md) (ep. 30, Wemmbu, 27 February 2025)
- [How I SURVIVED a Hardcore Minecraft Tournament](../episodes/season-2/049-how-i-survived-a-hardcore-minecraft-tournament.md) (ep. 49, Wemmbu, 25 June 2025): Elsewhere, Flame beats Willsion, Wyll and FerreMC win, [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md) accidentally kills his opponent outright, and Sharpness beats [Horace Altman](horace-altman.md) with his fists.
- [Dominating Minecraft's Deadliest PVP Tournament](../episodes/season-2/050-dominating-minecrafts-deadliest-pvp-tournament.md) (ep. 50, FlameFrags, 28 June 2025): FlameFrags wins the tournament, beating Willsion, Ferre, Theo and Wemmbu (see [The PvP Tournament](../events/the-pvp-tournament.md)).
- [How I "DIED" on a Hardcore Minecraft SMP](../episodes/season-2/073-how-i-died-on-a-hardcore-minecraft-smp.md) (ep. 73, Wemmbu, 24 September 2025): Waiting at the portal on his last invisibility, he sees Flame arrive to collect ender pearls, and FerreMC and the Nether guards deliver the shipment.
- [Hunting a Player Who Faked His Death](../episodes/season-2/074-hunting-a-player-who-faked-his-death.md) (ep. 74, FlameFrags, 27 September 2025): When the shipment comes, the city's guards are made to bow to FerreMC, Clown's right-hand man.
- [I Infiltrated Minecraft's Deadliest Faction](../episodes/season-2/076-i-infiltrated-minecrafts-deadliest-faction.md) (ep. 76, FlameFrags, 7 October 2025): Ferre is kidnapped and released.
- [The Hunt for Minecraft's Deadliest Vault](../episodes/season-2/077-the-hunt-for-minecrafts-deadliest-vault.md) (ep. 77, ParrotX2, 9 October 2025): During [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md)'s Nether festival, Parrot and [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md) team up with [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md), extract FerreMC from Clown's fortress, and plant a locator map on him.

### Season 2+

- [10,000 Player Minecraft War: Nether VS Overworld](../episodes/season-2-plus/161-10-000-player-minecraft-war-nether-vs-overworld.md) (ep. 161, ParrotX2, 2 November 2026): Clown, FerreMC and TorrinTheTitan attack; Theo, Parrot and Saparata defend, falling back to the second wall, until Clown takes the villagers.
