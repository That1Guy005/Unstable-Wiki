One of the four creators and protagonists. Flame first appears as one of [PrinceZam](princezam.md)'s guards and a Season 1 antagonist, and gets his own POV from Season 2 onward. He is one of the strongest PvP players on the server and [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md)'s great rival, and his arcs (Toxic War, Betrayal, Skill, Training, Warriors and Rebirth) revolve around proving he is the strongest.

## Profile

- Status: alive, presumed to be in the End (as of ep. 161).
- Roles: former high-ranking member of the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md), diamond tier of [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), member of [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md), and temporary leader of [LAW](../factions/law.md) in ep. 160.
- Known for: his title "Immortal Demon" and the claim to be the strongest player; he refuses to use maces, calling them dishonourable.

## Personality

Flame is a fighter whose self-image rests on his skill and his endurance: he's survived impossibly long fights and wears his title like a trophy. He can be condescending to weaker players, quick to pick a fight, and stubborn about defending his reputation, always taking on stronger opponents to prove himself. He struggles to show feelings outside battle and fears letting people close and losing them, yet he goes to great lengths for his best friend [Lomedy](lomedy.md). He prefers working alone and dislikes help in fights, though he has slowly let friends in. After Terminus he decided that his strength obliges him to help the server.

## Relationships

- [Lomedy](lomedy.md): his best friend, a pacifist farmer whose safety pulls Flame back again and again.
- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): his great rival; they've fought for the title many times and still owe each other a fair duel.
- [NotSkrilley](notskrilley.md) and [Spongs](spongs.md): the friends who live at [FlameFrags' Base](../places/flamefrags-base.md) with him.

## Story so far

- Season 1: a guard of the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) and later a Mafia diamond trim, Flame is an antagonist in Wemmbu's and Parrot's stories.
- [Skill Arc](../arcs/skill-arc.md) and [Toxic War Arc](../arcs/toxic-war-arc.md): rebuilding his netherite, he fights toxic clans that blow up his base and kidnap his wolf, and meets Wemmbu in the tournament.
- [Betrayal Arc](../arcs/betrayal-arc.md): Jaden disappears, LAW gets involved, and Flame forms [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md) against Wemmbu before Jaden betrays him.
- His quests and fights continue: the Great Farm War, the rumour of Wemmbu's death, Clown's Nether, LAW's new base next door, and unmasking the Invisible Knight. Then he and Wemmbu take on LettuceK's 1,000-player army ([The 2 VS 1000 War](../events/the-2-vs-1000-war.md)).
- [Training Arc](../arcs/training-arc.md): exiled and hunted by LAW, he learns from Swight, ManePear, ItzRealMe and Theobald, and fights in the war's end.
- [Kingdoms Arc](../arcs/kingdoms-arc.md): he works off a debt to LAW, assassinates Mistrul in [The Mist](../places/the-mist.md), rescues Lomedy, and fights for Cindercrest in [The Civil War](../events/the-civil-war.md).
- [Warriors Arc](../arcs/warriors-arc.md): rematches with TheG0dofWar and the fight against Antaggg's cheating Warriors.
- [Rebirth Arc](../arcs/rebirth-arc.md): tricked into [Terminus](../places/terminus.md), he escapes with LAW's help, leads LAW to victory over Cindercrest, rescues Skrilley, and resolves to save the server.
