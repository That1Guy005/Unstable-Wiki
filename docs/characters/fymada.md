A [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) citizen who once struggled to pay [Reddoons](reddoons.md)' taxes, ran for king, and was made mayor of Capital City by King [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md), later taking control of its police force.

## Profile

- Status: alive; former mayor of [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md).

## Story so far

- A struggling taxpayer under Reddoons, helped by Parrot and Theo.
- She runs for queen, befriends Parrot and Horace in the purge, and loses votes to Arachn1d.
- She fights LAW and votes to spare LettuceK, becomes mayor, and helps Parrot prove his innocence against the [Capitol City Police Force](../factions/capitol-city-police-force.md).
- She commands the Royal Army's police, and is jailed after the civil war until Parrot frees her to evacuate the city in ep. 161.

### Season 2+

- [100 Days as King of the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2-plus/118-100-days-as-king-of-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 118, ParrotX2, 28 February 2026): Fymada becomes mayor of Capital City; SirPig_ is removed.
