A deadpan, hard-working master builder who tries to stay out of conflicts, and who is also a builder for the series behind the scenes. In-story he builds [Wemmbu's Civilization](../places/wemmbus-civilization.md), [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md), Capital City, the Skyblock Civilization and District 13, and later leads the Far Lands Civilization.

## Profile

- Status: alive; mayor of [Newtopia](../places/newtopia.md).
- Known for: building [Wemmbu's Civilization](../places/wemmbus-civilization.md), [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md), [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), the [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md), [District 13](../factions/district-13.md) and [Eggchan's Civilization](../places/eggchans-civilization.md). He also builds for the series behind the scenes.

## Personality

Horace is hardworking and friendly, a gifted builder with deadpan humour who stays out of fights. He's proud of his builds and furious when they're destroyed, which gives him a grudge against the Mafia and a little one against Parrot, who promised the End Civilization wouldn't fall.

## Story so far

- He builds for Zam, Wemmbu and Parrot, and leads the [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md) until the Mafia destroys it.
- He joins District 13, then the Mafia, then Eggchan's civilization.
- He runs for king, befriends Parrot and Fymada during the purge, fights LAW, and votes to kill LettuceK.
- He builds Parrot's Spawn castle and rebuilds his civilization, then hosts the Overworld's last stand at Newtopia.
