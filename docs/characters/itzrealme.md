The world's number-one PvP player, who hunts [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) at the start of the Director Arc. Long retired and hunted by LAW himself, he later trains [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md).

## Profile

- Status: alive; once called the world's best PvP player.

## Personality

Itz is stoic and cold, curt even with fans, and completely focused on the job. He's confident and clever, and believes actions speak louder than words, which is the heart of his teaching about "the Zone".

## Story so far

- Hired by the Director, he hunts Parrot through BAT's defences with a mace, and later tells Parrot who hired him.
- Retired at the infinity base, he trains Flame in playstyles, far-pearling, grappling and the Zone.

## Appears in

- [I Got Hunted by the World's Best Minecraft Player](../episodes/season-2/029-i-got-hunted-by-the-worlds-best-minecraft-player.md) (ep. 29, ParrotX2, 17 February 2025): Itz stops hunting Parrot and names the Director as the one behind it.
- [I Got Trained By the World's Best Minecraft Player](../episodes/season-2/104-i-got-trained-by-the-worlds-best-minecraft-player.md) (ep. 104, FlameFrags, 20 January 2026): Flame passes Itz's test; one trainer remains.
