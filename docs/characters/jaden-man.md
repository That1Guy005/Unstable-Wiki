A talented trident-and-mace fighter who first appears as one of the "game masters" leading Parrot to [Proton](../places/proton.md). He goes on to found the Pirates and is the main antagonist of the Pirate and Betrayal arcs, before becoming one of [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md)'s closest friends in the Kings Arc and the Kingdoms saga.

## Profile

- Status: deceased, killed by Nitenly on Spoke's orders (ep. 154).
- Roles: founder and leader of the [Pirates](../factions/pirates.md); former [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) member; main antagonist of the [Pirate Arc](../arcs/pirate-arc.md) and [Betrayal Arc](../arcs/betrayal-arc.md).
- Known for: trident-macing, a match for Wemmbu near water or in rain.

## Personality

Jaden wanted to build the greatest pirate civilization, and was greedy and sly about it, manipulating FlameFrags among others. He was loyal to his friends and held grudges when they betrayed him. In later arcs he became generous, promising to help Wemmbu whenever asked, and said that chasing power never satisfied him: he was happiest with his friends. He regretted his fight with Flame.

## Relationships

- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): an enemy who became a close friend.
- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): a best friend he betrayed.
- [Eggchan](eggchan.md): whom he once held to ransom and later fought beside.

## Story so far

- He raids Wemmbu, exiles him and Egg to the Pirate Islands, and leads him into [The Abandoned Civilization](../places/the-abandoned-civilization.md).
- He forms [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md) with Flame, betrays him, and steals Lomedy's mace.
- He shelters Wemmbu in Pirate City, fights LAW's sea cannon, joins Wemmbu in the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md), and dies covering Wemmbu's escape from NULL.
