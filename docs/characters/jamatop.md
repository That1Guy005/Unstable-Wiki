[SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md)'s first ally on the server, who shows him the vault of ancient exploits in the very first episode. He starts out insisting on firm morals and refuses to "play God" with the game's code, but eventually becomes Spoke's arch-nemesis as the leader of NULL and the head of Purgatory.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: Spoke's first ally, later his arch-nemesis; former leader of [NULL](../factions/null.md) and head of [Purgatory](../places/purgatory.md); one of the first players on the server.

## Personality

Jamato starts out principled: he refuses to tamper with the game's code, calling Spoke's exploits "playing God", and he protected Spoke in the Mafia days by disappearing. Years in hiding make him self-righteous and condescending. He blackmails Spoke into silence twice, and his intelligence and focus let him take NULL from its previous leader. His great flaw is denial: he can't admit he's gone too far. Even beaten, he insists he only wanted his old friend back. He won't kill Spoke himself, because that would prove he's no better.

## Relationships

- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md): his former best friend and the reason for most of his choices.
- [Commander](commander.md): NULL's commander, who betrayed him for Spoke.
- [Mapicc](mapicc.md) and [JumperWho](jumperwho.md): whom he steered through the Far Lands.

## Story so far

- One of the earliest players, he left Spoke when the exploits began, then vanished during the Mafia arc.
- [Missing Players Arc](../arcs/missing-players-arc.md): he guides Spoke's group through the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md) while secretly blackmailing him with a full log of his crimes.
- [Purgatory Arc](../arcs/purgatory-arc.md): as head of Purgatory and leader of NULL, he runs the Hunger Games, until the Commander betrays him and Spoke takes NULL. The book Spoke stole from him later leads to the Citadel.
