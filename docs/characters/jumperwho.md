A clever, strategic player who starts as one of Spoke's crew. She later becomes a [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) member, leads her own network of spies, runs for queen, and is a key ally in the Missing Players and Purgatory arcs. She is almost always on the same team as [Derapchu](derapchu.md).

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: former member of the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) and [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), leader of [JumperWho's Spies](../factions/jumperwhos-spies.md), and a candidate for queen.

## Personality

Jumper thinks critically, like Parrot, and holds firm to the truth and her morals, though her methods can be coldly pragmatic, even sacrificing others. She won't act without proof, which is why she refused to kill Spoke or neutralise Parrot when he was accused of being the Director. She reads people well and wins trust by being honest about her motives. She protects her spies fiercely and is widely seen as one of the most trustworthy players on the server.

## Relationships

- [Leow0ok](leow0ok.md): her leader, whom she broke with over his extreme measures against Spoke.
- [Derapchu](derapchu.md): always on her team.
- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md): the target of her investigations, later her companion in the Far Lands.
- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): an old ally, who finds her hiding with her spies in ep. 161.

## Story so far

- Freed from a chunk ban by Parrot, she joins him against Clown and in Proton.
- As BAT's spymaster she investigates Spoke over the dead spies, then turns on Leo's methods.
- [Missing Players Arc](../arcs/missing-players-arc.md): she goes with Spoke and Mapicc into the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md).
- Ep. 161: hiding beneath a warehouse with her spies, she warns Parrot that Spoke is trying to take over the server and helps him reach Spawn.
