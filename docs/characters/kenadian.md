A careful, highly knowledgeable professional prison escapist who helps [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) break into the chunk-ban prisons. She later turns out to be the designer of [Proton](../places/proton.md), which she built for Clown, and she helps Wemmbu escape it. She also posts her own behind-the-scenes videos.

## Profile

- Status: alive.

## Personality

Ken is clever, adaptable and cautious, an expert in mechanics and prison escapes who likes to be prepared. Her fear of losing friends made her try to keep everyone in Proton rather than risk escaping, nearly sabotaging Parrot's plan, but in the end she gave him the last elytra, trusting the others to free her through her stasis chamber.

## Relationships

- [Wato1876](wato1876.md): her close ally.

## Story so far

- She helps free Wifies with a Wither Bore and is trapped in Proton.
- She joins the [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md)'s farmers and [District 13](../factions/district-13.md), then leaves for the Mafia.
- She helps break the Mafia's spawn prison, and defends Wemmbu's base from Theobald.

## Appears in

### Season 1

- [I Broke into Minecraft's Most Secure Prison](../episodes/season-1/007-i-broke-into-minecrafts-most-secure-prison.md) (ep. 7, ParrotX2, 9 June 2024): With [Derapchu](derapchu.md), [JumperWho](jumperwho.md) and the professional escapist Kenadian, they finally crack the most secure prison of all and free Wifies, and Spoke.
- [It's Impossible to Find this Player](../episodes/season-1/012-its-impossible-to-find-this-player.md) (ep. 12, ParrotX2, 22 July 2024): [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md)'s friends [Rekrap2](rekrap2.md), [JumperWho](jumperwho.md), Kenadian, [Derapchu](derapchu.md) and [Leow0ok](leow0ok.md) have vanished, along with [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md).
- [How I Escaped Minecraft's Largest Prison](../episodes/season-1/014-how-i-escaped-minecrafts-largest-prison.md) (ep. 14, Wemmbu, 17 August 2024): With [Eggchan](eggchan.md) and, later, Kenadian, Wemmbu gears up, survives Clown's "juggernaut" [FerreMC](ferremc.md), an orbital bombardment and Zam's mood swings, and builds a [Pearl Cannon](../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) to shoot himself over the walls and chunk bans.
- [Escaping Proton: Minecraft's Most Secure Prison](../episodes/season-1/015-escaping-proton-minecrafts-most-secure-prison.md) (ep. 15, ParrotX2, 31 August 2024): Parrot catalogues their habits (one sprint-jumps everywhere, another keeps turning around) and gets to know Kenadian, [Derapchu](derapchu.md) and [JumperWho](jumperwho.md) as they rotate.
- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): Kenadian warns that stasis chambers work across dimensions, which means the Mafia could pull Dean's.
- [Unstable SMP: The First War](../episodes/season-1/024-unstable-smp-the-first-war.md) (ep. 24, ParrotX2, 7 December 2024): [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) joins its Warriors, meets [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md), forms an alliance with the neglected Farmers (including Kenadian and a remorseful [Wifies](wifies.md)), and challenges the council's leader Purpled for actually fighting the war.
- [Unstable SMP: Doomsday](../episodes/season-1/025-unstable-smp-doomsday.md) (ep. 25, ParrotX2, 21 January 2025): Kenadian fixes the entrance and [Wato1876](wato1876.md) shows where new players' pearls are thrown.
- [Unstable SMP: The Mafia](../episodes/season-1/026-unstable-smp-the-mafia.md) (ep. 26, Wemmbu, 21 January 2025): Inside, he makes up with [Wato1876](wato1876.md) (not really a Mafia member) for ditching him in the sky city, meets Kenadian, and follows the pearls by throwing one and diving after it.
- [The End of the Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/027-the-end-of-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 27, SpokeIsHere, 30 January 2025)
