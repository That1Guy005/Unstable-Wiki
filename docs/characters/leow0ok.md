A businessman who runs teams of bounty hunters with one goal: killing [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md). He protects [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) as bait for Clown in Season 1 and leads the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) in Season 2, before becoming the main antagonist of Spoke's BAT Arc. He dies at Spoke's hands.

## Profile

- Status: deceased; he fell into the void alone, and JumperWho says Spoke killed him (ep. 161).
- Roles: Season 1 team leader, leader of the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) in Season 2, major antagonist of the [BAT Arc](../arcs/bat-arc.md).

## Personality

Leo never gives up. He can think things through, but reaches quickly for violence and drastic measures, and when he believes he's right he has little restraint. That makes him morally grey: his determination, like Parrot's, sometimes hides his own mistakes from him.

## Relationships

- [Nufuli](nufuli.md): his best friend and right hand, killed by the Director's railgun.
- [JumperWho](jumperwho.md): his closest ally, who left over his methods against Spoke.
- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md): his ally in the Mafia, later his enemy.
- [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md): the man he swore to kill.

## Story so far

- Season 1: his team saves Parrot from Clown, helps break Proton, and he infiltrates the Mafia with Spoke.
- [The Director Arc](../arcs/the-director-arc.md): he takes Parrot into BAT, loses Nufuli and his headquarters, and hunts the Director.
- [BAT Arc](../arcs/bat-arc.md): chasing the missing armour, he hands Spoke to LAW and leads a thousand-strong army into Spoke's traps, losing his spies, wealth and friends. He fell into the void alone.
