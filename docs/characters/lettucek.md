One of the main antagonists of Season 2. Lettuce starts as comic relief, then becomes the force behind [LAW](../factions/law.md) and the main antagonist of the Kings Arc, imprisoning his enemies and fighting the whole server in the battle for the crown.

## Profile

- Status: alive; former leader of [LAW](../factions/law.md), former captain of [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md), and one of the three kings united at the end of ep. 161.
- Known for: strategy and speeches, and for inventing the crown: he was the server's first self-declared king.

## Personality

In Season 1 Lettuce is goofy, easily intimidated and quick to give in to stronger players. Even then he dreamed of independence. Through Season 2 he becomes intelligent and opportunistic, using fear, leverage and violence. He's a meticulous planner who gets the most out of any army or gimmick, however costly, and sees unrest coming before others do. He rarely questions his own morality, and his idea of the greater good drifts steadily darker, which is what drove Deputy_Ace to betray him.

## Relationships

- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): who freed him from the Mining City; Lettuce later jailed him, and now stands beside him.
- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): his great opponent in the LAW years, and the ally who freed him from Cindercrest's leash.
- [Deputy Ace](deputy-ace.md): his right hand, who turned against his orders.
- [Saparata](saparata.md): who used him as a captive mouthpiece.
- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): his prisoner in the Kings Arc.

## Story so far

- Early on: comic relief in the Mafia era, then a pirate in Jaden's crew who hands his items to Wemmbu.
- He founds LAW, hunts Flame, Wemmbu and Parrot, and fights them in [The 2 VS 1000 War](../events/the-2-vs-1000-war.md).
- [Election Arc](../arcs/election-arc.md) and [Kings Arc](../arcs/kings-arc.md): he calls the election, declares himself king, jails Parrot, and loses [The War Against LAW](../events/the-war-against-law.md).
- Freed by Saparata, he's forced to be Cindercrest's captain and voice, overseeing Flame in [Terminus](../places/terminus.md).
- Freed for good by Flame and LAW in ep. 160, he helps beat Cindercrest's invasion and rescues Skrilley; in ep. 161 he defends [LAW City](../places/law-city.md) and speaks for the monarchy at [Newtopia](../places/newtopia.md).
