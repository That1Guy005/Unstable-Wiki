[FlameFrags](flamefrags.md)' best friend and teammate, introduced in early Season 2. Lomedy is a caring pacifist devoted to his farm, and he builds the traps, TNT dupers and bases that Flame relies on. His loyalty pulls Flame into the Great Farm War and later into [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md)'s side of the civil war.

## Profile

- Status: alive, hunted by Cindercrest (ep. 160).
- Roles: FlameFrags' best friend and deuteragonist.

## Personality

Lomedy is supportive and caring, devoted to his farm and a pacifist who tries to calm things down, though he'll fight when he must. He understands why others fight, and keeps his friends going. He hates seeing friends hurt when they're helpless, and blamed Parrot for the famine, which led him into Cindercrest. He has a strict moral compass.

## Relationships

- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): his best friend.
- [Spongs](spongs.md): rival farmer in the Great Farm War, later a housemate.

## Story so far

- Flame turns up at his farm in Season 2; he survives raids and the Toxic Civilization.
- Jaden steals his mace; he fights the Great Farm War.
- LettuceK holds him captive; he refuses Flame's rescue in LAW's prison, blaming Flame, then forgives him.
- Lost in [The Mist](../places/the-mist.md), he's found by Flame and Skrilley.
- Ep. 160: he leaves a book saying he's alive but hunted.
