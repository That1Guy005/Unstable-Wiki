A recurring player who turns up on many sides: a [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) guard, a member of the [Ice Mountain Civilization](../places/ice-mountain-civilization.md) and the [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md), a frequent companion of [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](eggchan.md), and later a [LAW](../factions/law.md) soldier and prison guard under [LettuceK](lettucek.md).

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: founder of the [Ice Mountain Civilization](../places/ice-mountain-civilization.md), former [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md) member and LAW soldier.

## Story so far

- A Zam prison guard and prisoner of Clown, she blames Wemmbu for Skyblock's fall.
- Exiled from Ice Mountain, she asks Wemmbu for help; Fantst blows it up anyway, and Wemmbu gives her netherite.
- As a LAW guard she watches Wemmbu in prison, and loses her job when the prisoners escape.
