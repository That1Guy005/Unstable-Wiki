A kind-hearted player who starts as a [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) guard and later helps [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) in the Far Lands Civilization journey, teaching newcomers to mine in Capital City.

## Profile

- Status: deceased, executed by Reddoons.

## Personality

Luigi was kind, welcoming new players and teaching them to mine in Capital City's mines.

## Story so far

- His journal tells of his time in the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md), helping chunk-ban Spoke and guarding his prison.
- Forced to mine under [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), he covers Parrot, Wifies and Dean's quota.
- Reddoons rigs a roulette game against him and, when the guard CoinMonke refuses, kills him himself. His compass leads Parrot to the [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md).

## Appears in

- [I Uncovered Minecraft's Greatest Secret](../episodes/season-1/005-i-uncovered-minecrafts-greatest-secret.md) (ep. 5, ParrotX2, 17 May 2024): Wemmbu, Zam, Merffinn and LuigiToan arrive; they had been tracking Spoke all along, which is why they wanted the hunt so badly.
- [It's Impossible to Catch this Player](../episodes/season-1/006-its-impossible-to-catch-this-player.md) (ep. 6, Wemmbu, 25 May 2024): With guards Merffinn and LuigiToan, Wemmbu follows Spoke's arrows and finds Parrot chasing Spoke through the sky; Spoke leads them into the final challenge rooms.
- [Unstable SMP: The PrinceZam Empire War](../episodes/season-1/013-unstable-smp-the-princezam-empire-war.md) (ep. 13, Wemmbu, 29 July 2024): They fight the guards ([Pangi](pangi.md), Dol9hin, [Loppezz](loppezz.md), LuigiToan, [WyLL](wyll.md) and What_Max) while Wemmbu goes for Zam.
- [I Survived Minecraft's Most Corrupt City](../episodes/season-1/018-i-survived-minecrafts-most-corrupt-city.md) (ep. 18, ParrotX2, 2 October 2024): LuigiToan is killed by Reddoons; CoinMonke is fired.
- [The Search for Minecraft's Far Lands](../episodes/season-1/021-the-search-for-minecrafts-far-lands.md) (ep. 21, ParrotX2, 4 November 2024): On the run after Mining City, [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md), [Wifies](wifies.md) and [Deanthebean9](deanthebean9.md) follow LuigiToan's compass towards the legendary [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md) to rally help against [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md).
