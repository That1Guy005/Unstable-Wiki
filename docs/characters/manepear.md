A cynical but highly skilled spear fighter who starts as a [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) guard and switches to [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md)'s side in the Empire War. He later trains both Wemmbu ("I Mastered Minecraft Combat") and [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md) ("I Got Trained By a Minecraft Spear Master").

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Known for: mastering the spear first, with a style built on speed and burst damage, no totems or shields.

## Personality

Mane is cynical but enormously skilled. He plays for fun rather than sweat, and told Flame to stop caring what others think. He has betrayed people out of boredom, yet keeps a brotherly bond with Flame.

## Relationships

- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): his student and "brother".
- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): whom he trained, then gave up on as a "lost cause".

## Story so far

- A high-level Zam guard who betrayed Zam "for fun" and helped Wemmbu kidnap her officers.
- In the Mafia arc he trains Wemmbu, until he learns the diamond-trim he fought was Flame and asks for Wemmbu's mace.
- Hiding in caves, he masters the spear and trains Flame in exchange for a robbery, then breaks Flame out of LAW and loots its vault as payment.
