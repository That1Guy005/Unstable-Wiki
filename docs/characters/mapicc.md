[SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md)'s best friend and teammate for much of the series. Mapicc is laid-back and mischievous like Spoke, but has firmer limits and is far less ruthless. He is Spoke's partner through the Exploit and Mafia arcs and the secret-base raids, until the two drift apart in the Missing Players and Purgatory arcs.

## Profile

- Status: alive, trapped in [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md) (ep. 152).
- Roles: Spoke's best friend and partner from Season 1 to the Purgatory Arc.

## Personality

Mapicc shares Spoke's mischief but has firmer limits and less malice. He's the fighter of the pair: strong enough to hold off ManePear, and known as a gladiator in Purgatory. He trusts too easily and follows Spoke's lead, which makes him easy to lie to, and he hates being deceived. Even so, he'll work with people he hates against a bigger threat, and has passed up killing Spoke three times for that reason.

## Relationships

- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md): his best friend, whose betrayals finally broke them apart.
- [JumperWho](jumperwho.md) and [JamatoP](jamatop.md): companions in the Far Lands.
- [MinuteTech](minutetech.md): who saved him and Spoke from the Mafia.

## Story so far

- He's at Spoke's side through the exploits, the Mafia, the secret bases and the BAT war, where he vanishes and is presumed dead.
- He leads Spoke to NULL's tampered pearl cannon, survives Purgatory, and is knocked into the Underworld by Spoke.
- Ep. 152: he and Spoke solve the Underworld castle's puzzles together, but Spoke escapes and seals him in. He swears to pop the Totem of NULL and kill Spoke.
