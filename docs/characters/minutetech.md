A stoic, cautious former diamond-tier Mafia member and one of the few players with maces. He saves Spoke and Mapicc from the Mafia, becomes the guardian of the End, helps [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) repeatedly, and is overthrown by Spoke's [NULL](../factions/null.md), later turning up alive as a prisoner in NULL's End prison.

## Profile

- Status: alive, held by NULL and sent back to the first floor of Spoke's End prison (ep. 158).
- Roles: former Mafia diamond tier and Guardian of the End.
- Known for: wielding two of the server's last maces, with three more stashed.

## Personality

Minute is stoic and careful after years on the run from the Mafia: traps everywhere, a base that keeps moving, always a plan first. He calls teammates a liability, yet keeps risking himself for friends, shares god apples, armour and XP, and once sacrificed himself so Spoke and Planet could escape. He's a superb fighter, and his presence alone reassures his allies. He has a strong moral sense.

## Relationships

- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md): the friend he saved in Season 1, who exiled and imprisoned him.
- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): his student in the End prison, whom he used and then trusted.
- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): an ally against NULL.

## Story so far

- Season 1: a Mafia deserter who rescues Spoke and Mapicc.
- He guards the End until NULL overthrows him ([NULL's Conquest of the End](../events/nulls-conquest-of-the-end.md)).
- Ep. 154–155: he recruits Wemmbu against Spoke and holds the Far Lands entrance; the Wraiths capture him.
- Ep. 158: he trains Parrot through the prison's floors, admits he planned to use him, and lets Parrot go free instead of himself.
