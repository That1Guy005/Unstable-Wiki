A redstone genius who sabotages [BAT Headquarters](../places/bat-headquarters.md) with bombs and traps on the Director's behalf.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Known for: genius redstone traps, always several steps ahead.

## Story so far

- He hunts Parrot inside BAT Headquarters with a void trap, a pressure-plate bomb and a TNT bomber.
- Caught, he escapes, steals BAT's gear for 4CVIT, and poses as the Director until milk unmasks him.

## Appears in

- [I Got Hunted by a Minecraft Redstone Genius](../episodes/season-2/031-i-got-hunted-by-a-minecraft-redstone-genius.md) (ep. 31, ParrotX2, 9 March 2025): MrCube escapes again, and the Director contacts Parrot directly.
- [Hunting a Minecraft Player Who Doesn't Exist](../episodes/season-2/045-hunting-a-minecraft-player-who-doesnt-exist.md) (ep. 45, ParrotX2, 31 May 2025): [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) chase the Director: through a zombie trap, [Redstone Town](../places/redstone-town.md), a fake Director (MrCube6), a fake intruder ([SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md)) and a planted clue.
