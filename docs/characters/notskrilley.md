An explorer who had been trapped in [The Mist](../places/the-mist.md), and knew it better than almost anyone. He helps [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md) find [Lomedy](lomedy.md), becomes one of Flame's closest friends, and shares the trap of [Terminus](../places/terminus.md) with him.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: FlameFrags' friend and deuteragonist of the [Rebirth Arc](../arcs/rebirth-arc.md).

## Personality

Skrilley is an eager explorer who's always up for an adventure, sometimes rushing ahead into danger. He's loyal, and promised to stand by Flame whatever happens.

## Relationships

- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): his friend, who brought him out of Terminus.
- [Lomedy](lomedy.md) and [Spongs](spongs.md): housemates at [FlameFrags' Base](../places/flamefrags-base.md).

## Story so far

- An explorer trapped in [The Mist](../places/the-mist.md), he helps Flame find Lomedy and escapes with him.
- He talks Flame into the Windgrove vault hunt, which turns out to be [Terminus](../places/terminus.md); he's held there as a "Level 4" prisoner until Flame and Lettuce rescue him in ep. 160.
