[Leow0ok](leow0ok.md)'s right-hand man and best friend in the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md), in charge of combat and the base. He kills TruOriginal with an orbital strike cannon and is killed moments later on the Director's orders.

## Profile

- Status: deceased, killed by the Director's railgun.

## Personality

Nufuli was kind and dependable, Leo's right hand, and a teammate who kept saving others.

## Story so far

- He shows Parrot around [BAT Headquarters](../places/bat-headquarters.md) and runs strike teams.
- He survives TruOriginal's attack, and on a night watch shares the stars with Parrot, who gives him his spyglass.
- He bans TruOriginal with a mirror receiver linked to BAT's orbital cannon.
