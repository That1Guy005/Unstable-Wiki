An upbeat, loyal player at [PrinceZam](princezam.md)'s side in the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md), who tries to stay out of conflict. He is imprisoned in [Proton](../places/proton.md) along with Zam and later lives with the Skyblock Civilization.

## Profile

- Status: alive.

## Personality

Pangi is positive, excitable and loyal, and tries to stay out of trouble and be useful.

## Story so far

- A Zam guard, loyal to Zam even in Clown's prison, where they built a mini empire; chunk-banned trying to escape through the Nether.
- A member of the [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md), he gives Wemmbu and Eggchan a barrel of XP bottles.

## Appears in

- [I Created Minecraft's Greatest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/008-i-created-minecrafts-greatest-civilization.md) (ep. 8, Wemmbu, 15 June 2024)
- [Unstable SMP: The PrinceZam Empire War](../episodes/season-1/013-unstable-smp-the-princezam-empire-war.md) (ep. 13, Wemmbu, 29 July 2024): They fight the guards (Pangi, Dol9hin, [Loppezz](loppezz.md), [LuigiToan](luigitoan.md), [WyLL](wyll.md) and What_Max) while Wemmbu goes for Zam.
- [How I Escaped Minecraft's Largest Prison](../episodes/season-1/014-how-i-escaped-minecrafts-largest-prison.md) (ep. 14, Wemmbu, 17 August 2024): Pangi is chunk banned.
- [I Discovered a Minecraft Sky Civilization](../episodes/season-1/020-i-discovered-a-minecraft-sky-civilization.md) (ep. 20, Wemmbu, 30 October 2024): Forced to become a citizen, he farms with Pangi, loses sumo duels to the enforcer Straight, finds [Wato1876](wato1876.md) in hiding, joins a mission to [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), and is handed one of the last maces, until Straight opens the gates to [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md).
- [Unstable SMP: The Eggchan Empire](../episodes/season-2/030-unstable-smp-the-eggchan-empire.md) (ep. 30, Wemmbu, 27 February 2025): In a strangely generated forest, an invisible Pangi attacks: he's guarding the house where [PrinceZam](princezam.md) retired after the prison.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Farlands](../episodes/season-2/053-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-farlands.md) (ep. 53, Wemmbu, 8 July 2025): Ghost players resembling Pangi, [ManePear](manepear.md), [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md), Sorhin and [PrinceZam](princezam.md) enter the castle: an echo of the past, replaying the empire's founding.
