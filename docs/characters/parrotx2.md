One of the four creators and protagonists of the Unstable Universe. Parrot is often written off as the weakest player on the server, but he gets by on puzzle-solving, planning and the people he gathers around him. His story runs from Spoke's scavenger hunt and the hunt for [Wifies](wifies.md) in Season 1, through being stalked by the Director, to becoming king and fighting the civil and dimensional wars.

## Profile

- Status: alive (as of ep. 161).
- Roles: former leader of [District 13](../factions/district-13.md), former king of [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md), former member of the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md), first King of the Overworld, and king of the Advanced Civilization in exile.
- Known for: puzzle-solving, redstone and mechanics tricks, and leading crowds of ordinary players rather than out-fighting anyone.

## Personality

Parrot is a planner. He knows Minecraft's mechanics well and turns them into escape routes, traps and codes, and he decides fast when things go wrong. He is weak in straight PvP and knows it, so he lets stronger allies take the fights while he works out the angle. He's kind and open-minded, gives second chances even to enemies, and treats killing as a last resort, though he can be cocky and cutting when cornered. His biggest trait is an almost reckless idealism: he believes he has to save everyone, gives his wealth away to new players, and keeps putting others first even when his convictions blind him to more practical options.

## Story so far

- Season 1: Spoke's scavenger hunt drags Parrot and Wifies into the server's secrets, then [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md), hired by [PrinceZam](princezam.md), hunts them. He breaks friends out of chunk bans and out of [Proton](../places/proton.md), founds [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md), survives the Mining City under [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), and leads [District 13](../factions/district-13.md) against [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) in the finale ([Invisible Mafia Arc](../arcs/invisible-mafia-arc.md)).
- [The Director Arc](../arcs/the-director-arc.md): living with the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md), he's targeted by a redstone genius, a hacker and his own suspicious teammates, until the Director is revealed as Wifies, who locks him in [Paragon](../places/paragon.md).
- [Treasure Arc](../arcs/treasure-arc.md): out of Paragon, he hunts Verdaria's treasure, the sunken empire in [The Great Sea](../places/the-great-sea.md), Clown's Nether vault and the Mafia's tomb with Theobald.
- [Election Arc](../arcs/election-arc.md): he runs for king, survives the purge and the assassination of Naparizel, and loses to [LettuceK](lettucek.md), who jails him; the war against [LAW](../factions/law.md) makes Parrot the first King of the Overworld.
- [Civil War Arc](../arcs/civil-war-arc.md): as king he opens [Spawn](../places/spawn.md), is framed for 43 murders, loses the Skymore settlers to Cindercrest, and builds the largest army on the server, but [Saparata](saparata.md) wins the civil war and exiles him.
- [Exile Arc](../arcs/exile-arc.md): he founds a new jungle civilization with immortality machines, is imprisoned in Clown's Nether mining prison, leads a thousand prisoners into [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md) where only he escapes, and clears all five floors of Spoke's End prison with Minute.
- [Dimensional War Arc](../arcs/dimensional-war-arc.md): he warns the Overworld of Clown's invasion, loses city after city, and unites the three kings at [Newtopia](../places/newtopia.md).
