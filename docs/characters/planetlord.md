A teammate of [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](mapicc.md), first seen guarding a skybase of duped items. He joins Spoke's fight against the Mafia, collects rare pets, and later competes with Spoke as a bounty hunter for BAT.

## Profile

- Status: unknown, last seen captured by [NULL](../factions/null.md).
- Roles: Spoke and Mapicc's teammate.

## Story so far

- He guards duped-item stashes Spoke destroys, then becomes Spoke's ally against the Mafia, introducing him to MinuteTech's circle.
- He infiltrates the Mafia with Spoke, and later has a village in the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md) on Spoke's route to NULL.

## Appears in

- [How I Stole 10,356,000 Duped Items](../episodes/season-1/004-how-i-stole-10-356-000-duped-items.md) (ep. 4, SpokeIsHere, 13 May 2024): At the Soul Sand Vault, Roshambo, PlanetLord and Leo greet him ominously, and lead him inside; it isn't a trap after all.
- [I Bankrupted Minecraft's Richest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/011-i-bankrupted-minecrafts-richest-civilization.md) (ep. 11, SpokeIsHere, 19 July 2024): An empty stockpile in a mountain, and PlanetLord, who escapes during a lag spike.
- [I Got Hunted by the Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/019-i-got-hunted-by-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 19, SpokeIsHere, 5 October 2024): MinuteTech, PlanetLord and Squiddo join Spoke's resistance.
- [I Infiltrated The Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/022-i-infiltrated-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 22, SpokeIsHere, 18 November 2024): Following [JamatoP](jamatop.md)'s compass, [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md) and PlanetLord find [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), team up with the undercover [Leow0ok](leow0ok.md), rescue [Mapicc](mapicc.md), and join the Mafia themselves, Spoke under the name "Fish Bucket".
- [I Survived 100 Days Inside a Hidden Minecraft Base](../episodes/season-2/035-i-survived-100-days-inside-a-hidden-minecraft-base.md) (ep. 35, SpokeIsHere, 8 April 2025): Spoke, Mapicc and Planet reunite and rob Red and Jaron's base.
- [I Became a Bounty Hunter on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/069-i-became-a-bounty-hunter-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 69, SpokeIsHere, 12 September 2025): Competing with SmileyyMC and PlanetLord, Spoke hunts ApexPoint in [The Amplified](../places/the-amplified.md), then secretly hires [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md) to do his missions.
- [Searching For 1000 Players Who Went Missing](../episodes/season-2-plus/126-searching-for-1000-players-who-went-missing.md) (ep. 126, SpokeIsHere, 28 April 2026): Planet and the escapees are taken by NULL; several hunters and their leader are killed.
