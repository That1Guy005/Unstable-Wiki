The ruler of the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) and one of the main antagonists of Season 1. Zam starts out as [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md)'s close ally against Spoke, but her paranoia and need for control lead her to betray him, which sparks the Empire War. She later plays a supporting role in Spoke's BAT Arc.

## Profile

- Status: has left the server.
- Roles: ruler of the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md); secondary antagonist of Season 1 (with FlameFrags) and tritagonist of Spoke's [BAT Arc](../arcs/bat-arc.md).

## Personality

Zam is ambitious and controlling, demanding loyalty and respect and seeking power over the whole server through planning and fortification: the infamous wall, and prisons where enemies were chunk-banned. Extreme paranoia drives her rule; she watches constantly for threats and expects total obedience.

## Relationships

- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): her ally, then her victim and enemy; Clown's prisons forced them back together.
- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](mapicc.md): whose theft made her want them banned.
- [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md): hired by her to kill Parrot, later her jailer.

## Story so far

- She teams with Wemmbu to hunt Spoke, chunk-bans TheRealSquiddo, and chases Parrot and Wifies through her castle.
- She betrays Wemmbu, wars with his civilization, and loses her empire to his orbital strike.
- Imprisoned with Wemmbu in [Proton](../places/proton.md) and the escape room, she rebuilds a mini empire among the prisoners; later she escapes LAW's prison with Wemmbu and joins Spoke's group in the BAT years.
