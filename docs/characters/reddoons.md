The greedy mayor of [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) and an ally of [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), who kept its miners in servitude and later taxed new businesses at 90%. [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md) spends two "100 days" episodes secretly living in and raiding his bases.

## Profile

- Status: unknown, presumed alive.
- Roles: mayor of [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) and its Mining City; ally of [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md).

## Story so far

- He sentences Parrot, Wifies and Dean to impossible diamond quotas, triples them, and runs deadly roulette games, executing LuigiToan himself.
- He shares a top base with Yeah_Jaron, where Spoke secretly lives for 100 days.

## Appears in

- [I Survived Minecraft's Most Corrupt City](../episodes/season-1/018-i-survived-minecrafts-most-corrupt-city.md) (ep. 18, ParrotX2, 2 October 2024): LuigiToan is killed by Reddoons; CoinMonke is fired.
- [I Infiltrated The Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/022-i-infiltrated-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 22, SpokeIsHere, 18 November 2024): Baconnwaffles0, their target, calls them traitors in chat, so they destroy his base; a trade with Reddoons follows.
- [I Spent 100 Days Hidden in a Secret Minecraft Base...](../episodes/season-2/032-i-spent-100-days-hidden-in-a-secret-minecraft-base.md) (ep. 32, SpokeIsHere, 12 March 2025): Spoke trades the loot for Reddoons' coordinates.
- [I Survived 100 Days Inside a Hidden Minecraft Base](../episodes/season-2/035-i-survived-100-days-inside-a-hidden-minecraft-base.md) (ep. 35, SpokeIsHere, 8 April 2025): Spoke, Mapicc and Planet reunite and rob Red and Jaron's base.
- [100 Days Hidden in a Minecraft Chunk Base](../episodes/season-2/037-100-days-hidden-in-a-minecraft-chunk-base.md) (ep. 37, SpokeIsHere, 26 April 2025): Spoke's feud with Reddoons and Jaron continues.
- [I Joined the World's Largest Minecraft Server](../episodes/season-2/062-i-joined-the-worlds-largest-minecraft-server.md) (ep. 62, ParrotX2, 14 August 2025): [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md) takes him to [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), ruled by Reddoons with a 90% tax.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon vs Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/064-orbital-strike-cannon-vs-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 64, Wemmbu, 20 August 2025): A player he corners by a lamp post says, on a sign, that only Reddoons knows where Flame is, and there's a queue.
