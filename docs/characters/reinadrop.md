A quiet, serious survivor of the failed [Skymore Civilization](../places/skymore-civilization.md) who joins [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md), and fights at Parrot's side through the civil war and the Dimensional War.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: survivor of the Skymore massacre and Parrot's tritagonist in the Kingdoms saga.

## Personality

Reina is quiet, serious and insecure, quick to frustration and occasionally explosive, as when the Advanced Civilization mocked her speech. Being a new player, part of a group hunted and looked down on, makes her doubt she belongs. She can be a little cocky, but she's clever and cares deeply about her friends.

## Relationships

- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md): her companions.
- Zoe444: the friend who fed her when she arrived, who died after the massacre.
- [Saparata](saparata.md): who captured and banished her.

## Story so far

- One of ten survivors of the Skymore massacre ([Skymore Civilization](../places/skymore-civilization.md)), she escapes Cindercrest's fields and joins Parrot.
- [Civil War Arc](../arcs/civil-war-arc.md): she beats the strongest bandit with a trident, runs the [arrow cannons](../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) against Cindercrest, and is banished after the war.
- Ep. 161: she brings the Advanced Civilization's army to Newtopia, turning the battle.
