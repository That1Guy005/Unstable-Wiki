A kind, slightly clueless player, first seen as a guard of [Proton](../places/proton.md) and later guarding a spire in [The Abandoned Civilization](../places/the-abandoned-civilization.md). He supports [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) against [Jaden_MAN](jaden-man.md) and is killed by a warden.

## Profile

- Status: deceased.

## Personality

Rejoice was kind and peaceful, a little clueless, fond of sunflowers, and lonely after a long isolation. He didn't want anyone to die, friend or enemy, and was one of the few players Wemmbu really liked.

## Story so far

- A Proton guard who later guarded a spire in [The Abandoned Civilization](../places/the-abandoned-civilization.md).
- He helps Wemmbu, Egg and Loppezz hunt Jaden, and dies to wardens despite Wemmbu's efforts.
