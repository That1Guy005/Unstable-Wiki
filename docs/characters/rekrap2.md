[ParrotX2](parrotx2.md)'s partner in breaking into the chunk-ban prisons to free [Wifies](wifies.md), and one of the players Clown imprisons in [Proton](../places/proton.md). He is briefly a member of District 13.

## Profile

- Status: unknown.

## Story so far

- With Parrot he breaks into chunk-ban prisons, freeing Derapchu, Fantst and JumperWho, and finally Wifies (and, by accident, Spoke), with Kenadian's Wither Bore.
- Boosfer's crew burns his tree and hires Wemmbu to destroy his valley base.
- He's trapped in [Proton](../places/proton.md), and briefly joins [District 13](../factions/district-13.md).

## Appears in

- [I Broke into Minecraft's Most Secure Prison](../episodes/season-1/007-i-broke-into-minecrafts-most-secure-prison.md) (ep. 7, ParrotX2, 9 June 2024): Chunk bans are now guarded by [PrinceZam](princezam.md)'s prisons, so Parrot and Rekrap2 break into them one by one, freeing Spoke's old crew to build a prison-breaking team.
- [It's Impossible to Find this Player](../episodes/season-1/012-its-impossible-to-find-this-player.md) (ep. 12, ParrotX2, 22 July 2024): [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md)'s friends Rekrap2, [JumperWho](jumperwho.md), [Kenadian](kenadian.md), [Derapchu](derapchu.md) and [Leow0ok](leow0ok.md) have vanished, along with [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md).
- [Escaping Proton: Minecraft's Most Secure Prison](../episodes/season-1/015-escaping-proton-minecrafts-most-secure-prison.md) (ep. 15, ParrotX2, 31 August 2024): Rekrap2 explains that Proton is brand new and holds every missing player, and that Wemmbu escaped before the prisoners started being moved.
- [Unstable SMP: Doomsday](../episodes/season-1/025-unstable-smp-doomsday.md) (ep. 25, ParrotX2, 21 January 2025): Horace builds a bunker near Spawn, District 13, and Parrot recruits Rekrap2, [JumperWho](jumperwho.md), Ratrick, Finiahn and others, but Wifies' beacons draw a Mafia attack while they recruit.
- [The End of the Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/027-the-end-of-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 27, SpokeIsHere, 30 January 2025)
