The leader of [Roshambo's Civilization](../places/roshambos-civilization.md) and the guard of one of [The Wonders](../places/the-wonders.md). He is caught up in the duped-items scheme, and later hires [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](eggchan.md) as base destroyers.

## Profile

- Status: alive; leader of [Roshambo's Civilization](../places/roshambos-civilization.md).

## Story so far

- A Wonder guard who reports its destruction to Parrot, then helps build a counter-cannon.
- He guards a vault Spoke escapes, traps Spoke in the End, and hires Wemmbu and Eggchan to blow up Spoke's End base.
- When FlameFrags and Merffinn invade his civilization, Wemmbu saves him and covers the evacuation.

## Appears in

- [How to Destroy the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/001-how-to-destroy-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 1, SpokeIsHere, 21 April 2024): Roshambogames, [JumperWho](jumperwho.md), [Derapchu](derapchu.md) and [Fantst](fantst.md) are paid in duped items to guard the Wonders and keep quiet.
- [This Button Destroys the World](../episodes/season-1/002-this-button-destroys-the-world.md) (ep. 2, ParrotX2, 27 April 2024): Parrot has learned to build orbital cannons, and knows Wemmbu and Roshambo are hiding something.
- [How I Stole 10,356,000 Duped Items](../episodes/season-1/004-how-i-stole-10-356-000-duped-items.md) (ep. 4, SpokeIsHere, 13 May 2024): With new partner [Mapicc](mapicc.md), he works through Roshambogames' vaults, the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) and [TheRealSquiddo](therealsquiddo.md)'s castle, and causes one disaster that changes his attitude to the [Orbital Strike Cannon](../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): they accidentally destroy the home of an innocent player, Jepexx.
- [Why I Destroyed the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/010-why-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 10, Wemmbu, 8 July 2024): First client: Roshambogames, who wants Spoke's crowded base destroyed.
- [I Bankrupted Minecraft's Richest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/011-i-bankrupted-minecrafts-richest-civilization.md) (ep. 11, SpokeIsHere, 19 July 2024): The duped items [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](mapicc.md) destroyed are back, and [Roshambo's Civilization](../places/roshambos-civilization.md) is richer than ever.
- [Unstable SMP: The PrinceZam Empire War](../episodes/season-1/013-unstable-smp-the-princezam-empire-war.md) (ep. 13, Wemmbu, 29 July 2024): His first assault fails, so he recruits the defector [ManePear](manepear.md), defends [Roshambo's Civilization](../places/roshambos-civilization.md) against [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md), and starts kidnapping Zam's officers, which troubles [Eggchan](eggchan.md).
- [I Got Hunted by the Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/019-i-got-hunted-by-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 19, SpokeIsHere, 5 October 2024)
