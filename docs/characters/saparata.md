The king and general of [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md), champion of the "old players" against the flood of new ones. He wins the civil war against [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and becomes the second King of Unstable, then stands with the other two kings against the Nether in the Dimensional War.

## Profile

- Status: alive; leader of [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md) and the second King of Unstable.
- Known for: championing the "old players" against the new, and for trapping Spawn to kill newcomers during the famine.

## Personality

Saparata is cold and determined. He blames Parrot for his friends' deaths and the server's disorder. Like LettuceK, he believes the end justifies the means, rigging Spawn with traps to hold off the famine. He's proud of his own fitness to rule, tenacious and hard to discourage. He's cautious and wants proof before he believes anything, and he understands betrayal well enough to use it. He does care about feeding his kingdom, and in the end he drops his grudge against new players to unite the Overworld against Clown.

## Relationships

- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): the king he dethroned, and finally his ally.
- [LettuceK](lettucek.md): freed and forced to serve as Cindercrest's captain and spokesman.
- [ShoeBilly_](shoebilly.md): his mercenary.
- [Arachn1d](arachn1d.md): an ally in the civil war who planned to betray him.
- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): on whom he put an 80,000-diamond bounty, then offered to lift it if Wemmbu beat NULL.

## Story so far

- Driven out of [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) by overcrowding after Parrot opened Spawn, he lost his friends to bandits and co-founded Cindercrest to dethrone Parrot.
- [The Civil War](../events/the-civil-war.md): allied with the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md), Cindercrest takes Capital City and Saparata becomes king.
- As king he traps Spawn, hunts Parrot's new civilization and loses to its totem teleport, but keeps Parrot's villager.
- He backs Cloudwatch's [Terminus](../places/terminus.md) to break FlameFrags and uses Flame's capture to boost recruitment, but loses the war with LAW.
- [Dimensional War Arc](../arcs/dimensional-war-arc.md): convinced only when Clown appears, he breeds the villagers with Parrot, defends Capital City, and holds Newtopia beside Parrot and Lettuce.
