A strict, patriotic [LAW](../factions/law.md) commander who serves under [LettuceK](lettucek.md) and later helps rebuild LAW with [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md) and [NotSkrilley](notskrilley.md).

## Profile

- Status: alive; former LAW commander, now rebuilding LAW under Morr0w.

## Personality

Sarge is strict and patriotic, still grieving his brother SargeRaxby, whom Wemmbu killed. He's ruthless in a fight and famous for blowing up his own men, yet he mourns the thousands who died when LAW City fell.

## Story so far

- He captures Flame with elite netherite troops, duels Wemmbu at Smuggler's Pass, and ambushes Theobald.
- In ep. 160 he hands Flame LAW's book of succession and announces him as temporary leader.

## Appears in

### Season 2

- [Unstable SMP: The Battle for The Great Sea](../episodes/season-2/101-unstable-smp-the-battle-for-the-great-sea.md) (ep. 101, Wemmbu, 28 December 2025): Lettuce maces him out of the air, and calls in [Deputy Ace](deputy-ace.md), The_Oroboros, [TheG0dofWar](theg0dofwar.md) and SargeLAW.
- [I Got Trained By a Minecraft Spear Master](../episodes/season-2/102-i-got-trained-by-a-minecraft-spear-master.md) (ep. 102, FlameFrags, 8 January 2026): SargeLAW arrives.
- [2 Minecraft Pros VS 1000 Hunters](../episodes/season-2/106-2-minecraft-pros-vs-1000-hunters.md) (ep. 106, ParrotX2, 24 January 2026): Through dolphin tunnels, a smuggler's nautilus route and SargeLAW's ambush, with [Eggchan](eggchan.md) undercover in LAW and [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md) killing a LAW commander, they burst into the debate.
- [4 Minecraft Pros VS 2500 Players](../episodes/season-2/113-4-minecraft-pros-vs-2500-players.md) (ep. 113, SpokeIsHere, 12 February 2026): Back in the cells, they hear SargeLAW, free Th3Blurpenstein and find [Lomedy](lomedy.md), who won't leave (Spoke locks him in a smaller cell), and meet Flame killing guards, then watch his [Orbital Dog Cannon](../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) summon a wolf army.
- [Unstable SMP: The LAW](../episodes/season-2/114-unstable-smp-the-law.md) (ep. 114, Wemmbu, 14 February 2026): SargeLAW commands the guards, but his own carts kill more lawmen than Flame or Wemmbu do.
- [2500 Players VS Minecraft Combat Master](../episodes/season-2/115-2500-players-vs-minecraft-combat-master.md) (ep. 115, FlameFrags, 16 February 2026): They beat SargeLAW's garrison; Sarge retreats to fetch Lettuce.

### Season 2+

- [100 Days as King of the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2-plus/118-100-days-as-king-of-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 118, ParrotX2, 28 February 2026): Theo scouts ahead and kills attacking lawmen; then SargeLAW stops Parrot, determined to keep him from being king.
- [I Created the World's Largest Minecraft Army](../episodes/season-2-plus/129-i-created-the-worlds-largest-minecraft-army.md) (ep. 129, ParrotX2, 4 May 2026): He recruits bandits by trickery and [Reina](reinadrop.md)'s dripstone trident, takes [Merchant City](../places/merchant-city.md)'s [Vanguard](../factions/vanguard.md) by kidnapping DanchoGold, buys 40,000 TNT from [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md), and blows up the LAW holdouts led by SargeLAW.
- [1000 VS 1000 Player Minecraft Civil War](../episodes/season-2-plus/137-1000-vs-1000-player-minecraft-civil-war.md) (ep. 137, FlameFrags, 1 June 2026): After hearing SargeLAW's account of Parrot blowing up LAW, and fighting at Highwater, Flame learns why [Lomedy](lomedy.md) joined [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md), and they turn on [Saparata](saparata.md).
- [I Got Hunted By the World's Deadliest Minecraft Player](../episodes/season-2-plus/142-i-got-hunted-by-the-worlds-deadliest-minecraft-player.md) (ep. 142, FlameFrags, 19 June 2026): SargeLAW says LAW is short on resources, but has stashes from Lettuce's era that only Lettuce and [Deputy Ace](deputy-ace.md) knew; Flame should ask Lettuce, who Sarge didn't know had been freed.
- [10,000 VS 10,000 Player Minecraft War](../episodes/season-2-plus/160-10-000-vs-10-000-player-minecraft-war.md) (ep. 160, FlameFrags, 31 October 2026): SargeLAW shows him LAW's book of succession, with Sarge's name last; Flame suggests leading LAW himself.
