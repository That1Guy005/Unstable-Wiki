A gullible, stubborn fighter (account name conexion) who keeps ambushing [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) in the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md), steals his mace, and is then talked out of it by [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md) with a lie about duplicating it.

## Profile

- Status: alive. Also known as conexion.
- Roles: minor antagonist of the [Power Arc](../arcs/power-arc.md) and [Skill Arc](../arcs/skill-arc.md).

## Personality

He's gullible and stubborn: he believed Flame's mace-duplication lie, and wouldn't give Wemmbu's mace back.

## Story so far

- A PvP tournament fighter, he steals Wemmbu's mace Crucible with a pot and flees by pearl cannon to the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md).
- Saved from the void by Wemmbu, he hides the mace, hunts Wemmbu with [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md), and is fooled by Flame.

## Appears in

- [How I SURVIVED a Hardcore Minecraft Tournament](../episodes/season-2/049-how-i-survived-a-hardcore-minecraft-tournament.md) (ep. 49, Wemmbu, 25 June 2025): Wemmbu beats Epaxialfx, Wyll and Sharpness to reach the final.
- [Dominating Minecraft's Deadliest PVP Tournament](../episodes/season-2/050-dominating-minecrafts-deadliest-pvp-tournament.md) (ep. 50, FlameFrags, 28 June 2025): Flame beats Willsion easily, then watches [WyLL](wyll.md), Ferre, Wemmbu, Sharpness (conexion) and Theo, who actually kills his opponent.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Farlands](../episodes/season-2/053-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-farlands.md) (ep. 53, Wemmbu, 8 July 2025): Sharpness keeps Wemmbu's mace, on condition.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Fartherlands](../episodes/season-2/054-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-fartherlands.md) (ep. 54, Wemmbu, 15 July 2025): The Avengers attack again, Spongs in weaker gear and without Flame, Sharpness or Jaden.
- [I Got REVENGE on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/055-i-got-revenge-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 55, FlameFrags, 16 July 2025): Flame tricks Sharpness out of the stolen mace with a fake dupe glitch, takes over as leader, and plans a box ambush.
