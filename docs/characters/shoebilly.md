A mace-wielding bounty hunter who works for the highest bidder. He terrorises Parrot's [Skymore Civilization](../places/skymore-civilization.md), then fights as one of [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md)'s two strongest killers alongside [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md). He was also [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md)'s former teacher.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: bounty hunter for [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md); former teacher and rival of TheobaldTheBird.

## Personality

ShoeBilly is openly cruel and spiteful, a mercenary with a long record of bloodshed who'll kill innocents for power and influence. He calls himself selfish and despises attachments, believing friends only hold people back. Even so, he has grown a little: he now shows slight care for the server, and fights to protect it and his allies against Clown. He only cares about beating Flame at his strongest.

## Relationships

- [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md): his former student, trained for two months in the Far Lands.
- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): the rival he wants at full strength.
- [Saparata](saparata.md): his employer.

## Story so far

- He attacks Parrot and Theo for the [Capitol City Police Force](../factions/capitol-city-police-force.md), massacres new players, and destroys [Skymore Civilization](../places/skymore-civilization.md) for Cindercrest.
- In Flame's story he confronts Flame on the [Blimp](../mechanics/blimp.md) and hunts him through [Terminus](../places/terminus.md), killing Flame's horse.
- Ep. 161: convinced of Clown's invasion, he defends Spawn, takes the villagers to breed, and rescues Parrot from Capital City.

## Appears in

### Season 2+

- [I Exposed a Corrupt Minecraft Civilization](../episodes/season-2-plus/122-i-exposed-a-corrupt-minecraft-civilization.md) (ep. 122, ParrotX2, 19 March 2026): He sneaks back in among a herd of cows, while [TheobaldTheBird](theobaldthebird.md) holds off the assassin ShoeBilly_, and exposes the police's plot to purge the new players.

- [I Joined a 10,000 Player Minecraft Army](../episodes/season-2-plus/132-i-joined-a-10-000-player-minecraft-army.md) (ep. 132, FlameFrags, 15 May 2026): On the [Blimp](../mechanics/blimp.md), only Flame and ShoeBilly_ ride: both killers, the strongest in Cindercrest.

- [I Went to WAR on a Hardcore Minecraft SMP](../episodes/season-2-plus/136-i-went-to-war-on-a-hardcore-minecraft-smp.md) (ep. 136, Wemmbu, 30 May 2026): [Arachn1d](arachn1d.md) secretly plans to betray [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md) and rule the server himself, and uses [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) as his weapon: against ShoeBilly_ at Steampunk City, against [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md)' army, and finally against everyone at [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), where Wemmbu's nukes kill hundreds on both sides.
- [Unstable SMP: Civil War FINALE](../episodes/season-2-plus/138-unstable-smp-civil-war-finale.md) (ep. 138, ParrotX2, 3 June 2026): ShoeBilly_ ambushes the ghasts with his mace and kills several soldiers.
- [I Created Minecraft's Greatest Military](../episodes/season-2-plus/148-i-created-minecrafts-greatest-military.md) (ep. 148, SpokeIsHere, 13 August 2026): Then a Cindercrest army marches up to retake the castle, ShoeBilly_ kills a few NULL, and they escape through the portal.

- [10,000 VS 10,000 Player Minecraft War](../episodes/season-2-plus/160-10-000-vs-10-000-player-minecraft-war.md) (ep. 160, FlameFrags, 31 October 2026): ShoeBilly_ blows up the base and fights him, listing his fall from Terminus to his vow to protect the weak and his friends.
