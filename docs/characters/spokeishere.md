One of the four creators and protagonists. Spoke kicks off the whole series by discovering impossible exploits, which makes him the server's first villain. He is a trickster and trapper whose schemes run from the Exploit Arc, infiltrating [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) and raiding secret bases to the missing players, Purgatory, and eventually leading NULL.

## Profile

- Status: alive (as of ep. 159). Leader of [NULL](../factions/null.md) and Emperor of the End.
- Roles: founder of the [Spoke Galactic Empire](../factions/spoke-galactic-empire.md), former member of the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) and [District 13](../factions/district-13.md); current overarching antagonist outside his own POV.
- Known for: exploits, from the dupe glitch and [chunk bans](../mechanics/chunk-ban.md) to remote block-breaking, and for holding the Totem of NULL.

## Personality

Spoke is chaotic and manipulative, and most players avoid him on reputation alone. He's driven and doesn't mind trampling others to get what he wants, and he would rather deny or play down his wrongdoing than admit it, even to his best friend Mapicc. He's socially gifted, loves pranks, and stays flippant when everyone else is deadly serious. He does care: about Mapicc, the missing players, Purgatory's prisoners, and lately Slicee. When he fears losing a friend he becomes desperate and stubborn. He's reckless with his own safety, but clever enough to outsmart JamatoP in Purgatory. Pulled apart again and again, he has hardened into a ruthless leader of NULL, close to the person his enemies always said he'd become.

## Story so far

- [Exploit Arc](../arcs/exploit-arc.md): his dupe glitch floods the server with illegal items; he tries to destroy every stash, builds [The Wonders](../places/the-wonders.md), and is chunk-banned by the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md).
- [Invisible Mafia Arc](../arcs/invisible-mafia-arc.md): saved by MinuteTech, he infiltrates [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) with Leow0ok and PlanetLord, and destroys it from within in the finale.
- [Secret Base Arc](../arcs/secret-base-arc.md): he secretly lives 100 days in Ashswag's Point Nemo, in Reddoons and Jaron's base, and in JumperWho's void base, then founds the Spoke Galactic Empire.
- [BAT Arc](../arcs/bat-arc.md): covering up a burned village pulls him into a war with Leow0ok and the Bounty Assassin Team; he's handed to LAW, loses Mapicc for a time, and turns a ring of biomes into a trap field.
- [Missing Players Arc](../arcs/missing-players-arc.md): the "mission: null" book leads him, Mapicc and Jumper to the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md), where Jamato blackmails him.
- [Purgatory Arc](../arcs/purgatory-arc.md): imprisoned in [Purgatory](../places/purgatory.md), he wins Jamato's Hunger Games and, with the Commander's betrayal, takes NULL.
- [End Civilization Arc](../arcs/end-civilization-arc.md): he conquers the End ([NULL's Conquest of the End](../events/nulls-conquest-of-the-end.md)), seizes the Overworld's End portals, visits the Citadel, and seals Mapicc in the Underworld.
- [The Interdimensional War](../arcs/the-interdimensional-war.md): he courts and then breaks Wemmbu, destroys the orbital cannons, fights Clown's [Nether Kingdom](../factions/nether-kingdom.md) for his capital, and follows it into the End.
