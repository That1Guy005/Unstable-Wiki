A chaotic, impulsive troublemaker whose plans rarely achieve anything. He is one of the "game masters" leading Parrot to [Proton](../places/proton.md) and the antagonist of The Great Farm War, and he becomes a regular comic-relief sidekick in [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md)' Season 2+ videos.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: antagonist of the Great Farm War, comic relief and tritagonist of Flame's Season 2+.

## Personality

Spongs is chaotic and unserious, impulsive with valuable items and baffled when people get angry. He overestimates himself, and although he means to help, he usually causes more problems than he solves. That cluelessness also makes him friendly.

## Relationships

- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md), [Lomedy](lomedy.md) and [NotSkrilley](notskrilley.md): housemates at [FlameFrags' Base](../places/flamefrags-base.md).

## Story so far

- He's Jaden's teammate, Clown's gamemaster and a [Proton](../places/proton.md) guard.
- He joins [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md), then griefs Lomedy's farm in the Great Farm War before teaming up with Flame.
- He serves in Flame's LAW squad, joins [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md), and saves Flame's life against Wemmbu and Jaden.
- He turns up as a Cindercrest guard in [Terminus](../places/terminus.md), stuck when his stasis fails, and escapes with Flame.
