One of [LAW](../factions/law.md)'s commanders and a feared fighter, who first beats [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md), then loses to him, and later trains Flame in exchange for help retaking his civilization from [The Warriors](../factions/the-warriors.md). His death sets up Flame's revenge in the Warriors Arc.

## Profile

- Status: deceased.
- Roles: one of LAW's four commanders and a major antagonist of the [Kings Arc](../arcs/kings-arc.md).

## Story so far

- He crushes Flame in the black market, humiliating him into running.
- After training with Theobald, Flame beats him in the desert and spares him.
- Exiled from his civilization by Antaggg's Warriors, he trains Flame, and is killed by an [Arrow Cannon](../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) in Antaggg's rigged arena.
