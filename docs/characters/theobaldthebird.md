A cocky TNT-minecart fighter who meets [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) in the Far Lands Warriors and becomes his best friend and partner after Wifies' death. Theo is at Parrot's side for the smuggling business, the treasure hunts, the election and the civil war, and he also trains [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md) in cart PvP.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: Parrot's best friend, bodyguard and Season 2 deuteragonist; ShoeBilly's former student and rival.
- Known for: [TNT Minecart](../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) PvP; he taught FlameFrags the technique.

## Personality

Theo is a gifted, charismatic cart fighter and fiercely protective of Parrot, putting him first even in life-or-death moments. He's the butt of friendly teasing and robbery, which he always protests. Outside fights he's airheaded, bad with reading, writing and numbers, and leans into it to lighten dark moments. He has a short temper and uses intimidation to get his way. He shares Parrot's dream of a server where newcomers are safe.

## Relationships

- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): his best friend.
- [ShoeBilly_](shoebilly.md): his former teacher and bitter rival.
- [ReinaDrop](reinadrop.md): a companion on Parrot's journeys.
- [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md): his student.

## Story so far

- Season 1: a tritagonist of the Mafia arc.
- Season 2: he griefs Wemmbu's new base to force a fight, then stands at Parrot's side through the treasure hunts, Clown's vault, the election, LAW's prison and the war, the civil war and exile, he trains Flame in cart PvP.
- Ep. 161: he fights TorrinTheTitan for their freedom and defends Capital City and Newtopia.
