A light-hearted, generous player who refuses to kill. She is first suspected of stealing Spoke's illegal armour, is later chunk banned by Wemmbu and Zam, and eventually joins [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) while still missing her old friends.

## Profile

- Status: alive.
- Roles: leader of the Master Builders; copper trim in [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md).

## Personality

Squiddo is unserious, generous and kind, and won't kill: she stayed a copper trim despite being close to Ashswag. She's loyal and sentimental, missing Spoke and Mapicc long after she left, and keeping a "Garlic Hamster" pet at every base. She brings out Ash's lighter side, and is one of the few he trusts.

## Relationships

- [Ashswag](ashswag.md): her close friend.
- [SpokeIsHere](spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](mapicc.md): old friends, who burned her house down by accident.

## Story so far

- Her Master Builders build [The Wonders](../places/the-wonders.md) for Spoke; she accidentally sees one of his exploits.
- Zam chunk-bans her; Parrot frees her.
- She lives with Ash at Point Nemo, which Spoke later raids, and Spoke burns her base in [The Amplified](../places/the-amplified.md).
