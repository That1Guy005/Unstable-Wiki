A seemingly unkillable player tied to the Director, who massacres a village and destroys BAT's base before Nufuli kills him with an orbital strike cannon.

## Profile

- Status: deceased.

## Story so far

- A hacker (teleporting, shapeshifting, flying) who first attacks Wemmbu's Civilization, then wipes out BAT Headquarters for fun, ignoring orders to keep Parrot alive.
- Nufuli bans him with BAT's orbital cannon.
