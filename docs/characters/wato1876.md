A reserved builder of escape rooms who takes commissions and avoids conflict. He refuses to respawn the Ender Dragon for Clown after the escape room, hides in the [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md), becomes the first citizen of [Eggchan's Civilization](../places/eggchans-civilization.md), and later quits the server. He also posts his own guest video.

## Profile

- Status: has left the server.
- Known for: building escape rooms on commission.

## Personality

Wato is reserved and avoids conflict and company. He grew a real friendship with Wemmbu, defying Clown's orders to keep him safe. He values loyalty, understands that people do what they must to survive, and is stubborn about his morals unless he's in danger.

## Relationships

- [Kenadian](kenadian.md): his closest ally.
- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): a friend from the escape room.

## Story so far

- He builds Clown's escape room for Wemmbu and Zam, then hides in the [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md) and the Far Lands' farming district.
- He joins [District 13](../factions/district-13.md), leaves for the Mafia, and helps break the Mafia's spawn prison.
