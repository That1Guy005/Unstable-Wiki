One of the four creators and protagonists. Wemmbu is a feared fighter and an orbital cannon expert, usually paired with his best friend [Eggchan](eggchan.md) and locked in a long rivalry with [FlameFrags](flamefrags.md). His arcs take him from founding a civilization for the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) and going to war with it, through the Mafia, pirates and the Far Lands, to faking his own death, fighting [LAW](../factions/law.md), and the Kingdoms saga.

## Profile

- Status: alive, last seen entering [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md) in search of [The Forge](../places/the-forge.md) (ep. 154).
- Roles: founder of [Wemmbu's Civilization](../places/wemmbus-civilization.md), former member of the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md), [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md), [Ice Mountain Civilization](../places/ice-mountain-civilization.md) and [District 13](../factions/district-13.md), and second-in-command of [Eggchan's Empire](../places/eggchans-civilization.md) and the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md).
- Known for: elytra-macing with his maces Gambit and Crucible, and his [orbital strike cannons](../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md), destroyed by NULL in ep. 154.

## Personality

Wemmbu is loud, sarcastic, competitive and quick to boredom, flipping from banter to violence depending on how threatened he feels. He plays for himself, rarely helps without getting something back, and sees fighting with honour as a pointless handicap: whatever wins is fair. That makes him one of the most dangerous fighters on the server, if impulsive. Underneath is total loyalty to [Eggchan](eggchan.md), whom he puts before his own life, even serving under people he despises to keep Egg safe. Over time he's learned to spare enemies rather than kill them. He treats the server more like a game than the others do, calling death "banning".

## Relationships

- [Eggchan](eggchan.md): his best friend, whose safety drives most of his decisions.

## Story so far

- [Empire Arc](../arcs/empire-arc.md): tied to [PrinceZam](princezam.md), he builds a colony that the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) turns on, wages war on the empire as a base destroyer for hire, and is trapped with Zam in [Proton](../places/proton.md) and Clown's escape room, where they craft a [Mace](../mechanics/mace.md).
- [Invisible Mafia Arc](../arcs/invisible-mafia-arc.md): he stumbles into the [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md), trains under ManePear, and joins District 13's finale to fight Clown.
- [Pirate Arc](../arcs/pirate-arc.md): settling down with Egg fails; Jaden's pirates exile them to the Pirate Islands and he chases Jaden through [The Abandoned Civilization](../places/the-abandoned-civilization.md).
- [Power Arc](../arcs/power-arc.md): rebuilding from nothing, he fights WyLL, the Ice Mountain bombers and the ender-dragon scheme, and enters the PvP tournament.
- [Farlands Arc](../arcs/farlands-arc.md): chasing a stolen mace into the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md), hunted by [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md), he reaches the city at the end of the Far Lands, takes its orbital cannons, and beats Flame.
- [Fake Identity Arc](../arcs/fake-identity-arc.md): he fakes his death and becomes the Invisible Knight, sails with Jaden's pirates, falls into the Underworld with Boosfer, and is unmasked by Flame.
- [Kings Arc](../arcs/kings-arc.md): jailed by [LAW](../factions/law.md), he escapes, destroys LAW's orbital cannon at sea, recovers his own cannons, and fights in [The War Against LAW](../events/the-war-against-law.md).
- [Kingdoms Arc](../arcs/kingdoms-arc.md): Arachn1d's alliance takes Egg hostage; Wemmbu joins the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md), is used as Arachn1d's weapon, survives the purge, frees Egg, and loses the crown to Yungyx.
- [The Interdimensional War](../arcs/the-interdimensional-war.md): hunted by Saparata's bounty, he refuses to join Spoke, loses his cannons, Jaden and the first-generation players, and follows Serpentbound's compass into the Underworld.
