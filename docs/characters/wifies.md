[ParrotX2](parrotx2.md)'s best friend in Season 1, and his partner on the scavenger hunt, in [Proton](../places/proton.md) and in the End Civilization. He is later revealed to be the Director, the manipulator who hunts Parrot through the Director Arc, believing anyone can be controlled through the thing they care about most. He dies in Paragon. Twifies is also the channel behind several blooper videos.

## Profile

- Status: deceased.
- Roles: Parrot's best friend and Season 1 deuteragonist, revealed as the Director, the main antagonist of [The Director Arc](../arcs/the-director-arc.md).

## Personality

Wifies seemed quiet, calm and self-sacrificing. Underneath he was brilliant and manipulative, often a step ahead of Parrot himself. Everything he did came from protecting Parrot, without whom he said he had no reason to be on the server. That devotion became obsession: he studied Parrot in staged hunts, ignored his choices, and finally imprisoned him in [Paragon](../places/paragon.md) to keep him safe. As the Director, his philosophy was that everyone has something they care about, and whoever controls it controls them.

## Relationships

- [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md): his best friend and the person he controlled "for his own good".
- [Ashswag](ashswag.md) and [ClownPierce](clownpierce.md): players he coerced.
- [MrCube6](mrcube6.md), [ItzRealMe](itzrealme.md) and [TruOriginal](truoriginal.md): players he hired to hunt Parrot.

- [Why I Destroyed the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/010-why-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 10, Wemmbu, 8 July 2024): The jobs take them into Spoke's base, [FerreMC](ferremc.md)'s tower and the [Treasure Hunters](../factions/treasure-hunters.md)' office, and lead to an alliance with [ParrotX2](parrotx2.md) and Wifies that ends with an [Orbital Strike Cannon](../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) heist and a declaration of war.
- [It's Impossible to Find this Player](../episodes/season-1/012-its-impossible-to-find-this-player.md) (ep. 12, ParrotX2, 22 July 2024): The Treasure Hunters are robbed of most of their wealth by Parrot, Wifies and Wemmbu.

- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): Dean joins Parrot and Wifies for good.
