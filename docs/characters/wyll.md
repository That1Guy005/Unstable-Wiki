A skilled fighter who starts as a [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) soldier, leads an emerald-trimmed crew that [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md) hunts in the Power Arc, gives Wemmbu advice in the PvP tournament ("I hate Sharpness more than I hate you"), and joins [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md).

## Profile

- Status: alive.

## Story so far

- A Zam soldier who takes over [Mafia City](../places/mafia-city.md) when the Mafia collapses.
- Hired to kill Wemmbu, he tries again and again, including a void trap, and loses a close tournament fight.
- He fights alongside Flame in the Far Lands against Wemmbu.

## Appears in

### Season 1

- [Unstable SMP: The PrinceZam Empire War](../episodes/season-1/013-unstable-smp-the-princezam-empire-war.md) (ep. 13, Wemmbu, 29 July 2024): They fight the guards ([Pangi](pangi.md), Dol9hin, [Loppezz](loppezz.md), [LuigiToan](luigitoan.md), WyLL and What_Max) while Wemmbu goes for Zam.

### Season 2

- [How I HUNTED the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/042-how-i-hunted-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 42, Wemmbu, 20 May 2025): Wyll's crew is killed, along with Gigasigma37 and the pet-owning attacker.
- [How I SURVIVED a Hardcore Minecraft Tournament](../episodes/season-2/049-how-i-survived-a-hardcore-minecraft-tournament.md) (ep. 49, Wemmbu, 25 June 2025): Wemmbu beats Epaxialfx, Wyll and Sharpness to reach the final.
- [Dominating Minecraft's Deadliest PVP Tournament](../episodes/season-2/050-dominating-minecrafts-deadliest-pvp-tournament.md) (ep. 50, FlameFrags, 28 June 2025): Flame beats Willsion easily, then watches WyLL, Ferre, Wemmbu, [Sharpness](sharpness.md) (conexion) and Theo, who actually kills his opponent.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Fartherlands](../episodes/season-2/054-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-fartherlands.md) (ep. 54, Wemmbu, 15 July 2025): The Avengers are FlameFrags, [Spongs](spongs.md), Willsion, [Jaden_MAN](jaden-man.md), WyLL, [Fantst](fantst.md) and Baablu, all after Wemmbu's ban.
- [I Got REVENGE on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/055-i-got-revenge-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 55, FlameFrags, 16 July 2025): Flame wanted to handle it alone, but Jaden insists on a team of Wemmbu's former enemies: WyLL, Willsion, [Spongs](spongs.md), [Fantst](fantst.md) and Baablu.
