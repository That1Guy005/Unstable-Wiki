A flippant former [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) member who becomes [Arachn1d](arachn1d.md)'s right hand in the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md), then [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md)'s teammate in the purge, and finally the kingdom's second and current king.

## Profile

- Status: alive; King of the [Kingdom of the Caves](../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md) (since ep. 150).
- Roles: former Zam Empire guard; secondary antagonist and deuteragonist of Wemmbu's Kingdoms saga.

## Personality

Yungy is flippant and immature, known for a terrible microphone, mocking captives and pranking Codmaster's chest full of cod. Underneath is a hunger for power and some arrogance towards subordinates. He's still genuinely loyal, claiming he cares about the Cave Kingdom more than Wemmbu does.

## Relationships

- [Wemmbu](wemmbu.md): rival, ally and finally the man he beat for the crown.
- [Arachn1d](arachn1d.md): the king he served and killed.

## Story so far

- A Zam Empire guard taken hostage by Wemmbu's civilization.
- He raids the Wheat Kingdom, loses to Wemmbu in the tournament, and commands a thousand-player army against him.
- Ep. 150: he helps Wemmbu destroy the Wraiths' vault, kills Arachn1d, and wins the throne in a duel.
