## Summary

The episode that starts it all. [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) stumbles on armour that should be impossible, and [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) shows him a hidden vault of ancient glitches. Together they start testing [Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md) in secret beneath a set of showpiece builds, [The Wonders](../../places/the-wonders.md), until Jamato walks away from Spoke's ever-bigger ambitions and [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) starts snooping. Spoke decides the evidence has to go, and fires an [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) at the Wonders.

## Full breakdown

### Cold open: the impossible armour

- Spoke tells Jamato the server is "running out of time": while enchanting at a zombie farm, he got all four protection enchantments on one set of diamond armour.
- Jamato explains this was possible in an old version of Minecraft, and takes Spoke to an underground vault of old glitches that should never coexist on one server: a spliced portal, a skeleton with no weapon, a redstone-trimmed netherite set with every protection enchant (which Spoke puts on), and a machine that strips block data to turn blocks into other blocks.
- They agree nobody can know. Jamato wants to start with the "safe" exploits; Spoke wants a working duplication glitch to fund everything else.

### Getting a shulker, and meeting Wemmbu

- The old dupe needs only one shulker box, so Spoke heads to the End, which turns out to be the hardest part.
- There he runs into Wemmbu, who is leaving on a "side-hobby project" and asks about the strange armour; Spoke claims he mined and enchanted it himself. Wemmbu shows off a shulker full of elytra, says "good luck finding an elytra", and leaves.
- Spoke gets shulker shells from end cities, and back home he and Jamato spend hours duping gold, debris, diamonds, emeralds, iron, XP bottles, anvils and redstone parts from a stash behind a painting.

### The Wonders

- They want to splice a portal with update suppression somewhere nobody would dare break into, so Jamato suggests asking [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md) and her builders.
- Spoke pays Squiddo thirteen shulkers of duped items to build five Great Wonders. Too impatient to wait, he digs under the Pyramid of Anubis to build a chunk suppressor, pulls the lever early, and crashes the server.
- When he checks on the builders, Squiddo is staring at a portal split in half. Spoke convinces her it's just lag, breaks the portal with a block, and tells her not to tell anyone. Jamato says that isn't how the exploit should have worked, and neither of them understands why.

### Testing glitches

- Spoke cycles between the raid farm (hunting a glitched crossbow) and hidden rooms under the Wonders: he makes uncraftable tipped arrows, fails a dupe and the "two billion bottles" glitch, and finally gets the crossbow.
- Jamato shows him a recipe-book trick that turns one dropped [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md) into a stack of 85, which Spoke tests by tanking Jamato's crits.
- Jamato gives Spoke the illegal netherite set from an armour stand. Worried about all the entities, Spoke rules out [chunk bans](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md) as protection, and they decide to hire guards instead.

### Hiring the Guardians

- [Roshambogames](../../characters/roshambogames.md), [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) and [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) are paid in duped items to guard the Wonders and keep quiet.
- The fifth applicant is Wemmbu, whose "talent" is vaporising an empty plains with his orbital cannon (built back on the Lifesteal SMP). Spoke hires him reluctantly: if he's ever found inside a Wonder, he's off the team.
- Back in the hidden room, Spoke accidentally unstacks his totems, and reminds himself he's only doing this to find answers; abusing the exploits would ruin the server.

### Dataless blocks and duped mobs

- Blowing up a brewing stand with a burning end crystal on the same tick as placing an enchanting table (then relogging) produces blocks with no data: an enchanting table loses its book, and an ender chest and wither skull turn invisible.
- For the next glitch Jamato needs "the goofiest, almost illegal mobs". Camping at the Crone, Spoke recreates the weaponless skeleton from the vault, plus a zombified piglin and a wither skeleton.
- The Crone sits on a stronghold, so the mobs can be split between the End and the Overworld and duplicated. The materials are hidden inside an End pillar.

### Wemmbu gets suspicious

- Wemmbu hears Spoke talking to himself inside the pillar. Spoke begs him to stay out, so Wemmbu respawns the dragon, which turns Spoke's shulker of duped redstone into obsidian.
- Spoke's excuses (an "End project", materials for Jamato) don't convince Wemmbu. Spoke attacks, Wemmbu pops his totem, and Spoke escapes through a gateway, then kills the dragon.
- Spoke decides to destroy every End portal so he can build in peace, and realises only one exploit could get rid of Wemmbu for good.

### Jamato walks away

- Jamato objects: his goal was to save the server by returning it to how it was, not to "play God". Spoke argues they found the armour for a reason and must build the final machine, the chunk splicer.
- Jamato refuses to be part of something that could break the server, and leaves. Spoke goes on alone.
- Without Jamato, Spoke struggles with a patched exploit, then stumbles onto a glitch never seen before, proof the server is truly unstable, and gets a command block and other illegal blocks. Jamato tells him to get rid of them.

### Destroying the evidence

- Spoke remembers Squiddo wearing red-trimmed netherite and fears she stole the illegal set. The armour is still there, but Wemmbu is waiting: in the fight Spoke uses his bedrock and equips the armour, Wemmbu breaks his helmet and boots, and Spoke escapes.
- Spoke builds an orbital TNT cannon a million blocks out to wipe the Wonders. Jamato arrives, not to stop him but to say goodbye before leaving, and they press the button together.

## Outcomes

- Jamato abandons the project and leaves Spoke.
- Wemmbu now knows Spoke is hiding something and has fought him twice.
- The first Wonder is destroyed by Spoke's cannon.
