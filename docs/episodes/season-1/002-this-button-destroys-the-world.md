## Summary

[ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s first episode. Craters start appearing where [The Wonders](../../places/the-wonders.md) stood, and Parrot tries to stop the destruction by learning to build his own [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md). Meanwhile [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Roshambogames](../../characters/roshambogames.md) are clearly hiding something from him. Parrot uncovers part of their secret, but the last Wonder falls anyway.

## Full breakdown

### The crater

- Wemmbu shows Parrot a giant crater and explains it was the test site for an orbital cannon; they work out it must be [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s, since Spoke has seen Wemmbu build one.
- Parrot assumes the Wonders are the only builds worth targeting. Roshambo interrupts their planning: the server is lagging, and the Gardens of Babylon Wonder he was guarding has been nuked.
- Wemmbu shrugs it off, so Parrot decides to protect the Wonders himself, which means learning to build a cannon from scratch.

### Learning redstone, and growing suspicions

- Parrot practises on small TNT machines; one gets griefed with obsidian. When he shows Wemmbu another, it launches them both into the air and keeps firing until Parrot breaks it. Wemmbu laughs that Parrot probably broke the first one himself.
- Parrot wonders why Wemmbu won't help when he knows how to build cannons.
- He overhears Wemmbu and Roshambo at the Marshland Mansion talking about illegal items and offering them to players, and Wemmbu worrying that Parrot will catch on.
- Parrot loses Wemmbu at his spawn base. A later machine kills an iron golem but lags the server, and Wemmbu suddenly flies off when he realises it shouldn't have.

### More Wonders fall

- The Crone is blown up; its guard [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) saw a ball of TNT appear. Parrot spots redstone parts in the debris, and Wemmbu suggests a cobblestone duper.
- Parrot builds a cannon that targets specific spots by button; he tests it on the ocean because he doesn't want to destroy anything.
- While Wemmbu is off hunting a "notes" book, Parrot finds a hidden lever in Wemmbu's base, a long tunnel and a 3x3 iron door, but doesn't dare press the button. He lies that the notes were at his own base.

### Parrot builds an orbital cannon

- Parrot doesn't know why Spoke is destroying the Wonders or what Wemmbu is hiding, but he builds an orbital cannon anyway. Wemmbu nitpicks it and calls it a waste of time.
- Parrot gives Wemmbu one last chance to explain, and threatens to dig under the Wonder they're standing on. Before he can, TNT destroys it, and there are redstone machines in the debris.
- In Wemmbu's secret room he finds one of those machines and duped fireworks, then hears Wemmbu and Roshambo coming. He realises Wemmbu is the better fighter, interrogates him instead, and Wemmbu flies off where Parrot's rockets can't follow.

### The guardians turn on Parrot

- Roshambo, [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) and Fantst try to stop Parrot firing, arguing it would only continue the cycle of destruction.
- Parrot leads them to Wemmbu's base for proof, but the secret door is gone. They chase him; his cannon is found blown up just before it fires, and a fight breaks off when the server lags again.
- Another Wonder is gone. Parrot blames Roshambo for letting it happen; Fantst asks if the last Wonder can still be saved.

### The last Wonder

- The guards bring materials and build an obsidian bunker in front of the final Wonder while Parrot builds a new cannon and presses the button.
- They wait four and a half hours. When the lag finally clears, Spoke's and Parrot's cannons have gone off at the same moment, and the final Wonder is destroyed. Fantst says they've lost; Parrot says everyone has.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Parrot learns to build one from scratch; Spoke's cannons destroy the Wonders.
- [Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md): the duped fireworks and machine hidden in Wemmbu's base, and the "illegal items" Wemmbu and Roshambo discuss.

## Outcomes

- All of The Wonders are destroyed.
- Parrot has learned to build orbital cannons, and knows Wemmbu and Roshambo are hiding something.
