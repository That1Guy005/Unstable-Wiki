## Summary

[Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s first POV, starting with him trapped in a minecart-filled room inside [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s vault of illegal items. In flashback, he follows Spoke's [Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md), joins the Wonder guardians as a spy, steers [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) to get Spoke's coordinates, and fights his way through a heavily guarded vault, only to walk into Spoke's revenge trap.

## Full breakdown

### Following the new player

- Bored, Wemmbu invisibly follows a new player, Spoke, and overhears him and [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) discussing illegal exploits. Knowing Spoke's history with exploits, he keeps following.
- After hearing about the illegal armour and that only the two of them know, he decides to learn every exploit they have, and trails Spoke for days.
- Confronted in the End about the armour, Spoke insists he ground for it, but Wemmbu saw him get it.

### Joining the guardians

- When Spoke recruits guards for [The Wonders](../../places/the-wonders.md), Wemmbu applies, suspecting they hide the exploits, and impresses Spoke by blowing up a field with his own [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md).
- Every time Wemmbu tries to enter a Wonder, Spoke somehow knows and stops him, then heads to the End.
- In the End, Wemmbu finds Spoke working inside hollowed-out End pillars. Spoke calls it an "End project" and turns the questions back on him, so Wemmbu respawns the dragon, resetting the pillars and deleting Spoke's shulkers.
- Spoke's excuses (checking shulkers, a package from Jamato) fall apart. He claims Jamato wants Wemmbu dead and attacks, barely scratching him; Wemmbu pops his totem with his fists before Spoke escapes to the outer islands.

### The fight inside the Wonders

- Jamato's and Spoke's bases are both empty. Wemmbu catches Spoke inside a Wonder relieved that some items are left, with an obviously suspicious "storage room".
- In the brawl Spoke puts on the illegal armour but still loses: Wemmbu pops his totem twice and breaks two armour pieces before Spoke slips into a wall, wrecking the machines as he flees and taking every exploited item.
- Wemmbu teams up with a new player, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), as the Wonders start being destroyed to hide what's beneath them, and manipulates Parrot into giving him Spoke's coordinates, millions of blocks out.

### Breaking into the vault

- He tunnels in under a huge vault, past soul sand and lava, and is spotted by the guard Tyrad; chorus fruit gets him out.
- Second attempt: an even fight with Tyrad, and an escape with invisibility potions. Third attempt: sneaking past underwater wardens and mining-fatigue defences, drinking milk as he goes, while the guards RealReeon and Sharkilz_ goof around.
- Inside, a 3v1 forces him to block off corridors and escape with swiftness. He kills elder guardians, goes deeper by elevator, traps Sharkilz and Reeon on a lower floor behind obsidian, and raids the guard storage room.
- With end crystals he kills Tyrad; a swarm of vexes saves Sharkilz. A third guard, Only_A_Squid, turns the next fight, and Wemmbu escapes up a water column while the guards fetch milk.

### Spoke's trap

- A bubble column leads to a decorated room with a trapped chest on a single TNT. Breaking it drops Wemmbu into a pit.
- Spoke gloats as TNT and lava rain down and wardens fire from all sides. This is payback, and he plans to actually kill Wemmbu. The wardens pop Wemmbu's totem.
- Chorus fruit lands him in a room packed with chest minecarts (the opening scene). He breaks enough carts to mine out with a milk bucket, while Spoke brags about hiring the best prison designers.

### The way out

- Wemmbu finds a gap in the lava defence, restocks, and jumps Spoke, who escapes the same way Wemmbu did, by blocking tunnels.
- A trapdoor drops Wemmbu into a lava room where hidden witches splash him with debuffs; he finds the flaw and kills them.
- Past a series of fake exits he finds the real one (bubble column, trapdoor, crawl tunnel, door, underwater exit) and finds Spoke crouching on the other side. Spoke pretends not to care, and escapes with an [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md).

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's guardian "audition".
- [Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md): Spoke's illegal armour and the stash of exploited items.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): totems popped on both sides.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Spoke's escape at the end.

## Outcomes

- Tyrad is killed by Wemmbu's end crystals.
- Wemmbu escapes Spoke's vault prison; Spoke gets away by stasis, and the hunt is on.
