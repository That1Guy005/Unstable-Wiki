## Summary

A week after millions of duped items flood the server, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) sets out to destroy every stash, which he admits are the fallout of his own dupe glitch. With new partner [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), he works through [Roshambogames](../../characters/roshambogames.md)' vaults, the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md) and [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md)'s castle, and causes one disaster that changes his attitude to the [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): they accidentally destroy the home of an innocent player, Jepexx.

## Full breakdown

### The first vault

- Spoke digs into a supposedly unbreakable vault, past a room of armour-stand "players", and pearls down into rows of chests of duped items, which he blows up.
- He falls through the floor into an obsidian box of end crystals where Roshambo has a bow aimed at his face, warning that someone is on the way to kill him. Spoke shoots the crystal himself and escapes by [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md).
- He decides to destroy the duped items for good, but keeps his last two pieces of exploited armour.

### Recruiting Mapicc

- Spawn has been set on fire. Following music, Spoke finds Mapicc, who hates Roshambo, used to steal from him, and can't understand how Roshambo suddenly got rich. Mapicc joins.
- In the Zam Empire, they bluff past guards [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) by claiming Spoke is Mapicc's silent little brother.
- Spoke sets a rule: the goal is to put things back the way they were, so no destroying builds and no killing players, or they'd be no better than Roshambo. They pearl into the castle vault, blow up as much as they can, and slip away into the Nether as the guards fly off.

### Confession

- Mapicc asks how Roshambo learned to dupe, and Spoke admits he found the glitch, and that he paid lots of players in duped items before seeing the consequences. Mapicc is angry until Spoke says an old friend convinced him never to touch exploits again.
- At Squiddo's base they burn duped barrels, accidentally setting her castle on fire when she logs on, and find a chest with what look like coordinates to Roshambo's vaults.

### Following the coordinates

- Vault 1 is a trading hall in a mountain owned by Jepexx; they burn duped barrels until Jepexx catches them and loses it, then hide the rest.
- They set an [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md) in [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s secret room under his house.
- Vault 2 is a sky vault in the jungle guarded by players on horses; attacking it doesn't work, so they retreat.
- Spoke wants to use the orbital cannon, which is probably made of duped items anyway. Part of it has been nuked, so they retrieve the builders' spare materials (pointed out by pillagers arranged as arrows) and fix it.
- Mapicc presses the button in the room where Spoke last saw [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md), but nothing happens, because the cannon has already fired. They race to warn the guards, who don't believe them, and Spoke shoots them off.
- Mapicc's base is blown up by someone who overheard their plans, so they fire at the next target straight away.

### Jepexx's home

- The third coordinates turn out to be Jepexx's base, his home since nearly the start of the server, and that's where the cannon is aimed. The real vault is under his wheat field.
- They can't stop it in time. The strike wipes out Jepexx's home, and he tells Spoke to leave because he has done enough.
- Guilt-stricken, Spoke and Mapicc swear never to use the orbital cannon again, even on duped items, and promise to find new homes for everyone hurt by the dupes.

### The "final vault"

- A red-and-white mansion holds a book by Roshambo, "the final vault", with coordinates. Spoke goes alone and has Mapicc stand ready to pull his stasis.
- At the Soul Sand Vault, Roshambo, [PlanetLord](../../characters/planetlord.md) and Leo greet him ominously, and lead him inside; it isn't a trap after all. Mapicc pulls the stasis, but it fails because the dragon is still alive in the End.
- In voiceover Spoke admits he stayed to prove he wasn't afraid, but really he was scared of losing the chestplate and leggings, the last exploited items on the server and the only things left of Jamato.
- After Roshambo's group are chased off by withers into the void, every chest turns out to be empty. Roshambo tells Spoke he's chasing a ghost and that destroying dupes won't bring Jamato back, and warns that someone (implied to be Wemmbu) is coming to kill him.

### Twist

- Mapicc points out it makes no sense that Spoke "found every vault". The only vault Roshambo was actually at was the first one, which only Spoke had visited.
- Back in that room, Spoke realises the lag came from hidden dupes, not the crystals: the obsidian walls are lined with countless barrels of duped items.
- The episode closes on Spoke's End base, meant to be his home, now exposed.

## Items & mechanics

- [Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md): millions of duped items, and Spoke's last two pieces of exploited armour.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): repaired and fired, destroying Jepexx's home; Spoke swears off it afterwards.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): set in Wemmbu's base as an escape, but it fails while the dragon is alive.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): escapes and travel.

## Outcomes

- Jepexx's home is destroyed by mistake.
- Spoke admits to Mapicc that he caused the dupe crisis; the two become partners.
- The biggest stash is found, hidden in the walls of the first vault.
