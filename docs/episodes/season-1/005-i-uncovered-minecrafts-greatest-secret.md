## Summary

[ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) receives a music disc from [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) promising a server-changing secret, and brings his friend [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) on a huge scavenger hunt. They're chased by [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) the whole way, and the final challenge reveals the "secret" was a trap: only one of them can leave without a [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md).

## Full breakdown

### The first discs

- Played with Spoke's texture pack, the disc says there's a huge secret and tells Parrot to bring a friend to some coordinates.
- Two towers with pressure plates blow open a hillside gate onto an abandoned city. The next disc says "follow the light", and TNT goes off as they grab it.
- Worried Spoke always knows exactly when they find a disc, Parrot gives Wifies his prized spyglass as a token of trust.
- At the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md), Wemmbu and Zam demand the discs; Wifies splashes [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) and they escape.
- The blinking lighthouse spells "minus 64" in Morse, leading to a staircase into the void, then a disc sending them 2,000 blocks north through the void, where both holes turn out to be blocked. Low on rockets, they use fishing rods to conserve them and reach [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md)'s void base.

### The Zam vault, and riddles

- The next disc gives 9783, the code to Zam's vault. Invisible, they sneak past [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md) and grab the disc and a lodestone compass just before Merffinn comes back from AFK.
- At a floating lodestone in an ice-spikes biome, the clue "it gets pretty straight to the point" means a straight line from a spike's tip to the lodestone, which leads to a buried chest and another compass.

### Challenges run by an invisible host

- Inside a hollow ice mountain, an invisible player makes them store their gear and climb a parkour course to the top; the disc there is just Spoke saying "good job". Parrot wonders again why he had to bring a friend.
- In an obsidian maze (Parrot secretly keeps two invisibility potions and golden carrots), they hear Wemmbu and Zam, and are trapped at a blocked exit by mining fatigue. Wemmbu finds them, and they trade the central disc and turtle egg for their freedom.
- They set an auto-stasis and steal the disc and egg back from Zam's vault, are chased into the Nether, promise to team up against Spoke, and break the portal behind them once Wemmbu and Zam go through.

### Shipwreck, jungle, and the Nether

- The hatched turtle leads them to a shipwreck. With turtle helmets and water-breathing, they fill four tower item frames to open a hatch into an underwater graveyard holding the next disc.
- A jungle temple disc sends them to 5000, 5000 in the Nether, where an escape room drops them into a fake mesa inside a 100x100 obsidian box. Parrot notes how much obsidian and manpower all this must have taken.

### The reveal

- The invisible host drinks milk: it's Spoke. The last obstacle is a series of tasks (puzzle, redstone parkour, maze, potion course, parkour, mini escape room), and only one of them leaves; the other is chunk banned.
- Tied at the end, they play the chess tiebreaker to a deliberate stalemate. Spoke refuses to let both go.
- Wifies asks if the secret is really worth it, gives the spyglass back to Parrot, and steps into the chunk ban himself.
- Spoke admits there was no secret. It was revenge for something Parrot did in the past, and since Parrot had nothing valuable to take, Spoke made him bring someone he'd grow close to. He could tell Wifies did it for someone he cared about.

### Spoke's exit

- Wemmbu, Zam, Merffinn and [LuigiToan](../../characters/luigitoan.md) arrive; they had been tracking Spoke all along, which is why they wanted the hunt so badly.
- They chase Spoke to the chess room. Cornered, he says "Parrot, I'm sorry man, but—" and steps into the chunk ban.

## Items & mechanics

- [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md): the price of the final challenge, taken by Wifies and then by Spoke.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): their escapes from Zam's forces; Spoke stays invisible as the host.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Parrot's auto-stasis before the vault heist.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): flying through the void with fishing rods to conserve rockets.

## Outcomes

- Wifies is chunk banned, by his own choice, to save Parrot.
- Spoke steps into a chunk ban to escape the Zam Empire.
- Parrot keeps the spyglass, a symbol of trust that matters again much later.
