## Summary

The same weeks from [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s side. After Spoke's stasis escape, Wemmbu allies with [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) to hunt [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and chunk bans his crew one by one, follows the scavenger hunt, and wins a marathon fight at Squiddo's castle, until Spoke chooses a [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md) over capture. The episode ends with the realisation that chunk bans will need prisons.

## Full breakdown

### Looking for Spoke

- Spoke's base is empty; [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) won't talk and [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) knows nothing. At Jamato's base, Wemmbu hides from three Zam guards ([ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md), Sorhin and [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)) who are also hunting Spoke.
- He follows them to the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md) to propose an alliance, but [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) ambushes them and a [Bomber Plane](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md) drops TNT on Zam's castle. A huge battle between Zam's guards and Spoke's crew ends with TeamKalal blown up and the rest escaping by [stasis](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md).
- [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md) agrees to send Spoke's last location, but contraband, stacked totems and a spawn egg in her chests get her chunk banned.
- At the lighthouse Parrot admits to the scavenger hunt; Wemmbu demands the disc and loses them in a chase.

### Into the End

- At the stronghold, Mapicc, Sharkilz_ and RealReeon guard the portal; Reeon is chunk banned.
- At a massive End base they ambush Spoke, JumperWho and Derapchu, banning the latter two, but Spoke escapes into the void.

### The mace plan

- Since Spoke always has totems, they plan to strip them and one-shot him with a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). After getting a trial-chamber map they collect the materials, are jumped by Arcn, Only_A_Squid, Fantst, Sharooh and Mapicc, and ban Arcn.
- With an arrow tracker they hunt Spoke's henchmen: Only_A_Squid and Sharooh are banned.
- At an obsidian box, invisible attackers (Spoke, Sharkilz and Fantst) take Zam hostage and demand the mace. Wemmbu burns it instead, and both are nearly killed before their stases pull them out, broke.

### The maze and the trap

- In another obsidian box, a maze, Wemmbu mines in through the roof with milk against mining fatigue, finds Parrot and Wifies without armour, and trades their freedom for the disc and turtle egg, then realises he needs Parrot's texture pack to hear it.
- The arrow tracker had pointed to Parrot and Wifies, so they must be linked to Spoke. Wemmbu and Zam put the disc in Zam's vault as bait and set their stases in a pile of gold and emerald blocks.
- At an underwater castle full of elder guardians they ban Sharkilz_, but the castle blows up, breaking the ban, and Sharkilz and a hidden Fantst attack until both are banned.

### Following the hunt

- Zam goes back for more people and chunk bans while Wemmbu stalls Spoke. An invisible player who communicates by signs offers to take him to Spoke if he does the hunt's challenges: the ice parkour with no armour, then the maze in two minutes.
- The host's sign says "Squiddo's castle", and Wemmbu realises it's Spoke himself; Spoke stasises away mid-fight.
- At Squiddo's burning castle, Spoke taunts him that no amount of bans or manpower can match his gear. After a very long fight, Wemmbu steals Spoke's shulker of materials and drains him, and Spoke stasises again, leaving tracking arrows behind on purpose.

### Endgame

- Wemmbu's stasis pulls him back to Zam's vault, where Zam, ManePear and Peentar are fighting two invisible thieves: Parrot and Wifies. The pair escape through a Nether portal after promising to help.
- With guards Merffinn and [LuigiToan](../../characters/luigitoan.md), Wemmbu follows Spoke's arrows and finds Parrot chasing Spoke through the sky; Spoke leads them into the final challenge rooms.
- Merffinn finds an active chunk ban under the chessboard. Spoke says a few last words to Parrot and steps in.
- Wemmbu realises that Spoke would rather be banned than die to the Zam Empire, so chunk bans will need prisons to stop them being broken. And the Zam Empire needs rebuilding.

## Items & mechanics

- [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md): the Zam Empire's weapon of choice; nearly all of Spoke's crew is banned, and then Spoke himself.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): crafted to one-shot Spoke, and burned rather than surrendered.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): everyone's escape route, including Wemmbu's own trap at the Zam vault.
- [Bomber Plane](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md): the TNT plane that bombs Zam's castle.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): Spoke's stacked totems are why he's so hard to kill.

## Outcomes

- Chunk banned: TheRealSquiddo, RealReeon, Derapchu, JumperWho, Arcn, Only_A_Squid, Sharooh, Sharkilz_, Fantst, and finally Spoke.
- TeamKalal is blown up in the castle battle.
- The Zam Empire decides to build prisons around the chunk bans.
