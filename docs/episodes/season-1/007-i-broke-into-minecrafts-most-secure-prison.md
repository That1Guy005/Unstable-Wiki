## Summary

[ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) refuses to accept that [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) is gone. Chunk bans are now guarded by [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md)'s prisons, so Parrot and [Rekrap2](../../characters/rekrap2.md) break into them one by one, freeing Spoke's old crew to build a prison-breaking team. With [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md), [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) and the professional escapist [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md), they finally crack the most secure prison of all and free Wifies, and Spoke.

## Full breakdown

### The plan

- Prison one, a purple hurricane-shaped prison: Parrot and Rekrap sneak in with [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) and pearls, past a bubble-column and sign maze, but are caught in the lava defence and chorus-fruit out.
- Parrot explains in voiceover: each prison surrounds a [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md), and Wifies is in the most secure one. To get there they need a team, so they'll free Spoke's former underlings first. Parrot builds TNT cannons to destroy each chunk ban, and Rekrap distracts the guards (Merffinn, yungwillx and Woogiex).
- Prison two, in the End (islands linked by flying machines): Rekrap is spotted, Parrot's cannon attempts fail, and they escape after a long chase.
- They ask Zam to lift the ban for sixty seconds; Zam refuses, and Parrot doesn't believe his excuses.

### Early breakouts

- An underwater prison: Parrot works out how to detonate submerged TNT using sand and withers. Inside an obsidian maze they're spotted and escape (Rekrap's "gyatt" moment), then return, spawn withers at the ban while Rekrap distracts, and free **Derapchu**, who joins the team.
- Derapchu knows of a jungle prison. They practise until they can build a simplified TNT cannon in under fifteen seconds. Inside they find strange redstone, Parrot runs the guards around, and Rekrap blows the ban, but the freed player doesn't want to join.
- Near the first prison, their game crashes before they get within 1,000 blocks: Zam's new server-crashing defence protects the prison holding Spoke and Wifies.

### New tricks

- A Nether prison inside a volcano has three chunk bans stacked apart. Parrot's new method is flying machines plus [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md); he is caught building and leads the guards off while Rekrap and Derapchu destroy the bans.
- At the Zam Empire, Zam lets slip that the defence is a "memory ban" but won't explain it. Parrot reasons that guards rotate between prisons, so there must be a way in and out that they use.
- A snowglobe prison with drowned patrolling a soul-sand elevator and a beacon frees **[Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md)**, a strong PvPer, who joins and knows of a sky prison. There, a fight with [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md), Willsion, SykerMC and SmileyyMC forces a retreat.
- Back at base, Fantst says their mission is impossible, and steps back into a chunk ban without hesitation, a heavy loss for the team.

### The last small prisons

- A second End prison (guards [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md) and SykerMC) falls easily, but the freed player again won't join.
- At the other End prison, guard [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) is eating pizza in real life; when he's done he attacks, and they leave.
- **JumperWho**, freed earlier, arrives and joins as the fourth member.

### The final prison

- They get around the memory ban by travelling through the Nether, but six guards still patrol.
- Parrot's trick: there's a tiny delay between entering a chunk ban and being banned, so a bait player can lure guards into an active ban. JumperWho volunteers, and it works: Merffinn, yungwillx, Peentar, SykerMC, Loppezz and ManePear are all banned.
- Parrot brings in a fifth member, the professional prison escapist _Kenadian_, who studies footage of the prison and plans the break-in.
- The plan works: Wifies and Spoke are freed.
- In response, Zam puts a bounty on Parrot's head, and he is now hunted by the server's deadliest player.

## Items & mechanics

- [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md): the heart of every prison, the bait trap that bans six guards, and Fantst's return to one.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Parrot's flying machine plus TNT minecart method for destroying bans.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): their standard way of sneaking in.
- Withers and sand: used to detonate TNT underwater.

## Outcomes

- Freed: Derapchu, Fantst (who goes back in), two players who refuse to join, JumperWho, and finally Wifies and Spoke.
- Banned by the bait trap: Merffinn, yungwillx, Peentar, SykerMC, Loppezz and ManePear.
- Zam puts a bounty on Parrot, and ClownPierce is coming.
