## Summary

[PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) calls in [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s favour: build a colony for the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md) to guard a secret vault. With [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) and the builder [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md), it grows into [Wemmbu's Civilization](../../places/wemmbus-civilization.md), while [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md), saboteur guards and assassins keep attacking. Wemmbu shelters [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) from [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md), discovers the vault is empty, and learns that Zam was behind it all, as she orders the colony blown up.

## Full breakdown

### A favour owed

- Zam says Wemmbu owes her for helping ban Spoke; she wants a colony guarding a vault. Wemmbu hands over all his elytras to free up ender-chest space.
- The vault is an obsidian box under a red tulip on a hill. Wemmbu builds a wooden starter house he calls "the shed".
- He invites Egg, finds his own house near Spawn blown up, and the two, now homeless, start a pumpkin farm and shulker storage. Realising Egg isn't good at the game, Wemmbu sets [stasis chambers](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md) for both of them.

### Flame attacks

- Flame arrives without his Zam armour or shield and attacks; Wemmbu stasises to the bunker. With Egg as bait (after a crash course in PvP), they fight him off with [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md), but the redstone shulker is destroyed.
- Wemmbu suspects Flame came for the vault, and refuses to let Egg look inside. They build a villager breeder, campfires and a two-block obsidian wall.
- Under-geared attackers sent by Flame ([LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md), Twirps, [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md), youri_mc, [Falconu](../../characters/falconu.md) and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md)) are driven off thanks to Wemmbu's elytra, but the colony is wrecked. Egg wants to cut ties with Zam; Wemmbu gives him a history lesson instead.

### Building a real civilization

- Horace critiques their builds, so Wemmbu hires him for twelve emeralds. Overnight he builds a castle and a village like the Zam Empire's, then more renovations and a town hall for another twelve.
- New residents include [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md), somehow unbanned, plus Dione4K and Block_Facts.

### Sheltering Parrot

- Clown, in netherite, searches the castle for an invisible Parrot, who messages Wemmbu "help me". Once Clown leaves, Parrot explains Zam hired Clown to assassinate him, and that Clown blew up Wifies' house.
- Wemmbu lets Parrot go; Parrot suggests freeing Fantst. Zam turns up with guards [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md) and Merffinn, claims Flame betrayed them, says nothing about Clown, and leaves the guards "to protect" the colony.
- They free and recruit [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md), and find Rekrap's base and the prison-breakers' HQ with god armour.

### Sabotage and an empty vault

- Horace adds a thirty-block wall, farms and a town hall. At night Wemmbu catches Mane and Merffinn rigging the [railgun](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) so it won't fire.
- Egg argues there's no reason not to check the vault now. The guards had moved the auto-loader, and the vault is empty: they've been guarding nothing. Either Zam is a terrible emperor or they don't know the whole story.
- Parrot returns asking for protection, having left the Clown hunters, who didn't care about his safety. Wemmbu takes him in as a test: if Clown and Flame show up right after the guards learn Parrot is here, it confirms Zam's involvement.
- Parrot builds chunk-ban traps ("flycatchers"), and Wemmbu gears up the citizens, makes amends with Fantst, warns everyone off the guards, and makes sure Merffinn hears Parrot is a citizen.

### The test confirms everything

- Flame, Falconu, Lettuce, Spongs and an invisible Clown attack the gate (Jaden gets chunk banned). They pearl in and the crop fields are bombed, but the defenders win without losses, though half the town is destroyed. Parrot leaves after thanking them.
- Flame attacks again; Wemmbu's plan at an ocean monument fails when the elder guardian despawns, and he escapes with a nearly broken trident while Egg distracts.
- Zam, Sorhin, Merffinn and Flame are waiting at the castle. Wemmbu gives Zam her usual tour, then confronts her. This time she admits it: Flame was working to kill him on her orders all along, and she no longer sees Wemmbu as a threat. She orders the colony blown up.
- Wemmbu says this is just Zam covering for failing to stop "the weakest player", Parrot, from breaking her prisons, and that Clown has already failed to kill him. As the colony burns, he tells her underestimating him was a big mistake.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Wemmbu and Egg's emergency exits.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the traps used against Flame.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the colony's railgun, sabotaged by Zam's guards.
- [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md): Parrot's "flycatcher" traps, which catch Jaden.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Wemmbu's escape weapon at the ocean monument.

## Outcomes

- Jaden_MAN is chunk banned in a flycatcher.
- Wemmbu's Civilization is destroyed on Zam's orders.
- Zam's betrayal is in the open, which leads to the Empire War.
