## Summary

The hunt [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) paid for begins. [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) stalks [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), and [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md)'s Clown hunters use Parrot as bait in a puzzle vault. Parrot learns the hunters don't care about his safety, sees [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) pressured into giving him up, and nearly crosses a line with Clown's friend BranzyCraft, before facing Clown in an explosive showdown.

## Full breakdown

### Clown arrives

- Clown breaks into Parrot and Wifies' home, nearly kills Parrot, then orders him to follow to his castle (a circus). Leo, [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md), Woogiex and Willsion storm it and Clown escapes.
- Leo has long been hunting Clown with hired bounty hunters, and offers protection in exchange for Parrot's help. They give Parrot and Wifies TNT to blow up Clown's castle; Clown ignores the explosions and keeps targeting Parrot.
- Parrot and Wifies suspect Zam hired Clown.

### The bait vault

- Parrot locks himself in Leo's vault (a little house, containers and a jungle biome); Leo won't reveal the escape routes ("you never know who could be listening"), gives him the hint "B5", and stasises away. Wifies has secretly come along invisible, since "two is better than one".
- Sirens signal Clown. The hunters should win 4v1, but one by one they "dip": Leo, then Derapchu and Jumper, leaving Woogie alone to stall Clown's team.
- While solving the vault's puzzles (falling through a shulker into layer one, finding an evacuation port), they hear [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md), Clown and Mugm_ inside. Clown has a team. They flee through the control room with [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) and Wifies pearls them out through the Nether.

### Losing faith in the hunters

- At HQ, Leo, Jumper and Woogie are lounging among villagers. Parrot scolds them for leaving without warning, then decides to live at spawn for a few days.
- Wifies' base perimeter has been bombed; Clown chases Parrot again.
- Parrot asks [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) for help, but Wemmbu says he's with the Zam Empire, so Parrot leaves, suggesting Fantst as a recruit. He realises Wemmbu could be a future ally.
- The hunters want to buy a yacht and nobody knows where Derapchu is. Jumper gives Parrot PvP training, and Wifies reveals he's been living on the fourth floor of HQ.
- At Clown's castle, chunk bans built around Clown and Ferre fail to catch them. Leo says Clown's weakness is BranzyCraft. At Branzy's mooshroom-island factory, Parrot asks him to make Clown stop involving innocent people, and Branzy goes off to tell Clown.

### Two sides of the war

- Parrot sides with Wemmbu's civilization, builds chunk-ban traps, and helps fight off Flame, Jaden, Falconu, Lettuce, Spongs and an invisible Clown (the same battle as Wemmbu's episode).
- He tells Wifies he has a plan he wants Wifies kept out of, and flies off invisible.
- With Woogie he talks the hunters into teaming up again. At the factory they trap Branzy in cobwebs and stone and Parrot pops his totem; Wifies crashes next to Parrot, returns his elytra, and boats away.
- In a new jungle base, Wifies splashes Parrot with invisibility and apologises: Clown's team threatened to kill him unless he gave up Parrot's coordinates. Clown's team blows up the jungle searching for them.
- The Wemmbu Civilization, the "last safe place", has been destroyed. Parrot declares he'll kill Clown and destroy everything Clown cares about.

### The showdown

- Parrot tells the hunters his plan to 1v1 Clown and nuke his castle; they call him insane but follow. Practising with Wifies goes badly ("stay safe").
- At the factory, Branzy is replacing the cow Parrot killed. Parrot spares him and thanks him.
- The next day, after some small talk, Parrot fights Clown at the ruined castle in so many explosions that the server lags, and escapes to Wifies' base. Clown has disappeared.
- Parrot thanks Wifies for convincing him not to kill Branzy, since there'd be no coming back from it, and tells Clown they "aren't the same".
- A framed book on two iron blocks at Parrot's base sets up the next step of the search for Clown.

## Items & mechanics

- [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md): traps at Clown's castle, and Parrot's flycatchers at Wemmbu's Civilization.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Wifies' tag-along and their escapes.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wifies returns Parrot's elytra mid-chase.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): how Leo leaves the vault.
- TNT: nuking Clown's castle, twice.

## Outcomes

- Parrot loses trust in the Clown hunters, and forgives Wifies for giving up his location under threat.
- BranzyCraft is spared.
- Clown vanishes after the fight, and the search continues.
