## Summary

With [Wemmbu's Civilization](../../places/wemmbus-civilization.md) in ruins, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) dig a hideout beneath it and go into business as base destroyers for hire, to fund a war against [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md). The jobs take them into Spoke's base, [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md)'s tower and the [Treasure Hunters](../../factions/treasure-hunters.md)' office, and lead to an alliance with [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) that ends with an [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) heist and a declaration of war.

## Full breakdown

### Going into business

- Egg helps dig a secret hideout under the ruins. Wemmbu's orbital cannon is millions of blocks away, and used up, since he spent its shot showing off to Spoke at the guard interview.
- First client: [Roshambogames](../../characters/roshambogames.md), who wants Spoke's crowded base destroyed. They go in under [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) and TNT almost the whole first floor by hand. Ro pays a shulker of netherite each, since risking your only life costs a lot.
- A sign on an obsidian pillar at exactly 50,000, 50,000 gives new coordinates; they talk to clients invisibly through signs to hide who they are.

### Woogie and Ferre

- At the Clown hunters' ruined HQ, Woogiex says [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) levelled it in the five minutes they were away, and hires them against Ferre, Clown's coach (Egg is amazed that Minecraft coaches exist).
- Invisible, they sneak through Ferre's tower and spot him entering a secret room, then wait in silence until Ferre mutters "There's no point", and mine out.
- Wemmbu rejects simply going in and blowing it up, and checks their stolen resources for a cannon (just short of activator rails). Egg is lost in Wemmbu's "technobabble", and says he came here to build a colony and keeps having to dig underground like an insect.
- Six missile planes, built outside Ferre's render distance, fly at the tower (two pick up stray blocks mid-flight). Egg comes in as bait, but Ferre goes for Wemmbu and pops a totem.
- Woogie admits he saw their invisibility run out, has no netherite to pay them, and can't get more from Leo, who has vanished, like Willsion and Derapchu before him. They part with "good luck".

### The Treasure Hunters

- Looking for the Treasure Hunters (undying loyalty costs a shulker of netherite; only money can be trusted), they find spawn transformed, and Zam bans them from it.
- [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md) escorts them out and quietly asks if Wemmbu needs help, since he hates serving Zam; Wemmbu tests him with a thrown arrow and a white tulip.
- At a jungle office, Kierrrr (no helmet) and Its_Dev_ (no boots), "a fashion statement", lead them to [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md), who hires them to grief a target. Egg nearly leaks Mane's offer; they come away with two stacks of diamond blocks plus netherite blocks and ingots.
- Egg logs off for his girlfriend, calling himself "a little man in a cruel world". Alone, Wemmbu finds two of his [bomber planes](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md) broken by chunk unloading.

### Rekrap's base, and a deal with Parrot

- At the target, Parrot and Wifies panic. It's Rekrap's base, part of their quest. Parrot suggests faking the destruction by sparing the underground part, so Wemmbu blows up the surface with their help; Wifies mentions he's been kidnapped quite a few times.
- Boosfer pays a black shulker of netherite blocks and asks for more jobs; Wemmbu asks what he really destroyed, and gets thrown out.
- At the ruins, Wemmbu asks Parrot and Wifies to join his war on Zam. Parrot only wants to find the missing players, but agrees after Wemmbu points out that Zam is a menace and they're already at war with Boosfer: five weeks ago he'd have said no.
- They build the cannon and strike the Treasure Hunters' vault, ender-chesting everything they can; Parrot keeps the diamonds for now, calling the Treasure Hunters hoarders.

### Declaring war

- At sunrise on the inner wall, Wemmbu says everything is ready. Egg is back ("finally gone!"). Egg refuses to blow up the village, since the people should be left alone.
- Wemmbu signs "- wemmbu" at the foot of the blown-up statue: war on the PrinceZam Empire.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): rebuilt for the heist on the Treasure Hunters.
- [Bomber Plane](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md): missile planes against Ferre's tower, and bombers that break in unloaded chunks.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): every infiltration.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): Ferre pops one of Wemmbu's.

## Outcomes

- Spoke's base is TNT'd; Rekrap's base is partly destroyed (only the surface).
- The Treasure Hunters' hoard is looted.
- Wemmbu and Parrot become allies, and Wemmbu declares war on the Zam Empire.
