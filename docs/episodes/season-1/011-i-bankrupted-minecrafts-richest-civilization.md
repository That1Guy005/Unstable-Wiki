## Summary

The duped items [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) destroyed are back, and [Roshambo's Civilization](../../places/roshambos-civilization.md) is richer than ever. Working down a coordinate book of the server's lag-causing farms, they quietly sabotage dozens of them, helped by the friendly loner [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md), until Spoke realises he has handed the server's weak points to its most dangerous player, and the first members of [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) appear.

## Full breakdown

### A new stash and a new friend

- An empty stockpile in a mountain, and [PlanetLord](../../characters/planetlord.md), who escapes during a lag spike.
- Attackers chase them who clearly aren't Roshambo's people; a loner named Ashswag, who has never interacted with anyone, drives them off, says he was "just passing", and offers help whenever they need it.
- Spoke's plan: work through a coordinate book of farms and quietly break the ones lagging the server.

### Farm after farm

- They fix a broken iron farm's cobblestone generator, escape a TNT house at spawn, and turn away [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md). Mapicc grows suspicious of Ash's obsession with the coord book; Spoke thinks he's just being helpful.
- They wall off a farm in the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md), and around Jepexx's prismarine guardian farm they splash his villagers with invisibility so they seem to vanish. Jepexx mentions an "ultimate farm".
- They destroy a lag-causing melon farm, block golems from an iron farm's kill chamber, and leave cover-up signs at ruined farms ("so sorry! - The Meteor", "so sorry! - the rogue cow") while saving the parts in chests.
- Along the way: Squiddo's sheep farm, a guardian farm where they spawn withers while [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) is AFK, a cobweb farm they don't understand, a Nether farm, hoglins overspawning from swapped fungus, and a chase by [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md) through the Nether. They give the armour they collected to Squiddo.

### The Mafia shows itself

- At a creeper farm, two invisible players start blowing it up. One, caught, says Ash is the leader, and is instantly teleported into the void and banned.
- Furious, Mapicc says Ash is behind everything, but Spoke still trusts him and gives him a full copy of the coord book so they can split up.
- At [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md)'s old base, Spoke remembers how Jamato and Mapicc were the only ones who befriended him, and decides not to lose Mapicc the way he lost Jamato.
- Explosions destroy their End base, leaving them in a temporary bunker.

### Too late

- At a flower farm, Roshambo and Planet are questioning them when the whole farm explodes in a huge amount of TNT; Roshambo accuses them, then believes their denial.
- The next farm has already been destroyed, and Spoke realises he caused it by giving Ash every coordinate.
- At the last farm, Ash laughs off Spoke's accusations, says this was his plan all along, and warns Spoke to choose the right thing.
- A huge invisible group in plain netherite attacks Mapicc; the episode ends with the sound of someone digging towards them. This is only the beginning.
