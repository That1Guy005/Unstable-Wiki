## Summary

[ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s friends [Rekrap2](../../characters/rekrap2.md), [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md), [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) and [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) have vanished, along with [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md). At a sky island, "game masters" [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) and [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md) set Parrot and [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) seven quests to recover each missing friend's prized possession, while [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md)'s [Treasure Hunters](../../factions/treasure-hunters.md) try to steal them. The last item is Parrot's own spyglass, and it opens the way into [Proton](../../places/proton.md).

## Full breakdown

### The first quests

- Heart of the Sea (Derapchu's): a gold box in a lake hides a compass; the Treasure Hunters' Kierrrr and Its_Dev_ "assist" on Boosfer's orders and Parrot flees invisibly; a book on Clown-themed ships says "dig where the winds meet", so they follow the sails. Blue beacon.
- Netherite boots (Jumper's) in a sky town: Kier and Dev pile on chores (32 fish, balloons, lanterns, crops, cauldrons) until Parrot sends them chasing an "arrow-shaped" balloon; a glowstone trail activates flying machines that reveal the boots. White beacon.
- Boosfer, at his jungle office, admits he steals prized possessions to sell, and won't stop even after Parrot explains what's at stake.

### The poppy, and Rekrap's tree

- A poppy hidden in a huge flower field (Parrot goes mad at the red tulips) leads through poppy caves to a valley and a giant tree escape room. Boosfer's crew burns the tree; Parrot rewrites their clue book with a fake hint ("...under the boulder below") and a "deez nuts" second page.
- The tree turns out to be Rekrap's base. With the poppy in Wifies' ender chest, the Treasure Hunters threaten to bomb the base unless it's delivered in thirty minutes.
- Evacuating the animals, they find bombers and [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md), paid anonymously to grief the base. It's Boosfer's job, and Wemmbu needs money for a war against [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md). Parrot persuades him to rob Boosfer instead. Beacon three.

### A diamond, a heist, and the Nether

- Jaden and Spongs keep being absent. The cave quest (an escape room, a maze, and a warden fight for a sculk catalyst to open a vault) yields Leo's diamond, but Kier and Dev ambush them at the temples and steal everything.
- At Wemmbu's Civilization, Parrot agrees to help with the [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) as long as it's only used on the Treasure Hunters and the Zam Empire; the heist takes most of their riches, including the stolen items.
- The Nether quest: Morse in quartz ("Rurple"), a bastion sign, block-ID clues, fire-resistant dives into lava, and a library over a lava ocean reached by strider, where the Dewey Decimal System opens a vault holding Kenadian's wither rose. Fifth beacon.
- The End quest: parkour to the outer islands, an end city with Wifies' dragon head, and Kier and Dev threatening them with a respawned dragon before being chased off. Sixth beacon.

### The last key

- At the meeting spot is Clown, not the game masters. There are seven temples but only six beacons, and Clown says Parrot is the last key.
- Parrot places his precious spyglass in the final temple. Clown empties their inventories and leads them to what they've been working towards all along: Proton, Minecraft's most secure prison.

## Items & mechanics

- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): escaping the Treasure Hunters.
- [Bomber Plane](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md): Wemmbu's bombers over Rekrap's base.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): found in the End quest chest.

## Outcomes

- The Treasure Hunters are robbed of most of their wealth by Parrot, Wifies and Wemmbu.
- Parrot gives up his spyglass as the "last key".
- Parrot and Wifies are taken into Proton by Clown.
