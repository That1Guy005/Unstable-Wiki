## Summary

[Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) takes the war to [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md). His first assault fails, so he recruits the defector [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md), defends [Roshambo's Civilization](../../places/roshambos-civilization.md) against [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md), and starts kidnapping Zam's officers, which troubles [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md). After losing everything again, Wemmbu "surrenders", smuggles his gear into Zam's prison, frees Egg and finally fires his [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) on the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md), just before [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) takes him and Zam away.

## Full breakdown

### The first assault

- With Egg, ItsLilKae, LuckyMixx and Block_Facts, Wemmbu plans to open with an orbital strike, but the whole empire now has a giant obsidian roof. They fight the guards ([Pangi](../../characters/pangi.md), Dol9hin, [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md), [LuigiToan](../../characters/luigitoan.md), [WyLL](../../characters/wyll.md) and What_Max) while Wemmbu goes for Zam.
- He gets Zam alone in a cave and nearly kills her, but her guards arrive; Zam taunts him and Wemmbu calls her unable to take a fair 1v1. He escapes.
- [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) has rebuilt the civilization with a bigger obsidian wall, paid in netherite stolen from the Treasure Hunters.

### Mane and Roshambo

- Badly outnumbered, Wemmbu uses firework traps as a distraction, sneaks in invisible and frees Mane, who had betrayed Zam and been left AFK in an obsidian box.
- Mane warns that Zam is about to conquer Roshambo's civilization. Not fully trusting him, Wemmbu boxes him in obsidian and goes to check.
- Flame, duelling Roshambo for the civilization with a wooden sword, has popped all of Ro's armour except the chestplate when Wemmbu steps in to duel Flame himself, buying time for Ro to evacuate everyone and everything, then stasises out.

### Picking off the officers

- The new plan is to take down Zam's high-ranking officers first. Target one is yungwillx, who is easy to trap. Mane distracts him while Wemmbu rigs a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) trap; Wemmbu disguises himself as Mane, and Mane, muted because "his mom is in the room", accidentally drinks invisibility instead of speed. The trap fails, but they pop yungwillx's armour and force Zam's forces to retreat by threatening to kill him. yungwillx is taken hostage.
- Next is Peentar, yungwillx's closest friend. Egg objects that Wemmbu is turning into Zam; Wemmbu doesn't care. A note forces Peentar to dig secret tunnels to Zam's castle and set a trap under her throne before surrendering. He is imprisoned too, and when Mane and Wemmbu talk about killing both prisoners, Egg talks them out of it, though he wants no part of the bloodshed.
- The throne trap fires, but catches the guard Sorhin instead of Zam, so they take him prisoner too.

### Losing again

- Withers spawn: Zam has freed yungwillx and Peentar and is bombing the civilization. Wemmbu is trapped and stripped to his chestplate, and surrenders, promising to leave the empire alone; Zam spares him.
- Mane challenges Flame, "the Immortal Demon", to a duel to the death, but takes his armour off mid-fight when he starts losing. Zam promises to leave the civilization alone if Wemmbu does the same.
- Fleeing invisibly, Wemmbu sees Egg being held captive; Zam lets Wemmbu go but keeps Egg. Wemmbu leaves him.

### The cake trick

- On an island, Wemmbu, Mane and Lucky regroup. Mane wants to keep fighting; Wemmbu has one more idea.
- He sets off TNT in the empire to get the guards' attention and asks to be taken to Zam. Since he has nothing left, he says, he'd rather play alone in singleplayer, so he wants to turn himself in. Zam agrees to imprison him with Egg.
- Zam checks his inventory is empty by giving him 37 cakes to fill every slot. Wemmbu hides his ender chests, elytra and a stack of notch apples in his four crafting slots. Inside, he gears up from his ender chest, frees Egg, escapes, and fires the orbital cannon.
- TNT rains on the empire in multiple strikes, lagging the server. Egg escapes; Wemmbu loses the guards and fights Zam alone. She berates him for destroying everything she built since the start; he asks how it feels to lose her work in minutes, and says it's all her fault.
- Drained, they end up on a rooftop over the harbour. Zam finally apologises (they've both made mistakes) and Wemmbu admits they're even.
- Clown lands, threatens to ban Wemmbu, and tells them both they're coming with him.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): blocked by the obsidian roof at first; it finally levels the Zam Empire.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the failed trap on yungwillx.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Wemmbu's escape from the Roshambo duel.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): freeing Mane, scouting, fleeing.

## Outcomes

- The Zam Empire is destroyed by the orbital strike.
- Zam's guards are kidnapped and then freed: yungwillx, Peentar and Sorhin.
- Wemmbu and Zam make peace, and both are captured by Clown and taken to Proton.
