## Summary

[ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) drops [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) into [Proton](../../places/proton.md): a roofless obsidian box holding a whole simulated Minecraft world, with three totems each. With [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) and, later, [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md), Wemmbu gears up, survives Clown's "juggernaut" [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md), an orbital bombardment and Zam's mood swings, and builds a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) to shoot himself over the walls and chunk bans.

## Full breakdown

### Inside the box

- Zam immediately attacks and pops all three of Wemmbu's totems. Clown announces new prisoners; expecting to kill them for totems, Wemmbu instead finds Egg, who arrived with [Pangi](../../characters/pangi.md) and has only one totem, so they trap Pangi for one.
- They build a rough sky base and get iron armour. Zam, in half diamond with a Flame/Power bow, invites them to "Zam Empire 2.0" and attacks when they refuse.
- The escape plan is to leave the way Clown brought them in, by ender pearl cannon, since chunk bans almost certainly line the walls. Egg builds a creeper farm for gunpowder while Wemmbu mines for diamonds, surfaces in the middle of Zam Empire 2.0, and loots it while Zam and Pangi are away.
- Fishing for enchantments goes great for Egg (Protection III, Looting, an Infinity and a Power IV bow) and badly for Wemmbu.

### Juggernauts and bombardment

- Clown sends in a juggernaut: pop his totem and he teleports out. Zam offers an alliance if they put her insignia on their shields; Egg agrees, Wemmbu refuses, then they team up anyway and pop the juggernaut (Ferre's) totem and helmet.
- Zam builds a Nether portal from raided obsidian, and Pangi steps through into a [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md). Egg's attempt to charm Zam out of obsidian only makes them friends.
- Their creeper farm doesn't work, so Wemmbu asks Zam for help. She explains her obsidian came from raids and zombie villagers spawn here, tells Egg never to use that tutorial again, and suggests making it bigger.
- Clown announces he knows about their escape plan, and sends a "gift" for the one obsessed with orbital cannons: repeated [orbital strikes](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) erase Zam Empire 2.0.
- Zam blames Wemmbu for everything since the Spoke hunt. Wemmbu points out that Clown won't let her kill him, because Clown enjoys messing with them, and Clown immediately announces another juggernaut.
- With gunpowder sacrificed for carts, Wemmbu and Zam fight Ferre, who complains carts aren't allowed; Zam distracts him while Wemmbu pops him from a cart with a charged bow.

### The escape crew

- New prisoners arrive (Kenadian, LuckyMixx, Spepticle, Loppezz, SykerMC and Dol9hin). Zam wants to rebuild her empire; Wemmbu recruits Kenadian, Lucky and Spepticle to escape.
- Ken doesn't know TNT mechanics, but they calculate a shot of about 350 blocks. Zam attacks again, then gives up, since Clown won't let Wemmbu die.
- Ken builds the cannon from redstone, honey blocks, activator rails and an anvil: the pearl rides along honey blocks pushed by pistons on angled rails, and TNT fires it over the chunk bans.

### One pearl

- Spepticle gathers pearls at night, but Clown sees the attempt, pulls the newcomers out through preset stases, and sends guards to break the machine. Spepticle had the pearls, so only one is left.
- When the guards come early, Egg tells Wemmbu to go, swaps bows and gives him his totem, and holds the guards off while Wemmbu sets the carts and pulls the lever.
- Nothing seems to happen. The guards pop his totem and Wemmbu surrenders. Then the screen goes black: the pearl has cleared the inner wall, spikes, chunk bans and outer wall.
- Egg messages that the guards have him, and Wemmbu runs for freedom.

## Items & mechanics

- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): the TNT-fired ender pearl Wemmbu escapes with, designed by Kenadian.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Clown's "gift" that erases Zam Empire 2.0.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): the prison's currency, three each, and Egg's last gift to Wemmbu.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): carting the juggernaut.
- [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md): lines the walls, and catches Pangi through Zam's portal.

## Outcomes

- Pangi is chunk banned.
- Zam Empire 2.0 is destroyed.
- Wemmbu escapes Proton alone; Egg, Zam and the others are still inside.
