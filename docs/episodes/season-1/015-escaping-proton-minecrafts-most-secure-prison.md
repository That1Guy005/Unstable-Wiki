## Summary

Inside [Proton](../../places/proton.md), [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) loses [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) to a hidden fox ban almost immediately, then is shuffled between three cells by silent guards. Piecing together the prison's rules with the other missing players (and bans on talking and messaging), he organises smuggled emeralds, honey farms, maps and a secret stasis chamber, and with [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) coming back to help, leads a mass breakout through the guards' own control room.

## Full breakdown

### The cells

- The first cell is a hilly plains with a river and leafless cherry trees; they turn the trees into slabs to climb the crying-obsidian walls, and Wifies steps on a green platform and is chunk banned (a "fox ban").
- A silent guard gives Parrot a pearl and walks him to a red cell, where signs say "jumperwho and derapchu were here". [Rekrap2](../../characters/rekrap2.md) explains that Proton is brand new and holds every missing player, and that Wemmbu escaped before the prisoners started being moved.
- Wifies is freed, but whispering to him crashes Parrot's game, and chat messages don't appear at all, so talking is banned too.
- Climbing to build height they map three cells (red, green and blue) around a central room, plus a thin outer wall, and leave a trail of sign messages about everything they learn.

### Gathering intelligence

- The guards rotate prisoners between cells. Parrot catalogues their habits (one sprint-jumps everywhere, another keeps turning around) and gets to know [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md), [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) and [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) as they rotate. Ken notes it's easier to plan escapes from the outside.
- The guards don't patrol inside the cells, so someone could smuggle in an ender chest. They pass maps around. Emeralds shouldn't exist in the cells, so a wandering trader might still be reachable, and Rekrap leaves diorite Morse code (which the guards later notice).
- Parrot gets hit, poisoned and given rotten flesh by the guards, keeps thanking them sarcastically ("thank you for killing my horse"), and hears that a voice Wifies heard while being moved was [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md)'s, and that Wemmbu is due to arrive.

### Building the way out

- Wearing a crying-obsidian skin, Parrot sneaks through the tunnels behind a guard and tells Jumper, who is in solitary, that they've found a way out.
- With honey from 20 prepared hives, they build a [pearl launcher](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) and stasis parts; Parrot tells Egg that Wemmbu says "keep pushing".
- Ken picks a cave below y=0 for the [stasis chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md). Wemmbu arrives and tests the launch timing with eggs, using up all the eggs and one of their three pearls. Ken admits Wemmbu nearly killed her, and promises not to break the stasis, since it's their only way out.

### The breakout

- Parrot tells Wifies something important at the top of the waterfall, then activates the stasis with Rekrap and Ken. All three get out and hand out contraband, including shulkers of gear for Derapchu.
- Silent and with no elytra, they enter a control room. [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md) greets them, and real guards ask Parrot if he's there to take over their shift. He nods and keeps pressing the buttons that open the chambers. Ken volunteers for the riskiest doubling-up step.
- With only two elytra between them, they take turns flying out and dropping them back down; one elytra lands in a chunk ban as the guards catch up.
- The episode ends as Parrot activates the final step of the escape.

## Items & mechanics

- [Chunk Ban](../../mechanics/chunk-ban.md): hidden "fox bans" on the platforms; one catches an elytra at the end.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the secret way out, built below y=0.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): the launcher, built with honey blocks and an anvil and timed with eggs.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): two shared between everyone in the final escape.

## Outcomes

- Wifies is fox banned, then freed.
- The missing players break out of Proton with help from Wemmbu.
- Proton's guards are fooled in their own control room.
