## Summary

Tired of being hunted and kidnapped, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) travel millions of blocks into the End to found [The End Civilization](../../places/the-end-civilization.md). Their stalker [Deanthebean9](../../characters/deanthebean9.md), a Mafia member, becomes their friend, and [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) builds them a city, but [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md), spies and [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md)'s bombers find it anyway, and Parrot ends up lighting the fuse himself.

## Full breakdown

### Dean

- An invisible player has been following them; a trap pops his totem and reveals Deanthebean9, who was sent by the Mafia but joins them instead.
- Ferrying villagers by flying machine to the outer End islands, they're attacked by the Mafia, and Parrot has Dean go invisible so the Mafia doesn't learn he has switched sides. One machine survives; the other's villager dies.
- Paranoid that the Mafia knows the location, Parrot recruits more members and builds four [stasis chambers](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md).
- [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md) warns that stasis chambers work across dimensions, which means the Mafia could pull Dean's. They stage a rescue: Parrot pops Dean's totem at a Mafia outpost, Wifies distracts the invisible guards, and Ken pulls Dean's stasis on cue. Dean thanks Parrot for saving his life.

### Building the civilization

- Horace answers Parrot's request by building a castle; the next day there are outposts 1,000–1,500 blocks out, then a finished interior and a village.
- Boosfer turns up uninvited, pestering everyone and boasting that he found the coordinates; Parrot says his citizens won't be bribed, and Boosfer leaves saying loyalty is a figment of the imagination.
- Dol9hin and What_Max promise to vote for Parrot in the civilization's election for king.
- [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) returns, warns the civilization is sure to be found and destroyed, and says Parrot needs to stay put. New members [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) and [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) join; Fantst warns of invisible players.
- They spot invisible players building a TNT cannon. The citizens decide they don't mind being trapped in the End as long as the civilization is safe.

### Election day and the end

- Horace finishes the orb, and Parrot and Wifies cover it in water so TNT can't destroy it. Boosfer arrives with Kierrrr and Its_Dev_, who vote for Parrot and play dumb, not knowing the citizens already agreed to stay.
- At a meeting, Dean reports an invisible intruder, and everyone hunts for them.
- A1C [bombers](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md) attack again. Horace points Parrot to the sewers, which he never knew existed, and they're full of shulkers. Parrot realises he needs to check the chunk bans.
- In the end Parrot lights the TNT himself, thinking back on their time leading the civilization.
