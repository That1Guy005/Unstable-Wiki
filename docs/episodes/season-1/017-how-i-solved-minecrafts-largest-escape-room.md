## Summary

Locked together in [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s giant escape room, built by [Wato1876](../../characters/wato1876.md), [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) crawl, craft and launch themselves through room after room of trick puzzles. When Wemmbu gets softlocked, Wato steps in and makes him solve things properly. At the top they craft a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), escape into the End, and Wemmbu refuses to give the mace back until Clown says where [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) is.

## Full breakdown

### Opening rooms

- Room 1: an End portal frame with four eyes and a [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md) in an item frame. Using it fires redstone that dispenses a trapdoor, and crawling under it gets them out. Room 2 is more trapdoor crawling. Zam says she could have done it faster, as Wemmbu remembers her trying to kill him in Proton.
- Room 3: hoppers of items and a drop onto pigs. A snowball knocks down raw iron, which he smelts into a crossbow; tipped arrows of weaving make a pig leave a cobweb to land in.
- Room 4: Zam milks a goat to cure mining fatigue, breaks the floor and an autocrafter, crafts copper into a block, and pink concrete powder is the key to crawling into the next hole.

### Softlocked

- Room 5: Wemmbu builds up to peek into the hoppers outside, and Zam builds out with her autocrafter. They pass items through a dispenser, but Wemmbu loses the concrete powder to mining fatigue and gets softlocked. Wato, the room's creator, appears, shows the intended solution and stasises out.
- Room 6, a fake mesa with powdered snow and an armadillo, is the first where blocks can be broken freely. They need leather boots, learn you can briefly jump on powdered snow, and after many tries Wemmbu makes three consecutive jumps to a hopper. Zam goes up a gold platform first and goes quiet; Wato returns and uses the emergency exit (an armadillo breaking a wall by gold ore), and gives Wemmbu milk to break a lectern.
- Outside the rooms, Wemmbu tries to cheese a water room with Wato's bucket and gets hit with a sword for it, but takes maxed netherite boots, eyes of ender and a maxed netherite sword. A chicken stack plus wind-charging potion launches him into a lava-dispenser room; he cheeses it again, and Wato makes him do it legitimately.

### The mace climb

- Wemmbu builds up with sand on tripwire hooks and redstone torches. Wato, worried, leaves him with a warning to tread carefully.
- Reunited, Zam gives him a heavy core and a Wind Burst book, and Wemmbu crafts and enchants a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). Macing an armour stand launches them from platform to platform.
- They brew oozing potions to turn magma cubes into slimes, craft slime blocks, then mace each other up and pull each other with a fishing rod, until a hole at the top drops them back at the start, where they fill the End portal.

### The End

- Clown congratulates them and wants the mace back; Wemmbu refuses until Clown says where Egg is, and quietly takes Zam's netherite and mace.
- Wato flakes on respawning the dragon. Wemmbu tells Zam to go through the portal when he drops Clown; Zam escapes, and Clown respawns the dragon himself.
- In the fight Wemmbu's armour breaks, so he goes to the mace. Clown says he has just given up one of the last maces on the server and that things are about to go down, and flies off.
- Wemmbu kills the dragon and leaves through the portal.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): crafted from a heavy core with Wind Burst; one of the last on the server.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): the first room's trick, plus wind-charging potions on the chicken launch.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Wato's way out after helping.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu and Zam escape Clown's escape room; Zam makes it out through the portal.
- Wemmbu keeps a mace and a maxed netherite sword and boots, and Clown is now his enemy.
