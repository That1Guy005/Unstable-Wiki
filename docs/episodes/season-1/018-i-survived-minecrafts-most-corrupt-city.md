## Summary

Caught robbing [Reddoons](../../characters/reddoons.md)' vault, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) and [Deanthebean9](../../characters/deanthebean9.md) are forced to mine in the Mining City under [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), with an impossible quota. Parrot befriends the miners, keeps robbing the vaults to buy freedom for others (including [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)), and organises a revolution after Reddoons murders [LuigiToan](../../characters/luigitoan.md), only to learn that [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md) of [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) has been running the whole game.

## Full breakdown

### Capture

- Reddoons and five invisible guards catch them and make them clear their inventories, then check for smuggled items with boats. The way out is a quota of 10,000 diamond blocks.
- When Reddoons threatens to double everyone's quota, Parrot selflessly hands over his spyglass. A boat escape fails and the quota becomes 10,000 per person.
- Wifies gives Parrot a spare spyglass and insists he stays to win the real one back.

### Life in Mining City

- They meet miners Ratrick, Pika and Luigi, and the warden CoinMonke. Luigi teaches Parrot about the place, and Parrot gives him an emerald he mined as thanks.
- Every night Coin collects the day's haul (the best was dizzyu, with 32 diamonds). Parrot starts keeping secrets from Wifies.
- With Luigi covering for them, they explore Capital City, which turns out to be mostly empty, and rob a vault. Dean gets stuck in pistons, and they have to fix the door before Reddoons notices.
- The stolen diamonds start buying people out: Pika is freed, then Lettuce hands in his diamonds, crafts armour, and walks free. Coin is baffled at how many people are leaving.

### Reddoons tightens the screws

- Reddoons finds the library vault broken into and warns Coin that Ash will kill them both. He lines everyone up; Luigi asks for his emerald back, and the quotas double.
- With wool smuggled in (sculk sensors) and TNT minecarts, the trio steals every shulker of diamonds. Reddoons overhears them, takes over from Coin, and reveals they tripped a wire, but inventory checks find nothing, since the shulkers are buried and hidden in decorated pots made from smuggled clay.
- A roulette table appears with a 9-diamond daily maximum. Parrot loses at the end of the week, but Coin can't bring himself to execute him, and tips Parrot off that one vault has three codes (a lever, crafter-slot and button puzzles).

### Revolution

- Coin gives them one last chance before Reddoons locks the vaults. When Reddoons finds out, he kills Luigi himself and fires Coin.
- At Luigi's funeral, Parrot and Wifies give speeches: steal every diamond as leverage, fight the Mafia guards, and free everyone. They break into the tower vault and cycle empty chest minecarts until they fill with diamonds.
- Reddoons catches them, says Mining City has been recaptured, then reveals Parrot actually won the roulette game, and that he freed everyone because that's what he wanted to do.
- He returns Parrot's spyglass, has them remove their armour, and takes them to Ash. Dean, terrified after leaving the Mafia, tries to walk away; Wifies says if they wanted them dead, they'd be dead. Ash notices Dean's fear, invites them into the Mafia, and reveals the roulette table was his idea.
- Parrot takes back Luigi's emerald and tells Wifies he has to get out of Mining City.

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): part of the final heist kit.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Reddoons' Mafia guards.
- Decorated pots: smuggled clay turned into pots to hide shulkers.

## Outcomes

- LuigiToan is killed by Reddoons; CoinMonke is fired.
- Pika and LettuceK are freed from Mining City, and Lettuce never forgets it.
- Parrot gets his spyglass back; Ash offers the trio places in the Mafia.
