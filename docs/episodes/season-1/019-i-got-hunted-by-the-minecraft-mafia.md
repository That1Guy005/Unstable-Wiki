## Summary

Hunted through the Nether by [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) are saved by the mace-wielding [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md), a deserter from the Mafia. Minute explains how [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md) turned it into an army, and Spoke builds a resistance team. Outmatched by trimmed elite hunters, he goes back to exploiting, and learns [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) may be dead.

## Full breakdown

### Rescue and backstory

- Mining through the Nether with the invisible hunters on their heels, Mapicc's armour is nearly gone when Minute arrives and kills them with a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md).
- Minute asks why the Mafia is after them (they don't know), and reveals he used to be a member. The Mafia wasn't always violent; when Ash took over he weaponised the members and forced each to set a stasis, the void leash. Minute fled before setting his and has been running ever since.
- Mapicc wants to march on Mafia City and kill Ash; a trimmed player that attacks gets away.
- [Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md) mark rank, so Ash is sending stronger hunters now. Minute will team up if they get better at survival and PvP. They think about inviting Jepexx, and recruit [PlanetLord](../../characters/planetlord.md) by tricking him into a Curse of Binding turtle shell.

### Elite hunters

- A diamond-trimmed hunter makes Planet glow with a spectral arrow; hundreds of Mafia members storm in, and they dig and fly out short on resources.
- With leather boots Minute gave them, they stand on powdered snow while diamond- and gold-trimmed hunters fall into one of Minute's traps.
- A gold-trim with a pack of wolves ambushes them; caught in a wall, Spoke messages Minute, who regears him. They hope Minute can gather a god team of [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md), [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md).
- Coordinates arrive attached to an ocelot and lead to [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md)'s castle, to meet Planet and Squiddo. Minute expects a trap and says they must spring it and push through. Planet says they need a plan to stop Ash.

### Exploits again

- Against Minute's wishes, Spoke decides to return to [Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md): stacked totems, bedrock, command blocks and duped god apples, starting with the apples. Planet reluctantly agrees, against his morals, and suggests finding Jamato.
- In an old stronghold stash of exploited mobs, one carries coordinates to Jamato's empty second base, where Spoke finds Jamato's will. Jamato is either dead or captured.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Minute's weapon, among the few on the server.
- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): the Mafia's rank system (untrimmed, gold, diamond).
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the Mafia's control over its members.
- [Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md): Spoke's plan to win by breaking the game again.

## Outcomes

- MinuteTech, PlanetLord and Squiddo join Spoke's resistance.
- Spoke returns to exploiting, and Jamato's will suggests he is dead or captured.
