## Summary

Following three invisible players, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) stumbles into the [Skyblock Civilization](../../places/skyblock-civilization.md), a secretive sky city ruled by Jaron that kills outsiders. Forced to become a citizen, he farms with [Pangi](../../characters/pangi.md), loses sumo duels to the enforcer Straight, finds [Wato1876](../../characters/wato1876.md) in hiding, joins a mission to [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), and is handed one of the last maces, until Straight opens the gates to [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md).

## Full breakdown

### Forced citizenship

- While Wemmbu plays skyblock at Spawn, three invisible players fly past; he follows them to the sky city, where Pangi warns him they kill outsiders and takes him to Jaron. Become a citizen or die, and Jaron will always be watching.
- Pangi teaches him the farms while other citizens just watch. Straight, the enforcer, keeps an eye on him; Pangi stirs up trouble by claiming Wemmbu talked trash, and they bet on a sumo duel. If Wemmbu wins he can leave; if he loses he stops complaining. Straight wins.

### Wato

- Wato explains he is hiding from [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) after refusing to respawn the dragon at the end of the escape room. Citizens can normally leave, but it's wartime.
- Wato shows him his secret home, and Wemmbu and Pangi dig a basement.
- Eavesdropping, Wemmbu hears Jaron send Wato to clear the airspace of intruders; he gets himself onto the mission by offering to show them his skyblock island at Spawn.
- They pearl to Spawn with stases set for Wato to pull later. Wemmbu gears up from an ender chest, and three invisible players (one iron-trimmed, "investigating") appear on his island. Forced to give up his items, he smuggles out six ender chests.
- Back in the city, Wato distracts Straight by sending him to destroy the stasis chamber. Wemmbu shows Wato the ender chests, and Wato warns him not to flaunt items that aren't obtainable in the city.

### Inside the castle

- Pangi drags them into building a drowned farm. Wemmbu agrees to join a plan if they help him distract Jaron.
- Caught sneaking into the castle by Straight, Wemmbu claims he's secretly helping Jaron install a new feature.
- Straight notices Wemmbu still isn't a citizen and starts interviewing people about the invisible players; Wato pops up out of the floorboards and spooks him.
- Jaron explains his philosophy: Capital City fell when the poor overthrew the rich, so if everyone has equal wealth, class division disappears.

### Mission to Capital City

- Jaron sends them to spy on the Mafia base in the abandoned End Civilization. Straight trains them on critical hits, and Wemmbu loses another rematch. Naparizel trains too, and [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) leads them via the old outposts.
- In Capital City they collect rare items until a fight at the centre turns as diamond-trims arrive; they regroup in a trial chamber whose ominous vaults have all been destroyed. Wemmbu and Straight train one final time.

### Betrayal

- At the meeting, Jaron appears on his throne and privately gives Wemmbu one of the only remaining [maces](../../mechanics/mace.md) on the server. Wemmbu starts questioning Straight's motives.
- Straight forces Wemmbu out, lights the Nether portal, and lets the invisible players raid the civilization.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Jaron's gift, one of the last on the server.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the scouting mission's way home, and Straight's distraction.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the Mafia's scouts and raiders.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu gets a mace and six smuggled ender chests.
- The Skyblock Civilization falls to the Mafia after Straight's betrayal.
