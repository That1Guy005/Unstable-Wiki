## Summary

On the run after Mining City, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) and [Deanthebean9](../../characters/deanthebean9.md) follow [LuigiToan](../../characters/luigitoan.md)'s compass towards the legendary [Far Lands Civilization](../../places/far-lands-civilization.md) to rally help against [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md). Ambush after ambush wears them down, until Dean confesses he has been leaking their location and runs, and Wifies burns the compass to stop Parrot getting himself killed. Parrot goes on alone, and convinces [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) to fight.

## Full breakdown

### The trail

- Hiding in a remote forest, they plan to reach the Far Lands and recruit fighters. Wifies gives Dean a goat horn to blow if he's in danger; when the Mafia finds them in a lush cave, the horn sounds and they escape.
- At Luigi's house, Parrot finds Luigi's diary and a lodestone compass to the Far Lands City.
- A Nether fortress gives brewing supplies until the Mafia blows it up. Jungle and desert temples give arrows and TNT; a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) trap pops a lone Mafia member, who is instantly stasis-pulled into the void, just as Dean almost was. Parrot asks if he can trust them, and both say yes.
- A woodland mansion gives everyone a [totem](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md); an ambush in an ancient city spawns a warden, and they rescue Dean.

### Mafia territory

- At a giant obsidian border built by the Mafia, they meet R4trick and Finiahn, ride camels across, and see the damage: craters, leafless trees, drained lakes. Parrot says it's to avenge Luigi.
- They blow up a Mafia iron farm and are chased. Parrot's armour breaks under arrows; Wifies gives him his chestplate, which Parrot throws away after Wifies tells him there are no arrows on it.
- A bastion, then a trial-chamber ambush. Wifies baits the Mafia with a second portal. They use horses on the Nether roof and are jumped again.

### Breaking apart

- Parrot overhears Wifies and Dean arguing, and Dean confesses: the Mafia has been finding them because of him, taking advantage of how unskilled he is (possibly getting him to fire arrows as signals). Parrot walks off angry.
- Dean leaves the compass in a chest, blows his horn and disappears; Wifies stops Parrot searching, since Dean is long gone.
- At a village, Wifies asks for the compass and burns it: the real one. He doesn't want to go to the Far Lands because he thinks Parrot will die there. They argue, Parrot throws away his spyglass and leaves, and ignores Wifies' apology messages.

### Alone to the Far Lands

- Parrot reaches the Far Lands but not the city. Finishing Luigi's journal, he tries to throw away the emerald, but his allay keeps bringing it back.
- A player named Qu1nten leads him to Horace. Parrot warns that the Mafia will find the city, and lists what it has done: killed Luigi, turned Dean's friendship into a weapon, and pushed Wifies to burn the compass out of fear. They have to fight back, and Horace agrees.

## Items & mechanics

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the trap that pops a Mafia member.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the Mafia's instant void execution.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): looted from a woodland mansion.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Parrot's escape after his armour breaks.
- Goat horn: the trio's danger signal, used for the last time by Dean.

## Outcomes

- Dean confesses and leaves; Wifies burns the compass, and the friendship cracks.
- Parrot throws away the spyglass.
- Parrot reaches the Far Lands and recruits Horace to fight the Mafia.
