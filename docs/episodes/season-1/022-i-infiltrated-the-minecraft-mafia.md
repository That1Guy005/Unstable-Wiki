## Summary

Following [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md)'s compass, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and [PlanetLord](../../characters/planetlord.md) find [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), team up with the undercover [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), rescue [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), and join the Mafia themselves, Spoke under the name "Fish Bucket". Climbing the ranks costs him more than he expected: he's sent after Parrot and his friends, and ordered to kill an innocent player to prove his loyalty. His final test, against a diamond-trim, exposes him to [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md).

## Full breakdown

### Into the Mafia

- The compass leads to a Mafia castle. Dropping dripstone from the ceiling kills one invisible player and pops another's totem, revealing the missing Leo, who has infiltrated the Mafia to get to [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md). TNT minecarts then level the castle.
- A gold-trimmed member leads them to [Mafia City](../../places/mafia-city.md), where they blend in, tour an invisibility-potion factory, and identify themselves by held items (Spoke's bucket of salmon, Planet's tipped arrow). They trick an untrimmed member into flying to a remote spot, while a diamond-trim watches them from a roof.
- At the city prison, Planet nearly dies flashing a golden chestplate at the guard; the diamond-trim stares the guard down. Prisoners include the Lifesteal Admin, who jumps into lava and is banned. They find Mapicc, Planet gives him his netherite and sneaks out invisible, and the new trio (Spoke, Leo and Mapicc) makes sure Planet gets away.

### Climbing the ranks

- Leo explains the ranks: missions earn upgrade tokens, and every three tokens earn the next [trim](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md) (copper, iron, gold, diamond). Missions are handed out by a gold-trim at a lake.
- Survive a raid in iron gear: done. Next, assassinate [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md). In the chase after Parrot, [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) and [Deanthebean9](../../characters/deanthebean9.md), it turns out Spoke and Mapicc were the hunters chasing Parrot towards the Far Lands. They fail; Ash kills the gold-trim who led them as punishment.
- Guarding a vault, they watch [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md) and [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) break in. A diamond quota is met with Leo's own diamond blocks, and Ash promotes them to copper. Baconnwaffles0, their target, calls them traitors in chat, so they destroy his base; a trade with [Reddoons](../../characters/reddoons.md) follows.
- For elytra, Spoke asks Wemmbu, who gave them all to Zam; they find nine in the old Zam Empire, and Wemmbu pops a tailing Mafia member who turns out to be [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md). Promoted to iron, they win a redstone contest by sabotaging the other plots.

### Loyalty tests

- Their target is Planet. They try to throw the mission and are caught. Ash has Clown kill the iron members one by one until whoever let Planet go confesses; when Mapicc is next, Spoke ("Fish Bucket") steps up.
- Stealing ominous keys from a trial chamber, they see a diamond-trim looting dead Mafia members. Ash promotes the others to gold, but not Spoke, and orders him to kill a stranger, iMajesticRose, to prove himself.
- Mapicc and Leo offer to do it for him, but Spoke insists. Mapicc asks, "Spoke, who are you bro?"
- Spoke spies on Rose playing with her brother BeckyTronVIII, introduces himself as "Jamato", earns their trust, and lures her into a dripstone trap. He tells her he has no choice; she cries that she just wants to play with her brother. He takes her flower for her brother and promises to avenge the server, then drops her. He gives the brother the flower and flies away apologising.

### Ash's trophies, and the final test

- Ash praises Fish Bucket as just as evil as himself, renames the dripstone "your blade", and shows off his trophy collection, including a netherite sword named Terminus and an exploited netherite chestplate like Spoke's. He also shows the civilizations he plans to take over.
- To reach diamond, Spoke must kill an assigned diamond-trim alone at a place of that player's choosing, and his real name would appear in chat. Shaken, he goes home and cleans up griefer damage until Mapicc, Leo and Planet arrive and they plan to kill Ash.
- In the duel, Spoke's totem pops and reveals his name to Ash. Ash tries to void him by stasis, but Spoke never set one, so Ash throws down his useless fishing rod. Ash orders the diamond-trim to kill them, but the diamond-trim attacks Ash instead, and Ash escapes by stasis.
- The diamond-trim is [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md), who tells them to run. Afterwards, Leo hands Spoke Ash's fishing rod: only Unbreaking I all along.

## Items & mechanics

- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): the Mafia's token-based rank ladder.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): mass-produced by the Mafia's potion factory.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Ash's fishing-rod "void" control over members, useless against Spoke, who never set one.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): Spoke's pop reveals his real name.
- Dripstone: Spoke's trap for Rose, renamed "your blade" by Ash.

## Outcomes

- Spoke kills iMajesticRose under orders, his darkest act so far.
- Mapicc is rescued; Lifesteal Admin and the gold-trim leader die; Baconnwaffles0's base is destroyed.
- Spoke's cover is blown to Ash, and MinuteTech turns out to have been the diamond-trim watching them.
