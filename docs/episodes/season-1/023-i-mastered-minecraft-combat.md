## Summary

[Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) asks [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md) to make him a real fighter. Mane trains him on strict terms, holding his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), and puts him through raids on Mafia outposts, restock drills and "fight dirty" lessons. Wemmbu finds the hidden [Ice Mountain Civilization](../../places/ice-mountain-civilization.md), gets [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) back from the Mafia, and pops a diamond-trim, but [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) takes Egg hostage for the mace, and Mane vanishes.

## Full breakdown

### Mane's conditions

- Mane says everyone wants to learn to fight but few will work for it. Wemmbu tries to mace him twice and fails. Realising the Mafia could kill Wemmbu for the mace, Mane agrees to train him, as long as he holds the mace until training is over.
- Wemmbu builds a PvP kit. They grief an untrimmed Mafia outpost, then a gold-trim base in a mesa (Wemmbu kills an iron-trim; Mane kills gold, iron and untrimmed players with the mace) before being chased off by gold-trims and a diamond-trim.
- Over dinners at the treehouse, Mane teaches the ranks and their gear; Wemmbu makes restock boxes, and learns to fight dirty, without honour, starting fights before Mane finishes talking.
- Reasoning that gold-trims must restock with shulkers, Wemmbu camps a shulker farm and comes back with three gold-trim chestplates. Mane warns him off diamond-trims, who "use enchanted golden apples as regular golden apples", and pushes him to develop a fighting style; then maces him to teach him to offhand his totem.

### Allies and an old friend

- Flying aimlessly, Wemmbu finds a hidden civilization inside an ice mountain, run by Arcn and [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md), who throw him out.
- At Spawn he trades with [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md): elytra locations (they find several in the old Zam Empire under a pile of valuable blocks) for the base of a diamond-trim. An invisible tailing player turns out to be Egg, who has joined the Mafia. His time there was boring AFK farm work, and he only knows Mafia City's location by following others.
- Mane is annoyed by a visitor (they seem to know each other), beats Egg without armour, and lets him stay if he builds farms.

### The diamond-trim

- Wemmbu and Egg win the ice civilization's trust to lure in a diamond-trim; Egg dislikes the betrayal. When gold-trims take the citizens hostage, Wemmbu challenges the diamond-trim to a fair 1v1 with no restocks, restocks anyway, and escapes when backup arrives. Egg tells him he can't do this alone.
- Mane returns the mace on hearing Wemmbu popped a diamond-trim, then goes back to the ice civilization himself; when Wemmbu and Egg turn up, the diamond-trim flees, ruining Mane's plan, though he later apologises.
- Wemmbu becomes proficient with the mace and is told to use Wind Burst I instead of III. Spoke, ranting about his Mafia missions, trades him a Wind Burst book in exchange for Wemmbu dropping the request for Mafia City's coordinates.

### Lost cause

- Mane suddenly begs for the mace, and says Wemmbu never listens, which is why he isn't improving. Wemmbu refuses and storms off to a field where Flame is holding Egg hostage: the mace, or Egg dies. He pops Flame's totem, but has to hand over the mace.
- Back at the treehouse there's no sign of Mane, only a sign reading "lost cause". Wemmbu breaks it and tells Egg he doesn't think Mane is coming back.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): held by Mane as collateral, returned, and finally surrendered to Flame for Egg's life.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Wind Burst enchanting for the mace.
- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): what Wemmbu learns to read in a fight.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): offhanding totems, and popping Flame's.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu is far stronger, but loses the mace to Flame to save Egg.
- Egg is out of the Mafia and back with Wemmbu.
- ManePear disappears, leaving a "lost cause" sign.
