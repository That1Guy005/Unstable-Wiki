## Summary

[The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) invades the [Far Lands Civilization](../../places/far-lands-civilization.md). [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) joins its Warriors, meets [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md), forms an alliance with the neglected Farmers (including [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md) and a remorseful [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md)), and challenges the council's leader Purpled for actually fighting the war. The war brings Parrot a council seat and his spyglass back, but also the death of [Deanthebean9](../../characters/deanthebean9.md), arranged by [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md), and the fall of the city.

## Full breakdown

### Joining the Warriors

- After the Mafia slaughters most of the Farmers District, [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) organises the city into Farmers, Warriors and Builders. Parrot joins the Warriors to avenge Luigi.
- At the statue he meets Theo, who regrets joining (they had him gathering resources), doesn't even know what the Mafia is, and is only there to prove he's the best.
- On a mission to raid a Mafia outpost, squad leader Dimofyy dies, and Lezy admits nobody liked him.
- Council leader Purpled mocks Parrot as a "farmer" for how he escaped and opens Dimofyy's seat to whoever pops his totem. Parrot loses; Theo wins with [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and takes the seat.

### The Farmers

- Wifies appears to apologise; Parrot refuses to talk. Ken now leads the Farmers, who feel abandoned by the Warriors and have stopped supplying them. Parrot accuses Wifies of being there to sabotage him, and says the war is unwinnable unless they put their differences aside.
- In a capture-the-flag training game, Parrot teams with the Farmers (plus meta64), hides their banner behind a bone-mealed tree, and they embarrass the Warriors.
- Wifies helps Parrot practise archery with debuff arrows and static targets, and Parrot wins a duel for a council seat. Wifies gives back the spyglass Parrot threw away, as a promise that Parrot won't die, and vows to fight beside him.
- Parrot visits Dean, now living alone, and promises they'll fix everything after the war.

### Council politics

- A raid on Mafia City with Theo's plan to take out a diamond-trim goes wrong; Parrot and Wifies build an ice platform above the colosseum where no one would look.
- At his first council meeting, Parrot calls the missions pointless; Purpled admits the Mafia outnumbers them and the missions are just false hope. Theo backs Parrot.
- With the Farmers, Parrot hunts a gold-trim across the Nether roof (it chorus-fruits away), then poses as a diamond-trim in Mafia City to lure the gold-trim into a trap, which Ken springs. Purpled rewards Parrot with silence-trimmed leggings; Parrot credits the Farmers and calls out the Warriors for abandoning them.
- Theo tells Parrot that Purpled sees him as a threat, and that Parrot is the leader the Warriors need. Purpled tries to have Parrot killed on a mission for "dragging them into war". Naparizel and Lamibee side with Parrot, and Parrot calls a vote on who should lead.

### Dean

- Dean is missing from his shack. On a mission with the Farmers to destroy the Mafia's A1C [bombers](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md), Parrot kills a Mafia member who turns out to be Dean, who had rejoined and was shooting at his fellow members to win back Parrot's trust.
- At Dean's grave, Parrot writes "I trust you". Ash interrupts to mock Dean as a pointless cockroach, and admits he put Dean on that mission so Parrot would kill him. Parrot attacks; Ash escapes by stasis.

### The fall

- The Warriors are kidnapped. Their prison is in the old End Civilization, retextured in Mafia style. Everyone sets stasis chambers before the rescue.
- Parrot tells the Farmers to pull their stases, asks Wifies not to pull his, and lets the diamond-trims take him, unarmed, to Ash. Ash says if he dies, the whole Mafia goes with him.
- Parrot pulls his stasis and looks out over the destroyed Far Lands Civilization.

## Items & mechanics

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Theo's signature style, which wins him a council seat.
- [Bomber Plane](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md): the A1C bombers the Farmers set out to destroy.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): everyone's exit plan from the End prison, and Ash's escape.
- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): Parrot's silence-trimmed leggings, and his diamond-trim disguise.

## Outcomes

- Deanthebean9 is killed by Parrot, set up by Ash.
- Dimofyy dies on a mission; Parrot and Theo join the Warriors Council.
- Parrot and Wifies reconcile and Parrot has his spyglass back, but the Far Lands Civilization is destroyed.
