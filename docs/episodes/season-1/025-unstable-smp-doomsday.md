## Summary

The Season 1 finale from [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s side. With the [Far Lands Civilization](../../places/far-lands-civilization.md) gone, Parrot founds [District 13](../../factions/district-13.md), loses [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) to the Mafia and the resistance to despair, and brings in [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md), [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md). To buy Parrot time, [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) joins [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), and his stasis becomes [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md)'s leverage. In the Battle of Stasis the Mafia's pearls are found, but Wifies is gone.

## Full breakdown

### District 13

- Nobody answered the beacons Parrot lit before the Far Lands fell. Horace builds a bunker near Spawn, District 13, and Parrot recruits [Rekrap2](../../characters/rekrap2.md), [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), Ratrick, Finiahn and others, but Wifies' beacons draw a Mafia attack while they recruit.
- Horace joins the Mafia: they let him build without destruction, he can see his friends, and he's tired of everything he builds for Parrot being destroyed.
- Parrot, Wifies and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) infiltrate [Mafia City](../../places/mafia-city.md) to kill Ash, who teleports away just as Parrot aims; [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) fights them with mace breach-swaps while Theo carts him. On the way out, Parrot kills Yusai, mistaking him for a Mafia member.

### Spoke's confession

- At [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md), the Mafia is building an obsidian box. Spoke and Mapicc catch up and confess: Spoke infiltrated the Mafia and was the one sent to kill Parrot, Wifies and Dean in the trial chamber. He was also sent to kill iMajesticRose.
- Following a diamond-trim from Rose's ruined house, they meet Quackenstein123, who lies about knowing the Mafia; there's a grave for Rose in his town. Wifies suspects Quackenstein is the diamond-trim.
- Spoke finds a book signed by Ash, "Mafia Eyes", a possible lead to the stasis chambers, so Parrot brings him and Mapicc into District 13, and they celebrate. Then Spoke produces "The Gateway", a book of nothing but < and > signs. Nobody understands it; Spoke and Mapicc are kicked out for spreading false hope, and everyone but Wifies leaves District 13, some joining the Mafia.

### Wemmbu, and the snow house

- At ManePear's burned treehouse, Parrot recruits Wemmbu, who only joins to kill Clown, and promises [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) he'll come back for him.
- Disguised as each rank in turn, Parrot and Wifies reach Ash's castle; Ash notices the "diamond players" aren't holding maces, and they flee.
- A million blocks away they build a snow house with a dog and traps. Ash leaves a sign: "found you". Wifies tells Parrot to keep lighting beacons, since they haven't lost.
- Ash returns, hands them invisibility potions, and orders them into the Mafia within ten minutes. Wifies joins to buy Parrot time and gather information, and Ash sends a diamond-trim squadron after Parrot.

### The spawn prison

- With Spoke, Parrot plans to use the Mafia's spawn prison to find the stasis chambers. It has grown far beyond a box. Wemmbu and Egg distract guards from above while Parrot, Spoke and Mapicc enter through the End.
- [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md) fixes the entrance and [Wato1876](../../characters/wato1876.md) shows where new players' pearls are thrown. In sandstone halls they watch a diamond-trim moving pearls, are chased, and split up; Parrot spares someone he can't bring himself to kill.
- At the snow house, Wifies doesn't respond. Ash taunts Parrot; Parrot calls using Wifies' stasis as leverage pathetic, then barely traps a diamond-trim with the mines he and Wifies laid.

### The Battle of Stasis

- Parrot tells District 13 that Ash has killed Wifies. Spoke reveals the < > book was a map to the stasis chamber; Parrot tells him to mine out the soul sand under the pearls. A Mafia ambush is wiped out, diamond-trim included.
- Parrot goes to the Far Lands one last time and lights beacons all over the server; Mafia members, even the pearl guard, start drinking milk and dropping their [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md).
- Remembering their talk about beacons, Parrot thanks Wifies for being his friend, and Wifies fades away.
- Spoke calls the mass milk-drinking beautiful, but says Ash is alive, and where he lives. In the Far Lands, Parrot tells Ash he forgives him for everyone he killed, including Wifies, and leaves refusing to believe what Ash tells him.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the Mafia's lifeline, and Ash's leverage over Wifies.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the Mafia's identity; drinking milk marks its end.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Clown's breach-swapping; diamond-trims are expected to carry maces.
- Beacons: Parrot's call to arms, and his final tribute.

## Outcomes

- Wifies dies, apparently, at Ash's hand (later shown to be faked).
- Horace and many resistance members defect to the Mafia; Yusai is killed by mistake.
- The stasis chambers are found and the Mafia's members unmask; Ash survives.
