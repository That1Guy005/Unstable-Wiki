## Summary

[ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) and [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md) burn down ManePear's treehouse, and [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) vows to kill Clown. He joins [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s [District 13](../../factions/district-13.md) in exchange for a shot at a diamond-trim's mace, wins a mace back from Ferre with [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md)'s "order 36", and acts as the distraction for the Season 1 finale: first at the spawn prison, then by challenging Clown to a fair duel.

## Full breakdown

### Fire

- Clown and Ferre set the treehouse alight and drain Wemmbu and Egg; Egg escapes first and digs an escape tunnel for Wemmbu, and they hide in the Nether for 24 hours. Wemmbu vows to kill Clown.
- Parrot appears (a flashback shows Wemmbu turning him down earlier): Wemmbu wants Clown dead and Parrot wants the Mafia gone. Wemmbu agrees to distract Clown, if he gets to fight a diamond-trim for a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) first.

### The beach trap

- Wemmbu, Egg, Parrot and Wifies set a fall trap at a beach near Spawn; Wifies' beacon calls in waves of Mafia (untrimmed, then iron, then a diamond-trim with escorts). Isolated, the diamond-trim is trapped and Parrot gets the kill.
- They argue over who earned the mace; Wemmbu says he has no more reason to help, and Parrot gives him Clown's location anyway.
- At Clown's castle, Wemmbu nearly blows his cover by breaking a sand block, ambushes Clown and Ferre, and flees when his armour breaks and restocks run out.

### Order 36

- District 13 is empty. Fishing at the treehouse ruins, Egg suddenly teleports away; Ferre whispers that he holds Egg hostage in a nearby jungle temple, and will free him if Wemmbu dies.
- Egg says "execute order 36", their code for a trap: dozens of [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) kill Ferre's two gold-trims. They break all of Ferre's armour; he stashes the mace in his ender chest and tries to flee, but caught one last time, hands it over to be spared.
- Egg's real plan had been to stasis back and pose as a Mafia member at a beacon, and he urges Wemmbu to rejoin Parrot, since working with others makes him stronger.

### Team Unstable

- At District 13, Spoke and Mapicc are waiting; Parrot arrives and blames Spoke for the resistance leaving. Wemmbu and Egg build a house inside while they argue. Spoke plans to end the Mafia without killing Ash.
- Wemmbu suggests a team name, and Spoke offers "Team Unstable". On top of the spawn prison, Wemmbu kills wave after wave of trimmed players with his mace, leaving one iron-trim alive to call for backup, then jumps into the hole when the third wave overwhelms them.
- Inside, he makes up with [Wato1876](../../characters/wato1876.md) (not really a Mafia member) for ditching him in the sky city, meets [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md), and follows the pearls by throwing one and diving after it. He and Parrot get out.

### The duel

- Parrot returns heartbroken: Wifies is dead. Spoke explains he has found where the pearls are and how to destroy them, and Wemmbu's job is to keep Clown busy. A Mafia raid hits the base, and Egg kills a diamond-trim.
- Realising another ambush on Clown would go the same way, Wemmbu, at Egg's suggestion, asks for a fair 1v1. Clown and Ferre argue, Ferre flies off, and the duel begins, until Ferre returns and everyone stasises out.
- Tired of waiting for Spoke and Mapicc, Wemmbu and Egg leave to build a base of their own.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): won from a diamond-trim, and won back from Ferre.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): "order 36", Egg's TNT-cart trap.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Egg's teleport, and everyone's escape from the duel.

## Outcomes

- ManePear's treehouse is destroyed; two gold-trims die to order 36.
- Wemmbu ends the Mafia era with a mace and a grudge against Clown, and his duel is left unfinished.
