## Summary

The end of [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), from [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s side. Spoke and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) race to warn the civilizations on [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md)'s "desire board", learn the truth about Rose from Quackenstein123, decode "The Gateway" map, and reach the Mafia's stasis chambers in the void. Spoke mines out the pearls that keep the Mafia alive, then spares Ash.

## Full breakdown

### Warnings nobody heeds

- In Square City, its leader Cash laughs off their warning; moments later Nico is killed by an iron-trim, whose stasis is pulled when he goes for Cash. Mapicc's coordinates get them out through a cave.
- [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md) explains Ash's desire board and gives them Sleepy Nation's coordinates, with [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) trailing them; Baconnwaffles0 blows up her castle, and they escape through her void route.
- At a ruined [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md) they meet [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md), and follow a diamond-trim from Rose's house to Quackenstein's town, which Quackenstein tells them to hide by building a mountain over it.

### Quackenstein's story

- Taking obsidian from the End pillars, they're spotted by Ash, who tells Spoke everything he loves has been destroyed by the Mafia.
- Asked to put his mace down, Spoke listens to Quackenstein: he and Rose left the server's early troubles far behind and built castles together, until Ash recruited them with promises of protection and endless resources in exchange for building. They say goodbye to BeckyTronVIII and leave with a lead.

### The Gateway

- At District 13, Parrot sends Spoke to destroy the stasis chamber. Near a giant crater, Mapicc finds a chest, and realises how to read the < > book.
- Chased by the Mafia, they follow its directions to a dead end, mine through, find a tunnel, and throw pearls into a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) that lands them before a huge artificial mountain: the one built over Quackenstein's town, hiding the stasis chamber.

### Breaking the pearls

- Spoke flies into the void breaking pearls until he runs out of fireworks, and Mapicc pulls his stasis to save him. Mapicc gives him a shovel and more fireworks, and Spoke mines out a whole section.
- Diamond-trims notice pearls missing and drop into the hole after him. Mapicc, chased, finds Spoke didn't throw his own pearl this time, so Spoke has to destroy every pearl or die trying.
- With the Mafia's lifeline gone, Spoke confronts Ash and lets him live, telling him to go far away from everyone. Ash says that when Spoke ends up like him, he'll be there.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the Mafia's mass stasis system, destroyed by Spoke.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): the hidden route to the chambers.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): flying in the void, and running out of fireworks.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Spoke puts his down to hear Quackenstein's story.

## Outcomes

- The Invisible Mafia falls; its stasis pearls are destroyed.
- Nico is killed; Squiddo's castle is destroyed.
- Ash is spared and leaves, with a warning about Spoke's future.
