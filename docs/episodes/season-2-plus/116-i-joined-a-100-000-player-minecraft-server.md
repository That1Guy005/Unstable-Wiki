## Summary

After the war, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) just wants to build his house, but [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md)'s followers and [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md)'s raiders keep interrupting. Defending [The Wheat Kingdom](../../places/the-wheat-kingdom.md), he agrees to recover its lost guardian's mace from a hidden tomb ([Mysterious Tomb](../../places/mysterious-tomb.md)). A 4v4 tournament wins him the directions, and he and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) fight through the traps, only for an invisible netherite player to grab the mace first.

## Full breakdown

### Uninvited guests

- Three of Arachn1d's subjects arrive declaring Arachn1d the true king and demand Wemmbu join them in the caves. He refuses and stab-shots all three.
- Out of froglights, he goes to a village shop that trades building blocks for gear salvaged from the war. Yungy's faction threatens to destroy the village unless it pays up; Wemmbu pulls out his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and they leave.
- The village leader, FieldsOfElysium, explains its guardian quit long ago, leaving a mace in a hidden tomb on a hard, dangerous route. Only LordSirWesley the Third knows where, and he's often at festivals near Merchant City, including one happening now. Wemmbu agrees, but may keep the mace: they're far too valuable to give away.

### The 4v4

- At the festival, Wesley, in netherite, will only reveal the tomb if they win a 4v4. Wemmbu offers a 1v4; Wesley insists on an honourable fight.
- Egg is too busy studying, until Wemmbu gives him a stab-shot fishing rod. Egg recruits [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) and Wemmbu picks up a random player.
- Rules: no elytra, no griefing, no excessive attacks. Egg calls a timeout, rattled by [TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md) on the other team. Their random fourth runs out of the arena; with a minute to replace him, an invisible player in full netherite drops from the crowd and offers to join.
- Popping the team leader's last totem disqualifies the whole team, so they focus Wesley. Jaden eliminates Yungy, and Wemmbu breaks Wesley's helmet and pops him.
- A man of his word, Wesley says: take the path at the wheat village, and when you see a blue cornflower, you'll find the mace. The invisible netherite player overhears. Wemmbu wishes Jaden luck and leaves with Egg.

### The path

- Egg has him test his orbitals in the wheat field, and he blows up part of it. The path behind the castle is a clearing through the trees.
- In a house, Egg loots a netherite chestplate and a book saying the server's violence becomes stale and repetitive. Invisible diamond players with Arachn1d's kingdom shields creep up; hearing footsteps, Wemmbu orbitals the whole house.
- Egg notes the book's warning that endless destruction leads to boredom, and Wemmbu once faked his death out of boredom.

### The tomb

- Past a bridge, breaking the blue cornflower opens a door in the mountain. Zombies in Protection IV diamond attack, and Wemmbu makes Egg handle them.
- Egg warns a second bridge may be trapped; Wemmbu walks onto it, triggers an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) that pops his totem, and a warden spawns beside him. Maces don't do it; stab shots do.
- Past another bridge, a sword build holds the mace in an item frame, and the invisible player from the tournament pearls in and grabs it, thanking Wemmbu for setting off the traps and saving their durability.
- The player uses full invisibility. Wemmbu plans to lure them in front of the arrow cannon, but Egg already broke it. Plan B: a stab shot launches Wemmbu upward and he maces down; the player drinks Turtle Master mid-fall and survives, though all their armour breaks. They put on another set, then tell Wemmbu to stop: there are other places to get a mace. They drop it and leave.

### Home

- Fields thanks them and offers free horses and building blocks. Leaving, Wemmbu apologises for blowing up the wheat field, and they ride into the sunset.
- After the credits, Wemmbu maces his own horse to test whether he gains height, and the horse burns to death.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): the wheat village guardian's lost mace.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): stab shots on Arachn1d's men, the house, the wardens, and the launch.
- [Fishing Rod Trigger](../../mechanics/fishing-rod-trigger.md): the stab-shot rod Wemmbu gives Egg.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the tomb bridge trap.
- Blue cornflower: the key to the tomb door.
- Turtle Master: the invisible player's survival trick.

## Outcomes

- The guardian's mace is returned to FieldsOfElysium.
- Wemmbu's team wins the 4v4; Yungy is eliminated.
- Arachn1d's kingdom is now openly hostile to Wemmbu.
