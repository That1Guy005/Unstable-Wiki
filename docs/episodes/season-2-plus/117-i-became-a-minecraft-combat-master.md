## Summary

Now a combat master, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) sets out to become a grandmaster, a fighter with no weaknesses. Rebuilding his base with [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md), helped by LAW's new, friendlier leadership and the irritating [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md) and Willsion, he fights off a challenger and bandits, and builds traps and an orbital dog cannon. [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md) arrives to warn him that his kingdom is coming.

## Full breakdown

### A new goal

- Flame has mastered every weapon; the next step is grandmaster. He and Lomedy return to his old base ([FlameFrags' Base](../../places/flamefrags-base.md)); the outside disgusts Lomedy, though inside only the LAW walls have changed, until an invisible netherite player pulls a lever and blows it all up.
- HernandoDomin3_ heard Flame is chasing grandmaster and wants a fight. He hesitates so much that Flame simply combos him; after four pops, he runs.

### Neighbours

- A LAW commander delivers a weekly newspaper. One story: more players than usual seen with a certain shield pattern. Under the new leadership Flame isn't wanted, the commander says, and since they're neighbours, he should ask if he needs anything.
- Remembering LAW built a base in 24 hours, Flame asks for lawmen to rebuild. They'll build, but not appliances, so Flame and Lomedy go to [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md) for potion ingredients, waiting at the portal for the Nether shipment. Effect arrows hit them: Spongs and Willsion, "hiding" behind a tree they then break. Flame overpays for Nether items. Back home, the lawmen have built a huge base.

### Spongs and Willsion

- The pair offer to help build traps, mess around the whole time, and send Flame for redstone. Spongs tags along to ragebait him and walks into a group of players who demand their items. Spongs hands over random junk, Flame refuses and walks off, and the group attacks Spongs. Flame tells him to drink Turtle Master and wipes them out with crossbow [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md).
- At [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md), Flame trades diamond sets and a netherite sword to [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) for two redstone kits. Spongs vanishes.
- Willsion and Lomedy show off a fall trap at the front, and Lomedy builds an XP farm for mending. Then Spongs reappears, chased by more players. Carts would wreck the base, so Flame borrows Lomedy's [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and kills them.

### Capital City

- For wolves they need cows to breed them. In [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), a player says Flame isn't welcome. Remembering the last time, when he knocked a player off the highway, Flame spares him and digs through the wall instead. It doesn't matter what he says; he has to prove he's good.
- They take some wandering cows. Lomedy mentions Willsion has taught him a lot about trapping. Flame won't go there himself, but sees value in combining PvP with traps when hopelessly outnumbered.
- A lawman warns them a wanted criminal nearby is after Flame. Flame thanks him, for that and the base. Lomedy breeds the cows while Flame builds his [orbital dog cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) and tells Lomedy to hide.

### Arachn1d

- Arachn1d stands on the roof. Killing his players was the worst mistake of Flame's life; Flame's base will soon be his territory, and nothing will stop him, not LAW, not a king, no one. Flame should keep looking over his shoulder.
- Spider-shield players arrive to avenge their dead. Flame's wolves kill half; a second rod from a shulker sends another wave, and only one survivor runs.
- Lomedy asks if they'll be attacked often, and if Flame will turn evil again. Flame says he won't kill without reason; he's only targeted because of his reputation. Lomedy wants to get better at trapping and Flame at combat, so they'll survive with traps and wolves, and in quiet times Flame will look for more trainers.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): Flame's orbital dog cannon, two waves against Arachn1d's men.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): crossbow carts against the bandits.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lomedy's, borrowed to protect the base.
- Fall trap and XP farm: the base's new defences.
- LAW newspaper: the first sign of the spider shield pattern spreading.

## Outcomes

- Flame's base is rebuilt with LAW's help.
- Arachn1d's soldiers are killed, and Arachn1d declares Flame an enemy.
- Flame begins working towards combat grandmaster.
