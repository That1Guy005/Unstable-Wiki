## Summary

Booed at his coronation, King [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) promises to make [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md) safe within 100 Minecraft days or resign. With [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md), [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) and [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) he clears Spawn's withers, has Spawn rebuilt, makes Fymada mayor of [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), and wins an auctioned chicken to feed the server from [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md). He succeeds, but the old players still hate him, and Lettuce hints Parrot doesn't know the whole story.

## Full breakdown

### The promise

- Only one in ten players makes it out of Spawn alive, and even they don't last. For the first time, the server has voted for a king.
- At the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md), Parrot promises power won't fall into the wrong hands again and that everyone can live in peace. The crowd boos and chants for Lettuce. So he vows: if players can't safely spawn within 100 Minecraft days (33 real hours), he'll resign. Lettuce never managed it.
- At his council, Theo calls it crazy and appoints himself royal guard; Fymada and Horace have their own plans. Decree one: take back Spawn.

### Spawn

- Parrot and Theo climb Spawn killing withers, drawing their attention away from new players while handing out protection and food. With their gear drained, Fymada and Horace arrive to finish the withers below and help them regear.
- By day five, no one dies to withers, and new players thank Parrot. They still die from fall damage, so he has Horace rebuild Spawn entirely.

### Capital City

- Mayor SirPig_ demands their taxes or he'll have them escorted out. Parrot points out he's king and makes Fymada mayor. She announces an expansion and lower taxes so players keep what they earn.
- At their old potion shop, DragonMC gives them horses on behalf of the city: the first horse Parrot picks will be his for his whole reign. He picks a lone white horse, tames it first try, and it's fast with a huge jump. Theo tames a donkey, which Parrot makes him keep: Dingus.

### Food

- Lezy gives them free train tickets to Redstone Town, since the king doesn't pay; Parrot, believing all players are equal, pays anyway. They ride the railway for days.
- At a village tavern, the room goes silent. Theo pays a few diamonds for "twenty steaks" and gets two; the owners say Parrot is ruining the server and nobody likes him, and the patrons boo them out.
- [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) won't help build an automatic chicken farm because his town would be upset, so Parrot and Theo spend days trying alone while 4C watches. He feels bad, but can't give them a chicken: there's one up for auction at [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md).

### The auction

- A chicken averages 1,000 diamonds; they have 400. At an archery game, Parrot keeps hitting the ten-diamond minecart at the back; the owner moves him further and further back, finally onto a house 50 blocks away, and he still hits it. The owner, now in debt, refuses to sell any more shots. They have over 900 diamonds.
- Theo bids on a LAW-owned spear and an enchanted golden apple until Parrot stops him. The player beside Theo bid over 800 on a book. On the chicken, Parrot bids everything they have and pleads that his kingdom will fall without it. The rival outbids him, and Theo bids over a hundred thousand diamonds, thinking it's only a hundred more, and wins. Parrot slips away with the chicken, promising payment later.

### The convoy

- Back at Redstone Town, 4CVIT and the residents have built farms producing more food than needed, and they fill them with bred chickens.
- On a hill, Theo asks if it bothers Parrot that people still hate him and want Lettuce. A little, Parrot admits, but he isn't doing this to be liked, only to do what's right.
- Moving the food by rail would take ages. Theo scouts ahead and kills attacking lawmen; then [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md) stops Parrot, determined to keep him from being king. Parrot warns that destroying the food will kill players; Sarge says if Parrot stays king, there won't be a server left. The minecart chests are empty: 4CVIT is carrying the chicken by [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), and the carts were a decoy. Parrot slips away.

### Day 100

- On day 94, Spawn has been fully rebuilt, with a food giver and a new spawn point. But on the obsidian highway every player he stops says he ruined the server.
- In the Northern Council prison, Parrot asks Lettuce why only new players support him, and whether it has to do with Lettuce saying the server couldn't be repaired. "Who knows?" Parrot calls him pathetic: he did in 30 hours what Lettuce never could. Lettuce says he could have saved everyone if he'd wanted to, and asks why Parrot thinks he didn't. Sometimes you have to do whatever it takes to protect people, and Parrot will find out soon enough. He's surprised Parrot has lasted this long.
- On day 100 the player base has exploded, and the kingdom with it. On the castle roof, Parrot wonders why anyone would spend so many resources putting withers at Spawn, trollers or griefers, but decides it doesn't matter: building the kingdom does.

## Items & mechanics

- Withers: Spawn's killers, cleared by Parrot's team.
- The auction chicken: the basis of Redstone Town's food farms.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): 4CVIT's real food transport; the minecarts were a decoy.
- The white horse: Parrot's royal horse; Dingus, Theo's donkey.
- Archery game: Parrot's 50-block minecart shot.

## Outcomes

- Spawn is cleared and rebuilt, and Parrot keeps his 100-day promise.
- Fymada becomes mayor of Capital City; SirPig_ is removed.
- The old players still resent Parrot, and Lettuce hints at something behind Spawn's withers.
