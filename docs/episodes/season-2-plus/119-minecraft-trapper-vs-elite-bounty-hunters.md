## Summary

Skinless hunters with jumbled names who don't fear death attack [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md). Searching for [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), missing for four weeks, Spoke runs to [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md)'s safehouse and learns the hunters belong to [NULL](../../factions/null.md), who drain targets with bounties. The bounty-setter, SirPig_, escapes, but Spoke and Jumper kill the hunters' leader with a triple-layered arrow railgun and find a book: "mission: null".

## Full breakdown

### The hunters

- Spoke's alarm sounds; expecting bandits, he waits for the traps. Instead, skinless players with jumbled names dodge the first trap and attack him. He lures them to the centre of the base, drops them onto dripstone with a piston trap, and wind-charges a lever that pulls a stasis to bring Jumper and her spies. No death messages appeared.
- Someone hired them, and they don't fear death. Jumper has seen them, but her spies found no leads. She offers coordinates to a safehouse, which Spoke refuses unless she helps find Mapicc.

### Mapicc

- Mapicc hasn't been seen in four weeks, and Spoke has hired Jumper to help. The strange part: the hunters carry the same shield pattern as the shield Mapicc gave him before the battle with LAW.
- Near Spawn, he kills the resident of a secret mountain base, then realises the death message is broadcast nearby. He rigs a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) trap over the entrance; it pops several hunters, who vanish immediately.
- The hunters have speed; Spoke uses pearls and his [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) to gain distance, restocks in someone's house, where the hunters kill the owner, gets spotted leaving invisibly, and outruns them on blue ice.
- He gives in and heads for the safehouse, towering up and using an elytra, which he dislikes, but it's an emergency. 200 blocks out, he hides in a mountain with toggle sneak for a bathroom break and lets them pass.

### The safehouse

- The house seems abandoned until Jumper's spies appear and fight the hunters. Jumper leads him off: it was just a house. Their leader, _1_88_____x, chases them to an escape tunnel, at the end of which is the real safehouse.
- Jumper wants him to stay put. Spoke says the hunters are unkillable, but he has a trap that kills through a totem, already built.
- It's the arrow railgun they built before: three layers of arrows stacked on a block, fired by a TNT railgun, so the game fires them milliseconds apart ([Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md)).
- Jumper's spies followed a hunter: her missing trader's base showed signs of NULL. The hunters walked thousands of blocks, stopped, stared, then lined up behind an unarmoured player with a bounty on his head; an hour later, the bounty was claimed. NULL drains its targets of resources first. Spoke decides stopping NULL matters more than finding Mapicc.

### SirPig_

- All their targets have bounties, including Spoke. Jumper kidnapped the man placing them in Merchant City, and he'll only talk to Spoke. Under a hidden ladder and an obsidian tunnel guarded by spies is an obsidian box holding SirPig_, former mayor of Capital City.
- It's personal, he says. He claims to know nothing about the hunters, refuses a bribe, and tells Spoke to worry about himself. His only hint: the hunters want a token proving Spoke's death, the chestplate he was gifted. Threatened, he pulls a fishing rod and stasises out.

### The railgun

- Dozens of hunters storm the box. Jumper gets Spoke to the surface, but the hunters, especially their leader, keep up through wind charges and pearls, and firework rockets shred their armour.
- Spoke sends Jumper ahead, then, invisible on a hill, grabs a potion from his ender chest, splashes them both, and they hide in a tree. Jumper's spies emerge as a distraction. Down to his chestplate and a turtle helmet, Spoke kites the leader at the house while Jumper readies the trap.
- Spoke wind-charges into the air as Jumper fires, killing the leader instantly. Among the scattered items is a book titled "mission: null".

## Items & mechanics

- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the triple-layered arrow railgun that kills through totems.
- Piston and dripstone trap: Spoke's base defence.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the trap over the mountain base entrance.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the lever-pulled stasis that summons Jumper, and SirPig_'s escape.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) and blue ice: Spoke's speed tricks.
- Shield pattern: the hunters carry Mapicc's design.

## Outcomes

- NULL's hunters are revealed as a bounty-hunting force; their leader, _1_88_____x, is killed.
- SirPig_ is exposed as the bounty-setter, and escapes.
- Spoke finds "mission: null", his first lead on Mapicc's fate.
