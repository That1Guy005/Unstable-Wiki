## Summary

[LAW](../../factions/law.md) comes to reclaim the mega base it built [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md), so Flame works off the debt as a LAW elite for 100 days, leading a squad with [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) and [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md). Ghast deliveries, a hostage rescue, recruiting drives and an escort mission put him up against [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md), who wants Flame to train his kingdom's army. Flame keeps his base, and visits the imprisoned Lettuce.

## Full breakdown

### The debt

- LAW says too many resources went into Flame's base without permission, and starts dismantling it. At LAW HQ, the commander says Flame owes 100 days for the free mega base; netherite won't do, because Flame's skills are worth more. He's hired as an elite with his own squad.
- Lomedy joins first, then two easy recruits; everyone else ignores him, until Spongs runs up, admits he's poor too, and joins. The commander gives Flame a horn for issuing orders.

### Deliveries and a rescue

- First job: take two happy ghasts to [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md). On day 9, Arachn1d's players follow on a ghast. Lomedy switches ghasts and shoots at them. Squad member Gunter_____ rides ahead into more of Arachn1d's minions and jumps down; Spongs would leave him, but an elite who loses a squad member gets fired, and Flame loses his base. Flame borrows Lomedy's [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and kills them all, and tells Gunter to come when the horn blows.
- Next: rescue scouts from a LAW compound at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md), eight days back the way they came. Their invisibility cover is blown at once. An elite Arachn1d guard is interrogating a scout; Gunter and JakeVC42_ die, and the rest retreat to the castle roof, where the attackers won't follow.
- A day later, Flame places [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) for Spongs and Lomedy to shoot and detonate, and it works, but the guard threatens the hostage. Days later, Flame drops down for a bow duel, blocks line of sight, distracts the guard with a spectral arrow, goes invisible, shovels under him and carts him instantly. The scout is saved.
- Losing squad members sets them back; their next task is simply recruiting.

### Recruiting

- On day 55, players won't trust someone who has killed over a thousand players twice. Spongs suggests a voice changer and invisibility, which works until it runs out unnoticed and Flame's identity scares them off.
- Day 64: a player stuck on a reverse landmine. Swapping Lomedy onto the plate doesn't help; Spongs's plan, fishing-rodding the player into the air, sets off the TNT and the player dies on landing. Flame admits dropping an item on the wooden plate would have saved them.
- Two players argue about who found their island first; their render distance only shows twenty blocks, so Flame leads them to the real mainland, which settles it. On day 78 they build graves for their fallen.

### The escort

- After wall duty from day 84, the commander brings the hardest job: escort a player home to the Wheat Civilization ([The Wheat Kingdom](../../places/the-wheat-kingdom.md)), attacked by Arachn1d at LAW HQ. LAW doesn't usually escort players, but has a deal with the civilization's leader. A lawman sacrifices himself for others in the name of justice.
- Arachn1d's group surrounds the area and comes from behind. Arachn1d says Flame shouldn't have killed his soldiers. Flame draws them off Spongs while Lomedy keeps escorting.
- In a cave, Flame digs a hole, Arachn1d orders his soldiers down after him, and Flame pearls up and drops a cart on them, then finishes the rest.
- Why is he hunted? Parrot beat Arachn1d in the election and robbed him of his chance, so Arachn1d is building a kingdom and plans to kill Parrot. People like Flame threaten it, so he must join or die. And he wants Flame to teach him to fight, in exchange for more power than anyone. Flame doesn't care who takes over the server; he only wants to be a grandmaster.
- At the Wheat Civilization, Spongs says the player died; Lomedy says he's joking, and the player goes home.

### Lettuce

- Flame hands the commander his progress journal. With his debt nearly at day 100, the commander has one more task: visit Lettuce, since he doesn't trust lawmen to see him without freeing him.
- Lettuce doesn't want him there. Flame says he's just checking Lettuce is alive. Asked what's happening outside, Flame explains he's changed and wants to be a grandmaster, and that LAW sent him. Lettuce chooses to believe it, and asks Flame to look after LAW for him.
- On day 100, Flame walks out of the Northern Council with his mega base secured.

## Items & mechanics

- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): LAW's delivery job, and Arachn1d's pursuit.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): shot by squadmates on the roof, dug under the guard, and dropped on Arachn1d's soldiers.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lomedy's, borrowed to save Gunter.
- Horn: Flame's elite signal.
- Reverse landmine: the TNT pressure plate the squad fails to defuse.

## Outcomes

- Flame works off his debt and keeps his base.
- Gunter_____, JakeVC42_ and the landmine victim die; a LAW scout and the Wheat Civilization player are saved.
- Arachn1d reveals his plan to build a kingdom and kill Parrot.
