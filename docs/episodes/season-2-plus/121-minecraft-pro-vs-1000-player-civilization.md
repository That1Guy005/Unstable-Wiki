## Summary

A thousand-player army from [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md)'s alliance, prepared with Turtle Master against orbitals, takes [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) hostage. [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) trails them to the Sculk Kingdom around an ancient city, beats its leader driftop99, and breaks Egg out. With gear Egg got from Linnwyrm, Wemmbu wipes out [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md)'s army. Then the Wraith kills two survivors and steals Egg's journal.

## Full breakdown

### Egg surrenders

- A huge army arrives at their base. Wemmbu lures it onto flat ground for an orbital, but they splash Turtle Master and the strike does nothing. Egg sees only one way out and surrenders.
- Without the gear to fight through, Wemmbu follows at a distance, killing two who spot him; their Turtle Master potions prove the army was built to counter him.
- The army forms a circle around Arachn1d and driftop99, leader of the Sculk Kingdom. Egg is captured, Wemmbu will be next, and if he attacks they'll send a messenger. The groups split and Wemmbu follows the one he thinks has Egg.

### driftop99

- At a castle leading underground, the guards open fire. Driftop says they kidnapped Egg because they felt like it, obviously a lie. Wemmbu taunts him for needing an army to capture one player while wearing Protection II diamond, and driftop agrees to a 1v1.
- Stab shots drain driftop's armour, but he mends it on the sculk under his feet. Wemmbu drinks Turtle Master, gets in his face and stab-shots at point-blank range, then maces him once he's low, and grabs the armour.
- The guards jump down and the army inside triggers a trap that launches wardens at him. He kills the guards, retreats from the wardens, and mends with driftop's dropped sculk. His stab-shot launch to the castle fails, so he walks.

### The ancient city

- The kingdom is built around an ancient city. Wemmbu overhears Yungy telling a soldier not to ask why he wants Wemmbu, just stick to the deal. He sneaks past fishing soldiers into the big castle.
- From Egg's view: two interrogators only want information on the Abandoned Civilization, since Egg is the server's lore holder. They take his journal and cage him. He breaks out, but is stuck on a platform surrounded by lava.
- Egg finally notices Wemmbu and pearls over. Wemmbu stab-shots a spot he knows leads to the surface and asks Egg to throw a pearl for him to launch, but forgets to pull it. Alerted, the army attacks; Wemmbu kills two guards and gives Egg their armour, then stab-shots Egg into the air and into a river.

### Linnwyrm's gift

- Egg returns with a handoff, but too many players are nearby. Wemmbu digs to Egg in the walls, where Egg places a shulker: full netherite, golden apples, XP, breeze rods and a netherite [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md), from Linnwyrm, the great sage of the sea. Egg had it all along, saving it for emergencies.
- Wemmbu kills soldiers with ease. Egg warns the thousand-player army is coming; Wemmbu digs in, pots up, and waits.

### The thousand

- Hearing Yungy, Wemmbu targets him and fires a stab shot that kills hundreds in seconds. Yungy orders them to break his armour, but with all the sculk on the battlefield, Wemmbu keeps repairing to full. Yungy retreats.
- Wemmbu spares a few who stay back peacefully. Egg is back in a cage with his armour off; Wemmbu frees him. The loot might not be worth selling, and Egg has no space, so they leave.

### The Wraith

- Survivors BaneC0untry and marine2021 talk about the evacuated civilization. The Wraith carts marine, kills the begging Bane, and takes Egg's journal from Bane's inventory.

## Items & mechanics

- Turtle Master: the army's counter to orbital strikes, and Wemmbu's point-blank trick.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): stab shots at close range, and the one that kills hundreds.
- Sculk: driftop's, and Wemmbu's, endless mending source.
- [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md) and Linnwyrm's shulker: Egg's emergency gear.
- Egg's journal: the lore of the Abandoned Civilization, stolen by the Wraith.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the Wraith's weapon.

## Outcomes

- Egg is rescued; driftop99 is killed and Yungy's army destroyed.
- The Sculk Kingdom is evacuated.
- The Wraith ([Wraiths](../../factions/wraiths.md)) steals Egg's journal.
