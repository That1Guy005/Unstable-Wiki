## Summary

With Spawn open, tens of thousands of new players flood [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), and the city's [Capitol City Police Force](../../factions/capitol-city-police-force.md) frames King [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) for 43 murders. Sentenced to death, Parrot is freed by Merchant City's [Vanguard](../../factions/vanguard.md), who need him to pay his debts. He sneaks back in among a herd of cows, while [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) holds off the assassin [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md), and exposes the police's plot to purge the new players.

## Full breakdown

### Arrested

- Rumours say the police are arresting new players. Parrot ignores them because [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) runs the city, until the police storm the castle and arrest him for 43 deaths in Capital City. Theo says Parrot couldn't kill that many; Parrot goes quietly.
- Judge JudgeCourtalomew tries him. Parrot says he hasn't been in the city for days. Witness pineengine only saw a death message, and is then killed by a falling anvil. Fymada testifies he's innocent; Theo yells at the whole courtroom.
- Courtalomew notes every victim was an older player, and accuses Parrot of conspiring with the new players he let in to eliminate the old. Asked whether he'd save old players or new, Parrot says both. Guilty: execution within 24 hours, after which the police take over and the Capital City purge begins.

### The Vanguard

- Locked in the black market, now a prison, Parrot explains the police force has always existed, but serves whoever pays most. The guard won't budge.
- Merchant City's vanguard storms in, frees them, and brings them to DanchoGold, Merchant City's leader. His knight ArthurTheKnight2 has Parrot read the newspaper aloud: Parrot convicted of murder. Dancho laughs: Parrot's too soft to kill one player, let alone 43. He needs Parrot to stay king so Parrot can pay off his debts.
- Parrot leads a vanguard squad. His theory: the killers used dogs named with his username. They need Fymada, and a confession from the police.

### The cows

- [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) has stopped supplying Nether resources to the Overworld, so cows are the best cover for sneaking in. Parrot hides in the herd in a white leather cap.
- At the gate, the police kill three new players simply for being new. Arthur claims they're cow traders; the guard sees through it and extorts a stack of diamonds. Once inside, the team spreads out.
- From Theo's view: Arthur asks the hero of the LAW war to train him. Theo mentions an assassin he trained with months ago, ShoeBilly the Butcher, who might be near. Billy instantly maces Arthur: he's been paid to kill Theo and Parrot.

### Fymada

- With a borrowed bow, Parrot reaches Fymada's tower and overhears a policeman: step down as mayor for 10,000 diamonds, or die. She has a day to decide.
- Parrot apologises for putting her in this position. She can't help: her own police turned on her. Parrot promised to protect everyone, and he'd rather be banned than break that promise. He tells her his plan, which will come together in about 30 minutes.

### Billy

- Billy has an elytra and a mace; Theo roofs himself in to avoid being maced. In a tavern, Billy says he thought Theo would take his place; instead he's an old player helping new ones, which is wrong. He taught Theo that caring about someone makes you weak, Parrot would drop him in a heartbeat, and killers aren't supposed to have friends. Does Theo think Parrot sees him as a friend? Theo attacks.
- Police come up Fymada's elevator to arrest Parrot. On a tightrope to an unfinished building, police on both ends, his arrows miss, so he fights in melee, disabling shields, then runs past them and dives into a fountain.
- Civilians gather to watch Theo and Billy, and some die in the crossfire. Out of carts, Theo can't land sword hits on Billy's movement. A horn signals Billy that Parrot's attackers failed, and he flies off.

### The trap

- Parrot hides in a random room; the police threaten the residents, then give up. The residents say the police told them not to leave their rooms, that they'd be fed, and that outside was dangerous. Billy blows up the building, killing many; Parrot evacuates the rest.
- Theo rescues him on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), until Billy TNT-launches himself into the air and maces the ghast. Theo sends Parrot running and faces Billy on a streetlight: Parrot is making the server worse, Billy says. Theo doesn't care if Parrot is weak; anyone who wants Parrot has to go through him. Billy says Theo has changed, and that he'll wait for Theo to understand.
- Cornered, Parrot credits the police for the clever dog plan, and their leader admits it: after Parrot's death, they'll kill thousands of new players. Parrot calls out Fymada and hundreds of hidden witnesses. The police say they'll leave no witnesses; Parrot tells them to look up, where Theo calls them garbage.

### Justice

- At the police's meeting in the black market, the leader promises the new players will die and the city will be saved. Parrot's team barges in, and the entire force goes into a single cell. Parrot thanks the vanguard and Fymada, and frees the three policemen who informed on the others.
- The newspaper reports Parrot is innocent, the real killer unknown, and Fymada now controls the police. Parrot suspects the person who framed him also put the withers at Spawn: someone trying to get rid of the new players, which is why the old players dislike him as king. They need to find whoever it is.

## Items & mechanics

- Named dogs: killing under Parrot's username to frame him.
- Cow herd and white leather cap: Parrot's disguise.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): ShoeBilly's weapons.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Theo's weapon, until he runs out.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): Theo's rescue ghast, maced by Billy.

## Outcomes

- Parrot proves his innocence; the Capital City police are jailed and Fymada takes control.
- pineengine, ArthurTheKnight2 and many civilians are killed.
- Parrot suspects a hidden enemy targeting new players.
