## Summary

The "mission: null" book lists a hundred missing players and their coordinates. Following them, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) rescue trapped players from a void trap, but King [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) dismisses [NULL](../../factions/null.md) since its victims are all "bad" players. A hidden journal points to NULL's pearl cannon, which NULL blows up, killing Jumper's spies. Back at base, [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) turns up at the door.

## Full breakdown

### The book

- Thousands of players are online, and a small percentage have vanished in the past month, mostly unnoticed since death bans are so common. Mapicc, missing a month after years on the server, is one of them.
- The book holds usernames and coordinates. As Jumper goes through its hundred pages, hunters arrive; the two hide invisibly on lanterns while the hunters wreck the house. Another leader, _x_6_00, comes through a Nether portal with supplies, builds a grave and leaves.
- Under the grave's wither rose, a chest holds a book from the leader: they know Spoke stole it, it isn't his, he should surrender and return it, and rebuild the portal. That the leader knew he'd break the portal scares Spoke.

### The missing

- A cherry-wood house holds an abandoned dog: proof the players really are gone. Nether portals along their path show the hunters are watching, so Spoke digs an escape tunnel.
- Cornflakecapt4in says his teammate Calvin6 vanished fetching an enchanting table. Cornflake's name is in the book at the same coordinates, so Spoke tells him to flee, and he digs straight down.
- Hunters attack from a mountain portal. Out of food and blocks, Spoke gets invisibility from his ender chest and rescues Jumper. In the chase, Jumper is already in the getaway boat, spinning it to show she got there first. The hunters chase them on boats; Spoke can't sink theirs.
- At the escape tunnel, Spoke breaks the melon covering it: the hunters have broken the bedrock and turned it into a void trap. He glides down on his elytra with Jumper's rockets as the hunters seal the entrance.
- Jumper realises everyone in the book is a trapper, so the coordinates may have void traps too. At the second set, a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md) floats in the void with five players stuck on it. Water won't flow below bedrock, so Spoke pearls up through the obsidian and breaks it. With too many players for the ghast, one jumps into the void; one pearls up, and Jumper saddles the ghast and flies the rest up.
- On signs, they say MisterMinion, a name in the book, trapped them there over three months ago. Spoke and Jumper let them keep the base.

### The king

- NULL targets bad players, Jumper says. Spoke says they're ruthless, killing random people, friends and griefers alike, and they need help rallying players: they'll see the king.
- At the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md) the guards refuse them. Theo charges two totems to enter and two netherite at the castle; Spoke writes directions to some netherite and runs past while Theo writes them down, then tips him a stack of TNT. Theo makes them bow to Parrot, who stops him: they're friends.
- Parrot has had zero reports of disappearances, though most of the book's coordinates are in his kingdom. When Jumper explains the victims are trappers, griefers and scammers, Parrot says NULL is doing his kingdom more good than harm. He doesn't believe NULL exists, thinks they're using him to find Mapicc, gives what resources he can, but won't prioritise it. Spoke loses his temper.

### The hunters strike

- Walking the obsidian highway with five of Parrot's guards, Spoke sees a portal on a lava cast. The hunters burst out of the walls and kill the guards instantly.
- Boats, trees, wind charges and towering all fail; Jumper misclicks and knocks them both down; Spoke's boat sticks in a pothole. Diving and digging, he jukes most of the hunters, then hides under a waterfall and jukes the rest.
- Spoke digs an escape tunnel, Jumper pearls to him, and he drains the water and places dripstone where it was, popping four hunters' totems and teleporting them away. They crawl away.
- Without Parrot's regear they'd be dead, and two players aren't enough. The last option is Jumper's spies, which she hates: it would mean war with NULL.

### Road to NULL

- The last coordinates hide a passage behind a painting in a chimney, and a book in an item frame, "road to null", by anticsparty06, who isn't in the mission book.
- Antics joined to build farms with friends and give new players gear, wanting to be remembered as a good player. NULL raided his base and marched him tens of thousands of blocks to a mountain [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) said to lead to the Road of NULL. He tampered with his pearl so it landed in the Far Lands instead, just before he was killed, and built his own cannon there. NULL keeps hunting him. He asks whoever finds the book to save him, at 50,000 in each direction.
- Spoke thinks that's the cannon, their only chance to save the missing players and Mapicc. They split up to gather spies; Jumper tells Spoke to give them the choice.
- Fourteen spies fly to the cannon on happy ghasts, and NULL's ghasts arrive from the other side at the same moment. Spoke goes invisible and throws a pearl into the cannon; the leader sees it fire and blows the cannon up, and the hunters kill Jumper's spies. Jumper is crushed: she promised to keep them out of conflict.
- At Spoke's hidden base, a lamp signals someone at the entrance: an invisible player in a copper helmet, diamond chestplate, copper leggings and iron boots. He drinks milk: it's Mapicc.

## Items & mechanics

- "mission: null": a hundred names with coordinates.
- "road to null": anticsparty06's journal.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): NULL's route to the Road of NULL, destroyed by NULL's leader.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): stuck in a void trap, and the spies' transport.
- Dripstone tunnel trap: pops four hunters' totems.
- Void traps: NULL turns Spoke's escape tunnel into one.

## Outcomes

- Five trapped players are rescued; Parrot's guards and Jumper's fourteen spies are killed.
- Parrot refuses to treat NULL as a priority.
- Mapicc returns.
