## Summary

LAW hires [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) to assassinate Mistrul, the warrior who hunts anyone trying to leave [The Mist](../../places/the-mist.md), a hidden civilization millions of blocks out. Infiltrating it, Flame wins a trial by combat against a suspicious roommate, brings in [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) as his partner, and slips out to find Mistrul's cave. Their duel goes Mistrul's way, and as Flame and Lomedy escape, Lomedy is shot off their ghast.

## Full breakdown

### Into the Mist

- Fewer than one percent of players know the Mist exists, because no one who enters is seen again, and those who try to leave are hunted down by Mistrul.
- Flame rides in on a happy ghast with elemental R3D, telling him he's bored and wants fights. Elemental warns fights are rare, the mist makes navigation hard, and once inside, you can never leave: only death gets you out.
- Flame takes an empty room. He took the job mainly for the duel; becoming a grandmaster is his real goal. Asking around, he learns Mistrul is almost a myth, living in his own base outside the civilization.

### FluffalSupreme_

- Elemental objects to Flame's flame banner, and pairs him with a new player until he's trusted: FluffalSupreme_, who's noticed Flame asking about Mistrul and will be watching him.
- Raiding nearby trial chambers for trade goods, Supreme says a friend died in Flame and Wemmbu's 2v1000; Flame calls it self-defence, and Supreme promises to expose him. On the third wave, Supreme blocks the path with obsidian and leaves; Flame finishes the room alone and breaks out.
- In the storage room, Supreme loudly announces that Flame is here to kill Mistrul. Elemental orders a trial by combat under Mist law: Supreme gets a second fighter; if Flame loses he's expelled, if he wins the accusation is withdrawn and he gets a new partner.
- Both opponents wear diamond, which is vulnerable to knockback, so Flame uses a knockback sword and wins. Supreme takes it back.
- Elemental lets Flame bring in a partner from outside, who will then belong to the Mist. Flame chooses Lomedy, who isn't thrilled; Flame needed someone who wouldn't rat him out.

### The cave

- Invisible, they slip over the walls; Flame overhears Elemental saying joining only to hunt Mistrul would be foolish. At a cobblestone wall, [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md)'s soldiers attack. Flame's [Orbital Dog Cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) fails, because they've learned to stock Turtle Master, and spear-boating doesn't work either. With their armour failing, they flee into a cave that the soldiers are afraid to enter, not because of Flame, but because of who's inside.
- Past ice rivers and spikes is a house where Mistrul stands AFK reading a book. Flame explains he was hired to kill him but wants a real duel. Mistrul calls him stupid for entering his cave: he's the strongest player Flame will ever fight, and Flame's mistake is thinking they're on the same level. Then he maces Flame out of nowhere.
- Flame dodges into water, they drop into another part of the cave and fight with swords. After his totem pops, Flame mends, grabs two more wolf rods, and with his dogs pops Mistrul's totem and starts to turn it around.
- Mistrul flies off towards Lomedy to finish it. Lomedy tells Flame to jump; he wind-charges into the dark onto their waiting [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md). Mistrul says it was hardly a fight, Flame popped four to his two, and he won't tell the Mist Flame came: Flame is beneath his concern.

### The arrow

- Flame says his skills live up to his name, even without honour. Lomedy asks whether a grandmaster title is worth this, now the Mist knows they've gone. Flame prefers growth over stability, and must beat Mistrul to progress.
- Lomedy stands to speak, and is shot off the ghast by an arrow.

## Outcomes

- Flame survives his first duel with Mistrul without winning it.
- Lomedy is shot off the happy ghast.
- The Mist learns Flame has left.
