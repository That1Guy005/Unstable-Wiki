## Summary

A thousand settlers King [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) sent to found [Skymore Civilization](../../places/skymore-civilization.md) have vanished, along with most of his guards. Following coded messages left by a survivor, Zoe, and a trail of white flowers east, Parrot and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) are stalked by horn-blowing hunters. They uncover [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md), a hidden kingdom of old players preparing to take back Spawn, with [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) as its enforcer.

## Full breakdown

### Skymore

- Parrot sent a thousand players over 100,000 blocks out to found Skymore and test whether the land was safe, and gave them a week. When he returns, everyone is gone. Nothing is looted or griefed, and houses are unfinished: they disappeared all at once.
- A sign tells someone named Reina to run and follow the farmland. Building up, Parrot sees Morse code written in the farmland: "I RAN EAST, -ZOE".
- As he's about to blow his horn, Cachetale says he already heard it, though Parrot never blew it. When he does, only five of his twenty guards return.
- Riding east, they follow the same false horn to a hidden staircase. The squad is too afraid; Parrot and Theo go down alone and find chests of the guards' loot. Back on the surface, the remaining guards have vanished too.

### The town of red names

- Theo is angry that Parrot keeps blowing the horn and giving away their position, but Parrot needs to know if these people are alive.
- In a town of new players who won't acknowledge them, a wall of red usernames includes zoe444. Capybaradunk explains: twenty residents went for food and never came back. Zoe passed through asking strange questions, and left a book only for Reina.
- It's a cipher. Parrot pretends he can't crack it, to mislead anyone listening, but the first letters of each word spell "Find the white flowers". Whoever's doing this is smarter and stronger than an average player, which is why Zoe left clues.

### The followers

- 2,500 blocks east, a deserted camp has tents bearing a banner like a flame. Parrot hears digging below them, and their horses vanish. Why take horses rather than kill them? Parrot writes Theo a note: pretend to be out of food, to draw the followers out.
- A bed, chest and furnace hold another "Zoe" book, a Caesar cipher sending Reina south. It's too simple, and there's no white flower to mark it as hers. Parrot plants a farm and leaves bait; their crops and items are taken, but not the book, proving it fake.
- Deeper into the forest, now genuinely short of food, they tower up on spruce saplings. The horn blows again and again, but no one attacks. A chest beneath them holds two maps showing an icon ahead and one behind.
- Parrot realises the followers back off whenever they hear Theo: they've taken everything, but never touched the two of them. The maps are meant to split them up. He writes Theo a plan; Theo calls it stupid and storms off home.

### Zoe's book

- The flowers grow thicker, horns following him, until a house ringed by white flowers for hundreds of blocks. Zoe's book explains: the survivors called it "the monster", anyone who left never returned, and after two days they were attacked by a kingdom of far stronger, older players: Cindercrest.
- The survivors were relocated to Cindercrest; some died of starvation, natural causes or trying to escape, until only Zoe and her friend Reina were left. Reina asked if the world was infinite, which made Zoe realise something was wrong with it. They overheard two players talking about taking back Spawn; one, [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md), heard Zoe, and she fled before they could execute them, back to Skymore, leaving clues for Reina. If Reina is reading this, Zoe has most likely starved to death.

### Billy

- The horn sounds and ShoeBilly_ is at the door. Parrot accuses him of terrorising these players and declaring war on his kingdom. Billy works for the highest bidder. He felt no remorse at all for the thousand: only the strong survive, and surviving means being a bit selfish.
- Asked for last words, Parrot shouts for Theo, whose [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) kill the Cindercrest members behind Billy. In flashback: Theo's "stupid plan" reaction was an act, and he'd been tailing Parrot with the map. Theo holds Billy off while Parrot runs.
- At Skymore, Theo arrives battered and in diamond instead of netherite. A player runs in: [Reina](../../characters/reinadrop.md), asking for Zoe. Parrot gives her Zoe's book and apologises; he wanted the civilization to protect everyone.
- Parrot wants to end Cindercrest before it takes back Spawn. Theo says it's impossible, he's afraid of Billy, and Parrot trusts his judgement and holds off on war. They need to warn the server first. Reina wants to fight, since without Cindercrest, Zoe would never have died, and joins them.

## Items & mechanics

- Morse code in farmland, an acrostic cipher and a Caesar cipher: Zoe's clues, and the fake.
- White flowers: Zoe's trail to her last house.
- Horns: Parrot's guard signal, faked by Cindercrest to lure guards away.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Theo's ambush on Cindercrest's soldiers.
- Locator maps: Cindercrest's attempt to split Parrot and Theo up.

## Outcomes

- Skymore's thousand settlers and fifteen guards are lost; Zoe is presumed dead.
- Cindercrest is revealed as a kingdom of old players planning to take back Spawn.
- Reina joins Parrot and Theo.
