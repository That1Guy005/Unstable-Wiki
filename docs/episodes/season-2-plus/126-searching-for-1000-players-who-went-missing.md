## Summary

[Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) leads [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) to a pearl cannon that NULL has tampered with, dropping them twelve million blocks out at the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md). Crossing its strange biomes with NULL's hunters close behind, they find a raided rebel camp, reluctant allies among NULL's escapees, and [PlanetLord](../../characters/planetlord.md)'s village, before an interrogated squad leader shows them NULL's underground route. At the bottom stands [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md), offering help.

## Full breakdown

### Revenant44

- Mapicc hasn't much time. He tells them to pack everything and hands over a book from Revenant44 with meeting coordinates.
- A tree being broken and replaced hides a bunker ladder, watched by a NULL hunter. Below are four identical redstone-lit tunnels. Mapicc was captured by NULL and escaped, with a chance to save the rest. A pearl cannon leads to a camp where they can gear up and meet Revenant, the one who saved him.
- Hunters burst out of the tunnel walls. Spoke and Jumper hold them off and they pearl into the cannon.

### The Far Lands

- The cannon misfires, probably tampered with: F3 shows twelve million blocks out. Mapicc says that's actually good, and heads for the Far Lands, inside the terrain rather than on top, watching for hunters until he recognises a biome.
- A flower biome isn't it. They watch a hunter squad leader brief his team before they leave by their own travel methods.
- Others have escaped NULL, and since NULL captures bad players, they'll do bad things even here, so Spoke and Jumper should blend in and hide their netherite. Spoke suggests using the hunters' own shortcuts. In a cave, invisible, they let hunters run past, then reach the red plains, where the camp is.
- Why not the Nether? Nether portals are a bad omen, Mapicc says: anyone not in NULL who's gone through has never returned. He suspects the hunters take players into the Nether.

### The escapees

- The camp has been blown open with cobwebs, and the gear taken. No Revenant.
- A group of missing players charge at them and tower after them up to a floating mineshaft. They think Spoke's NULL shield means he raided the camp. Spoke explains he and Jumper are the only ones who know what NULL is doing, and they team up. The escapees give them their horses and send them north while they fight the hunters, who strip them and march them through a Nether portal.
- Spoke finally asks why Mapicc gave him the NULL shield. Before the final battle with LAW, he and [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) split up in the End, and Mapicc met NULL's hunters for the first time: they gave him the shield and invited him to join. With a war to fight, he couldn't yet, and mid-battle they pulled his stasis because he was too trusting.

### Planet's village

- Where chunks of biome float overhead, they outrun hunters on horseback into the Far Lands Rings. Following nametags, they find players around a campfire, whose leader is PlanetLord.
- Planet lets them gear up at his enchanting table. He's been out here a while, exploring until NULL began patrolling, and his village believes the biomes weren't natural: whoever first found the Far Lands made them.
- While they farm and fish, a hunter kills the player who greeted them. Invisible, they watch the hunters' leader ask Planet where Spoke went; Planet protects them, and is forced through a Nether portal. Spoke goes back for the shulkers he left in the house, and the hunters chase Jumper and Mapicc.

### The dark biome

- After an empty plains, a biome of total darkness, where only enchantment glint is visible. They take off their armour, place a torch to see each other, and pick off hunters one by one. One drops chainmail, meaning Planet may be dead.
- They jump the leader. Spoke knows leadership isn't easy, and has questions. Why bring players from Spawn to the Far Lands? He's only a middleman. Who sent them? He can't say, but he can show where they come from.
- Disarmed of his netherite, he leads them to a trapdoor, too scared to be seen with Spoke, and tries to run; Jumper kills him.
- Below is a seemingly endless stone-brick staircase, then fibres that may lead to the different biomes. Lighting the way with redstone torches, they reach a village-like area in the dark. Someone walks out of the darkness: Jamato. He's there to help.

## Items & mechanics

- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): Revenant's escape route, tampered with by NULL.
- NULL shield: Mapicc's gift, and the reason the escapees mistake Spoke for NULL.
- Nether portals: NULL's way of taking players away.
- Enchantment glint: the only light in the dark biome.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): their cover while hunters pass.

## Outcomes

- Planet and the escapees are taken by NULL; several hunters and their leader are killed.
- Mapicc explains how NULL recruited and then abducted him.
- Spoke finds NULL's underground route, and Jamato.
