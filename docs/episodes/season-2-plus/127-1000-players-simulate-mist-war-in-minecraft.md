## Summary

Hunted by Mistrul's whole army in [The Mist](../../places/the-mist.md), [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) searches for [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) with the help of refugee explorer [NotSkrilley](../../characters/notskrilley.md). Betrayed by Flowtives, Mistrul's secret trainer, and told Lomedy is dead, Flame breaks down, then enters "the zone" and kills Mistrul in a rematch. He spares Flow, burns Mistrul's mace and elytra, and goes home, to find Lomedy alive.

## Full breakdown

### The hunt

- Mistrul maces their [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md) to death; Flame wind-charge-clutches into the ice spikes. Mistrul demands a rematch while Flame is unprepared, calls him a coward, and blows a horn that brings hundreds of Mist members.
- They all have wind charges, so the high ground doesn't help, and his game is lagging. Flame retreats; Mistrul mocks the "combat master" for running. Someone on an ice spike throws him an invisibility potion, and he hides inside a spike.
- Yelling for Lomedy, Flame wind-charges up to a glowing player: NotSkrilley, who says he knows the Mist's secrets. Flame agrees, on condition: lead him away from Lomedy and he's dead.

### The refugees

- If Lomedy has any sense, Skrilley says, he's hiding. The Mist is bigger than Flame thinks, hidden from the server. Skrilley and his friends were explorers until Mistrul found them and forced them to work. An ice wall is heavily guarded, and anyone even trying to escape is killed.
- At Skrilley's refugee village, he promises Flame will help them escape and destroy the Mist if they find Lomedy. They spread out.
- Flowtives, said to be very good at PvP, joins Flame and Skrilley and suggests searching a cave. In it, Skrilley explains the Mist has no real leader: the strongest player leads. Flow gets irritated by Flame's questions.
- A Mist horn: they've found something. Flow has spare Mist armour, cursed with binding, and they try to blend in invisibly. Mistrul calls them out at once. Realising invisibility gave them away, Flame and Flow trap players in a hole and splash them all invisible; Mistrul kills his own invisible players while the three escape.
- At the village, griffincoat6 found Lomedy, but panicked when the Mist attacked, and sent him to an old escape route that's a dead end. The refugees are too scared to go without training. Losing patience, Flame threatens to kill them all; Skrilley calms him, and Flame agrees, if Lomedy dies meanwhile, he'll kill them.
- Flow warns that showing the refugees can fight will just get them killed by the Mist, and heads for the tunnel himself. Mid-lesson, Mistrul's army arrives to end the village. Unable to tell friend from foe, Flame makes Skrilley abandon his village. Flame apologises, and promises to take on the Mist alone once Lomedy is found; Skrilley only wanted to play with his friends, and if he stopped being kind, he wouldn't be himself.

### Flow

- In the escape tunnel, a glowing player and a tomb: Flow, apologising, it wasn't personal. Mistrul says the escape route was always blocked, a rumour desperate PvPers told themselves. Flow trained Mistrul, and his job is to make sure no one ever leaves.
- Mistrul tells Skrilley to switch sides or die. Flame wonders: if Flow killed Lomedy, where's Lomedy's mace? Mistrul sets off TNT, dropping Flame and Skrilley down an ice-spike shaft. Skrilley says when Mistrul says someone's dead, it's true, and begs for help: Flame is all he has left.
- Flame tells Skrilley to run and fights them both. Flow pops a totem to fall damage. Skrilley hasn't fled. Flame accidentally throws his sword; Flow tosses it back, not wanting a "combat master's" sword, and taunts him for losing his composure over Lomedy. Flame and Skrilley escape on an ice boat.

### The zone

- In an abandoned tower, Flame sends Skrilley away. Alone, he blames himself: he brought Lomedy into the Mist for his own grandmaster ambitions, and took away Lomedy's chance to live his life. He calls himself pathetic and weak.
- He remembers ItzRealMe's teaching. His mind was stuck in the Mist; alone, he can control the zone.
- Mistrul waits in the arena and notices Flame is quieter. Flame lands more hits, keeps Mistrul from getting him low, lands a six-hit combo then an eight-hit combo, pearls onto the fleeing Mistrul and kills him.
- The civilization attacks. Flame picks some off and carts a group with a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md); the rest flee. Flow arrives, and Flame gives him Mistrul's remains, then the fair 1v1 he wanted. Flow calls him the most worthy opponent he's faced. With Flow nearly dead, Flame stops: Flow should live with the same feeling Flame has.
- He throws Mistrul's elytra and mace towards Flow, then burns them. Flow says they were beyond any player and belonged to the Mist; they only did it to break him. It worked, Flame says, and leaves the civilization to Flow.

### Home

- Back at base by happy ghast, Skrilley arrives with good news: Lomedy. He'd hidden; someone found and ditched him, and he acted tough with his mace until the Mist ran off.
- Flame realises he killed Mistrul and many Mist members, and made an enemy of Flow, over nothing. Skrilley lost his friends and village for this. Lomedy apologises for being bad at the game; he's just a farmer. Flame and Skrilley chase him round the base, jokingly threatening to kill him.

## Items & mechanics

- The zone: Flame's focus, the key to beating Mistrul.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Mistrul's, burned by Flame.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Flame's clutch, and the Mist's weapon for taking high ground.
- Curse of Binding Mist armour: Flow's disguise.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the cart on the crowd, and Mistrul's TNT trap.

## Outcomes

- Mistrul is killed; the Mist is left to Flowtives.
- Skrilley's refugee village is destroyed.
- Lomedy is alive, and NotSkrilley joins Flame.
