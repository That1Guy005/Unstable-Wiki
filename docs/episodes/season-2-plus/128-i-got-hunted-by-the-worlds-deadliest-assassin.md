## Summary

An invisible assassin, the Wraith, announces they're going to ban the pacifist [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), and nearly kills [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) with a hidden railgun. Parrot won't help, so Wemmbu interrogates the bandits the Wraith hired, is captured in a moving trap machine, and is rescued by [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) after all. He goes back and kills the Wraith, only for a second player in the same trims to call his name.

## Full breakdown

### The Wraith

- Wemmbu has plenty of enemies, but Egg is a pacifist everyone likes. So it's strange when the Wraith arrives at their base vowing to ban Egg, and warning that if Wemmbu steps outside, he dies.
- He steps out: a railgun ([Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md)) pops his totem, and the Wraith drops with a mace and pops him again. Wemmbu flies up and maces back, and the Wraith stasises out. An explosion goes off beneath him; the Wraith reappears in the crater, then stasises away again. Wemmbu blows up the railgun.
- He gives Egg a stack of wind charges and his mace, Crucible. With no ideas, they ask Parrot, who tracked down the Director and Wemmbu himself. Parrot says he has no resources or time.

### The tavern

- Laying low outside a tavern, they list what they know: the Wraith has a mace, no elytra, many stasis chambers, decent redstone skills, and could be anyone, invisible and with a voice changer.
- A waiter says they can't loiter, so Egg orders the most expensive item, an enchanted golden apple, and eats it immediately in case the assassin is near. Diamond-armoured players with two netherite fighters jump Egg and kill civilians; Wemmbu tells bystanders to run and kills all the attackers. The waiter asks for payment ("later") and knows nothing about them.
- Egg has an "unethical" plan: they're just outside Parrot's kingdom, so if the assassins strayed inside, Parrot would have to help. Wemmbu refuses, and they wait on top of the 2v1000 statue to be attacked. Egg takes off his armour, rests, and thinks so hard that he concludes the attackers were ordinary bandits, whose leader could lure out the assassin.

### dizzyu

- Wemmbu walks into the bandits' base unarmed. Their leader, dizzyu, has him item-checked and says it's unlike Wemmbu to come with nothing. Wemmbu already knows who hired them, and points out they'd have no proof of a kill since he carries nothing.
- Dizzy orders him locked up, and Egg dispenses his gear up from under the floor. Wemmbu offers dizzy one chance to talk or his team dies; in the fight Wemmbu switches to a guard's diamond armour and blows up the base, killing everyone but dizzy, who surrenders and drops his items.
- Dizzy knows only that the Wraith paid a fortune in diamond blocks to kill them both. As they plan to use him as bait, the Wraith appears and crossbow-carts them. In boats on the water, where carts can't reach, they flee; Egg riptides away while Wemmbu draws the chase, then stab-shots for height and digs underground.
- No rockets, and he's lost his stasis to the Far Lands, so no more orbitals. He grindstones random gear for XP to mend his netherite.

### The machine

- At a village where he hopes to buy gear, the Wraith is waiting, though even Wemmbu didn't know he'd land there. His stab shots are out. A contraption beneath him launches him into cobwebs and ancient debris, and starts to move, the Wraith riding along. Mining fatigue makes fighting pointless.
- The Wraith won't say how they teleport so freely. They'd planned to ban Wemmbu, but his many enemies will pay more for him alive. Egg must die for personal reasons. The machine stops; the Wraith goes to check, telling Wemmbu to stay.
- Parrot drinks milk inside the machine and they break out. Parrot brought ingredients to craft rockets, but needs time. Wemmbu holds off the Wraith underground while Parrot digs out, and they escape.
- Egg convinced Parrot to come: if he saved Wemmbu, Wemmbu would owe him a favour. Parrot offers a place in his guard force or protection in his kingdom; Wemmbu won't run, and goes back to ban the Wraith.
- The Wraith says he should have left with Parrot: the plan was to weaken him until he complied. Wemmbu wants answers when he wins. He lands twelve hits in a row and kills them.
- The assassin's voice calls his name, and Wemmbu turns to see another player in exactly the same trims.
