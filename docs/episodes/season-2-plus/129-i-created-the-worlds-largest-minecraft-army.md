## Summary

[Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md) will invade in five days, so King [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) sets out to build the largest army the server has seen. He recruits bandits by trickery and [Reina](../../characters/reinadrop.md)'s dripstone trident, takes [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md)'s [Vanguard](../../factions/vanguard.md) by kidnapping DanchoGold, buys 40,000 TNT from [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md), and blows up the LAW holdouts led by [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md). By the time Cindercrest's blimp appears, with Flame aboard, Parrot has tens of thousands.

## Full breakdown

### The bandits

- Thousands of players are online, but Parrot only has Theo and Reina, drained after Skymore. On the way to Merchant City, RocketBandit3's bandits rob them. Parrot's bluff that Theo can 20v1 them fails, and RocketBandit hits him down to half a heart.
- Panicking, Parrot invites them to join his army, with more gear and food than robbing him would bring. They agree; any bandit can join if they promise not to steal or hurt anyone.
- At the bandit base, Parrot tells King OutlawAndrew about Cindercrest. Andrew says bandits take what they want by force, so Parrot proposes a 1v1 between champions. Andrew knows who Theo is and refuses him, so Parrot picks Reina, and Theo yells that he's sold.
- Andrew's champion is ralamus, in an arena with a ceiling of decorative dripstone. Parrot gives Reina a [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md). On the countdown she throws it into the dripstone, which falls and kills ralamus. Silence, except Theo cheering. Andrew joins.

### Merchant City

- The bandits aren't allowed into the city. Parrot asks DanchoGold for 150,000 diamonds and 300 men at a 3:1 return. Dancho reminds him he's still in debt and has him surrounded for arrest.
- On Parrot's signal Theo carts the guards and takes Dancho hostage: the plan all along. With the vanguard spread across the city, Parrot promises Dancho's release after the war if they join. A bystander points out that if Dancho dies, nobody has to work for him; the city turns on him and Dancho escapes on a stray horse. 300 vanguard join.

### ClownPierce

- Theo and Reina set up at the old bandit base while Parrot enters the Nether, normally deadly, but an exception for the king. He wants 40,000 TNT from Clown.
- Clown reminds him of the vault raid half a year ago and triples the usual 20,000 price for damages. Parrot calls it a scam and turns to leave. Clown says Cindercrest is already marching and will arrive within 24 hours, that it bought loads from him too, and that Parrot is more fit to be a jester than a king. Parrot pays everything.
- Clown admits he wasn't a fan of opening Spawn, but thinks it's one of the best decisions ever made. Parrot can come back to buy anything, but for any other reason, Clown will kill him.

### The blimp

- Theo is shaken that Parrot spent everything on TNT; Reina says Parrot has a plan: grow the army to 10,000.
- Scouting Cindercrest territory, Theo and Reina hear a horn in a snowy biome and see a massive [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md) heading for Spawn, with hundreds or thousands in full netherite walking alongside. On top stand [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md). Theo accidentally spills water down the hill; two Cindercrest members inspect it, and the pair hide right beneath their feet.

### LAW

- LAW, once the largest army, still has a thousand soldiers, twice Parrot's army, and the gear to equip thousands. SargeLAW tells him to leave, believes him about Cindercrest, but is joining Cindercrest.
- Parrot asks the lawmen to choose. Many cross to his side; he begs for more. Sarge says Theo isn't even here and they can't win.
- RocketBandit confirms everything is set. Parrot shoots a target, which blows up the front of the base and many lawmen still loyal to Sarge. Parrot's army wins the battle and loots the base.

### Tens of thousands

- Spawn becomes a recruitment drive: a new path leads new players either to Capital City or to the army's castle full of gear, while recruits farm, mine a huge chunk and practise fighting.
- Theo and Reina return saying they're doomed. Parrot is about to address his army. Theo says they'll need thousands; Parrot says tens of thousands, and shows them the vast army, with [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) arriving with her own group.

## Items & mechanics

- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) and dripstone: Reina's arena kill.
- TNT: 40,000 bought from Clown at triple price, and the bomb at LAW's base.
- [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md): Cindercrest's airship heading for Spawn.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Theo's carts in Dancho's hall.

## Outcomes

- Parrot's army absorbs the bandits, the Merchant City vanguard and much of LAW.
- DanchoGold escapes; ralamus and Sarge's loyalists are killed.
- FlameFrags is seen with Cindercrest, as the [civil war](../../events/the-civil-war.md) approaches.
