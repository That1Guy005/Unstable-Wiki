## Summary

Deep in the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md), [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) guides [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md), [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) "towards" the missing players while secretly blackmailing Spoke with a full log of his crimes. Spoke defies him, attacks him in disguise, and loses everyone: Jumper is captured, Mapicc learns the truth about Jamato and Leo and turns on him, and Spoke's last exploited chestplate breaks. Jamato, the leader of [NULL](../../factions/null.md), takes Mapicc and sends Spoke through the portal.

## Full breakdown

### The blackmail

- Mapicc wonders how Jamato is alive; Jamato says his death was what Spoke wanted them to believe, and turns to the real problem, NULL. Spoke feels his return is no coincidence.
- In Jamato's shulker of gear is a book addressed to Spoke and signed by Jamato: Spoke will never find the players, Mapicc and Jumper will turn on him, and if he tries anything, Jamato will reveal everything. Signing it is Jamato's mistake, Spoke thinks.
- At the exit, Spoke stalls so he can pass the book to the others, but Jamato "accidentally" picks it up and rewrites it: enjoy the kit, stay safe. Spoke burns it. On the stairs he finds "Spoke_Log_Full", Jamato's record of everything Spoke has done since joining, and burns that too. If Jamato wants to lead them away, Spoke will sabotage it.

### The bridges

- They emerge among terracotta spikes linked by bridges, with hundreds of villager houses below. NULL holds most of the Nether with thousands of players, Jamato says; they can't fight it, but they can't find the missing without it. He offers a secret back way.
- Spoke refuses: they'll head north to find more rebels. Jumper sides with Jamato. Spoke goes anyway; Mapicc follows, Jumper hesitates, and Jamato mutters that Spoke is making a mistake.
- Nether portals appear on the pillars ahead. Spoke blows up bridges to dodge them, and they fight through NULL's hunters, Spoke breaking their portal and falling to half a heart. Spoke and Mapicc knock hunters off with knockback swords.

### Pickletopia

- At a new biome, Jamato says it's not too late to turn back. Revenant mentioned a rainbow forest with stacked players; Jamato says he's never been. Spoke can't work out what Jamato wants, and they're heading into a trap.
- Mapicc wind-charges a player, chromesanctuary, off a tree; Chrome runs and leads them into a piston fall trap. Spoke's netherite and NULL shield make Chrome distrust them, so they strip their gear to meet the leader.
- In Pickletopia, the leader, DylPickle (Dylan), says he can tell they're good people from their vibes, cages Chrome for questioning that, and gives Spoke a lodestone compass to a secret stash whose location he's "forgotten".

### The stash

- A red-sand desert spawns ravagers endlessly; they tower up and kill them with lava. Where the compass loops, Spoke digs down to diamonds, golden apples and books, fetches the others, and finds it emptied. Behind a painting, a tunnel leads to NULL hunters. They kill one, but he has none of the loot.
- NULL may have followed them underground the whole time. Jamato never fought, only watched. They can't go back to Dylan without his gear.

### Cookie's village

- In a mythical forest with too little sculk to mend, Jamato has boats ready. Spoke can't shake Jamato, and decides killing him is the only way.
- Taking off their NULL gear, they let Jumper talk to CookieBunsquat's village. Cookie says they'd never have survived the sand biome; never trust Dylan, who wants everyone dead. Cookie used to be one of Jumper's spies, and remembers a file on Spoke. They admit their mission, and Cookie helps with supplies.

### The ambush

- When Jamato asks to use an anvil in Cookie's house, Spoke slips away claiming he's mining sculk, drinks invisibility, and sees Jamato isn't holding a totem. He builds a Nether portal from an ender chest's obsidian, puts on the NULL armour to pose as a hunter, digs under the house, and as Jamato removes his chestplate, crits him with strength. Jamato pulls a totem at the last second and stasises away.
- Jumper and Mapicc think NULL has come, and the whole village attacks the "hunter". Portals appear across the village. Jumper goes to protect Cookie.
- Mapicc chases Spoke through a cave; a trident riptide doesn't lose him, and with a minute left, Mapicc pops his totem, stripping his invisibility.
- Mapicc asks if he's insane: Jumper was captured because of him. Spoke says Jamato leads NULL and has been blackmailing him from the start. Mapicc calls it ludicrous: Spoke lost them all their allies. He won't go with Spoke to save Jumper, and leaves.

### The maze

- With no food, millions of blocks out, Spoke remembers old adventures: blowing up farms, the Mafia hunting him, his lava-cast house. He climbs through a crack into a maze roofed with sculk, runs out of hunger, and NULL hunters follow him without attacking. A portal opens in front of him.
- Jamato says it was always going to end like this. Spoke asks why he took a thousand players. Everyone deserves to explore and make their own choices, Jamato says, but it always ends with Spoke: death and destruction. NULL exists to bring bad players to justice. Capturing Spoke wasn't easy, and he resents that Spoke survived and kept killing.
- Mapicc was taken long before NULL went after Spoke. Jamato realised there was no point capturing Spoke himself when someone else could bring him: he had NULL let Mapicc escape, knowing Mapicc would find Spoke. And Spoke led NULL to every rebel camp; their leaders are now Jamato's.
- Jamato owns the truth and could tell anyone anything, but nothing he made up would beat what Spoke did to himself. How long, he asks, does Spoke think he can hide it?

### Mapicc

- Spoke breaks the portal and finds a white gateway, and Mapicc in the maze. Jamato has told him everything: it was Spoke, not Leo, who tried to kill Jamato. They could have roamed free if Spoke hadn't wanted his moment with Leo.
- Spoke argues that if he'd confessed, they'd have turned on him, and they did kill Leo. He just wants to go home. Mapicc puts on fresh netherite: he's been manipulated all along, and won't let Spoke leave. He webs Spoke and crits him.
- Ctrl, Alt, Delete, and Escape, the last piece of an exploited armour set, finally breaks after two years. Mapicc says Spoke just wanted to get away with it. Spoke asks him to finish it; instead Mapicc asks for the exit, and tells him to live out in the Far Lands or push NULL and die.
- Before he can finish, NULL hunters pour from a portal, break all Mapicc's armour and drag him into the Nether, as Spoke softly apologises.
- Jamato: this is Spoke's legacy, not a hero, but the reason the rebels fell. To see Mapicc again, Spoke must drop everything. Jamato burns it all, then everything in his ender chest, and sends him through the portal: not the end of the server, but a better era, without Spoke.

## Items & mechanics

- "Spoke_Log_Full": Jamato's record of Spoke's every action, used as blackmail.
- Ctrl, Alt, Delete, and Escape: Spoke's exploited chestplate ([Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md)), broken by Mapicc.
- NULL armour and shield: Spoke's disguise for the attack on Jamato.
- Nether portals: NULL's way of appearing anywhere.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Spoke's failed riptide escape.

## Outcomes

- JamatoP is revealed as the leader of NULL.
- Jumper and Mapicc are captured; the rebel leaders are in NULL's hands.
- Spoke loses everything and is taken through NULL's portal.
