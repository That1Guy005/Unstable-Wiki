## Summary

The assassin [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) killed, Cytharam, was only one of them: another identical invisible player says [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) is too valuable and must be found before others capture him. Searching for Egg, Wemmbu clashes with [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md), finds [The Wheat Kingdom](../../places/the-wheat-kingdom.md) destroyed, and discovers [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) working with the assassin for [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md). At the assassin's island, Egg is put up for auction, and [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md) wins.

## Full breakdown

### The second assassin

- Wemmbu has few friends besides Egg, missing since they split up. The player he killed was Cytharam, but an identical player appeared beside him and told him to meet at his base.
- The player doesn't know where Egg is: Egg is valuable, and must be killed or he'll be captured, and that ends badly for everyone. Wemmbu agrees to work with him, and is told to restock his orbitals.
- In a town, wheatgust's shop gives them armour, paid in diamond blocks. Yungy and three guards are interrogating townsfolk about Egg for Arachn1d. Fishing by the pond, Wemmbu eavesdrops, then maces Yungy, kills his guards, and fights off the town guards as Yungy flees. The assassin says he should have kept a low profile; the town has emptied.

### The Wheat Kingdom

- At Egg's fishing spot, the assassin finally explains: Arachn1d isn't the only one hunting Egg, and wants him to control him. Arachn1d also paid him to capture Wemmbu alive, since Wemmbu's enemies will pay more for that.
- A planted wheat seed points to the wheat village. It's been destroyed. A survivor's sign says "This invis guy kidnapped Egg", and the assassin's mace looks like the one Wemmbu gave FieldsOfElysium. The deal's off: Wemmbu's stab shot kills a survivor by accident, and orbitals level a chunk of the wheat farms until the assassin stasises away.
- The survivors say someone was with the invisible player: FlameFrags.

### Flame

- Wemmbu orbitals the two guards at Flame's base and finds Flame, [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) and the assassin: they have Egg. Flame says he's forced, as part of Cindercrest; cooperate and Egg won't die.
- Wemmbu orbitals the base and fights Flame. Flame tells Lomedy to kill Egg; Wemmbu chases and finds an obsidian box: the assassin took Egg. Flame says Egg refused to answer his questions, so they boxed him.

### Linnwyrm

- Wemmbu and Flame sail to Linnwyrm, who is AFK getting DoorDash. Linn hasn't seen Egg, but gave Wemmbu armour because since LAW's fall many players are stirring, and he wanted Wemmbu at full strength. He knows the trimmed player and his base.
- [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) arrives to help; he and Flame argue, then agree to work together. Wemmbu disenchants fishing rods and bows for XP and snatches a whole shulker of totems from Flame. Linn warns how dangerous the player is, and they have to come back for the coordinates.

### The auction

- At an enormous island, Yungy and Arachn1d with three guards are there for Egg too. Up top, Egg greets Wemmbu. The assassin's proposition: whoever pays most gets Egg. He puts Egg in a remote stasis for safety.
- Flame announces he's here on behalf of Cindercrest, which needs Egg's knowledge. Impatient, Wemmbu says he'll kill everyone, and fires an orbital. In the free-for-all, Wemmbu and Jaden kill two of Arachn1d's guards; Flame's armoured wolves join; Wemmbu duels Yungy into a cave, where Flame and the assassin follow.
- Invisible, Wemmbu explores a maze in the cave, nearly falls into a trap, and finds dripstone to use. A dripstone drop pops the assassin's totem: Flbthp.
- In a big room, the invisible player waits with Arachn1d and Jaden for Cindercrest's representative; Flame and Yungy arrive, and the auction resumes. Arachn1d offers the entire treasure of the caverns. Wemmbu considers offering Gambit, but the assassin accepts Arachn1d's bid.
- Why do they want Egg? With Egg, Yungy says, they have Wemmbu, and with Wemmbu, the power to make Arachn1d the rightful king and win many battles. His ultimatum: join them in the caves, or Egg dies.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's stab shots and orbitals at the Wheat Kingdom, Flame's base and the island.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): the assassin's, apparently FieldsOfElysium's; Gambit, Wemmbu's possible bid.
- Remote [stasis](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): where the assassin keeps Egg.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): Flame's armoured wolves.
- Dripstone: the trap that reveals Flbthp's name.

## Outcomes

- The Wheat Kingdom is found destroyed.
- The assassins are revealed as Cytharam and Flbthp ([Wraiths](../../factions/wraiths.md)); Arachn1d buys Egg.
- Wemmbu must join the [Kingdom of the Caves](../../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md) or lose Egg.
