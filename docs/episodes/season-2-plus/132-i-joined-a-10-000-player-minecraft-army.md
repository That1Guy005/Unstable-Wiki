## Summary

The civil war's opening from [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' side. [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) joins [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md) to help the starving old players, and Flame reluctantly follows. Flame leads the blimp drop that takes Fort Feather, is sent with an assassin to kidnap [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) for the orbital cannons' location, loses Egg at the auction, and walks into [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s trap at [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md).

## Full breakdown

### Joining Cindercrest

- After the Invisible Mafia, Flame swore off big teams. Then Lomedy says opening Spawn has cost the old players: they're running out of resources, starving and being robbed, and Parrot does nothing. Cindercrest aims to overthrow him; help once, and they can live in peace.
- Flame thinks it's terrible. Lomedy goes alone, and when Flame tries to stop him, calls him selfish for letting people starve. Flame doesn't believe Parrot would let players die; Lomedy tells him to tell that to the dead.
- King [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md) isn't interested in a farmer, until Lomedy mentions he has a mace. Parrot lets old players starve, and Cindercrest will make him pay. Flame admits he doesn't believe in the cause, but joins anyway. He questions fighting a war that has nothing to do with them; Lomedy respects the cause.

### Fort Feather

- On the [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md), only Flame and [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) ride: both killers, the strongest in Cindercrest. Taking Spawn is a game of control over the surrounding locations, starting with Fort Feather, where Parrot trains his army: a ground assault as a distraction, with a large group dropping from above.
- Flame splashes everyone with slow falling and opens the trapdoors. Inside the main building, an explosion trap kills everyone inside; Flame pulls his people back. Distracted by the blimp, the defenders mostly fled, and Fort Feather falls.

### The orbitals

- Saparata wants Merchant City next, and a better weapon: Wemmbu's [orbital cannons](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md), the only thing that's ever beaten Flame. He gives Flame an elytra (which Flame says has nothing to do with combat mastery) and pairs him with an invisible assassin who knows a lot about the server. Taking down Parrot matters more than Flame's quest.
- The assassin's plan: take Egg hostage and interrogate him, or kill him. Forcing Wemmbu himself would be impossible.
- In a desert, the assassin stasises away and back, having found Egg, at the Wheat Civilization, already robbed of all its food. Egg knows why they've come. When the residents attack, Egg agrees to go so they won't die.
- At Flame's base, Egg is caged in obsidian. He says he can't tell them, for integrity's sake. Flame argues that "can't" means he knows; Egg says being best friends doesn't mean knowing Wemmbu's secrets. Flame blocks off his light.
- Two guards die: Wemmbu. Flame says he's forced to work with the assassin as a member of Cindercrest, and will return Egg once he answers. Wemmbu orbitals the base. Flame signals Lomedy to kill Egg, drawing Wemmbu after Lomedy, but the obsidian is empty: the assassin has taken Egg.
- Betrayed, Flame agrees to work with Wemmbu. It sucks going behind Wemmbu's back, he admits, but without the cannons Cindercrest can't beat Parrot, and Lomedy is at risk. Linnwyrm points them to the assassin; [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), with whom Flame has bad blood, joins.

### The auction

- On the assassin's island, [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md) and [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md) warn that fighting means Egg dies. The highest bidder gets Egg: Yungy offers three diamonds, Egg asks to be valued higher, and the assassin stasises him away for safety. Wemmbu offers his whole ender chest, Yungy a stack of diamond blocks, Flame two stacks and his combat skills, on behalf of Cindercrest.
- Wemmbu fires an orbital and a free-for-all begins. Flame's fishing-rod [orbital dog cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) savages Yungy, who falls down a stab-shot hole. Flame webs him, pops his totems, and spares him in exchange for rockets. Yungy suggests they find Egg together; Flame prefers trying to kill him, and they fight through a maze to a lava platform.
- Wemmbu warns Egg will die. The assassin accepts Arachn1d's offer: the caverns' entire treasury for Egg as a prisoner, half on delivery. Wemmbu won't kill them, since that kills Egg, and warns Flame he could be put in the same position over Lomedy. Flame leaves without the orbitals.

### Merchant City

- At Fort Feather, Saparata expected more from a former Mafia member. Flame blames the assassin's betrayal and hands back the elytra without caring. Saparata will go without Arachn1d for now. Flame warns that Parrot is smart and may counter the blimp.
- At Merchant City, white flags fly and Parrot says they surrender, and invites them to walk in. Saparata sees through it and calls in the blimp instead, telling Parrot to try harder.
- Lomedy hopes the surrender was honest and the deaths unnecessary. Over the town centre, Flame splashes slow falling again, and as they drop, arrows fire upwards at incredible speed. Lomedy goes silent, then comes into range alive. Theo thanks Flame for the loot; the vanguard flood in. Flame sends Lomedy away, finds no other survivors, and pearls out, missing a crossbow cart on the vanguard.
- Saparata will ally with Arachn1d's kingdom. Flame says Lomedy and he would be dead if he'd listened to Saparata. "Best shot, not guaranteed," Saparata says; Flame failed to get the orbitals, and death happens in war.
- Flame asks Lomedy to leave the war: Flame isn't improving and Lomedy isn't saving anyone. Lomedy calls him selfish. Flame admits it: being selfish is how you survive on Unstable. Lomedy marches off, and Flame watches angrily from a rooftop.

## Items & mechanics

- [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md): Cindercrest's airship, slow-falling drops into Fort Feather and Merchant City.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Cindercrest's goal, and Wemmbu's answer.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): Flame's fishing-rod wolves at the auction.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Saparata's gift, returned.
- Parrot's arrow volley: the counter to the blimp at Merchant City.

## Outcomes

- Cindercrest takes Fort Feather but is crushed at Merchant City (see [The Civil War](../../events/the-civil-war.md)).
- Arachn1d's kingdom wins Egg at the auction; Cindercrest allies with it.
- Flame and Lomedy fall out over the war.
