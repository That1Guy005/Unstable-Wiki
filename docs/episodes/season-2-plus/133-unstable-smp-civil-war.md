## Summary

The civil war from King [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s side. [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md), the old players' kingdom, takes Fort Feather with its blimp, but Parrot's fake surrender and anti-blimp machine crush it at [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md). A captured spy reveals the next target, Highwater City, the kingdom's breadbasket, and Parrot's arrow cannons nearly destroy Cindercrest there, until [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md)'s kingdom arrive and the city falls. [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md) cuts off the kingdom's food.

## Full breakdown

### Fort Feather

- Since Parrot became king, the server has split between the new players pouring in and the old players who feel their world is being destroyed. The old players formed Cindercrest, aiming to wipe out Parrot and the new players. Parrot stations his army across his cities, expecting the first strike at Fort Feather, his eastern gateway.
- From the walls with [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) and [Reina](../../characters/reinadrop.md), he sees boats. Saparata uses one of his own players as bait to set off the hidden mines. One of Parrot's TNT cannons blows up his own soldiers.
- A redstone [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md) appears. Parrot's army builds upward and [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) maces them out of the sky. The fight is lost: Parrot evacuates through a tunnel in the storage room on horseback, and his TNT trap there kills a large part of Cindercrest's force.
- Cindercrest is far stronger and smarter than he thought, and his army is mostly new players. Fort Feather opens the direct road to Merchant City, where the [Vanguard](../../factions/vanguard.md) waits.

### PrinceTetra

- At the tunnel's end, PrinceTetra has already heard about Fort Feather and threatens to blow everything up unless Parrot gives them a reason to fight. What makes him think they won't die like the others? Parrot goes quiet as Tetra counts down from ten.
- At five, Parrot speaks: anyone can leave, but if they don't beat Cindercrest, tens of thousands of new players die and the survivors live in a world of scarcity and pointless death, the server before Parrot, which he became king to change. If they want to kill him, do it now; Cindercrest is coming. The guards stand down and ask for his plan.

### The fake surrender

- White flags go up. Theo is excited to see Dingus and Parrot's horse being ridden by the enemy. Parrot announces the surrender; Saparata doesn't believe it, Flame wants to walk in, and Saparata sends the blimp instead, exactly as Parrot planned. There was no trap under the gate.
- During Cindercrest's march, Parrot had a new machine built to counter the blimp. He marks the trapdoors with an arrow, and when the blimp is over the city centre and its trapdoors open, Reina fires it: TNT detonates and the falling Cindercrest troops die. The vanguard pours in targeting anyone in netherite, and Saparata marches away.

### soldier73

- Players line the obsidian highway to cheer. Outside the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md), TNT blows up dozens. Reina digs to a fleeing player and stops Theo killing her: soldier73.
- In the prison she won't talk. Parrot apologises for what comes next and leaves it to Reina, who drowns her to half a heart. Cindercrest's target is Highwater City, and she was sent to kill Parrot.

### The council

- With all his city leaders, Parrot explains: Highwater supplies 80% of the kingdom's food; if Cindercrest seizes it, they win.
- He wants [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) to build a cannon there; 4C lacks time and materials. Parrot asks [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) to tear down Capital City's railways and automatic farms for parts; she worries, since they're used for evacuations, but without a new weapon there'll be nowhere to evacuate to. 4C estimates eight hours; Parrot says six, and goes to delay Cindercrest with LAW's leader, CorporalPaul.

### The bridge

- They won't use the blimp again, Parrot reasons, and he predicts their route. Meeting the army, he talks with Flame as an old friend from past wars: these are the players who destroyed a civilization he built, and they'll kill tens of thousands of new players. Flame says it's a long story, and brings [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md).
- Lomedy calls Parrot's reign horrible: people starve, bandits kill, the kingdom is unsafe. Parrot says he's trying; Lomedy says it isn't enough, and asks if he really cares. Would he be standing here if he didn't? Flame admits Parrot has a point, and that he's been attacked by bandits inside the kingdom himself. Parrot warns that if they push across the bridge instead of going round the mountain, his legion won't hesitate.
- LAW's army isn't there. The front line holds behind bows and a cobweb wall; Theo slows ShoeBilly with slow falling and carts clumps of attackers, but Parrot's army keeps losing ground. As Cindercrest reaches the end of the bridge, LAW finally arrives to cover their retreat.

### Highwater City

- The city has been evacuated and garrisoned. 4C needs thirty more minutes and no extra hands, and the mayor is absent.
- Waiting by the river, Reina asks if Lomedy is right: in an infinite world, why are people starving? Parrot doesn't know: Highwater should feed most of the kingdom, but there's never enough. Bandits keep returning stronger. After the war, Reina plans to quit the server; Parrot lists places to go and people to meet, unconvincingly, as Theo practises a triple neo jump. Reina remembers Zoe giving her a white flower and a promise that they'd both survive, and picks one up from the ground.
- The horn sounds. When Parrot holds out both totems, Reina fires.

### Saparata

- Parrot stalls for nightfall. Is starving his kingdom really necessary? Saparata asks for a reason not to; how many have already suffered? Flame says his opinion of Parrot has changed over time.
- Saparata offers him the chance to surrender the crown. He lived in Capital City with his friends until Parrot opened Spawn and there wasn't enough food. They left to farm on their own, without the city's protection, and bandits killed all his friends. Parrot ruined the server, and vowed to protect them.
- Parrot apologises; he's heard that before. Asked who he'll save, the new players or the old, he says that one day nobody will have to fight to survive, and his kingdom will be big enough for everyone. Too late, Saparata says: Parrot can't bear to watch his people die, can't even bring himself to kill Saparata. Parrot raises his second totem.
- The [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) kills 70% of Cindercrest's army and Saparata retreats into the water. As expected, Billy flies at the cannon, and Theo fires the second at him, chasing him off. Cindercrest is down to sixty players.
- They launch a full invasion and Reina keeps firing, but Flame, invisible, sneaks down and blows up the artillery cannon, and Billy destroys the arrow cannon.
- Saparata blows the horn, looks at Parrot, and says nothing. Through his spyglass Parrot sees Wemmbu flying in, and orders everyone down before the orbital lands. Arachn1d is there too. Yungy tells Wemmbu to kill Parrot. With three elytra-macers and Flame against them, Parrot, Reina and a reluctant Theo flee by boat. Saparata sacrificed his army as a distraction for Flame.

### The proclamation

- Saparata addresses the remaining forces: anyone who joined before Parrot put on the crown should see Parrot, not him, as the enemy. He remembers when Capital City had enough food, when farms fed farmers, when every house and road meant something because they'd built it. Parrot called opening Spawn mercy, but it invited thousands of starving players into a kingdom that could barely feed its own.
- Holding Highwater, Cindercrest cuts the kingdom's food supply: starve or surrender. Anyone who joined before Parrot's reign may join Cindercrest and be spared; everyone else will be killed. Parrot wanted a world big enough for everyone; Saparata is building one big enough for those who deserve it.

## Items & mechanics

- [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md): Cindercrest's airship, beaten by Parrot's TNT machine at Merchant City.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): 4CVIT's two cannons at Highwater, destroyed by Flame and Billy.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's arrival, which decides the battle.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Theo's carts on the bridge.
- White flower: Zoe's promise to Reina.

## Outcomes

- Fort Feather and Highwater City fall to Cindercrest; Merchant City holds (see [The Civil War](../../events/the-civil-war.md)).
- Wemmbu and the [Kingdom of the Caves](../../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md) join Cindercrest's side.
- The kingdom's food supply is cut off.
