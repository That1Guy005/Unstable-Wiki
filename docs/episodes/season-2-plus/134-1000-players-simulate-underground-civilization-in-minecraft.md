## Summary

To get [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) back, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) joins [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md)'s underground [Kingdom of the Caves](../../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md) stripped to wooden tools, with [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) at his side. He climbs from "bottom feeder" to royalty: summoning wardens in the mines, an arena deathmatch, robbing Dione4k's firework factory, sacking [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md) and assassinating Highwater's leader. He finally sees Egg, guarded by sixteen stases, and wins the civil war's decisive battle, but spares Parrot.

## Full breakdown

### The bottom feeders

- [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md) splashes night vision and explains: nobody carries better than iron until they've proven loyalty to King Arachn1d. Wemmbu is placed with the "bottom feeders". He means to find Egg and escape, and the way to do it is to prove his strength.
- Jaden has joined so Wemmbu isn't captured alone. When Wemmbu starts explaining his plan, Jaden's sign warns that Yungy is listening above, and Wemmbu changes the subject.
- Their night vision runs out and they find The_Codmaster14, whose house has invisible chests and scuffed everything. It's dark to ward off attackers; only higher-ups get night vision. Ranking up means impressing them, which is impossible without items, and that's the point: they're stuck down there to enjoy life. Yungy crashes through the roof and sends them to the mines.

### The mines

- They must mine all day for the king or die, three chests each, shipped by minecart. Codmaster's last friend group died down here. Wemmbu and Jaden mine top and bottom while Codmaster lays rails, and they hit a deep dark.
- Wemmbu breaks shriekers, sets one off unseen, and a warden spawns. Players die, a second warden appears, and some players hit Wemmbu to draw the wardens to him, so he kills them. Yungy's guards kill the wardens, and everyone snitches on Wemmbu.

### The arena

- In jail, they bicker over whose fault it is; Wemmbu calls the warden "CGI". In interrogation, Wemmbu breaks the tinted glass and runs, Yungy catches him by elytra, and Codmaster honestly names Wemmbu, earning his freedom at the cost of his items.
- Wemmbu and Jaden get iron armour and are made to fight each other. Wemmbu accidentally shoots Jaden; they web and shield until Yungy shoots Wemmbu to three hearts. They drain each other's healing until Arachn1d storms in: they're the most valuable players in the civilization, and Yungy is wasting them.
- Arachn1d apologises and offers to rank them up to citizens for one mission. Wemmbu wants Egg back afterwards; Arachn1d says Egg is safe, and it would be a shame if that changed. Across a lake, Wemmbu sees Flame watching him.

### BanditKing

- Missions usually need netherite. Fishing for books in shallow water just yields fish. Codmaster knows the richest bottom dweller, who fails missions on purpose. He whispers "burger" into a wall, and "Regrub" opens the door.
- The_BanditKing asks why he shouldn't kill them. Wemmbu's two pieces of sugar cane don't help; Jaden's reminder that they're the strongest players outside does. He lets them enchant, but not take diamonds. Wemmbu then steals the enchanting table, sneaks diamonds and iron from the mines for an anvil, and steals cobwebs and another anvil. Codmaster prefers his peaceful life to joining them.

### Dione4k

- Yungy's loyalty test: take the resources of a "friend" who lives in a mountain wall. Saying hello repeatedly gets them Dione4k, who once made rockets for Wemmbu's civilization and was a pirate as a "side plot". His chests are full of firework rockets; how he gets creeper resources, he can't say.
- They kill his two guards and demand everything. Yungy sentences Dione to make fireworks for the underground forever, and promotes them to level two.
- They get group-splashed night vision and Unbreaking III shields. The storage is bare after the Eggchan purchase; next is taking Merchant City to win the war. Codmaster, resentful, doesn't want to be seen with "spiders".
- At the market, Yungy exposes a scamming book seller and lets them kill him, calling it self-defence. In the enchanting area, built around a mineshaft with three spider spawners and five-minute limits, Jaden stalls Yungy with talk of water-bucket clutches until Wemmbu has decent diamond gear. Yungy teaches webbing and water draining, which Wemmbu already knows.

### Merchant City

- At the tavern, Weave's Drop, BanditKing is the bartender. Trixxio says Wemmbu killed six of his friends and hundreds of Spiders, and attacks; Wemmbu fights him for his netherite until Yungy stops them: you don't fight royalty. They're allowed their items, but not elytra. BanditKing joins the attack.
- In trimmed armour, they storm Merchant City. Yungy splashes the vanguard invisible, Wemmbu webs and maces the guards, and the rain powers Jaden's trident. The vanguard retreats; Cindercrest and more Spiders will secure the city.
- The fire-shield players are Cindercrest visitors, allies. Yungy gives them a broken-roofed house right below his mansion. While Jaden distracts Yungy with clutch talk, Wemmbu slips off.

### Royalty

- He wind-charges up a spider statue's leg, digs into the castle and walks in confidently. Royalty member Andaboar assumes he's already been promoted and takes him to Arachn1d, items stored away.
- Arachn1d: Wemmbu has no power; he decides whether Egg lives. One more mission brings royalty, with access to everything, prisons included. They're at war with Parrot, and Highwater City feeds his kingdom: assassinate its leader, ParamountX, with no witnesses, using an elytra just this once. And he won't find Egg sneaking around uninvited.
- On a mountain, fully potted, Wemmbu dives onto ParamountX's nautilus convoy: one mace hit kills him, then three of four guards. In the rain the fourth riptides away; Wemmbu chases on elytra and kills him with a [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md) strike.
- Arachn1d announces Wemmbu has surpassed every rank: endless rockets, Merchant City, and Highwater's leader dead. He gets royalty, all his items, and second-in-command. Yungy protests that Wemmbu is only here for Egg; Arachn1d gives Wemmbu permission to kill him if he doesn't step down. With Wemmbu, Arachn1d could be king of the whole server.

### Egg

- Jaden can't pass the royal water elevator without a royalty shield. Yungy shows Wemmbu his mansion, then has him gear up fully before seeing Egg, past guards and mining fatigue in a secret passage.
- Egg has been bored: up, reading, writing, bed. He's set sixteen stases; if any is tampered with, they all trigger and he's teleported elsewhere. Netherite guards push him around and tell him where to throw pearls.
- Arachn1d's proposition: Egg goes free if Wemmbu wins the most important battle on the server.

### Highwater

- Wemmbu and Yungy fly in while the royal army boats. His orbital hits a crowd; Saparata on a ledge is an ally. A stab shot launches him into the air by accident, and he keeps killing Parrot's army.
- Parrot says the city has fallen and to run. Yungy tells Wemmbu to focus Parrot. He maces Parrot's last totem and lines up the kill, then remembers asking Parrot for help with Egg, and Parrot rescuing him from the Wraith's machine. He lets Parrot escape.
- Back in the city, people joke they did Cindercrest's dirty work. Wemmbu listens from a rooftop to Saparata's speech: Parrot has lost the war.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's strikes at Highwater.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md): the ParamountX assassination.
- Wardens and shriekers: the mine disaster.
- Firework rockets: Dione4k's factory, now the kingdom's.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Egg's sixteen linked stases.
- Spider spawners: the kingdom's enchanting XP.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu becomes royalty and Arachn1d's second-in-command.
- ParamountX is assassinated; Merchant City and Highwater City fall (see [The Civil War](../../events/the-civil-war.md)).
- Wemmbu spares Parrot.
