## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) becomes the newest prisoner of [Purgatory](../../places/purgatory.md), [NULL](../../factions/null.md)'s prison over an endless lava sea in the Nether. A first escape with Far Lands rebels ends at an arrow cannon, but [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) keeps him alive to suffer as the prison's villain. Through the black market, gladiator fights and a mission with [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), Spoke is thrown into solitary, escapes it, and saves [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and Jumper from execution, exposing Jamato to the whole prison.

## Full breakdown

### Arrival

- Purgatory holds over a thousand of the server's "most dangerous criminals", surrounded only by lava and watched by NULL guards. Each prisoner gets a personal escort to a cell. Spoke vows to see the world one last time and finish what he set out to do.
- In the cafeteria line, prisoners are thrown rotten flesh. A fistfight kills a prisoner who drops an iron pickaxe and three logs; Spoke grabs them and slips back to his cell.
- At the item check, he hides his items on his ceiling trapdoor, fills his inventory with the wooden shovels the guards hand out, passes thanks to his rotten flesh, and points the leader at a "prisoner flashing an iron pickaxe" to get rid of him. A prisoner caught smuggling a stick is killed on the spot.

### The first escape

- Behind his cell are the guards' chambers. Parkouring on lanterns to a staircase, he meets a guard's eye and digs into the next cell: devMCchamp, a Far Lands rebel who thought Spoke had escaped after giving them the horses. Bribed with rotten flesh and steak, Dev still doesn't give him away, asking only where his friend ItsAli is.
- Dev knows rebels who can help: Spoke breaks out avxo31, den_72 and subway87, who has an invisibility potion. Invisible, they dodge guards on the stairs, mine through a keycard door, and find a room of happy ghasts behind a redstone door. Out of time, they break the door and bridge out, but run out of blocks a hundred blocks out, surrounded by guards bridging and on ghasts. Spoke hands over his pickaxe but keeps three crimson stems.

### Jamato's office

- Cobwebbed before an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md), Dev asks why Spoke freed them without a plan; he's been out in the Far Lands for months, never saw Ali in the prison, and has forgotten what the main server looked like. They say goodbye. A totem appears in Spoke's hotbar as the arrows hit: he survives.
- A nameless high-ranking guard, [_________](../../characters/commander.md), takes him by keycard doors and elevator to the leader's quarters, flanked by nameless guards marked X, Y and Z. Jamato drinks milk. Dozens of elder guardians are on their way to fill the prison, and he burns Spoke's iron pickaxe.
- No one here knows who Jamato is, only that NULL exists; they'll remember Spoke as the reason they're here. Spoke promises to escape, return with an army never seen before, free everyone, and execute Jamato himself. Jamato will wait.

### The black market

- Guarded in a new cell, with mining fatigue, Spoke claims he's starving and is let out. He blocks the stairs with netherrack, honey-block-clutches down to the cafeteria, and as the guards kill prisoners, Vixuality on a chandelier tells him to follow. They boat-drop to the cells, and a secret redstone door under a dead end opens into the black market.
- Jumper is there, amazed he hasn't been killed, since everyone knows who he is. She refuses to work with him: last time they both ended up captured. Vix doesn't know who "Jamato" is either; he just wants to get Spoke out.
- Prices: iron boots for one kill, a sword from found2 for four bodies. n3tm3tr1x has something better, a piglin that trades rather than sells, but only gladiators, winners of the prison's arena fights, may use it. Spoke says he'll be back in diamond with a mace.
- A horn means the guards are back; players rush the elevator, some falling to their deaths. Lost, Spoke enters a random cell and kills the occupant who punches him, in front of the guards, who warn him he knows what's coming.

### Gearing up

- Trading rotten flesh for cobblestone, he and Vix hide a crafting table inside a canteen table under carpet. p3dal_squ1d644 offers a shield, cobblestone and iron leggings to "slime out" minerd64; Spoke lures him upstairs and they kill him. His amethyst buys ten iron ingots from brozo_60; nuggets cost a honey block, leather, flint and cobblestone. c1ro4 won't sell his iron chestplate, so they chase him down at the elevator; he also drops a guard-type goat horn.
- In full iron with Vix's healing potions, Spoke watches his arena opponent: diamond armour, nothing special, until the kill message shows it's Mapicc.

### The arena

- Mapicc nearly kills him and asks the crowd whether to spare him. Then ItsAli_, the rebel who once held Mapicc hostage, arrives with two others, blaming Spoke for Dev's death: Spoke gave him hope.
- Spoke and Mapicc team up; Mapicc kills the other two, and they knock Ali off a ledge (he boat-clutches). Mapicc knows Spoke isn't NULL's leader, but he's still the reason Mapicc is here. When the horn blows, Mapicc stays as a distraction.
- Spoke robs the empty black market, trades gold to the piglin, and gets one ender pearl as the merchants return. Caught, he says he won his gladiator fight, returns some gold, and explains his escape plan: a pearl cannon with massive TNT. found2 has shulkers of TNT, for a job more dangerous than killing. Come back in an hour.

### The job

- Ali and three others ambush him in a cell; he walls himself in, offers his iron helmet and leggings "for a fair fight", then blows the guards' goat horn, which will draw the chain of command, and bed-bombs his way out as they flee.
- found2's job is an escape: she's stockpiled invisibility, milk and more, and Jumper has the redstone for a pearl cannon. They need Spoke for the pearl, and because he's the only one who's seen the lava ocean and survived.
- NULL raids the black market, tipped off by Ali. They hide in the piglin room (Spoke kills the piglin), build a Nether portal against found2's wishes, and come out at a hole over the void. found2 warns about NULL's newest ability, the "strike": portals appear all around them. They surrender; Spoke picks up found2's dropped book.

### Solitary

- A portal opens and Jamato steps out. Purgatory ran perfectly until Spoke arrived: "Everything you touch, breaks." At a hole in the centre of the prison, Jamato says he's found someone better to kill: Vixuality, whose detailed escape plans never included Spoke. Spoke argues for Vix's life and calls Jamato just as evil as he is; the commander kills Vix.
- Solitary is a single block in a lava lake, reached by happy ghast. No food, not even rotten flesh, and front-row seats for Mapicc and Jumper's execution at the end of the day. Jamato splashes him with poison and leaves.
- Jamato missed found2's book and a three-minute fire resistance potion. The book is a map: "FOOD", a 3×3 grid of zeros with a one in the corner, and "ENTRANCE". Spoke swims up a lava stream with five seconds of fire resistance to spare.
- found2, stunned he escaped solitary, gives him bread and invisibility. He steals a commander's level-two keycard, and raids the guard quarters for wind charges, pearls, bread, invisibility and poison potions, then three gear sets for himself, Mapicc and Jumper, and a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) from an item frame, though he's never used one.
- Back in solitary, he finds Jamato's execution cannon uses water in the Nether, and quietly removes the bubble columns so the arrows will sink.

### The execution

- The commander reports the cannon broken; Jamato calls it coincidence and moves the execution to the centre. Spoke escapes again, blows up the elevator to Jamato's office with TNT, turns invisible, knocks Jamato off his platform into the centre, and maces the Z commander to death. Jamato's totem pops; he can only manage "you" and "how", and flees.
- Spoke arms Mapicc and Jumper, tells the watching prisoners to put on armour if they'll fight NULL, and explains Jamato is behind everything. The guards pour in; most prisoners die, Spoke runs out of wind charges against cobwebs, and they surrender.
- Spoke demands to speak to Jamato: everyone has now seen who put them here, not SpokeIsHere but JamatoP; Purgatory isn't impossible to escape, and they can fight back. Jamato will always be watching his back for a player like Spoke. When Spoke returns with the armies of Unstable, Jamato will be the first to go.
- Spoke, Mapicc and Jumper are led back to their cells, their friendship restored, and Spoke promises they'll escape.
- Jamato rants to the nameless commander that it wasn't supposed to be like this, then asks if the arena is ready. It's time to begin the games.

## Items & mechanics

- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): Purgatory's execution machines, one disabled by removing its bubble columns.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): taken from the guard armoury, and used on the Z commander.
- Piglin: the black market's trader, for gladiators only.
- found2's book map: the route out of solitary.
- Fire resistance: three minutes, for the lava swim.
- Keycards and the guards' goat horn: stolen access, and a weapon against Ali.
- Nether portal "strike": NULL's instant mass deployment.

## Outcomes

- Dev, Vixuality, minerd64, c1ro4, the Z commander and most of the rebelling prisoners die.
- Spoke escapes solitary twice and saves Mapicc and Jumper.
- The prison learns Jamato leads NULL; Jamato announces "the games".
