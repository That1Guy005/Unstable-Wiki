## Summary

[Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md) secretly plans to betray [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md) and rule the server himself, and uses [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) as his weapon: against [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) at Steampunk City, against [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' army, and finally against everyone at [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), where Wemmbu's nukes kill hundreds on both sides. When Wemmbu comes to collect [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), Arachn1d wishes him "good luck" and drops him through a trapdoor.

## Full breakdown

### Steampunk City

- With the war going Cindercrest's way, [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md) plans to rule the server, and Arachn1d means to take his place.
- Arachn1d sends Wemmbu to beat ShoeBilly, Saparata's commander at Steampunk City, without banning anyone or using orbitals. Wemmbu spears an innocent player anyway. Billy pops all his totems and blows up the pipe he's regearing under. Wemmbu webs Billy and lands a stab shot, breaking his helmet, and lets him go as a "warning from Arachn1d".
- Arachn1d scolds him for the stab shot and the dead civilians. As punishment, Egg, who'd been allowed some freedom living in Codmaster's house, has his stasis pulled back into his cell.

### Flame's army

- Upset over the duel, Cindercrest sends Flame with 100 players to the Spider Web Faction. Wemmbu points out the hundred are pointless, since he could ban them all, and they retreat, leaving Flame, [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) and [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md).
- Wemmbu starts losing his duel with Flame until [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) breaks almost all of Flame's armour; then Spongs and Lomedy join and it stalls. [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md) steps in and apologises to Flame on the kingdom's behalf.
- Arachn1d is proud that Flame is no longer a threat, and rewards Wemmbu with Wind Burst I and Thorns III books: Gambit's first enchantment change in a long time, from Wind Burst I to II, and Thorns on his leggings.
- Arachn1d has rigged TNT throughout the caves in case Cindercrest infiltrates: better ruins than surrender. Wemmbu is sent to rig the only base without it, Codmaster's.

### The deal

- Wemmbu overhears Arachn1d and Saparata agree to keep working together. Then Arachn1d orders him to lead the army to a hostage exchange at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md). Wemmbu refuses until Egg is freed.
- Arachn1d apologises and sets a stasis chamber for himself: if he lies about freeing Egg after the war again, Wemmbu may pull it and kill him. And during the big battle with Parrot, Wemmbu must betray Cindercrest and kill as many players as possible.
- At the Council, [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) is handed to Cindercrest in exchange for [ReinaDrop](../../characters/reinadrop.md). Why free Lettuce? Saparata says he's the final piece of the puzzle.

### Capital City

- During Saparata's speech to a huge crowd in Capital City, Parrot announces a 500-strong legion at the gates; Saparata goes to meet it and Parrot tells [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) to evacuate.
- Wemmbu drops a nuke shot on the crowd, killing hundreds, then follows Arachn1d's orders, spamming nuke shots and stab shots across the city, destroying it, and macing Saparata, [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md), Parrot, ShoeBilly, Fymada and Flame, taunting them all. He tells Flame they'll have a rematch another day, and returns to the caves.
- Arachn1d says he'll free Egg now that Wemmbu has done his job, and leads him into a cave. At its end he says "Good luck", presses a button, and drops Wemmbu into an unknown place.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Gambit, upgraded to Wind Burst II.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): banned for the Billy fight, then unleashed on Capital City.
- [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md): Wemmbu's first rule-break at Steampunk City.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Egg's, pulled as punishment; Arachn1d's, offered as a guarantee.
- Cave TNT: Arachn1d's scorched-earth failsafe.

## Outcomes

- Lettuce is released to Cindercrest; Reina returns to Parrot.
- Hundreds die and Capital City is destroyed by Wemmbu.
- Arachn1d betrays Wemmbu, dropping him into the start of [The Purge of the Caves](../../events/the-purge-of-the-caves.md).
