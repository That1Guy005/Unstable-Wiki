## Summary

The civil war from [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' side, to its end. After hearing [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md)'s account of Parrot blowing up LAW, and fighting at Highwater, Flame learns why [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) joined [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md), and they turn on [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md). Confronting Parrot changes their minds. At [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), they stand with Parrot against Saparata, until [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s orbitals kill everyone indiscriminately. Lomedy leaves to find his old friends.

## Full breakdown

### The bridge

- Flame joined for Lomedy; Cindercrest, allied with the [Kingdom of the Caves](../../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md), targets Highwater City. A bridge that saves three hours is held by Parrot, who asks Flame if he knows what team he joined. Lomedy says Parrot's reign has made everything worse: starvation, bandits. Flame asks Parrot if it's true; Parrot says yes. He has 600 players behind the mountain, including LAW and Skymore.
- Saparata forbids Flame pearling in alone. Cindercrest nearly wins the crossing until LAW arrives; Flame disobeys and blows people up with TNT, and they wipe out LAW's army.

### SargeLAW's story

- Fighting LAW, one of their only allies, is bad for Flame and Lomedy. They split off to restock and argue. Sarcastically, Flame suggests asking LAW how they feel about Cindercrest, and Lomedy agrees: if LAW is truly against Cindercrest, they'll sit out Highwater.
- LAW's base is mostly blown up. Sarge says Parrot came to recruit them, threatened to kill anyone who refused, and blew up those who did. Flame doubts Parrot would do that; Lomedy says he clearly did. A deal's a deal, so they rejoin.

### Highwater

- Saparata mocks Parrot for being too weak to kill him, and Parrot's double totems trigger the [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) that kills half their army. Billy fails against the second cannon. Saparata sends Flame to flank; Flame warns Lomedy to carry lots of totems, sneaks round, pearls to the lower cannon and blows it up, to Theo's surprise. Billy takes the upper one.
- Wemmbu and the Kingdom of the Caves turn the battle. Flame orders everyone after Parrot, loses him, and sticks close to Lomedy. During Saparata's speech, Flame realises Saparata meant for most of Cindercrest to die while Flame flanked. He tells Lomedy he's leaving, with or without him.

### Lomedy's reason

- Lomedy explains: his friends at the farm starved to death, and he believes he could have saved them if he'd been there. Joining Cindercrest is the only way he's found to ease the guilt. Flame doesn't have to understand.
- Flame laughs: he once sent all his friends into a battle he knew they'd lose because he was too lazy to help, and when [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), one of them, still cared, Flame betrayed him too. After failing again and again, he's learned never to leave behind someone who truly cares about you. They have a war to win.

### Steampunk City and the exchange

- Steampunk City falls smoothly, with Flame and Wemmbu stealing each other's kills and mocking each other's counts. Saparata complains a mole is confusing them and causing casualties.
- At the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md), Flame watches [ReinaDrop](../../characters/reinadrop.md) exchanged for [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md). Wemmbu complains about freeing Lettuce; Flame has lost Lomedy, and finds him still standing where it happened.
- Flame suggests killing Parrot now, and assassinating Saparata right after. They jump Parrot, Reina and Theo in the council room, until Parrot stops the fight.
- Parrot asks if this is really best. Lomedy repeats what he said at the bridge. Parrot breaks: he knows everyone's dying and it's his fault; he's doing everything he can and it isn't enough; even if the server hates him, he'll keep trying. If they really think killing him fixes anything, do it now. Theo objects; Flame and Lomedy don't attack.

### Capital City

- Lettuce, with Saparata crouched behind him holding a crossbow and Wemmbu above, tells the server to decide for themselves whether someone is a bad ruler; he won't take part in the war. Saparata calls on old players to join him by the count of five.
- At one, Parrot says "zero" behind him. Parrot, Flame, Theo, Lomedy and Reina hold crossbows and spears on Saparata. They've destroyed the items meant for his recruits, and 600 players wait outside the gate.
- Saparata asks Flame why he betrayed them when they were so close. Close? Saparata used them, Flame says. Saparata warns Parrot that one day someone will stand across from him the same way, believing they're improving the server. Not true, Flame says: Parrot is probably the one person on the server who truly cares about everyone, and nothing like Saparata.
- As Parrot asks [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) to evacuate, Wemmbu calls it futile and fires an orbital; over 800 players die instantly. Billy taunts Theo. Parrot opens the gates for his army as Saparata's emerges. Flame pearls into Cindercrest's lines with TNT until more orbitals blow everyone up.
- Parrot says they need to stop Wemmbu. Flame pops Saparata's totem as he attacks Parrot, then Wemmbu maces everyone, taunting Flame for hiding during a strike. Flame tells him to come down and fight; he does, then flies off. Wemmbu levels a building and a museum. Parrot complains his own army is attacking him; Flame decides to attack everyone.
- Flame confronts Wemmbu: this is exactly what LAW was created to stop, and he thought Wemmbu had changed. Wemmbu says he doesn't have a choice. "You always have a choice." Wemmbu, bored, sits on a wall; Flame refuses an unfair fight, accidentally carts Wemmbu off the building, and chases him higher and higher until Wemmbu stops answering and flies beyond reach.

### Lomedy leaves

- At their base, Flame rants that he thought they'd both changed, and that the war has cost everyone everything. Lomedy wants to go back to his farm to help any friends still alive, and take a break from Flame's influence: being around someone whose whole thing is combat is corrupting his morals. Flame understands, wishes him luck, and says he's welcome back any time. Lomedy leaves, and Flame is alone again.

## Items & mechanics

- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): Highwater's cannons, destroyed by Flame and Billy.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's strikes on Capital City.
- TNT and [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Flame's weapons throughout.
- Crossbows and spears: the five holding Saparata at the countdown.

## Outcomes

- LAW's army is destroyed at the bridge; Highwater and Steampunk City fall.
- Flame and Lomedy turn against Cindercrest and side with Parrot (see [The Civil War](../../events/the-civil-war.md)).
- Over 800 die to Wemmbu's orbital at Capital City; Lomedy leaves Flame.
