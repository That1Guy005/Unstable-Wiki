## Summary

The civil war's finale from [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s side. Parrot destroys [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md)'s blimps and wins a string of battles, but [Reina](../../characters/reinadrop.md) is captured and traded for [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md). Her flashback reveals her painful first days on the server and her friendship with Zoe. At [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s nukes destroy everything, Parrot's own army turns on him, and [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md) beats him at [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md) and crowns himself the second king.

## Full breakdown

### The blimps

- Parrot's soldiers cover Cindercrest's blimps in TNT while he does the central one. He runs into Saparata, who asks why an old player like Parrot cares about new players. Parrot will make his kingdom big enough for both; Saparata knows he can't change his mind. Parrot wishes him fun losing the war, blows up the blimps, and water-bucket-clutches down as they explode.
- Riding happy ghasts with the stolen supplies, Theo keeps demanding to know who's carrying the boats until Reina tells him to shut up and Parrot just wants to get home in peace. Theo tells Reina about the Treasure Arc and Verdaria, and needles her for being a new player who missed it all.

### ShoeBilly

- [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) ambushes the ghasts with his mace and kills several soldiers. Parrot sends the survivors into a forest where the mace is less effective, and orders Theo to retreat, since Billy is stalling for Cindercrest's army. Soldier vwingplank drinks invisibility and goes to Ironclad City for more legions.
- Theo taunts Billy, who instantly pops his totem. Complacent, Billy makes them wait until morning, not knowing Ironclad's legions have arrived. At sunrise they start moving the boats to the river, as Cindercrest's army appears while paths are still being built.

### Victories

- With soldier73 turned into a mole inside Cindercrest, Parrot's legions swarm and kill most of their soldiers. New players hold a festival for Parrot, Theo and Reina.
- Reina asks Parrot if she deserves to play on the server, then delivers food to old players. Cindercrest soldiers reject her and mock her friends' deaths at Skymore; she kills both, and is captured.

### The trade

- A messenger, PenelopeZPenguin, says Cindercrest is marching on the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md) with Reina, unarmoured: Lettuce for her life. Theo jokes it was fun knowing her, and Parrot scolds him, but Reina says Theo is right: if killing Lettuce helps beat Cindercrest, she'll die. Parrot angrily asks why, after everything she's done and everything others did for her, she thinks her life is so unimportant.

### Reina's story

- Flashback: Reina spawns for the first time and walks down Spawn's castles to boos and snowballs; players shout she doesn't deserve to play. A farming civilization chases her off as a thief. RathofKelo, whose base she's asking about, starves to death in front of her.
- Starving herself in the ruins of an old battle, she's found by Zoe444, who feeds her suspicious stew made with oxeye daisies for regeneration. They become friends and travel to Skymore. When Parrot sends a thousand players to build it, Reina asks Zoe if she deserves to play; without Zoe and Parrot, she'd be dead. Zoe, uncomfortable, talks instead about hoping Parrot will make Skymore flourish.
- When Cindercrest destroys Skymore, Zoe hands her an oxeye daisy, promises she'll be okay, and disappears.
- Back in the present, Reina admits she doesn't want to die: she wants to keep playing with Parrot, Theo and her friends. The trade goes ahead: Reina for Lettuce, who can rally every old player. Wemmbu watches from a lamppost.

### Flame and Lomedy

- [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) attack Parrot as the cause of the war; after a brief brawl, Parrot tells them to kill him if they think it'll help. Instead, they defect.

### Capital City

- After Lettuce's speech, Saparata promises to spare anyone who joins him, and claims he's more worthy than Parrot. Parrot, Theo, Flame, Lomedy and Reina intercept him, and Parrot has [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) evacuate when the city guard arrives.
- From above, Wemmbu calls that "quite a futile effort" and fires a nuke shot, massacring hundreds of civilians. His nukes and elytra-macing overwhelm everyone. Saparata escapes to Spawn; Theo and Reina go missing; Parrot's army loses faith in him and turns on him.

### Spawn

- Broken-armoured, Parrot runs to Spawn, where Saparata admits he was lying all along: he plans to kill every player who joined after Parrot was crowned. He kills new players as they spawn, while Parrot begs them to leave.
- Parrot gives up on changing Saparata's mind and fights him. Saparata barely breaks a sweat and keeps killing new arrivals. Parrot's helmet breaks; Saparata mocks his "pretty little crown". Parrot breaks the balcony railings trying to outmanoeuvre him, then leaps off and water-bucket-clutches away as Saparata promises Parrot will be next once every new player is dead.
- Stripped of his crown and exiled, with no idea where Theo and Reina are, Parrot watches Saparata build a death trap under the server's spawn point, send soldiers across the server to kill every new player, and crown himself the second king of the Unstable SMP. Parrot swears he'll take his kingdom back, however long it takes.

## Outcomes

- Cindercrest's blimps are destroyed; Reina is traded for Lettuce.
- Capital City is destroyed and hundreds of civilians die.
- Parrot is overthrown and exiled; Saparata becomes the second king (see [The Civil War](../../events/the-civil-war.md)).
