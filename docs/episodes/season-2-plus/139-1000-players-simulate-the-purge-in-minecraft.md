## Summary

Dropped into [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md)'s "purge", a vast arena where the whole [Kingdom of the Caves](../../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md) hunts each other, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) is the highest-value target. With no blocks, wood or crafting tables, he allies with [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md), [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), The_Codmaster14 and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), crafts a mace from a heavy core, and outplays the cheating InfamousJJ. They tower out alive, but Egg's stasis is pulled, and Wemmbu vows to kill Arachn1d.

## Full breakdown

### The drop

- Yungy, Jaden, Codmaster and the whole civilization gather as Arachn1d explains the purge and drops everyone into a huge arena. Death messages flood chat from players who can't break their fall.
- Wemmbu loots a chest in the middle and runs: as second-in-command he's a high-value target, and killing him means reputation. In a cave he kills most of his pursuers with dripstone, and the last with his tools.

### Allies

- There are no blocks, no wood and no crafting tables. Wemmbu duels Yungy to a stalemate and makes peace: a new teammate. BanditKing, with Codmaster, wants to kill them to rise in the ranks.
- A "forbidden" crafting table is a trap set by a guard allowed to keep full netherite. They escape to an island, where Wemmbu crafts a golden pickaxe with very little durability. BanditKing corners them, and Jaden saves them with TNT.
- Jaden leads them to Egg, waiting in a pit, whose "moody" brightness setting causes problems later. Codmaster rejoins them, explaining BanditKing threatened his life.

### The mace

- Codmaster has a fishing rod; Egg fishes up two more. Underwater, Wemmbu finds a structure he calls a clam with a heavy core inside, struggles to break it, and needs breeze rods; he kills a player carrying the materials and crafts a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md).
- With breeze charges, he wipes out the opposing team, but InfamousJJ, who smuggled in netherite and diamond gear and fought them earlier, is near, so they escape.

### InfamousJJ

- They find BanditKing without armour and force him to lead them to an enchanting table. JJ is waiting, holding what they need; they flee, but snag a kill and its armour. Dione4k, on the enemy team, holds a grudge for being brought to the caves.
- Cornered in a cave, netherite players ambush both teams from behind. One netherite player keeps chasing both, so Wemmbu and the opposing team team up to kill him, then go straight back to fighting.
- Wemmbu lures JJ to a trapped chest and blows him up with TNT, takes his gear, regroups in the middle, and they tower out of the purge.
- Egg's stasis is pulled. Wemmbu has had enough of Arachn1d's threats, and vows to kill Arachn1d to free Egg for good.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): crafted from a heavy core and breeze rods found in the arena.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): breeze charges against the enemy team.
- Golden pickaxe: Wemmbu's fragile only tool.
- Trapped chest and TNT: InfamousJJ's end.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Egg's, pulled at the end.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu survives the purge with Yungy, Jaden, Codmaster and Egg (see [The Purge of the Caves](../../events/the-purge-of-the-caves.md)).
- InfamousJJ is killed; BanditKing is humbled.
- Egg is taken again, and Wemmbu turns against Arachn1d.
