## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) holds the title of strongest player, and only [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) can challenge it. Hunting him in the underground civilization, Flame fights [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) in the rain until The_BanditKing offers a deal: guard [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md)'s hidden stasis chambers, the thing Wemmbu is after. Flame prepares a cobweb-and-obsidian counter to orbitals, wins the fight, and hands over the pearls anyway: he only wanted a real fight.

## Full breakdown

### The title

- Both have grown since their last rematch, and Flame wants to settle it. [NotSkrilley](../../characters/notskrilley.md) asks why. An invisible assassin linked to Wemmbu comes asking for training; Flame beats them until they stasis out.
- Flame lists his grievances: Wemmbu 2v1'd him at Arachn1d's civilization, blew up [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), and refused to fight when asked. He thinks he knows where Wemmbu is.

### Jaden in the rain

- In the underground civilization, Jaden says he and [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md) are busy, so Flame fights Jaden instead, drawing out three netherite royal guards. Jaden pulls netherite from his ender chest.
- When rain starts, Jaden and the guards drink invisibility and riptide through the air to confuse him; Jaden's mace pops several totems, and Flame counters with the [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md) as they dive. Flame restocks in a cave, grows trees from saplings for cover, and pulls his fishing-rod [Orbital Dog Cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md). The rain stops, and The_BanditKing calls a halt.

### The pearls

- BanditKing heard Flame wants Wemmbu, and knows where Egg's stasis chambers are, which is what Wemmbu is looking for. Flame doesn't see what BanditKing gains, but follows.
- In a forest house, Flame clears the guards so BanditKing won't get in trouble. Below is an upside-down copy of the house and forest. A punch-bow shot knocks Flame off the bridge; he saves himself, pearls behind the archer, and knocks them into the void. Another player in the walls guards a redstone door with the pearls behind it.
- Pulling Egg now would get him killed if the guards noticed. Flame's best option is to stop Wemmbu reaching the pearls first; BanditKing guards them while Flame gears up.

### Spongs's advice

- [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md)'s outskirts are a ghost town, and a guard won't let a Cindercrest traitor in until Flame threatens him. Inside, [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md) is running a bread "game" that scams players out of their whole hotbar.
- Spongs's advice against orbitals: cobwebs to slow players, obsidian overhead so the TNT can't reach you. He traded his Cindercrest gear for nine stacks of obsidian, which he'll give if Flame makes him his sidekick. Flame gets him to drop the obsidian, then walls Spongs into a small room with it and leaves.

### The fight

- BanditKing brings Wemmbu, Jaden and Yungy. Wemmbu wants the pearls and isn't in the mood to fight. Flame warns that if anyone else joins in, he'll pull Egg's pearls and alert Arachn1d.
- Wemmbu spams stab shots; Flame uses crossbow carts to pop him when he dives for mace hits, and the cobwebs absorb much of the damage. In the upside-down section Flame seems trapped; Wemmbu offers to save him for the pearls, but Flame attribute-swaps his spear and lunges to safety.
- Wemmbu stops trying, flying into webs with no water to escape. Flame says he'd have given the pearls whether he won or not: friends don't ban each other off the server; he just wanted a fight. Wemmbu asks if he means it; he does.
- Flame asks Wemmbu to promise they'll have a real fight one day, and Wemmbu agrees.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Egg's hidden stases behind the redstone door.
- Cobwebs and obsidian: Spongs's counter to orbital strikes.
- [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md): attribute-swap lunge out of the trap.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): crossbow carts against Wemmbu's dives.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) and [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Jaden's rain tactics.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): the Orbital Dog Cannon.

## Outcomes

- Flame wins the fight, and gives Wemmbu Egg's pearls (see [The Purge of the Caves](../../events/the-purge-of-the-caves.md)).
- Spongs is walled in at Merchant City.
- Flame and Wemmbu promise each other a real fight.
