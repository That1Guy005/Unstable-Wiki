## Summary

In [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md)'s Hunger Games inside [Purgatory](../../places/purgatory.md), last player standing wins freedom. [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) scavenges, reluctantly teams with [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), allies and falls out with found2, and gets his mace back from [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), while Jamato breaks his own rules with bounty books. Jamato separates the friends and turns DylPickle and Mapicc against him; Mapicc secretly helps Spoke mace Jamato. Spoke is offered freedom, fakes leaving, and NULL's nameless commander offers to reveal what NULL really is.

## Full breakdown

### The snow quarter

- Hundreds die in the opening seconds. Spoke flees to the snowy section, kills a player with a bow for an iron axe, and finds a copper chest with diamonds and a golden apple. Attacking a team on a mountain gets him a diamond axe and enchanted books. From the highest peak, he sees an endless wall.
- Mapicc attacks him on sight. When another team shoots both of them, they agree to a truce and take that team's enchanted armour. Spoke tries climbing the outer wall; a NULL guard knocks him back down with slow falling. The inner walls, he decides, must lead to more of the arena.
- A hostile team in a stone castle is led by n3tm3tr1x, "Matrix". Caught sneaking in, Spoke hands over his enchanted books to stall, then tells Mapicc to run.

### found2

- They raid a stacked team's mountain base from below, drive them off, and loot it. Back home, found2 reveals it was her team's base: every person she knows matters. She won't let them join; they have nothing to offer. Spoke suspects she's rigged his base to explode, and proves it by putting a pressure plate under one of her teammates. They run as TNT levels the base.
- Mapicc is annoyed that everyone they meet hates Spoke. Underground, DylPickle welcomes them into his new pickle army and helps make an enchanting table; Spoke, with absurd XP, enchants for them, until the army gets desperate to steal the table and they flee, killing most of them, and a fully diamond player near the castle. Spoke takes a Sharpness V sword.
- Three isn't enough. found2 agrees to team up, warning that Matrix will try to kill everyone to win alone. Her team bombs the castle's front while Spoke, Mapicc and Dylan hit the back, and the battle is over in minutes. Matrix refuses to join and pearls away.

### The jungle quarter

- A horn drops one of the gravel inner walls. At an Aztec-style temple, Mapicc picks an unnecessary fight. From a tree, Spoke sees found2, who wanted to show them her secret stashes in a maze beneath the temple, which reminds Spoke uncomfortably of another maze.
- As she opens a stash, a player kills her instantly. They kill him and find a book from Jamato: freedom in exchange for killing found2. Jamato is breaking his own rules. The stash has been emptied, probably by NULL guards; Mapicc shares out what he took earlier.
- Atop a village, they find Jumper, nervous since everyone she's met has tried to kill her. Before the games, Spoke gave her something important; shown the bounty book, she reluctantly returns his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md).

### Bounties

- Bands of players with bounty books keep attacking. At a safe house with traps, Dylan falls off; they jump down to save him, and a bounty book says: capture DylPickle alive. The team has already taken him.
- A portal opens and Jamato steps out: Spoke ruins everything. When Spoke says he's breaking his own rules, Jamato says he makes them. More portals bring guards. Spoke drops his armour, bow and the bounty hunter's netherite sword. Jumper goes back to Purgatory, Mapicc somewhere "special", and Spoke stays. Jamato gives him iron armour and knocks him off the base; he clutches with powdered snow.

### The desert quarter

- The last inner walls fall. In a crowded base, Dylan catches him sneaking in, shows him his new army, and slowly admits: Jamato gave him all this on condition he kills Spoke, or his army kills him. Spoke escapes, Dylan apologising as he chases, and hides underground until they leave. His mace scares off a netherite player.

### The colosseum

- Mapicc stands in a colosseum. Spoke begs for help; a portal opens in the stands and Jamato, who can't wait to watch, says everyone has their price. Mapicc attacks.
- Building a wall between them and Jamato, Mapicc writes a book out of Jamato's sight and throws it to Spoke with pearls and wind charges: bring the fight close to Jamato and mace him. Spoke pretends to go for the kill, webs Mapicc at one heart without finishing him, then wind-charges and pearls onto Jamato and maces him. Jamato's totem pops, portals open everywhere, and the guards surround them.
- Cornered, Spoke tells Jamato he knows why he hasn't been killed: killing him would spark the biggest revolution yet, and Jamato would lose everything. The games are meant to break Spoke, and they won't.
- On the watchtower roof, by a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md), Jamato orders him back to Spawn: he breaks everything in Purgatory. Try anything, and his friends die. Spoke throws one pearl in, says goodbye, and quietly throws a second. The first lands him on a rooftop in [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), among Wemmbu's destruction; the second brings him back to the arena.
- Spoke digs in and starts a secret base. A portal opens behind him: NULL's commander with an underscore name ([Commander](../../characters/commander.md)), offering to tell him who NULL really are.

## Items & mechanics

- Copper chests: the arena's loot boxes.
- Bounty books: Jamato's rule-breaking contracts.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Spoke's mace, kept safe by Jumper, used on Jamato.
- Gravel inner walls: dropped by horn to open new quarters.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): Jamato's offer of a way home, and Spoke's double-pearl trick.

## Outcomes

- found2 is killed on Jamato's bounty; Matrix escapes.
- Mapicc reconciles with Spoke in secret; Jumper is returned to Purgatory and Mapicc taken elsewhere.
- Spoke stays in Purgatory, and the Commander approaches him.
