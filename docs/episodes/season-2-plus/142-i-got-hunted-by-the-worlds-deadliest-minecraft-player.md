## Summary

[TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md) keeps returning for a rematch, and keeps leaving whenever there's no one to watch. While [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) gets his ruined base rebuilt, using a LAW certificate to get stash locations from the jailed [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md), he and the God of War fight at LAW's base, at a remote stash, and finally at Flame's new base. Flame wins, spares him, and asks to be trained by him.

## Full breakdown

### Interruptions

- Flame sits in his ruined base with [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md) and [NotSkrilley](../../characters/notskrilley.md) when the God of War arrives for a rematch. Spongs keeps interfering until both fighters agree he's ruined it, and the God of War leaves, promising to return.
- Flame needs his base rebuilt. [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md) says LAW is short on resources, but has stashes from Lettuce's era that only Lettuce and [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) knew; Flame should ask Lettuce, who Sarge didn't know had been freed. He gives Flame a LAW certificate.
- The God of War reappears: he rose through the ranks by fighting, and he's here to reclaim his strength. Sarge says Flame is LAW's ally, then starts carting Flame and his own lawmen; more lawmen arrive and the God of War calls it another wasted opportunity. Flame tells Sarge not everyone needs a combat speciality, and thanks him for nothing.

### Lettuce

- On the way, Flame catches Skrilley up on the server's lore after his time in the Mist: Lettuce founded LAW and created the election that gave the server a king.
- In his cell, Lettuce asks what happened in the war. Parrot is exiled and Saparata is king, which Lettuce half expected: Parrot meant well but didn't plan enough. Lettuce was jailed again for not fully endorsing Cindercrest.
- The God of War already came asking for the stashes. Lettuce believes a former commander over Flame, until Flame shows the certificate, and realises he's been played. He gives the nearest location, still very far from Spawn.

### The stash

- Arriving on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), Flame is attacked by guards who still blame him for the thousand dead; the news hasn't reached this far, Skrilley notes. The certificate convinces them; a commander is supposedly already there.
- The God of War appears on the fountain: he overheard Flame and Sarge and got here first to catch him off guard. With no distractions, they fight. He summons a dog army, and Flame matches it with his own [wolves](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md). His opponent's dogs lack fire resistance, so Flame sets them alight to break their armour, and summons another wave, taunting him for copying a strategy he doesn't fully understand.
- Skrilley finishes looting and says there's no point fighting with no one to see it. Flame asks if he wants to continue; the God of War says no and flies off.

### The new base

- A lawman thanks them for the resources: both LAW HQ and Flame's base will be rebuilt. Flame goes AFK until Spongs and Skrilley wake him to a brand-new base, including a downstairs bedroom Spongs commissioned for himself.
- The God of War appears on the elevator and orders the others to leave within two minutes, which they do. He left before because there were no witnesses, which made it pointless.
- Flame enters the zone, and they fight from Spongs's bedroom to the outside and all the way to LAW HQ. Flame doesn't want to kill him. The God of War promised to return when he was ready to win, and failed again; he's improved, but Flame has improved more. He gives Flame permission to kill him.
- Flame picks up his sword, but spares him: he respects his strength and how he sticks to his standards, and asks the God of War to teach him how he fights. Shots of the God of War's base, including unseen areas, show he's taken Flame in.

## Items & mechanics

- LAW certificate: Sarge's proof that Flame is an ally.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): both fighters' dog armies; fire breaks the God of War's dogs' armour.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Sarge's carts, which kill his own lawmen.
- The zone: Flame's focus in the final fight.

## Outcomes

- Flame's base and LAW HQ are rebuilt from Lettuce-era stashes.
- Flame beats TheG0dofWar and becomes his student.
