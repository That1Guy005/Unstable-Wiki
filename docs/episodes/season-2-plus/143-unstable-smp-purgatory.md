## Summary

Hidden under the arena, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) is approached by NULL's [Commander](../../characters/commander.md), who betrays [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) because NULL should be led by the strongest. Commander reveals the Totem of NULL controls the organisation. Spoke builds an army in the Hunger Games with [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), is caught and nearly executed, then challenges Jamato for the totem. Jamato destroys [Purgatory](../../places/purgatory.md) with his wither cannon and holds Mapicc hostage. Spoke knocks Mapicc into the void, takes the totem, and becomes leader of [NULL](../../factions/null.md).

## Full breakdown

### The Commander

- Over 10,000 players are trapped in Purgatory, and Spoke means to free them all. After tricking Jamato's pearl cannon, he's hidden under the arena when the nameless Commander steps out of a portal: Jamato doesn't know Spoke is here. Commander wants to help, and there's a lot about NULL Spoke doesn't know.
- Commander has him strip his armour to stay anonymous while NULL guards clear all player builds and rebuild the arena, as they've done before. He's been watching Spoke defy everything Jamato threw at him; Jamato is a fool to think Spoke left.
- NULL works differently: its leader is its strongest, and Jamato has fallen from what he was. Spoke is the first to outsmart him. Commander wants only the strongest to lead NULL. He hides Spoke in the rarely visited stony mountains, helps build a bunker, gives him five invisibility potions, and says to fire a firework to call him. Once the games start, Spoke is on his own.

### Diamond armour

- Keeping his name out of death messages, Spoke can't use his mace; he kills with lava instead. He gets diamond boots and a helmet, avoids a diamond-legged player's team, and targets a full-diamond player in a large team: a dripstone kill on a teammate fails when the loot is stashed, so he spleefs the target into a basement trap and lava-kills him, getting leggings and boots but losing the chestplate and his lava. Another chestplate slips away too. Out of invisibility, he digs in and drinks his last.
- Back at the bunker he calls Commander, claiming "full" diamond without a chestplate. He wants an opportunity above Jamato to mace him. Commander says he can't take Jamato alone, even with a mace: he needs an army. And he needs a farm; Commander will be away.
- Out of invisibility, Spoke pearls around, breaks something that turns out not to be grass, and steals seeds from a team.

### The totem

- Commander, replacing the bunker floor, brings bread, invisibility, blocks and one wither skull. The enchanted totem controls NULL. NULL has existed since the server began, led by whoever is strongest; Jamato took control by outsmarting and killing the previous leader. If the totem pops, every NULL stasis chamber is sent into the void. And there are players in the arena Spoke will recognise.

### Recruiting

- Spoke lava-kills a full-diamond player chasing Mapicc, then, on signs, leads Mapicc to the mountains, where Mapicc assumes he's Jumper until Spoke reveals himself. They kill players who may have heard his voice.
- They find Jumper in a tree base with two teammates; Spoke sets it on fire while Mapicc introduces him as his "little brother", then "older sister", since he doesn't have a younger brother. Jumper recruits; Spoke and Mapicc kill for diamond armour. Back at the bunker, about thirty people are waiting, told their leader is Mapicc's little brother. Spoke sends them mining. Jumper asks why they haven't gone to Spawn; they have to kill Jamato first.
- Commander says the walls will drop soon, a chance to recruit. They must mine diamonds and go through the portal to challenge Jamato for the totem. He gives Spoke a maxed firework crossbow: time is short, and Spoke can't rely too much on his friends.
- Recruiting turns into killing refusers. When Spoke's invisibility briefly runs out, he kills everyone in sight, supporters included, and chases a surviving witness. Commander warns his identity is out and he still has no army.

### Jumper's choice

- The base is empty. In the Mines of Doom, Jumper is helping everyone escape over the wall: why challenge Jamato if they can just leave? Spoke kills a player walking past, and tells her Spawn is in ruins; she says anything's better than Purgatory, and that they've only been lucky so far. She calls him insane and leaves.
- Spoke drops his caution, kills a netherite trio with his mace and no invisibility, is jumped by most of the sand biome, and kills them all. He tells the gathered army today they overthrow Jamato.
- A portal opens behind him. Jamato kills everyone on the spot with a blue wither-skull [Orbital Wither Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-wither-cannon.md), the deadliest weapon the server has seen, takes Spoke through the portal, and has him drop all his items, mace included, to Commander.

### The pressure plate

- In the execution room, Spoke stands on a stone pressure plate; step off and an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) fires. Everyone leaves. A firework goes off: Commander has rigged the cannon. His army is bridging towards him, and a shulker holds all his gear and a book: meet at the centre for one final challenge.

### The challenge

- At the centre, Spoke challenges Jamato for the totem. Jamato expects a 3v1, but Commander turns on him. Spoke pops ____Y____ repeatedly until their helmet breaks and kills them.
- Jamato says there's always another plan, and his wither cannon destroys the entire prison. Through the lag, everyone falls beneath the platform; Jamato lands on a block just above the void, surrounded by Spoke, Commander and NULL's army.
- Jamato's final trick: a portal, and Mapicc. Give up, or Mapicc dies. Spoke apologises, says he'll see Mapicc another time, and wind-charges him into the void, presumably into [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md). Jamato is stunned that Spoke seems to have no empathy left.
- They seal the void. All Jamato ever wanted was the old Spoke, the one he'd have gone to the ends of the earth for. Spoke says Jamato doesn't want to die, because his work and the server's history would be erased with him. Jamato surrenders his gear and the totem, and Spoke repeats Jamato's own words back: this is a new era on the server.
- Spoke tells Commander to take care of Jamato, and pillars away from the void as the new leader of NULL, the Totem of NULL in his inventory.

## Items & mechanics

- [Totem of NULL](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): the enchanted totem that controls NULL and its stases.
- [Orbital Wither Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-wither-cannon.md): Jamato's blue wither-skull cannon, which kills Spoke's army and destroys Purgatory.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the pressure-plate execution, rigged by Commander.
- Lava bucket: Spoke's anonymous killing tool.
- Maxed firework crossbow: Commander's gift.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): the blow that knocks Mapicc into the void.
