## Summary

[TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md) agrees to train [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) if Flame helps him retake the civilization of former LAW elites that exiled him. Fighting through its arenas of champions, Flame learns spear-swapping and argues about what a true fighter is, but the God of War is killed by an arrow cannon. Flame faces the new ruler, [Antaggg](../../characters/antaggg.md), whose beacon tricks and debuffs let him flee and claim victory.

## Full breakdown

### The deal

- After LAW's defeat, the God of War's base became a civilization, which overthrew and exiled him. He'll train Flame if Flame helps take it back, after testing his sword skill; Flame combos him five times. The members, he warns, will do anything to win.
- They kill the first pair of champions, and the crowd mocks them. The God of War teaches spear-swapping. Flame asks why they fight champions while everyone else just watches: the civilization is made of LAW elites, once more loyal to him than to LAW, who believe in total strength. When he showed weakness and kept losing, they lost faith, until Antaggg's warriors beat and exiled him, believing strength comes from victories and the power of weapons. Swiftness potions will help next.

### The second arena

- One champion has Turtle Master, the other an elytra and a [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md). Flame counters the elytra-spear user by placing blocks in his flight path and holding out his own spear as he dives; the God of War finishes them. Flame burns the elytra, then regrets not selling it.
- Two members tell them they're getting weaker and don't understand fighting: the point is to win. True fighters don't take shortcuts, Flame and the God of War say; they rely on skill. Standards only bend if both sides agree.
- The God of War asks if Flame can use crossbow carts for the next round. Flame thanks him: every past trainer misunderstood his path to grandmaster; the God of War feels the same.

### The third arena

- Three champions, one with a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). Flame beats the mace-wielder, and takes one of the God of War's opponents when his mentor runs low. The last champion calls in henchmen; Flame runs out of carts and potions, and the God of War's helmet breaks. As he beats down the champion, the champion kills him with an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md).
- With no other chance, Flame uses the dead champion's mace to kill them all. The crowd mocks him and the God of War's death; furious, Flame burns the mace, and vows to retake the civilization for him.

### Antaggg

- At the last arena, Antaggg's henchmen raise a full Jump Boost beacon. When Flame pops his first totem, Antaggg doubts his combat master title, then has his henchmen give Flame mining fatigue and weakness while Antaggg gets haste.
- Flame turns it around with carts. Antaggg runs, claims victory, and boasts to the jeering crowd that he's the combat master. Flame vows a rematch, for the God of War.

## Items & mechanics

- [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md): spear-swapping, and the counter to elytra-spear dives.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the weapon that kills the God of War.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): taken from a champion, used once, then burned.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): burned after the second arena.
- Beacons and debuffs: Antaggg's Jump Boost, mining fatigue, weakness and haste tricks.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): crossbow carts against Antaggg.

## Outcomes

- TheG0dofWar is killed.
- Antaggg flees and claims victory; Flame vows revenge.
