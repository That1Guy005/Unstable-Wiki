## Summary

[Antaggg](../../characters/antaggg.md) invites [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) to a PvP tournament. Flame wins every round in arenas suspiciously suited to his opponents, befriends [Falconu](../../characters/falconu.md), once Lettuce's first second-in-command, and reaches a final duel with Antaggg under strict rules, then loses. Falconu reveals Antaggg cheated by secretly swapping places with another player, and offers Flame a deal for a fair fight.

## Full breakdown

### The rules

- Flame comes to avenge [TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md). A diamond-armoured player gives him a tour to Antaggg's meeting, where entrants sign a book agreeing to the rules, which seem reasonable.
- Round one, a castle arena: a plain sword fight against WitherSt0rm_, which Flame wins.
- Antaggg escorts him to round two, a pit of rocks and pools, where Flame falls and pops a totem on landing. LordWhirlwind uses a [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) for speed; Flame is annoyed the arena suits him, but wins.

### Falconu

- At a break by the fountain, other contestants mock Flame for having nothing special in his style. Falconu points out that with the same kit, Flame would beat them all, and the mocking stops.
- They realise they've met: Falconu was Lettuce's first second-in-command. He's here not to fight but to gather information on Antaggg, who's been terrorising his new kingdom. Flame is here for the God of War.

### The semi-final and final

- Round three, a deep dark arena against PhantomZONE. Antaggg's only introduction is "FlameFrags from the mesa", and Flame can't think of anything better. A warden spawns and targets Flame; he kills it, and PhantomZONE, and reaches the final, to Antaggg's astonishment.
- In the final, Flame and Smokebomb_ (who beat Falconu) each pick a teammate: Flame picks Falconu out of respect; Smokebomb picks ItzSharpen. Falconu warns that Smokebomb mixes maces with TNT carts. When Flame complains about the arenas, Falconu is confused: only Flame's arenas have been changing each round. In a volcano arena, Smokebomb and ItzSharpen surrender.

### Antaggg

- The winner earns a fight with Antaggg. Remembering his tricks, Flame and Falconu ask for rules: no restocking from ender chests or shulkers, and only two stacks of XP bottles. Antaggg agrees.
- Flame leads by a large margin, but as it drags on he's drained while Antaggg seemingly isn't. His armour breaks and he surrenders to a smug Antaggg.
- With only his chestplate left, Flame is stopped at the docks by Falconu, who stayed to watch: every time Antaggg disappeared, he was swapping places with another player. Flame was winning, but outsmarted. To beat Antaggg, they need to catch him outside his own environment when he least expects it.
- Falconu asks Flame for help with something, in exchange for guaranteeing a fair fight with Antaggg. Flame agrees.

## Items & mechanics

- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): LordWhirlwind's speed in the rock arena.
- Rotating arenas: chosen to favour Flame's opponents.
- Tournament rule book: signed by every entrant.
- Body-double swap: Antaggg's hidden cheat in the final.

## Outcomes

- Flame wins the tournament bracket but loses the final to Antaggg's cheating.
- Flame allies with Falconu against Antaggg.
