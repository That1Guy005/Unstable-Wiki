## Summary

Now leader of [NULL](../../factions/null.md), [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) invades the End, defeats [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md)'s guardians, and builds the [End Empire](../../places/end-empire.md) in its southern quadrant. When Minute destroys the End fountain to trap NULL inside, Spoke wipes out civilization after civilization for the four end crystals needed to respawn the dragon, forcing [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md) to build wither cannons. Jamato vanishes with the last crystal, and leaves a book: "Goodbye".

## Full breakdown

### The invasion

- A month after winning Purgatory, Spoke has NULL dismantle the Hunger Games arena and rebuild the pearl cannon to a village above a stronghold. The [Commander](../../characters/commander.md) has its civilians killed with NULL's powers.
- At the End portal, Spoke worries about facing the End's guardian, once his friend. They throw harming potions and arrows in first. Minute, barricading the entrance, warns an attack is coming, then realises Spoke is the attack. He refuses to give up a quadrant, and NULL kills all his guardians. Spoke spares Minute and claims the southern quadrant.

### Building the empire

- NULL mines out the islands to terraform. Spoke shows the captive Jamato what he's done with NULL and boasts he'll surpass him. Jamato doesn't think Spoke can lead an army this big, says he sounds just like him, and warns he's given Minute time to prepare.
- At a nearby under-geared colony, NULL kills everyone. Minute arrives to defend what's left, and asks what happened to the Spoke he knew. Minute was never there when Spoke needed him, Spoke says, and everything he's been through led here. Minute flies off and leaves him the End.
- The first tower and platform are done, but Jamato's cannons destroyed many resources, so Spoke plans to invade the Overworld too.

### Trapped

- At the main island, the End fountain is gone. Minute's book: he destroyed it; if he can't protect the End, he'll protect the rest of the server. They're trapped unless they can respawn the dragon with end crystals.
- The lockdown affects everyone in the End, so civilizations are pooling resources. NULL splits up to search them. Spoke's group, armed with firework crossbows, destroys a village and kills everyone, and finds no crystal.
- Too slow on foot, Spoke has Jamato's guards build a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md).

### VoidMoon

- On a ship-town, leader VoidMoon knows who he is and offers her crystal if he finishes a parkour course with no items, ending in a near-impossible triple neo between shelves. While Spoke rages, an invisible NULL soldier places blocks on the far side where she can't see, and Spoke "makes" the jump. She keeps her word; Spoke has NULL kill the civilians. VoidMoon escapes with pearls she stole from him.

### The wither cannons

- Jamato accidentally gives Spoke an idea: make him build [wither cannons](../../mechanics/orbital-wither-cannon.md). Spoke takes him by pearl cannon to a remote island and tells him he stays there with guards until ten are built. Jamato stands at the edge of the void and refuses, then obeys.

### MinerMan

- For the next crystal, Spoke disguises NULL as friendly, poor players. At a city, he flatters its leader MinerMan and his pet sniffer Frank ("the real leader"), claims his group is redesigning the central island, and pressures him into producing the crystal from his ender chest. When MinerMan refuses to hand it over, Spoke orders a massacre, kills Frank, then MinerMan. A soldier picks up the crystal and only hands it over when Spoke threatens to pop the totem. The city is torn down to its end stone.

### "The Tomb"

- Jamato's guards have built a little town in their free time. Jamato has built one cannon, not ten; when Spoke presses its buttons, the wither skulls vanish and the withers escape. Spoke kills them and screams at Jamato for sabotage.
- The Commander has found Jamato's stash: books documenting everything he's discovered on the server. Spoke doesn't care about the server's history, but Jamato does, so if the cannons aren't built, the books burn. He burns three and throws one into the void.
- Most were boring, except "The Tomb": nine pages of scrambled words and one page reading "but why?" Jamato says it isn't for his eyes. Spoke calls Jamato his greatest creation as he leaves.

### TimeDeo

- The Commander finds Minute heading for a crystal. Spoke goes alone and finds Minute negotiating with TimeDeo, a trader of rare things; Minute stops Spoke from killing him. Spoke offers his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), which Minute won't match, and gets the third crystal. Having set no stasis, he faces a long walk until Deo points him to a nearby end gateway.
- The Commander agrees trading the mace was right in a crisis. Jamato has finished the cannons. Villages are empty: Minute is gathering everyone in a bunker. Spoke orders every city destroyed until they get the last crystal.

### The bunker

- VoidMoon says nobody there has a crystal; go away. They're pawns in his way, Spoke says, and fires the wither cannons on the bunker. She calls him a psychopath; a random NULL member kills her. No items survive.

### Goodbye

- Jamato has gone missing, along with his NULL escort, after the Commander called him back without Spoke's order. On Spoke's throne is a chest with Jamato's book, titled with coordinates on the spawn island: he has the last crystal; come alone or he destroys it.
- Paranoid, Spoke accuses the Commander of working with Jamato, which he denies. Spoke goes alone, with all the wither cannons aimed at the coordinates. Under the block is the final crystal and a book, "Goodbye": Spoke has won; every player is dead and every civilization eradicated; take the crystal and finish what he started.
- Spoke spawns the dragon, and Minute maces him from above. Minute didn't expect Spoke to work with someone he thought was dead: Jamato gave Minute a book too, hoping Minute would kill Spoke. They both admit they don't want to kill each other and never wanted this. The wither cannon goes off, to Minute's shock. Minute breaks Spoke's boots and helmet; Spoke says Minute has already lost, with no one left in the End to protect.
- The Commander pulls Spoke's stasis. Gather the army, regear him fully: they're heading to Spawn.
- Minute kills the dragon. He doesn't have time to destroy the portal again, and he can't stop Spoke and NULL alone: he has the perfect player in mind, and returns to Spawn.

## Items & mechanics

- End crystals: four needed to respawn the dragon and escape the locked End.
- [Orbital Wither Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-wither-cannon.md): built by Jamato under threat, used on the bunker and at the spawn island.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): NULL's fast travel between End islands.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): traded to TimeDeo for the third crystal.
- Jamato's books: his record of the server, including "The Tomb" and "Goodbye".
- [Totem of NULL](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): Spoke's threat to make a soldier hand over a crystal.

## Outcomes

- The End's civilizations are wiped out; VoidMoon and MinerMan are killed (see [NULL's Conquest of the End](../../events/nulls-conquest-of-the-end.md)).
- Jamato escapes with his escort.
- The dragon is respawned and killed, and NULL heads for the Overworld.
