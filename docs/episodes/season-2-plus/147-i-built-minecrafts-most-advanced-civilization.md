## Summary

Exiled by Saparata, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) founds a new civilization in a jungle with PestoPasta2009. Following clues to a zombie villager hidden by [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), Parrot cures it and sells its mending books, builds an "immortality machine" to win a battle against [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md), and loses Pesto, who sacrifices himself to save the villager. Then his own trade partner pulls his stasis, and [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) takes him prisoner.

## Full breakdown

### Pesto

- Thousands of blocks out, Parrot builds his first house in a small jungle, and meets the friendly PestoPasta2009. Leo, after losing everything, hid villagers around the server; Pesto is searching Mount 3-12 for one, though ClownPierce has killed most of them.
- Leo was Parrot's friend before he mysteriously disappeared, so Parrot invites Pesto to join him. He shows him a redstone-free, two-person automatic wheat farm that moves AFK players with water streams.
- The mountaintop is empty. Thinking like Leo, Parrot remembers BAT's original base was a perfect figure eight, notices a figure-eight lake, and digs down in the middle of it: the villager. Leo would hide it somewhere meaningless to anyone who didn't know him.

### The stolen villager

- It's a zombie villager that needs curing. After AFKing the farm, they find it stolen. Pesto knows another group hunting it, on a cliff-walled island, whose leader denies everything while holding Parrot's netherite axe.
- Too under-geared to fight, Parrot plans a trap: he crafts a brush, shows Pesto how to brush suspicious sand in desert temples for TNT, and crafts TNT minecarts. Standing in a dip while the team surrounds them, Parrot gives them one last chance, then detonates a cart, killing two of four; the other two jump off the island. The villager is atop a lighthouse.

### The civilization

- They bury the villager in a hole near the base. Pesto recruits twenty-odd players and Parrot gives each a role: a copper-golem item sorter, a bigger wheat farm, diamond armour, a maxed enchanting table.
- Parrot announces a trip to the Nether, feared because ClownPierce's team kills on sight. When he reveals the villager, Beec4n doesn't believe it, so Parrot shows him, against Pesto's advice. Saparata took everything from Bee and chased him for a week; Bee pledges loyalty and convinces the others.
- Pesto asks why they need other players. On a mountaintop, Parrot quotes the proverb about planting trees whose shade you'll never sit in; Pesto jokingly plants a sapling. Parrot explains: societies grow great when everyone works for the common good.

### The Nether

- At a fortress with working blaze spawners, two of Clown's guards attack. Too strong to fight directly, they're lured in front of an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) Parrot builds while his team distracts them. Both guards die, and so does Beec4n.
- Pesto hid instead of fighting. He admits he's scared of dying. Everyone was scared, Parrot says, but only Pesto hid; when it counts, you have to help.

### Mending books

- Parrot cures the villager and gets mending trades. At the Vael Company near Spawn, built on top of an End portal, DukeVael agrees a deal: every day at noon Duke pulls Parrot's pearl, Parrot hands over 23 mending books, gets his share, and leaves through the End portal. On the other side they find carnage, and a NULL member watches them enter.
- Teams form for mining, building, exploring and innovation. PhillipBuzzstar2 challenges Parrot for leader; Parrot calmly agrees, does no campaigning, tells everyone to vote for Phillip if they really want to, and wins by a landslide. He's just over elections.

### The immortality machine

- Phillip confesses: furious after the election, he ran to Spawn and told Saparata everything, including the villager. An army is coming. Parrot forgives him; the day was coming anyway, and he warned them.
- The civilians prepare defences and gather gold and redstone. To give them an edge, Parrot builds an "immortality machine": a glitch that makes a totem placed in a barrel duplicate on your screen, so you hold it in both places; redstone by the barrel detects the item, and when the totem pops and disappears from the barrel, it pulls a linked stasis. He builds one for everyone.
- Pesto is still hesitant. Parrot gives him a choice: get a totem from the raid farm and fight, or be considered gone; if he fights, meet on top of a tree. Cindercrest's horn sounds, and Pesto doesn't come.
- Saparata's army scales the walls, and the machine wins the battle. In a field, Saparata admits defeat, and laughs: Parrot has lost what matters most.

### Pesto's sacrifice

- The villager is gone from the raid farm. A sign says "they took the villager go straight", and a trail of saplings topped with string leads Parrot on, to an invisible Pesto: he'd gone for a totem, caught Cindercrest stealing the villager, and followed, leaving the trail.
- Cindercrest plans to load it onto a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md) from a scaffolding tower. Parrot would rather kill the villager than let them have it. They plan to break the scaffolding once all three climb, but only the one carrying the villager does.
- Pesto asks if Parrot still has a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and is a good shot. He'll place the cart by the two Cindercrest members and the scaffolding. Parrot refuses, since Pesto would die; Pesto refuses his totem too, in case Parrot is discovered. If Parrot doesn't shoot, Pesto says, he's the selfish one. Parrot fires, killing both Cindercrest members, Pesto, and the third as the scaffolding breaks.
- Parrot brings the villager home and buries Pesto by the tree he planted.

### ClownPierce

- Parrot hires [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md), reluctant since Parrot never pays, until he sees the mending book profits, and Horace builds a giant city.
- Exploring it, Parrot's pearl is pulled. DukeVael apologises and backs away. Clown descends: two of his guards went missing weeks ago, and right after, the market flooded with mending books. Parrot refuses to reveal the villager. Clown takes him into custody: everyone breaks eventually.

## Items & mechanics

- Zombie villager: Leo's hidden villager, cured to trade mending books.
- Immortality machine: the totem-in-barrel duplication glitch linked to stasis chambers ([Exploits](../../mechanics/exploits.md)).
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): brushed-for TNT, the lake island trap, and Pesto's sacrifice.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the Nether fortress ambush.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the immortality machine, and Duke's daily pearl pull.
- Brush: finding TNT in suspicious sand.

## Outcomes

- Parrot's new civilization beats Cindercrest's army.
- Pesto, Beec4n and several Cindercrest members die.
- Parrot is captured by ClownPierce, setting up [Trapped in the Underworld](../../events/trapped-in-the-underworld.md).
