## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) leads [NULL](../../factions/null.md) back into the Overworld to seize every End portal, building a vast Purgatory-like capital. Following Jamato's book on "the Wraith", he finds only [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md), who warns of an army that outmatches him. Mysterious invisible players start destroying NULL's strongholds, [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) reappears asking him to pop the totem, and Spoke razes the Vael Company and sets off alone for [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md).

## Full breakdown

### The portals

- NULL rolls TNT minecarts through the exit portal to destroy Cindercrest's Spawn trap, then emerges invisibly at a rebuilt Spawn. Their first goal: secure every End portal. Eyes of ender lead them to a Cindercrest fortress over the nearest one. When the guard won't open the gate, the [Commander](../../characters/commander.md) portals behind him, kills him and opens it; NULL kills everyone and levels the place.
- Spoke shows the Commander a current-seeming Jamato book: "The Wraith", an assassin who has killed many kings and leaders and never failed. It includes coordinates. The Commander builds him a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) and NULL builds a base while he's away.

### The Wraith's lair

- On an island Spoke finds familiar craters and a cave full of signs of a fight. He chases an invisible player into a large building, through a long hall into a maze, which he hates, sets off traps, mines through the walls, and follows signs of fighting up a staircase to a room with a statue.
- An invisible player reveals himself: Jamato. There are threats Spoke hasn't met: an army that already outmatches him. Spoke throws "The Wraith" into lava. Jamato isn't here to help: NULL has no idea what's coming, and Spoke doesn't even know what's happening to himself. Spoke pops Jamato's totem and he vanishes. The invisible player watches from atop the statue.

### The capital

- Pulled back by stasis, Spoke runs off the platform and water-clutches; the Commander points out whose fault that was. The Cindercrest fortress is gone, replaced by a giant city with a skull entrance like Purgatory's, built big enough to live in and hold prisoners. NULL isn't that evil, the Commander says.
- The tour: storage, the End portal, and a meeting room with a redstone lamp for every captured End portal.

### Jumper

- A visitor has been waiting: Jumper. Still sore that she took his army, Spoke asks how she found him and why she's here; she only says "you know me". He punches her into a corner and boxes her in. The last friend he let live turned on him; they could execute, imprison or interrogate her. Interrogate first.
- She's half-dug into the wall when the Commander sees six lamps go dark: six strongholds lost at once. Spoke has her guarded.

### The invisible attackers

- The Spawn fortress is empty, its forty NULL gone. Whoever did it can't have left. Invisible diamond-armoured players jump them; NULL drives them off and kills a few without losses. Then a Cindercrest army marches up to retake the castle, [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) kills a few NULL, and they escape through the portal. Cindercrest doesn't follow.
- Spoke thinks the two groups are unrelated: Cindercrest just wants its territory back, while the invisible players are the threat Jamato meant. NULL has been too aggressive; they'll leave Spawn's portals alone and avoid a two-front war.
- Jumper is in an obsidian box in Spoke's chambers, still answering "you know me". Spoke has a NULL member bring water and drowns her for about ten ticks of damage until she talks: she just wanted to see him, after escaping Purgatory. She asks how he got NULL; he won it despite her stealing his army, and wants the most powerful civilization ever. She's been at Spawn "not that long". He's in a war, and her timing is awkward.

### The pearl cannon trap

- Heading for the second-to-last stronghold, Spoke spots an invisible player among his team. They run and fire the cannon, teleporting everyone into an obsidian box with wardens and dripstone. Spoke escapes; his whole team dies. He chases the player through caves, takes their gear, and asks who their leader is: they're from Purgatory. He kills them mid-sentence on a second sign.
- Back at base, Jumper is gone. Besides Spawn's, one portal is left, where Spoke expects to meet the invisible team's leader.

### The Vael Company

- At the Vael Company, DukeVael says NULL isn't welcome. Spoke calls out the invisible player beside him: Jumper. She helped him because she saw a good heart, now blackened. It's not too late: pop the Totem of NULL, and don't make choices he can't undo. Spoke decides she must be working with Jamato. She betrayed him twice; he's willing to kill her.
- He orders the "strike": Nether portals open around the base and release TNT minecarts. NULL storms in, and Spoke kills several players as a beautiful build is wrecked. Jumper begs him to stop killing innocents and tries to destroy NULL's TNT; she's saving something already destroyed, he says, and orders her killed. She escapes through the portal, saying she was wrong that he ever had a good heart.
- NULL kills the owners of the stasis chambers they find. Using the TNT strike again is too dangerous: the Nether's players are too hostile, and will have to be dealt with eventually.
- In private, Spoke tells the Commander he wants to understand Jamato's book "The Tomb", which may hold the power to control the server. He'll go to the Underworld alone, while the Commander holds the End portals.

## Items & mechanics

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): rolled through the exit portal, and released en masse in the "strike".
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): NULL's travel, hijacked by an invisible saboteur.
- Portal lamps: redstone lamps tracking each captured End portal.
- Jamato's books: "The Wraith" (burned) and "The Tomb".
- [Totem of NULL](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): what Jumper begs him to pop.

## Outcomes

- NULL captures most of the End portals, but loses six strongholds to unknown invisible attackers.
- Spoke's team dies in the warden trap; the Vael Company is destroyed.
- Jumper breaks with Spoke; Spoke heads for [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md) (see [Trapped in the Underworld](../../events/trapped-in-the-underworld.md)).
