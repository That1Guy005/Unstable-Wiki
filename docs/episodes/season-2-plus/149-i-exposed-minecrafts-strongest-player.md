## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [Falconu](../../characters/falconu.md) join the Canary civilization in its war with [Antaggg](../../characters/antaggg.md)'s Warriors, determined to expose Antaggg's cheating. Ambushes, hostages, a TNT-duper raid and a deadly exchange go Antaggg's way, until Flame pulls Antaggg's hidden stasis for a forced 1v1 and Antaggg's own arrow cannon pops him. The Warriors defect, and Flame, avenging [TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md) at last, heads home with Falconu.

## Full breakdown

### Canary

- Antaggg cheated both times and built a reputation on it. Falconu brings Flame to the Canary civilization, at war with Antaggg's Warriors; LeaderCanary welcomes Flame, who sees a route to a fight. Antaggg delivers a final warning: surrender or be taken over. LeaderCanary is afraid; Flame says Antaggg isn't as strong as he claims. They follow the route of a scout group that never returned, sent off with LeaderCanary's battle cry.

### The ambush

- A "stray scout" leads them to the Warriors' base, actually a Warrior wearing a dead scout's armour. Antaggg's fireworks start the ambush and he leaves for the bigger war. Against numbers and a mace user, Flame pearls out of range and pulls a wolf rod; the invisible dogs win the fight. Two Warriors flee.
- Invisible in Warrior armour, Flame follows one on a spare horse, rides alongside him on his camel towards their main base, then forces him back to camp. He and Falconu take two Warriors hostage.

### Antaggg's base

- Scouting alone and invisible, Flame overhears that Antaggg needs TNT and slime for a contraption. On his throne, asked if Flame is better than him, Antaggg says he never claimed to be more skilled, just smarter. In the library, Flame watches Smokebomb_ use a secret bookshelf entrance, and finds two hidden stasis chambers: Antaggg's and Smokebomb's.
- One hostage eventually says Antaggg plans to destroy Canary with TNT dupers and they must evacuate. Nobody listens until LeaderCanary's battle cry. In the new town, wardens drop from the sky and buildings are griefed; most players die, flee or are killed by Warriors, and Flame digs an escape route for a handful of survivors.

### The exchange

- Falconu blames himself; Flame says not to, Antaggg took one of his friends too. A messenger offers an exchange: Antaggg's two Warriors for captured Canary citizens. LeaderCanary sees a death trap; Falconu won't leave citizens to die, and Flame respects him enough to go too, with three of Canary's elite fighters.
- On a walled island, Flame frees the Warriors, and Antaggg sets off a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) trap under the prisoners, killing them all. Antaggg's Warriors kill Flame's squad.
- In matching trims and invisible, Flame and Falconu fake a "banana cart" (one player places the cart, the other fires a flaming arrow) as a distraction, since Flame can cart alone. Flame webs Smokebomb, blows him up and takes his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). The stragglers flee, and with them Flame's chance at Antaggg.
- A new wave arrives and Falconu's armour breaks, exactly as the God of War's did before the arrow cannon killed him. An [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) fires, killing Warriors and popping Flame's totem, and they flee. Whenever Antaggg is beaten, he has a backup plan.

### One word

- Back at Canary, LeaderCanary has them describe each other in one word. Falconu says "strength": Flame keeps persevering in terrible situations, fought a tournament for a friend and a war that wasn't his, all while keeping his standards. Flame says "selfless": Falconu always has people's best interests at heart, and this was about helping him too.
- LeaderCanary says they've already done more than he could ask, and despair is what Antaggg wants. Flame gives him Smokebomb's mace; LeaderCanary gives them an orange and a red flower.

### The last battle

- They set traps around the civilization. Antaggg, tired of trying to recruit them, comes to kill everyone, and says the Warriors lied about his plans. Falconu tells Flame to run past the fight and pull Antaggg's stasis for his 1v1; Canary will hold with its traps.
- Flame kills a witness on the way, walks past Antaggg's intimidated guards, and pulls the pearl. Antaggg, shocked, asks when Flame was ever there, and why he shouldn't just leave; because Antaggg needs victories, and running from a fight that hasn't happened is the opposite.
- Everything is on the line: a third failure would mean failing the God of War again and Canary's destruction. Minutes in, Flame says Antaggg is far worse than he thought; Antaggg pulls in two more Warriors, who die. Canary and some defeated Warriors arrive to watch.
- Antaggg signals the arrow cannon; Flame blocks himself in, and it pops Antaggg instead. Antaggg admits Flame caught him off guard, lets him keep Canary, takes offence at the suggestion he needed the win, calls his Warriors useless, and flies off.
- LeaderCanary gives a victory battle cry. The Warriors burn their shields and join Canary.
- Flame reflects that strength is what each player makes of it, different for everyone, and he's still learning what it means to him. LeaderCanary sends Falconu back to the main server with Flame to help people there, with a farewell battle cry.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): the wolf rod that breaks the ambush.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the trap under the hostages, and the "banana cart" bluff.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): Antaggg's backup plan, which finally hits Antaggg.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Antaggg's hidden stasis, pulled to force the duel.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Smokebomb's, given to LeaderCanary.
- TNT dupers and wardens: Antaggg's destruction of the evacuees' town.

## Outcomes

- Antaggg is beaten and flees; Canary survives and the Warriors defect.
- The Canary hostages, three elite fighters and Smokebomb_ die.
- Flame avenges TheG0dofWar, and returns to the main server with Falconu.
