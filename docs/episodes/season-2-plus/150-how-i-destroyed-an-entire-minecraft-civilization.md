## Summary

Betrayed by [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md), [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) gathers his purge survivors to free [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) and take the [Kingdom of the Caves](../../factions/kingdom-of-the-caves.md). They destroy the Wraiths' stasis vaults, Wemmbu settles things with [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) over the last pearls, and he humiliates Arachn1d in a duel, only for Arachn1d's hidden [thorns machine](../../mechanics/windscourge.md) to turn the kingdom against him. Wemmbu shuts it down, [Yungyx](../../characters/yungyx.md) kills Arachn1d, and beats Wemmbu for the crown.

## Full breakdown

### The stasis

- Thousands lived in the Kingdom of the Caves until the purge cleared out the weakest. Wemmbu means to kill Arachn1d, free Egg for good, and conquer the kingdom himself, with his purge team: Yungy, [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), The_Codmaster14 and The_BanditKing, all loyal and all betrayed.
- Arachn1d once set a stasis so Wemmbu could kill him if he lied. In a snowy mountain they pull it, and an unarmoured Arachn1d appears. Step down and live, Wemmbu says. Kill him and Egg's pearls go into the void, Arachn1d replies; forget this happened. Wemmbu attacks, Arachn1d pops a totem and stasises away, and TNT blows up the whole mountain.

### The Wraiths' vault

- Arachn1d announces they'll march on Spawn to take over Cindercrest and then the server. Codmaster points out only Wemmbu could pull that off. Instead, they'll free Egg: Yungy knows where the pearls are, guarded by the Wraith, but fifteen others would alert Arachn1d, who'd void Egg. Wemmbu's idea: deal with all of them at once. Yungy's price for helping: the crown.
- At an island castle, invisible, Yungy and Jaden put on armour (Jaden in Wemmbu's) so the Wraith thinks only two came, while Jaden plays pre-recorded Wemmbu lines. The real Wemmbu climbs for height and maces the invisible guard, and they build a bedrock breaker to destroy the pearl without teleporting Egg.
- Another Wraith approaches. Wemmbu copies the Wraiths' trims and goes invisible while the others hide, says on signs that bandits came by, and is taken to the Wraiths' facility to guard more stases. He screenshots its coordinates, has the gates opened, drinks milk, mines out through the last door and flies off.

### The carnival

- At a festival in the kingdom, a citizen talks them into an archery carnival game, secretly a scheme to collect their arrows. Arachn1d tells Wemmbu to prepare for war with Cindercrest; Wemmbu says he could have handled it already. They use the "preparation" as cover.
- The facility holds ten pearls and five are elsewhere. Yungy knows where; Codmaster, Jaden and BanditKing go for four in an old house guarded by mercenaries.
- Wemmbu and Yungy lure out the Wraith guards one by one, four with Yungy as bait, two inside with Wemmbu as bait and Yungy's [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md). Drinking milk against mining fatigue, they drop all ten pearls in the void.
- Only Jaden and Codmaster are back: Flame showed up at the entrance looking for Wemmbu, Jaden fought him and lost, and BanditKing ran off with Flame. BanditKing returns demanding Wemmbu's mace or he'll tell Arachn1d; Wemmbu hands over the unenchanted purge mace. They follow him invisibly, and he leads them to the four pearls, with Flame waiting.

### Flame

- Flame wants a 1v1, or he pulls the pearls. Wemmbu answers with a nuke shot. In the upside-down forest over the void, Wemmbu is losing badly, keeps trapping himself in Flame's cobwebs, doesn't restock or refill his water. Flame nearly falls into the void, refuses Wemmbu's blackmail, and spears out.
- Flame calls a truce: no fighting someone who isn't trying. He was always going to give the pearls back; friends don't take away each other's friends. Wemmbu promises a fair duel later.
- They've lost BanditKing, so Arachn1d probably knows. Flame agrees to help with Wemmbu's plan.

### The duel

- Before the castle, Arachn1d holds the final pearl in the open. Wemmbu dares him to touch Egg. Arachn1d pulls the pearl, and Wemmbu's fishing-rod stasis, linked to one of Egg's pearls in the forest, teleports Egg away instantly. Arachn1d orders everyone to attack; no one moves. His kingdom runs on power, Wemmbu says, and they're no longer sure who has it.
- Wemmbu maces him through the castle to the throne room and pops him repeatedly. Arachn1d leads him to a room of fifty-odd pearls, every one of them Egg's, and pulls one: Egg appears. Wemmbu alternates between keeping Arachn1d off Egg and pulling stases, handing Egg the remaining rods and wind charges as his own resources dwindle.
- With the last pearl pulled, he gives Egg invisibility and tells him to run. Arachn1d loses Egg; Wemmbu chases Arachn1d to the balcony. Arachn1d admits defeat: he should have built the kingdom on intelligence, not strength.

### The machine

- Arachn1d pulls a fishing rod and Wemmbu starts taking tick damage; another rod, and the whole kingdom except Egg and Flame takes it too. Kill the traitors, he orders, or suffer forever. The army obeys, and the damage stops for everyone but Wemmbu's group.
- It must be Thorns damage, and Wemmbu realises when it happened: the carnival arrows. Fighting through the purge arena with Flame carrying and Egg and Codmaster kept back, Wemmbu searches for something big and laggy, and under a lava lake finds the machine: wind charges, Thorns-armoured zombies and the collected arrows. He kills two guards, shuts off every circuit except BanditKing's, which he switches on.
- He gets everyone's attention "his way", by macing a player, and announces the machine is gone, so there's no reason to follow Arachn1d.

### The crown

- Under the throne, Arachn1d stands by the truly last pearl, in a machine that drops Egg into the void if pulled. He apologises: he'll hand it over and never come near Wemmbu again if Wemmbu leaves the kingdom alone. Before Wemmbu answers, Yungy charges in yelling and kills Arachn1d. Egg's stasis fires and Egg falls, onto black concrete, water-bucket-clutching: there was never a void.
- Yungy is happy he can rule; Wemmbu never agreed, and maybe wants it himself. They settle it with a duel using current inventories, though Wemmbu's is depleted.
- Flame, leaving, advises him to win respect without elytra-macing or tricks. Wemmbu elytra-maces anyway. Yungy holds on; Wemmbu's chestplate nearly breaks and he fights on without wearing his spare, runs out of golden apples, and concedes. Yungy is king.
- Outside, Jaden and Codmaster go off to build a house in Highwater City; Jaden will help whenever asked. Wemmbu and Egg head back to their base near Spawn, and Wemmbu tells Egg never to throw pearls for anyone again.
- Yungy rules the Kingdom of the Caves; BanditKing sits caged with golden apples against the Thorns damage.

## Items & mechanics

- [Windscourge](../../mechanics/windscourge.md): Arachn1d's thorns machine, fed by the carnival arrows.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Egg's dozens of pearls, Arachn1d's stasis, and Wemmbu's fishing-rod counter-stasis.
- Bedrock breaker: destroying a pearl without teleporting Egg.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): the purge mace, given to BanditKing; Wemmbu's elytra-macing.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): the nuke shot that opens the Flame fight.

## Outcomes

- Arachn1d is killed by Yungyx, who becomes king.
- Eggchan is finally freed (see [The Purge of the Caves](../../events/the-purge-of-the-caves.md)).
- The Wraiths' pearl vault is destroyed; Wemmbu promises Flame a fair duel.
