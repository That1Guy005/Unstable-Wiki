## Summary

Imprisoned by [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) in a Nether mining prison of endless wastelands, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) joins an escape team led by SnowLicker_. Mining ancient debris for a corrupt city where "freedom" means being dropped into the void, he loses friend after friend, unmasks Snow as Clown's informant, and uncovers Clown's plan to invade the Overworld. Realising void deaths show fall damage, Parrot leads the prisoners down into [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md).

## Full breakdown

### The wasteland

- Clown promises freedom for the villager's location, which Parrot will never give. As the flying machine emerges into a vast wasteland, Parrot breaks its slime, clutches on the falling blocks, and makes a wooden pickaxe. The guard doesn't chase. Tunnelling, Parrot hits an obsidian wall with Clown's face on it, and mining fatigue: the wasteland is the prison.
- Up a staircase is a city; citizenship costs a stack of ancient debris. Food comes from the city or from a wasteland shop: one debris for one cooked cod, six for a pickaxe, no haggling.
- Mining debris needs diamond tools. The shopkeeper sends him to SnowLicker_ at the tavern, where a fight leaves a player dead. Snow charges 50% to use his pickaxe; AxelDash468 charges 10%, then walls Parrot off and steals the debris. Parrot finds another vein and takes Snow's deal, which Snow honours. With half a hunger bar, he buys food just in time.

### The team

- Ten debris later, he trades nine for iron pickaxes, smelts them into nuggets and an ingot, crafts flint and steel, and starts carving a Nether portal frame into the obsidian wall. Snow interrupts: it won't work, they've tried everything. Does Parrot want to join them?
- Parrot points out that starving in Clown's prison isn't so different from the Overworld, and tells Snow how he went from king to exile. Snow introduces DrMae, MarcusTheVictor and IhaveNoM1c, and shows him a room of graves of former members. Why escape? "I'm melting." Parrot joins.
- The plan, a rail-and-minecart glitch onto the Nether roof, gets Marcus up, where invisible players instantly kill him. They bury him.

### The city

- Snow has heard freedom can be bought in the city. In twelve hours they gather just over nine stacks of debris. Citizenship tokens are blaze powder engraved with house numbers. In the market, four cod cost one debris, and freedom costs a thousand.
- In their cave houses, Parrot teases NoMic about being "the biggest bird", listing himself, Theo, Wemmbu, Eggchan "when he hatches" and Spepticle; neighbour hey_b1o and friends vote Parrot, for his bigger beak. They've never thought about leaving.
- Parrot wonders where the food comes from, and where the debris goes. He finds an elevator hidden in a pillar to the Nether roof; guards go up for ten minutes every hour. Mae is afraid, but all four sneak up: rows of barrels, guards, and at the end a dispenser machine with a lectern listing civilizations and the debris needed for each. Mae confirms the chests are full of debris, and Parrot fills his inventory.

### The traitor

- Parrot now wants to save everyone. Stolen debris can't buy freedom, since Clown will be watching for it. But guards carry ender pearls: get one, make an ender chest, get his escape kit, and tunnel through the wall.
- Their dripstone pitfall catches a guard, who survives: the dripstone has gone missing.
- Parrot tells Snow he suspects a leak: he told everyone he had 1,000 debris, but really had 2,000, telling Mae it was in the graveyard and NoMic it was under house 851, Mae's. Days later, the debris under 851 is gone. Snow yells that NoMic is the traitor and kills him on the spot.
- The trap works with Mae as bait, yielding the guard's pearls. Then Parrot asks Snow how long he's been lying: Parrot only ever told Snow about the debris. Snow stole it and killed NoMic to silence him. Snow says nothing, survives a second fall trap, and escapes through the walls.
- Hiding by the graveyard, Parrot and Mae watch Snow arrive with Clown: Clown will attack the Overworld in six weeks, and then Snow goes free.

### Wasteland after wasteland

- Mae says he can't save everyone; he should escape and warn the Overworld. They make an ender chest from NoMic's blaze powder; Parrot takes out his silk-touch pickaxe and leaves the debris with Mae.
- Through the wall, he kills guards' [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md) and they fall into the void to fall damage. Beyond is another, abandoned wasteland and a blown-up, deserted city. A diary lists its escape team, including SnowLicker, former leader of the Snow Licker Clan. As food and debris ran low, the author's team was killed while he was AFK, the guards abandoned the gate, the wasteland stormed the city for food that no longer came, and everyone starved.
- Parrot finds only more wastelands, runs low on food, and returns. He shows Mae the diary, gives her the debris to buy her freedom, and a book for Saparata about Clown's plan; he'll stay and free as many as he can.
- The guards escort Mae to a platform over the void hole, tell her to stand in the centre, and drop her in. "Freedom".

### The void

- Clown reminds Parrot that the villager buys his freedom; maybe every prisoner's death will change his mind. hey_b1o tells him his friends died, but he didn't, so he can keep going, and helps spread a plan.
- At Mae's new grave, Snow explains he's been here longest and knows how it ends; working with Clown was the only way back to his friends. Parrot tells him: in ten minutes everyone escapes through the void. People who die in the void should "fall out of the world", but they die of fall damage, so something's down there. The wasteland prisoners are storming the gates right now. Snow could warn Clown, but all his Overworld friends are dead. Reluctantly, as Mae's last wish, Parrot invites him along.
- At the abandoned gates, Snow takes armour and TNT from Parrot's ender chest: he can't come; Clown's reinforcements are coming, and he'll buy time. He plants the TNT and detonates it as the guards kill him, wiping out the reinforcements.
- In the city, Parrot's horn signals everyone to place boats around the void hole. He jumps first into the Underworld, blows his horn, and the prisoners follow. They all survive and start exploring.

## Items & mechanics

- Ancient debris: the prison's currency, food and "freedom".
- Blaze powder: citizenship tokens with house addresses.
- Dripstone pitfall: the guard trap that yields ender pearls.
- Ender chest: Parrot's escape kit and silk-touch pickaxe.
- Fall damage in the void: the clue that the Underworld lies below.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): the guards' mounts, killed beyond the wall.

## Outcomes

- Marcus, NoMic, Mae and SnowLicker_ die; Snow redeems himself at the end.
- Clown's plan to attack the Overworld in six weeks is uncovered.
- Parrot leads the prisoners into the Underworld (see [Trapped in the Underworld](../../events/trapped-in-the-underworld.md)).
