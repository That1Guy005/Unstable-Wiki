## Summary

Seeking the Citadel, a library described in Jamato's stolen book, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) drops alone through the Nether's bedrock into [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md), where blocks can't be placed or broken. He runs into [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), stranded there since Spoke knocked him into the void, and they bicker their way through a castle of puzzle towers. At the Citadel, a scholar warns him about the server's balance; Spoke steals a weapons book, escapes, and seals Mapicc in behind him.

## Full breakdown

### The descent

- NULL breaks the bedrock at the bottom of the Nether. The [Commander](../../characters/commander.md) hands over his shulkers and offers company; Spoke goes alone. The Underworld may be his only chance to decipher Jamato's book, which mentions the Citadel, a library of knowledge far to the west.
- An endless plain of broken structures. He can't place or break blocks, so his shulkers of supplies are useless, and there's no obvious way out. Reading the Underworld book aloud, he heads west, rationing everything.
- Someone is following him: Jamato out for revenge, he suspects, or worse, his old friend. The ground cracks into wider and wider void gaps; he spends his wind charges and then throws pearls as far as he can, until a castle appears.

### The towers

- Placing his Underworld book on a lectern opens the gate onto many paths. One tower drops him into a black concrete box with a redstone-lamp floor and an iron door that locks behind him.
- Mapicc enters and attacks, furious at being knocked into the Underworld. The lights go out, four lamps light, and dripstone falls from the ceiling, popping Mapicc's totem. They truce until they're out, surviving four more rounds before a door opens.
- Mapicc keeps trying to knock Spoke into the void. Before being knocked in, he says, he was trying to help: he got his NULL gear by killing a NULL general. Spoke thought Jamato had captured Mapicc again and had to show Jamato he had no leverage. They could have won the war, Mapicc says, and all been back in the Overworld.
- An underwater maze, with air pockets opening to keep them alive. Spoke gives a sprint-less Mapicc golden carrots.
- A copper-bulb room with a slime patch: buttons drop pufferfish, then other mobs (Spoke adopts an immortal salmon), then breezes, whose wind charges hit enough buttons to open the door.
- Floating sand parkour: Mapicc tries to leave Spoke behind, fails the jump and knocks out the only sand that leads out. Spoke's Punch II bow launches them both, Mapicc first.
- A pitch-dark parkour where Spoke falls into what turns out to be black concrete, not void; he marks platforms with arrows. They wonder how it fit inside. Spoke considers cheesing the next tower and lies to Mapicc about having no pearls.
- A game of Connect Four against what Spoke calls the "Eldritch Being". They nearly let it win before Mapicc wins for them.

### The riddle

- The sky turns blood red and the light keeps fading. After a maze, a ceiling dropper delivers books: solve the riddle to pass. Spoke throws in his shulker of golden apples; the being gives back exactly one.
- The riddle describes something born from a chime you can't hear, that helps you find what death took, and only takes shape where the dark once screamed. Mapicc writes "poop". A promised hint on page 100 isn't there. "Recovery compass" is correct. The being is sad to see them go, calling them bozos. Beyond, the light is so dim their skins look pitch black.

### The Citadel

- The largest structure, a library. Athenaeum724 confronts Spoke, knowing about Jamato and the Totem of NULL. She's one of the server's scholars, devoted to exploring and preserving its history and knowledge.
- Spoke claims to be on neutral terms with Jamato and gives her the book on "The Tomb". Jamato only writes in cipher when he doesn't trust the holder, she says; she can't decode it, knows nothing of the Tomb, and wouldn't tell him if she did. Spoke is meddling with the server's balance.
- The scholars fear a war bigger than any before. The library holds writings on every weapon on the server, and only one surpasses Wemmbu's [orbital cannons](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md).
- Athena guards the Citadel and can't leave. When Mapicc asks how to get out, she leads him to one of the few exits. Spoke swaps his Tomb book for "Weapons Vol. 5" and follows.

### The exit

- Mapicc hits him and runs for the exit: now Spoke will be stuck, as Mapicc was. Spoke says it was a mistake; Mapicc lists all his other betrayals and refuses to join him. They fight on the stairwell; Spoke breaks Mapicc's helmet, uses his last pearl to get ahead, escapes through the broken bedrock, destroys the ladder with a piston, and seals it with end stone.
- Mapicc swears that once he finds another way out, he'll pop the Totem of NULL and kill Spoke. Spoke says maybe he'll send someone, but they won't see each other for a long time. Mapicc has a feeling that won't be the case.
- Spoke seals his tunnel and goes to find the Commander: he knows what has to be done.

## Items & mechanics

- Jamato's books: "The Tomb" (given to Athena) and the Underworld guide.
- "Weapons Vol. 5": stolen from the Citadel, describing the one weapon stronger than orbitals ([Orbital Mob Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-mob-cannon.md)).
- Recovery compass: the riddle's answer.
- Punch II bow: their way across the broken parkour.
- Piston and end stone: Spoke's seal on the exit.

## Outcomes

- Spoke reaches the Citadel and learns of a weapon stronger than orbitals.
- Mapicc is left trapped in the Underworld, vowing revenge (see [Trapped in the Underworld](../../events/trapped-in-the-underworld.md)).
