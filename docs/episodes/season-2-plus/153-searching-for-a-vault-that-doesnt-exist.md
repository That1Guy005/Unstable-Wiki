## Summary

Cloudwatch of the peaceful civilization Windgrove recruits [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [NotSkrilley](../../characters/notskrilley.md) to find a legendary vault whose riches could help the server's weakest players. Racing the Windgrove Resistance through traps, a tunnel railway, a deep dark and one of Clown's fortresses, they reach the coordinates, where Cloudwatch reveals there was never a vault: it's a trap to eliminate overpowered players like Flame.

## Full breakdown

### The pitch

- Windgrove protects innocent players, but has never been strong enough to matter, until its king found a book by the first king describing a vault of immense riches. Flame is sceptical; Skrilley wants an adventure.
- Resting in a spruce village, Cloudwatch explains how the treasure would be shared; one recruit refuses to share with people he could easily kill and attacks. Flame splashes strength and kills him.
- Unimpressed that so many couldn't handle one player, Cloudwatch proposes the three continue alone. They slip out the back while the others search a barrel, and escape by boat. Somehow the abandoned recruits catch up on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), but won't follow them into a huge city.

### The creaking trap

- A guide offers horses for a tour. Skrilley notes every member of his group carries a different shield. On a central island ringed by a dripstone pit, at sunset, the guide teleports away and hundreds of creakings spawn.
- Flame has a "stupid" idea: Skrilley and Cloud wall them in while the city's residents shoot at them, and Flame builds a TNT launcher that fires them into the sky. They commandeer a happy ghast, grab their horses while their pursuers distract the city, and reach Windgrove.

### Windgrove

- A hole at the entrance looks like a stab-shot crater; Cloud says it isn't what Flame thinks. Up a parkour course (which Cloud keeps failing) and a wind-charge launcher, KingSkybound, one of the server's most experienced explorers, hires Flame and Skrilley. Beware the Windgrove Resistance, exiled for wanting to keep the treasure for Windgrove alone.
- The railway belongs to the Resistance. Skrilley runs ahead and is cornered. Invisible, Flame sneaks close while Cloud counts to thirty and pushes [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) down the track; Flame splashes Skrilley with Turtle Master just before the carts blow up the Resistance members.
- Cloud explains: a few players, like Flame, are so powerful they decide wars the moment they join, as in the LAW war. Spreading wealth to ordinary players might even out the imbalance.

### The Resistance's base

- Invisible, they overhear that the Resistance has searched everywhere and found only confusing clues in a village; one member doubts the vault exists. In the storeroom they steal redstone to slow them down, until a member seals the entrance with obsidian and their invisibility runs out. Hiding Cloud, Flame and Skrilley pose as innocent explorers; the member gives them chorus fruit, and Flame takes a second for Cloud.
- Escorted out, they're given away by three horses for two "explorers". They flee out the back, lose the Resistance in a cave, and hide the redstone and horses in a base deep inside.

### The clues

- In the village, three players each have a different clue. Over drinks, they decide it means a deep dark, while the bartender listens and puts on a Resistance shield.
- The Resistance jumps them in the mountains; bridging across a chasm, their horse Soccer Ball dies. In a deep dark, both groups find a tunnel to a book. A Resistance member takes it; Flame's group boxes itself into the obsidian room, mining fatigue holding the Resistance off.
- Over Cloud's protests, Flame builds a Nether portal in the obsidian wall. They need wither skulls from one of [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s fortresses, which turns out empty. A guard captures Cloud, who reveals nothing in interrogation. Flame's rescue fails and he drops his armour; as the guards march them towards Clown, the Resistance ambushes them and holds them for ransom over lava.
- Skrilley, hiding above, grabs the wither skulls being traded and places a cart for Flame to shoot; the explosion kills the Resistance members and they escape through the portal. On the other side, more Resistance: a deal, the book for the redstone, retrieved from their cave.

### The vault

- Both teams race to the coordinates. Flame carts the Resistance members already there, and a wither breaks into the box. Wardens, a lava-filled box, a Nether portal to a warden tunnel, another portal to an obsidian platform over a pit of ravagers. No treasure.
- Cloud turns on them: it was a ploy to eliminate players with too much power, like Flame. There never was a vault. The Resistance was exiled for overhearing him and KingSkybound plan it, though they truly believed in the treasure. Cloud stasises away, and TNT falls from the ceiling.

## Items & mechanics

- TNT launcher: the escape from the creaking island.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): down the railway, and against the Resistance at the coordinates.
- Wither skulls: needed to break into the vault box, taken from Clown's fortress.
- Chorus fruit: the escape from the Resistance's storeroom.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): the pursuers' and then Flame's transport.

## Outcomes

- The vault is revealed as a trap; Cloudwatch and KingSkybound want Flame dead.
- Many Resistance members are killed; the horse Soccer Ball dies.
- Flame and Skrilley are left trapped under falling TNT.
