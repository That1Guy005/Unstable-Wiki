## Summary

Hunted by King Saparata's bounty during a server-wide famine, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) learns from [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) that [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) has conquered the End with [NULL](../../factions/null.md). Spoke tries to recruit him, then threatens his orbital cannons; Wemmbu refuses and flees. Spoke destroys the cannons in the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md), wipes out the first-generation players, and Jaden dies. Serpentbound's compass points Wemmbu and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) to [The Forge](../../places/the-forge.md), through a portal into [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md).

## Full breakdown

### The bounty

- The world's largest hardcore server is starving. Saparata promised to fix it and instead bans new players. Wemmbu only cares about his friends and his shelter, but his base is in ruins.
- Bandits surround Wemmbu and Egg as they salvage it. The master assassin Flowtives kills them, announces an 80,000-diamond bounty on Wemmbu from Saparata, and uses slow-falling arrows to neutralise elytra-macing. As Wemmbu's armour fails, Minute arrives and regears him mid-fight. They beat Flow's reinforcements and take his pearls; Flow escapes underground, and Egg refuses to chase.
- Minute was the End's guardian until an army of machine-like players, obeying Spoke, exiled him. Wemmbu and Egg agree to look, staying neutral, and find rows of fully enchanted netherite NULL soldiers in perfect formation.

### Spoke's offer

- Spoke shows off his End civilization and offers Wemmbu a place at his side: he wants to preserve the server's uniqueness by conquering and remaking it. Wemmbu refuses, so Spoke calls in the favour Wemmbu owes him: bring him the Wraith. With Egg's help, Wemmbu does.
- Spoke was never going to let him go: if Wemmbu won't join, he's a threat. He knows Wemmbu is working with Minute. Pick a side, Minute or Spoke, or 200 NULL gathered at Wemmbu's [orbital cannons](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) deep in the Far Lands will destroy every one.
- Minute says he's bluffing. Wemmbu checks through his Far Lands stasis, to Minute's alarm. Spoke admits it was a bluff, but not any more, and reads out the cannons' exact coordinates: join him and watch the server change forever, or join Minute and lose every orbital. Minute warns that nothing good comes from a man who only wants everything for himself.
- Wemmbu says Spoke is assuming his NULL will still be on the server to do it, and signals [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), whose surprise mace nearly bans Spoke; a NULL soldier takes the hit. Wemmbu, Minute, Egg and Jaden barely escape the End.

### The Far Lands

- At The_Codmaster14's, they decide they must secure the cannons first, which needs a pearl cannon. Saparata, the false king, arrives: he wants NULL gone, and if they do it, the bounty disappears. He'll provide a cannon.
- [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md)'s cannon takes them to the Far Lands entrance as NULL arrives; they can't stop NULL entering. Minute stays to hold them off. Needing far more gear, they head for Serpentbound333 and the first-generation players, but there's no time for both. Wemmbu sends Egg and Jaden for help and goes to guard the cannons alone, and fails: NULL destroys the orbital cannons.
- Out of food mid-battle, Wemmbu is rescued by Firestar2008 and taken to the original players' civilization, which NULL and a wither cannon then destroy. Serpentbound destroys every valuable item so NULL can't take it.

### Serpentbound's compass

- Hiding, Serpentbound tells Wemmbu that Jamato led NULL before Spoke, and that there's a way to beat them: the real Forge holds what's needed to defeat NULL and anything else that threatens the server, because NULL is only the start. Wemmbu is the only one willing to go far enough. He creates a distraction so they can escape, then logs off rather than be killed after capture. His last instruction: follow the compass all the way, or the server is destroyed.
- Firestar wants the compass, his friend's; Wemmbu refuses: it was given to him. Following it, they reach what seems to be the Forge's entrance: a huge circular portal-like structure with a swirling purple centre, floating over the void among islands and clouds. Along giant beanstalks stand knight statues, one blue-and-gold and one black-and-gold, apparently Lakerek and someone else. The untouched gold suggests no one has been here. They continue into the Wavylands.
- When NULL attacks some players there, Egg and Jaden want to help, and Wemmbu fights anyway since NULL is in their way. Firestar is furious that Wemmbu's recklessness nearly killed Nitenly and will get them all killed, and demands the compass. Wemmbu refuses. Firestar leaves, Nitenly separately, and Nitenly ends up joining NULL and the Wraiths.

### Jaden

- In a pitch-dark biome they narrowly evade NULL, find a fleeing player called MagickArthur_, and reach CookieBunsquat's civilization, where Egg fights Cookie for the throne over her citizens' rudeness, and loses.
- Cookie wants them to stay and fight NULL behind her defences, and tells the attacking NULL that the "fugitives" are her citizens. They lose. Jaden dies saving Wemmbu: his last words tell Wemmbu to fly away and that he'll catch up. Nitenly kills him, on Spoke's orders.
- In a maze, they meet Firestar again, who followed the compass's direction and is shocked to hear Nitenly banned Jaden. They travel together again.
- Spoke appears: Minute is dead because of Wemmbu. Minute had split off to draw the army away; Spoke's word is their only confirmation.
- From the top of the maze's tunnels, they take the right one, retracing Spoke's own Far Lands route in reverse, while Firestar holds off NULL. In the forest with the Underworld portal Wemmbu once visited, where the compass points, they flee NULL and the wither cannons and step through into the Underworld.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's cannons, destroyed by NULL.
- Serpentbound's compass: the path to the real Forge.
- [Orbital Wither Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-wither-cannon.md): NULL's weapon against the original players' city.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): 4CVIT's route to the Far Lands, arranged by Saparata.
- Slow-falling arrows: Flowtives' counter to elytra-macing.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu's orbital cannons are destroyed.
- Jaden_MAN is killed by Nitenly; MinuteTech is reported dead; the first-generation civilization falls.
- Wemmbu and Egg enter the Underworld in search of the Forge.
