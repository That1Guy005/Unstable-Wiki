## Summary

The same campaign from [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s side. Emperor of the End with a monopoly on shulkers, he fears only [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md). He courts Wemmbu while building a shulker tracker to find his cannons, recruits the Wraiths, and marches [NULL](../../factions/null.md) into the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md), accidentally destroying the orbitals he wanted to steal. In the first-generation players' city, he meets his old Mafia boss [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md), who says they are exactly the same.

## Full breakdown

### The empire

- With only one life, there was always a limit to what he could do, until the Totem of NULL. Spoke overthrew the End's guardian, became Emperor of the End, invaded the Overworld and colonised strongholds. His wither cannons uncovered one of the last shulkers in the rubble, and NULL duplicated it into hundreds: a shulker monopoly.
- He fears no army, only Wemmbu, whose orbital cannons he wants. When Wemmbu returns to Spawn, Spoke greets him and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) at NULL's Overworld base and invites them to the End.
- Across a lava-ringed obsidian platform and a flying machine, fireworks welcome them. NULL builds them a house, Spoke gives Egg gear, god apples and XP, and sends them exploring for 30 minutes. Egg has let slip that [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) is around; dressed in Minute's trims, Egg confirms the three are working together.

### The shulker tracker

- The [Commander](../../characters/commander.md) doubts this approach; Spoke has him build a machine from the weapons book stolen from the Underworld.
- At a banquet, Spoke tells Wemmbu privately that he isn't trying to destroy and rule the server, but to preserve its powers and places, like the Great Sea, the Far Lands and the Underworld, for every player. He asks about "the Tomb", rumoured to hold infinite items; Wemmbu knows nothing and stays neutral. Spoke calls in his favour: recruit the Wraith, which Spoke believes doesn't exist, a wild goose chase to buy time.
- Every weapon in the book relies on mobs, impossible under the mob cap, except one on page 8: the shulker tracker. It fires shulker bullets at a target, and when the target teleports, the bullets' trajectories can be triangulated.
- Walking his immortal salmon, Spoke is surprised when Egg returns with "the Wraith", a group of invisible players posing as one, who want to join because of his totem. Spoke recruits them.

### The ultimatum

- Wemmbu tries to leave with Egg; Spoke reveals Minute is hiding below. He makes Wemmbu choose, threatening the cannons. Wemmbu calls the bluff and fishing-rods to the Far Lands, and the shulker tracker gives Spoke the cannons' coordinates, which he reads aloud.
- Wemmbu sides with Minute. [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) drops in to mace Spoke and kills a nearby NULL instead. Spoke lets them reach their flying machine, then has the wither cannons destroy it and sends the Wraiths after them.
- To NULL, gathered in the Overworld city, he declares that tonight their strongest legion will take the Far Lands, steal Wemmbu's superweapons and kill him: the beginning of their rule.

### Into the Far Lands

- By [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) to the Far Lands, mid-speech, Wemmbu drops an orbital on them. NULL holds up shields and builds an obsidian bridge to block the blasts; TNT carts from Wemmbu and Jaden don't stop them, and Wemmbu's team retreats.
- The Wraiths capture Minute. The Commander worries about the toll: he's seen only NULL death messages. Minute is stripped of elytra, armour and mace and escorted to the End.
- At the ruined [Far Lands Civilization](../../places/far-lands-civilization.md), destroyed in the Mafia era, NULL strips everything. The Commander makes Spoke promise to return to Spawn once they find the cannons. At the ruined [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md), an echo replays Wemmbu ambushing an invisible Egg during the Mafia days. They find only a barrel of elytra; rockets don't work here.

### The cannons

- Wemmbu, perched on a tower, rains orbitals on NULL as a distraction, and Spoke finds the cannons, but loading their chunks makes them blow themselves up. Unable to monopolise them, Spoke orders Wemmbu banned instead. The Commander begs him to leave; Spoke has every wither cannon fire on Wemmbu, who survives.
- A Wraith reports Wemmbu heading towards the sun, to a rainbow bridge and a city. The Commander keeps asking to go home: Nether portals don't work out here. Then Minute's escort returns through a portal. Spoke theorises the orbitals were blocking portals; with them gone, NULL can beat Wemmbu to his last sanctuary. The Commander's only job is to arm the cannons.

### Ashswag

- The wither cannons destroy the city and its rainbow bridge. NULL finds Serpentbound333, who says nothing and logs off. Spoke sees Wemmbu bridging into the void, expresses his respect, and orders his army to fire.
- Mining into the city, Spoke enters a room that turns the game into an older version, empties his inventory into a chest except for the Totem of NULL, and finds Ashswag fishing by a pond.
- Ash calls him "Fish Bucket": Ash searched every civilization for the last piece of the puzzle, the totem, and Spoke found it. Spoke is cursed by it too, and his friends don't stand by him any more, just like Ash. Ash is proud of him.
- Spoke insists this won't end like the Mafia. Ash disagrees: they're exactly the same, and asks how it felt to kill for his own gain. Spoke says he'll unify the server under one player, himself, but won't end up like Ash. Ash lists it: Mapicc abandoned in the Underworld, JumperWho betrayed, the freed prisoners slaughtered in front of her, all life in the End eradicated. Nothing to be ashamed of, he says, releasing the fish in his bucket: stop denying it, and Spoke will get what Ash searched for, a server unified under one player. What Spoke does now will decide the server's fate.
- Ash pulls his fishing rod and is gone as the Commander flies in. Spoke orders NULL to strip the Far Lands of resources and keep chasing Wemmbu; only when Wemmbu is no longer a threat will they return to Spawn and finish what they started.

## Items & mechanics

- Shulker tracker: the one mob weapon NULL can build, used to locate the orbitals.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's cannons, destroyed when NULL loads them.
- [Orbital Wither Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-wither-cannon.md): NULL's barrage on Wemmbu and the rainbow city.
- [Totem of NULL](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): the only thing Spoke keeps when meeting Ash.
- Duplicated shulkers: NULL's resource monopoly.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): NULL's way into the Far Lands.

## Outcomes

- The orbital cannons are destroyed; MinuteTech is captured.
- The Wraiths join NULL.
- Ashswag reappears, urging Spoke to accept what he has become.
