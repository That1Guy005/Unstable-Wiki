## Summary

Having led a thousand under-geared prisoners into [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md), [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) tries to get them home. In a land where blocks can't be placed or broken, TheOathBinder says only Parrot will escape. Through massacres, splits, a warden tomb, a snowfield of dead friends' illusions and a lava lake, the crew dwindles, and his last friends die so he can take the only way out. Back in the Overworld, [NULL](../../factions/null.md) seizes him and takes him to the End.

## Full breakdown

### TheOathBinder

- Parrot must protect the thousand prisoners who followed him down. Blocks can't be placed or broken and coordinates are broken, so his obsidian for a portal is useless. A structure suggests someone can build here. They walk for twelve hours.
- At a tower in a wheat field, TheOathBinder can place and break blocks, and refuses to build them a portal: not all thousand get to survive. As he talks, the crew disobeys Parrot and walks into the wheat. His terms: reach the Underworld's spawn and Parrot may leave, though he thinks Parrot is the only one who can. Parrot disagrees.
- A wall rises around the tower and TheOathBinder starts massacring. Parrot tries to land his boat on top of the wall and misses; the players can't follow his demonstration, and he has to abandon half his crew.

### The splits

- At a cliff over the void, a scout finds a spot to cross using boats as stepping blocks. One player blames Parrot for the dead and refuses to follow; most of the crew goes with him. The rest jump single file, and some fall.
- Between two mountains, the entrance to a maze. At a crossroads, two scouts go each way; after eight hours one returns, having found "something" far off. They leave an arrow for the other and follow.
- An abandoned village and castle. They stay 24 hours to gear up while Parrot tests what still works: chests and barrels, books, flowerpots, trapdoors, statues and crafting. When they leave, many stay behind for the endless food and the illusion of safety. With less than half his crew, Parrot enters the maze.

### The Mage of the Deep

- Dead end after dead end, walls too steep to climb; after nine hours Parrot admits they're going in circles. A dark canyon leads to a cave with a pit ringed by statues. Parrot boats in first and signals with his goat horn that it's safe.
- A room of sculk sensors and shriekers: they crouch across, but a warden spawns and many die. At a locked door, Parrot finds the code just ahead of the wardens, and desperate players shut the door behind them, locking most of the crew out.
- A book reveals they're in the tomb of "the Mage of the Deep". Parrot writes in it, asking any reader to help the players in the village.
- Past a tripwire Parrot boats over, a player triggers it and lava floods the route; Parrot's boat burns instantly in it. A crew member finds a secret path up.

### The snowfield

- In a snow biome, players sink into powder snow, which buckets can't stop. They see dead friends walking away: Parrot spots Wifies, DrMae and SnowLicker, then Leow0ok, Deanthebean9 and PestoPasta2009. Another player chases an illusion and sinks. Six are left.

### The lava lake

- A Nether-like barren land and a lava lake about 40,000 blocks across. A player with a lava boat says it seats only three. Parrot kills him and takes all six aboard, and tells them [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) will invade the Overworld in a few weeks.
- The land they reach is an island in the lake, with only three striders and supplies for three: that's why the boat seated three. kamp3 attacks and demands a place. The others plan to poison him with the suspicious stew Parrot made in the village. Parrot offers to die as bait; StellarNuro takes his place, since Parrot is a king, and dies, but kamp3 drinks the stew and they kill him. como_squad gives up the saddle, drops his armour and says goodbye.

### The only way out

- Parrot, hey_b1o and dunea91 ride striders to shore and back towards spawn. In a ruined castle, two pressure plates open the doors to a Nether portal, which close when Parrot steps off. Only one person can leave.
- His friends convince him to go: as a king, he has the influence to stop Clown. As the door opens he walks into another room and hears them scream as TheOathBinder kills them. The prophecy holds: of the thousand, only Parrot reaches the portal, the first ever to do so, and TheOathBinder lets him live.
- In the Overworld, NULL kidnaps him at once and takes him underground to [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s End portal. The [Commander](../../characters/commander.md) wishes him luck as he enters the End.

## Items & mechanics

- Boats: Parrot's tool for impossible jumps, since blocks can't be placed.
- Goat horn: his "safe to follow" signal.
- Suspicious stew: the poison that kills kamp3.
- Striders: the only way off the lava island.
- Pressure-plate portal: the Underworld's one-person exit.

## Outcomes

- Of the thousand prisoners, only Parrot escapes the Underworld (see [Trapped in the Underworld](../../events/trapped-in-the-underworld.md)).
- StellarNuro, como_squad, hey_b1o and dunea91 die or stay behind.
- NULL captures Parrot and sends him into the End.
