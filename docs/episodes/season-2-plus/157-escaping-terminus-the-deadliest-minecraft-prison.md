## Summary

Tricked by Cloudwatch, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [NotSkrilley](../../characters/notskrilley.md) are trapped in [Terminus](../../places/terminus.md), [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md)'s maximum-security prison, built to wear down even the strongest player. Bled of resources by TNT, wardens, Flowtives and [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md), Flame resolves to use his strength for the server and finally agrees to join Cindercrest, only for Cloud to try to kill him. A raid by ten factions, led by [LAW](../../factions/law.md), breaks him out.

## Full breakdown

### The boxes

- Hidden millions of blocks out, Terminus is designed to hold the strongest players. Flame and Skrilley start in an obsidian box with mining fatigue, under an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) and falling TNT.
- Blowing a 3×3 hole releases wardens from below. Chorus fruit takes them from box to box: guardians and illagers, invisible netherite guards with Cindercrest shields, TNT, more wardens. Flame's shield breaks. They finally land on stone instead of obsidian, having escaped only a small part of the prison. Flame is down to one shulker.

### The prisoners

- BannerBurger was jailed for making Cindercrest banners for non-members, the first prisoner in this section. Cloudwatch is one of the wardens and the prison's architect, working with Cindercrest.
- Waylonblade founded the Windgrove Resistance; three of his twenty survived. Cloud specialises in illusions, like the vault. Cloud appears: the Resistance got too close to the truth. He built the prison to outlast strength, and Skrilley was meant to die in the first box. Strength only decides how long the journey takes, he says; every journey ends at Terminus.
- Beyond their barricade wall, the wasteland is full of wardens. Every day TNT falls through the roof and a warden is dropped in, so every day is spent gathering blocks to repair the barricade.
- The alarm sounds and the TNT falls harder than usual because Flame is there. Out of blocks, they cross the wall wearing banners for supplies; Flame lures a fixated warden up and over the barricade. Without mining fatigue they could dig, but it returns near the obsidian. The prison has a contingency for everything.
- When Flame tries a Nether portal, an arrow cannon kills a player: everyone who tried the Nether has died. Cloud says the elder guardians toggle mining fatigue at will.

### Spongs

- The invisible guard Cloud leaves behind is [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md), who rejoined Cindercrest because his old friends were there. His stasis doesn't work, so he's stuck too, and throws Flame only minecarts. Any friend of Flame's is an enemy, Cloud says, and leaves him to rot.
- Waylonblade says crossing has only got people killed; the difference this time is Flame. Flame climbs to block the TNT dispensers while the others build a sky base. Invisible guards bridge towards him, TNT blasts his platform, he clutches the fall, and grabs a dead player's loot on the way up.

### Lettuce and Flowtives

- [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md), Cindercrest's new Rank 3 captain, is there to oversee Flame: the harder Flame tries, the harder it gets for everyone. Flame tries to bow him out. Skrilley wonders whether Flame's capture affects anyone outside.
- In [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md) tells Lettuce to do what he does. Lettuce has done this before and it backfired, but Saparata says restoring order needs the players' support, and Flame's capture is the show of strength to win them. Lettuce addresses the crowd.
- Flowtives was sent to drain Flame's resources, but wants to kill him. Lettuce says Cindercrest is satisfied; Flowtives keeps going until Lettuce kills some guards and Flowtives' stasis is pulled.
- After Skrilley blows up the sky base, Spongs kills another of their team. Across the wasteland is "Prisontown", whose mayor paperlad wrote about his business. Under a wheat field, BannerBurger leads them to a hidden base of books, food and an enchanting table; one book says someone left through an opening in the viewing chamber. The plan: get armour and blend in as guards.
- As BannerBurger sets out to make Cindercrest shield banners, Lettuce maces her. After waves of guards they collect a shulker of armour and, with no experience, botch the banners.

### The lava maze

- Invisible, Flame choruses into the viewing chamber, but their homemade shields give them away. He choruses through into the outer wall over lava, knocks guards off, and climbs obsidian stairs past prisoners caged above the lava.
- Cloud and Lettuce argue over authority; Lettuce tells Flame every escape is accounted for. Flame fishes Skrilley and Spongs out of the lava. Prisoner Hungrysoup1293 scolds him for wasting his resources.
- Flowtives joins them: strength should build something of your own, not attack civilizations like [The Mist](../../places/the-mist.md). The caged prisoners break out to kill Flame and are fought off. Lettuce drops by: the groups Flame fought could have taken the server; Flame underestimates his impact just by being alive and isn't the person Lettuce fought in the LAW arc. The surface is changing fast.

### Outside

- DreamRoyal101 and OrionCat, who captured a Cindercrest guard to find the prison, think the server has got worse since Flame's capture. Morr0w_ brings wither skulls from an old stash. [Antaggg](../../characters/antaggg.md) wants to find the prison to make sure Flame dies; Alix49 objects and dies as he leaves.

### ShoeBilly

- A bow arrives reading "at your own risk, check under lava". [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) appears: he knew Flame would end up here since their talk on the [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md). Cindercrest kept Flame alive to recruit him on new terms: throw them a stasis, and if he betrays them, they finish him. The prison was meant to break him bit by bit. Flame refuses; they hurt his friends.
- ShoeBilly griefed [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md)'s base and took Flame's horse; Lettuce brings it out and ShoeBilly maces it.
- Flame wind-charges him off the platform. Flame and Flowtives fight the elytra-macing ShoeBilly with bows, slow falling, a water-flattened floor and cobwebs. Flowtives won't share his ender-chest loot but gives Flame strength. Flame's totems pop, including in lava, until he has none; ShoeBilly blows up their shelter, escapes a hole trap by chorus, and pops totems agreeing to a ground fight before taking to the air again. Flowtives' stasis is pulled, leaving Flame alone, still refusing.

### Limbo

- Skrilley has found something. Through the lava into an obsidian box, they chorus into a village whose players call it limbo; Spongs griefs their wheat. Chorus can't teleport into lava, so there's no way through the wall.
- Lettuce sent the arrow. This is the end of the prison, without threat or danger, reinforced all round. The only correct answer is to join Cindercrest.
- Flame realises Flowtives was right: he's so focused on his own goals that he doesn't do what's necessary, and with his strength he has an obligation to help the server. Skrilley promises to stand by him if they escape.
- Waylonblade explains Cloud: Windgrove was a secret branch of the Mist, meant to expand its influence. Cloud, Mistrul's apprentice, ran it behind KingSkybound's cover. When the world proved hostile, Cloud wanted to fight. After Mistrul died, Cloud designed the vault illusion; members who thought he was hiding treasure were exiled. He hates Flame for killing his mentor, and Flowtives for letting it happen and taking the Mist from him. Cindercrest wanted Flame alive; Cloud wanted him dead.

### The way out

- Flame agrees to join Cindercrest. Lettuce leads him through the wall, ordering the others to stay. Flame drops through a trap into an obsidian box with Cloud, while invisible diamond-armoured players attack Lettuce. Cloud wants to be free of the many who hurt him, pulls his stasis, and leaves Flame under waves of TNT. His shield breaks.
- Outside, Cindercrest fights lawmen. In a flashback, Lettuce had warned Saparata that announcing Flame's capture would tell his enemies too. Death messages pile up. Someone spawns a wither at Flame's box, which breaks the obsidian; given gear, Flame climbs out with the lawmen and walks out of the prison with hundreds of players chasing him.
- Lettuce, unsure what happens if he lets Flame go, chases him; Flame begs him to turn around, splashes invisibility and loses them. The lawmen bow Lettuce away; he mutters that Saparata won't be happy. Spongs escapes too, but Skrilley is left behind. Flame heads back to HQ.
- Lettuce reports that ten factions raided the prison, LAW among them, and that Cloud went rogue. Saparata doesn't care: recruitment is at its highest. He tells Lettuce and ShoeBilly to figure it out.

## Items & mechanics

- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the prison's guns, killing anyone who tries the Nether.
- Elder guardians: switchable mining fatigue near the obsidian.
- Chorus fruit: the only way between the prison's boxes, useless into lava.
- Banners and Cindercrest shields: prisoners' passes, and the disguise that fails.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lettuce kills BannerBurger; ShoeBilly's elytra-macing.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Cloud's and Flowtives' escapes.
- Wither: the raiders' way into Flame's box.

## Outcomes

- Flame resolves to use his strength to help the server.
- BannerBurger and Alix49 die; Flame's horse is killed; Skrilley is left in Terminus.
- Ten factions, including LAW, raid the prison and free Flame; Cloudwatch goes rogue.
