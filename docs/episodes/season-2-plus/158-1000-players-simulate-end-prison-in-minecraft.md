## Summary

Captured by [NULL](../../factions/null.md), [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) wakes in [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s five-floor End prison, where every floor filters out prisoners. With [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) he clears a coliseum, a parkour pit, a sword-duel floor where Minute trains him, and a riddle of twelve doors, only to learn the last floor makes teammates fight. Minute, who used him to get through, can't bring himself to win. Parrot refuses Spoke's offer to join NULL and returns to the Overworld.

## Full breakdown

### Floor one: the coliseum

- A NULL general with a mic queues Parrot in a room where prisoners' numbers are called. After hours, his turn: one survivor per game. In a coliseum watched by NULL and a commander, VexCrester attacks him for his king, Saparata. Parrot wears him down at range with spear, arrows and fire, then leaps from a netherrack tower and spears him. Five floors stand between him and freedom.

### Floor two: the pit

- After four hours in a cell, a general takes him to a prison almost identical to [Purgatory](../../places/purgatory.md). At the bottom, a crowd cheers a player doing parkour over lava; RazedEdel dies.
- Minute is here: Spoke designed and built this prison. Parrot remembers Spoke calling him the rightful king; the Spoke they knew is gone, Minute says. The Guardian of the End and the former King of the Overworld agree they have nothing to lose and attempt the gilded blackstone double neo that stops most people. Both make it.

### Floor three: the duels

- Under a bright artificial sky like a simulation, a general (employee of the month) notes Minute's skin looks like Spoke's. Win three sword duels, losing no more than once, to move on. Fighters set a stasis "immortality machine", drink instant-saturation stew and get diamond armour.
- Parrot beats fernoedfelix, who accuses him of hacking; Felix's stasis fails and he dies. oTilted easily outskills Parrot, using up his one loss. Minute will train him, on condition he doesn't rematch until Minute says so.
- Minute teaches three pillars: spacing (unpredictable strafing); tempo and combos (w-tapping for maximum knockback); and critical hits, including punish crits. Wemmbu and FlameFrags have practised for years, Minute says. Then aim training.
- After two days, Parrot still only gets Minute to seven hearts, and he's furious. Minute says he's ready. With no stasis set, Parrot faces oTilted again, who needs one more win to leave, and wins by a heart; then wins the demanded rematch. oTilted saw him training; Parrot says he had a good teacher, and kills him.

### Floor four: the twelve doors

- A pale room with twelve doors. Minute arrives later; he's smuggled items in. Crowds gather at doors they think they've cracked. Parrot sees that every floor is a filter, so some doors must be unsafe. He throws a map to a player leaving through Door Twelve; the icon vanishes over pure void.
- b0mo_rex misses a door while eating cereal and loses it. Parrot cracks the timing: the domed ceiling is a clock, with north as twelve. Each opening time's hour names the next door, its minute the next hour, and its seconds the next minute. That predicts when, but not which door is safe, and days of tracking find no pattern.
- Reminiscing about their kingdoms, Parrot mentions [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s planned invasion; Minute says Spoke told him NULL will invade the Overworld too. A war between all three powers is coming. Parrot then notices the cycle: converting the seconds into letters spells "ELEVEN IS SAFE".
- Beyond Door Eleven, players given diamond armour in an elevator are maced by ____X____. Parrot and Minute, drained fast, kill him with a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md). Parrot gives Minute golden apples, a netherite chestplate and sword, but keeps the [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md).

### Floor five: the teammate

- Minute has climbed these floors three times before, losing on the last one with his teammate JinCorvus. The final floor is an arena where players must kill their teammate. His plan was to find someone good enough to get through, but not good enough to beat him.
- Spoke narrates. Minute apologises for lying; Parrot understands, nothing personal, but won't surrender. Parrot maces a supervising NULL general for netherite armour and drains Minute, but Minute reaches Parrot's ender chest and they both have maces. Minute begs him to resign; towering up, Parrot apologises: he can't.
- Minute gives in. Unable to kill Parrot, he tells him to save everyone else while he starts over a fourth time; his last words are "I trust you". The [Commander](../../characters/commander.md) takes him back to the first floor.
- Spoke echoes [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md)'s ideals: the prison is for the server's criminals. He asks Parrot to join him against [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md). Parrot refuses and cuts ties. Spoke reminds him that six months ago, when Spoke came for help, Parrot turned his back; he'll let it go, but now Parrot must choose again: join NULL, or turn his back once more. Parrot warns him not to invade his kingdom and drops through an End portal home.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the duel floor's "immortality machine".
- Clock-dome doors: the timing code and "ELEVEN IS SAFE".
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): taken from ____X____; both Parrot and Minute end up with one.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the kill on ____X____.
- Sword PvP pillars: spacing, w-tapping, punish crits, aim.

## Outcomes

- Parrot clears all five floors and escapes; Minute sends himself back to floor one.
- Parrot learns NULL and Clown's Nether Kingdom both plan to invade the Overworld.
- Parrot breaks with Spoke and refuses to join NULL.
