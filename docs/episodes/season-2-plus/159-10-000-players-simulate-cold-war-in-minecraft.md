## Summary

After taking the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md), [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) is ambushed by [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s [Nether Kingdom](../../factions/nether-kingdom.md) and loses his capital. Rebuilding with new machines like the [Orbital Automatic Pearl Launching Contraption](../../mechanics/orbital-automatic-pearl-launching-contraption.md), he raids civilizations, signs a truce with Clown that neither means to keep, and frees the "Worms" mining under a monarch civilization. With them, NULL wardens and his mace Vengeance, he retakes his capital, but the Nether Kingdom has reached the End.

## Full breakdown

### The feast

- Recap: the End's technology carried NULL to the Far Lands, which they conquered, beating forces led by [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), who was death-banned by Nitenly. Emma, on her throne, warns that Spoke has started a war with the whole server; Spoke says it ends when he rules it.
- NULL prepares a victory feast: Wemmbu is trapped in [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md), the Far Lands are taken, and Spoke has two maces. Clown steps out of a Nether portal: Spoke has been travelling through Clown's dimension, so he was easy to find. Spoke laughs, shows his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), and as Emperor of the End orders him out. Clown threatens him and leaves.
- Portals open around the city and in the sky. NULL attacks a commander in gold trims, 4Blaze, while the Nether army drops [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) from the sky portals. Spoke orders a retreat into the End, waits until every NULL is through, faces 4Blaze at the last portal, and escapes.

### The bed and the machines

- Now an arms race. The Commander still has hope: the Scholarly Association's book. Every NULL's spawn point is one special bed that pistons can move without breaking. Spoke sneaks past patrols into the base and, with his immortal fish Raphael as a distraction, pushes the bed into the sky and out.
- In a new Overworld base, the Commander has combined a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) with Wemmbu's [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): the Orbital Automatic Pearl Launching Contraption, a [fishing rod](../../mechanics/fishing-rod-trigger.md) that summons NULL. Spoke gets a few rods; more need resources. The Commander has found seven civilizations, three worth something.

### Shelldon

- _Shelldon's people wear turtle-shell helmets. Shelldon won't trade, insisting on the 350 turtle species; Spoke forces four shulkers for one Turtle Master potion. Invisible, NULL steals eggs from the turtle farm. 4Blaze arrives demanding Turtle Master too.
- Spoke flees and calls the contraption: one naked NULL appears, then more one by one, then a real force, which bans everyone except Shelldon.
- The Commander "made Spoke a friend", reOTJDkfewn, speaking through an awful voice changer; Spoke kills him. A new machine can fire any mob anywhere, wardens included, but tested on Shelldon it produces one dead turtle. Spoke crashes out.
- Nether soldiers come near; NULL roofs the whole compound just in time. Spoke and three NULL pretend to explore, ignore the portals, build a fake machine and stand in the open as bait. The Nether Kingdom takes it, the contraption brings reinforcements, and 4Blaze retreats.

### Birchington

- Birchington, in emerald Silence armour, proudly shows off farms and machines. Searching for ties to Clown, Spoke finds nothing conclusive, so he tests him: join, and Spoke helps rebuild; or relocate the whole civilization. Birchington won't abandon his friends. Spoke summons NULL onto the central tree and they burn the civilization down. Birchington still refuses, and leaves the game.

### The truce

- A book comes through a portal: Clown wants to meet. Spoke's NULL escort seems gone and he has no rods, until invisible NULL reveal themselves and the Commander gives him a goat horn.
- For the Nether Kingdom, Clown proposes that NULL stop trespassing in his territory, and that both leave the Overworld neutral: neither invades it. Spoke blows the horn, the invisible NULL gear up around them, and he agrees with no intention of keeping it.
- Spawn faces its second food shortage. The first, the Commander says, came when NULL was first used for war, when the server nearly ended.

### The monarchs and the Worms

- A castle civilization showers him with diamonds. Below its bridges is a crater of houses; NovaD46 shakes his head when Spoke asks to go down. One NULL wonders if this is [The Forge](../../places/the-forge.md), which Spoke doesn't know of. The monarch minth4ra, in fully enchanted chainmail, says the people below kill on sight, and wants a NULL as a servant. An armourless player coming up the bubble elevator is killed on sight; the citizens stop Spoke's pearl.
- Giving minth4ra his two NULL as a distraction, Spoke gets a pearl into the crater: she lied. The crater was once a city, mined away by the players below, who send everything up the bubble elevator for the monarchs.
- Under a sign reading "WORMS", _slicee has never seen a mace, TNT or wind charges ("magic balls"). The monarchs "protect" the Worms from the "fire people", the Nether Kingdom, which arrived through a portal. Spoke, "magic man", promises to free and protect him; they become friends.
- Spoke realises Clown plans to betray him, and must be rushing if he's already using cross-dimensional technology, so Spoke must strike first. Clown appears and tells him never to return; Spoke agrees, to get rid of him.

### The uprising

- Spoke returns with NULL generals and the Commander, hidden in the walls. The Commander equips the Worms in enchanted copper armour, the only thing they'll wear. After Spoke's speech, Slicee demands Vengeance, his mace, and gets it. The Worms raid the towers and kill the monarchs; minth4ra stasises away just before Slicee's swing. They build Slicee a diamond throne; he returns the mace.
- The same army raids the Turtle Kingdom, now held by the Nether Kingdom, destroying every Turtle Master potion.
- The Commander demonstrates the [Orbital Mob Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-mob-cannon.md) on a cage of armourless players: hundreds of creakings, though the players mostly kill each other. Then a rod linked to two hundred wardens.

### Retaking the capital

- NULL and the Worms march into the capital past Nether soldiers, Slicee holding Vengeance. Spoke refuses to surrender for breaking the truce, waits until enough of the Nether Kingdom arrives, and summons NULL and the wardens. They retake the capital; 4Blaze escapes into the End.
- Slicee hands back Vengeance. The Nether Kingdom has reached the End, and Spoke and the Commander go through to see what waits.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Automatic Pearl Launching Contraption](../../mechanics/orbital-automatic-pearl-launching-contraption.md): rods that summon NULL anywhere.
- [Orbital Mob Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-mob-cannon.md): creakings and two hundred wardens on call.
- The NULL bed: everyone's spawn point, moved by pistons.
- Vengeance: Spoke's [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), lent to Slicee.
- Turtle Master potions: the resource both empires fight over.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the Nether Kingdom's bombardment from sky portals.

## Outcomes

- The Nether Kingdom takes NULL's capital; NULL retakes it with the Worms and wardens.
- Shelldon's and Birchington's civilizations fall; the monarchs are overthrown and Slicee rules the Worms.
- Spoke and Clown's Overworld truce is broken; the war moves into the End.
