## Summary

Free of [Terminus](../../places/terminus.md), [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) takes temporary command of [LAW](../../factions/law.md) in its war against [Cindercrest](../../factions/cindercrest.md)'s 10,000 players. He makes an unpopular deal with [Antaggg](../../characters/antaggg.md)'s [Warriors](../../factions/the-warriors.md), frees [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) from Cindercrest's stasis, chooses a friend over a vault, crushes Cindercrest's invasion, and rescues [NotSkrilley](../../characters/notskrilley.md) from Terminus. Then he hands LAW to Morr0w, stays a solo player, and sets out to save the server.

## Full breakdown

### Taking over LAW

- Back at his base, Lettuce pops Flame's totem: of course Cindercrest would look here. Some 2,000 players surround the base. Lawmen arrive on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md); Lettuce begs Flame not to drag LAW in, since Lettuce doesn't want war on his own banner, but Flame has no choice and flees to LAW headquarters.
- [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md) shows him LAW's book of succession, with Sarge's name last; Flame suggests leading LAW himself. Sarge objects, then announces that Flame will lead LAW for the Cindercrest war. Flame asks LAW to stand with him. OrionCat is supportive; Morr0w challenges his position. LAW has no training ground, so Flame has one built.
- A horn sounds and Cindercrest marches up, Lettuce in front. He warns that Cindercrest has grown stronger and declares war on LAW and its allies.

### The outpost

- On OrionCat's advice they hit a Cindercrest outpost across the bridge; Flame scouts with a small group and blows his goat horn when Lettuce finds him. Under the outpost, prisoners tell him Cindercrest plans to execute Skrilley, then their stasis is pulled.
- No army answered the horn. Morr0w says they attacked from the rear and won with fewer losses; Flame says he was left to die. They argue.

### The Warriors deal

- LAW has lost its redstone specialists. Flame's idea is the Warriors, the faction that killed [TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md); Morr0w and OrionCat refuse. At their HQ, Antaggg mocks Flame for making demands in enemy territory, then names his price: all twenty Warriors LAW holds prisoner. Flame agrees.
- Freed, Antaggg threatens Flame, and LAW erupts: Flame loses much of its support. Lettuce's speeches are swelling Cindercrest's ranks.
- In [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), Flame pearls onto Lettuce's stage. Lettuce asks him not to interfere; Flame tells the crowd Cindercrest doesn't care about them. Lettuce debates him and reveals that LAW is working with the Warriors, destroying Flame's credibility and recruiting more players.
- Half of LAW now wants to negotiate and oust Flame; half stays loyal. They resent being dragged into Flame's war.

### ShoeBilly

- Flame's base has been griefed; a book from [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) says he's alive but hunted. [ShoeBilly_](../../characters/shoebilly.md) blows up the base and fights him, listing his fall from Terminus to his vow to protect the weak and his friends. Flame says he can't control how others interpret his strength; his friends are why he's strong. ShoeBilly only cares about fighting Flame at his strongest, and killing him now would mean nothing. He leaves.

### Freeing Lettuce

- Morr0w and OrionCat give Flame a second chance. Lettuce's stasis is probably at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md). Flame distracts the front with [TNT carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) while his team pushes through. Cindercrest pulls the stasis to force Lettuce to fight LAW; when he leads LAW to the Council anyway, they pull it fully. Flame throws slow falling, Lettuce maces a wolf to bounce back up from the void, and he's free.
- Lettuce gives them coordinates to his loot stash in [The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md) and leaves to undo his speeches.

### The vault and Morr0w

- In a civilization near the stash, two players lead Flame and Morr0w underground to a safe with mining fatigue, offering to split it. Surf149 privately offers Flame a share if he lets them take revenge on Morr0w and LAW. Flame accepts, and is locked in a room where prisoners attack him for their freedom while pirates attack Morr0w.
- The [Orbital Dog Cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) thins the room out. Morr0w tells Flame to go for the vault; remembering everything between them, Flame saves his friend instead, even as the vault is breached. Morr0w admits Flame isn't so bad. They then kill the looters and celebrate.

### The invasion

- LAW HQ is deserted, and an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) nearly kills Flame. Lettuce has repositioned LAW and asks to command most of the army; Flame reluctantly agrees.
- Invisible on an elytra, Flame watches a Cindercrest army so big the server lags. Arrow cannons, Great Sea loot and Lettuce's tactics demolish it.

### Back into Terminus

- Morr0w backs storming Terminus for Skrilley. Flame and Lettuce enter with Cindercrest shields. Guards take Flame for a new recruit and give him the tour; when Lettuce returns with Skrilley, Lettuce claims he was about to interrogate him and the "trainees" are invited to watch.
- The real trainee shows up. Everyone is made to drink milk, and Lettuce maces the others. At the exit, ShoeBilly duels Flame; the Orbital Dog Cannon pops his totems. Flame and Skrilley run through every section of the prison, ShoeBilly chasing, to limbo and through by chorus. ShoeBilly baits Flame to kill him; Flame doesn't care to, he wants to help his friends and the world, and won't prove ShoeBilly right.

### The parade

- LAW celebrates with a parade. Morr0w would like Flame to stay as leader. Flame thanks the commanders and Lettuce, says he's a solo player, and steps down for Morr0w.
- Going home with Skrilley, Flame meets Lettuce, who brings up [NULL](../../factions/null.md)'s shield. Flame's goal now is to save the server.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Dog Cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): Flame's answer when outnumbered, twice.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): LAW's defence against the Cindercrest invasion.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the leash on Lettuce, broken at the Northern Council.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lettuce's wolf-bounce out of the void, and his kills inside Terminus.
- Cindercrest shields: the disguise back into Terminus.
- LAW's book of succession.

## Outcomes

- LAW defeats Cindercrest's invasion; Lettuce leaves Cindercrest.
- Skrilley is rescued from Terminus.
- Flame steps down and Morr0w becomes leader of LAW.
