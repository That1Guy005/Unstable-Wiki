## Summary

As [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s [Nether Kingdom](../../factions/nether-kingdom.md) invades, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) and [ReinaDrop](../../characters/reinadrop.md) fight their way to [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md) with Parrot's villager, convincing a sceptical [Saparata](../../characters/saparata.md) only when Clown appears. City after city falls, including [LAW City](../../places/law-city.md) and [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), until a last stand at [Newtopia](../../places/newtopia.md) turns the Nether back. The Overworld's three kings, Lettuce, Saparata and Parrot, unite against Clown.

## Full breakdown

### The villager

- The Overworld stands little chance against the Nether, which has fractured into many factions. Parrot's village welcomes him back, and he sets off by [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md) to breed their villager with Saparata's. At Fort Feather he maces his way through; at [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md) there's a huge lava blockade.
- Saparata boards, refuses to believe Clown is invading, and steals the villager. Theobald launches himself up with a TNT launcher and ReinaDrop joins in; they escape to Ironclad City with the villager, named Pesto.
- At a base in a shipwreck, Parrot tells them about his civilization and its immortality machines; convinced they're always in danger, they come along.

### Ironclad City

- They buy [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and iron. A Cindercrest member quizzes them: asked about the sixteen election candidates, Parrot blurts "me", then claims it's a nickname for [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md); Reina correctly names [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md)'s five tiers.
- [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md), who has left Cindercrest since Flame freed him, is furious with Parrot. Parrot urges him to evacuate the city; Lettuce says Flame is at his base. Thousands of Cindercrest troops appear and they flee. Back at the base, Theo is missing, with the food and the villager, and is found in the wall watching YouTube and eating a "pesto sandwich".

### The smuggler

- OutlawAndrew will smuggle them to Spawn for thirty minutes with the villager. Parrot mentions SnowLicker_'s death, then reveals he knows Andrew has betrayed them: a Cindercrest soldier is hiding in the walls. Theo reminds Andrew that Reina killed his best friend.
- After hours boating the King's River to a statue of Theobald, they skirt a checkpoint (Theo doomscrolling again), and Cindercrest surrounds them. Parrot makes a deal: he'll drop his totem, linked to an immortality machine, and kill the villager, if Reina can boat out. Saparata agrees; Parrot kills the villager, Reina leaves, and a fight with carts and maces breaks out. Parrot's helmet breaks.
- He had shot the villager with [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Reina took it. Following her white flowers, they find her.

### JumperWho

- In the warehouse where Parrot once confronted the Director, a hidden passage leads to [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) and [JumperWho's Spies](../../factions/jumperwhos-spies.md), who say [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) is trying to take over the server. Theobald compares it to the Mafia; the spies mock him.
- They catch PomPomPurr998, a Cindercrest member who says Cindercrest is hunting her. Parrot hides her in a hole, keeps her a secret between himself and Reina, feeds her nightly, and invites her onto his team. Jumper tells him [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) is dead, killed by Spoke some time ago.
- They plan to travel through Steampunk City to the Northern Council, while Pom waits by a red beacon: she has betrayed them, and Cindercrest is coming. Parrot has been tricked again.

### Spawn

- At Steampunk, ShoeBilly attacks, wanting only to fight Theobald, but comes to believe Parrot's warning. In the distribution network they kill an invisible Nether Kingdom soldier: Clown is scouting.
- Parrot sneaks into the Northern Council while Saparata makes a sandwich, hovers over him with his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), decides not to kill him, and they talk. At Spawn, defended by ShoeBilly's warriors, Parrot convinces Saparata to let the villagers breed and hands his over; ShoeBilly takes them far away.
- Mid-argument, Clown comes through a portal. Saparata realises Parrot was telling the truth, too late. Parrot frees Fymada from jail to evacuate the citizens; Cindercrest fortifies Capital City and evacuates to Newtopia. Reina is furious Parrot is allied with the people who killed hundreds.

### The fall of the cities

- Clown's army moves on Highwater City for the villagers; Parrot and Theo flee with them, and Highwater falls in thirty seconds. Reina goes to the Advanced Civilization and is told to send its army to Newtopia.
- At Flame's base, LAW takes the villagers. A dog teleport shows Flame is in neither the Overworld nor the Nether: the End, or dead. Clown invades LAW; Lettuce arrives with LAW, thought to have fled; a kinetic launcher flings Parrot and Theo away as LAW falls.
- Surrounded by TorrinTheTitan's forces, Theo fights the mace-wielding TorrinTheTitan for their freedom and is losing until Cindercrest attacks. They escape by blimp. The Advanced Civilization won't move for Reina, only for Parrot; she leaves, insulting them.
- Capital City has anti-portal machines but Cindercrest is out of carts and potions. Clown, [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md) and TorrinTheTitan attack; Theo, Parrot and Saparata defend, falling back to the second wall, until Clown takes the villagers. Parrot kills them and escapes on chains; ShoeBilly rescues them.

### Newtopia

- [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md)'s Newtopia is built to look like the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md). Parrot, Theo, Saparata, Bossstream, ShoeBilly, Lettuce and Horace debate fighting or evacuating. Bossstream reveals Clown paid the [Capitol City Police Force](../../factions/capitol-city-police-force.md) to frame Parrot for killing 43 old players: Clown has been dividing the Overworld to conquer it. Parrot decides: if Clown attacks after 22 hours, they fight; before, they evacuate through a tunnel.
- Clown arrives, negotiates, counts down, and they charge. Cindercrest plugs breach after breach in the first wall until one breaks. After a long fight, Reina arrives with the Advanced Civilization's enormous army, and the Nether army is torn apart and retreats.
- The three kings each speak after a goat horn. Lettuce on inventing the crown and the trust in the monarchy; Saparata on old and new players, war and unity; Parrot apologises for leaving after [The Civil War](../../events/the-civil-war.md), promises to protect the citizens, and names the true enemy: Clown. If they fight, the Overworld will never fall. Three generations of the monarchy stand side by side.

## Items & mechanics

- Pesto the villager: the breeding plan, and the bargaining chip Parrot kills (or seems to).
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): how the villager "dies" and escapes with Reina.
- [Blimp](../../mechanics/blimp.md): Parrot's transport, and his escape from TorrinTheTitan.
- Anti-portal machines: Capital City's defence.
- Kinetic launcher: the escape from LAW HQ.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): the fight with Cindercrest at the King's River.

## Outcomes

- The Nether Kingdom invades; Highwater City, LAW and Capital City fall.
- Clown is revealed to have framed Parrot through the Capitol City Police Force.
- Newtopia holds; Lettuce, Saparata and Parrot unite the Overworld against Clown.
