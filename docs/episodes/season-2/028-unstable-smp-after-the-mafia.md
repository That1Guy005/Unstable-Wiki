## Summary

After the Mafia, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) try to settle down in a Pale Garden millions of blocks away, but [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) keeps griefing their new base to force Wemmbu into a fight. With [Kenadian](../../characters/kenadian.md)'s help they fortify it; Theo breaks in anyway, takes Ken hostage, and finally gets his duel, which Wemmbu wins. Wemmbu learns that bases survive on intimidation.

## Full breakdown

### A quiet life, interrupted

- Their first real base since [ManePear's Tree](../../places/manepears-tree.md) goes up in a Pale Garden. Theo arrives: he followed them because he wants a fight, and thinks Wemmbu is good enough to make it entertaining. Wemmbu refuses.
- Theo griefs the base and promises to come back once Wemmbu has had time to prepare. A creaking trap meant for him fails, and he griefs it again.

### Kenadian's defences

- Tired of the cycle, Wemmbu commissions Ken to make the base secure. She's scared at first but agrees and explains what they need.
- Theo interrupts again. Wemmbu bargains: if Theo wants a fight, he has to let them finish the defences and then break in. Theo agrees to come back in a week.

### The break-in

- Theo starts tunnelling in while an invisible Wemmbu watches. When it looks like it'll work, Wemmbu griefs the tunnel, steals some of Theo's shulkers and taunts him.
- Theo gets in anyway and triggers both Wemmbu's and Egg's stasis chambers. When Wemmbu still won't fight, Theo takes Ken hostage.
- Wemmbu accepts the 1v1, but Theo, annoyed by the creakings, leaves with Ken and demands the fight outside. Asked about Ken, Theo claims he killed her, then admits he let her go.
- Wemmbu breaks Theo's helmet and wins. Theo leaves saying it was worth it.

### The lesson

- Wemmbu realises bases and civilizations only thrive through intimidation, and decides that's what he'll use next time.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Theo triggers Wemmbu's and Egg's on his way in.
- Creakings: the Pale Garden mob behind both the failed trap and the base's defences.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu beats Theo in a 1v1; Ken is let go unharmed.
- Wemmbu adopts intimidation as his strategy, which leads straight into building an empire.
