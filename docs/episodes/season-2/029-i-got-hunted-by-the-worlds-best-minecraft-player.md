## Summary

[ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) is attacked by ItzRealMe, called the world's best Minecraft PvP player, and taken in by [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md)'s [Bounty Assassin Team](../../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) at [BAT Headquarters](../../places/bat-headquarters.md). Still grieving and insisting he works alone, Parrot keeps going behind the team's back, until Itz cuts him off inside the base and BAT saves him. He joins the team, and Itz reveals who sent him: the Director.

## Full breakdown

### The world's best player

- Itz attacks Parrot outside his house, and his escape route is blocked. Leo, [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), [Nufuli](../../characters/nufuli.md) and [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) arrive to kill Itz, who escapes with a Turtle Master potion.
- Leo brings Parrot to BAT Headquarters, an infinity-shaped base with more than thirty members, reasoning that if Itz is after Parrot, he'll come there and BAT can kill him.
- Going back for Parrot's things by chicken blaster, Parrot notices a chest under a tree has one more dirt block than before: his front-door pressure plate drops a block each time someone passes, so one block means someone is still inside. Through the back window they see Itz waiting. They plan an enclosure around the house.

### BAT's structure

- Nufuli shows Parrot his room, A5, a two-storey suite. Each of Leo's close friends runs a team: Jumper's spies watch the whole server, Derapchu's is "not worth explaining", and Nufuli is Leo's right hand for combat and base work.
- Parrot secretly messages the lead builder in room C1 to change the enclosure's suspended walls to sand.

### The sand trap

- Leo's team and five invisible players fight Itz inside the enclosure; the sand walls drop to shrink the arena so Itz can't isolate anyone or escape to restock.
- It backfires: Itz maces five players to death and keeps replacing broken armour without a shulker. Back at base, Leo is furious that Parrot changed the plan without telling anyone, and puts him under house arrest. Parrot says he works alone.

### Recon, and a reason

- Leo catches Parrot sneaking out at night and comes along, promising only to keep him safe. Parrot reveals he had one of Jumper's people plant a fake postage box in the enclosure, with false coordinates and a tracking map, for Itz to pick up.
- Near Itz, his icon vanishes. Invisible, they follow DylPickleYT, who begs Itz to train him and offers information in exchange, pointing out Parrot and Leo.
- Parrot fights alone, refusing Leo's potions; Leo helps anyway. TNT minecarts pop two of Itz's totems and force him onto the mace, and they leave.
- Leo asks why Parrot always wants to be alone. Parrot explains that two of his best friends were killed after the war and he won't go through that again. Leo apologises for the house arrest and offers him a permanent place; Parrot says they're only working together to kill Itz. Back in his room, he blames BAT for getting caught and breaks the note block mailbox instead of reading the message.

### Trapped in the base

- The next day Parrot has mining fatigue, everyone is gone, and Itz is inside. He barricades his suite, hides under the stairs, and mines from suite to suite towards the airlock.
- Itz catches him there, with a netherite pickaxe against Parrot's diamond, and Parrot gives up, until Leo's team flies back and blows open the airlock. Itz leaves. Jumper's intelligence team had found him.
- Parrot apologises for going behind their backs, thanks everyone, and Leo welcomes him into the team.

### Itz's warning

- Itz appears in Parrot's room. Now that Parrot has a team, killing him isn't worth the trouble. He reveals the Director sent him, warns that more people will come for Parrot, and tells him to run while he can.
- When Nufuli rushes in, Parrot says he pressed the emergency button by accident: he doesn't fully trust the team yet.

- Chicken blasters: BAT's automated transport.
- Pressure-plate dirt counter: Parrot's intruder detector.

## Outcomes

- Five BAT players die in the sand trap.
- Parrot joins the Bounty Assassin Team, still keeping secrets.
- Itz stops hunting Parrot and names the Director as the one behind it.
