## Summary

[Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) start the [Eggchan Empire](../../places/eggchans-civilization.md) on the site of ManePear's treehouse. Wemmbu's job is to supply experience bottles, which turns into a running feud with [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md) and the [Pirates](../../factions/pirates.md). He recruits [Wato1876](../../characters/wato1876.md) and others, raids [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md)'s base, and grows the empire, but [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) uses Spongs as bait, takes Egg hostage, and strips Wemmbu of everything except his mace, Gambit.

## Full breakdown

### Founding the empire

- They flatten the land around [ManePear's Tree](../../places/manepears-tree.md). Egg explains that a leader has to provide resources for everyone, so Wemmbu decides to supply experience bottles, but every nearby villager is dead.
- He threatens players and loots stashes across the server. Spongs attacks him after Wemmbu takes his bottles, then hands over the rest.
- Building sugar cane and gunpowder farms for rockets, they're robbed by players carrying pirate shields. The player Wemmbu robbed earlier warns he's outnumbered; Wemmbu kills the attackers with his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), but their drops despawn while he fights, and he burns through some of his bottles.

### First citizens

- Wato, irritated but persuaded, becomes the first citizen; his first job is clearing the treehouse ruins. The bottles run out, so Wemmbu and Egg go looting abandoned bases again.
- In a strangely generated forest, an invisible [Pangi](../../characters/pangi.md) attacks: he's guarding the house where [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) retired after the prison. He lets them take the experience, but they have no shulkers, so they send Wato to collect it.
- Arcn joins as a defender, and Dione4k from Wemmbu's old civilization makes rockets.
- Wato returns empty-handed and furious: pirates robbed him and wiped his ender chest.

### The pirates

- After a week of searching, Wemmbu finds the pirates in Spongs' base. Their leader, Jaden_MAN, tries to rob him; mid-fight, Spongs begs for help from a cage with no food. Wemmbu frees him and kills a pirate, but loses Jaden.
- Under the threat of his house being blown up, Spongs shows Wemmbu his experience stash.
- [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) has built the empire a tower with Egg's eyeball on it. Egg says Wato has found a pirate base.
- Inside, Wemmbu fights Loppezz and her army until he runs out of rockets, elytra durability, experience, spare armour and finally iron armour. He digs straight down; Loppezz has no pickaxe and gives up.
- Wemmbu repairs his gear at a spawner, steals sugar cane to make rockets, spawns withers to destroy the base, and brings the experience back to Egg. Loppezz flees with her army dead.

### The trap

- Over the following weeks the empire expands with raiding parties and new citizens; Wemmbu trains players like Willerhide, argues with a still-resentful Wato, and asks Pangi when Zam stopped caring about his empire.
- Collecting taxes, Wemmbu spots Spongs, who flies off; Wemmbu follows him to another pirate base, then realises he's been lured away.
- Back home, Jaden holds Egg hostage. Arcn has betrayed them too, and Spongs was bait to get Wemmbu away from the empire. Jaden demands his items or Egg dies; Wemmbu drops everything, but keeps Gambit hidden in his ender chest.

## Items & mechanics

- Experience bottles: the empire's founding resource, and Wemmbu's way of mending gear.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Gambit, saved in the ender chest when everything else is surrendered.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wemmbu's lifeline in the Loppezz fight, as long as rockets last.
- Pirate shields: how the Pirates' members are recognised.

## Outcomes

- The Eggchan Empire is founded, with Wato, Arcn, Dione4k and Horace among its first members.
- Loppezz's pirate base is destroyed by withers.
- Arcn betrays the empire; Jaden_MAN takes Egg hostage and Wemmbu loses all his gear except Gambit.
