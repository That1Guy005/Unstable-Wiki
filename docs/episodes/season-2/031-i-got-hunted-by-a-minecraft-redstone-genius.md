## Summary

A redstone genius, [MrCube6](../../characters/mrcube6.md), targets [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) inside [BAT Headquarters](../../places/bat-headquarters.md) with a void trap, a wire bomb, a bomber tank and a tampered messaging system. Parrot catches him with a milk trick, loses him, recovers BAT's stolen armoury with [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) from [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md), and walks out of MrCube's final trap holding a book from the Director.

## Full breakdown

### Traps in the base

- An emergency signal leads Parrot into a "lava trap" that is really a void trap; MrCube taunts him and his pearls can't get him out. Derapchu saves him by mining through the obsidian, and [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) is unhappy with Derapchu even though he didn't know about the trap.
- Spotting an invisible player who might be MrCube or his accomplice, Parrot changes the subject to redstone practice in the courtyard, a ruse to catch him in the act.
- Parrot calls a 10 AM courtyard meeting but brings the team early. Derapchu is missing, then found standing on a "wire trap": if he steps off his pressure plate, the whole base explodes. MrCube gives them 20 minutes and reveals a bomber tank aimed at the base. Parrot defuses the bomb while BAT destroys the tank.

### The milk test

- Messages sent to Leo's and Derapchu's rooms never arrived: MrCube has been tampering with the messaging system. Parrot builds a sealed meeting room and explains how to catch anyone sneaking in.
- He messages a spy to sneak into the meeting and drink milk, knowing the real spy wouldn't come. The invisible intruder drinks the milk and is revealed as MrCube, who is imprisoned. Parrot suspects he was hired by the Director.
- When they come back to question him, MrCube has escaped while Derapchu, who claims he was in the bathroom for 30 minutes, was trapped. A trap then blows up the cell.

### Redstone Town

- The armoury has been robbed. Leo blames Derapchu and sends him with Parrot to get it back; Derapchu sees a chance to prove himself.
- They follow MrCube's rails to Redstone Town, where 4CVIT is helping him hide the loot in an underground vault he says will explode if anyone steps on the wrong plate. They get through safely, and 4CVIT admits there were never any explosives.
- On the way home, bandits led by Mixiku steal some of the items; Parrot and Derapchu kill all of them except Mixiku, who runs.
- Derapchu asks for all the credit. When Parrot refuses, he admits he wishes his teammates saw him differently; Parrot tells him to stop seeing himself that way first. Leo is impressed they recovered everything.

### The twelve hallways

- A book from MrCube asks Parrot to meet alone, bringing certain items. MrCube praises him for surviving every trap, mocks Derapchu, and says only one of twelve hallways is safe. Parrot stands still, and all twelve explode.
- Derapchu appears and arrests MrCube. Interrogated about the Director, MrCube gives them nothing useful, so they leave him to die. Jumper hands him a dandelion, and MrCube hands Parrot a book from the Director.
- While Parrot reads it, MrCube teleports out of the cell; a page jokes that "he's not a big fan of dandelions anyway", and the Director says he'll be meeting Parrot soon.
- Parrot tells BAT he'll do whatever it takes to kill the Director.

## Items & mechanics

- Void trap: MrCube's disguised trap, escaped by mining the obsidian.
- [Bomber tank](../../mechanics/bomber-plane.md): MrCube's ground bomber, destroyed by BAT.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): unmasked with Parrot's milk trick.
- Wire trap: a pressure-plate bomb tied to Derapchu.

## Outcomes

- BAT's armoury is recovered; Mixiku's bandits are wiped out except their leader.
- Derapchu earns some respect from Leo.
- MrCube escapes again, and the Director contacts Parrot directly.
