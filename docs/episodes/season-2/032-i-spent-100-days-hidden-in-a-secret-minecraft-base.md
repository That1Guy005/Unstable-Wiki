## Summary

After the Mafia falls, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) tracks [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md) to his hidden base, Point Nemo, and challenges himself to live inside it for 100 days without being caught. He builds secret entrances and a hideout, maps every alarm, and spies on [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md), then raids the base empty ahead of a trade with [Reddoons](../../characters/reddoons.md). Ash catches on, and Spoke trades the loot for Reddoons' coordinates.

## Full breakdown

### Moving in

- After weeks of stalking Ash, Spoke finds Point Nemo. On day one Ash flies off and Spoke sneaks in, hiding when he sees Squiddo disappear down a hidden path.
- On day two he vows to stay a hundred days unseen, mapping hidden entrances and pocketing valuables. Squiddo nearly catches him draining a Nether fountain, but leaves before it empties and before his Fire Resistance runs out.
- Spoke builds his own secret entrances. In a hidden room he finds Squiddo talking to her pets and building them a house; with his [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) about to run out and unable to drink a potion without being heard, he parkours over the cloud decorations onto the roof, and a launcher takes him to a sky base full of shulkers.

### Alarms

- Ash returns and sounds the alarm over a missing leather armour set. Squiddo doesn't know, and leads him to her secret room instead, unknowingly covering for Spoke.
- Ash says they need more alarms; Spoke follows Squiddo to learn where each one goes so he can disarm them all, then slips out through a Nether portal he builds from the obsidian walls and back in through the hidden Nether room.

### The heist

- Spoke buries his pet fish and takes another George Junior from his shulker of replacements. For days he barely moves, afraid of being noticed.
- On day 67 he overhears Ash and Squiddo: Reddoons wants to complete a trade in six hours. Spoke plans to hijack it, accounts for every chest, and fills dozens of shulkers until the base is empty.

### Caught out

- Before day 100, Ash returns and tells Squiddo he's known about the intruder for a while, knew about the trade, and rigged the room with hoppers. Spoke realises he never broke the hoppers that led to his hideout, grabs a few important shulkers and burns the rest.
- With everything gone, Ash tells Squiddo to leave and start a new life, and apologises for wasting her time.
- Instead of keeping the loot, Spoke trades all of it for the coordinates of Reddoons' base.

## Items & mechanics

- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Spoke's cover for 100 days, and his near-miss with Squiddo.
- Alarms and hoppers: Ash's security, and the hopper line that gives Spoke away.
- George Junior: Spoke's pet fish, replaced from a shulker full of spares.

## Outcomes

- Ash's Point Nemo base is emptied; Ash sends Squiddo away.
- Spoke trades the loot for Reddoons' coordinates.
