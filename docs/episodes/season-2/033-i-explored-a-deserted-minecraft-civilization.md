## Summary

Exiled by [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md)'s pearl cannon to the Pirate Islands in [The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md), [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) start again from nothing: scavenging hidden rooms, fishing for enchantments, mugging [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) and [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md), and losing a duel to Arcn. A mystery helper keeps them alive, and turns out to be [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md), who helps Wemmbu beat Jaden.

## Full breakdown

### Stranded

- Jaden makes them throw pearls into his [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md), which teleports them to the Pirate Islands. A local accuses Wemmbu of stealing food and attacks; Wemmbu kills him and his friend, takes their weapons and food, and sails to another island, where Egg is in a cell. He distracts the jailer and kills the whole group while Egg escapes.
- Mining is useless (digging straight down brings mining fatigue). In a hut, Egg digs down into a hidden room with two golden apples and a [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md).
- Under an "abandoned" house Egg finds a base with an enchanting table, and they loot a trial chamber whose vaults the Invisible Mafia had already destroyed.

### The ice circuit

- They rob a house belonging to two diamond-armoured fishers and find nothing, but a ladder leads to an underground ice circuit, where Lettuce and then Loppezz catch them.
- Wemmbu pulls a lever that seals the house in glass; Loppezz warns that breaking any glass blows the house up, so Wemmbu uses the threat to make both of them drop everything.
- He ignores a giant trident in the circuit, then fishes for enchanted books with Egg to upgrade his gear.

### Arcn

- Arcn's pirates demand Lettuce and Loppezz's armour. To avoid being hunted before he can beat Jaden, Wemmbu goes along with them to an underground arena for a 1v1. His shield breaks early, his totem pops, and he loses.
- Refusing to join the [Pirates](../../factions/pirates.md), he flees to an ender chest and pulls out his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), stored there after Jaden made him empty his inventory, kills several pirates, and retreats when his helmet breaks.
- Egg gives Wemmbu his Protection IV helmet and they swap armour, since Egg's is almost maxed. Egg somehow gets invisibility potions for a stealth mission to find the pearl cannon.

### Jaden's castle

- They overhear Loppezz, Arcn and Lettuce arguing with Jaden, who pulls a lever and sends Loppezz to an underground civilization.
- At the pearl cannon an invisible player's sign tells Wemmbu to look up, just in time to see Jaden diving with a mace. Jaden and Arcn 2v1 him; Wemmbu finds golden apples, pops Jaden several times, and Jaden retreats into the water to keep breach-swapping where Wemmbu can't hit him.
- Theo joins the fight. Jaden tries to take Egg hostage again, but the pearl cannon explodes and kills some guards, and Wemmbu and Egg sail away.

### The helper

- Theo reveals he's been helping all along: the invisibility potions, the golden apples, the sign. The trident room really had a trident, buried.
- With the [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md), Wemmbu and Theo 2v1 Jaden and beat him, and Wemmbu follows Jaden to end it for good.

## Items & mechanics

- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): Jaden's exile machine, destroyed in the fight.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Gambit, hidden in the ender chest, and Jaden's breach-swapping from the water.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): the buried trident from the ice circuit.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): found in Egg's hidden room, and popped in the Arcn duel.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu and Egg survive exile and rebuild their gear.
- Loppezz is cast out by Jaden; Jaden's pearl cannon is destroyed and Jaden is beaten.
- Theo is revealed as Wemmbu's secret ally.
