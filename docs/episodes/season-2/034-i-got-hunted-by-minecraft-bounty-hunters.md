## Summary

[BAT Headquarters](../../places/bat-headquarters.md) goes into lockdown, and [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) is hunted by his own team, who now suspect he is the Director. [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) warned him, smuggles him back in disguised as a spy called "Alkaline", and is then forced to set a trap for him. In the end the real Director appears in person, admits to killing [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md), and escapes BAT's ambush.

## Full breakdown

### Framed

- A sign reading "run" and some items wait in Parrot's room; the base locks down and [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), [Nufuli](../../characters/nufuli.md), JumperWho and [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) arrive with [JumperWho's Spies](../../factions/jumperwhos-spies.md). Assuming he's being framed as the Director, Parrot hides and escapes.
- BAT finds his hiding place but won't go in, fearing a trap. Parrot flees across the Nether roof and outruns them with [wind charges](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md).
- In an abandoned base he meets Jumper, who says she was the one who warned him.

### Alkaline

- Jumper disguises Parrot as a new spy, codename "Alkaline", and introduces him to Leo and Derapchu. She explains the suspicion: the Director's book that MrCube6 gave Parrot mentioned the dandelion Jumper had handed over moments earlier, so BAT believes Parrot wrote it himself while pretending to read it.
- Leo announces a spy check in a room of green banners: on his signal, every spy must raise the green shield they were issued. Parrot dyes a shield green while Leo isn't looking, and becomes the only spy holding one, which gives him away. He escapes through the chicken cannons.

### The meeting

- A book from the Director waits at the abandoned base, inviting Parrot to meet in a day. Jumper talks him into going, with wither skeleton skulls and soul sand.
- Parrot hides, fully invisible, on a black glass pane above the meeting room, a huge obsidian cube with mining fatigue and lights. Leo reveals that Jumper has betrayed Parrot and attacks; Parrot spawns withers to break the obsidian and escapes.
- Jumper explains she had to set the trap to prove her loyalty. When a spy spots them, they blow a "Call" goat horn to summon him, as the spies' rules require; Parrot boxes him in obsidian, and Jumper reluctantly kills him, marking the grave: "There's something about the truth that keeps it from being perfect."

### The Director

- Parrot tells Jumper about the Director's book, and she leaves to gather the rest of BAT.
- Twenty-four hours later, an invisible player in netherite with quartz-flow trims arrives. The Director says he killed Wifies because he had to, and that if Parrot knew who he was, he wouldn't be angry, he'd be terrified.
- BAT ambushes him and the Director stasises away. Jumper leaves BAT.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Parrot's escape across the Nether roof.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Parrot's hiding spot, and the Director's disguise.
- Wither skulls and soul sand: Parrot's way through the obsidian meeting room.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the Director's escape.
- Green shields: BAT's spy check.

## Outcomes

- Parrot is suspected of being the Director and cast out of BAT.
- A BAT spy is killed by Jumper to protect Parrot.
- The Director confesses to killing Wifies; Jumper leaves BAT.
