## Summary

Still chasing [Reddoons](../../characters/reddoons.md), [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) gets scammed by Yeah_Jaron, then follows Jaron and Red to their base and secretly moves in. [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and [PlanetLord](../../characters/planetlord.md) track him down and join in: bedroom contests, puzzle rooms, pranks, and a final raid that ends in a scramble for the dragon egg. Red keeps it, and Spoke vows to strip Red of everything.

## Full breakdown

### Into Red's base

- Spoke buys coordinates from Yeah_Jaron, gets scammed, and simply follows Jaron and Red home instead.
- Invisible, he finds secret rooms and plays tricks on them. When he accidentally kills their pet white fox, he replaces it with a white fox from PlanetLord's rare-pet collection.

### Reunion

- Mapicc and Planet find him, their first meeting since the end of Season 1. Spoke claims he's stalking Red because Red killed his pet fish, and tries to send them away; Mapicc won't leave his side.
- In their hideout they hold a bedroom-building contest: Spoke goes minimalist, the others maximalist. Planet blows up his room and part of Mapicc's, and Spoke wins by default.
- Mapicc suggests inviting [TheRealSquiddo](../../characters/therealsquiddo.md) and the pair reminisce, but Spoke shuts it down, saying she's busy with commissions.
- Planet finds Spoke's shulker of replacement George Jr. fish, and Spoke swears him never to tell Mapicc.

### The puzzle room

- A secret puzzle room fires arrows and won't open, until Planet remembers seeing Jaron solve it and they spot the path carved into the ceiling. Behind it: an armoury full of netherite and diamonds, and an empty villager trading hall with a single farmer.
- Their cruel pranks during Jaron's base tour embarrass him so badly that Baconnwaffles0 shuns him and leaves.

### The raid and the egg

- They steal endless valuables. When Jaron catches Mapicc, Planet takes the blame and is chased; Spoke and Mapicc step in to protect him.
- Red logs on, the two owners argue, and Red goes for the dragon egg. Spoke races him for it, the egg passes between the groups, and Red ends up locking it in his ender chest.
- Spoke tells the viewer this isn't the outcome he wanted: he'll stalk Red until Red dies, quits or gives him what he wants, and will be the force that tears Red from his wealth.

## Items & mechanics

- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): how Spoke lives in Red's base unseen.
- Dragon egg: the prize Red keeps in his ender chest.
- George Jr.: Spoke's replaceable pet fish.

## Outcomes

- Spoke, Mapicc and Planet reunite and rob Red and Jaron's base.
- Jaron loses Baconnwaffles0's respect.
- Red keeps the dragon egg, and Spoke declares a personal war on him.
