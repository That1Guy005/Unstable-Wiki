## Summary

[Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) dives into [The Abandoned Civilization](../../places/the-abandoned-civilization.md) after [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), a vast underground ruin full of wardens, with elder guardians giving the whole place mining fatigue. With [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), an exiled [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md) and the civilization's last guardian, [Rejoicin](../../characters/rejoicin.md), he chases Jaden through spires, armouries and warden cages. Rejoicin dies to Jaden's wardens; Wemmbu finally wins, takes his mace back, and leaves Jaden trapped there alive.

## Full breakdown

### Into the pit

- Wemmbu falls into the pit after Jaden, picks up mining fatigue, and Egg follows. They look for armour in a village (Wemmbu's is almost broken), gather chorus fruit, and climb for a view.
- Jaden is pillaring out. His bow outdoes Wemmbu's [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md), but when the fatigue wears off Wemmbu mines out the pillar; Jaden's helmet breaks, Wemmbu seals the hole, and a fight on a vine ends with Jaden knocking both Wemmbu and Egg off.

### Rejoicin

- In a spire, Egg takes a lore book from a lectern wired to a comparator and sets off a warden trap. At the top they meet Rejoicin, who has been guarding the civilization from the Mafia without knowing it has disbanded. He points them down an ice-boat road to the armoury, where he saw Jaden.
- Jaden pretends to be AFK without armour, gears up when they jump him, and nearly kills Wemmbu, but ends up stuck behind two doors with mining fatigue and no TNT. Wemmbu taunts him and adds more blocks.
- In a trading hall they find a shulker of enchanted books and add Protection to their gear. Egg suggests using wardens on the trapped Jaden; they lure wardens from the castle, but Jaden has already escaped, and Wemmbu kills the wardens with his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). When Egg mentions a treasure vault, Wemmbu insists nothing beats a mace.

### Loppezz, and the elder guardians

- In a warped forest they find Loppezz, banished there by Jaden, and kill most of her pigs for food. She joins them against Jaden. Gold blocks Egg finds turn out to have been placed by her; they craft golden apples.
- Chorusing through a wall, they discover elder guardians being moved on rails through transport stations: the source of the civilization-wide mining fatigue. Loppezz reasons that empty minecarts would show where Jaden is.

### Cobwebs and fire

- Impatient, Wemmbu finds Jaden killing elder guardians and ambushes him. They end up in the same hole; Wemmbu pops Jaden's totem, and Jaden traps them both in cobwebs in a 1×1 space. They pause to break a cobweb, resume, then realise Jaden has flint and steel and they're surrounded by wool.
- Out of food and with his boots failing, Wemmbu runs, grabs raw cod from Jaden's guardian kills, and escapes; Jaden chorus-fruits after him and Wemmbu's boots break.
- At the spire, Rejoicin gives Wemmbu golden apples and tries to make peace. Jaden attacks him, so Rejoicin splashes Wemmbu with strength and speed. Rejoicin warns him not to follow Jaden into the warden spawning facility, and Jaden gets away.

### Rejoicin's death

- Egg and Loppezz have spent the time writing note-block songs and building a house from parkour blocks. Everyone trims their armour and goes after Jaden.
- In a stone cube full of wardens, Wemmbu mines in late, breaks Jaden's helmet (he has a spare), and Jaden releases the wardens. Jaden escapes; Rejoicin's armour breaks and the wardens kill him. Wemmbu takes some of his items and fights on in silence while Jaden taunts him.

### The end of the feud

- Sealed in a warden cage they reached by water, Wemmbu guesses there's a shulker behind a cobblestone wall. Egg and Loppezz blow it open: inside are an [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md) and fireworks.
- With flight back, Wemmbu pops all of Jaden's totems. He lets Jaden live if he drops everything, burns the drops, and takes his mace back.
- Because Rejoicin wanted peace rather than killing, Wemmbu spares Jaden, leaves him food, destroys all the chorus fruit so he can't escape, and leaves him in the Abandoned Civilization.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu gets his own mace back from Jaden at the end.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): the sealed shulker that finally turns the fight.
- Elder guardians on rails: the machine that gives the whole civilization mining fatigue.
- Wardens: Rejoicin's spire traps, and Jaden's weapon in the stone cube.
- Chorus fruit: everyone's escape tool, destroyed so Jaden can't leave.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): outmatched by Jaden's bow.

## Outcomes

- Rejoicin is killed by wardens released by Jaden.
- Jaden_MAN is beaten, stripped of his items, and left trapped in the Abandoned Civilization.
- Wemmbu recovers his mace; Loppezz becomes an ally.
