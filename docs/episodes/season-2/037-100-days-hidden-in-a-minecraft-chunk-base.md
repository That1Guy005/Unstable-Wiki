## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) hides for 100 days inside the server's top base, belonging to [Reddoons](../../characters/reddoons.md) and Yeah_Jaron, building a secret bunker in the walls and secretly rigging their farms with hoppers. His plans to hijack their trades keep going wrong (bandits, an AFK bait, a lost trade), and when raiders find his bunker, his own trap blows up the entire base.

## Full breakdown

### Getting in (days 1–8)

- Out of 1,000 players online, this is considered the number-one base, with goals posted on the wall: "Build An Empire, Get Rich and Get More Rich".
- An observer door keeps shutting before Spoke can follow Jaron in. Once inside, invisible, he hides behind enchanting bookshelves and risks making noise with fall damage while Red is nearby.
- He restocks potion ingredients, pearls and chorus fruit, nearly gets spotted using a boat to hop up, and finds cactus, wheat, carrot, potato and sweet berry farms. Red sees the start of his bunker but covers it up, assuming it's Jaron's tunnel.
- By day 7 he has a bunker by the cactus farm with brewing, crafting and furnaces, and stashes the loot from his previous two 100-day raids under the floor. Day 8: a room for mapping the base.

### Leeching the farms (days 9–36)

- He finds the storage room and a trade board listing upcoming trades and their countdowns, plus chests of pumpkins, melons, honey bottles and ominous potions. His [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) runs out right next to Red, who somehow doesn't hear him.
- He rebuilds his walls in melons and pumpkins, models a hopper system for siphoning their farms, and taps the automatic sugar cane farm, tunnelling it into his bunker.
- Jaron stands on the roof meeting two bandits who demand his items; Spoke strikes a deal with them for later. He sets up an [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md) as an escape.
- He finds lava, cobblestone and chorus farms (fixing the broken chorus farm right behind Red's back), then rigs the mushroom farm, a key ingredient for fermented spider eyes and so for invisibility potions. Red returns from a flying machine run with diamonds, torches and golden carrots.

### Trades gone wrong (days 37–82)

- From an automatic brewer he takes harming and poison potions.
- At Jaron's trade, Spoke's bandits use their stases to ambush and kill the trader, but Spoke rigged their stases, knowing they'd turn on him, and kills them with poison and harming. He walks back in as if nothing happened, and new trades still go up on the board.
- He rigs more farms into one central chest, and fixes it when it yields nothing. FartSteve08 tries to kill him for interrupting another trade, and Spoke escapes by elytra.
- He changes the coordinates of an upcoming trade on the board to send it elsewhere, then follows Red's flying machine to a meeting where hopper minecarts hide under the snow. His bait, Jepexx, is AFK, and Red leaves.
- He rigs a sniffer farm for pitcher pods and torchflowers, and fails to fish the items towards him at the next trade.

### Unravelling (days 83–100)

- Spoke sulks: therapeutic building, throwing eggs into lava, calling himself a failure.
- A raid bar appears: the Invisible Mafia killed every villager and mobs don't spawn, so the base must have a villager. Spoke tries to steal it, gets stuck in a water chute with Jaron right there, and escapes by breaking glass.
- On days 98–99 raiders place TNT in the base and find his bunker. He crafts and enchants diamond armour, and his own trap accidentally blows up the whole base.
- On day 100, Jaron and Red return to the ruins, confront Spoke, and he escapes.

## Items & mechanics

- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): constantly running out at the worst moments; the mushroom farm feeds it.
- Hopper siphons: Spoke's system for leeching their farms into one chest.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Spoke's escape, and the bandits' rigged stases.
- Trade board: the list of upcoming trades that Spoke reads and rewrites.

## Outcomes

- The number-one base is destroyed by Spoke's own trap.
- The trader, then Spoke's bandit allies, are killed.
- Spoke's feud with Reddoons and Jaron continues.
