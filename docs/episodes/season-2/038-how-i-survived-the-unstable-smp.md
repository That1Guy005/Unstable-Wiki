## Summary

Back from exile, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) rebuild from basic gear, stealing leather and books from local teams and turning one team against its own leader. Egg leaves to focus on his studies, giving Wemmbu his gear, and Wemmbu challenges [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) to the rematch he's waited for since losing the tournament. It ends in a draw neither will admit to.

## Full breakdown

### Starting over

- A player in iron armour threatens Wemmbu, who kills him with his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). They mine basic gear, but with no mobs spawning on the server, leather for an enchanting table is hard to come by.
- At the threatening player's base, Egg distracts the team while Wemmbu steals five leather; when Egg is robbed, they flee, and Wemmbu kills all but one of the team. They enchant their gear at the team's village.

### The cow pen

- Heading towards Spawn for better books, they find an active base packed with players and cows. Digging under the pen to steal two cows, they're caught when the leader notices odd cobblestone.
- Wemmbu kills one pursuer and uses the weakest member as leverage to stop the rest, then steals books from their enchanting table. Sick of their leader's orders, the team kills him instead of Wemmbu.

### Egg logs off

- Egg tells Wemmbu to stop risking himself on side quests. He won't watch the Flame rematch: he's leaving to focus on his studies, and hands over his armour, elytra and rockets before logging off.
- Wemmbu returns to find the team holding a funeral for the leader they hated, kills the rest of them, and loots the base.

### The rematch

- Wemmbu walks into Flame's house and demands a rematch. Flame's combo pops Wemmbu's totem, so Wemmbu moves to open ground and maces him, breaking his shield and nearly killing him.
- Flame retreats inside; Wemmbu griefs the house to drive him out, but Flame splashes him with Slow Falling before another mace hit lands.
- They agree to stop since it would take too long, argue over who won, and end up with Flame giving Wemmbu a tour.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's mace attacks once he gets Flame into open ground.
- Slow Falling: Flame's counter to the mace.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): Wemmbu's pops to Flame's combos.
- Leather and enchanted books: scarce because mobs don't spawn.

## Outcomes

- Eggchan steps away to study; Wemmbu carries on alone.
- Two local teams are wiped out.
- The Wemmbu–Flame rematch ends unresolved (see [Wemmbu and FlameFrags' Rematch](../../events/wemmbu-and-flamefrags-rematch.md)).
