## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) sets out to rebuild a full netherite set, fighting through a dripstone ambush, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s hired hunters, [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md)'s cart-PvP students and the [Bounty Assassin Team](../../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md). He trades at [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md), fights [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) to a draw in their rematch, and walks through Spoke's void traps to claim the last piece.

## Full breakdown

### Ambushes

- Coming home, Flame falls into a dripstone trap, clutches before landing, and fights off the trappers, one of whose [TNT carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) blows up too early. With most of his armour broken and the team dead, he stashes their gear and resolves to get full netherite again.
- With new diamond gear, he's confronted by the team Spoke assembled to kill him. He wipes out most of them, follows a survivor who tells his team they need a netherite sword to kill Flame, kills them all except that player, and takes the netherite sword from their base.

### Merchant City

- Theo's cart-PvP students rob Flame against Theo's orders, and Flame kills them.
- At Merchant City he trades his donkeys' load of loot to a fan for a netherite helmet and totems.
- Leaving, he's attacked by BAT. [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) warns them it's a waste of resources better spent on the Director. Flame's armour fails mid-fight, but he notices the spies' armour has Curse of Vanishing so no one can take it; one spy carries a spare set without it, so Flame kills him and puts it on, and wipes out the spies with their XP bottles. BAT retreats. Among the loot: netherite boots without the curse.

### The rematch

- Wemmbu shows up wanting a rematch, since he's never beaten Flame. It goes well until Wemmbu starts elytra-macing; Flame's shield breaks and his last totem pops, so he brews Slow Falling potions to counter the mace. Wemmbu gives up and they argue about who won.
- Flame shows Wemmbu his base and complains about Spoke's minions, and Wemmbu gives him the coordinates of one of Spoke's bases.

### Spoke's traps

- At the base, Spoke taunts Flame and demands his items, then tries TNT minecarts. Chasing him, Flame drops into a void trap and pearls out, walks into another cart trap, then a void trap they'd been standing on all along, and clutches with the Slow Falling he brewed against Wemmbu.
- Spoke runs, a group of players emerges, and Flame kills them all, getting netherite leggings to finish his full set.

## Items & mechanics

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the trappers', Theo's students' and Spoke's weapon of choice.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's elytra-macing, countered by Slow Falling.
- Curse of Vanishing: BAT's spy armour, which can't be looted.
- Void traps: Spoke's traps, escaped by pearl and Slow Falling.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wemmbu's elytra-mace attacks.

## Outcomes

- Flame completes a full netherite set.
- Spoke's hunters, Theo's students and BAT's spies are wiped out.
- The Wemmbu rematch ends without a winner (see [Wemmbu and FlameFrags' Rematch](../../events/wemmbu-and-flamefrags-rematch.md)).
