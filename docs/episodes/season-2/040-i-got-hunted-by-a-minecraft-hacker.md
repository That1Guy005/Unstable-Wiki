## Summary

A hacker, [TruOriginal](../../characters/truoriginal.md), wipes out [BAT Headquarters](../../places/bat-headquarters.md) for fun. He teleports, shapeshifts, clones himself and nukes blocks from under them. With [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) believed dead, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), [Nufuli](../../characters/nufuli.md) and [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) go into hiding and set up an [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) strike, which finally kills Tru. Then the Director has Nufuli killed.

## Full breakdown

### The hacker

- At an abandoned village, Tru says he killed everyone because he was bored. Leo attacks, Tru teleports, and Leo fires an orbital strike that destroys the village. Tru maces Nufuli and breaks his helmet; without Respiration they can't stay long in the river, so they hide in an ocean monument and escape through the Nether.
- At the infinity base, Derapchu and Ciguato report that Tru has killed everyone and destroyed it. When "Ciguato" one-shots two creepers with an unenchanted bow, which should be impossible, Parrot realises it's Tru in disguise.
- BAT barricades itself in the garden; Tru nukes the blocks beneath them and chases them into a tunnel system. Leo stays behind to sacrifice himself, but no death message appears.

### In hiding

- Parrot, Nufuli and Derapchu regroup, and decide not to report Tru to the admins because of his ties to the Director. Leo turns up alive with broken armour. They build a small temporary base and cover it with lava against x-ray.
- The only way to kill Tru is their orbital cannon, which needs a mirror receiver. At [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md), [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) won't hand over materials without payment, until Leo, an old friend, arrives.
- Two farmers give away their hiding place in a flower farm. Tru clones himself until Nufuli webs him and a [cart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) pops his totem, and Tru flies off.

### The strike

- At 5 AM, Nufuli muses about the last pair of touching stars. In the morning they build the mirror receiver at the cannon, and Nufuli sets it to the angle of those stars.
- Tru attacks, calling himself a god. Parrot lures him to the cannon's target and they fire, but Tru swaps places with Parrot, who escapes with chorus fruit. BAT bluffs that a second charge is ready, and Tru leaves.
- Tru taunts Parrot into attacking, clears his inventory and teleports him a thousand blocks up; Nufuli catches him by flying underneath with a leashed horse, and says he wants to be like Leo.

### Tru's end

- Their friends are missing, with a sign telling them to come to BAT. At the infinity base, Tru says the Director wanted them alive, but he's above the Director. Nufuli fires the orbital cannon again and kills him.
- Nufuli decides to leave BAT, and before he can, an [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) kills him. The Director arrives and says Nufuli was killed because he'd stopped serving his purpose.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): BAT's only weapon able to kill a hacker, aimed with a star-angled mirror receiver.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the weapon that kills Nufuli.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): pops one of Tru's totems.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Tru's weapon against Nufuli.
- Hacks: teleporting, shapeshifting, cloning, nuking blocks and clearing inventories.

## Outcomes

- BAT Headquarters is destroyed and its members killed by Tru.
- TruOriginal is killed by BAT's orbital strike.
- Nufuli is killed on the Director's orders.
