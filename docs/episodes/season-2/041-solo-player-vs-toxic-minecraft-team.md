## Summary

A toxic team led by Razor2gud blows up [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' base and kills all but one of his wolves. Following lodestone compasses from base to base, Flame dismantles their network one bunker and town at a time, even agreeing to downgrade his gear for "fair" fights, and survives Razor's final TNT trap under a colosseum before wiping them out.

## Full breakdown

### The griefing

- Flame's wolf teleports to him growling, which only happens when it's attacked. His base has been blown up and all his other wolves killed.
- At a temporary camp, a branch of the toxic team uses potions to knock his weapon away; he beats them with his fists. They loot and destroy the camp, name their leader as Razor2gud, and one drops a lodestone compass after Flame's wolf kills a member.

### Razor's rules

- The compass leads to Razor, who taunts him and runs, and insists Flame downgrade his gear before they fight. In iron, Flame faces Razor's whole army, strength potions and all, and kills them. A map shows they own many more bases.
- In a cave camp he gives his wolf new armour and a name: Ashen.

### Bunkers and towns

- Expecting TNT after what happened to his base, he targets a bunker first, kills its defenders in waves using looted fireworks and a crossbow, and blows it up.
- In another town he kills everyone in case one carries a lodestone compass, finds a stash of strength potions, and protects Ashen when Razor's team arrives. He crafts [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md), adds Blast Protection to his iron leggings, gathers Turtle Master potions, and wipes out the army. Razor flees again when his armour breaks; Flame levels the town and finds another compass.
- He clears and blows up the rest of the city's towns.

### The colosseum

- At Razor's base, the team complains that Flame's netherite beats their diamond. He reluctantly downgrades for a fair duel in a colosseum, and it goes well, until Razor's team leaves and the TNT hidden under the arena goes off. A Turtle Master potion saves Flame.
- He kills the remaining members and blows up houses in front of Razor, who begs for his life, warning that there are worse people out there. Flame says he'll kill them all too, then kills Razor.

## Items & mechanics

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Flame's answer to Razor's army.
- Lodestone compasses: the trail from one toxic base to the next.
- Turtle Master potions: what saves Flame from the colosseum TNT.
- Ashen: Flame's surviving wolf.

## Outcomes

- Razor2gud's toxic team and its entire network of bases are destroyed.
- Flame's base is lost; his wolf Ashen survives.
