## Summary

Looting the abandoned [BAT Headquarters](../../places/bat-headquarters.md), [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) is hunted by a netherite player with emerald trims and his crew: [WyLL](../../characters/wyll.md). Wyll lures him into a fake alliance and a void trap, so Wemmbu infiltrates Wyll's group in [Mafia City](../../places/mafia-city.md), steals from them, and turns their own [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) on them, killing all but Wyll.

## Full breakdown

### The arrow cannon

- Three players attack Wemmbu at the old BAT base, one in full netherite. When they climb an obsidian pillar, Wemmbu realises why and holds up a [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md) to survive the arrow cannon shot, then flees.
- He spies on one attacker, takes his pets hostage while he's mining, kills two, hands back a dog, then kills the dog and the player.
- The emerald-trimmed player attacks again; when Wemmbu goes in with the mace, the player pulls his stasis.
- At another base an invisible player puts on armour, says Wemmbu is about to die, and blows the base up. Wemmbu survives, kills the two invisible ambushers with his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md), and gets a free netherite set and a lodestone compass.

### Wyll's alliance

- The compass leads to Mafia City, where the emerald-trimmed player and four untrimmed followers wait. He reveals himself as Wyll, tells Wemmbu to step aside before blowing up the spot, and argues he can be trusted because he could have killed Wemmbu and didn't.
- Wyll tours him round his new base, [Ashswag](../../characters/ashswag.md)'s old castle, and asks him to kill Gigasigma37. Wemmbu finds and kills him in an old house in the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md).
- Wyll then admits he lied and traps Wemmbu in the void; Wemmbu barely escapes through MrCube6's old void trap in the BAT base.

### Turning the cannon

- Wemmbu infiltrates Wyll's group invisibly and steals their resources. Suspicious, Wyll makes everyone drink milk; Wemmbu goes for him, pops his totem, and Wyll mends.
- Wemmbu goes back into the castle for more, but forgets to switch his shield and gives himself away. They jump him, and he kills all of them with the arrow cannon. As he's about to kill Wyll, Wyll stasises out.

## Items & mechanics

- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): first used on Wemmbu, then by Wemmbu against Wyll's crew.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): held up to survive the arrow cannon shot.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Wyll's escape, twice.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Wemmbu's infiltration, exposed by Wyll's milk check.
- Void traps: Wyll's betrayal, and MrCube6's old trap as Wemmbu's escape.

## Outcomes

- Wyll's crew is killed, along with Gigasigma37 and the pet-owning attacker.
- Wyll escapes, and becomes Wemmbu's enemy.
