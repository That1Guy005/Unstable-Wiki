## Summary

[Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md) asks [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) to help win back her ice mountain from [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md)'s team, who have rigged it to explode if anyone attacks them. Loppezz wants it done without killing; Wemmbu can't manage that. Fantst blows up the mountain and stasises away, and Wemmbu leaves Loppezz a full netherite set.

## Full breakdown

### The hostage mountain

- Loppezz startles Wemmbu while he's looting a trial chamber, and he maces her before realising who it is. A team has taken over her ice mountain.
- Wemmbu attacks Fantst's team, and Fantst threatens to blow up the mountain if he keeps going. Loppezz tells Wemmbu they're not bluffing.

### Trying it her way

- Sneaking back in invisible, they're spotted by homunculus1031 and thrown in a cell, then annoy him until he leaves. Wemmbu admits he can't solve problems without killing people.
- Loppezz's plan is to defuse the bomb by flooding the base with water, but homunculus catches them halfway through.

### The explosion

- Wemmbu sneaks in again and kills a player who spots him. Homunculus wakes Fantst from being AFK, and the two attack Wemmbu, who nearly kills both before Fantst sets off the explosion. Loppezz watches helplessly.
- Wemmbu kills homunculus; Fantst escapes by stasis just before Wemmbu can finish him.
- Wemmbu gives Loppezz a full set of netherite armour and leaves.

## Outcomes

- Loppezz's ice mountain ([Ice Mountain Civilization](../../places/ice-mountain-civilization.md)) is destroyed.
- homunculus1031 is killed; Fantst escapes.
- Loppezz is left with a full netherite set from Wemmbu.
