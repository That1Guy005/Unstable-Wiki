## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) spends 100 days hiding inside [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md)'s void base, the new home of the [Bounty Assassin Team](../../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md). Discovered on day 16, he survives lockdowns, flooded vents and a ban on invisibility ingredients, then turns to sabotage under the name "the Director", wrecking the base's food, potions and pets before escaping with chorus fruit.

## Full breakdown

### The void base (days 1–15)

- What looks like an abandoned base turns out to be Jumper's, and on day 2 she shows it to BAT. Spoke drinks invisibility and follows them in: a base suspended over the void, patrolled by invisible spies in full netherite, with tree and mushroom farms and a broken mob grinder.
- He eats bread in corners to muffle the noise, eavesdrops on Jumper and [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), and is nearly caught by [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md), Parrot and Jumper. He finds a vent network, a spies' bedroom with invisibility potions, the meeting room, a map of nothing but numbers, a storage room, a cemetery, and the server racks and control room that run lockdowns, lights and airlocks.
- He listens in on a meeting of Parrot, Derapchu, Jumper and [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), and, short on food, finds the farm.

### Hunted (days 16–30)

- Parrot catches him eating a carrot and orders a lockdown, believing the intruder is the Director. Spoke hides in the vents while Jumper and Derapchu search them.
- An escape attempt fails for lack of rockets; Parrot spots him in the ceiling and he jukes Leo. Back in the control room, he lifts the lockdown and turns everything off, plunging the base into darkness.
- Parrot has the vents flooded so anyone inside drowns unless they have a Respiration III helmet, bans every invisibility ingredient, and then removes all sugar cane: no paper, so no rockets, and the nearest void hole is 50,000 blocks away. Spoke finds enough potions for five days.
- Not knowing who the Director is but liking the name, he starts calling himself the Director.

### Running dry (days 30–65)

- Hunting for an ender chest, he finds he can't mine one through the mining fatigue, gets locked out of his own hideout, and finds the cows massacred and no milk left. The map turns out to be flipped and useless.
- He nearly drowns in the graveyard, brews potions only for a spy to wander in and drink them, fishes, and gets himself trapped in the mob grinder after crafting rockets.
- He swaps important items for junk, throws out the food, breaks the auto-brewer and the fishing rods, and builds laughing-face emojis on the floor. Parrot sets up a decoy room, and Spoke walks into it, confirming he's still there.

### Sabotage (days 66–93)

- He tries to start a famine by breaking every crop, drops all the saplings and bone meal, destroys the nether wart and the brewer again, and removes all the lapis.
- After a full minute of mining, he opens a hole to the void, accidentally drops a cat named Whiskers into it, and dangles another, Kittens, over it on a lead. He pours water into the filtration room and crafts TNT and a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md).
- Jumper discovers the map has been tampered with. Spoke posts signs: "I AM THE DIRECTOR", then "THE DIRECTOR IS HERE". When Jumper tries to rescue the pets dangling over the void, he cuts the leads and they all die. He tries to seal them in with dirt, but Jumper has milk and mines out.
- His void holes kill the pig-riding spy; Jumper falls into one but escapes by elytra. He watches Parrot stash armour in a chest marked with an oak door.

### Caught (days 94–100)

- He leads BAT from their meeting into a cart trap, but it doesn't kill anyone, and they chase him around the base. He tries to turn Leo against the others and fails.
- Captured and placed in custody, he escapes with chorus fruit, and BAT has to accept he's gone.

## Items & mechanics

- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Spoke's lifeline, and the ingredients Parrot bans.
- Sugar cane: banned to stop Spoke making paper, and so rockets.
- Control room: lockdowns, lights and airlocks, all switched off by Spoke.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Spoke's cart trap for BAT's meeting.
- Void holes: Spoke's traps, which kill a spy and the pets.
- Chorus fruit: his escape from custody.

## Outcomes

- BAT's void base is sabotaged; its pets and a spy are killed.
- Spoke's "I am the Director" signs send BAT after the wrong person.
- Spoke escapes custody.
