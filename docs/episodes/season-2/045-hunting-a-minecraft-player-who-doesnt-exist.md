## Summary

[ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and the [Bounty Assassin Team](../../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) chase the Director: through a zombie trap, [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md), a fake Director ([MrCube6](../../characters/mrcube6.md)), a fake intruder ([SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)) and a planted clue. With two weeks to guess who he is, Parrot falls out with [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), gives up the hunt to save his friends, and refuses to guess. The Director drinks milk: he is [Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md), alive all along.

## Full breakdown

### The obsidian room

- Picking up from the hacker episode, BAT chases the Director across the ruined headquarters. He escapes by [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) and asks Parrot not to follow; they follow anyway.
- In an obsidian room, the Director releases zombies in full netherite. BAT kills them and keeps the gear, and Parrot wonders where the Director found that much netherite when the Mafia mined out nearly all of it.

### Following the money

- At Redstone Town, [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) won't list his bulk buyers for privacy reasons, until Parrot bribes him with a shulker of the zombies' netherite. 4C remembers an invisible buyer in armour like the Director's.
- They ambush the buyer in an abandoned town and force him to reveal himself: MrCube6, claiming to be the Director. When he gives them nothing, they try to kill him, until the real Director appears and criticises MrCube.
- The Director gives a final warning and a deadline: guess who he is within two weeks, or bad things happen. He sets off TNT; Parrot's elytra breaks as they fly up, and [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) drops him a new one, saving his life and earning her way back into BAT.

### The void base

- Jumper's void base is in the same abandoned town where Parrot first met the Director. A spy brings a book: the Director will meet Parrot in a week, anywhere he likes, since he's always watching.
- Invisibility particles inside the base lead to a lockdown and days of strange happenings. Parrot is convinced it's the Director; it's Spoke, claiming the title. Cornered and interrogated, Spoke says he knows nothing and the hunt was a waste of time, then escapes with chorus fruit.
- At the meeting, with spies watching every suspect, the Director "accidentally" shows Spoke's chestplate. Jumper confirms Spoke was elsewhere; Parrot suspects Jumper, but she passes a trust test.

### Leo snaps

- Spying on Spoke goes nowhere. On the last day Leo sends Jumper and Derapchu away and attacks Parrot: Parrot doesn't care about BAT, only the Director, and BAT was normal and rich until he came.
- Knocked off, Parrot drifts towards the void on slow falling and an elytra with no rockets. He tells Leo he misses the supportive friend Leo used to be, and repeats something Leo once said, which calms Leo and saves him.
- Parrot tells Leo to leave, and goes back to the snow house he built with Wifies.

### The choice

- Jumper and Derapchu find him there. The Director arrives, says the chestplate was shown deliberately, and when Jumper and Derapchu chase him they fall into a powdered-snow trap.
- Parrot begs Leo to rescue them, and Leo agrees only when Parrot promises to stop chasing the Director. Leo flies in on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md); Jumper and Derapchu refuse to be saved until he explains the deal.
- Parrot tells the Director he chooses not to guess.

### Wifies

- The Director takes Parrot to the snow house and asks him to make the dog stand up. It won't: it isn't Parrot's dog. Parrot asks once more what happened to Wifies, and the Director drinks milk: he is Wifies.
- Wifies faked his death, and sent the assassins to "kill" Parrot while sparing him, because he wanted to study how Parrot works and behaves. Parrot was never in real danger; everything was done to protect him.
- His plan is to create a world where nobody has to fear dying ever again, and he asks Parrot to follow him. Parrot refuses, knowing Wifies won't kill him; Wifies says that's true, but he won't hesitate to kill Parrot's friends. Realising Wifies has changed, Parrot follows him.

## Items & mechanics

- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): the Director's escape route.
- Netherite zombies: the Director's trap, and a mystery, since netherite is almost gone from the server.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): Leo's rescue of Jumper and Derapchu.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the Director's disguise, ended with a bucket of milk.
- Powdered snow: the Director's trap.

## Outcomes

- The Director is revealed to be Wifies, who faked his death.
- Parrot stops chasing the Director and follows Wifies.
- Jumper rejoins BAT; Leo and Parrot part on bad terms.
