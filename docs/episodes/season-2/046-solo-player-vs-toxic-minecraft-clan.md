## Summary

A toxic clan raids [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' camp and kidnaps his wolf, Ashen. Flame follows their path through seven bases, each built around a different trick (strength, poison arrows, harming arrows, soul speed, underwater tridents, a dark ambush), and finally faces their leader T0X1N and his six commanders in a battle for Ashen.

## Full breakdown

### The trail of bases

- Coming back to camp, Flame finds players griefing it and taking Ashen. In their first base they fight with strength potions; Flame death-bans them all, blows up the base, and follows the path to the next.
- Base two relies on poison-tipped arrows and base three on harming arrows; both are wiped out and blown up. Flame upgrades his armour at an enchanting table and destroys another base before he finds where Ashen is held.
- The next base is ringed with soul sand, and its players wear soul speed boots with speed and weaving effects, so cobwebs don't stop them and they hit him with slowness. Flame kills them with the harming arrows he took earlier, loots and blows it up.
- An underwater base full of water breathing, turtle shells and tridents falls easily, and Flame fills their houses with obsidian. In the last, he climbs down a ladder into darkness and is ambushed from behind doors; he barely wins and blows it up.

### T0X1N's battle

- T0X1N and his six commanders, one for each base, offer a battle for Ashen. Flame kills several, and when his helmet and chestplate are about to break, he puts on armour from the dead and finishes the rest.
- T0X1N threatens to kill Ashen unless Flame drops his gear. Flame drops his potions and water bucket and is allowed to keep his armour and weapons, but T0X1N demands a screenshot of his inventory and has him climb a ladder above a pit of chickens.
- T0X1N splashes him with Wind Charged and harming potions that launch him high into the sky. Flame clutches with a [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md), kills T0X1N, and frees Ashen.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Flame's clutch after T0X1N's launch trap.
- Tipped arrows: poison and harming, turned back on the clan.
- Soul speed and soul sand: the fifth base's movement trick.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): the underwater base's weapon.

## Outcomes

- T0X1N and his whole clan, with all seven of their bases, are destroyed.
- Ashen is rescued.
