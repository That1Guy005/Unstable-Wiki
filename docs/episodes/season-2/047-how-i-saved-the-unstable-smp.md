## Summary

Hunting elytra to sell, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) is talked by Baablu into respawning the ender dragon, despite [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md)'s warnings. Baablu was using him: he and [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) plan to duplicate dragons and destroy [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md). Wemmbu teams up with Minute, regears in a far-off end city, and beats their team, popping all of Fantst and Baablu's totems.

## Full breakdown

### Baablu's request

- At a stronghold Wemmbu finds people living inside, including Baablu, who warns that someone is keeping the End portal for themselves and asks Wemmbu to respawn the ender dragon. They need end crystals.
- They set stases and find crystals in a stash in the Wormhole Vault, leaving by [stasis](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md).

### Minute's warning

- In the End, Minute asks why Wemmbu's there and refuses to let him respawn the dragon. Wemmbu fights him, spawning withers as a distraction; Baablu joins in.
- Minute warns him about what he's about to do. Wemmbu respawns the dragon anyway and helps Baablu attack Minute, until Fantst appears and attacks Wemmbu. Baablu admits he was just using him.

### Allies

- On the outer islands, Minute explains: Baablu wants to duplicate the ender dragon and use them to destroy Spawn. Wemmbu doesn't care about Spawn, but he does care that Fantst and Baablu are on the same team. They realise they'd have won if they hadn't wasted resources fighting each other.
- Fantst's team jumps them; low on supplies, they try to flee, are followed, and kill all but one.
- 100,000 blocks out they find two unlooted end cities, one with a ship, and loot them, but the survivor they spared has followed. Fantst, Baablu and the team attack, and now geared up, Wemmbu and Minute fight them off and restock.

### Three dragons

- Back at the main island there are now three ender dragons. Wemmbu nearly kills one with his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md).
- Fantst, Baablu and their team attack. Wemmbu and Minute kill several members and pop all of Fantst's and Baablu's totems; Fantst escapes with the survivors.
- His job done, Wemmbu returns to the Overworld.

## Items & mechanics

- End crystals: needed to respawn the dragon, taken from the Wormhole Vault.
- Duplicated ender dragons: Baablu and Fantst's weapon against Spawn.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wemmbu's original goal; the end cities provide them.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): used on a dragon and on Fantst's team.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): how they leave the vault.

## Outcomes

- Fantst and Baablu's plan is beaten; both escape after losing all their totems.
- Wemmbu and MinuteTech end as allies.
- Three ender dragons are loose in the End.
