## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) takes on a toxic civilization led by VEN0M, a former Mafia diamond-trim with a mace. Living at [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md)'s base ([Lomedy's Farm](../../places/lomedys-farm.md)), he trains Lomedy, raids the clan's vault, borrows a mace from Lomedy's dead teammate, and sends TNT dupers into their castle before beating VEN0M with almost no armour on.

## Full breakdown

### VEN0M

- A group of toxic players orders Flame to drop his items; he wipes them out, takes refuge at Lomedy's base, and wipes out the next wave too. One drops a lodestone compass.
- It leads to a castle on an island in the ocean. When Flame asks for their leader, VEN0M appears with a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): like Flame, he was a diamond-trim in the Invisible Mafia. Unimpressed, Flame fights the whole army at once; with only VEN0M left, Flame switches swords and starts winning, and VEN0M runs, calling it a stalemate.

### Training Lomedy

- Back at the base, more toxic players are pressing Lomedy; Flame kills them all except one, who trades a map for his life.
- So Lomedy won't make him look bad, Flame teaches him PvP, including using cobwebs to slow opponents.

### The vault

- The map leads to one of the clan's vaults inside their base. Flame drags Lomedy along, kills the guards outside, inside and in the vault, and they loot good gear. When more guards come, he uses looted [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and a flame bow, then clears the rest of the base.
- Lomedy reveals his old teammate was a Mafia member with a mace, and Flame sends him a thousand blocks out to set up TNT dupers.
- VEN0M and his players confront Flame over the missing loot, mocking his "immortal demon" reputation. Flame uses Slow Falling arrows to stop VEN0M macing, Lomedy brings his dead teammate's mace, and Flame kills the clan; VEN0M runs again.
- Lomedy explains he's been alone since his teammate died and wants to join Flame's journey, but Flame says he works better alone.

### The castle falls

- Flame starts Lomedy's TNT dupers, which creep towards the castle, pearls in and attacks the guards as the TNT arrives and blows up the base.
- With only VEN0M left, Flame strips off all his armour except the chestplate and still maces VEN0M to death.
- He returns the mace to Lomedy, collects Ashen, and leaves.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): VEN0M's weapon, and Lomedy's dead teammate's mace, lent to Flame.
- TNT dupers: Lomedy's moving TNT machine that destroys the castle.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): looted from the vault and used on its guards.
- Slow Falling arrows: Flame's counter to VEN0M's mace.
- Cobwebs: Lomedy's first PvP lesson.

## Outcomes

- VEN0M is killed and his toxic civilization destroyed.
- Lomedy becomes Flame's friend, though Flame still insists on working alone.
