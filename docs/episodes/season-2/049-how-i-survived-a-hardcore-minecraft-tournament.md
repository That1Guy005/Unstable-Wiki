## Summary

[Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) enters AJTHEBOLD's PvP tournament for another shot at [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md), with no [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md) allowed. He beats Epaxialfx, survives [WyLL](../../characters/wyll.md)'s god apples, and takes Wyll's advice to beat [Sharpness](../../characters/sharpness.md), but in the final Flame reads his mace patterns and wins three totems to none.

## Full breakdown

### The rules

- The tournament decides who is the strongest PvPer. Each fighter has three totems; when the last pops, a ghost-totem glitch and a comparator pull their stasis back to the lobby and they're out. No elytra, a big disadvantage for Wemmbu.

### Early rounds

- Round one against Epaxialfx: Wemmbu pillars above him to land mace hits and pops all three totems.
- Elsewhere, Flame beats Willsion, Wyll and [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md) win, [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) accidentally kills his opponent outright, and Sharpness beats [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) with his fists. Wemmbu lends Horace a pickaxe for arena clean-up when he sees him using a wooden one.
- At the festival Flame teases him, and Wyll predicts their next match. Wyll brings stacks of god apples and slow-falling arrows, but after some close calls Wemmbu wins.

### Wyll's advice

- Wyll tells Wemmbu how to beat Sharpness, and not to use his mace techniques while Flame is watching, "because I hate sharpness more than I hate you".
- On an ice arena, Wemmbu uses mace techniques only while Flame is away. Down to his last totem, he follows Wyll's advice and wins. Flame wins his semi-final too.

### The final

- Wemmbu mixes mace and melee, but Flame quickly reads the pattern, blocks the mace hits, and combos him for the first pop. Wemmbu answers by pillaring and macing him repeatedly.
- Out of wind charges, Wemmbu launches himself with armour stands and his Wind Burst mace, and Flame, expecting it, dodges. Wemmbu's helmet and leggings break; Flame's armour breaks too, and Wemmbu pops him.
- Wemmbu's chestplate breaks, Flame shoots him for the second pop, and the third pop wins Flame the tournament. Flame teases him afterwards.

## Items & mechanics

- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): three per fighter; the last pop pulls your stasis.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the tournament's elimination system.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's pillar-and-smash style, and his Wind Burst launches.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): running out is what makes Wemmbu predictable.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): banned, taking away much of his mace game.

## Outcomes

- FlameFrags wins the tournament (see [The PvP Tournament](../../events/the-pvp-tournament.md)).
- Wemmbu beats Epaxialfx, Wyll and Sharpness to reach the final.
