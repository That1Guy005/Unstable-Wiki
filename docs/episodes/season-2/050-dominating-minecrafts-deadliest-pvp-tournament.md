## Summary

The same tournament from [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' side. He cruises past Willsion, reads [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md)'s playstyle, buys Blast Protection to counter [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md)'s carts, and beats [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) three totems to none in the final.

## Full breakdown

### Round one

- The rules are the same: three totems each, and a fighter whose last totem pops has their stasis pulled back to the lobby.
- Flame beats Willsion easily, then watches [WyLL](../../characters/wyll.md), Ferre, Wemmbu, [Sharpness](../../characters/sharpness.md) (conexion) and Theo, who actually kills his opponent. AJTHEBOLD has [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) clean the arena.
- Flame teases Wemmbu by taking his shulker boxes and hitting him with his fists, then watches Wemmbu beat Wyll.

### Ferre and Theo

- Before beating Ferre, Flame recaps his playstyle and history.
- Talking to contestants at the minigames, Flame realises how strong Theo's carts are, and goes to [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md) for Blast Protection. Against Theo, he swaps between Blast Protection diamond leggings when carts are coming and Protection IV netherite leggings for melee, and wins.

### The final

- As in Wemmbu's video: Flame reads Wemmbu's mace-and-melee pattern, combos him for the first pop, survives a pillar-and-mace counter, and dodges the armour-stand Wind Burst launch he saw coming.
- Both sets of armour break; Flame shoots Wemmbu for the second pop and takes the third to win, then teases him.

## Items & mechanics

- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): three per fighter.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Theo's carts, countered by swapping in Blast Protection leggings.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's main weapon, read and blocked.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): how eliminated fighters leave the arena.

## Outcomes

- FlameFrags wins the tournament, beating Willsion, Ferre, Theo and Wemmbu (see [The PvP Tournament](../../events/the-pvp-tournament.md)).
