## Summary

[Wifies](../../characters/wifies.md) locks [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) inside [Paragon](../../places/paragon.md), a "perfect" prison built to keep him safe forever. Parrot's escape attempts all fail, so he plays along while looking for a way to turn Wifies, learning the guards are friends Wifies is blackmailing. In a TNT pressure-plate chamber, Parrot gives Wifies the spyglass one last time. Wifies runs for it and dies in the blast, and Parrot walks out.

## Full breakdown

### The cell

- Wifies brings food to Parrot's cell. Using smuggled items, Parrot hooks him off his flying machine with a fishing rod and rides a [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md) onto it, only to find the prison is far bigger than one cell.
- Wifies says escape is impossible, and that Parrot will come to understand: they'll build a world where Parrot doesn't have to worry about dying, and Wifies doesn't have to worry about Parrot dying.
- Wifies clears Parrot's items, finds one boat left, and tells him to throw away his spyglass. Parrot angrily hurls it off the tower and asks why Wifies would want it.

### Escape attempts

- Parrot crafts a wooden sword and attacks; when Wifies' totem pops, he teleports away. Parrot runs to retrieve the spyglass he threw.
- He bridges towards the walls, but two guards splash him with Slow Falling and push him off. The Nether is walled in too. He finds Wifies' house and burns it down, builds his own, and goes fishing; Wifies comes fishing with him.
- He trades piglins for pearls, sets a stasis at his house, and fakes an escape over the Nether roof to draw the guards. Once the stasis fires, he taunts Wifies and towers up the walls. Wifies tells him it isn't the walls keeping him there, it's himself, and talks him down before he "hurts himself".

### Playing along

- Exploring, Parrot realises the prison is an extension of Wifies' mind, which is what makes it inescapable. He decides to get close to Wifies and wait for a chance to turn on him, telling Wifies he's given up and doesn't care how long it takes. Wifies is surprised he gave up so quickly.
- For weeks they gather resources and play hide and seek and parkour, building their own world as they once promised.
- Wifies opens the wall to show him the dandelions outside, explaining he knew about Jumper's dandelion from when BAT imprisoned MrCube. He has leverage over [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) (he'll leak her spies' identities if she disobeys), and that's his principle: control what someone cares about and you control them. It's why the guards work 24/7.
- Parrot traps a guard: it's Jumper, who reveals she, [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) and [Derapchu](../../characters/derapchu.md) are all guards, and begs him not to escape for her spies' sake. Wifies drops in and dismisses her. Parrot suggests they leave together, but it doesn't work on him.

### The pressure plates

- Wifies has been tracking Parrot's entire inventory and recites it, then clears it again, missing only the spyglass.
- He takes Parrot back to the tower, to a chamber like the one MrCube once used, empties his own inventory, and has them both stand on pressure plates: if either steps off, the TNT below kills them both.
- Parrot shows him the spyglass and explains why he never let it despawn. Wifies says he killed that version of himself, because he had to, and calls for the guards. Parrot says he never gave up on him, and will remember him as who he used to be, not for the people he killed.
- Asked to hand it over, Parrot says "It's all yours", just as Wifies did before his chunk ban, drops the spyglass on the plate and steps off.
- As a clock ticks, Wifies remembers their friendship and runs for the spyglass, seemingly himself again. He reaches it as the TNT explodes, and dies. Parrot looks back and sighs.
- Parrot gathers some food and blocks, looks back one last time, and leaves Paragon to start over.

## Items & mechanics

- The spyglass: the symbol of their friendship, thrown away, kept, and returned.
- Pressure-plate TNT: Wifies' final trap, and his death.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Parrot's first escape from the cell.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Parrot's stasis for his fake escape.
- Slow Falling: the guards' way of pushing him off the walls.

## Outcomes

- Wifies dies in the TNT explosion, reaching for the spyglass.
- Jumper, Leo and Derapchu are revealed to have been forced to guard Paragon.
- Parrot leaves Paragon with almost nothing.
