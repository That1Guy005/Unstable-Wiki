## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' best friend [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) has vanished, and [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)'s bounty hunters, [LAW](../../factions/law.md), know why but won't say. Flame spies on them, learns crossbow carting from [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), wrecks their auction, and uses Leo as bait to find the [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) that leads to Jaden. He finds Jaden starving in [The Abandoned Civilization](../../places/the-abandoned-civilization.md), and Jaden says it was [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) who put him there.

## Full breakdown

### Lettuce's warning

- Asking around, Flame hears from Lettuce that Jaden isn't someone he should be looking for. Following Lettuce invisibly to his bounty hunters' base, Flame learns Lettuce worked to have Jaden captured, and attacks.
- He pops some totems, but weakness arrows drain his resources. Lettuce proposes a truce if Flame stops looking; Flame agrees just to end the fight.
- Spying on LAW's meetings, he learns Leo beat five of them in a 1v5, and that they're holding an auction in a few days.

### Leo's lesson

- At the ruined [BAT Headquarters](../../places/bat-headquarters.md), Leo thinks Flame has come to kill him. Flame wants to learn Leo's PvP, for an IOU: using a crossbow and flint and steel to set off [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) instantly. They practise together.
- At the auction, Flame's crossbow carting wipes out a good number of players. Lettuce argues killing him won't get Flame the answer, and that he can't let anyone else go through what he went through, so Flame lets him go.

### The bait

- For a stack of experience bottles, Leo agrees to turn himself in so they can see where LAW takes prisoners. LAW marches him to an ender pearl cannon, where Leo drops the act, fights, and breaks the scaffolding up to it while the lawmen stack up.
- Flame pays Leo the other half and tries to stop LAW escaping, but too late. He uses the cannon himself and lands in the Pirate Islands ([The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md)).

### Jaden

- Flame chases the lawmen island to island, carting them on the last one until they flee into a castle and drop down the hole into the Abandoned Civilization. Lettuce insists Jaden has to stay locked up for everyone's good; after Flame kills one more, Lettuce concedes this round and flees.
- Flame finds Jaden in a house and feeds him before he starves. When Flame says he dealt with LAW for him, Jaden doesn't know what he's talking about. Asked who put him there, Jaden replies: "It was Wemmbu."

## Items & mechanics

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): crossbow carting, learned from Leo.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): LAW's route to their prisoners in the Pirate Islands.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Flame's way into LAW's meetings.
- Weakness arrows: LAW's counter to Flame.

## Outcomes

- Jaden_MAN is found alive in the Abandoned Civilization.
- Flame learns that Wemmbu was the one who trapped Jaden.
- LAW loses several lawmen; Lettuce escapes.
