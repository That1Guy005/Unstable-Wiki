## Summary

[Sharpness](../../characters/sharpness.md) steals one of [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s maces and flees by [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) to the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md), where rockets don't work and ender chests come back empty. Chasing him with a returning [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), Wemmbu finds glitched terrain, ghostly "echoes" of the past (including the founding of the Zam Empire), and fights Sharpness repeatedly, twice saving him from the void so he doesn't lose the mace for good.

## Full breakdown

### The theft

- Eggchan is back on the server. At the spot where Egg logged off, Sharpness challenges Wemmbu, throws a potion, steals one of his maces and escapes by stasis.
- Nobody knows where he went, until [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) admits Sharpness commissioned a pearl cannon. 4C takes them to it, and it sends them to the edge of the Far Lands.

### Far Lands City

- They spot Sharpness by Far Lands City. In a barely standing house, he breach-swaps and rebuilds broken blocks to dodge Wemmbu's mace; after a while he starts blocking the hits. Wemmbu blows up the house with TNT and they fight on in a tight tunnel. Sharpness nearly kills him, Egg helps him escape, and Sharpness leaves.
- The glitched Far Lands break firework rockets and wipe their ender chests, so Wemmbu flies with [wind charges](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md) instead. Glitched chests hold nothing valuable, and old structures appear in glitched versions.

### Echoes

- A struggling-to-respond resident points them in Sharpness's direction, where they find a perfectly intact [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md). Ghost players resembling [Pangi](../../characters/pangi.md), [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md), [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md), Sorhin and [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) enter the castle: an echo of the past, replaying the empire's founding.
- Sharpness attacks with a mace and retreats into another house. Wemmbu plays passive until his helmet breaks, then leaves the Far Lands' edge for the surface, where, after several near-deaths, he stashes his mace in his ender chest. Annoyed, Sharpness demands a rematch in 30 minutes, breaks the ender chest and leaves.

### The rematch

- On floating platforms above the void, Wemmbu makes obsidian from lava and water, and crafts an eye of ender from Egg's blaze rod to build a new ender chest. With his mace back and gear repaired, he accepts.
- They fight in a resource-rich mineshaft; Wemmbu runs out of food and Egg brings more. He knocks Sharpness into the void, and when Sharpness misses his pearl, Wemmbu fishes him out with a rod, since if Sharpness dies, the stolen mace is gone too.
- Out of food again, Wemmbu eats cave spiders, maces Sharpness into the void a second time, and saves him again. Sharpness says he'll return the mace only when Wemmbu does something with it, breaks the ender chest, and leaves.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): the stolen mace, and Sharpness's breach-swapping.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): 4CVIT's commission for Sharpness, the way into the Far Lands.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Wemmbu's substitute for broken rockets.
- Ender chest: emptied by the Far Lands glitch, rebuilt from scratch, and broken twice by Sharpness.
- Fishing rod: how Wemmbu saves Sharpness from the void.

## Outcomes

- Sharpness keeps Wemmbu's mace, on condition.
- Wemmbu and Egg are stranded in the Far Lands without rockets.
- Echoes reveal the Far Lands replaying the server's past.
