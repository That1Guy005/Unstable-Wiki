## Summary

Deeper in the Far Lands, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) are hunted by [The Avengers](../../factions/the-avengers.md), an alliance of everyone Wemmbu has wronged. A golden temple leads them to Lakerek, who carries items from [The Forge](../../places/the-forge.md) and attacks with orbital strikes. A final battle turns [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) into a temporary ally, and Flame gives Wemmbu his mace back. Wemmbu resolves to find the Forge.

## Full breakdown

### The Avengers

- The Avengers are FlameFrags, [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md), Willsion, [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), [WyLL](../../characters/wyll.md), [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) and Baablu, all after Wemmbu's ban. Fantst finds his hiding place, but Wemmbu escapes and they give up.
- A dungeon holds nothing useful. They cross "gravel lands" to spiky mountains, where an echo replays their own deaths by starvation. Wemmbu's [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md) breaks, so they walk.

### The golden temple

- In a lusher stretch of gravel lands stands a huge golden temple with five statues lining its path. Inside are two people; Wemmbu punches them to check they aren't ghosts, then robs them.
- One of them, Mxsterwu, gives him Protection I boots and explains that Lakerek has items from the Forge, and will hand them over if Wemmbu beats him.

### Lakerek

- Lakerek wakes up and attacks, and the floor opens into a huge pit. Mid-fight he gives Wemmbu golden apples and XP to mend his armour, to prepare him for the next stage: [orbital strikes](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md).
- Wemmbu survives the barrage and restocks. When Lakerek can't fire any more orbitals, he gives up and lets Wemmbu kill him. Wemmbu kills Mxsterwu too and loots both.

### Battle in the spikes

- The Avengers attack again, Spongs in weaker gear and without Flame, Sharpness or Jaden. One by one they leave, except Fantst, whom Wemmbu beats mercilessly. Fantst says he knows about the stolen mace because [Sharpness](../../characters/sharpness.md) is on his team, and hands over his boots and Egg a shield.
- Back in the spiky mountains, Flame and Jaden attack, then the whole team. Wemmbu fights Sharpness and Wyll; when the team turns on Flame too, Wemmbu teams up with him. Egg passes them shulkers of armour.
- Sharpness escapes. Flame gives Wemmbu his mace back, saying he doesn't need it.
- The shulkers came from their secret hideout, Egg explains. Wemmbu says they're not leaving the Far Lands: they have to find the Forge.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Lakerek's orbital strikes in the temple pit.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): returned by Flame at the end.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wemmbu's breaks, leaving him on foot.
- Forge items: Lakerek's loot, Wemmbu's first glimpse of the Forge.

## Outcomes

- Lakerek and Mxsterwu are killed.
- The Avengers' attack fails; Wemmbu briefly allies with FlameFrags and gets his mace back.
- Wemmbu and Egg stay in the Far Lands to search for the Forge.
