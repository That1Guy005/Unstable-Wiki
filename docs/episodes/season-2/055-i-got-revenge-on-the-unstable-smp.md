## Summary

[Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) wants revenge on [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md), so he and [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) form [The Avengers](../../factions/the-avengers.md) from Wemmbu's old enemies and follow him into the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md). Flame tricks [Sharpness](../../characters/sharpness.md) out of the stolen mace with a fake dupe glitch, takes over as leader, and plans a box ambush. When the team turns on itself, Flame betrays them, saves Wemmbu, and gives him his mace back. Then Flame's stasis is pulled.

## Full breakdown

### Assembling the team

- Flame wanted to handle it alone, but Jaden insists on a team of Wemmbu's former enemies: [WyLL](../../characters/wyll.md), Willsion, [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md), [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) and Baablu.
- [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) tells them Wemmbu is hunting Sharpness in the Far Lands. Flame and Jaden set a remote stasis chamber, and the team takes the pearl cannon to the start of the Far Lands, past the sites of Wemmbu and Sharpness's fights.
- They spot [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) and jump Wemmbu, who flies off. Baablu finds him again, but Spongs cobwebs the team and Wemmbu escapes.

### Sharpness and the fake dupe

- Splitting up to escape Spongs and Willsion, Flame and Jaden find Sharpness. Flame attacks for the mace; Sharpness stashes it in his ender chest, and Jaden recruits him instead.
- Sharpness says it's Wemmbu's mace and he's here to dupe it. Since the Far Lands are full of glitches, Flame pretends to know a dupe, "duping" his two netherite swords. Sharpness falls for it and hands over the mace; after a chase they agree Flame will return it once Wemmbu is dealt with.

### Flame takes charge

- In the gravel lands the rest of the team is badly beaten. Fed up, Flame declares himself leader: lure Wemmbu into a box where everyone will be hiding.
- Fantst says Wemmbu asked to meet at the ice spikes, so they build the box at the bottom of the biome. Flame gives Jaden the mace and they go to meet Wemmbu on top of an ice spike.

### The betrayal

- Wemmbu seems to be losing when someone breaks the box open early, and the team starts fighting each other instead.
- Flame asks Jaden for the mace, as if to use it on Wemmbu, and maces the team instead. Low on gear, he and Wemmbu are saved when Egg drops shulkers left inside the box. Flame pops all of their totems and they leave.
- Flame gives Wemmbu his mace back, since he doesn't use maces as his main weapon. Wemmbu wonders if they're friends now; Flame tells him not to get it twisted.
- Flame starts walking back, and his pearl stasis is suddenly pulled.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's stolen mace, passed from Sharpness to Flame to Jaden and back to Wemmbu.
- [Fake dupe glitch](../../mechanics/exploits.md): Flame's trick, made believable by the Far Lands' glitches.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Flame and Jaden's remote stasis, pulled at the end.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): the team's way into the Far Lands.

## Outcomes

- The Avengers turn on each other and fall apart (see [The Avengers' Hunt for Wemmbu](../../events/the-avengers-hunt-for-wemmbu.md)).
- Wemmbu gets his mace back from Flame.
- Flame's stasis is pulled, leading into the LAW trap.
