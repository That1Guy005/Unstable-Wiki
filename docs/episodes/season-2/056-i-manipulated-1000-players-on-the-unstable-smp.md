## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) founds the [Spoke Galactic Empire](../../factions/spoke-galactic-empire.md) and gathers a huge following of players. He kidnaps [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) to build his castle, reunites with [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), and sends his army against Sleepy Nation and [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md), but his citizens are violent, assassination attempts never stop, and the army finally turns on him.

## Full breakdown

### Founding the empire

- After attacking Baconnwaffles0 for calling him broke, Spoke sails to a piece of land and declares it the capital of the Spoke Galactic Empire, with four rules: "No Treason", "Never Hit Spoke", "Don't touch the Rules", and "Have fun".
- He attacks Yeah_Jaron for revenge; Jaron escapes, and they take his cows (neither the cows nor the players taking them are seen again). He punishes citizens who kill others, such as Odessify_Sigma.
- He raids Horace's secret base and brings him to the capital to teach the citizens to build, and survives an assassination attempt.
- An invisible diamond-trim stalking him turns out to be Mapicc. Spoke shows him the obsidian highway at [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md), used by players leaving the Mafia's huge spawn prison.

### A city at war with itself

- They return to find the capital under attack by its own citizens: houses burning, players killed, assassination attempts everywhere.
- Spoke leads the army to the remains of Sleepy Nation, which once joined the Invisible Mafia, and orders them to attack Hannahxxrose. She pops several totems before escaping through her escape route. Spoke notices how few of his soldiers have armour, and survives a respawn-anchor bomb.
- Horace has finished the castle, built on top of a gravestone. A rival trying to build a taller house turns out to be a trap; Spoke pops a totem and survives. He concludes the assassinations happen because they haven't had enough conquering and violence.

### Losing control

- He leads the troops into the End to find MinuteTech, realising too late he's about to grief Minute's End base. The players overwhelm Minute, and Spoke brings them back to the Overworld to limit the damage.
- He slowly loses control of his army, and in the end they turn on him.

## Items & mechanics

- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): popped in the house-trap assassination attempt.
- Respawn anchor: used in a bombing attempt on Spoke.
- The four rules: the Spoke Galactic Empire's whole constitution.

## Outcomes

- The Spoke Galactic Empire is founded, and its army turns on Spoke.
- Sleepy Nation and MinuteTech's End base are attacked.
- Mapicc rejoins Spoke; Horace is forced to build for the empire.
