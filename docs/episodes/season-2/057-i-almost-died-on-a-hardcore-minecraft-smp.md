## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' pulled stasis drops him into a [LAW](../../factions/law.md) void trap. He fights his way out, then helps [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) collect a full netherite set in exchange for Jaden's mace, but every move seems to bring [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)'s lawmen down on them. At LAW's base Flame survives a TNT box and wardens, and learns Jaden has been setting him up all along.

## Full breakdown

### The void trap

- The stasis teleports Flame straight into a trap built to drop him into the void. He clutches, and Lettuce's lawmen open fire to knock him off.
- He places a block above himself; LAW blows it up with TNT. Flame knocks the TNT away with [wind charges](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md), the explosions destroy the buttons, and he places ender chests above himself, which TNT can't break. Digging upward, he restocks and kills half the lawmen before they retreat.

### Jaden's deal

- Jaden appears, and Flame expects a fight. Instead Jaden says he's done with all this and offers his mace if Flame helps him get full netherite.
- On the way to a town, LAW springs a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) trap that doesn't kill Flame; they've learned cart combat but run out and retreat. One lawman's netherite helmet is the first quarter of Jaden's set.
- In a town built into a mountain, Jaden reveals he's a pirate and means to rob it. They do, and Flame takes netherite leggings from a player for Jaden. LAW attacks as they leave; they spawn a wither, and Flame tunnels through a cave using TNT, which frightens LAW into backing off.
- Flame can't understand how LAW keeps finding them. Jaden suggests they're just being followed. When Flame accuses him, Jaden reminds him they're best friends. Flame decides to hit LAW's base, kill Lettuce, and take the rest of the netherite there.

### LAW's base

- Lettuce spots them and sends his guards; several die and the rest retreat inside. Chasing them, Flame is knocked into an obsidian box by an invisible player.
- Dripstone lines the bottom, then hundreds of TNT pour in, which Lettuce believes is unsurvivable. Flame survives, so phase two releases wardens: he kills one, then two more at once.
- With no traps left, Lettuce and his guards jump in. Flame calls for Jaden, who runs. Lettuce reveals Jaden has been working with LAW the whole time: every encounter happened because Jaden led them to Flame.
- Slow-falling arrows drain Flame's resources while he waits for the effect to wear off; then he pearls out through a hole and kills more guards.
- He tells Lettuce they failed: TNT, wardens, and even his best friend, and he's still standing. Lettuce and his men refuse to fight on and walk away.

## Items & mechanics

- Void trap: LAW's destination for Flame's pulled stasis.
- Ender chests: blast-proof blocks for climbing out under TNT fire.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): knocking TNT away.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): LAW's newly learned cart combat.
- TNT and wardens: the two phases of LAW's obsidian-box trap.
- Slow-falling arrows: LAW's way of draining Flame's resources.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Jaden's offered payment for the netherite set.

## Outcomes

- Flame survives LAW's traps and kills many lawmen.
- Jaden_MAN is revealed to have been working with LAW to set Flame up.
- Jaden ends up with netherite pieces taken for him by Flame.
