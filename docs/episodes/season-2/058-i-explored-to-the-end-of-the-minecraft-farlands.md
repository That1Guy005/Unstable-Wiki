## Summary

Tired of losing fights, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) push towards the very end of the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md) in search of [The Forge](../../places/the-forge.md). Following a lodestone compass glimpsed in a fight with Firestar2008, they cross ever stranger glitched terrain, find infinite loot, an echo of Lakerek, and a mace-wielding guardian named Nitenly, and reach a city floating in the void.

## Full breakdown

### Firestar2008

- Past cherry groves and mountains they find a trial chamber. Since the Mafia destroyed every trial chamber on the main server, Wemmbu is one of the last mace users, so he loots it and destroys every ominous vault.
- A full-netherite player, Firestar2008, attacks; Wemmbu nearly kills him before he pulls his stasis.
- Egg points out he might know about the Forge, and sends Wemmbu screenshots from the fight showing Firestar's lodestone compass, so they follow its direction.

### Stranger lands

- More glitched terrain, including a biome built from chunks of every biome. The Nether doesn't work out here.
- In terrain with repeating patterns, a repeating dungeon gives them endless enchanted golden apples and experience until the chests get cut off.
- At the bottom is water leading to the void. By boat they reach a glass-covered ocean monument holding another echo: Lakerek and his friends fighting an elder guardian.

### Nitenly

- Flying through a fractal pattern of grass blocks that grow further apart, they're attacked by Nitenly, who also has a mace. Wemmbu knocks him into the void, but he survives, suggesting one of the stases they'd passed was his. Egg guesses they're defending their home because Wemmbu killed Lakerek.
- A stronghold gives them eye trims, which no longer exist on the main server because the Mafia used them all, and they build a cobblestone generator in the portal room for bridging blocks. Wemmbu finds another trial chamber farm and blows up a house with TNT made from dungeon gunpowder.

### The edge

- A platform with a single tree is guarded by Nitenly. Wemmbu breaks some of his armour and Nitenly stasises away.
- Ignoring a sign warning them to turn back, they keep bridging and find a city in the void.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's, and Nitenly's; ominous vaults destroyed so no one else gets one.
- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): eye trims, extinct on the main server.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Firestar's and Nitenly's escapes.
- Lodestone compass: the trail towards the city.
- Repeating dungeon: infinite golden apples and experience.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu and Egg reach the city at the end of the Far Lands.
- Firestar2008 and Nitenly both escape.
