## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) goes after [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) for betraying him. Jaden, now leading his own pirates, nearly wins with a rain-powered trident-and-mace combo. With [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md)'s slow-falling arrows, paid for by griefing [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md)' farm, Flame beats Jaden with skill alone at the Pirate Islands and cuts him off for good.

## Full breakdown

### Jaden's offer

- With no leads, Flame waits at his old base ([FlameFrags' Base](../../places/flamefrags-base.md)), the one place Jaden would look. Jaden offers one last chance to join his pirate civilization, or be hunted. Flame refuses, and Jaden's [Pirates](../../factions/pirates.md) die trying to kill him.
- Lomedy helps rebuild the ruined base. While Flame brews potions inside, Jaden attacks Lomedy and steals one of his maces.

### Rain

- When it starts raining, Jaden's Riptide [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) works perfectly with the [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). Flame's shield breaks and his totems pop, and he retreats into a cave and dares Jaden in. Jaden taunts him for running, the "immortal demon" refusing a fight.
- Neither will move, so Jaden gives him a compass to the Pirate Islands: come when it's raining.

### Slow falling

- Flame's counter is slow-falling arrows. Lomedy has some, for a price: grief the farm of his rival farmer, Spongs.
- On their [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), Lomedy distracts Spongs while Flame kills his animals and blows up his house, and they escape.
- Lomedy hands over the arrows and offers his other mace against Jaden's "overpowered" combo; Flame wants to win with real skill.

### The Pirate Islands

- Flame waits nearby for rain, then kills a wave of iron-armoured pirates on the way to the castle.
- Flame says he saved Jaden. Jaden says Flame betrayed him first, after Jaden gave him everything: his pirates, his castle, his mace.
- The slow-falling arrows work, and they agree that Jaden drops his combo if Flame drops slow falling. Jaden loses the sword fight and calls Flame the villain; Flame says Jaden was using him, and that Jaden was his last brother.
- Jaden says Flame has started a war with the pirates, and they'll be stronger next time.
- Flame tells Lomedy he's cut Jaden off for good and they can rebuild in peace, but Spongs and Willsion are waiting outside for revenge.

## Items & mechanics

- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Riptide in the rain powers Jaden's combo.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): stolen from Lomedy and used in Jaden's combo; Flame refuses one.
- Slow-falling arrows: Flame's counter, bought by griefing Spongs.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): Flame and Lomedy's transport.

## Outcomes

- Flame beats Jaden in a fair sword fight and ends their friendship.
- Spongs' farm is destroyed; Spongs and Willsion come for revenge.
- Jaden declares war between Flame and the Pirates.
