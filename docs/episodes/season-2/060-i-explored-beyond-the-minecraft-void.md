## Summary

In the city in the void, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) are welcomed by its leader Serpentbound333, while the residents (the builder GeeGeeGamer, the fighter Nitenly and the eager Firestar2008) seem determined to keep them busy instead of telling them where [The Forge](../../places/the-forge.md) is. Egg finds the city's repeating [orbital cannons](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md), then is shot through an End portal, and Serpentbound pushes Wemmbu in after him.

## Full breakdown

### The city

- Citizens open fire with TNT cannons; Wemmbu fights Nitenly, who's operating them, until Serpentbound stops him and brings the pair to a dorm guarded by GeeGeeGamer while the others meet. Egg finds the floor is TNT, but the trap is defunct, and they slip out when GeeGee stops guarding.
- The city has an arena, a potion brewer, an armoury full of rare items, and a villager trading hall, impossible on a server where mobs are extinct. They loot the armoury and fight Nitenly in the arena, until Serpentbound intervenes again.
- Serpentbound lets them take anything and ask anything. The city isn't the Forge, he says, and asks what they like: Wemmbu loves fighting skilled players, Egg loves building, so GeeGee tours them round.

### Distractions

- During GeeGee's tour, the game switches to old Minecraft textures. Wemmbu threatens him for the Forge's location; GeeGee says only the fighters know, he just builds.
- Nitenly gives them PvP kits with Thorns netherite; Wemmbu keeps his diamond armour rather than wear Thorns or Frost Walker. They trade with villagers for an ideal netherite set, find every trim, and find chests of maces, which Wemmbu burns so no one can match him.
- Firestar asks to be taught PvP and claims he'll reveal the Forge afterwards. A brief lesson pops his totem; he wants more, pesters Wemmbu for his mace, and starts beating Egg in a duel until Wemmbu pops him again. He admits he never knew what the Forge was, and sends them to Lakerek's room, emptied since Lakerek died. Wemmbu loses his temper.

### The orbital cannons

- Invisible, Wemmbu spies on Serpentbound and Nitenly, and hears Serpentbound say they can wait for Wemmbu and Egg to leave before going to the Forge.
- In Lakerek's room, Egg had solved a puzzle that gave him a lodestone compass. Following it, they find a field of repeating orbital strike cannons in the Far Lands: this is how Lakerek struck Wemmbu.
- A lever raises the tick rate, speeding everything up, which explains how Lakerek bypassed the orbitals' delay. With no Nether to unload chunks, Egg has Wemmbu set a stasis, lower his render and simulation distance, start the tick machine and set his fishing rod, then teleports him by stasis. The test strike works.

### The portal

- When time stops speeding up, they go back and meet Serpentbound, GeeGee and Nitenly. Wemmbu calls the cannons the Forge; Serpentbound says they aren't, but he knows where it is.
- He leads them up Lakerek's staircase to an End portal: the Forge is on the other side. GeeGee aims a Punch bow at Wemmbu; Egg takes the arrow and is knocked into the portal.
- Realising it's a trap, Wemmbu fights Nitenly, at a disadvantage from slow falling and no strength until the slow falling runs out. He kills GeeGee; Nitenly and Firestar escape.
- Serpentbound offers to work something out. When Wemmbu tells him to set the orbital cannons, Serpentbound takes him back to the portal and pushes him in.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): the repeating cannons, with a tick-rate machine that removes their delay.
- [Fishing Rod Trigger](../../mechanics/fishing-rod-trigger.md): part of Egg's setup to fire the test strike.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): how Egg teleports Wemmbu away from the cannons.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): chests of them, burned by Wemmbu.
- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): every trim, found in the city.
- End portal: the city's supposed route to the Forge.

## Outcomes

- GeeGeeGamer is killed.
- Eggchan and then Wemmbu are pushed through the End portal.
- The secret of Lakerek's orbitals is discovered.
