## Summary

[Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) drags [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) into the Great Farm War against rival farmers [Spongs](../../characters/spongs.md) and Willsion, with a horse with maxed-out stats as Flame's reward. Flame tries following the farmers' rules (leather armour, no weapons), but Spongs keeps wrecking [Lomedy's Farm](../../places/lomedys-farm.md). After nearly losing Lomedy to despair, Flame destroys the rivals' farm with Lomedy's TNT dupers, leaving them alive to live with it.

## Full breakdown

### Farmer's rules

- Lomedy's "war" turns out to be about crops, but the horse wins Flame over. The rules: wear leather, use no weapons. Flame sets traps across the farm instead.
- At night Spongs' farmers rob the farm; two die to traps, and Spongs and Willsion chase the leather-clad Flame around before leaving the farm wrecked.
- At a farmers' trading town, players are too scared of Flame's reputation to trade; Lomedy takes over, and Flame loses his temper and drops all the enchanted books.

### Hoe and strength

- Spongs is trashing the farm again and laughs that Flame can't do anything under the no-weapons rule. Flame kills Spongs' whole team with strength potions and a hoe, and beats Spongs and Willsion nearly to death.
- For payback, they rob the rivals' crops and steal their camel and a sniffer egg; Lomedy takes the camel away on their [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md) while Flame distracts the pursuers, and from a mountaintop they keep knocking Spongs' team off.
- Under a house in an abandoned jungle village, they find a maze full of ravagers with a netherite hoe at the end. With a camel, a sniffer egg and a netherite hoe, they're richer than any normal farmer.

### The third destruction

- Back home they dance to "Lava Chicken" and tidy up, until Flame falls into a fall trap and survives with a pearl clutch that glitches him into a block. Spongs and Willsion blow up the farm again, mock Lomedy for not protecting it, and leave him with no crops or animals.
- Lomedy is upset: Flame's violence dragged him into this, and it could all have been avoided by giving the rivals what they wanted. He's ready to quit farming. Flame asks him to farm with him one last time, and they rebuild it better than before.

### TNT dupers

- Flame sets off alone to get his pets back, but Lomedy helps by aiming TNT dupers at the rivals' farm. Flame moves his dog and the horse to safety and blows a horn as the signal.
- He walks out in the open and tells Spongs and Willsion he'll never be a farmer, but he knows how to take things one step further, and to look around. The dupers level their entire farm.
- He spares them both, to live knowing they'll never be the greatest farmers. Willsion gives up all his items, and Spongs gives up farming.
- With his rivals gone, Lomedy can become the best farmer, and gives Flame the horse as promised.

## Items & mechanics

- TNT dupers: Lomedy's machine that destroys the rivals' farm.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): used to steal the camel.
- Netherite hoe: the prize at the end of the ravager maze.
- Leather armour and no weapons: the farmers' rules Flame keeps bending.

## Outcomes

- Spongs and Willsion's farm is destroyed; Spongs quits farming and Willsion loses everything.
- Lomedy's farm is rebuilt and he becomes the top farmer.
- Flame gets the maxed-out horse.
