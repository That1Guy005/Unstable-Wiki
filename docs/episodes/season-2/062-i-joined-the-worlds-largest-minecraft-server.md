## Summary

Leaving [Paragon](../../places/paragon.md) with nine carrots, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) finds [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md) destroyed and swarming with withers. [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) takes him to [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), ruled by [Reddoons](../../characters/reddoons.md) with a 90% tax. Running a failing potion shop, Parrot and Theo start smuggling new players in from Spawn, beat [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md)'s scammers, recover Parrot's happy ghast, and win the whole city's debts cancelled.

## Full breakdown

### Spawn in ruins

- The End portal brings Parrot to Spawn: lava-cast towers and withers in the sky. Out of food, he finds Theo in a small shelter, fighting withers in iron armour. Theo kills two, feeding Parrot, and plans to pay his taxes with the nether stars. Paranoid, Parrot expects Theo to kill or abandon him, but Theo means no harm.

### Capital City

- Reddoons, a greedy tyrant who kept the poor in servitude during the Mafia years, lets anyone start a business, as long as they pay 90% of their earnings.
- Parrot gets a small Market Street shop with leftover brewing supplies, and makes one sale in a day. Seeing the crowds, he realises he'll be stuck for a while.
- In a secret underground black market, Theo's friend Puqi sells illegal explosives and offers 64 diamonds to rescue his friend from Spawn. Parrot refuses until [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and another player rob him in broad daylight and break his potion chest.

### Smuggling

- Invisible, they rescue DragonMC (fifteen minutes late) and escape the withers; DragonMC is amazed by the city's size.
- Word spreads, and smuggling players from Spawn becomes their hidden income. On one run they meet Boosfer's scammers and their rigged minecart gambling game; as Theo loses a second time, Parrot works out the trick, loots the hopper under the rails, and they flee.
- At tax time Parrot has nothing and gets a second chance. His neighbour owes three stacks of diamonds and faces eviction, so Parrot secretly leaves the scammers' totems, netherite and enchanted apples in her chest.

### Sky

- A letter lost somewhere in the town hall's minecart post system turns out to be from [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md): BAT is starting over, everything is fine, and a lodestone compass points to the [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), Sky, that Parrot used in the BAT rescue during the war against the Director.
- On train 50 the scammers ambush them. Theo goes for the ghast while Parrot bluffs about hidden loot, then escapes with pearls, speed and [wind charges](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md) up a steep mountain where Theo picks him up. They leave Sky with the mayor's "Animal Care" department.

### Hexatope

- The business brings chests of diamonds, but for every player they save, hundreds die. They quit, and Parrot closes his shop.
- Reddoons has found out. He offers a deal, rescue a player named Hexatope, and Parrot demands the entire city's debt be cleared. Reddoons agrees at once.
- Boosfer's crew intercepts them while Hexatope is loaded onto the ghast; they end it there, killing all his men as Boosfer flees.
- Reddoons announces all debts forgiven. A procession follows Parrot home, and his neighbour gives him an elytra, the one Theo had wanted.
- On the city wall, they decide to be a team again. Parrot points out that most players on the server die at Spawn, and for most survivors Capital City is their whole world. Asked what to do about it, he answers: "We save them."

## Items & mechanics

- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): Sky, returned by Leo and used to carry rescued players.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the smugglers' cover at Spawn.
- Rigged minecart gamble: Boosfer's scam, beaten by looting its hopper.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): the neighbour's thank-you gift.
- Withers: Spawn's new residents.

## Outcomes

- Capital City's debts are cancelled; DragonMC and Hexatope are rescued.
- Boosfer's crew is killed; Boosfer escapes.
- Parrot and Theo team up to save players.
