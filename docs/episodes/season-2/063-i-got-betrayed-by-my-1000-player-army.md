## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s old 1,000-player army comes for him under a new leader calling himself "The Executive". Spoke and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) flee their base, their sky base and the Nether, holding off the horde with TNT, wind charges and TNT cannons, until they portal into [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md)'s base and Leo helps them kill the hunters.

## Full breakdown

### Protocol 32

- Spoke is busy building a zombie farm and ignores Mapicc's warnings, twice, until Mapicc calls Protocol 32. Hundreds of players storm the base and bomb it from above; Spoke and Mapicc take their secret escape and chicken cannons to Mapicc's sky base.
- The hunters pillar up after them. An invisible player with a voice changer starts lecturing Spoke that his actions have consequences; Spoke interrupts to mock his microphone quality.
- The player drops a book and a raw porkchop and jumps off. The book, signed "THE EXECUTIVE", says the rebelling players only needed a leader who shared their desires, that Spoke's crimes are catching up with him, and to "better start running".

### The sky base falls

- Spoke crafts TNT and grabs wind charges, lava and pearls. Mapicc wind-charges attackers, Spoke shoots one off a pillar and slows the rest with TNT, and a fishing-rod fling fails, so they turn to bombing.
- Spoke is knocked off, and the players overrun and pillage the sky base. Their Nether portal is broken and they have no obsidian, so Spoke pearls to the ground and they build a portal underground to escape.

### The Executive

- In the Nether, Mapicc builds an unattractive wooden hut, and Spoke finds his Riptide III trident has one durability left.
- The Executive appears on a basalt pillar, throws a book that burns in lava, accidentally switches his voice changer off and on again, and warns that the army is going to kill Spoke.
- Running low, Spoke builds a Nether platform with five TNT cannons (one explodes, leaving four) while Mapicc waits in the lava. They hold out a long time, but Spoke has to retreat.

### Leo's base

- Spoke portals into Leo's base, where the hunters start griefing. Leo agrees to defend them, and together they kill all the hunters.

## Items & mechanics

- TNT cannons: Spoke's Nether defence.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): knocking attackers off their pillars.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Spoke's Riptide III, down to one durability.
- Chicken cannons: the escape to the sky base.
- Voice changer: The Executive's disguise.

## Outcomes

- Spoke's base and Mapicc's sky base are destroyed by his old army.
- The Executive emerges as the army's new leader.
- With Leo's help, the hunters are killed.
