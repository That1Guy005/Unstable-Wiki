## Summary

Armed with the [orbital strike cannons](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) from the end of the Far Lands, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) sets out to take [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' title as the strongest player. He nukes his way through [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), [LAW](../../factions/law.md)'s city and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md)'s castle for clues, follows a compass to an old Mafia stash, and finally beats Flame with stab shots.

## Full breakdown

### Capital City

- Wemmbu wants revenge on his enemies, to beat Flame, and to become the strongest player.
- Following players into Capital City, where new players go for safety, he's annoyed that nobody pays him any attention. A player he corners by a lamp post says, on a sign, that only [Reddoons](../../characters/reddoons.md) knows where Flame is, and there's a queue.
- Threatened with a Stab Shot, the player takes him to Reddoons' office, where only SirPig_ is present. After Wemmbu nukes part of the city again, SirPig_ says Flame went to LAW, south-west of the city.

### LAW

- [Falconu](../../characters/falconu.md) and [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) send new players they pulled from Spawn, in iron and leather, to kill him. Wemmbu wipes them out with a Nuke Shot.
- Lettuce thinks Flame is going after Jaden, but won't give the coordinates until Wemmbu blows up part of the city. Then Wemmbu destroys the entire city anyway, so they won't hunt him again.

### Jaden's castle

- An abandoned pirate base with cobwebs reminds him of Flame's massacre there. He finds a pirate-branded castle, Jaden's, and stab-shots part of it while demanding Flame's location.
- Jaden suggests Flame's base and pretends to search his chests for books. When it starts raining, Jaden leaves, then attacks with his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and Riptide [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md).
- Wemmbu soon cares more about the mace than about Flame. When the rain stops, he demands it; Jaden drops a bucket, which Wemmbu picks up thinking it's the mace, and escapes. Wemmbu nukes the castle.

### The duel

- At Flame's abandoned base ([FlameFrags' Base](../../places/flamefrags-base.md)), TREELEAF2021 in gold armour offers a scavenger hunt; Wemmbu stab-shots him instead and finds a compass named "FlameFrags…".
- It leads to an old Mafia stash he raided while training with [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md). He waits on a pillar until Flame arrives, taunts him that the title is about to change hands, and opens with a stab shot.
- The fight drops into an ancient city where wardens attack them both. Back outside, Wemmbu breaks Flame's chestplate and keeps blasting him with stab shots until he wins.
- He taunts Flame for hiding, tells him he'll never be the strongest again, and stands on the same pillar before leaving.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's Stab Shot and Nuke Shot, used on three cities and on Flame.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Jaden's, which Wemmbu tries and fails to take.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Jaden's Riptide in the rain.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu beats FlameFrags and takes the title of strongest player (see [The Orbital Strike Cannon War](../../events/the-orbital-strike-cannon-war.md)).
- Parts of Capital City, all of LAW's city and Jaden's castle are destroyed.
- TREELEAF2021 and LAW's new recruits are killed.
