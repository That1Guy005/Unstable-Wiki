## Summary

Short on resources, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) takes quests from a mysterious invisible player: recover stolen potions, fetch a dried ghast from [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s Nether, kill the Skullhound bandits. The quest-giver is [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md), and his final "reward" sends Flame straight into [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md), whose orbital strikes break Flame's prized chestplate and end his reign.

## Full breakdown

### The quests

- An invisible player offers riches for quests. First, recover stolen potions from MisterLegend101. [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md) is in ruins and empty; a passer-by says Wemmbu came looking for Flame. Flame pops MisterLegend101's totems until he hands the potions over. The reward: one enchanted golden apple.
- More if Flame brings a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), but the Nether is guarded by Clown's army. Nether scavengers, who supply BAT and LAW, guide him in exchange for protection, and they find a dried ghast in an unlooted area.
- The reward is a beacon, which Flame doesn't think much of. The player reveals himself as Spoke; Flame, knowing Spoke's history of manipulation, attacks, and Spoke drops a book and escapes down a tunnel.

### The Skullhounds

- The book asks him to kill the Skullhound bandits, warning it could be his last moments on the server.
- LAW's base has been blown up too. [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) says the Skullhounds are about 15,000 blocks from Spawn, that he's no reason to lie in his current state, and asks Flame to get Wemmbu to stop blowing things up.
- Following griefed villages, lava casts and undespawned loot, Flame finds them. Two stall him while a third builds a small railgun ([Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md)) behind him; Flame notices and blocks himself in. The fight takes him down to diamond armour, but he kills them all and takes their shields and the leader's trimmed chestplate as proof.

### Spoke's reward

- Spoke, hovering above Spawn, says he's trying to build alliances to make up for past mistakes, and gives Flame a book leading to a Mafia stash that will lead to a lifetime of stashes. He hasn't gone himself because he's afraid of the people nearby.

### Wemmbu

- The stash, built into a Badlands mountain, has been blown up and looted, its rooms full of empty chests. Flame finds diamond armour and XP to mend his netherite.
- Wemmbu is waiting above the exit and asks for a rematch. Flame laughs, having beaten him every time, and Wemmbu drops a stab shot on him.
- Confused by the new powers, Flame lands a few hits, but Wemmbu flies in and out and spams orbitals. The blasts expose an ancient city, and Flame breaks sculk with a hoe for experience to mend.
- An invisible player drops him potions and then totems. Out of totems, he lives on Turtle Master. A stab shot pops a totem and breaks his chestplate, his most prized possession, and Wemmbu blows up his shulkers before he can regear.
- Flame digs to bedrock and hides. Wemmbu says he doesn't want to kill him, only for him to know he'll never be the strongest again, and flies off.
- At Spawn there's no Spoke, only a sign reading "Thank you my friend" and a wither rose. Furious, Flame destroys everything in his ender chest and inventory, and vows it's over for Wemmbu.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's stab shots and orbital barrages.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): the Skullhounds' small railgun.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): the dried ghast Spoke wants from the Nether.
- Turtle Master: Flame's last line of defence.
- Flame's netherite chestplate: his most prized possession, broken by a stab shot.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu defeats Flame, who loses his title (see [The Orbital Strike Cannon War](../../events/the-orbital-strike-cannon-war.md)).
- The Skullhound bandits Flame finds are killed.
- Spoke reveals the quests were a setup to deliver Flame to Wemmbu.
