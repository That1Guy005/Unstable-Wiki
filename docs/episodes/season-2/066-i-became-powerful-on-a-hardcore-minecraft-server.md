## Summary

Stripped of his title, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) starts again in iron. He builds a new base with [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md), breeds a wolf army and builds the [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) to counter Wemmbu's orbitals, escapes [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s Nether guards, and wipes out the Skullhound bandits to rebuild a full netherite set, only to hear that [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) is dead.

## Full breakdown

### Starting over

- A recap of how he lost to Wemmbu, and his title of "The Immortal Demon".
- RottenBanana steals his iron and runs; Flame kills him. On the way to his old base, the Skullhound bandits ambush him and mock him for losing; he pillars up, escapes on his horse, and leaves the bandit-occupied base with its giant Wemmbu-made hole.
- On a mesa hill he starts a new base, puts on a diamond chestplate, and tames a wolf: the plan is to breed a wolf army for revenge.
- In a town where nobody fears him any more since his loss, he massacres the residents, taking their chests and their armadillos for wolf armour and a silverfish XP farm.

### Lomedy's help

- Lomedy builds an armadillo farm for stacks of scutes, which Flame turns into a cobweb farm that also produces lots of experience, one of the most prized things on the server. Lomedy asks why he's rebuilding, hears the whole story, and motivates him to go back after the bandits.

### Gold and guards

- With no golden apples, Flame mines gold in the Nether and is ambushed by Clown's Nether forces, a guard hoping for a promotion. Flame kills him and grabs his netherite chestplate, but two more guards in full netherite arrive. He drinks [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) and escapes through lava, and they don't follow.

### The wolf machine

- At [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md) he gathers materials for a redstone wolf machine, and builds it.
- Back at his old base he kills every Skullhound bandit there, including their leader CraftedJoker, and takes netherite helmet, leggings and boots: a full netherite set again.
- In [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), asking a citizen where Wemmbu is, he learns that Wemmbu has died.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): built to counter Wemmbu's orbital cannons.
- Armadillo and cobweb farms: scutes for wolf armour, and experience.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Flame's escape from Clown's netherite guards.
- Netherite: rebuilt piece by piece from Clown's guard and the Skullhounds.

## Outcomes

- The Skullhound bandits and CraftedJoker are killed.
- Flame has full netherite and a wolf army.
- Flame learns of Wemmbu's death (see [Wemmbu's Fake Death](../../events/wemmbus-fake-death.md)).
