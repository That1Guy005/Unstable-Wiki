## Summary

DragonMC hands [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) a clue signed by Verda, one of the three founders of Verdaria, a legendary lost city built to hide their treasure. With [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) and their ghast Sky, Parrot follows the trail through a floating shulker, a man-made lush cave and a massacred mining town, chased all the way by [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md). The "final clue" is a dead end, but Verda's execution chamber holds a compass, and Verdaria's treasure.

## Full breakdown

### The legend

- In the server's early days, MyraMoon, Luma317 and Verda built Verdaria as a safe haven for themselves and their treasure. When word spread, they set up a scavenger hunt to its location and vanished. Nobody has ever found it.
- Parrot thinks it's a myth, until DragonMC gives him a book with coordinates, signed by Verda herself. Dragon couldn't find the next clue but believes Parrot can. A sceptical Theo comes along, and they leave on Sky, their [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md).

### The floating shulker

- At the coordinates is a crater dug by hundreds of searchers, empty even below bedrock. Parrot reasons nobody has looked in the right place, and they fly thousands of blocks up, to a shulker box floating in the sky, name-tagged with the next coordinates.
- Boosfer's gang ambushes them; Boosfer knows about Verdaria too. Cornered at a cliff, Parrot stalls by telling him the next destination, and escapes on a saddled pig Theo flies past with.

### The lush cave

- Travelling over the Nether roof, they find a waterfall hiding a lush cave, which can't generate naturally in plains, so it's man-made. Theo sets off a harmless trap. Inside: a mural of Verdaria, and a caged fox holding the next clue in its mouth.
- Expecting the cage to be rigged with TNT, Parrot covers the floor in wool to muffle the sculk sensors and dispenses a steak at the fox, since food replaces whatever a fox is holding. The clue is in pigpen cipher, and reveals that [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md) was among those who searched for Verdaria.
- Boosfer attacks for the book; Parrot crafts a crossbow and rockets for heavy damage, and they escape through a Nether portal just as Boosfer comes through.

### jagle's story

- The next stop is a ravine that was once a mining town, now abandoned except for jagle, who claims never to have heard of Verdaria. They camp there for a week, searching.
- Finally jagle explains: five years ago the three founders asked him to hide something. When Clown came looking, jagle hid it, and Clown killed everyone but him. He's never been to Verdaria, but knows what it holds, and since Parrot wants to help the server, he hands over the next clue.
- Boosfer ambushes them again. Low on resources, they surrender, and Boosfer brings them along as hostages to solve the next clue.

### Verda's sacrifice

- In a jungle, a path ends at a book on top of a build of Clown's head. Boosfer can't decipher it, so Parrot reads it; it ends, "I'm sorry. The final clue dies with me." There is no final clue.
- Nearby, a crater hides a staircase down to a huge obsidian box with pillars and an execution chamber. Boosfer's gang leaves with nothing. Parrot keeps digging, scoops up the lava in the execution chamber, and finds a compass to Verdaria.
- Clown had tried to find the city by force, then threatened Verda for its location. She refused, gave her life to protect it, and planted the compass.

### Verdaria

- After several in-game days the compass turns. Verdaria lies below, untouched for years, but its chests hold only junk and a hidden path leads only to empty chests.
- A small sculpture matches the lush cave they visited. Behind its painting is a lever, opening a secret drop: hundreds of ore blocks, dozens of diamond and netherite sets, and every enchanted book.
- They fill boat chests, tie them to Sky, and fly home. Theo wonders if the founders would be happy; Parrot hopes so.

## Items & mechanics

- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): Sky, their transport, and the treasure's tow.
- Fox and steak: the dispenser trick that frees the clue.
- Pigpen cipher: the code used for the third coordinates.
- Verda's compass: hidden in the lava of the execution chamber.
- Verdaria's treasure: ore, armour sets and every enchanted book.

## Outcomes

- Verdaria is found and its treasure claimed by Parrot and Theo.
- ClownPierce is revealed as the killer of jagle's town and of Verda.
- Boosfer leaves empty-handed.
