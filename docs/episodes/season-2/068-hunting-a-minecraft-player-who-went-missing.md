## Summary

To find out how [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) supposedly died, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) traces the rumour back through Luke4472, who has been captured by the Skullhound bandits. With the reluctant The_Codmaster14, Flame gets himself taken hostage, follows a trail of clues, frees Luke with his "Orbital Dog Cannon", and is pointed towards [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md). Finally, [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md) arrives with information.

## Full breakdown

### The rumour

- _keycapper says he heard about Wemmbu's death from Luke4472. Asking around, Flame finds everyone running from him; SpringMike attacks and dies in two hits.
- The_Codmaster14 claims ignorance, then leads Flame to Luke's base, marked with a Skullhound banner, which suggests Luke was captured or killed. Codmaster persuades Flame to help.

### Taken hostage

- A gravel path leads to a railway, where Flame guesses bandits intercepted Luke. He decides to let the bandits capture them, with Codmaster going first as bait.
- At a brick wall guarded by three bandits, Flame refuses to drop his sword, places an ender chest, and refuses to empty it, then demands to be taken hostage along with Codmaster. They're locked in an obsidian box to rot until he hands over the ender chest's contents.
- Told "there is no Luke", Flame starts singing, and Codmaster joins in. When AlbertJustice_ insults Codmaster's skin and tells them to drop their armour, Flame agrees, then kills him while he's sorting his inventory. The other bandits flee.

### Clues

- Ransacking the outpost, Flame finds Luke's armour: iron with a redstone wild trim. He thinks the sweet berries suggest a taiga; Codmaster calls it a reach, the two trade insults, and they part ways.
- Flame finds another Skullhound banner, and a group (AdvancedKathrin, Elm_0Paid2, VanadiumBaldine) who say they're hunting the same bandits. They demand his full netherite as payment and point him north-west, the way he came. Furious, he chases their leader Elm into a hole; Elm drops a map. Northwest was correct, and Flame feels bad for attacking them.

### The rescue

- A large group of bandits guards Luke. Flame's [Orbital Dog Cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) kills all eight, and Luke reveals he heard about Wemmbu's death from MinuteTech, the guardian of the End.
- At the nearest stronghold (merged with a trial chamber) Flame repairs his gear. In the End, he kills a player talking to Minute, and they fight, Luke cheering Flame on, until Minute retreats through an End gateway. Suspecting a trap, Flame doesn't follow.
- Back at his base, Boosfer approaches with information about Wemmbu.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): the "Orbital Dog Cannon" that wipes out Luke's captors.
- Ender chest: Flame's leverage, and what the bandits want.
- Luke's armour: iron with a redstone wild trim, proof he'd been there.
- Map and sweet berries: Flame's trail north-west.

## Outcomes

- Luke4472 is rescued; AlbertJustice_ and eight Skullhound bandits are killed.
- The trail leads from Luke to MinuteTech, who escapes Flame.
- Boosfer offers Flame information about Wemmbu.
