## Summary

Betrayed by [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) into an ambush, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) find it's really a recruitment test for the rebuilt [Bounty Assassin Team](../../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md). Competing with SmileyyMC and [PlanetLord](../../characters/planetlord.md), Spoke hunts ApexPoint in [The Amplified](../../places/the-amplified.md), then secretly hires [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) to do his missions. Spoke's quests set up the Flame vs Wemmbu fight, and he passes off Flame's shulker as proof he killed the BAT's top target.

## Full breakdown

### The ambush

- Invisible assassins knock Spoke, Leo and Mapicc off a height, and they survive a 100-block fall on powdered snow under carpet. Leo set the trap.
- Spoke guesses Leo sold him for about 64 diamonds and Mapicc for some copper; Mapicc insists he's worth 1,000. Leo demands Spoke's Riptide III trident and threatens Mapicc, and Spoke gives it up.
- At BAT Headquarters 2.0 they learn they aren't being killed: they're being tested for membership.

### ApexPoint

- First mission: kill ApexPoint, who has griefed over 30 bases, somewhere in the glitchy Amplified. Since furnaces only smelt near a player, Spoke places furnaces across the terrain and checks them an hour later, halving the search.
- ApexPoint's base is a cramped obsidian box with a mooshroom, an enchanting table and six mangrove logs. He's AFK watching YouTube Shorts. Spoke's [cart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) doesn't kill him, and badly under-geared, Spoke notices ApexPoint raises his shield whenever a rail goes down. So Spoke places a pot on a rail: ApexPoint right-clicks it and stores his sword inside, and Spoke breaks the pot and steals it.
- With only a netherite axe left, ApexPoint escapes through a tunnel. Spoke shows Leo fake evidence; Leo gives him partial credit. Meanwhile SmileyyMC has finished two missions and PlanetLord three, Smileyy steals Spoke's next kill, and Planet's tower beats Spoke's in the building mission.

### Drain kits

- To escape cobwebs faster he makes Efficiency V Mending shears, and shows off a "drain kit": 17 stacks of cobwebs, sponges, 64 buckets and two stacks of chorus fruit.
- Asked to clear trees, he ignores DrinkHotdogwater's "fire is too dangerous" and starts a fire that spirals out of control. TREELEAF2021, whose base ApexPoint griefed, needs help.
- On TREELEAF's tower, ApexPoint demands his sword back. Spoke drains him three times, pops a totem in a fall, and loses his boots, helmet and leggings in turn, swapping in iron. He makes ApexPoint waste his pearls, then trident-pearls 500 blocks from a mountain water source. He tells Leo that ApexPoint is dead.

### Hiring Flame

- Losing to two teammates who are helping each other, Spoke "has no choice": invisible on a mountain, he drops Flame a netherite ingot and offers more for quests, while passing off the results as his own.
- Spoke handles some missions himself: fruitguppy4 dies in his own obsidian box, ironezekiel's "most secure base" is a house with a lava moat, and another trapper falls into his own pitfall. The Executive tries to talk to him, and Spoke runs.
- He pays Flame with Smileyy's enchanted golden apple and asks for a happy ghast. Training with Leo to get close to him, he learns BAT's highest-priority target is Flame, and in the BAT library he learns about Flame's rival, Wemmbu. TREELEAF brings him "Mjolnir", a Loyalty III Channeling trident.
- He pays Flame with a beacon, escapes his anger, and drops the Skullhound book. He gives TREELEAF a lodestone compass to lure Wemmbu to the "Mafia stash", and sends Flame to the same coordinates.

### The setup pays off

- Invisible, Spoke follows them and watches Flame and Wemmbu fight, stealing one of Flame's shulkers as "proof".
- He tells Leo he killed Flame, but Leo asks if he got the mace: Leo thought he'd killed Wemmbu. Spoke claims he blew up Wemmbu's mace, and learns people are saying Wemmbu died at the stash.
- Leo lets Spoke join BAT and gives him back George Junior.

## Items & mechanics

- Decorated pot: the trick that steals ApexPoint's sword.
- Furnace search: furnaces only smelt near players.
- Drain kit: cobwebs, sponges, buckets and chorus fruit.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Riptide III, taken by Leo; trident-pearling 500 blocks; and "Mjolnir".
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Spoke's opener against ApexPoint.

## Outcomes

- Spoke joins the Bounty Assassin Team on a lie.
- Spoke's quests deliver Flame into the fight with Wemmbu.
- The rumour of Wemmbu's death starts at the Mafia stash (see [Wemmbu's Fake Death](../../events/wemmbus-fake-death.md)).
