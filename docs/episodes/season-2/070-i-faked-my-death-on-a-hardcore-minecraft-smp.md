## Summary

After beating Flame, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) fakes his death and takes on a new identity: diamond armour with dune trims, signs instead of speech, then a voice changer. Undercover in [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), he joins the guard force, buys potions from [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md), and defends the city against [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md)'s bandits without killing anyone, until he reveals himself to Boosfer and swears to silence the two survivors who know.

## Full breakdown

### A new identity

- In the End, [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) suggests a fake identity and promises to tell anyone who asks that Wemmbu is dead. Wemmbu chooses dune trims like Wyll's, but netherite-material, on diamond armour.
- [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) asks why he looks so different. Wemmbu explains he's tired of everyone wanting to fight him and wants a fresh start; Minute warns that Flame won't end on a loss.
- At [Spawn](../../places/spawn.md), he's not supposed to use his mace or elytra, but a wither forces him to mace it, and someone watches. Low on invisibility, he heads to Capital City as if nothing happened.

### The guard force

- Guards stop him, since someone nuked the city; he answers on signs. A guard with voice chat likes his trims and invites him to join the guard force.
- The city is emptier than usual, which he knows is his fault. When a player says Egg killed Wemmbu, he nearly replies, then takes a leaf out of the Director's book and starts using a voice changer.
- Salvationism, the guard force's leader, tests the recruits (one dies); Wemmbu is accepted without a test because of his wealth. The armoury chests turn out to be empty.
- On the walls he feeds a starving player, who is then killed by Boosfer. Wemmbu fights the bandits with a wooden sword so he won't kill anyone and reveal his name, and breaks Boosfer's armour, breaking the city's treaty with Boosfer. When that isn't intimidating enough, he goes back to his netherite sword, with weakness on himself.

### Parrot's potion shop

- He orders shulkers of weakness and invisibility potions from Parrot and Theo, supplying golden carrots and shulkers. While they brew, they talk about protecting players and Wemmbu bombing the city, and Theo brings up Wemmbu's "death" and the Flame fight. Parrot tells him to try harder to keep his identity secret.

### The bandit war

- Wemmbu goes to tell the guards he's leaving and finds a crowd: mayor SirPig_ announces Boosfer has declared war, and they raise the walls.
- Splashed with weakness so he can't kill, he only maces players who are holding totems. Boosfer notices the invisible fighter has a mace, and pulls out his own. A second wave arrives, Boosfer's helmet breaks, and the bandits retreat.
- Salvationism asks why he wanted others to do the killing. Wemmbu tells him to train the guards better and get more resources, and says he'll kill Boosfer himself.

### Branzy's Castle

- With Boosfer's location from Egg, he reveals himself at Branzy's Castle and kills all the bandits except Boomie and Boosfer, who barely escape.
- He changes back into his disguise and checks that his identity hasn't leaked in Capital City; it hasn't. He vows to hunt down Boosfer and Boomie before they can expose him.
- Elsewhere, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) asks a guard about Wemmbu and is told he's dead.

## Items & mechanics

- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): dune trims on diamond armour, the disguise.
- Voice changer: borrowed from the Director's playbook.
- Weakness potions: so Wemmbu can fight without killing anyone.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): used only on players holding totems.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): bought from Parrot's shop.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu's death is faked and widely believed (see [Wemmbu's Fake Death](../../events/wemmbus-fake-death.md)).
- Boosfer's attack on Capital City is repelled; his bandits are killed at Branzy's Castle.
- Boosfer and Boomie escape knowing Wemmbu is alive.
