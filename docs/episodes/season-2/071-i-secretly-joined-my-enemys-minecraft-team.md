## Summary

Hearing that [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md) is after treasure in [The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md), the disguised [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) signs up with [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md)'s pirates. Fighting the Marine Rangers, he finds [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) with Boomie, one of the two players who know his secret, and kills Boomie with a stab shot in front of Parrot. He kills the Rangers' leader, revealing himself to Jaden, and leaves Jaden his empire on one condition.

## Full breakdown

### Joining the pirates

- At Spawn, Jaden is recruiting new pirates. Wemmbu joins to get at Boosfer, without telling Jaden why.
- Island-hopping, they infiltrate the Marine Rangers, and find Parrot and Theo infiltrating them too. Outnumbered 2v5, Wemmbu and Jaden escape.

### Boomie

- A few pixels on a map lead them to an empty island, where they're ambushed again. Wemmbu spots one of the two players who know his identity and follows him; as it rains, his Riptide [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) lets him catch up.
- Parrot is talking to Boomie when Wemmbu drops a stab shot and kills Boomie instantly. Parrot says he only wanted peace, and wonders who this player really is.
- Guilty but satisfied, Wemmbu riptides back to Jaden, who has massacred the Rangers.

### The empire

- Jaden's "treasure" is just an empty area to build a new pirate empire, and Wemmbu says he's already got one for him: the Rangers' base.
- There they meet TheLobsterMan, the Rangers' leader. Wemmbu kills him, and Jaden sees the real name in the death message.
- Wemmbu lets Jaden keep the empire, but threatens to kill him if he ever leaves the Great Sea, so his secret stays safe.
- A flashback to the Pirate Arc underlines how far Wemmbu has come: now the one taking Jaden's place, and killing an innocent who wanted peace in front of Parrot. Jaden and Wemmbu remain on good terms afterwards.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): the stab shot that kills Boomie.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Riptide in the rain to catch up.
- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): Wemmbu's disguise.

## Outcomes

- Boomie and TheLobsterMan are killed.
- Jaden learns Wemmbu is alive and gets a pirate empire, on condition he stays in the Great Sea.
- Parrot witnesses the killing and starts to suspect who the invisible player is.
