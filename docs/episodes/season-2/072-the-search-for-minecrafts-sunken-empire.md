## Summary

DragonMC hands [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) a clue to a sunken empire in [The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md). Imprisoned by the Ocean Rangers, they escape with an axolotl trick and follow clues with Boomie in tow, while Theo grows sick of Parrot's habit of giving everything away. Parrot finds the treasure, but an invisible player kills Boomie in front of him, and Parrot realises who it is.

## Full breakdown

### The Ocean Rangers

- No hesitation this time. At the first location the chest has already been looted, and a nearby group, the Ocean Rangers, claim never to have heard of a lost city and bring them in for questioning.
- Made to empty their inventories, Parrot smuggles in a bucket of fish and a bucket of axolotl. Their underwater cell also holds Boomie, and gives them mining fatigue. Theo complains that Parrot always shares their treasure with new players.
- When the guard leaves for a meeting, Parrot uses a little-known mechanic: helping an axolotl kill a fish clears negative effects. They retrieve their items and are spotted; Boomie runs.
- A rival squad arrives, led by an invisible player with a voice changer and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md), at war with the Rangers. The cell guard chases Parrot, who kills him with fall damage off the ice from his Frost Walker boots.

### Raised deck

- The next clue leads to a field of sunken ships where both groups are already searching. Parrot throws wind charges to draw the invisible player's group onto the Rangers, and in the chaos finds the clue: a piston, called a "raised deck" in the game's pirate language, the phrase in the clue book.

### Theo's patience

- Fishing for food, Theo sarcastically asks if Parrot's going to give the fish away to some new player, and warns that if he gives everything away, he'll be left with nothing.
- Parrot answers that there are players who have everything, players who have nothing, and a third kind who could have everything but choose to share, because treasure and power are temporary and taking them leaves you alone. He says the server needs help and he's going to save it. Theo mocks him, and asks since when he's been like this; Parrot says that's a story for another time.

### Dolphins, turtles, and Boomie

- They feed a dolphin to lead them to the nearest buried treasure, which points to another island, and breed turtles, knowing the mother returns to her home beach to lay eggs.
- TheLobsterMan's Rangers catch up, holding Boomie hostage: the clue, or Boomie dies. Theo begs Parrot to sacrifice him. Instead Parrot throws an invisibility potion at Boomie and hooks him away with a fishing rod, and everyone escapes, but Theo has had enough of Parrot risking everything to save everyone, and abandons the mission.

### The sunken empire

- By boat, Parrot and Boomie reach the underwater city. Boomie says maybe Parrot should have left him; Parrot promises to do everything he can for him.
- A stab shot comes down and kills Boomie. The invisible player says Boomie was a threat to his identity, and since Parrot has no idea who he is, there's no reason to kill Parrot. He doesn't want the treasure either.
- Parrot collects the sunken city's resources and returns to the first archipelago, where Theo is waiting with Sky, their [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md). Theo apologises and Parrot forgives him.
- Parrot now has a clear idea who the invisible player is, and vows to foil his plan.

## Items & mechanics

- Axolotl trick: helping an axolotl kill a fish clears mining fatigue.
- Frost Walker boots: their ice gives Parrot a fall-damage kill.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): luring one group into the other.
- Pirate language: "raised deck" means piston.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): the stab shot that kills Boomie.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): Sky.

## Outcomes

- The Sunken Empire's treasure is claimed by Parrot (see [The Sunken Empire Treasure Hunt](../../events/the-sunken-empire-treasure-hunt.md)).
- Boomie is killed by the invisible player.
- Parrot works out the invisible player's identity; he and Theo reconcile.
