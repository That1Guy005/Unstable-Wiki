## Summary

With Boomie dead, only [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md) knows [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) is alive, and Boosfer has teamed up with [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md). Still disguised, Wemmbu restocks at [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md), recruits Salvationism and [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md)'s guards, and lures Boosfer and Flame into a giant void trap. When Flame realises Boosfer has been lying, they turn on him together, and Boosfer escapes into the Nether, with Wemmbu following.

## Full breakdown

### Merchant City

- Parrot is suspicious of the invisible player, so Capital City is off-limits. In Merchant City's inner layer, potion ingredients are out of stock until a shipment from the Nether, a trade monopolised by a team of the deadliest players.
- Waiting at the portal on his last invisibility, he sees Flame arrive to collect ender pearls, and [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md) and the Nether guards deliver the shipment. Pearls are in high demand, so he trades some of his for potion ingredients, then digs into a mountain to brew.

### The End

- Looking for [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), he finds Salvationism hunting Boosfer, and raiders who claim to have beaten [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md); he lets Salvationism kill them so he stays anonymous.
- Egg is hiding behind leaves. Flame came looking for Wemmbu, Minute refused to give him up, and after their fight Minute left the main island for good. Boosfer is now working with Flame. Wemmbu shares the location with Salvationism.

### The first raid

- Salvationism insists on reinforcements; a public call gets no takers, so three guards come, one of them never in a fight before.
- Wemmbu scouts Flame's base and finds Boosfer alone. Boosfer asks why he's attacking when he's keeping Wemmbu's secret from Flame. The guards charge in early, and Wemmbu lets them finish the job.
- Flame returns, kills every guard and lets Salvationism run. Unable to use any signature weapons in disguise, Wemmbu flees, is caught mending, and escapes by Riptide [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) to the ocean.

### The world eater

- Salvationism plans a void trap in the desert next to Flame's Badlands base. Wemmbu doesn't help, and plays Clash Royale while the guards dig a huge hole with a world eater, then cover it with signs and sand.
- Wemmbu baits them: he targets Boosfer, and Boosfer tells Flame he'll reveal what he knows if Flame kills the invisible player. Flame jumps Wemmbu, who riptides towards the trap.
- A lag spike hits as they cross it. Flame saves himself with cobwebs and Boosfer lands on the edge. Boosfer points Flame to a stash inside the walls, and Flame kills the whole guard force and Salvationism.

### Boosfer exposed

- Alone, Wemmbu riptides up a waterfall and bridges across the void, trying to knock Boosfer in. Out of the trap, he and Flame fight while Boosfer watches.
- Wemmbu asks if Flame enjoys working for Boosfer, and whether Boosfer has ever proved he has the information. Boosfer claims the trap was aimed at Flame; Wemmbu says Capital City only wanted Flame dead because he was working with Boosfer.
- Flame realises Boosfer has been lying, and they turn on him. Boosfer flees down the escape tunnel Flame built. Wemmbu misses his clutch and pops a totem, briefly showing his name, but drinks invisibility just before Flame drops down.
- At the bottom, Boosfer claims ClownPierce killed Wemmbu, and jumps into a Nether portal. Wemmbu tells Flame to break the portal with water and pearls through. The screen goes black, and Boosfer can be heard cheering.

## Items & mechanics

- World eater: the machine that digs Salvationism's giant void trap.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Wemmbu's Riptide escapes.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the disguise, nearly blown by a totem pop.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): the pop that briefly shows Wemmbu's name.
- Ender pearls: in short supply at Merchant City, controlled by Clown's Nether.

## Outcomes

- Salvationism and the Capital City guards are killed by Flame.
- Flame stops trusting Boosfer.
- Wemmbu follows Boosfer into the Nether, straight into a trap.
