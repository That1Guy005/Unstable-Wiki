## Summary

The same days from [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' side. Desperate for news of Wemmbu, Flame becomes [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md)'s errand boy: fetching pearls, stealing [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s helmet, digging an escape tunnel to bedrock. Parrot laughs at him for it, and when "the Invisible Knight" attacks, Flame realises Boosfer has nothing to give him.

## Full breakdown

### Working for Boosfer

- Boosfer wants payment Flame doesn't have, so Flame agrees to work for him: brew potions, repair his gear, and gear up for battle.
- At [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md) the pearl booth is empty: Nether resources are monopolised by [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s team. Flame meets a player called the Invisible Knight. When the shipment comes, the city's guards are made to bow to [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md), Clown's right-hand man.
- Back home, Boosfer has covered Flame's base in Boosfer banners, and is losing to the Invisible Knight and Salvationism's army, which retreats when Flame arrives. Boosfer says the attackers were after Flame, not him, and won't give the information yet.

### Parrot's helmet

- Next, Boosfer wants Parrot's helmet, because Parrot's colourful skin annoys him. Flame demands Parrot's potions and helmet. Parrot sees that the great Immortal Demon is being bossed around, and when Flame says by whom, Parrot bursts out laughing.
- Parrot tells him Boosfer is using him and knows nothing about Wemmbu, and leaves him a choice: take the potions and he's just a Boosfer henchman. He drops the helmet, hints that the fight Flame wants is closer than he thinks, and quotes Wifies: "You need me more than I need you."
- Boosfer has built a shrine to himself in Flame's base. He calls the Unbreaking III helmet fake and trash, and refuses again.

### The tunnel

- Boosfer has Flame dig an escape tunnel down to bedrock, splashing him with strength instead of haste. Flame puts a Nether portal at the end and starts hitting Boosfer in frustration.
- The Invisible Knight appears and attacks Boosfer; Flame only joins once Boosfer promises the information. The Knight splashes him with weakness and leads him into a sand-pit trap, which fails to kill either of them.
- In the pit, Flame and Boosfer massacre Salvationism's horde, leaving only the Knight. Chasing him, Flame is persuaded to turn on Boosfer, and they both chase Boosfer, who escapes down the tunnel Flame built for him.

## Items & mechanics

- Ender pearls: the scarce Nether resource controlled by Clown's team.
- Parrot's helmet: Unbreaking III, dismissed by Boosfer.
- Escape tunnel and Nether portal: built by Flame, used by Boosfer to flee.
- Weakness potions: the Invisible Knight's way of fighting without killing.

## Outcomes

- Salvationism's army is wiped out.
- Flame turns on Boosfer, who escapes through Flame's own tunnel.
- The Invisible Knight's true identity stays hidden from Flame.
