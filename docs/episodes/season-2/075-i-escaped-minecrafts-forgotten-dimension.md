## Summary

Boosfer's Nether void trap drops [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and Boosfer into [The Underworld](../../places/the-underworld.md), a dimension beneath the Nether where blocks can't be broken or placed. Captured by the [Soul Keepers](../../factions/soul-keepers.md), they're forced into a truce: Wemmbu steals a happy ghast, kills the Soul Keepers' leader, and gets them both out, then spares Boosfer in exchange for his silence.

## Full breakdown

### Below the Nether

- Wemmbu enters the Nether straight into Boosfer's void trap, pearls back up, and knocks Boosfer in, but Boosfer hooks him down with a fishing rod.
- Instead of the void, they fall into a new dimension where no blocks can be broken or placed.
- Wemmbu follows a player on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), waits until it flies low enough, and steals it. A tower holds brewing stands and ingredients, so he brews invisibility and combat potions.

### The Soul Keepers

- Three Soul Keepers arrive on ghasts with Boosfer as a hostage and try to imprison Wemmbu too; they're driven off.
- In front of a giant castle stand cages full of players. Wemmbu sneaks in, overhears Boosfer talking to the Soul Keepers, and tries to make Boosfer drop his mace; the noise brings the Soul Keepers back.
- Unable to break blocks, Wemmbu is cornered while they break his armour, and surrenders to imprisonment. Some Soul Keepers leave to find more ghasts.

### The truce

- Boosfer escapes his cage with a smuggled pearl, and they agree to help each other get out. Boosfer gives Wemmbu his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and a pearl, and Wemmbu kills a Soul Keeper.
- Exploring the castle, they find a gold block that makes their game lag; its purpose is unknown.
- At the tower, Wemmbu attacks Boosfer for his pearls, and Boosfer promises to genuinely help if he's spared.

### Getting out

- Invisible, they sneak back to the castle to steal a ghast; Boosfer is spotted, but they get one, with the Soul Keepers in pursuit.
- Where the void trap was, the Soul Keepers have built a base. They pearl-glitch through the bedrock to avoid being seen. Boosfer gets up first and regears, and comes back with wind charges and experience so Wemmbu can use the mace.
- Wemmbu kills the Soul Keepers' leader, the rest retreat, and both escape.

### The deal

- Boosfer asks if he's about to die. Wemmbu gives his mace back and tells him not to leak his identity; Boosfer agrees, and promises not to attack Capital City, in return for not being banned.
- Wemmbu drinks invisibility again. The camera pans over the Underworld's landmarks and settles on the mysterious gold block.

## Items & mechanics

- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): stolen by Wemmbu; the Soul Keepers' transport.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Boosfer's, lent to Wemmbu, then returned.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): brought by Boosfer so Wemmbu can mace.
- Ender pearls: smuggled into a cage, and used to glitch through bedrock.
- The gold block: a lag-inducing mystery in the Soul Keepers' castle.

## Outcomes

- The Soul Keepers' leader is killed, and Wemmbu and Boosfer escape the Underworld.
- Boosfer agrees to keep Wemmbu's secret and leave Capital City alone.
