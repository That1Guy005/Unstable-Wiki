## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) goes into [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s Nether to kill him, and runs into [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) disguised as Clown's guards. They form an uneasy alliance, kidnap [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md) and track him towards Clown's vault. When Flame overhears that Parrot is using him, he sells them out to Clown for news of Wemmbu, then changes his mind and holds off Clown's army so Parrot can reach the vault.

## Full breakdown

### The guard shield

- Flame recaps Clown's achievements and enters a supply room of Clown's Nether guards, killing two. Then he spots Parrot carrying a shield that matches the guards' banner, assumes Parrot has joined them, and chases him.
- Parrot's chute trap, lava from the ceiling and a broken block over a hole all fail: Flame block-clutches and pearls past each one. At Theo's side, Theo splashes them invisible, but Flame catches Parrot and pops his totem.
- Parrot bluffs a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) trap that would kill all three, and tells Flame to get back to working for Clown. Flame says he's actually trying to kill Clown, so they ally.

### The festival and Ferre

- At the Nether Civilization's festival, Theo pushes Flame into fighting "GeneralChungus", and Flame kills him.
- Flame and Theo jump Ferre in the central building. Clown and his guards come up to save him but fail, and Ferre is taken hostage.
- Parrot deliberately keeps Flame from fighting Clown by taking Ferre to a remote bunker, drops him some maps and junk, and lets him go. Furious, Flame attacks Parrot, who explains he's tracking Ferre's general direction and wants a stakeout for passing guards.

### The betrayal, and the change of heart

- Flame pretends to be AFK and overhears Parrot saying he's just using Flame to reach Clown's vault. Flame goes to Clown's castle and offers Parrot's location in exchange for information on Wemmbu.
- They jump Parrot and Theo. Parrot escapes but Theo is captured; Parrot tries to negotiate and is captured too. Clown admits he knows nothing about Wemmbu.
- Before the execution, Parrot apologises for using Flame. Flame accepts, and tells Clown he'll have to get through him first.
- That buys Parrot and Theo time to find the vault. Clown abandons his army to stop the vault being ransacked, and Flame defeats the army.
- In [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), Flame offers Parrot a deal: the tomb of [The Invisible Mafia](../../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md), in exchange for finding Wemmbu. Parrot accepts.

## Items & mechanics

- [Banner shields](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): Clown's guard pattern, copied by Parrot and Theo.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Theo's splash, and Flame's way into the festival.
- Locator map: Parrot's hidden tracker on Ferre.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Parrot's bluffed trap.

## Outcomes

- GeneralChungus is killed; Clown's army is defeated by Flame.
- Ferre is kidnapped and released.
- Flame and Parrot strike a deal over the Mafia's tomb and Wemmbu.
