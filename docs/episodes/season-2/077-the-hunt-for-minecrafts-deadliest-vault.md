## Summary

The same raid from [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s side. During [ClownPierce](../../characters/clownpierce.md)'s Nether festival, Parrot and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) team up with [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md), extract [FerreMC](../../characters/ferremc.md) from Clown's fortress, and plant a locator map on him. Flame sells them out, then stands up for them, and Parrot loots Clown's lava vault and escapes. Flame then asks him to find the Mafia's tomb and what happened to Wemmbu.

## Full breakdown

### The festival

- Clown, King of the Nether, kills trespassers on sight and controls a mysterious vault. Every few months he hosts a festival open to all, which is Parrot's chance to find it.
- Clown and Ferre chase them on arrival, Clown suspecting they're after the vault. They escape, set traps and a shelter, and craft shields with Clown's banner to blend in with his guards.
- Flame demands to know why Parrot has Clown's shield and chases him; the traps only slow him. At the shelter Parrot bluffs it's rigged to explode. Flame reveals he's there to kill Clown, and they form a fragile alliance.
- At the festival Flame burns an innocent shopkeeper's enchanted golden apple, her favourite item, and won't replace it. Parrot wins another by acing a ten-question quiz on Minecraft mechanics and gives it to her.

### The fortress

- Plan: kidnap Ferre and interrogate him for the vault coordinates. Inside the fortress, invisible, Theo and Flame start breaking Ferre's armour, and Ferre triggers an alarm.
- Parrot slips past Clown and the guards to the alarm's PIN panel and breaks it; Clown realises an outsider did it and heads up to the fight. Parrot pulls a lever that floods the lower floors with mining fatigue to slow him, climbs the outside of the huge building (a full minute), and blasts a hole in the wall with TNT so they can extract Ferre.

### The locator map

- Ferre won't talk. Parrot slips random items and maps into his inventory and offers an elytra for the coordinates; Ferre just flees, and the others call Parrot stupid and even try to kill him.
- His real plan: one of those maps was a locator map, so Parrot's copy will show Ferre's position while he's in range. They watch an open plain for anyone following Ferre to the vault.
- On watch, Theo asks if anyone could outsmart Parrot. Parrot describes the players on the server, including those who want everything for themselves, and warns that someone with nothing to lose who decided to take the whole server could do it. Flame is that kind of person, he says, though he doubts Flame will try.
- They step away so Flame, AFK, won't overhear, and come back to find him gone. Parrot reflects that he always wants to make the server better, but keeps messing things up, and that using Flame for his own goals wasn't right.

### Flame's stand

- Clown attacks with Flame guiding him, and they surrender in under a minute. Flame heard everything; he made the deal for news of Wemmbu. Clown laughs at the idea, since he's been in the Nether the whole time.
- Parrot apologises to Flame and waits to die. When Clown decides to take them both, Flame stands his ground: "you'll have to go through me first", which many count among the server's most heroic moments.

### The vault

- While Flame and Theo hold off the army, someone tampers with a redstone mechanism and leaves; Parrot presses a button and a tunnel opens into the vault: a vast obsidian cuboid filled with a lava lake and many bastions.
- Clown's voice comes from behind him. Parrot flees on the elytra Theo gave him, looting chests from bastion to bastion under the low ceiling. A chorus fruit teleports him into the obsidian box housing the elder guardians, the source of the mining fatigue.
- He drops his flint and steel into the lava and realises he needed it for a portal out, then finds another in a chest and escapes to the Overworld.
- Back in [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), Theo is surprised he's alive. Flame comes into the potion shop with a task only Parrot can do: find the Mafia's tomb and its massive treasure. Parrot agrees out of kindness, remembering the Great War; Flame's condition is that Parrot also finds out what happened to Wemmbu.

## Items & mechanics

- Banner shields: the disguise as Clown's guards.
- Locator map: hidden in Ferre's inventory to track him.
- Elder guardians: the mining fatigue machine inside the vault.
- Chorus fruit and flint and steel: Parrot's way around, and out of, the vault.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Theo's gift, used to loot the vault.

## Outcomes

- Parrot loots Clown's Nether vault and escapes (see [Raid on Clown's Nether Vault](../../events/raid-on-clowns-nether-vault.md)).
- Flame betrays, then saves, Parrot and Theo.
- Parrot agrees to find the Mafia's tomb and Wemmbu's fate.
