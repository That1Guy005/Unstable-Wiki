## Summary

Someone is impersonating "the Invisible Knight", [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s disguise, and framing him for theft. Wemmbu tracks the impersonator and a partner, "Agent 22", from [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md) to [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md)'s factory, kills their happy ghast, and walks into a courtroom trap full of fake Invisible Knights. Popping totems, he unmasks them as [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) and [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), and pearl-glitches his way out.

## Full breakdown

### Framed

- Wemmbu and [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) are chatting in a [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md) restaurant when [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) and LAW accuse the invisible player of stealing from Capital City. Wemmbu says he doesn't need to steal, and escapes when Lettuce pots up.
- Sneaking back into Capital City, he builds a hidden base by the front gate to watch for his impersonator, and soon sees an invisible player in his exact armour and trims. The impersonator insists Wemmbu is the fake.
- Wemmbu goes for their offhand totem to reveal their name, not to kill. The impersonator takes the totem off, so killing them would reveal Wemmbu's name too, tells him to "count his days", and launches thousands of blocks into the sky on a hidden contraption.

### Boosfer's trials

- Only three people know his identity: [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), Boosfer and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md). Egg says Boosfer is back on the mushroom island, and the impersonator is already there.
- Boosfer can't tell them apart and sets trials: a one-block neo (both fail) and fetching a coal block (Wemmbu refuses, the impersonator goes). The impersonator tries to get Boosfer to say something only the real Wemmbu would know.
- Wemmbu tells Boosfer to stay quiet and goes for the impersonator's totem. The impersonator runs out to "swap out", and a second one appears, "Agent 22". Two against one, Wemmbu brings out his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md).
- They flee on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md). Wemmbu flies high to avoid being seen, since his cape and elytra are purple, drops down and kills the ghast, and one of them yells "SKY!", indirectly giving themselves away. They still escape.

### The courtroom

- Egg says Jaden and Capital City have built a dolphin highway to Jaden's pirate civilization, and that one impersonator came through the End talking about a courtroom trial: a chance to pop their totems.
- In Capital City, dozens of invisible players in Wemmbu's armour are heading to the courtroom. The main impersonator announces they've gathered to uncover the Invisible Knight's identity, and then everyone else is stasised out, leaving Wemmbu, the main impersonator and Agent 22.
- The room is encased in obsidian with elder guardians underneath, so mining fatigue stops him digging out. The main impersonator boxes himself in glass while Agent 22 attacks.
- Wemmbu pops Agent 22's totem: it's Theo. Theo's cart accidentally blows up the glass around his partner, and Wemmbu pops that totem too: Parrot. Flame was supposed to be there and never showed up.
- Parrot and Theo chorus-fruit out. Wemmbu sneaks pearls from his ender chest and tries to pearl-glitch through the obsidian; Parrot sees it and traps him in glass while he's crawling. Since the courtroom is wood, Wemmbu makes a crafting table and ladders and pearl-glitches out with a ladder.
- Wemmbu reflects that Parrot is one of the smartest players on the server, and wonders why Parrot cares and what he meant about having no choice but to hunt him. It won't be the last time they meet.

## Items & mechanics

- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): Wemmbu's exact trims, copied by the impersonators.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): popping one reveals a player's name; taking it off prevents that.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): Sky, killed by Wemmbu.
- Elder guardians and obsidian: the courtroom's mining-fatigue trap.
- Pearl glitch: Wemmbu's escape, using a ladder.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's answer to two impersonators.

## Outcomes

- The impersonators are unmasked as ParrotX2 and TheobaldTheBird.
- Sky, Parrot and Theo's happy ghast, is killed.
- Wemmbu escapes the courtroom.
