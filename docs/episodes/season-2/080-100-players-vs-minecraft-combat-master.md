## Summary

[LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) builds a new [LAW](../../factions/law.md) base a hundred blocks from [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' home, raises an army of about a hundred recruits, and gives Flame three days to surrender. Instead, Flame and [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) infiltrate LAW, steal its gear shipment, and Flame wipes out the army with a new wolf machine, which is why he never made it to Parrot's courtroom.

## Full breakdown

### The ultimatum

- Recap: the Wemmbu–Flame fight, with Spoke watching invisible from the sidelines.
- As Flame heads for the courtroom, Lettuce appears and calls him to the new LAW base, built in one day about 100 blocks away, with roughly 100 recruits from the Nether. Lettuce introduces his right-hand man, [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md).
- Lettuce wants Flame to drop everything in his inventory and ender chest and spend a fortnight in a cell. Flame refuses; Lettuce gives him three days to become a law-abiding citizen.

### Infiltration

- Flame calls Lomedy to Merchant City and tells him what happened. Disguised with LAW shields, they sneak into the base, using only signs so their voices don't give them away, and listen in on Lettuce, who announces a gear shipment.
- At the gate, Lettuce tells them invisibility is banned inside LAW's base. Flame breaks Lomedy's signs before he can answer. Told to drink milk or speak, they flee into a river. Lettuce kills 42hunterdude.

### The shipment

- Lomedy urges Flame to turn himself in; Flame won't listen. They intercept the shipment and kill the entire escort, including their happy ghast, and take the supplies.
- Lomedy says the killing wasn't necessary and questions Flame's violence. Flame explains: he's hunting Wemmbu to prove that true skill doesn't come from power, orbital cannons, maces or dishonour, and if players like Wemmbu keep winning, people will forget what skill means.

### The battle

- Lomedy pushes for an immediate attack; Flame builds another [wolf machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md).
- Lettuce gives a speech: the bloodshed ends if Flame turns himself in. Flame refuses, and wins the battle, forcing Lettuce to flee and killing his whole army.
- [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) asks why he never came to the courtroom. Flame says he was fighting LAW.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): the wolf army that destroys LAW's recruits.
- LAW shields: Flame and Lomedy's disguise.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): banned in LAW's base, which blows their cover.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): LAW's shipment ghast, killed in the ambush.

## Outcomes

- LAW's army is wiped out and Lettuce flees.
- 42hunterdude and the shipment escort are killed.
- Flame misses the courtroom trap.
