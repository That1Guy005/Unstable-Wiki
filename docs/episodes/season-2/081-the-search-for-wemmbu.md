## Summary

[ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) takes [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' deal: find out what happened to [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) in exchange for the Mafia's tomb. Contradictory stories from [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) lead Parrot to impersonate the Invisible Knight, stage the trials and the courtroom, and finally lure Wemmbu, Director-style, into a TNT dome far from Spawn. The explosion kills their dogs and exposes Wemmbu's name, and Wemmbu knocks Parrot into the void, where a stasis saves him.

## Full breakdown

### Contradictions

- Flame offers the coordinates of the Invisible Mafia's stash in the End if Parrot helps. Flame explains Wemmbu vanished after beating him, and Parrot shares his concern.
- Asking around Capital City, Parrot hears Egg said Flame killed Wemmbu, which Parrot knows is impossible. The End seems deserted, so Parrot loudly announces he'll blow it up; Egg appears and claims Wemmbu was maced to death, a different story.

### The impersonation

- Parrot copies Wemmbu's armour and trims and robs a store as the Invisible Knight, framing him to draw him out. Guards stop him for stealing, and Wemmbu appears.
- Parrot takes off his totem, so killing him would reveal the attacker's name, then escapes at a silverfish farm, launching himself thousands of blocks up with Wind Charged and harming potions.
- Back in the End, Egg ignores him, and Parrot mentions a courthouse meeting on his way out.
- At [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md)'s base, Boosfer asks why he's invisible "again", and Parrot tries to get him to admit the invisible player is Wemmbu, until Wemmbu arrives. After the trials, Parrot swaps out with [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md); Wemmbu maces them and kills their [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), and they escape through the Nether.

### The courtroom

- Parrot tells Flame the invisible player has an elytra and a mace, not enough proof. He trades netherite sets for replicas of Wemmbu's armour and hires Capital City players to pose as the Invisible Knight.
- At the meeting, everyone else is stasised out, leaving Wemmbu. Theo fights him while Parrot waits for Flame, who never comes. They chorus out, and Wemmbu escapes.
- On a mountain, Wemmbu asks why they care. Parrot says he killed Boomie, an innocent; Wemmbu says Boomie was a bandit who killed random players. Parrot won't stop until he's revealed. When they roof themselves in, Wemmbu's stab shot blows the roof off and pops both their totems.
- Flame says he was dealing with LAW.

### The Director's playbook

- Parrot copies the Director's tactics. In a stronghold he gives Wemmbu an ultimatum to reveal himself, then flies down a long tunnel to the meeting room once used to trap MrCube6, leaving Theo to drain Wemmbu's resources.
- He throws a pearl into the Director's old [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) and lands millions of blocks out, where Flame has built, on Parrot's instructions, a dome of obsidian lined with powdered snow, lava and TNT.
- On the central platform, Parrot tells Wemmbu this is the end of his fake identity and explains how he worked it out. Wemmbu says he faked his death to enjoy the server like a normal player. Parrot admits he knew the Invisible Knight was Wemmbu all along, but pretended not to, to save his own life.
- When Wemmbu says he could kill Parrot now, Parrot reveals Flame is there. Wemmbu's escape wind charge passes through the powdered snow and lava and sets off the TNT. The dogs Parrot and Flame tamed die, and Wemmbu's name appears in their death messages.

### Loose ends

- Parrot wonders whether exposing him was right. With no way out, all three end up back on the platform, and Parrot tells Wemmbu to drop his invisibility.
- Flame hands over the book with the coordinates to the Mafia's tomb. Parrot says he had to "tie up some loose ends", and accuses Wemmbu of killing Boomie, Sky and innocent people in Capital City.
- Wemmbu wind-charges him off the platform. Falling into the void, Parrot looks in a specific direction, which triggers a mirror receiver and fires his stasis.

## Items & mechanics

- [Armour Trims](../../mechanics/armour-trims.md): Parrot's replica Invisible Knight armour.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): removed so nobody's name can be revealed.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): the Director's old cannon, the route to the final trap.
- TNT dome: obsidian, powdered snow, lava and TNT, built by Flame.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): triggered by Parrot looking at a mirror receiver.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Wemmbu's escape attempt, which sets off the dome.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): the stab shot on the mountain.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu's identity is exposed to Flame and Parrot (see [Wemmbu's Fake Death](../../events/wemmbus-fake-death.md)).
- Parrot gets the coordinates to the Mafia's tomb.
- Their dogs die; Parrot survives the void by stasis.
