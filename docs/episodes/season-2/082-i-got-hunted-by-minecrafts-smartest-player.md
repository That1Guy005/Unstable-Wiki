## Summary

The hunt for [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) from his side. Chased by [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) after the courtroom, he restocks at the End stronghold, escapes a second obsidian trap under the old BAT base by pearl-glitching, and takes a pearl cannon straight into Parrot's TNT dome, where his own wind charge reveals his name. [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) demands a rematch on the spot.

## Full breakdown

### The chase

- Out of the courtroom, he flies to a nearby mountain to hide his bright purple elytra and restock. Parrot and Theo attack, justifying it by Boomie's and Sky's deaths.
- His resources drained, he needs an ender chest, but most options risk his identity. The End is the last, where [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) keeps their valuables, though Parrot knows he goes there. In the stronghold he loots an untouched potion room and finds ender chests.

### The second trap

- Near the portal Parrot offers him a choice: reveal himself. Wemmbu goes for the kill, and chases Parrot down a huge man-made 3×3 tunnel to the hidden meeting room under BAT's old base, where Theo is waiting.
- This time they're prepared: elder guardians around the room and slow-falling arrows to shut down his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md). Theo breaks the Nether portals he lights in the walls.
- Wemmbu blows up logs with TNT to craft ladders for a pearl glitch. As Parrot and Theo run low, Parrot leaves, and after several failed attempts Wemmbu gets out.
- With under a minute of invisibility left, he recognises a base he's looted before and finds potions with seconds to spare. Theo leaves to restock; they fight again, but Parrot's absence feels suspicious, so Wemmbu leaves.

### The dome

- He takes a nearby pearl cannon, thinking it's an escape. It sends him into a Wind Burst machine that fires him down into a huge semicircular build, onto a platform over the void, opposite Parrot.
- Parrot declares the end of his fake identity, explains how he worked it out, and reveals Flame is there. Wemmbu insists he's not Wemmbu.
- His escape pearl-catch fails when a wind charge misses, passes through powdered snow and lava, and sets off the TNT. The explosion kills Flame's dogs, and chat shows they were blown up by Wemmbu.
- Parrot says he had to tie up loose ends, and that they disliked each other; Wemmbu says he never disliked Parrot. Parrot lists everything Wemmbu has done while invisible, and Wemmbu wind-charges him into the void.
- Flame demands a rematch then and there. Wemmbu splashes speed and dives in.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): shut down by slow-falling arrows in the second trap.
- Pearl glitch: TNT-blasted logs into ladders, and out through the obsidian.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): the false escape into Parrot's dome.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Wind Burst launcher, and the missed charge that sets off the TNT.
- Elder guardians: mining fatigue around both obsidian traps.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): down to seconds before he finds more.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu's identity is revealed by the dogs' death messages.
- Parrot is knocked into the void.
- The Flame–Wemmbu rematch begins.
