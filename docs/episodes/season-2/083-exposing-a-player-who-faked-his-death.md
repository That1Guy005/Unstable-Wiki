## Summary

The Invisible Knight is unmasked: in Parrot's dome, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) drinks milk behind him, the TNT goes off, and the dogs' death messages expose [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md). The rematch is repeatedly interrupted by [LAW](../../factions/law.md), so Flame and Wemmbu team up, beat LAW's army, and corner [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md), who talks them into a deal instead of a kill.

## Full breakdown

### The reveal

- A recap of the Hunt for Wemmbu Arc: Wemmbu "unfairly" beat Flame, his death looked faked, and the Invisible Knight's appearance convinced many it was him. Flame allied with [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and a team of builders to trap him.
- In the dome, Parrot talks with the Knight while an invisible Flame waits behind him, then drinks milk. The Knight's escape wind charge misses, ignites the TNT through the powdered snow and lava, and kills Flame's and Parrot's wolves; the death messages name him. He drinks milk: it's Wemmbu.

### The rematch

- They pearl to the main island and duel, until LAW ambushes them. They escape, Flame using his [Orbital Dog Cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md), originally meant for Wemmbu.
- Flame tracks Wemmbu down again; LAW interrupts again, and both are drained. Wemmbu argues they need to team up against LAW for now.

### Allies against LAW

- They regear at Flame's base, kill LAW's army when it attacks, and chase Lettuce, who flies off on elytra; their Riptide [tridents](../../mechanics/trident.md) break trying to follow.
- They sneak into LAW's headquarters, only about 100 blocks from Flame's base, and corner Lettuce. He makes a fair point: the whole server loves him, so killing him would cost them access to [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md), [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md), [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md) and more, and [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) would just replace him.
- Wemmbu proposes a deal: Lettuce brings his entire force against the two of them. If they win, LAW stops attacking them; if they lose, they sit in a cell.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): the missed escape that sets off the dome.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): the Orbital Dog Cannon, used against LAW instead of Wemmbu.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): both tridents break chasing Lettuce.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): dropped by both Flame and Wemmbu with milk.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu is publicly exposed as the Invisible Knight (see [Wemmbu's Fake Death](../../events/wemmbus-fake-death.md)).
- Flame and Wemmbu team up temporarily and beat LAW's army.
- A deal is set: LAW's whole force against Flame and Wemmbu (leading to [The 2 VS 1000 War](../../events/the-2-vs-1000-war.md)).
