## Summary

To keep anyone from reporting that he griefed their village, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) secretly traps and kills the residents of several hidden bases, then lures two of [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md)'s spies into TNT carts. [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) gives him an alibi and Leo suspects [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), but the truth is quieter: Spoke is using the loot to pay off his debt to Quackenstein123's family.

## Full breakdown

### The first base

- An hour after a group of players saw Spoke griefing their village, he follows them to their secret base and rigs the staircase and other spots so they can't snitch.
- Leo is there doing a trade, and leaves. Spoke's chest trap kills one bystander, the staircase trap misses when the victim clutches, and another trap kills a second. The last player finds his hidden redstone room; Spoke kills them as they run.
- He cleans up the evidence, rigs more traps for the base's other residents, and kills them too. Leaving, he spots spies coming to investigate, fails to trap them, and slips away.

### Being watched

- In the BAT library he looks up the next hidden base. Leo questions him and brings him to the griefed village; Spoke claims he was building with Mapicc.
- Noticing he's being followed, he goes through the Nether to the fortress Mapicc is building, then sneaks out while the spies watch it.

### The second base

- Three players leave to collect seagrass for their turtle while he rigs their base. He traps two; the third spots him, and a Knockback sword sends them into a backup trap.
- Hearing more people are coming, he rigs the base further, then floods it with lava and blows it up, only to see the spies watching him.
- Since the spies have to neutralise threats before going back, he lures them into a tunnel of [TNT carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): one dies to the carts and Spoke kills the other. He frames someone else.

### Alibi

- Back at the fortress, Leo is talking with Mapicc: two spies have died, and Leo knows Spoke was at the secret bases. Mapicc says Spoke was with him. Spoke claims the spies were watching them all along; Leo says the spies only showed up at the secret bases, and hints it could have been JumperWho, who gave Leo his spies.

### Why

- At the border of [The Amplified](../../places/the-amplified.md), it's revealed that everything Spoke has collected is going to pay off his debts to Quackenstein123 and his family.
- JumperWho and one of her spies watch Spoke from a distance.

## Items & mechanics

- Redstone traps: rigged chests and staircases hidden in each base.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the tunnel trap that kills BAT's spies.
- Knockback sword: knocks the last resident into a backup trap.
- Lava: floods and destroys the second base.

## Outcomes

- The residents of several secret bases and two BAT spies are killed by Spoke.
- Mapicc covers for Spoke; Leo's suspicion falls on JumperWho.
- Spoke's loot goes to repaying Quackenstein123's family.
