## Summary

[FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) take on [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)'s 1,000-player [LAW](../../factions/law.md) army at a giant statue. Through crippling lag, Flame's [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and wolves and Wemmbu's nukes destroy the horde, with Flame even picking up a mace and elytra from the dead. Then Flame turns on Wemmbu and makes him admit who's strongest, while Lettuce reveals the army was never his whole plan.

## Full breakdown

### On the rails

- Flame recaps how Wemmbu beat him "dishonourably". They travel to the statue by minecart for the "aura", Flame wary of a cart trap at the end of the line.
- Wemmbu says he's satisfied and doesn't want to fight Flame any more, having already won. Flame accuses him of faking his death to dodge him; Wemmbu says he doesn't care about Flame that much.

### The horde

- Lettuce's army loads in and the lag is immense. Two nuke shots kill a chunk of it but don't fix the lag. Flame jumps into the pit while Wemmbu circles above; Flame's wolf rod kills some players, but he can barely eat golden apples through the lag.
- Swarmed, some attackers in full netherite, Flame asks Wemmbu for space to pull carts from a shulker, then kills whole groups. Wemmbu gets him into a corner for stab shots that kill hundreds.
- Both run low on experience bottles for mending. Flame moles back to the surface, restocks, and kills hundreds more with carts in a cave, popping his own totem. His helmet breaks; he kills over 20 more anyway, then races up the statue for gear. Some players steal Wemmbu's armour shulkers.
- Flame takes a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md) Wemmbu picked up from dead attackers: if the enemy fights unfair, so will he. He lands cart-into-mace stun slams, mace-to-mace combos and double-digit combos as the army is decimated.

### The betrayal

- Only Venthatguy remains; Wemmbu kills him while Flame restocks. While Wemmbu restocks, Flame attacks.
- Flame's wolf rod counters the nukes; Wemmbu's last stab shot misses, and a nuke shot barely kills one wolf, though it badly damages the wolf armour. Wemmbu's armour breaks and he falls into a hole.
- Flame tells him to accept that Flame is the strongest player on the Unstable SMP. Wemmbu reluctantly agrees, and Flame leaves.

### Lettuce's plan

- At LAW's headquarters, [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) asks why Lettuce marched a thousand players into a meat grinder. Lettuce asks which idiot plays all their cards in one move: he has a plan to kill or capture them both.

## Items & mechanics

- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Flame's main weapon against the horde.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): Flame's wolf rod, which counters Wemmbu's nukes.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's nuke shots and stab shots.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): looted from the dead and used by Flame.
- Experience bottles: the resource that runs out first.

## Outcomes

- LAW's 1,000-player army is wiped out (see [The 2 VS 1000 War](../../events/the-2-vs-1000-war.md)).
- Flame beats Wemmbu and takes back the title of strongest player.
- Lettuce reveals a second plan.
