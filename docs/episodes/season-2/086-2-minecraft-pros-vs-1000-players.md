## Summary

The same war from [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s side, from LAW's ambushes and the alliance with [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md), through robbing [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md)'s black market and trading at [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md), to the battle at the statue. Wemmbu concedes the title to Flame, throws away a spare mace, and [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) picks it up and takes the exhausted Wemmbu prisoner.

## Full breakdown

### Ambushes

- Their rematch is ambushed by LAW. Fighting would cost too much, so they leave; when LAW catches up again, Wemmbu's orbital barely dents them, and he flees.
- Flame chases him through the water and accuses him of running. They agree to deal with Lettuce first, restock at Flame's base, and beat the next army (spotted while Wemmbu farms sugar cane for rockets). Lettuce flies off, and their tridents break chasing him.

### The deal

- Inside LAW's base, Lettuce spots Wemmbu at once and they jump him. Flame says Lettuce will never beat him. Lettuce answers that killing him would change nothing because of the chain of command, would cut the link between Capital City and Merchant City, and that he'll keep hunting them until they submit or become prisoners.
- Wemmbu proposes they fight Lettuce and all his lawmen, and Lettuce accepts.

### Preparing

- They collect the dead lawmen's loot, then rob experience from Capital City's black market; intimidation fails until Wemmbu starts killing those who refuse, and the civilians can't catch them as they leave.
- At Merchant City, a wealthy trader wants netherite ingots for Mending books. Wemmbu has none, so he trades Mending netherite sets instead, plus more sets for shulkers of materials.
- On the minecart ride, Wemmbu falls into an already-sprung trap and survives. They fly the rest of the way to the top of a massive statue and wait days.

### The battle

- Thousands of players come into view and Lettuce blows a horn. Three [orbital strike cannons](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md) kill hundreds instantly, and the first half is easy.
- Later, attackers with maces and elytra appear. Wemmbu kills an AFK mace-wielder, then a few more, and gives Flame a [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md), which Flame accepts reluctantly, out of pity.

### The title

- With the last lawmen dead, Wemmbu surveys the destruction. Flame burns the mace and elytra and attacks him, using a fishing rod to teleport his dogs in while Wemmbu tries to orbital them.
- Flame wants Wemmbu to admit he's stronger. Wemmbu has an epiphany and tells Flame he can have the title again. Flame leaves.
- Wemmbu has three maces, one extra from a lawman, and throws the spare down a stab-shot crater. Lettuce appears, says Wemmbu has seen better days, elytras down for the mace, thanks him for it, and takes him prisoner at threat of death.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): three cannons fired at once against the army.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): the spare mace thrown away, and claimed by Lettuce.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): Flame's fishing-rod wolves.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): broken chasing Lettuce.
- Mending netherite sets: Wemmbu's trade currency at Merchant City.

## Outcomes

- The LAW army is destroyed, and Wemmbu concedes the title to Flame (see [The 2 VS 1000 War](../../events/the-2-vs-1000-war.md)).
- Lettuce gets a mace and captures Wemmbu, leading to [The LAW Prison](../../events/the-law-prison.md).
