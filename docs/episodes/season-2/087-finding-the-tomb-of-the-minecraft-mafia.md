## Summary

With [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' coordinates, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) travel to the outer End in search of the Invisible Mafia's tomb, rumoured to hold the resources the Mafia took from the server. It's guarded by Marlowww, who strikes from the darkness. Parrot gambles away his gear for a key, steals the real one, and loses Theo in the maze, but Marlowww's journals reveal she's protecting people, and Parrot leaves the treasure untouched.

## Full breakdown

### The door

- Rumour says the Mafia's resources were never destroyed, but stored in a tomb hundreds of thousands of blocks out in the End. Parrot and Theo nearly fall into the void at the first gateway, then take a flying machine for hours to a cherry-blossom island. A path leads to a massive door with two hoppers: it needs a key.
- They hide as a group arrives, including Parrot's friend CoinMonke. One tries to break the door and is given darkness; when it lifts, an invisible player in netherite with a quartz eye-trimmed chestplate is behind them, and drinks milk: Marlowww. The group thinks numbers will win, and quickly dies. Parrot and Theo save Coin.

### Eight-fold

- At an End station full of gamblers, everyone stares at Parrot. An argument between two players, one of them Rasplin, ends in a death.
- Rasplin says he has a key and wants to play eight-fold for it; Theo suspects a scam. The wagers rise each round, and in the last Parrot stakes everything (armour, totems, shulkers) on a good hand, and loses.
- Darkness falls, and Marlowww kills Rasplin instantly. Parrot escapes through a tunnel, and sees Marlowww staring back at him.

### The real key

- With Theo's elytra (Parrot has no armour), Parrot plans to lock the hoppers so Marlowww can't use the key, then knock her away with a Punch bow while Theo grabs it.
- As she's about to insert it, she says she can see someone right there. Parrot hides, but she means another player hiding nearby, and kills them. She drops the key, Parrot's shot lands, and Theo grabs it. They regroup at a lake and hide in a cave for days until Marlowww goes into the tomb.

### The maze

- An elevator drops them into a vast maze. Darkness hits, and Theo tells Parrot to run while he holds Marlowww off. When Parrot returns, Theo is gone.
- Instead of leaving, Parrot keeps searching, and finds a chest of Theo's loot with Marlowww's sign: keep going and end up dead like Theo. He keeps going.
- The maze ends in a huge cave with two invisible diamond-trim statues before the stash door. Darkness again; he flies off before she can confront him, and finds her secret base.

### Marlowww's journals

- Her bookshelves are full of daily journals. Her best friend joined the Mafia, so she joined to protect them, but the stash is set to kill any remaining Mafia members if anything is taken. She was never a monster, only appearing to be one to protect her friend and everyone else.
- The last entry says she didn't kill Theo: he reminded her too much of her best friend, both willing to protect their friends with their lives.
- Marlowww is behind him. Parrot acknowledges she's only doing what's best for others. She pulls a hidden lever revealing the Mafia's remaining [stasis chambers](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md), and walks away.
- Parrot apologises, goes through the door, down another cave and through a final door into the stash, rooms built for each resource, and takes nothing.

### Done with treasure

- He reunites with Theo and returns his items, while Marlowww watches from the island and turns invisible again.
- Parrot says he took nothing and was wrong about Marlowww and everyone else. He's done hunting treasure and doesn't know what comes next, but asks if Theo will come with him once he does. Theo happily agrees.

## Items & mechanics

- Darkness effect: Marlowww's warning before every attack.
- The key and hoppers: what opens the tomb door.
- Eight-fold: the gambling game that costs Parrot everything.
- Punch bow: knocks Marlowww away from the key.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the Mafia members' remaining stases, tied to the stash.

## Outcomes

- The Mafia's tomb is found and left untouched.
- Rasplin, CoinMonke's group and others are killed by Marlowww; Theo is spared.
- Parrot gives up treasure hunting.
