## Summary

[JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md)'s spies are investigating [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) over the dead spies. Spoke leads them into traps, kills a spy and takes his identity to walk into Jumper's archive, and eavesdrops as [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) argues Spoke should simply be killed. A bounty scheme clears his name for now, but Leo is watching him at Quackenstein's family's clock tower.

## Full breakdown

### Followed

- Two invisible spies follow Spoke; he pretends not to notice and leads them to a trapped base. They strip their armour to go fully invisible, but he spots their particles. A hidden lever turns the door's pressure plate into a TNT fall trap; the spies don't fall for it, but one drops a book with Spoke's name on it: this is an investigation into him.
- Jumper and four more spies demand their dead spies' armour back, and realise none of them has the book. Cornered, Spoke plays innocent, then pulls a lever that breaks the moss under their feet, sets off the TNT anyway once he's in the escape tunnel, and gets away.

### Back to BAT

- At his fortress, Leo is arguing with [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), and has come to take Spoke back to [BAT Headquarters](../../places/bat-headquarters.md). Spoke gives Mapicc the book and goes along.
- At BAT, two players want a bounty taken out, and one of them says there's someone he can take care of for free, if the client meets him at coordinates in the book in 90 minutes.
- Jumper is waiting at the fortress. Spoke renames the book "list of materials" and heads into the Nether with Mapicc, where they drop into lava hiding a tunnel. The spies follow from above, and when one comes through a portal after them, they jump him and kill him.

### Kimchandler26

- Spoke takes the dead spy's identity. The other two, thinking he's their partner, ask what happened; he says two diamond-armoured players jumped him and clears Mapicc's name.
- In a cave vault of archives, Jumper has them write their usernames, and Spoke writes Kimchandler26, the dead spy's name, and passes. Jumper's view: if Spoke really has the armour pieces, he deserves to die; if not, there's no reason to kill him.
- Leo reports another dead spy and takes Jumper aside. Told not to follow, Spoke goes fully invisible and listens: Leo says a spy is pretending to be a spy; Jumper says that's hard to pull off and they need evidence. Leo says everything has gone downhill since Spoke joined, and that's evidence enough. Jumper refuses without proof, and Leo leaves.

### Cleared, for now

- Spoke narrates how it played out: the two bounty clients planned to betray BAT, starting with Mapicc, who escaped; Spoke beat the one who jumped him. Jumper and Leo concluded the other was the real infiltrator, out to rob BAT, which cleared Spoke. Jumper would defend him from Leo as long as the armour wasn't found.
- Spoke brings more resources to Quackenstein and his family, and they start building a clock tower. Leo watches from nearby. He stays silent through Spoke's small talk, then finally speaks; when Spoke asks if that's a threat, Leo answers "I don't know, am I?" and flies off.

## Items & mechanics

- Investigation book: Spoke's file, renamed "list of materials".
- TNT fall trap: the pressure-plate trick in the trapped base.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the spies' full invisibility, and Spoke's eavesdropping.
- Lava tunnel: Spoke and Mapicc's hidden Nether route.

## Outcomes

- Kimchandler26 and another spy are killed; Spoke impersonates Kimchandler26.
- The bounty clients take the blame, clearing Spoke's name for now.
- Leo is convinced Spoke is behind it, and watches him.
