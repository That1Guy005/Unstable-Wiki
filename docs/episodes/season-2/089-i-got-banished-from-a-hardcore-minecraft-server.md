## Summary

Banned from [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md) and blacklisted by [LAW](../../factions/law.md), [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) spies on [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)'s announcement at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md): the server should have a king, and Lettuce's first act would be executing the captive [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md). Assassins come for Flame, [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) turns against him, and a hidden army of netherite lawmen beats him by strategy. Flame goes into exile.

## Full breakdown

### Exiled

- Capital City attacks Flame on sight: they've heard about his fight with LAW, and he's banned. His armour breaks under the crowd and he retreats.
- Luke4472 tells him LAW has told everyone to avoid him, and invited tens of thousands of players, everyone except Flame, to a meeting at the Northern Council.

### The announcement

- Flame goes invisible and pearls onto a statue to listen. Lettuce says Wemmbu and Flame have terrorised the server for years, and proposes a ruler who brings justice to anyone who harms it, with a vote on candidates.
- To show why he should be king, Lettuce has guards walk out Wemmbu, with no armour or gear. If elected, his first act will be to execute Wemmbu off the server. Flame, he adds, is monitored at all times and will be brought to justice too.

### Assassins and friends

- A potted-up netherite assassin attacks Flame at his base, hired by BAT, now working with LAW. Flame breaks his armour and kills him.
- Needing supplies but unable to go near Spawn, Flame calls Lomedy. At Merchant City, Lomedy has changed: he thought Flame's intentions were good and that players looked up to him, but now sees him as a liar. He'll help this one last time.
- Lomedy doesn't come back. Merchant City is a ghost town, and in the circus in the plaza, Lettuce and a dozen lawmen are holding Lomedy hostage: he lives only if Flame leaves now.
- As Lettuce raises his mace over Lomedy, Flame pearls in and splashes Lomedy invisible so he can escape. The lawmen drain Flame's resources and break some armour, and he hides and flees.
- At Flame's base, Lomedy says Flame is the problem; saving his life doesn't fix that. Flame says he doesn't enjoy killing, he just won't give up being the strongest to become a nobody. Lomedy says he's too far gone, hands over the trap materials, and tells him not to drag him down.

### Strategy over skill

- Flame sets traps and another machine that teleports hundreds of dogs. When Lettuce's diamond-armoured lawmen attack, a dripstone trap kills many and Flame finishes the rest, leaving only Lettuce.
- Lettuce tries to lure him into a cave; Flame restocks first, then follows. Deeper inside, Lettuce blows his horn and his real army appears: dozens of full-netherite players.
- The dog machine kills two. Constant weakness potions limit Flame to two more kills; 21 attackers die to wardens, and the rest drain him. He digs into the cave to escape.
- Lettuce says he can't beat Flame through skill, so he beat him through strategy. Every empire on the server tried to rule with power; Flame followed them around like a dog while Lettuce learned, and he'll use his influence for good. Flame isn't needed any more.

### Into exile

- Lawmen already hold his base. Flame accepts exile and rides off on his horse. He doesn't care about the base or gear, only his name, and vows not to return until he's stronger and has mastered Minecraft combat.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): the dog-teleporting machine, of little use against the netherite army.
- Dripstone trap: kills many of the first wave.
- Weakness potions: LAW's way of stopping Flame's kills.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lettuce's, raised over Lomedy.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Flame's splash to free Lomedy.

## Outcomes

- Flame loses to Lettuce, is exiled, and loses his base.
- Lomedy breaks with Flame.
- Lettuce declares an election for king, with Wemmbu's execution as his promise (see [The Election for King](../../events/the-election-for-king.md)).
