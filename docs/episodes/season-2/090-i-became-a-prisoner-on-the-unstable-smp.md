## Summary

In the [LAW prison](../../places/law-city.md), [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) is stripped, put in a prisoner skin and guarded by [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md), alongside old enemies [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) and Baablu. He's paraded at [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)'s Northern Council speech, escapes briefly with help from an invisible stranger, and is recaptured. In solitary, the stranger reveals himself: [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md), offering freedom in exchange for Wemmbu's orbital cannons.

## Full breakdown

### Prison rules

- Wemmbu nearly dies trying to escape Lettuce, who clears his inventory and assigns him a cell and a prisoner skin. At night every prisoner must be in their cell, on their bed; disobeying the guards means solitary.
- Loppezz guards his cell and catches him trying to break out. In the morning, after a meal, the prisoners clean the floor.
- Fantst and Baablu are inmates too. Wemmbu says they'll have to settle their differences to escape. A guard emerges from the walls and punishes them with work in a ravine.

### Escape attempts

- Loppezz does an item check and hands out wooden pickaxes; smuggling attempts draw suspicion. Fantst covers for Wemmbu and Baablu as they try for an exit, but Loppezz brings them back.
- Lettuce has a "special assignment" for Wemmbu: through an empty Spawn to the Northern Council, where he sits in a corner during Lettuce's speech about the server needing a king.
- On the way back, an invisible player splashes Wemmbu with invisibility and he runs. Looting a base for food, he's spotted by the owner, who snitches, and Lettuce recaptures him. Loppezz corners him with iron bars; he tries to escape on a nearby horse, but it's too slow.

### Solitary

- Lettuce knew he'd smuggled items and makes him hand them over, then enacts a new protocol: every prisoner must wear Curse of Binding armour. Wemmbu goes into solitary.
- The lights go out. An invisible player at the window drinks milk: Spoke, the one who helped him escape.
- Spoke offers to get him out if Wemmbu helps him with his remaining orbital strike cannons. Wemmbu suspects he'd just become Spoke's slave and refuses. Spoke says the offer stands, and that he'd like to see how Wemmbu escapes.

## Items & mechanics

- Curse of Binding armour: the prison's new protocol after Wemmbu's escape.
- Prisoner skin and wooden pickaxes: prison issue.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Spoke's splash that frees Wemmbu briefly.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): what Spoke wants from Wemmbu.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu is imprisoned in LAW's prison (see [The LAW Prison](../../events/the-law-prison.md)).
- His escape fails and he's put in solitary.
- Spoke offers him a deal, which he refuses for now.
