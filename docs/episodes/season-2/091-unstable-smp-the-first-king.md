## Summary

A book from a mysterious "Owl" sends [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) to [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)'s announcement, where Parrot decides to run for king, and then to the remote Treebo Village, terrorised by bandits called the Phantoms. With [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md), Parrot rescues a hostage and defends the village, and its residents give him the 100+ votes he needs to stand. He scrapes into the election at the very bottom.

## Full breakdown

### The announcement

- A lectern in Parrot's shop tells him to attend Lettuce's server-wide announcement. From the front, with spyglasses, he hears that anyone with over a hundred votes can stand for king, and decides to run.
- Theo is thrilled someone wants to save the server, but Parrot tells him Lettuce rigged the election before it started.
- Another book, from "The Owl", gives him a lodestone compass pointing to a tree. Theo wants him to campaign but comes along. On the way they pass reminders of the Mafia: an obsidian road made from the spawn prison, and bomber wreckage in the sky.

### The Phantoms

- Parrot calls out invisible players following them from Spawn: the Phantoms, bandits who rob players. Kibaxmc says he's doing it for his boss; he's short on kills and resources are tight.
- Theo kills two in seconds (the view switches to him). Parrot leads their leader into a deep cave, secretly pearls up, jumps back down, and shoots him when the pearl lands.
- Parrot offers to let Theo go home rather than face danger; Theo says they're in it to win it.

### Treebo Village

- In a jungle, a huge tree village is burning. Its leader, Dtowncat, says the Phantoms did it. In rain and heavy fog Parrot finds the Phantoms with a hostage, who'll be killed if he steps forward. Kibaxmc wants revenge and insults him as worthless, and Parrot, outmatched in unfamiliar terrain, retreats.
- Treebo was built years ago in this remote spot, and its residents didn't even know about the election. The Phantoms raid them regularly, and LAW says they're too far away to care. Theo reminds Parrot the election is close; Parrot says this is the right thing to do, though it won't win him anything.

### The rescue

- Following the Phantoms' trail through amplified jungle, they shelter from the rain in a mountainside and find a cave entrance in a ravine leading to the Phantoms' base. Parrot digs in just above, close enough for the hostage, PajamaSammy, to hear, and grows glow berries for him to climb.
- They hear the Phantoms plan to destroy Treebo tomorrow, and escape. Parrot shoots down the Phantoms' [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md) with an elytra and bow, killing one, to slow them.
- Back in the village, Dtowncat holds a meeting and the residents vote for Parrot: 108 votes, enough to stand. Parrot says he's staying to defend the village.

### The defence

- They've removed the village's railings, so knockback swords can push attackers off. Theo fights the Phantoms' leader, HerobaneNair; losses are seven each, until only Herobane and his right hand Kiba remain.
- Kiba is outnumbered and killed; Parrot lets Herobane go, on condition he never comes back. Treebo is at peace again, though it lost a few players.
- Sammy shows them a hidden ice-boat highway from the Phantoms' hideout to Spawn. Parrot realises the Owl wanted to show him there are players in need who can't get help, and wonders who the Owl is, and why he chose Parrot.
- Lettuce announces over sixteen candidates have passed 100 votes; the field will be halved every two weeks. Parrot is at the very bottom, on 109.

## Items & mechanics

- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): the Phantoms' transport, shot down.
- Knockback swords: Treebo's defence, with the railings removed.
- Glow berries: Sammy's way up out of the base.
- The Owl's lodestone compass: the lead to Treebo.

## Outcomes

- The Phantoms are destroyed; Kibaxmc is killed and HerobaneNair banished.
- PajamaSammy is rescued and Treebo Village saved.
- Parrot qualifies for the election with 109 votes (see [The Election for King](../../events/the-election-for-king.md)).
