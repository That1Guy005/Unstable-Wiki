## Summary

Even in exile, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) is hunted by [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) and [LAW](../../factions/law.md). Restocking at [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md)'s farm, he develops a new wind-charge pearl technique, beats the deputy, and helps defend the farm from a dozen netherite lawmen, but slow-falling arrows leave him helpless, and Lomedy surrenders to LAW to save him. Flame sets out to find teachers.

## Full breakdown

### Uninvited

- Leaving Spawn, Flame is followed by Deputy Ace and three lawmen, though Lettuce told him to get lost. With nowhere else to go, he talks a reluctant Lomedy into letting him restock ([Lomedy's Farm](../../places/lomedys-farm.md)).
- He practises a technique combining [wind charges](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md) and pearls to pearl faster and more unpredictably. Low on charges, he heads to a nearby trial chamber, where two bandits tell him to leave. Keeping his promise to Lomedy not to cause trouble, he tries to trade instead.
- A horn signals LAW. Flame hides in a wall while the lawmen strip the bandits and force them to surrender, then grabs the wind charges and leaves.

### The deputy

- Lomedy agrees to help test the technique, on condition Flame leaves afterwards. Testing with the farmers, Lomedy tries to mace him, really trying to kill him.
- Deputy Ace appears: he's only here for Flame, dead or alive, preferably alive. Flame says he's obeyed the exile; the deputy offers to spare him and boasts he's killed a thousand players. Flame says he can take his sword if he kills him. The deputy runs out of resources, warns Flame he has nobody but himself to blame for what comes next, and leaves.

### The farm falls

- A dozen full-netherite lawmen charge in. The farmers beg for help; Flame refuses because they called him "not good" earlier, until they take it back and Lomedy hands him his [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md).
- As LAW blows up the farm, Flame kills several with the mace and his new technique, but repeated slow-falling arrows leave him helpless with failing armour.
- Flame says he's about to die, and Lomedy turns himself in to LAW to save him. Flame flees on his horse; two lawmen chase him, one loses track, and he kills the other in seconds.
- He escaped, but failed against LAW, and his best friend has been arrested because his combat still isn't good enough. He sets out to find the few remaining players who can teach him, already with one in mind.

## Items & mechanics

- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Flame's new wind-charge pearl technique.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lomedy's, lent for the defence.
- Slow-falling arrows: LAW's counter to Flame.

## Outcomes

- Lomedy's farm is destroyed and Lomedy surrenders to LAW.
- Flame beats Deputy Ace but loses the fight for the farm.
- Flame begins the Training Arc.
