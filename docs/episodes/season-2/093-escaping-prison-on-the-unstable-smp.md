## Summary

After a week in solitary, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) plans a real escape from the [LAW prison](../../places/law-city.md) with [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md), [Fantst](../../characters/fantst.md) and Baablu: smuggling, signs under mining fatigue, a sergeant's training camp, and a raid on a guard room's ender chest. Each attempt fails, until [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) returns, kills two guards, and forces Wemmbu to accept his deal. The prisoners break out, and Wemmbu heads back to the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md) for orbital cannons.

## Full breakdown

### Smuggling

- Out of solitary, Wemmbu reasons that if Spoke got past the security, there's a way out. On tree-chopping duty, [Loppezz](../../characters/loppezz.md) orders one milk bucket per prisoner; the guard hands out more. Fantst tries to slip Wemmbu a bucket and Loppezz makes him drop it, but he keeps one.
- At the inventory check, Zam, Baablu and Fantst wind Loppezz up until she's shouting; Wemmbu quietly puts a cake back in the chest to look like he has more free space, keeping a milk bucket, some logs and one cake. Loppezz takes a break.
- With the lights off, he crafts wooden tools, a boat, signs and trapdoors, hides the evidence, and waits.

### Sergeant Raxby

- Loppezz hires SargeRaxby, who calls them the future of LAW. During his speech in the rec room, Wemmbu scoops water from a pond.
- During archery training, Wemmbu and Zam plan on signs. Zam wants to leave the other two; Wemmbu says they must all work together, or Lettuce executes him. Mining fatigue makes the signs slow to break. Zam has no invisibility; Fantst isn't sure. The sergeant catches them and breaks the sign.
- In the arena, fights end at the first totem pop. Wemmbu beats Baablu, learns Baablu thinks he has invisibility, then beats an already-weakened Zam, and gets to keep his totem as a reward. Nobody item-checks them for days.

### The guard room

- On farm duty, Wemmbu climbs the lights with his water bucket and takes levers from the glowstone lamps. They learn the guard rooms have ender chests. Lettuce brings a new prisoner: [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md).
- At lunch, Zam distracts the guards while Wemmbu and Baablu try the key-access door with a button taken from the guards' panel. Loppezz comes running, and they hide inside the panel and slip past while the door's open.
- On the second level, Wemmbu pops his totem with fall damage to clear the mining fatigue, breaks the dispenser that acts as the key reader, and places a button to crack the door. Baablu pulls a shulker of invisibility potions from the ender chest just as Loppezz catches them. After another check, Wemmbu still has a cake and Baablu one invisibility potion.
- In solitary, Spoke comes through a secret door. The invisibility potion is the last key, he says, and he needs Wemmbu as much as Wemmbu needs him. Wemmbu still refuses.

### The breakout

- Mining, Wemmbu and Baablu secretly wear everyone's helmets down to one durability. In a tunnel Baablu splashes invisibility and they try to break each other's armour, but run out of food, and Lettuce overhears them.
- Wemmbu and Baablu dig away as Lettuce and Loppezz blow up the area; they nearly make it through a cave before their hole is blown open. Forced to mend their armour, all four go into permanent solitary.
- With the lights off, Spoke kills two guards, and says the deaths will be pinned on Wemmbu unless he accepts the deal. Wemmbu says yes.
- They free Baablu and Fantst and fight the guards. Lomedy refuses to leave, saying he's safe and will abide by LAW. Loppezz's large army of guards dies and she flees. On LAW's main grounds netherite guards arrive; Wemmbu 1v1s Loppezz until she backs off, and escapes.
- He could have handled the guards with the right resources, but he's missing his two techniques, elytra-macing and orbital strikes, and can't take on LAW without them. He heads back to the Far Lands for more orbital cannons.

## Items & mechanics

- Cake and milk buckets: smuggled through inventory checks.
- Wooden tools, signs and levers: crafted and collected in secret.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): kept as a reward, then popped by fall damage to clear mining fatigue.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the shulker from the guards' ender chest, "the last key".
- Curse of Binding armour: worn down to break it off.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): what Wemmbu goes back to the Far Lands to get.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu, Zam, Fantst and Baablu escape the LAW prison (see [The LAW Prison](../../events/the-law-prison.md)).
- Two guards are killed by Spoke; Loppezz's guard army is destroyed.
- Wemmbu owes Spoke, and Lomedy stays in prison by choice.
