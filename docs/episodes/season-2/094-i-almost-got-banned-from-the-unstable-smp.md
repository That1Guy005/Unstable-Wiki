## Summary

[Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md)'s hunt for the missing armour closes in on [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md): the spies blow up Quackenstein's family's home and Leo hands Spoke over to LAW. Spoke slips away at the Northern Council, plots to have [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) kill Leo, and breaks the prisoners out of LAW's prison to make it happen. When they pull Leo's stasis, Leo survives, and Spoke's invisibility running out was Leo's plan all along.

## Full breakdown

### The family's home

- Leo and his spies blow up the base of Quackenstein123 and his family looking for the armour pieces. A spy reveals Spoke's hiding place; Spoke and the family run down an escape tunnel to where [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) waits with boats.
- Caught up, they dig into a cave, and Spoke sends the family through a Nether portal and breaks it. Leo, [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) and the spies make him empty his inventory and ender chest. No armour is found, so Jumper still defends him.

### Handed to LAW

- Leo takes Spoke to Spawn to hand him to LAW, by way of [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md)'s speech at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md). When Wemmbu is paraded out, Spoke gets an idea: have Wemmbu kill Leo.
- After the speech he pearls into the crowd, loses Leo, goes invisible, and splashes the LAW escort and Wemmbu with invisibility, but loses track of Wemmbu.
- Mapicc has infiltrated BAT as a spy; Spoke has him report on LAW's prison building and every guard's routine.
- Spoke follows Leo, who's asking players where Spoke is, to LAW's base and to Jumper's vault. He bridges above them with chests to drop dripstone on Leo, but a water droplet drips and Leo notices; Spoke escapes unseen.

### The prison

- Using a blind spot from Mapicc's report, Spoke digs into the prison and offers Wemmbu freedom in exchange for a favour. Wemmbu refuses; the offer stands.
- Days pass with no progress on either front. At the vault, Spoke finds Leo's note about guarding his spawn point at the old BAT Headquarters, and follows recently broken blocks to Leo's hidden base and pearl stasis.
- Decision made, Spoke breaks Wemmbu and the other prisoners out. When the netherite mercenaries arrive Wemmbu runs, and so do Spoke and [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md).

### Leo's plan

- At BAT's base in [The Amplified](../../places/the-amplified.md), Wemmbu finds them and asks for a favour; Spoke wants his own favour done first. At the other BAT base they pull Leo's stasis.
- Leo survives a mace hit from a great height and seems unfazed by the gang attack. As his armour starts breaking, Spoke's invisibility runs out, which was what Leo wanted. Having seen Spoke, Leo stasises away.
- It was never about the armour: Leo wanted to see when Spoke would get desperate enough to make a mistake and prove his guilt.
- Knowing the rest of BAT will come, and with nothing left to lose, Spoke waits for them.

## Items & mechanics

## Outcomes

- Quackenstein's family's home is destroyed; the family escapes.
- The LAW prisoners are freed by Spoke.
- Leo proves Spoke's guilt, and Spoke is left waiting for BAT.
