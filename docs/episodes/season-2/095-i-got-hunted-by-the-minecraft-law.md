## Summary

Out of prison, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) finds Flame gone and every door closed: [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) snitches, LAW pours into the End, and [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) sends him to the one player who can help, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md). After Spoke's failed hit on [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), Spoke tells him how to get [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) out of the End. Wemmbu and Egg fight through LAW-held [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md)'s pirates save them from [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md).

## Full breakdown

### Nowhere to go

- Flame's base is griefed and empty. Wemmbu kills the guards chasing him and hides in a cave until his pickaxes break. A village has no ender chest but points him east to [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md). Passing the 2v1000 battlefield, he kills a few lawmen.
- He sneaks into Redstone Town and asks 4CVIT for the redstone cannon. 4C got rid of it after people threatened him for it; when Wemmbu asks him to build another, 4C doesn't trust him and snitches.
- At the End stronghold he finds an ender chest with most of his gear nearly broken. Lawmen hesitate at the portal; he drops through, they follow, and Minute's trapped spawn platform and Minute himself kill them.

### The End won't fall

- Minute brings him to Egg to restock. Wemmbu wants to head to the Far Lands, but Minute won't abandon the End: it's his duty to defend it, and he doesn't know how many lawmen Lettuce will send.
- To get to the Far Lands without walking or redstone, Minute recommends someone in [The Amplified](../../places/the-amplified.md). They brew invisibility, and Wemmbu finds it's Spoke.

### The favour

- Spoke knows how to get a player out of the End without using their spawn point, but wants his favour first. With [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) they cross the Nether roof to the old BAT base, where an obsidian box holds Leo's ender pearl stasis.
- They pull it and jump Leo, who quickly gets out; Wemmbu watches from a distance as Spoke and Mapicc fail and Leo stasises away.
- Defeated, Spoke loses his temper when Wemmbu asks again, and tells him how, in exchange for another favour later.

### Through Capital City

- As Wemmbu and Egg prepare to leave, a huge force of lawmen arrives; Minute goes to talk to them, and the pair pearl into the portal to world spawn.
- LAW now owns Capital City. At a lightly guarded wall, Egg stands in front of a guard and gives them away; Wemmbu kills the guard, keeps losing sight of Egg, and players keep reporting their invisibility particles.
- Wemmbu decides to brute-force it, kills guards to reach the train station, and is surrounded by guards killing the dolphins that lead to the Great Sea. They outrun most of them through the tunnel.

### The pirates

- Deputy Ace waits at the far end with a dozen lawmen. Wemmbu's elytra breaks and they surrender, and the deputy starts explaining why executing them is best for the server.
- Jaden drops in and maces a lawman, who drops a Riptide [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md); Egg hands it to Wemmbu for the rain, and Jaden's pirates help wipe out the lawmen. Only the deputy escapes.
- Wemmbu warns that Lettuce will be king and execute Jaden for helping him. Jaden says LAW doesn't cover the Great Sea, and takes them to his new and improved pirate base.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Leo's stasis in the obsidian box.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): dropped by a lawman, used in the rain.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): brewed with Minute for the trip to the Amplified.
- Dolphin highway: Capital City's route to the Great Sea, being killed off by LAW.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wemmbu's breaks before the surrender.

## Outcomes

- Egg is freed from the End; Minute stays to defend it.
- Leo survives a second ambush.
- Jaden's pirates rescue Wemmbu and Egg; Deputy Ace escapes.
