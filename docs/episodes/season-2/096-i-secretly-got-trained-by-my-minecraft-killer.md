## Summary

Disguised and invisible, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) enrols in the dojo of Swight, the world's third-best PvPer, who flies a LAW banner. Through sword-and-axe shield breaking, water-bucket draining and real missions against bandits, Flame outlasts every other student, including the suspicious [Yungy](../../characters/yungyx.md), and becomes Swight's partner.

## Full breakdown

### Enrolment

- Swight is recruiting students in a town. Spotting LAW's banner pattern on his shield, Flame goes invisible to sign up, and adds a voice changer when Swight finds his silence suspicious.
- In the arena Swight teaches sword basics. Flame plays badly on purpose, then trades hits after one tip, impressing him.
- Next: disabling shields with a sword and axe together. Most students fail; Flame and Yungy succeed. The worst are eliminated, and the rest practise archery. The other students press Flame on his invisibility, voice changer and suspiciously fast learning.

### The first mission

- Two groups go to retrieve payments Swight is owed, watching for bandits. Flame's compass leads to a cave base whose owners say anything in their base is theirs, so Flame pots up and attacks.
- Popping a totem would risk revealing his name, so he focuses on keeping his teammates alive and letting them get the kills, weakening and webbing the bandits for Shandrea. One teammate still dies.
- Yungy and Fredrick3D_ faced just one bandit. Yungy argues Flame's group should be eliminated for the loss; Swight counts the death as the elimination.

### Water buckets

- Swight teaches draining an opponent's water bucket when they're webbed. Paired with Fredrick, Flame runs low on invisibility, and Fredrick steals the shulker he places. Flame tunnels under the arena to the auto-brewer, gets more potions, and makes Fredrick return the shulker.
- Fredrick brags he beat Flame, and Yungy backs him, accusing Flame of pretending to be bad to get Swight's advice. Swight says he only cares about real skill, not identity. In the rematch Flame beats Fredrick easily.
- Fredrick and Shandrea are eliminated. Fredrick refuses to leave and attacks Swight, who kills him.

### The elite bandits

- The final task decides Swight's partner: beat two elite bandits. Yungy calls Flame a cheat for using invisibility and being an unknown.
- The bandits use [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md). Yungy's armour runs low and he flees; the bandits mock Flame and walk away. Flame follows them home, lets his invisibility run out, reveals himself, and pops their totems. One blows himself up and Flame chases down the other.
- Back at the dojo, Yungy is telling Swight that Flame died, when Flame walks up behind him. Swight eliminates Yungy, who calls Flame a fraud as he leaves.
- Flame is Swight's partner. Swight sets his LAW mission aside and tells Flame to follow him.

## Items & mechanics

- Sword and axe: Swight's shield-disabling technique.
- Water-bucket draining: Swight's lesson for webbed opponents.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md) and voice changer: Flame's disguise.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the elite bandits' weapon.

## Outcomes

- Flame becomes Swight's partner (Training Arc).
- Fredrick3D_ is killed by Swight; the elite bandits are killed by Flame.
- Yungy is eliminated and leaves resenting Flame.
