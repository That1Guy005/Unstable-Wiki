## Summary

[Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) offers [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) refuge in [Pirate City](../../places/pirate-city.md) if they help fight [LAW](../../factions/law.md), which is building a super-powered orbital cannon at the edge of [The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md). It needs a full set of music discs, and only a rare item collector has one. Wemmbu tracks down the collector, Ouroboroid, but [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) follows him invisibly, takes the discs, and kills the collector.

## Full breakdown

### The cannon

- Jaden also opposes the server having a king. Wemmbu and Egg accept; Jaden takes the Riptide tridents the lawmen dropped and brings them to Pirate City. The market sells nothing useful for battle, and Egg gets scammed on water-breathing potions.
- In Jaden's meeting room: LAW has started an outpost at the start of the Great Sea and is shipping redstone to build a far more destructive version of the [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md). Its encoder needs every music disc to aim, and with the mob cap no new discs can be obtained. Rumour says a rare item collector in the Great Sea has a full set. LAW outnumbers the pirates twenty to one, so destroying the cannon directly isn't an option.

### Frog Island

- They buy a small custom boat with extra storage and an anchor while the pirates besiege LAW. After three fruitless hours, Egg suggests islands from Wemmbu's earlier adventures.
- On an island of frog lovers, nobody knows of a collector, but they have slimeballs, rare because of the mob cap. While they're threatening the locals, a death message shows Lettuce is nearby, alone. Wemmbu jumps him, Lettuce blows his horn, and lawmen invade. Egg gets to the boat while Wemmbu makes the island's leader point towards the collector. Lettuce lets them sail off.

### DaFroDo

- Bandits who've hunted Wemmbu for over a year over "the tree incident" demand their items; Egg complies, Wemmbu fights. Their leader, DaFroDo, was one of the old pirates who robbed Wemmbu's civilization back when Jaden and Wemmbu were enemies.
- Egg shoots down DaFroDo's boat, and in exchange for his life he points them to a floating marketplace. He slips away on the way, and they let him go.

### The Floating Parrot

- The marketplace looks deserted, apart from a busy tavern, the Floating Parrot, where Jaden is restocking. His pirates have found the island where the cannon is being built; the cannon could be finished soon.
- When the market opens, a seller wants both their armour sets for a stack of slime and won't name his supplier until they dangle him over lava: the supplier comes invisible and he doesn't know the name. Egg burns his stock.
- Wemmbu chases an invisible player through the market who escapes by Riptide, accidentally holding out a compass. They'll triangulate it, a strategy they've used before.
- LAW attacks the market and Jaden comes to help. Lettuce fires a railgun ([Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md)) that kills innocent players, and he and his other deputy, The_Oroboros, use debuff arrows to stop Wemmbu killing anyone. Egg finishes the calculation and Jaden holds LAW off. Lettuce is seen splashing invisibility and removing his armour.

### Ouroboroid

- The sea floor is close to the surface here. With water breathing, they find a sculk-sensor door into a secret base around a huge tree. A player in full netherite, the collector Ouroboroid, refuses to give up the discs, and they fight through another hidden door onto a massive bridge by a waterfall.
- Ouroboroid's stray XP orbs let Wemmbu mend his elytra enough to elytra-mace again. With only his chestplate left, Ouroboroid asks on a sign what Wemmbu wants. Hearing that LAW will take over the Great Sea with the discs, he agrees to help: he doesn't want his home destroyed.
- At his stash, he opens a chest of shulkers holding every disc. Some are pulled out, and Lettuce reveals himself. The collector's wind charge hits a self-destruct button, too late. Lettuce blows his horn, netherite lawmen attack, and Lettuce kills Ouroboroid.
- Wemmbu escapes, climbing the boat anchor Egg left as the base explodes. Lettuce tries to elytra-mace him and fails badly; Wemmbu lands a mace hit and Lettuce flies off.

### The plan

- On the main island, Lettuce tells [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) and The_Oroboros they have everything to build the cannon. The deputy doubts that blowing up the pirates and banning them is a good idea; Lettuce agrees it isn't, but says the pirates chose their side, and orders him to start charging the cannon. The deputy may yet resent the order.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): LAW's new, disc-encoded super-cannon.
- Music discs: the encoder's key, impossible to farm under the mob cap.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): Lettuce's railgun at the marketplace.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) and [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wemmbu's elytra-macing, restored by Ouroboroid's XP.
- Slimeballs: a rare-item clue in a mob-capped world.
- Compass triangulation: how Egg finds the collector.

## Outcomes

- LAW gets the full disc set; Ouroboroid is killed and his base destroyed.
- Innocent players die in the marketplace attack.
- LAW prepares to fire on the pirates.
