## Summary

[Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) vanishes after a fight with BAT's spies, and [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) claims he killed him, showing Mapicc's shield as proof. Grief-stricken, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) nearly blows up [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md) until [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) talks him down. With [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md), Spoke follows Mapicc's trail and finds him alive, rescued by [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md). Then Spoke turns on Jamato, fakes a LAW-style attack, and convinces the others Leo killed him.

## Full breakdown

### Mapicc disappears

- Drained of resources, Spoke sends Mapicc for supplies and talks him into setting a stasis, with blue ice and lily pads for a quick escape. After an hour, Spoke pulls it: Mapicc appears for a split second, full of arrows, yelling "We have to run", then disappears.
- At the Mafia stash, blue ice shows Mapicc fought the spies there recently. Spoke heads to Zam's base, and eight spies follow him. In an ice biome he springs TNT launchers he'd set up, which fire hundreds of TNT into the sky and kill every spy except Leo.
- Leo's [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) tactics beat Spoke's TNT-and-fishing-rod stunt. Spoke runs, asking if Leo's hiding anything, and shakes him off.

### The shield

- Zam's base is ransacked, but a hidden sign gives her new coordinates. A flashback shows Spoke and Mapicc finding banner patterns, and Mapicc putting an orange-and-yellow brick pattern on his shield.
- Zam hasn't seen Mapicc in a while, and warns Spoke not to go back to his bunker, but Spoke won't leave Mapicc's items there.
- In the bunker, an invisible player carries Mapicc's shield. He drinks milk: Leo. Mapicc put up a good fight, Leo says, but lost, and the shield is his proof. A void trap opens under Spoke, who saves himself; Leo stasises away and spies burst from behind paintings. Spoke chorus-fruits out.

### Capital City

- Spoke decides to blow up Capital City to force proof that Mapicc is alive, and has Zam warn Lettuce.
- At the gates, [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) waits with lawmen and guards. Spoke flings lit TNT with a fishing rod, kills three guards, and drives off the deputy. He mines the city with TNT and builds a TNT cannon.
- Parrot dismantles the redstone behind him. Broken, Spoke asks if that will fix anything; Parrot says he's about to destroy hundreds of homes. Spoke says he never wanted this, it's nothing next to what BAT and LAW did to him, and he has no home or friend left. Parrot says he's lost people too.
- When Spoke describes the shield pattern, Parrot recognises it as one [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) used: the shield may be a decoy, and Mapicc may be alive. Spoke tells Parrot to be the best king the server has ever had.

### The trail

- At the stash with Zam, Spoke shows a hidden floor where the spies talked on signs: they planned to kill Mapicc, but Leo wanted to do it himself, and then someone the spies didn't know, but who knew Spoke, helped Mapicc fight them off with weapons the spies had never seen.
- They follow blue ice and lily pads across biomes to a snowy area with light under the snow: a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md). It lands them near a lighthouse, then a house ringed with flowers. Climbing the field with wind charges, Spoke sees the Far Lands in the distance and thinks of the one person he knows there.
- Inside are Mapicc and Jamato, Spoke's first friend and one of the few who know the server's exploits. Spoke feels betrayed that Jamato only now came back. Mapicc says BAT is no longer a problem; Spoke says they're not winning, they're running.
- Jamato has always wanted to explore, and had planned to go with Spoke before things ended badly between them. The others would rather explore than fight, and Jamato invites Spoke. Spoke secretly sets a stasis first, then agrees.

### The lighthouse

- Jamato leaves to "tie up loose ends". Spoke follows invisibly to the lighthouse, where Jamato knows he's there. Jamato says Spoke uses his friends for himself; Spoke says Jamato was nowhere during the Mafia's reign. Jamato replies that the real threat to the server isn't the Mafia, or Leo, or LAW: it's Spoke, who is never satisfied.
- When Spoke threatens him, Jamato strips his armour, throws his totem off the lighthouse and dares him. Spoke hesitates; "That's what I thought," Jamato says. Spoke attacks, and Jamato's other totem teleports him to a stasis.
- Invisible and posing as Leo, Spoke attacks Mapicc and Zam with TNT carts. Near death, he pulls his stasis, blows up the netherite armour, and returns visible, acting confused. He convinces them Leo followed them through the pearl cannon.
- At the wrecked lighthouse, he sends them up top, throws an XP bottle where Jamato's totem fell, and "finds" the totem and XP as proof Leo killed Jamato. They build Jamato a grave.
- Alone, Spoke laughs: Jamato is smart enough not to cross him, and Jamato almost turned Mapicc and Zam against him. In another universe they could have been best friends. On the Far Lands, Jamato looks down at his house.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Mapicc's split-second pull, and the stases Spoke and Jamato secretly set.
- Mapicc's shield: the orange-and-yellow brick pattern, used as a decoy.
- TNT launchers: Spoke's ice-biome trap for the spies.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): hidden under the snow on Mapicc's trail.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Leo's tactics, and Spoke's fake "Leo" attack.
- [Totem of Undying](../../mechanics/totem-of-undying.md): Jamato's thrown-away totem, and the second one that saves him.

## Outcomes

- The spies following Spoke die to his TNT launchers; only Leo survives.
- Mapicc is alive, rescued by Jamato.
- Spoke drives Jamato away and makes Mapicc and Zam believe Leo killed him.
