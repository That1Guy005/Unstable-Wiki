## Summary

The election for king begins at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md). [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) protects the identity of his secret ally the Owl by killing a spying guard, campaigns with a misspelled "VOTE PAROT" sign, and debates [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) himself, exposing that [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) has escaped. Lettuce accuses Parrot of murder, and the Owl, Spepticle, takes the blame and is executed. Parrot makes the top eight, and Lettuce declares "The Purge".

## Full breakdown

### The rules

- Lettuce gathers the fifteen other candidates: the pool halves every two weeks; votes aren't permanent and can be changed at any time; and all candidates live in the Northern Council, the top eight in the castle and the bottom eight in the towers.
- Two days earlier, the Owl, presumably a LAW insider who got Parrot his votes, left a book in his room offering information about Lettuce at a meeting in a week. Theo is too busy with the (useless) provided gear to listen, and goes to move their things from the potion shop.

### The guard

- Scouting rivals, Parrot asks [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md) how he got 380 votes and is shown his room, where Parrot notices a lectern book and realises he left the Owl's message in his own room.
- A "guard" Lettuce never mentioned is waiting there, makes an excuse, and leaves; the book has been replaced with a fake. The guard goes invisible. Parrot shoots at the particles, misses several times, and finally kills him, far enough away that the death message doesn't load for the other guards. The Owl's identity is safe.

### The debate is announced

- Lettuce announces a debate in a week, so the server can get to know the candidates. Boosfer calls it garbage; anyone absent is removed. Dispensers randomise the pairings and [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) reads them out: Parrot against Lettuce. Lettuce mentions a guard has gone missing.
- Parrot suspects the draw was rigged, but sees a chance to beat Lettuce in front of thousands.

### Campaigning

- Door-to-door in Capital City goes badly (Theo barges into houses) and gets no votes. Parrot wants votes from people who care, so they build a giant sign in the middle of the ocean: "VOTE PAROT". Theo thinks the misspelling still gets the point across, and Boosfer mocks it.
- Boosfer's secret: getting players to close their eyes and quickly write and sign his name in a book.
- After three days of little progress, Lettuce stops them for an item check over the dead guard. The Owl's books are unsigned, so Parrot deletes the text and fills his inventory with cake. Lettuce finds nothing but warns surveillance has been stepped up. Parrot tells Theo, on a book, that he had to kill the guard.

### The Owl

- Rehearsing, Theo wants Parrot to attack Lettuce's record, but Parrot thinks it's wrong to tear someone down. At night, players arrive for what turns out to be Lettuce's small memorial for the guard. Invisible, Parrot follows another invisible player to the castle roof.
- It's Spepticle, a LAW higher-up: the Owl. He sent Parrot to Treebo Village. Every town he joined, he ended up at the bottom, then fell into debt in Capital City, and Parrot's gift paid it off. Inspired, he joined LAW to help people, but realised Lettuce's justice was skewed. When Lettuce declared his candidacy, he remembered Parrot, and became the Owl (a bird, like Parrot) to help him, changing and adding rules from inside.
- His secret: LAW doesn't have Wemmbu any more; he escaped. Spepticle has been assigned to commentate the debate.

### The debate

- Most candidates set out their plans. [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) says only one person was rooting for her and drops out; Boosfer says nothing. Capital City's mayor SirPig_ arrogantly insists nothing needs improving, while [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) shares her experiences with taxes, and the crowd backs her.
- Asked what he'd do as king, Lettuce lists what he's already done (wealth, protection for Capital City and [Redstone Town](../../places/redstone-town.md)) and plans to expand into the Amplified and the Far Lands. Parrot points to Treebo Village, far from LAW's reach, and to the danger for anyone outside Spawn's area; Lettuce cites his war against the "Pirate oppressors", to cheers.
- Parrot says most new players never make it out of Spawn, and those who do have no food or shelter. He brings up the thousand players Lettuce sacrificed to capture one, and asks if Lettuce can produce Wemmbu twelve hours from now, revealing Wemmbu has escaped.
- Lettuce says he won't take orders from a murderer: his investigators found the dead lawman's items in Parrot's room. He'd known all along and waited to expose Parrot in front of the server.
- Spepticle steps up and claims he killed the guard and hid the loot in Parrot's room. Lettuce takes his armour and has him step into the cauldron. Parrot tries to take the blame, but Spepticle accepts the execution so Parrot can keep running.

### Afterwards

- Deputy Ace runs from Capital City shouting that it's under attack. Parrot finds TNT everywhere and [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) on a cannon, and talks him down (see Spoke's episode), giving him the drive to find his best friend.
- Despite the debate, Parrot's votes grow and he reaches the top eight.
- In private, Lettuce is stressed; Deputy Ace reminds him he's top of the leaderboard. Lettuce knows Spepticle was a traitor who wanted Parrot to win, and wants Parrot gone. The deputy says killing a candidate is wrong; Lettuce agrees it's too risky to do himself, so he'll leave it to the other candidates, and declares "The Purge".

## Items & mechanics

- Unsigned books: the Owl's messages, wiped before an item check.
- "VOTE PAROT": the campaign's ocean sign.
- Lava cauldron: LAW's public execution.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): the spying guard, and Parrot's night meeting.

## Outcomes

- Spepticle, the Owl, is executed; a LAW guard is killed by Parrot.
- JumperWho drops out; Parrot reaches the top eight (see [The Election for King](../../events/the-election-for-king.md)).
- Wemmbu's escape is made public, and Lettuce orders "The Purge".
