## Summary

Still invisible, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) works mercenary jobs as Swight's sidekick, defending a village from mountain bandits while Swight teaches him shield disabling, bow boosting and game sense. When [LAW](../../factions/law.md) arrives with intel on Flame, Swight realises his partner is the man he was hired to kill. Flame talks his way out with a vault and his silence, and leaves with Swight's last lesson.

## Full breakdown

### The mountain bandits

- A poor village fears it'll be wiped out by repeat bandit raids, and offers the bandits' own vault as payment. Swight accepts. Between jobs, Swight teaches shield-disabling basics and other tips.
- A horn signals the attack. The leader shows them dripstone traps and TNT cannons, then hides. Flame has Swight bait bandits into the holes for the cannons, but the cannon blows itself up on the second shot.
- Flame draws the bandits into the village and knocks them into the dripstone trap with a knockback sword, killing them without his name showing. Swight falls in; Flame keeps popping totems. When Flame is knocked into a trap with two bandits, Swight drops in and kills them.
- Swight asks why Flame wasn't fighting directly; "popping totems" satisfies him.

### The second village

- On the way to rescue survivors from a raided village, Swight says it doesn't matter who his targets are: only their skill and his pay. He despises people who kill for titles or vengeance.
- Sweeping houses, Flame falls into a fall trap with dripstone, pops a totem and loses his invisibility. He refuses Swight's help, breaks the armour stands and re-splashes before Swight arrives. One survivor falls into a powdered-snow trap on the way back; the other is welcomed home with cheers.

### Training

- Swight notes Flame's aggressive style, and since he can't close the distance, teaches him to bow boost. Then game sense: watching what your opponent does. They spar so Flame learns Swight's style.
- Scouts find the bandits' base. On its glass roof, Flame drops [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) while Swight jumps in, killing many, until a bandit carts Flame and pops his totem. He re-splashes invisibility, reads the bandit's style to avoid more carts, and they win. Swight praises how well their styles match.

### Unmasked

- A LAW horn sounds: LAW bans invisibility. A lawman has intel that Flame went missing, probably faked his death, uses cobwebs a lot and learns fast, which matches Swight's partner.
- Out of excuses, Flame lets his invisibility run out. The lawman leaves for backup. Swight is furious: he's been working with the man he was hired to kill, and wants the credit for himself. Flame explains LAW took everything from him; Swight doesn't care.
- Flame challenges him, offering his reputation if Swight wins. Swight puts on his netherite. Mid-fight Flame proposes a deal: otherwise he'll tell LAW that Swight trained and teamed with him, destroying his reputation; instead, Swight can have the vault.
- The vault holds hundreds of armour sets and plenty more. Satisfied, Swight lets him go, and credits him for making him put on his netherite. He suggests Flame become a mercenary when this is over, and leaves him one piece of advice: learn the rest of the items, and find players with different skills to master them.
- Flame sets out to find his next trainer.

## Items & mechanics

- Shield disabling and bow boosting: Swight's lessons.
- Knockback sword and dripstone traps: Flame's nameless kills.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): Flame's glass-roof bombardment, and the bandit's cart that pops him.
- [Invisibility](../../mechanics/invisibility.md): Flame's disguise, banned by LAW.
- The bandits' vault: Swight's payment, and Flame's bargaining chip.

## Outcomes

- The mountain bandits are destroyed; two survivors are rescued.
- Swight learns Flame's identity and lets him go in exchange for the vault.
- Flame continues the Training Arc.
