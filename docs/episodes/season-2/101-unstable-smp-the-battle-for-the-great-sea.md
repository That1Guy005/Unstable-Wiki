## Summary

[LAW](../../factions/law.md)'s new orbital cannon is finished, and its strikes are already landing as [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md), [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md)'s pirates storm its island. [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) blasts Wemmbu across [The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md) with a breeze machine. Wemmbu returns with old man Linn's trident and armour, learns the [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md), and with Egg destroys the cannon, though Jaden is captured and the pirates are almost wiped out.

## Full breakdown

### The first assault

- The cannon would take hours to wipe out [Pirate City](../../places/pirate-city.md), so the pirates attack its island. An orbital strike lands between the two sides and kills a dozen players. Egg waits for LAW's traps to be set off before jumping in.
- Wemmbu only needs to destroy the cannon, but LAW's numbers and slow falling stop his mace, and most of the pirates are soon dead. Lettuce maces him out of the air, and calls in [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md), The_Oroboros, [TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md) and [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md).
- They back off when Wemmbu pots up, and Lettuce triggers a machine that releases a mass of breezes, launching him millions of blocks away. He shields and stops against an island with a watchtower.

### Linnwyrm's book

- The tower holds a book about the Great Sea's islands and civilizations, and a map showing the hidden Atlantis treasure, which [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) had left unlooted, but none of it helps him cross the sea.
- Rereading, he finds a layer beneath the temple. Two players live there, who need their Riptide tridents to get around. Wemmbu considers killing them for one, remembers the rare item collector, and doesn't.
- They ask him to kill the elder guardians protecting the temple so they can build again. He digs through its floor and kills them, mending his elytra and maces. They give him a turtle helmet and send him to old man Linn.
- On an island of wizard-like towers, Linnwyrm asks what a trident is to him. Wemmbu explains and shows the book, which Linn wrote and lost. Because Wemmbu returned it and spared the builders, Linn gives him his own [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md) and lets him regear in his armoury; Wemmbu trims a netherite set.

### Back at the island

- LAW has built a huge base around the cannon. Mining fatigue slows Wemmbu's digging, and Deputy Ace attacks before he breaks a block, saying Jaden has surrendered. Lettuce says the pirates are gone and there's nothing left to fight for. Wemmbu pops a totem to clear his effects and breaks the first layer, but can't handle three attackers at once, and riptides back to Pirate City.
- The city is deserted except for Egg and a few pirates huddled by the main tower. Jaden was captured and the rest retreated. Egg wants Wemmbu to lead; Wemmbu's plan is to sneak in for Jaden and the cannon while the pirates distract the guards.

### The second assault

- Invisible, Wemmbu and Egg swim to the island. Mining fatigue and obsidian stop them breaching the walls, so they wind-charge over the watchpoint. Under the main base, Wemmbu hears Deputy Ace say the cannon is ten minutes from ready, and kills a guard to signal the pirates.
- The_Oroboros spots them. Wemmbu's obsidian box to protect Egg fails, the entrance is sealed, and their armour wears down. On the shore, Linn arrives with a new netherite set and advice: use the spear. He won't fight; he's a pacifist.
- Wemmbu crafts a spear and impales lawmen, switching between spear and mace, until he runs out of rockets. When it rains, he riptides to the base and spears the entrance guards, opens a door with a key, but lawmen force their way in and he flies back to Egg.
- Egg says to place a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md); Wemmbu has lost his rails, so they craft more. The cart exposes the cannon, they break some components, and it blows itself up.
- Lettuce asks Deputy Ace why he didn't fire the cannon. The deputy just watches Wemmbu escape, making no attempt to stop him.

### Leaving the sea

- Back in Pirate City, they're the only ones left, and agree to leave so the city survives. Wemmbu heads for the Far Lands to replenish his strikes.

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): LAW's disc-encoded cannon, destroyed by Wemmbu and Egg.
- [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md): Linn's advice, and Wemmbu's new weapon.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Linnwyrm's gift.
- Breeze machine: Lettuce's launcher that blasts Wemmbu across the sea.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the cart that exposes the cannon.
- Elder guardians: the temple's guards, killed for XP.

## Outcomes

- LAW's cannon is destroyed.
- Jaden_MAN is captured and the pirates are nearly wiped out.
- Wemmbu and Egg abandon Pirate City; Deputy Ace quietly lets them go.
