## Summary

To master the [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md), [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) tracks down his old teammate [ManePear](../../characters/manepear.md), hiding in a cave for over a year. Mane trains him in exchange for errands, including an attribute-swap trick, and agrees to hunt [LAW](../../factions/law.md)'s hidden netherite base for the loot. LAW captures Flame; Mane comes back to break him out, and they wipe out the lawmen and loot the vault.

## Full breakdown

### The hermit

- Rumour says Mane, the only player on the server who has mastered the spear, vanished into a cave over a year ago. In the cave Flame finds Mane's banner and the skeleton Mane says nearly banned him.
- Mane appears behind him and, assuming Flame is here to settle the score from Mane's betrayal of the [Zam Empire](../../places/zam-empire.md), attacks. Flame explains he wants training, which Mane finds strange; Flame should be training him. Reminding him they used to be brothers doesn't help, given how things ended.
- Mane will help if Flame helps him kill a group of stacked players. To show good faith, he demonstrates the spear's basics. Flame can't use it like Mane: Flame plays to survive, Mane plays aggressively for fun.

### Errands

- At the players' base, Flame holds and survives while Mane quick-drops them, and Flame lands a few spear kills. They wipe them out and loot their gear.
- Mane tries to leave, calling it a one-time deal and treating Flame like a fan, but Flame keeps following and offers to fight it out, until Mane agrees to train him more if he'll then leave him alone.
- Walking back, Mane moves and lunges faster with a secret mechanic, which he explains at his base: an attribute swap with the spear.

### The pitch

- Flame asks Mane to help find LAW's hidden base, where he believes they gear up their army. Mane suspects Flame led LAW to him; Flame denies it. Mane doesn't believe a whole faction could be stacked in netherite, having hunted stacked players himself, but Flame is a man of his word, so he agrees, keeping all the loot.
- Riding out, Flame explains his plan to learn every weapon and become a combat master worthy of his reputation. Mane says he was the same, but caring about reputation stole the fun. Flame says his name is all he has; Mane suggests caring for people over a title. Flame says there was a time he did, but not now. Mane warns that as such a prominent figure, he'll be failed, betrayed and used, and would be freer without the title. Flame says he doesn't mind competition, he just wants recognition for his skill.

### SargeLAW

- In a nearby town, Flame walks up to the first civilian and announces he wants to kill LAW. Mane tells him to be slick: the way Flame presents himself is why he has bounties, and people have tried to hire Mane to kill him.
- Mane talks to the leader peacefully, but LAW has told them not to trust Flame or his companions, so Mane declares himself leader, blows up the village and kills the civilians. They tell the survivors to summon LAW.
- [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md) arrives. Flame admits to it, and Mane says Sarge should have brought more people. Sarge blows a horn, and netherite lawmen drop from the sky with weakness potions. Mane lunges away; elytra-wearing lawmen catch Flame, follow him underground, and corner him.

### Prison and rescue

- Mane has left him. Sarge makes Flame drop everything except his spear, escorts him up a staircase (Flame can't time an escape with the spear) to a temporary LAW compound, puts him in Curse of Binding copper armour, and locks him in a cell. The prisoner opposite ragebaits him successfully.
- Mane blows open the wall, splashes Flame invisible and hands him a shulker of gear. Mane breaks the copper armour and they lose the lawmen invisibly. A lawman lets slip where Flame's items are; Flame breaks a window and gets them back.
- On horseback with the spear, Flame mows down a group of lawmen. Mane came back because when money calls, you come.

### The vault

- Mining fatigue stops them breaking into the vault, so they wait on a hill for LAW. Flame uses crossbow [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and Mane spears through the army; the last lawmen, with elytra and spears, are hardest, but they massacre them all, and Mane loots the vault.
- Mane probably won't return to the main server: he likes not having to worry about anyone. Before Flame leaves, Mane says that no matter how harsh he's been, Flame will always be his little bro, and thanks him for finding him. Once Flame has learned everything, he could take down LAW alone.
