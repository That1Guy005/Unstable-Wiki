## Summary

The second round of the election starts with an assassination: leading candidate Naparizel is killed. [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) declares a 24-hour "purge" where candidates may steal each other's vote books. [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) refuses to fight, saves [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) from [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md) and later from Purpled, and outwits [Arachn1d](../../characters/arachn1d.md) with [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md). Horace and Fymada hand him their votes and endorse him, and he reaches the final four.

## Full breakdown

### Napa

- Parrot trails Lettuce by 7,000 votes. At a celebration in the banquet hall, netherite assassins storm the castle. Parrot throws invisibility to help Naparizel escape, but Napa stays, and dying, says he knows Parrot will be a good king.
- At an emergency meeting, Parrot points out that living in the Northern Council was supposed to keep them safe. Lettuce ignores him: the rules stand, only the top four go on.
- Napa left instructions in case he died: each remaining candidate gets a signed book worth 120 votes, and whoever holds the books in 24 hours gets the votes. For those 24 hours, all crime between candidates is allowed. Parrot argues it should be a civil election, and at the lectern urges everyone not to resort to violence, until a candidate pots up and attacks him.

### Purpled

- Parrot hides invisibly, then finds Theo missing from their room. Purpled demands his books and sends guards up; Parrot sets an automatic arrow dispenser down the ladder.
- Theo arrives and tells him to run. From Theo's view: Parrot saved Purpled's life in the Far Lands, and Theo should have killed him then. Theo's [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) kill both guards in seconds, and Purpled runs.
- In [Merchant City](../../places/merchant-city.md), Parrot buys different arrows, since he's best with a bow.

### Books

- Boosfer has unarmoured Horace in a trap for his book. Parrot calls it out and gives Boosfer his own book, and Horace is freed.
- Following Boosfer invisibly to [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), they watch him demand Fymada's book; she says someone already took it, and points to Arachn1d behind a tree. Arachn1d attacks Boosfer, who drops his books one by one, and Parrot pearls in and grabs the last.
- Parrot, Horace and Fymada team up. They follow Arachn1d thousands of blocks out to a camp; he enters a cave and doesn't return. Horace asks to see Boosfer's book and is shocked when Parrot hands it over.
- Meanwhile Theo is fooled by "PearitX2", wearing Parrot's skin with a different voice ("a voice changer", he says), and is extorted for golden carrots.

### Arachn1d's cave

- Arachn1d re-emerges with re-enchanted netherite and heads into another cave. Horace goes down with a goat horn and blows it when attacked; Parrot gives Fymada Boosfer's book and jumps in.
- Arachn1d threatens to set off a TNT trap unless they hand over their books; Parrot realises he re-enchanted his armour for Blast Protection. Since killing Parrot would lose Boosfer's 120 votes, Arachn1d holds them in the cave for the rest of the purge, playing loud Clash of Clans while they play tic-tac-toe.
- Renamed arrows keep their names, so Parrot shoots messages up to Fymada: brew Turtle Master and come down. Arachn1d takes her hostage too, thinking she stumbled in. She splashes them all with Turtle Master and Parrot sets off the TNT himself. Outnumbered, Arachn1d drops all his books.

### Horace's rescue

- Riding the rails back, Horace falls into a sand trap built by Purpled and Arachn1d. Purpled betrays Arachn1d and takes Horace hostage, giving them four hours to bring the books to a nearby village. Parrot lies that Boosfer took them. "We're going to have to rescue him."
- At the viking village Purpled is AFK. Parrot lures a guard into the forest with an arrow and a trail of signs, traps him, and makes him write where Horace is: the house with a furnace in front.
- Horace is caged above lava. Purpled appears and asks if they've brought the books; Parrot says they're there for Horace, and doubts Purpled would free him anyway. Parrot shoots the target that drops Horace, then shoots him with a Fire Resistance arrow, and loses Purpled in the forest.

### Endorsements

- Horace and Fymada talk privately, then ask Parrot for the books, and he hands them over. He says Lettuce is the enemy, not them. It was a test: Parrot trusted the people beside him and never asked for the books back.
- Horace gives Parrot all the books and his own votes, endorses him, and promises his civilization's votes; Fymada endorses him too. Parrot won't promise to fulfil their wishes, but will give them positions to help run the server, since he can't do it alone. Horace says the server needs him.
- In his room, Theo is talking to PearitX2, who runs when Parrot arrives.

### Loose ends

- Lettuce admits to Deputy Ace that he hired the assassins to kill Napa. The deputy can't believe he killed a leading candidate and his own lawmen, and says he can't keep supporting him. Lettuce thought Parrot wouldn't reach the top four without the purge, and has one loose end left to tie.
- The final four: Lettuce, Purpled, Parrot and Boosfer. Theo thinks it may be over; Parrot says there's one more way to win, and they need to find someone. "Mechanical God" by AZALI plays, hinting at Wemmbu.

## Items & mechanics

- Vote books: Napa's 120-vote signed books, the prize of the purge.
- Renamed arrows: Parrot's way of sending messages up out of the cave.
- Turtle Master: protection from Arachn1d's TNT trap.
- Fire Resistance arrow: saves Horace from the lava.
- Arrow dispenser: Parrot's ladder defence against Purpled.

## Outcomes

- Naparizel is assassinated, on Lettuce's orders.
- Horace and Fymada endorse Parrot, who reaches the final four (see [The Election for King](../../events/the-election-for-king.md)).
- Deputy Ace turns against Lettuce.
