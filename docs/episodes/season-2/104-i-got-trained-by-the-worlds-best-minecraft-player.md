## Summary

At the old BAT base, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) finds the retired former world number one, [ItzRealMe](../../characters/itzrealme.md), who agrees to train him. Itz grades Flame on every weapon, teaches advanced [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md) techniques, and introduces "the zone", a state of total focus. Flame uses it to wipe out a LAW patrol, then passes Itz's final test without his mace.

## Full breakdown

### The assessment

- With two combat techniques left to master, Flame meets Itz at the infinity base. Itz assumes Flame has come to kill him; he's avoiding conflict these days, past his prime, and hunted by LAW too, on the verge of turning himself in.
- Flame says he's on his way to destroy LAW and needs Itz's training. Itz has heard of him and agrees.
- On the roof Itz explains: every player has strengths and weaknesses; weak players can't execute strategies, while combat masters can execute all of them. A master is strong because of the player, not the weapon; an average player's skill disappears with their weapon.
- Flame must pop a totem with every weapon or the training ends. He pops Itz with sword (breaking his helmet), spear and cart easily, and struggles with mace, his weakest, but gets it.
- Verdict: sword best, spear good, cart needs work, mace weakest. Itz recommends sword and cart as primaries with spear as backup. Flame wants to improve his mace.

### Mace school

- Itz teaches the basics and attribute swapping while attacking and falling; Flame gets it first try, then stun slamming first try too.
- Advanced techniques: force grappling (launching the enemy into the air, pearling onto them and macing them), and the hardest, horizontal pearl catching (angling a pearl, looking down and throwing a wind charge right after, which puts you unexpectedly above the opponent for a mace hit).
- Before Itz finishes explaining wind-charge bhopping, Flame demonstrates it: a combat master always finds new strategies, Itz says.

### The zone

- Even knowing everything, Itz says there's one gatekept thing all combat masters do: not mechanical, but mental, "the zone". Total focus: faster reactions, spotting mistakes, learning on your own, seeing patterns in your habits and your opponent's.
- Flame says there's always been someone distracting him in fights, so he hasn't noticed using the wrong items. Itz says to use his hatred as fuel, stay quiet to hear more, and shut out every unnecessary thought; only those who practise earn the right to enter it. They meditate on the roof at sunset.
- A LAW horn interrupts. Itz goes invisible and watches from above. Asked what he has to say for his crimes, Flame says nothing, pots up, and with his mace, visibly in the zone, kills the diamond lawmen; the two netherite players retreat.

### The final test

- Walking on, Itz says he used to be hired to kill people and was unstoppable. Flame says LAW's hunting and humiliation drove him; Itz was the same, always learning, until he won every fight, got bored and retired.
- On a huge floating island Itz found exploring, Itz lends him an elytra to get up, then asks for his mace. It's Lomedy's, but Flame hands it over. The test: first to pop three totems.
- Itz pops two of Flame's before Flame lands one. Flame hides in a cave, restocks, remembers the zone, realises it's a test of adapting, and finds slow-falling arrows in a pot. He comes up claiming he gives up; as Itz dives with the mace, Flame hits him with slow falling.
- Itz plays by his rules and goes melee only. Flame goes quiet, enters the zone, and pops all three of Itz's totems, admitting he only had four arrows and was bluffing.
- Itz took the mace because it's hard to lock in when angry, and Flame showed he controls when he enters the zone. He returns the mace: Flame is on his way to combat master, and only needs to focus on carts to finish off LAW and BAT.
- Flame heads back to the main server for his last trainer.

## Items & mechanics

- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): attribute swapping, stun slams, force grappling, horizontal pearl catching and wind-charge bhopping.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): the key to horizontal pearl catching and bhopping.
- [Spear](../../mechanics/spear.md), [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) and sword: graded in Itz's assessment.
- Slow-falling arrows: Flame's counter in the final test.
- The zone: Itz's mental technique.

## Outcomes

- Flame masters the mace and learns the zone (Training Arc).
- A LAW patrol is wiped out.
- Flame passes Itz's test; one trainer remains.
