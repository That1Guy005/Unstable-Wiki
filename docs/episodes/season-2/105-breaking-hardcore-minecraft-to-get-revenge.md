## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) uses a new exploit that lets him break blocks and trigger redstone from far away. After razing a village with it, he, [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) hunt [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md) in [The Amplified](../../places/the-amplified.md), but someone smarter than a normal spy is stalking them. It's Leo with LAW soldiers, and Spoke's exploit-rigged sky base wins the battle, until Spoke's secret stasis is pulled.

## Full breakdown

### The village

- Spoke has learned to break Minecraft's laws and physics, and swears to end Leo's time on the server.
- At a village, Zam poses as a trader while Spoke and Mapicc tunnel under every house and wire them to a redstone machine rigged to [TNT minecarts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md). From a platform, Spoke tells the villagers they'll pay for not handing over resources, and uses a glitch to break the obsidian under it from afar, triggering the machine. The village is destroyed and Spoke kills its residents.

### The stalker

- The Amplified has changed: its jungle is now snow, its bases abandoned. Spoke remembers when he and Leo were friends.
- In [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md)'s vault, freshly dropped blocks show they've just missed Leo. Someone is brewing nearby; they tear the vault apart searching and find water flowing up the entrance that wasn't there before. Whoever is stalking them is smarter than an average spy.
- Back at their castle, Mapicc accidentally blows up his trap while building it. Spoke designs an invisible trap using floating waterlogged water that only triggers when a block is placed on it or string touches it. Expecting the stalker to see through the others' traps, he plans a backup.
- Through Spoke's secret Nether tunnel and a portal into a cave, all three set secret [stases](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md) they can pull with a fishing rod.
- Returning, they find their traps set off and destroyed, redstone torches lighting up the area, and an arrow on a tower. Spoke still hasn't glimpsed the stalker, but senses Leo. They build a sky base at height limit a hundred blocks away, and Spoke shows them how the remote block-breaking exploit works.

### Leo and LAW

- Hours later, beacons of light approach and vanish. Spoke sets another stasis and investigates: at an abandoned base, Leo is working with LAW soldiers. Now Spoke has the upper hand.
- He kills a soldier heading for the sky base, then goes fully invisible, only arming to jump stragglers. Leo keeps the lawmen grouped; Spoke's carts and a TNT ambush in a cave don't kill them. Trapped in cobwebs, he blocks himself in, and is stasised back to the base when Zam accidentally blows up part of it practising carts.

### The siege

- Leo and the lawmen bridge up towards the base, with more coming from below. Spoke sets another stasis to go on a rampage and pull himself back. He pearls onto Leo's bridge, blows it up, and returns; Zam carts the lower bridge.
- They still get in, and it's hopelessly outnumbered. Spoke sets up the exploit one last time while Zam bows lawmen off. The three escape across a sand bridge, and Spoke sets off the TNT, the bridge collapsing behind them. They run off laughing.
- Invisible, they go back for Leo. He fights, then pulls his stasis when his totem pops. They use an exploit to calculate the stasis's coordinates from his arrows on the ground, and head there.
- Spoke's own stasis, the secret one, is pulled.

## Items & mechanics

- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): secret stases with fishing-rod triggers, Leo's escape, and the final pull.
- Floating waterlogged trap: Spoke's invisible trap design.

## Outcomes

- A village and its residents are destroyed by Spoke.
- Leo's LAW force is blown up in the sky base; Leo escapes by stasis.
- Someone pulls Spoke's secret stasis.
