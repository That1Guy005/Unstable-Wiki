## Summary

To expose [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) at the final debate, [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md) travels to [The Great Sea](../../places/the-great-sea.md) and persuades the escaped [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) to come back with him. Through dolphin tunnels, a smuggler's nautilus route and [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md)'s ambush, with [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) undercover in LAW and [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) killing a LAW commander, they burst into the debate. Lettuce attacks Wemmbu on stage, suspends the election, and reveals he's holding Theo hostage.

## Full breakdown

### The plan

- Lettuce has held a huge lead since the start, but Parrot is one of the few who know Wemmbu escaped LAW custody and fled to the Great Sea. Lettuce announces a final-four debate in a day; Parrot plans to bring Wemmbu to it to prove LAW's incompetence. If Lettuce finds out, his lawmen will go after Wemmbu.
- After sixteen hours of travel, [Pirate City](../../places/pirate-city.md) is abandoned, but Wemmbu and Egg are there. Wemmbu, busy being hunted, knows nothing about the election. Parrot argues that if Lettuce wins, Wemmbu can never return. Wemmbu punches him off the edge to talk to Egg, then agrees, leaving Egg behind for safety.

### Getting back

- The fastest route is the dolphin tunnel to [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md), heavily guarded. Wemmbu isn't geared to fight, so Parrot runs onto the island pretending to rat Wemmbu out; the lawmen dive into the ocean, and the netherite guard who stays is told he'd be promoted for capturing Wemmbu, so he leaves too. Parrot and an invisible Wemmbu reach the tunnel.
- Lettuce suspects Parrot is bringing Wemmbu back and orders his lawmen to stop them, killing them if necessary. When lawmen come down the tunnel the other way, the pair dig out and boat the rest on the surface.
- Parrot's plan was a loot-laden [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md) in a cherry biome to fly over LAW territory, but his sculk detector chest has gone off over 105 times, and his spyglass shows lawmen behind the trees. At a village Wemmbu walks out in plain sight, alerting guards, and they escape down a mineshaft.
- Lettuce sends his four strongest guards after them. When [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) questions it, Lettuce asks if he's defending them, and Ace apologises.

### The Smuggler's Pass

- At a special river is a nautilus shop owned by CoinMonke, a notorious smuggler, who doesn't trust Wemmbu but is persuaded that Parrot will lose otherwise. His route: down the Kings River, north-west through the Smuggler's Pass, past LAW's graveyard to the professional smugglers. He lends them two nautiluses.
- They sneak through a checkpoint, then three lawmen spot them. On a hill Wemmbu maces one and loots him. Parrot leads a saddled pig, wind-charges the water and bows off climbers, and when Wemmbu won't give up his elytra, Parrot rides the pig while teaching Wemmbu to fly endlessly without rockets, until the elytra breaks, far enough away.
- At a huge mountain build along the pass, they swap armour, and Parrot spots invisibility particles: SargeLAW, with many more lawmen. Parrot bluffs a TNT cart trap beneath them; Wemmbu points out Lettuce wants him alive, and stalls on signs while Parrot plans.
- Parrot spots Egg among the lawmen and shoots him a renamed arrow asking for armour, which goes to Wemmbu through a chest. Wemmbu switches to a LAW shield, Parrot splashes invisibility among the lawmen so Wemmbu blends in, and SargeLAW starts killing his own men. Egg says he infiltrated LAW simply because he wanted to.

### The reckoning

- At the end of the pass, Wemmbu has Egg box Parrot in: he's going to kill him. Everything traces back to Parrot revealing his identity: without that, Lettuce would never have run for king.
- Parrot apologises. After Wemmbu knocked him into the void, Wemmbu disappeared until Lettuce paraded him; Parrot meant to apologise at Pirate City. He revealed Wemmbu for a tomb of items he wanted to give to players in need, and realised it was wrong. Killing him changes nothing; Lettuce will keep hunting Wemmbu. Egg agrees Wemmbu's real enemy is Lettuce, and Wemmbu lets him go.

### The graveyard

- A player pretending to mourn at LAW's graveyard offers to guide them, but flashes a LAW shield. Asked the name of the nautilus shop's owner, he sets off a [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md), blowing up himself and the graveyard.
- The_Oroboros arrives to investigate, and finds Theo standing above him. Theo's carts slowly drain his armour and kill him, and Theo marks his tomb "loser".

### The debate

- Parrot hasn't arrived. Lettuce moderates himself: he liberated the Great Sea and captured the pirates' leader, [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md). Purpled says he fought back in the purge and wants to save people. [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md) says nothing.
- Lettuce announces Wemmbu died in an explosion escaping custody. Purpled doubts his sources, and the doors burst open: Parrot brings Wemmbu on stage. Lettuce asks if Parrot broke a criminal out; Parrot says Wemmbu has been living in the Great Sea all along.
- Parrot says Lettuce sent a thousand players to die just to catch Wemmbu and Flame, that Wemmbu isn't a bad person, and that LAW tried to blow them up. When Parrot asks Wemmbu for his side, Lettuce pots strength and tries to mace him, and Wemmbu stasises away.
- Parrot tells the server Lettuce never cared about those thousand players, only about becoming king, and will lie and blackmail to get what he wants.
- Lettuce declares himself the server's interim leader and suspends the election until it's safe. His commanders have captured Theo: if Parrot wants him back, he must endorse Lettuce and go to jail. Parrot says Lettuce should have kept that between the two of them.

## Items & mechanics

- Dolphin tunnel: the route from the Great Sea to Capital City.
- Nautiluses: CoinMonke's smuggling boats.
- Pig and lead: Parrot's trick for endless elytra flight without rockets.
- Renamed arrows: Parrot's messages to Egg.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): the fake smuggler's suicide trap, and Theo's weapon against The_Oroboros.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lettuce's attack on Wemmbu on stage.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu's escape is proven publicly at the debate (see [Parrot Recruits Wemmbu](../../events/parrot-recruits-wemmbu.md)).
- The_Oroboros is killed by Theo, who is then captured by LAW.
- Lettuce suspends the election and demands Parrot's surrender.
