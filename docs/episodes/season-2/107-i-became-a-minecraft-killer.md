## Summary

The same journey from [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s side. He agrees to [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md)'s plan intending to kill him, with [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) secretly shadowing them inside LAW. On the way, Parrot's apology changes his mind. After the debate, Wemmbu stasises to where Egg and [4CVIT](../../characters/4cvit.md) wait with a pearl cannon to the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md).

## Full breakdown

### A plan of his own

- Wemmbu wants to ban Parrot, who revealed his identity and set everything in motion. He knocks Parrot into the water to talk to Egg, who thinks the offer is good. Wemmbu thinks Lettuce will be king either way and wants Parrot dead, but not yet, with so few resources. He gives Egg his trident and tells him to follow secretly.
- At the dolphin tunnel island, Parrot gives Wemmbu invisibility and clears the lawmen off. There aren't enough dolphins, so Wemmbu wrestles one in from the sea, struggling to get it across the fence, until Parrot says there are spares in the tunnel. The guards return and watch them go.
- Nametags approach in the tunnel, so they dig out. Parrot's movement detector chest has gone off over a hundred times: LAW is staking out his ghast. At a village Parrot tells Wemmbu to crouch, too late; one lawman's hit takes half his health, and they run.

### Egg undercover

- Near the river, Egg whispers that he's following as planned and joined LAW to blend in. Asked why Parrot's still alive, Wemmbu says Parrot may have a contingency plan.
- CoinMonke doesn't trust Wemmbu but helps. On the river, lawmen force them ashore; Wemmbu kills one but can't grab the loot. With the pig trick, Parrot teaches him rocketless flight until his elytra breaks.
- Wemmbu asks how LAW knew where they were; Parrot says they're covering most of their territory. At the man-made mountain, Wemmbu mines ice while Egg catches up; asked if he still means to kill Parrot, Wemmbu says he doesn't really care.

### SargeLAW

- Wemmbu notices Parrot keeps stopping and veering off. Parrot takes the armour and calls out the invisible follower: SargeLAW and his lawmen. Wemmbu taunts Sarge that he can't kill him, even in three-quarters diamond, and they fight.
- Parrot gets armour from Egg in the crowd, splashes invisibility among the lawmen, and Wemmbu switches to a LAW shield to blend in. SargeLAW starts killing his own men.

### The apology

- At the end of the pass, Wemmbu kills four lawmen with Egg's strength potions, then tells Parrot he's going to kill him. Parrot laughs, then realises he means it.
- Wemmbu keeps pressing: LAW hunts him because Parrot revealed his identity. Parrot apologises and says he'd meant to apologise at Pirate City. He sounds close to tears, and Wemmbu spares him.
- Wemmbu says he'd have killed Parrot at full strength. Parrot asks what he's missing, and offers to help get it.

### The debate, and away

- They run across the Northern Council castle into the debate, and Parrot shows the server LAW no longer holds Wemmbu. When Lettuce attacks, Wemmbu stasises out.
- Egg is waiting with 4CVIT at an ender [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md); 4C says he's only helping because Parrot asked. Wemmbu throws a pearl in, heading for the Far Lands.

## Items & mechanics

- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): given to Egg to follow them.
- Dolphins: needed to power the tunnel to Capital City.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): Wemmbu's, which breaks on the pig-led flight.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Wemmbu's exit from the debate stage.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): 4CVIT's route to the Far Lands.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu spares Parrot and helps expose Lettuce (see [Parrot Recruits Wemmbu](../../events/parrot-recruits-wemmbu.md)).
- Egg is revealed to have joined LAW undercover.
- Wemmbu heads to the Far Lands to restore his strength.
