## Summary

The pearl cannon drops [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) on the wrong side of the [Far Lands](../../places/far-lands.md), at [JamatoP](../../characters/jamatop.md)'s house. Jamato guides them through an ancient Nether shortcut and the [Soul Keepers](../../factions/soul-keepers.md) to Serpentbound's kingdom, until Wemmbu reveals he never meant to meet them peacefully. After fights with Jamato and Nitenly, Wemmbu reloads his orbital strikes and heads for the End.

## Full breakdown

### Jamato

- They land twelve million blocks out on the negative X axis, rather than the positive Z, near Jamato's house and the grave made for him, and decide to rob it. Jamato appears at the door.
- Wemmbu says they're there to meet the original players who live out there. Jamato is surprised anyone knows about them, and has Wemmbu press F3 to see they're on the opposite side of the world. They spent their last pearls getting here, so Jamato offers another route.
- Towering up the Far Lands, Wemmbu asks Jamato for blocks and knocks him down while he's distracted. Wemmbu tells Egg the problem: he promised Serpentbound he'd never return, so they'll betray Jamato once they arrive.

### The old portals

- Nether portals used to work at very specific coordinates, allowing fast travel. After 10,000 blocks they reach a mushroom forest with a portal inside a giant tree. Jamato says to stay on the path or set off traps, and not to take an off-path bridge.
- Among the monuments, Wemmbu notices one resembling the Underworld; Jamato says he isn't supposed to know about that place, and it's a bad place. Wemmbu and Egg step on the grass, and the defences summon withers. With his elytra broken, Wemmbu bows them down, and Jamato lets him kill the last for XP to mend it.
- Jamato says to let him do the talking. Wemmbu warns Egg the people on the other side may be the Soul Keepers he killed before.
- Stuck inside the portal and unable to break blocks, they're freed by Soulkeeper67 breaking the glass. Jamato says they're heading to Serpentbound's kingdom, and he leads them to another portal.

### The betrayal

- In the decayed Far Lands, Jamato says they need to bridge a few thousand blocks more. Wemmbu says this is where they part ways. Jamato realises Wemmbu was never friends with the Far Lands people, calls him just like "the rest of them", and asks how he really knows Serpentbound. Wemmbu says nothing.
- Jamato attacks. Wemmbu pops him several times, and Jamato teleports away instantly.

### Nitenly and Serpentbound

- They bridge the other way so the Far Lands people won't know they're back. Nitenly comes fast with swift sneak, firing crossbow fireworks. From a desert temple, Wemmbu lands two mace hits in a row and keeps macing him to stop him landing, then breaks a cobblestone block beneath him; Nitenly leaps into the void and stasises out. Wemmbu loots the temple.
- Firestar appears, but it's Serpentbound on Firestar's account, telling them to leave. Wemmbu reminds him of their last fight, and he stasises away.
- They load up on [orbital strikes](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md), and Egg gives Wemmbu a fishing rod that will stasis him back to the cannons whenever he needs more ([Fishing Rod Trigger](../../mechanics/fishing-rod-trigger.md)).
- At a stronghold, Wemmbu remembers LAW has taken over the End. He stops Egg before the last eye goes in, readies a few stab shots for any lawmen, and jumps through the End portal.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): reloaded at the end of the Far Lands.
- [Fishing Rod Trigger](../../mechanics/fishing-rod-trigger.md): Egg's rod linked to a stasis back to the cannons.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Wemmbu's repeated hits that keep Nitenly in the air.
- Ancient Nether portals: fast travel at precise coordinates.
- Withers: the mushroom forest's defences.

## Outcomes

- Wemmbu betrays Jamato, who escapes.
- Nitenly and Serpentbound are driven off.
- Wemmbu has his orbital strikes back.
