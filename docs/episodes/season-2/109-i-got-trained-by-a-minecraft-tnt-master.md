## Summary

For his last technique, [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) sneaks into [Capital City](../../places/capital-city.md) to find [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md), the master of [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) PvP. Theo agrees to train him over helping Parrot. Flame proves a natural, restocks at the black market, loses a first duel to LAW commander [TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md), then masters crossbow and pearl carting and beats him in a rematch, declaring himself a combat master.

## Full breakdown

### Finding Theo

- Flame explains the five main weapons people think of: sword, bow, axe, spear and mace. Very few players can do TNT minecart PvP. Demonstrating on an AFK netherite lawman, he explains the difficulties: shields and blocks negate damage, blast protection, missed shots, even hitting yourself. He wants to master it so he stops blowing himself up.
- Still exiled, he goes into Capital City invisibly. LAW is recruiting and campaigning for Lettuce. The potion shop is empty, but passers-by say Parrot now lives at the Northern Council.
- On an invisible horse on the obsidian highway, a player in iron pretending to be a lawman blocks the road and boasts TheG0dofWar would kill Flame. Flame punches him off and he falls to his death.
- At the Northern Council, his spyglass finds Theo's and Parrot's nametags. He throws a wind charge at their window; Parrot shoots back a renamed arrow telling him to follow them somewhere safer. He drinks milk to show himself.
- Parrot needs to find Wemmbu in the Great Sea, and Theo is torn. Parrot says Flame was exiled for the thousand dead lawmen; Theo points out Parrot killed a lawman too. Flame says LAW won't stop hunting him. When Flame calls himself and Wemmbu the two strongest, Theo clears his throat, and chooses the Great Sea. Flame argues that without training he can't take down LAW. Parrot thinks Flame's intentions aren't bad and leaves it to Theo, who agrees: more interesting than walking for hours.

### A natural

- Flame explains LAW counters his sword with weakness. Theo can't be seen with an exile, so they go to a remote man-made forest where he once trained students, the ones Flame killed.
- Theo explains carting's potential. Flame lands one cart first try, then two in a row, using terrain and aiming at the opponent's feet, where the blast does most damage. Nobody else on the server has done that, Theo says. He admits he'd have ditched Flame if he missed, and that he has no carts prepared.
- On the way to Capital City's black market, a lawman stops the invisible Flame. Theo claims it's Parrot, accidentally splashed; Flame does his best Parrot impression, but the lawman wants him visible. Theo hints, and Flame carts and swords him to death.

### The black market

- The traders remember Flame robbing them and block him; he threatens to kill everyone and they back off. A stack of golden apples buys two shulkers of carts, and a shulker of netherite gear from dead lawmen buys a trader's whole XP stock.
- A LAW horn: TheG0dofWar arrives and everyone hides. A player admits they're evading taxes; the God of War says taxes in Capital City don't matter any more, since they don't affect Lettuce. The traders rat Flame out.
- The God of War insists on a duel. Flame is boxed in and crit down, pearls to the exit, and Theo splashes them all invisible to escape. Flame says he had no speed or shield.

### Mastery

- Theo teaches shooting carts with a crossbow (arrow speed sets the damage), the best cart placements, especially ledges, and on flat ground, placing a block at your feet and lighting the cart on top. Flame adds standing on a block to bow from above.
- Next, pearl carting: charging a bow while the pearl is in the air. Flame gets it. Theo says he's already among the best cart PvPers on the server; he just needs consistency. Theo suggests a rematch with the God of War.
- Flame meditates on a lava cast at Spawn to enter the zone. A lawman, theQuad_, tells him to leave; Flame makes him lead the way to the God of War, meditating in a desert colosseum, and makes theQuad_ scream to break his meditation.
- Melee first, then down a waterfall into carts. Flame pops several totems, restocks outside the God of War's cave, pops more, and keeps carting while lawmen gather to watch. The God of War vows to fight to his last breath.
- Flame doesn't want to kill him, only to prove he could beat the "God of War". He calls him a worthy opponent and tells him to come back for a rematch when he's strong enough.
- Having mastered every weapon, Flame considers himself a combat master, to be proven by taking down LAW, showing his skill is his and not his weapons'. His first step is already decided.
