## Summary

[SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) dares the whole server to come and get him, and turns a ring of biomes into a giant trap field for [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md)'s thousand-strong army. The stalker turns out to be [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md), who pulled his secret stasis and wants his help to kill Leo. Spoke distrusts her at every step, until Leo's railguns blow up his base and Jumper helps him save [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md). They leave as allies.

## Full breakdown

### The declaration

- Spoke's book challenges the whole server to a duel, with his and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md)'s and Zam's coordinates on a remote mountain. Zam and Mapicc think it's madness. Spoke compares it to Wemmbu and Flame's 1v1000, but worse, because Leo commands this army.
- His secret command base has maps of every surrounding biome, each rigged with hundreds of traps, and a sculk sensor that shows which direction attackers come from. When it fires, Spoke goes alone with a stasis and keeps the others inside, since someone pulled his secret stasis last time.

### Jumper

- In the dark oak forest, whose short-range traps blend into the terrain, Spoke goes invisible, knocks a lone lawman into a fall trap, then dives into the army and fires the [carts](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md) hidden under the mushrooms. The lawmen burn down the forest to disable his traps, and the scouts retreat.
- Chasing an invisible scout, Spoke falls into an 8×8 fall trap and clutches halfway down. The scout is no ordinary lawman.
- Flashback: when his stasis was pulled, three invisible players were waiting in this base, and the trimmed one drank milk: Jumper. She'd disabled his traps, built the beacons that led him to Leo, and fought Leo herself. She'd followed them through the Nether and seen them set the stasis. They share one goal, killing Leo, for different reasons: she hates that he uses spies as soldiers instead of spies. Spoke said he'd manage alone and threw her out of his new base.
- In the present, Jumper says he doesn't know half the players hunting him and won't survive without her. "Is that so?" Spoke asks, and fishing-rods a TNT cart into her and her spies. He chorus-fruits down into a cave, digs to bedrock, and Zam pulls his stasis.
- Zam and Mapicc have found a sealed room with an enchanting setup and chests of loot: Jumper built this base, and wants to help. Spoke can't take the risk, and keeps the others inside, remembering Jumper still holds their stases somewhere.

### The birch forest

- A book "from Spoke" brings Zam and Mapicc to the birch forest, rigged with landmines under the trees; Jumper wrote it. Zam sets off a landmine. Lawmen chase them; a spleef trap gets kills, and the landmines thin them further.
- At a chunk mined down to the void, the three drop onto beds placed on one side to cushion the fall, while the pursuing lawmen fall to their deaths. Having his friends there worked out, Spoke admits, and no lawman survived to report back.
- Spoke tells Zam vaguely that the stalker is still watching. They cobweb a straggler, who only knows "LettuceK", not Leo, until Spoke claims to hold his friends hostage: Leo is building something in another snow biome. Spoke kills him.

### The rose field

- Outside Leo's small snow base, Spoke taunts him to finish them off while they're weak. Leo pearls in, and the lawmen chase all three over the roses, where buried TNT carts kill almost everyone. A second wave from inside dies to the remaining landmines.
- Leo targets Zam, the only one in diamond: a cart pops her totem and he webs her. Jumper appears invisibly, splashes Zam with Turtle Master, and wind-charges a dandelion, releasing ten charged creepers that pop Leo's totem.
- Leo calls her stupid for leaving the election and now trying to kill him; BAT hasn't changed, he says, he's just going after a bounty. Jumper says he's corrupt, misusing her spies, and turning into Lettuce. Spoke refuses to work with her, citing her piston trap, the stalking, and their stases.
- A four-way fight breaks out until Leo's armour breaks and he flies off on an elytra. Spoke's elytra is in his ender chest, and Jumper doesn't chase: she's fought Leo before and nearly died.
- Spoke asks what makes her different from Leo, with traps set in case he betrays her and their stases in her hands. Leo has sent thousands to die, she says, and she's a better person than Spoke. She chose him because they share the motivation, and the server needs to break the Lettuce–Leo alliance, starting with Leo. She leaves.
- Zam thinks she's right; Mapicc points out she holds their lives. Spoke says Jumper isn't one-dimensional: he's seen her genuine and lying. He admits he kept the stasis secret because once he knew she was the stalker, anyone near him could be compromised.

### The railguns

- In the desert biome, his most intricate traps, they stand on the corners of a sandstone block above a huge sand trap and wait for Jumper. Lawmen arrive instead; low on durability, Spoke springs it early and kills most of them.
- Jumper pulls Spoke's stasis. She's had many chances to kill them, he points out. She admits she didn't especially want to work with him, but he's the person closest to being able to kill Leo. Skilled spies used to be the tradition, until Leo sent them all into battle, and if Leo and Lettuce conquer the server, imagine what else gets taken. He sets another stasis and follows.
- Behind a map is a black space with a chain bridge into the void. With her rockets they fly under bedrock to another hole, into a cave where they hear Leo ask if the machine is ready: a railgun ([Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md)) aimed at Spoke's base, one of four.
- Leo fires; it blasts through every side of the bunker, and Zam and Mapicc pull Spoke's stasis. The lawmen will come from all sides, never from the surface.
- Jumper says it's their chance to kill Leo; Zam would rather run. The redstone materials survived, so they build a mini railgun that blows up hundreds of lawmen from each direction, then tower out. Zam is trapped below; Jumper still has Spoke's pearl, so they set it off and save her.
- Spoke never expected his declaration of war to end beside someone he considered an enemy, but he can only kill Leo with her help. Flying off on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md), Jumper says they need recruits. Spoke has a certain mace wielder in mind.

## Items & mechanics

- Trap biomes: dark oak fall traps and mushroom carts, birch landmines and a spleef trap, the bed-cushioned void drop, the rose-field carts and the desert sand trap.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): the secret stasis Jumper pulled, and the stases she still holds.
- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): Leo's four railguns, and Spoke's mini railgun.
- Charged creepers: Jumper's dandelion trap that pops Leo's totem.
- [TNT Minecart](../../mechanics/tnt-minecart.md): buried carts, and Leo's cart on Zam.
- [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md): their escape.

## Outcomes

- Hundreds of Leo's lawmen are killed in Spoke's traps.
- Spoke's command base is destroyed by railgun fire.
- Spoke and Jumper ally against Leo, and set out to recruit Wemmbu.
