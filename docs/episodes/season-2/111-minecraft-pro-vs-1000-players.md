## Summary

With his orbital cannons back, [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) arrives in an End turned into a [LAW](../../factions/law.md) base, where the cannons don't work. With [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) he escapes to the outer islands, convinces [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) that Lettuce lied to him, raids Baablu's hidden stash, and fights back to Spawn. Wemmbu wipes out the army at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md), but [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md) holds a fishing rod linked to [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md)'s life.

## Full breakdown

### The LAW End

- The End has been rebuilt as a LAW base with lawmen on every side. Wemmbu kills a few for their gear as dozens more break through the ancient debris wall. He pulls his orbital fishing rod, and realises the cannons only work in the Overworld.
- Egg has built a bridge; Wemmbu breaks it behind them. Below is an ice bridge: Wemmbu breaks the ice, the whole bridge turns to water, and Egg riptides across the gap.

### Minute

- On the outer islands they kill stragglers for armour. Minute may have left the End. Egg recalls Baablu's backup stashes, hidden after his failed attempt with the portal, and wrote one down: 10k, 10k.
- At a glowing house where the islands run out, they find Minute; this is his temporary home, not the stash. He gave up the End because Lettuce said he'd bring peace to the Overworld, though Minute never saw it.
- Wemmbu tells him Lettuce has been killing candidates and rigging the election. Minute isn't fully convinced, but trusts him: they'll go back to the main island together, and if LAW reacts as Wemmbu says, he'll believe it.
- The stash is under a chorus plant at specific coordinates, reachable only by elytra. Counting branches, Wemmbu spots the glowing bottom of the central one. As he breaks the last block, an observer sets off TNT; he mines north, grabs two shulkers of items and elytra, and gets away as the island blows up. They share the stash.

### Back to Spawn

- Minute doesn't want innocent players banned, so the fight must happen away from Spawn, where there are only lawmen.
- Flashback: before Wemmbu left for the Far Lands, Parrot warned him he'd feel unstoppable with orbitals, but Lettuce won by blackmail and manipulation, and Wemmbu would need to be smart.
- At the main island, Wemmbu distracts and kills lawmen while Minute digs a safe tunnel for Egg. TheG0dofWar, said to be incredibly strong, has his totem popped instantly. Egg came early because the signal fired; Minute guards him on the high ground.
- Egg's pearls keep flying the wrong way, so they guide him to the right tower; Wemmbu lends him Minute's elytra, and they follow Minute under the island. The God of War attacks again; Wemmbu kites and loses him, and they all pearl through the portal.
- At Spawn, lawmen are killing unarmoured new players. Minute stops Wemmbu firing orbitals near civilians, and Egg suggests the obsidian highway. Wemmbu's stab shots hit some lawmen, and Egg hides in the wall.
- While they restock, the God of War appears above, gloating. They cobweb him underground; repeated stab shots don't pop a single totem. Wemmbu's leggings and shield break; Minute gives him a new shield and rockets, tells him to go, and not to kill Lettuce.

### The Northern Council

- Hundreds of lawmen await. Orbitals, nuke shots, stab shots and mace hits wipe out the entire army.
- Inside, Lettuce stands alone, "just observing". Wemmbu tells him to give his army better armour next time, if there is a next time.
- Lettuce wanted to see if he could use something: the fishing rod in his hand is linked to Jaden's life. Wemmbu doesn't respond.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Overworld-only, which is why it fails in the End.
- [Fishing Rod Trigger](../../mechanics/fishing-rod-trigger.md): Wemmbu's orbital rod, and Lettuce's rod tied to Jaden's life.
- Baablu's stash: two shulkers and elytra, guarded by an observer TNT trap.
- [Trident](../../mechanics/trident.md): Egg's riptide across the broken ice bridge.
- [Elytra](../../mechanics/elytra.md): needed for the stash, and shared to get Egg across.

## Outcomes

- MinuteTech turns against Lettuce and allies with Wemmbu.
- LAW's army at the Northern Council is wiped out.
- Lettuce holds Jaden_MAN's life over Wemmbu.
