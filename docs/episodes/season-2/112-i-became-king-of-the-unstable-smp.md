## Summary

[LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md), who began as a miner freed by [ParrotX2](../../characters/parrotx2.md), declares himself king and jails Parrot, holding [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) hostage for an endorsement. Parrot refuses on stage, and [Deputy Ace](../../characters/deputy-ace.md) gives his life to save them. From [District 13](../../factions/district-13.md), Parrot raises a resistance that joins [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s alliance, and together they defeat LAW at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md). Lettuce surrenders, and Parrot wins the resumed election.

## Full breakdown

### Lettuce's story

- Many have tried to rule the server; only Lettuce succeeded. He started at the bottom, mining for the corrupt Capital City with no hope of escape, until Parrot freed the whole mining civilization. Lettuce founded LAW to hunt the corrupt, but needed more power: a thousand players sent to die against Flame and Wemmbu, then a rigged election.
- He blackmailed, framed and tried to have Parrot killed, and when the second debate showed he'd lose, he declared himself the server's first king. He has become what he swore to destroy.

### Prison

- On happy ghasts with Ace and eleven lawmen, Parrot calls it a power grab. Lettuce says he's afraid of what happens if he doesn't win; it's nothing personal. He wanted to be like Parrot after Parrot freed him, and hates that Parrot is his opponent.
- At LAW HQ, Lettuce says, almost sadly, that he'd kill Parrot if he resisted. Parrot gets Curse of Binding copper armour (protocol since Wemmbu's escapes) and solitary. To see Theo again, he must admit he was wrong and endorse Lettuce.
- New commander BenjaminTheI guards him. Th3Blurpenstein, brought in next door, says Wemmbu escaped this prison when the lights went out during a riot. Benjamin blocks the bars between them.
- The lights go out; a secret door opens behind Parrot with a shulker of gear. He blows the keycard doors and escapes LAW HQ.

### Theo

- A lawman who does eight hearts per hit spots him. Parrot breaks his own copper armour on a cactus, switches to diamond, and breaks all the lawman's armour. Theo is at the Smuggler's Pass, the lawman says, and he'll report Parrot; Parrot blows him up with a crossbow cart.
- Inside the pass's mountain is a huge castle. Invisible, Parrot climbs a lava flow with fire resistance, punch-bows himself onto a man-made stalactite, bridges to Theo's platform above the lava, clutches with water, and splashes them both invisible, and they escape.
- Theo says Lettuce made him set a stasis, and is teleported away mid-sentence. Lettuce appears: Theo's life is always in his hands. Parrot asks what kind of king does this; one willing to do whatever it takes, Lettuce says.
- Parrot must endorse him at an announcement in two hours or many people will be hurt. Parrot attacks and Lettuce's commanders protect him; Parrot has no choice but to go.

### The announcement

- Lettuce says the election is on hold. [Boosfer](../../characters/boosfer.md) says he was never interested, and asks his supporters to move their votes to Lettuce; Purpled does the same.
- Parrot says Lettuce is still a hypocrite, won't endorse a man who kidnapped his best friend, and tells the crowd not to make Lettuce king.
- It was Parrot's last chance. Lettuce takes him to the mining city where Theo is held by two LAW elite guards. Does Parrot regret helping him back then? No: he saw a kind person, not the one in front of him now.
- Lettuce can't do it himself, leaves the killing to Ace and the guards, and blows a horn from across the island. Parrot tells Theo it was fun playing with him.

### Deputy Ace

- Two wither sounds: Ace has killed the guards. Lettuce maces Parrot, who hides in a mine with Theo. Ace explains Lettuce ignored him too long, and it was Ace who freed Parrot from prison. He hands them his pickaxe, says Parrot would be a great king, and holds Lettuce off.
- Ace tells Lettuce what he's doing is wrong. His last words: "I'd rather die standing than live kneeling." Parrot and Theo dig out and build him a tombstone.

### The resistance

- At District 13, Parrot calls out [Horace Altman](../../characters/horace-altman.md) and [Fymada](../../characters/fymada.md) for following. Players living in the base agree to help: Horace rebuilds, Fymada recruits, Parrot looks for Wemmbu and Theo for Flame. Wemmbu is impossible to find, so Parrot trains the newcomers in PvP.
- Horace knows a LAW stash. Theo coughs unmuted while Parrot mines towards a potion room, and with only four carts (the rest are in his ender chest, but he gave his pickaxe away) they retreat on their ghast. The lawmen build up and water saves them from Parrot's arrows.
- An invisible player with a trident summons withers and blows up many lawmen: Spoke. BAT has had a revolution, [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md)'s spies are on his side, and he's been looking for Parrot to join forces.
- A new wave arrives just as [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md) fires an orbital on the castle, then [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) arrives: Spoke had already recruited them. At Spoke's castle, [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md), Jumper, [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md), [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) and [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) join them, and everyone gears up at District 13.

### The battle

- Parrot and Spoke speak: everyone has been hunted, blackmailed or nearly killed by Lettuce. They'll storm the Northern Council. Lettuce, knowing, has gathered all of LAW.
- Lettuce says the server's problems are standing behind Parrot, everyone he sacrificed will have died for nothing, and he won't let the world take everything from him again. Parrot says it's too late to surrender. They wish each other luck.
- Wemmbu's three orbitals kill hundreds. But Lettuce and his commanders weren't in the fight; when the resistance goes up to them, hundreds of netherite lawmen emerge from hiding.
- Parrot acts as a distraction for the stronger fighters. Theo blows up several lawmen per cart. Lettuce maces Parrot and Wemmbu off the tower as they regear; Parrot climbs the back of the election board to reach his shulkers.
- After an hour, only Lettuce and a few lawmen remain. With just his chestplate left, Lettuce flies off; Wemmbu and Minute report he escaped.

### Surrender

- Lettuce walks back in through the front: kill him if they want; he doesn't want to live on a server that doesn't want him. If the server doesn't want justice, he surrenders.
- Flame wants him dead; a vote ties. Killing him would make them no better, Parrot says. He'll resume the election, and if Lettuce wins, that's the server's choice. Lettuce says he knew he'd lost when Parrot's execution failed, and the server is too far gone. He goes to prison for a long time.
- Wemmbu and [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md) ask if Parrot will be king; Egg won't vote for him, and Wemmbu would vote for Egg. They leave, saying it was fun fighting alongside him.
- Results: Parrot 5,828 votes, Lettuce 3,973. Wearing a crown on his skin, Parrot prepares to step out. Theo is proud of him, but reminds him he's still not the biggest bird.

## Outcomes

- Deputy Ace is killed by Lettuce after freeing Parrot and Theo.
- LAW is defeated at the Northern Council and Lettuce surrenders (see [The War Against LAW](../../events/the-war-against-law.md)).
- Parrot wins the election and becomes king (see [The Election for King](../../events/the-election-for-king.md)).
