## Summary

The same war from [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md)'s side. After his bunker falls, Spoke frees [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md)'s spies and, raiding LAW HQ for Parrot, frees [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) and teams up with [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) and [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md). A TNT-boosted pearl cannon gets him to Parrot first, the alliance beats [LettuceK](../../characters/lettucek.md), and Spoke finally corners [Leow0ok](../../characters/leow0ok.md), whose stasis drops him into the void.

## Full breakdown

### Recruiting

- Their makeshift [arrow cannons](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) in Leo's blown-open tunnels kill part of the invading force, not enough. [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) falls back in and Jumper pulls her stasis. The bunker is overrun.
- Jumper sends them to a prison holding her spies, in a castle in Invisible Mafia style built by neither BAT nor LAW, and splits off. Spoke's invisibility runs out at the walls; [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) re-splashes him. They tunnel in and Spoke frees prisoners one by one.
- Zam and Mapicc blow the floor with carts despite him. The heavily geared guards nearly kill Spoke (Jumper said there'd be no fight), until Jumper arrives with [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) and they slaughter them.
- Minute threatens Spoke for griefing his End base; Spoke apologises, saying he's trying to bring all his friends back. The spies will gather intel on LAW and BAT. Minute returns to the End for his stashes, and Mapicc goes to help, since Minute is wanted there.

### LAW HQ

- Next: Parrot, rumoured to be in the prison Spoke once broke Zam out of, which will be more secure now. Zam doesn't want to go back after four months inside.
- The prison is LAW's headquarters. Spotted by SilentMountain, they run downstairs as Spoke's TNT goes off, seal the path with obsidian, blow a Level 3 door, kill guards, and loot keycards and milk. They blow up the item checker and fight crowds of guards.
- Parrot isn't in the main cells, solitary or the backrooms. Back in the cells, they hear [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md), free Th3Blurpenstein and find [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md), who won't leave (Spoke locks him in a smaller cell), and meet Flame killing guards, then watch his [Orbital Dog Cannon](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md) summon a wolf army.
- SargeLAW targets Zam, who carries the shield of SargeRaxby, Sarge's brother. Spoke breaks ender chests without silk touch for obsidian and walls Sarge out.
- Six guards around solitary means something's hidden. A [wither bored](../../mechanics/wither-bore.md) through the obsidian towards a nametag reveals Jaden, king of the pirates.

### The alliance

- Jaden, Wemmbu and Flame join. At the Amplified headquarters there's friction: Wemmbu and Flame are rivals, Jaden betrayed Flame and stole Lomedy's mace, and Flame once tried to kill Minute. Flame tries to kill Jaden and is stopped.
- A spy's book says armies are converging on one point: probably Parrot. Wemmbu flies off at once, Minute follows to reason with him, and Flame goes on foot.
- To beat an elytra, Spoke, Jumper and Mapicc build a [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md) boosted by TNT minecarts. Spoke arrives instantly, lures guards into cart traps, and sees Parrot and Theo on their ghast. Wemmbu arrives but won't fight until Flame does.
- They load the base's loot into chest boats on a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md). Parrot takes them to District 13 and his army. Mapicc gives Spoke a shield.

### The Northern Council

- Lettuce, [TheG0dofWar](../../characters/theg0dofwar.md), Purpled, SargeLAW and Leo stand waiting. No peace is made. Wemmbu's three nukes kill over half their forces; the diamond players fall easily.
- In the palace, Lettuce asks how stupid they think he is: full-netherite players hidden in the walls surround them. After a long fight, Lettuce and LAW flee. Flame tries to kill Jaden; Wemmbu intervenes.
- Lettuce returns without armour and surrenders. In this telling the majority votes to imprison rather than execute him. Parrot says Lettuce may still run, and win, which causes uproar; Lettuce says he knew Parrot would win the moment the execution failed.

### Leo

- Jumper says Leo is a few hundred blocks away. They break all his armour except his leggings. Leo says this will never end until he's dead, and stasises away, but the stasis has been rigged to drop him into the void. Leo dies.
- Spoke walks into a Nether portal in triumph to ominous music, not knowing that portal will haunt him through the next season.

## Items & mechanics

- [Arrow Cannon](../../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md): makeshift cannons in the tunnels.
- [Wither](../../mechanics/wither-bore.md): bored through obsidian to find Jaden's cell.
- [Pearl Cannon](../../mechanics/pearl-cannon.md): boosted with TNT minecarts to outpace an elytra.
- [Wolf Summoning Machine](../../mechanics/wolf-summoning-machine.md): Flame's Orbital Dog Cannon in LAW HQ.
- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): Wemmbu's three nukes.
- [Ender Pearl Stasis Chamber](../../mechanics/ender-pearl-stasis-chamber.md): Leo's rigged stasis, his death.
- Keycards: LAW HQ's access levels.

## Outcomes

- Jumper's spies, Th3Blurpenstein and Jaden are freed; Lomedy stays.
- LAW is defeated and Lettuce surrenders (see [The War Against LAW](../../events/the-war-against-law.md)).
- Leow0ok dies in the void, killed by his own rigged stasis.
