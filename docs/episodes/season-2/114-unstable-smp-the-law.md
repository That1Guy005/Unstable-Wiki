## Summary

The war against [LAW](../../factions/law.md) from [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md)'s side. [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) asks him to free [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) before Lettuce bans him, so Wemmbu teams with [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md) to raid LAW HQ, finds Lettuce's old diary, and faces Lettuce's fishing-rod threat, which fails because Jaden has already been freed. He joins the alliance, fires the opening orbitals at the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md), and casts the deciding vote to spare Lettuce.

## Full breakdown

### Jumper's request

- Lettuce warns that if Wemmbu crosses LAW again, Jaden will be banned. Wemmbu considers a stab shot straight at him, and flies away.
- At an old underground base with [Eggchan](../../characters/eggchan.md), he spots invisibility particles at the enchanting table and boxes them in. Egg says they've been chill: it's Jumper, Jaden's friend. Lettuce will execute Jaden no matter what, so Wemmbu must free him first.
- Wemmbu sends Egg to meet him at Flame's base, and finds Flame gearing up: they both want to break a friend out of prison.

### LAW HQ

- [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md) commands the guards, but his own carts kill more lawmen than Flame or Wemmbu do. They kill everyone except Sarge. Wemmbu takes a dead lawman's netherite and wrecks the site, and tells Flame where [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) is while he watches from above.
- In Lettuce's office, a book on the lectern is Lettuce's old diary from the Sky Civilization days: he felt like a side character in an unstable universe and wished for control of his own life. Wemmbu takes it and kills a lawman spying from below.
- A LAW horn: lawmen and Lettuce pour in, and Wemmbu starts stab-shotting. He claims he's a bystander acting in self-defence. When his totem pops, he flies out with Lettuce in pursuit.
- Wemmbu asks what he ever did to deserve this. Lettuce says Wemmbu killed his friends and destroyed every civilization he joined. He's no hero, but he'll do what needs to be done. Wemmbu says he's trying to bring order where it doesn't belong.
- Lettuce pulls the fishing rod, and no death message appears. Jaden, [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md), Jumper and [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md) arrive to kill Lettuce. More guards come, not enough, and Lettuce flies away; Wemmbu runs out of rockets and his rocketless flight is too slow.

### The alliance

- At Spoke's Amplified castle, with [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md), a spy's book reports lawmen converging on a remote spot opposite Spawn, probably where Parrot is. Wemmbu flies straight there, but Spoke beats him.
- When the lawmen arrive, Wemmbu fires an orbital and then stubbornly refuses to fight, until Flame turns up. Parrot and Theo tie the loot to a [Happy Ghast](../../mechanics/happy-ghast.md).
- Parrot proposes merging forces at his revamped [District 13](../../factions/district-13.md). Flame suggests Egg might be at Flame's old base; Egg is there, ready to fight, though Wemmbu has already freed Jaden.

### The Northern Council

- Wemmbu breaks the redstone door into District 13 as Parrot's speech begins: today they destroy LAW. The march to the Council makes the frames drop.
- Wemmbu holds the group back while Parrot talks to Lettuce, then flies up and fires three orbitals, killing hundreds in seconds.
- At the palace, Lettuce reveals netherite guards hidden behind the watchtowers. Wemmbu blows up much of the Council in the second battle. They strip Lettuce's armour and he flies away; Wemmbu runs out of rockets, switches to wind charges, and Minute reports Lettuce had an escape route ready.
- Minute says it's not so bad he got away: the server now sees him for who he is, and he has to live with his mistakes.

### The vote

- Exhausted, everyone regroups. Flame attacks Jaden for Lomedy's mace and Wemmbu defends Jaden. Lettuce walks back in, empty-handed, and surrenders.
- Parrot spares him; Flame calls a vote. Wemmbu votes last, with Minute staring at him, reminding him of their talk, and sides with Parrot, making it a tie. Lettuce is encased in obsidian.
- Wemmbu hands Lettuce his old Sky Civilization diary as a reminder, and Lettuce calls him cruel.
- Egg suggests the library; Wemmbu has other plans. On the highway, Flame explains Jaden's mace belongs to another friend. Asked where he's going, Wemmbu says he has an idea, and turns right off the highway.

## Items & mechanics

- [Orbital Strike Cannon](../../mechanics/orbital-strike-cannon.md): stab shots in LAW HQ, and the three opening orbitals.
- [Fishing Rod Trigger](../../mechanics/fishing-rod-trigger.md): Lettuce's rod linked to Jaden, which fails.
- Lettuce's diary: his Sky Civilization-era book, returned to him in his cell.
- [Wind Charge](../../mechanics/wind-charge.md): Wemmbu's substitute when rockets run out.
- [Mace](../../mechanics/mace.md): Lomedy's mace, still in Jaden's hands.

## Outcomes

- Jaden is freed; LAW HQ is wrecked.
- LAW is defeated at the Northern Council (see [The War Against LAW](../../events/the-war-against-law.md)).
- Wemmbu's vote spares Lettuce, who is caged in obsidian.
