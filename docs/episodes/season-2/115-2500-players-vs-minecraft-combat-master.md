## Summary

The war's end from [FlameFrags](../../characters/flamefrags.md)' side. Months of training lead to this: freeing [Lomedy](../../characters/lomedy.md) from LAW HQ alongside [Wemmbu](../../characters/wemmbu.md), reluctantly joining an alliance of old enemies, and fighting through the [Northern Council](../../places/northern-council.md) battle. Lomedy refuses to leave until his sentence is done, but after Lettuce's surrender and advice, Flame returns, frees him, and gives back his mace.

## Full breakdown

### The last stand begins

- Flame recaps his battles with LAW, from the 2v1000 to his exile; all his months of training lead here.
- His base is griefed and covered in LAW banners. Lettuce comes to talk him out of fighting, and fails. As Lettuce leaves, Wemmbu digs in: "Have you seen an eggchan?" Both need a friend out of prison, so they team up.

### LAW HQ

- They beat [SargeLAW](../../characters/sargelaw.md)'s garrison; Sarge retreats to fetch Lettuce. [PrinceZam](../../characters/princezam.md), [SpokeIsHere](../../characters/spokeishere.md) and [JumperWho](../../characters/jumperwho.md) are killing guards inside too.
- Wemmbu asks whether Lomedy actually wants to leave, and stalls Lettuce while Flame frees the prisoners, killing guards and destroying doors and security. Th3Blurpenstein runs.
- Lomedy refuses to escape until his sentence is served; he won't break the law. He doesn't trust Flame, who stole his mace, destroyed his farm, keeps fighting LAW and never admits he's wrong. He'll only leave if LAW lets him go, and asks for his mace back; Flame won't give it until he leaves.
- With only LAW left to take down, Flame maces the army on top of the HQ. He spots [Jaden_MAN](../../characters/jaden-man.md) fighting and asks for Lomedy's other mace; Jaden says ask later. Wemmbu orbitals the HQ into rubble.

### Old enemies

- Spoke asks about Parrot: they need him to sway the public, so nobody forms another LAW. At Spoke's Amplified base, Flame lists his grievances: Spoke led armies to kill him, Jaden betrayed him and stole Lomedy's mace, Jumper worked with BAT against him, and [Mapicc](../../characters/mapicc.md) and [MinuteTech](../../characters/minutetech.md) are enemies too. Spoke's rallying barely helps; Minute hands out shulkers.
- A spy reports thousands of lawmen converging on one spot, probably Parrot's. Asked what he'll do after killing Lettuce, Wemmbu says he'll ride off into the sunset (Zam and Spoke call him stupid); Flame says he'll get all the aura, be welcomed back as the Immortal Demon, and then slime out Jaden. He attacks Jaden over the mace, and Wemmbu fires orbitals to stop them.
- Wemmbu flies, Flame walks; with Parrot, [TheobaldTheBird](../../characters/theobaldthebird.md) and Wemmbu, he kills every lawman there, and they fly to base on a happy ghast.

### The Northern Council

- Parrot asks who was around for the Invisible Mafia days and leads them to [District 13](../../factions/district-13.md), then rallies the troops and marches on the Council.
- They beat the diamond lawmen, confront Lettuce and his generals in the palace, and barely survive the netherite legions. Drained, Flame fights Jaden for Lomedy's mace and Wemmbu intervenes. Lettuce comes back and surrenders.
- In the palace, a vote spares Lettuce for prison. Lettuce tells Flame he should change, so that everything terrible he's done isn't in vain, and prove the world wrong.
- Flame goes back to LAW HQ, frees Lomedy, and gives him back his mace.
