An elite team of assassins, spies and bounty hunters led by [Leow0ok](../characters/leow0ok.md), dedicated to investigating crime and taking down wanted players, especially [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md). [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) and [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) were both members at points, and the team becomes Spoke's enemy in the BAT Arc.

## Overview

- Status: disbanded. Leow0ok's elite team of assassins, spies and bounty hunters in Season 2, operating from [BAT Headquarters](../places/bat-headquarters.md), with [Nufuli](../characters/nufuli.md) as his right hand and [JumperWho's Spies](jumperwhos-spies.md) as its intelligence arm.

## History

- [The Director Arc](../arcs/the-director-arc.md): it shelters Parrot from ItzRealMe, survives MrCube6 and loses its base to TruOriginal, and finally exposes the Director.
- [BAT Arc](../arcs/bat-arc.md): rebuilt, it recruits Spoke and Mapicc with a test ambush, then investigates and wars on Spoke over the dead spies and the missing armour, allied with LAW.
- It fell apart as members fell out: Leo and his spies were killed, and the rest were absorbed by LAW or destroyed by NULL.
