[Capital City](../places/capital-city.md)'s police, which secretly answered to someone other than the city. It arrests new players and puts King [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) on trial for murders he was framed for, then has its leadership seized and is later merged into the Royal Army.

## Overview

- Capital City's police, led first by ReinforcedLad and bossstream, later by Fymada after reform.

## History

- Probably hired by Cindercrest or Clown, it kept arriving new players crammed in an apartment, telling them the world outside was deadly, and killed newcomers at the gates.
- It staged the deaths of 43 players to frame King Parrot, arrested him, and jailed him and Theo in the Black Market, until the [Vanguard](vanguard.md) freed them. Parrot exposed its confession publicly and its leaders were jailed.
- Reformed under Fymada, it joined the Royal Army. In ep. 161, bossstream reveals Clown paid it to destabilise Parrot's kingdom.

## Involved in

- [I Exposed a Corrupt Minecraft Civilization](../episodes/season-2-plus/122-i-exposed-a-corrupt-minecraft-civilization.md) (ep. 122, ParrotX2, 19 March 2026): Parrot proves his innocence; the Capital City police are jailed and Fymada takes control.
- [10,000 Player Minecraft War: Nether VS Overworld](../episodes/season-2-plus/161-10-000-player-minecraft-war-nether-vs-overworld.md) (ep. 161, ParrotX2, 2 November 2026): Clown is revealed to have framed Parrot through the Capitol City Police Force.
