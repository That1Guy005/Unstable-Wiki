[Saparata](../characters/saparata.md)'s army of "old players", fighting to put veterans first and to kill or enslave new players. It wins the civil war against [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) and makes Saparata the second king.

## Overview

- Status: active. The ruling monarchy of the Spawn Kingdom under King [Saparata](../characters/saparata.md), with [ShoeBilly_](../characters/shoebilly.md) as second-in-command and, for a time, LettuceK as captain.
- Ideology: survival of the strongest, favouring "old players" and killing or enslaving new ones. It's one of the richest factions ever, able to dress thousands in netherite, and has had nearly every top fighter work for it.

## History

- It massacres the [Skymore Civilization](../places/skymore-civilization.md) and invades the Spawn Kingdom; with the [Kingdom of the Caves](kingdom-of-the-caves.md), it wins [The Civil War](../events/the-civil-war.md) and makes Saparata king, though its veteran ranks are worn down.
- It traps Spawn during the famine, funds [Terminus](../places/terminus.md) to break FlameFrags, and uses his capture to recruit, but loses its 10,000-player war with LAW (ep. 160).
- Ep. 161: it allies with Parrot against the [Nether Kingdom](nether-kingdom.md), loses Capital City, and helps hold [Newtopia](../places/newtopia.md).
