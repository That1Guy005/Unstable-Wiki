[ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md)'s rebellion, first against [The Invisible Mafia](the-invisible-mafia.md) and later against [LAW](law.md), based in a bunker built by [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md).

## Overview

- Status: inactive. Also called the Resistance. A rebellion against [The Invisible Mafia](the-invisible-mafia.md), later revived against [LAW](law.md).
- Founded by [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) with Wifies, Wato1876, Kenadian, TheobaldTheBird, R4trick, F1nlo, Derapchu, Rekrap2 and JumperWho, in a bunker built by Horace Altman (who left for the Mafia the same day).

## History

- Parrot kills a suspicious recruit, Yusai, angering members; Spoke and Mapicc join with a book supposedly listing every Mafia stasis chamber, which seems fake. Progress is so slow that everyone except Parrot and Wifies leaves or joins the Mafia.
- With Wemmbu and Egg, it breaks the Mafia's Spawn prison and destroys every stasis chamber, ending the Mafia.
- During the election, Parrot, Theo, Horace and Fymada revive it from the town that grew in the old base; led by Parrot and Spoke, it defeats LAW at the Northern Council.

## Involved in

- [Unstable SMP: Doomsday](../episodes/season-1/025-unstable-smp-doomsday.md) (ep. 25, ParrotX2, 21 January 2025): With the [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md) gone, Parrot founds District 13, loses [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md) to the Mafia and the resistance to despair, and brings in [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md), [Mapicc](../characters/mapicc.md) and [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md).
- [Unstable SMP: The Mafia](../episodes/season-1/026-unstable-smp-the-mafia.md) (ep. 26, Wemmbu, 21 January 2025): He joins [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md)'s District 13 in exchange for a shot at a diamond-trim's mace, wins a mace back from Ferre with [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md)'s "order 36", and acts as the distraction for the Season 1 finale: first at the spawn prison, then by challenging Clown to a fair duel.
- [I Became King of the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/112-i-became-king-of-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 112, ParrotX2, 10 February 2026): From District 13, Parrot raises a resistance that joins [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md)'s alliance, and together they defeat LAW at the [Northern Council](../places/northern-council.md).
- [4 Minecraft Pros VS 2500 Players](../episodes/season-2/113-4-minecraft-pros-vs-2500-players.md) (ep. 113, SpokeIsHere, 12 February 2026): Parrot takes them to District 13 and his army.
- [Unstable SMP: The LAW](../episodes/season-2/114-unstable-smp-the-law.md) (ep. 114, Wemmbu, 14 February 2026): Parrot proposes merging forces at his revamped District 13.
- [2500 Players VS Minecraft Combat Master](../episodes/season-2/115-2500-players-vs-minecraft-combat-master.md) (ep. 115, FlameFrags, 16 February 2026): Parrot asks who was around for the Invisible Mafia days and leads them to District 13, then rallies the troops and marches on the Council.
