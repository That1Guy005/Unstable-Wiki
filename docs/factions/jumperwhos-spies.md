[JumperWho](../characters/jumperwho.md)'s team of invisible, netherite-armoured spies with codenames, first under BAT's command and later Jumper's own.

## Overview

- Status: active. Invisible spies in netherite with codenames, led by [JumperWho](../characters/jumperwho.md), originally BAT's spy unit under Leow0ok. Their work is espionage and investigation, rarely assassination.

## History

- Formed after the Mafia fell, they investigate the Director and later Spoke.
- They split when Leo turned spies into LAW soldiers over Jumper's objections, then dwindled through the [Kings Arc](../arcs/kings-arc.md); many died and Jumper was captured by NULL in the [Missing Players Arc](../arcs/missing-players-arc.md).
- After Purgatory, Jumper rebuilt the team, and in ep. 161 they hide Parrot and help him reach Spawn.
