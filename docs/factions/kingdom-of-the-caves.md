An underground kingdom built around an ancient city, founded by [Arachn1d](../characters/arachn1d.md) to make him the server's "rightful" king and later ruled by [Yungyx](../characters/yungyx.md). It holds [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) hostage to control [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md), and sides with Cindercrest in the civil war.

## Overview

- Status: active, ruled by [Yungyx](../characters/yungyx.md) since ep. 150. Founded by [Arachn1d](../characters/arachn1d.md) to make himself the server's "rightful" king.
- It lies inside a snowy mountain, pitch dark (night vision is handed out free), built around a mineshaft for spider spawners, near a sculk biome where wardens can spawn. Its trade partners included the [Wraiths](wraiths.md).

## History

- It rose after the Kings Arc, took Eggchan hostage to force Wemmbu into its service, allied with Cindercrest in [The Civil War](../events/the-civil-war.md), and overran Merchant City and its Vanguard.
- Arachn1d's purge culled the weak ([The Purge of the Caves](../events/the-purge-of-the-caves.md)). Wemmbu's revolt and Yungy's blade ended Arachn1d's rule, and Yungy won the crown in a duel.
