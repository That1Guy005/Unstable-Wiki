The law-enforcement army behind [LettuceK](../characters/lettucek.md). It imprisons Wemmbu, Lomedy and Jaden, and takes on the protagonists in the 2v1000 and the server-wide war of the Kings Arc. It later returns allied with [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) in the Dimensional War.

## Overview

- Status: active, led by Morr0w_ since ep. 160. Founded by [LettuceK](../characters/lettucek.md) to "serve justice" and protect players, and to deliver newspapers about events on the server; it became the server's police and, briefly, its government.
- Past leaders include Lettuce, CircusMaximus_, CorporalPaul, SargeLAW and FlameFrags; commanders included [Deputy Ace](../characters/deputy-ace.md), [TheG0dofWar](../characters/theg0dofwar.md), the_Oroboros and SargeRaxby.

## History

- Lettuce builds [LAW City](../places/law-city.md) beside Flame's home, hunts Flame and Wemmbu, and fights them in [The 2 VS 1000 War](../events/the-2-vs-1000-war.md); it jails Wemmbu ([The LAW Prison](../events/the-law-prison.md)).
- It tries to control [The Great Sea](../places/the-great-sea.md) with an orbital cannon, wiping out the Pirates, until Wemmbu destroys the cannon in the Battle for the Great Sea (ep. 101).
- Lettuce declares himself king; [District 13](district-13.md) defeats LAW at the Northern Council ([The War Against LAW](../events/the-war-against-law.md)).
- Rebuilt, it builds Flame's base and makes him work off the debt, and hires him against the Mist; Parrot destroys LAW City in the civil war.
- Ep. 157–160: it leads the raid that frees Flame from Terminus, and under Flame beats Cindercrest; ep. 161: Clown's army overruns it.
