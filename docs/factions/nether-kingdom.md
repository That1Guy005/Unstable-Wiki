[ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md)'s absolute monarchy over the Nether, with [FerreMC](../characters/ferremc.md) as second-in-command. It aims to monopolise the Nether's resources, hosts Clown's festivals and vault, and invades the End and the Overworld in the Dimensional War.

## Overview

- Status: active. An absolute monarchy over most of the Nether under [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md), with [FerreMC](../characters/ferremc.md) second and Mugm_ third; commanders include 4Blaze, TorrinTheTitan and EmberRat.
- It monopolises the Nether's resources, netherite and most brewing ingredients, and kills or imprisons intruders. It's one of the oldest surviving empires, formed after the Mafia fell.

## History

- It guards Clown's vault ([Raid on Clown's Nether Vault](../events/raid-on-clowns-nether-vault.md)) and runs his Nether mining prison.
- It makes the Capitol City Police Force and other Overworld groups its vassals, and fights NULL for its capital and the Turtle Kingdom.
- Ep. 161: it invades the Overworld, taking Fort Feather, Highwater, [LAW City](../places/law-city.md), Steampunk, Ironclad, [Merchant City](../places/merchant-city.md) and [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md), before losing at [Newtopia](../places/newtopia.md).
