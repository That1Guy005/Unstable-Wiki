The overarching antagonists: an anonymous army of players with scrambled names and black skins. First led by [JamatoP](../characters/jamatop.md) from [Purgatory](../places/purgatory.md), it is taken over by [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md), who uses it to build the [End Empire](../places/end-empire.md).

## Overview

- Status: active, led by [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md), with the [Commander](../characters/commander.md) as second-in-command and lettered commanders below him. Its soldiers are skinless players with scrambled names who don't fear death.
- Under [JamatoP](../characters/jamatop.md), the "NULL Hunters" exiled bounty targets and troublemakers and held them in [Purgatory](../places/purgatory.md). Under Spoke, it aims to conquer the server, now from the [End Empire](../places/end-empire.md).
- Technology: wither cannons, the shulker tracker, the [Orbital Automatic Pearl Launching Contraption](../mechanics/orbital-automatic-pearl-launching-contraption.md), the [Orbital Mob Cannon](../mechanics/orbital-mob-cannon.md), a shared piston-moved spawn bed, and a monopoly on duplicated shulkers.

## History

- [Missing Players Arc](../arcs/missing-players-arc.md) and [Purgatory Arc](../arcs/purgatory-arc.md): it abducts players across the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md), until Spoke takes it from Jamato.
- [End Civilization Arc](../arcs/end-civilization-arc.md): it conquers the End ([NULL's Conquest of the End](../events/nulls-conquest-of-the-end.md)) and the Overworld's End portals.
- Ep. 154–159: it destroys Wemmbu's cannons and the first-generation city, recruits the Wraiths, runs Spoke's End prison, and wars with the [Nether Kingdom](nether-kingdom.md) over its capital.
