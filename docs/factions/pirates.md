[Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md)'s band of raiders, which became the Pirate Civilization based in [Pirate City](../places/pirate-city.md). They rob Wemmbu's empire, later shelter him, and fight [LAW](law.md) for [The Great Sea](../places/the-great-sea.md).

## Overview

- Status: destroyed. [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md)'s bandit crew, later the Pirate Civilization of [Pirate City](../places/pirate-city.md) in [The Great Sea](../places/the-great-sea.md). Members included Spongs, Arcn, Loppezz, LettuceK, DaFraDo and, briefly, Theobald.

## History

- They raid the Eggchan Empire, ransom Egg, and exile Wemmbu to the Pirate Islands, where Theobald secretly helps him.
- Wemmbu traps Jaden in the Abandoned Civilization and destroys his castle; then, as the Invisible Knight, he helps Jaden take Ranger Point.
- LAW wipes them out in the Battle for the Great Sea, killing innocents flagged as pirates.
