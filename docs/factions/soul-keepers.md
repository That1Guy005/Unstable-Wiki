The keepers of [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md), who capture players trapped there and patch the holes in the Nether's bedrock to stop anyone escaping.

## Overview

- Status: active. Guardians of [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md) who capture anyone trapped there and patch holes in the Nether's bedrock. Allied with JamatoP. Members include DocDespair8 (their late leader), SoulDestroyer67, DarkStalker41 and TheOathBinder.

## History

- Wemmbu steals AuvmorUndead's happy ghast and they capture Boosfer; Wemmbu escapes, frees him, and kills Auvmor, ThirdEyeOpened and DocDespair8, while KiaranTheHold0 dies to Boosfer's explosion.
- SoulDestroyer67 lets Jamato, Wemmbu and Egg through a portal to the far side of the Far Lands.
- Ep. 156: TheOathBinder kills all but one of Parrot's thousand prisoners.

## Involved in

- [I Escaped Minecraft's Forgotten Dimension](../episodes/season-2/075-i-escaped-minecrafts-forgotten-dimension.md) (ep. 75, Wemmbu, 6 October 2025): The Soul Keepers' leader is killed, and Wemmbu and Boosfer escape the Underworld.
- [I Explored the Minecraft Farlands](../episodes/season-2/108-i-explored-the-minecraft-farlands.md) (ep. 108, Wemmbu, 31 January 2026): Jamato guides them through an ancient Nether shortcut and the Soul Keepers to Serpentbound's kingdom, until Wemmbu reveals he never meant to meet them peacefully.
