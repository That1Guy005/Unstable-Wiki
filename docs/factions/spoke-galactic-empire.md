A short-lived faction [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) founded for fun and revenge. He was terrible at leading it, and its thousand-player army rebelled under "THE EXECUTIVE".

## Overview

- Status: disbanded. A short-lived faction Spoke created for fun and revenge, with no goals or promises, which still drew 1,017 players.

## History

- Spoke's neglect turned it into anarchy and its members turned on him and Mapicc. "The Executive" took over, and Spoke, Mapicc and Leow0ok destroyed it.
