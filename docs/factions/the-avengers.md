A team of all of [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md)'s old enemies, founded by [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md) and led by [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md), formed to hunt Wemmbu across the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md). It fell apart through infighting and betrayal.

## Overview

- Status: disbanded. A ragtag alliance of everyone Wemmbu had wronged, assembled by [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md) and FlameFrags.

## History

- They chase Wemmbu and Egg through the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md). In the Spikelands battle they're winning until Flame switches sides, and he returns Wemmbu's mace. The betrayal starts Jaden and Flame's feud ([Betrayal Arc](../arcs/betrayal-arc.md)).

## Involved in

- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Fartherlands](../episodes/season-2/054-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-fartherlands.md) (ep. 54, Wemmbu, 15 July 2025): The Avengers' attack fails; Wemmbu briefly allies with FlameFrags and gets his mace back.
- [I Got REVENGE on the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/055-i-got-revenge-on-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 55, FlameFrags, 16 July 2025): The Avengers turn on each other and fall apart (see [The Avengers' Hunt for Wemmbu](../events/the-avengers-hunt-for-wemmbu.md)).
