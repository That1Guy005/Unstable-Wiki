The main antagonist faction of Season 1: a huge, authoritarian, always-invisible army that rules by fear and executes members by void, aiming to conquer the whole server. Led by [Ashswag](../characters/ashswag.md), it is fought by Parrot, Spoke and Wemmbu across the Invisible Mafia Arc until its stasis chambers are destroyed.

## Overview

- Status: destroyed. The first server-wide ruling force: a huge authoritarian faction that ruled through fear, made its members stay invisible on pain of execution by void, and aimed to conquer the whole server. It was founded early under an unknown leader before [Ashswag](../characters/ashswag.md).
- Ranks by armour trim: untrimmed, copper, iron, gold and diamond, each with its own level of [Mafia City](../places/mafia-city.md); promotion took three mission tokens. [FerreMC](../characters/ferremc.md) led the diamonds, and [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md), [Reddoons](../characters/reddoons.md)' [Capital City](../places/capital-city.md) and the Director were allies.

## How it worked

- Spies and moles inside rival civilizations, bomber planes, stasis chambers for its own escapes, and press-ganging new players at [Spawn](../places/spawn.md).

## History

- It wrecks farms around Spoke and Mapicc, hunts Mafia deserters like MinuteTech, and conquers [The End Civilization](../places/the-end-civilization.md), the [Skyblock Civilization](../places/skyblock-civilization.md) and the [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md).
- Spoke, PlanetLord and Leow0ok infiltrate it; [District 13](district-13.md) rises against it.
- In the Season 1 finale, District 13 destroys its stasis chambers and Spoke brings it down from inside ([Fall of the Invisible Mafia](../events/fall-of-the-invisible-mafia.md)); WyLL takes over the leftovers of Mafia City.
