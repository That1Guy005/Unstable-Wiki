[Antaggg](../characters/antaggg.md)'s elite faction, trained on the idea that you don't need raw skill to beat stronger players. It exiled [TheG0dofWar](../characters/theg0dofwar.md) and becomes [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md)' enemy in the Warriors Arc.

## Overview

- Status: active. [Antaggg](../characters/antaggg.md)'s elite soldiers, trained to believe raw skill isn't needed to beat stronger players. Former members include Smokebomb_ and WitherSt0rm_.

## History

- They expel [TheG0dofWar](../characters/theg0dofwar.md) and kill him with an [Arrow Cannon](../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md) in a rigged arena.
- They run a PvP tournament where Antaggg cheats Flame.
- Flame, Falconu and Canary hunt them, and Antaggg loses a fair duel.
- They try to kill Flame in Terminus, then build LAW an arrow cannon against Cindercrest in exchange for twenty prisoners.
