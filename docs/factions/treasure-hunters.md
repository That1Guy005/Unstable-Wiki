A crew led by [Boosfer](../characters/boosfer.md) with Kierrrr and Its_Dev_, which got rich by stealing items and auctioning them off. Its hoard is raided by [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md), and the team falls apart after Wemmbu, Parrot and Wifies destroy its base.

## Overview

- Status: disbanded. [Boosfer](../characters/boosfer.md)'s gang with Kierrrr and Its_Dev_, rich from stealing and auctioning other players' items.

## History

- Boosfer hires Wemmbu to blow up Rekrap2's valley; Parrot talks him round, and Wemmbu, Parrot and Wifies destroy the gang's base with TNT and withers, taking shulkers of diamond and netherite blocks.

## Involved in

- [Why I Destroyed the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/010-why-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 10, Wemmbu, 8 July 2024): The Treasure Hunters' hoard is looted.
- [It's Impossible to Find this Player](../episodes/season-1/012-its-impossible-to-find-this-player.md) (ep. 12, ParrotX2, 22 July 2024): The Treasure Hunters are robbed of most of their wealth by Parrot, Wifies and Wemmbu.
