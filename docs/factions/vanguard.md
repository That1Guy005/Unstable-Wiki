[Merchant City](../places/merchant-city.md)'s private security force in redstone-trimmed diamond armour, hired by [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) for his royal army in the civil war.

## Overview

- Status: destroyed by the Kingdom of the Caves. Merchant City's private security force under DanchoGold, in redstone-trimmed diamond armour with red-and-yellow shields. Notable members: ArthurTheKnight2 (killed by ShoeBilly) and Brobro2.

## History

- They free Parrot and Theo from the Black Market prison, smuggle Parrot into Capital City among a herd of cows, and help him expose the police.
- When Dancho refuses Parrot an army over his debt, Parrot takes Dancho hostage and merges the Vanguard into the Royal Army against Cindercrest.

## Involved in

- [I Exposed a Corrupt Minecraft Civilization](../episodes/season-2-plus/122-i-exposed-a-corrupt-minecraft-civilization.md) (ep. 122, ParrotX2, 19 March 2026): Sentenced to death, Parrot is freed by Merchant City's Vanguard, who need him to pay his debts.
- [I Created the World's Largest Minecraft Army](../episodes/season-2-plus/129-i-created-the-worlds-largest-minecraft-army.md) (ep. 129, ParrotX2, 4 May 2026): He recruits bandits by trickery and [Reina](../characters/reinadrop.md)'s dripstone trident, takes [Merchant City](../places/merchant-city.md)'s Vanguard by kidnapping DanchoGold, buys 40,000 TNT from [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md), and blows up the LAW holdouts led by [SargeLAW](../characters/sargelaw.md).
- [I Joined a 10,000 Player Minecraft Army](../episodes/season-2-plus/132-i-joined-a-10-000-player-minecraft-army.md) (ep. 132, FlameFrags, 15 May 2026)
- [Unstable SMP: Civil War](../episodes/season-2-plus/133-unstable-smp-civil-war.md) (ep. 133, ParrotX2, 16 May 2026): Fort Feather opens the direct road to Merchant City, where the Vanguard waits.
