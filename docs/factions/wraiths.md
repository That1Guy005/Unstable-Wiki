Mysterious assassins in diamond rib-trimmed netherite, hidden behind invisibility and voice changers, who hunt [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) and [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) in the Kingdoms saga.

## Overview

- Status: active. A group of invisible contract killers who pose as a single player, in diamond rib-trimmed netherite (later emerald trims) with voice changers. Skills vary: some cart-PvP or elytra-mace, others are redstone specialists. They're strongest as a coordinated squad. Members include Nitenly and Flbthp; Cytharam is dead.

## History

- One fights Wemmbu at the [Mysterious Tomb](../places/mysterious-tomb.md); others hunt Eggchan for the [Kingdom of the Caves](kingdom-of-the-caves.md), guarding his stasis pearls, until Wemmbu destroys their vault (ep. 150).
- Ep. 154–155: they join NULL; Nitenly, now one of them, kills Jaden, and Wraiths capture MinuteTech.

## Involved in

- [Minecraft Pro VS 1000 Player Civilization](../episodes/season-2-plus/121-minecraft-pro-vs-1000-player-civilization.md) (ep. 121, Wemmbu, 15 March 2026): The Wraith (Wraiths) steals Egg's journal.
- [I Got Hunted by the World's Deadliest Assassin](../episodes/season-2-plus/128-i-got-hunted-by-the-worlds-deadliest-assassin.md) (ep. 128, Wemmbu, 2 May 2026): dizzyu's bandits are wiped out; one Wraith is killed.
- [Searching For My Friend Who Went Missing](../episodes/season-2-plus/131-searching-for-my-friend-who-went-missing.md) (ep. 131, Wemmbu, 9 May 2026): The assassins are revealed as Cytharam and Flbthp (Wraiths); Arachn1d buys Egg.
- [I Created Minecraft's Greatest Military](../episodes/season-2-plus/148-i-created-minecrafts-greatest-military.md) (ep. 148, SpokeIsHere, 13 August 2026): Following Jamato's book on "the Wraith", he finds only [JamatoP](../characters/jamatop.md), who warns of an army that outmatches him.
