Decorative armour patterns that became ranks and identities. In the Invisible Mafia they marked tier (untrimmed, copper, iron, gold, diamond) and were earned with upgrade tokens. Parrot and Wifies later pass through Mafia City by dressing as each rank in turn.

## Used in

### Season 1

- [I Got Hunted by the Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/019-i-got-hunted-by-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 19, SpokeIsHere, 5 October 2024): The Mafia's rank system (untrimmed, gold, diamond).
- [I Infiltrated The Minecraft Mafia](../episodes/season-1/022-i-infiltrated-the-minecraft-mafia.md) (ep. 22, SpokeIsHere, 18 November 2024): The Mafia's token-based rank ladder.
- [I Mastered Minecraft Combat](../episodes/season-1/023-i-mastered-minecraft-combat.md) (ep. 23, Wemmbu, 4 December 2024): What Wemmbu learns to read in a fight.
- [Unstable SMP: The First War](../episodes/season-1/024-unstable-smp-the-first-war.md) (ep. 24, ParrotX2, 7 December 2024): Parrot's silence-trimmed leggings, and his diamond-trim disguise.

### Season 2

- [I Explored to the END of the Minecraft Farlands](../episodes/season-2/058-i-explored-to-the-end-of-the-minecraft-farlands.md) (ep. 58, Wemmbu, 26 July 2025): Eye trims, extinct on the main server.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Void](../episodes/season-2/060-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-void.md) (ep. 60, Wemmbu, 7 August 2025): Every trim, found in the city.
- [I Faked My Death on a Hardcore Minecraft SMP](../episodes/season-2/070-i-faked-my-death-on-a-hardcore-minecraft-smp.md) (ep. 70, Wemmbu, 13 September 2025): Dune trims on diamond armour, the disguise.
- [I Secretly Joined My Enemy's Minecraft Team](../episodes/season-2/071-i-secretly-joined-my-enemys-minecraft-team.md) (ep. 71, Wemmbu, 20 September 2025): Wemmbu's disguise.
- [I Infiltrated Minecraft's Deadliest Faction](../episodes/season-2/076-i-infiltrated-minecrafts-deadliest-faction.md) (ep. 76, FlameFrags, 7 October 2025): Clown's guard pattern, copied by Parrot and Theo.
- [This Player Stole My Identity](../episodes/season-2/079-this-player-stole-my-identity.md) (ep. 79, Wemmbu, 14 October 2025): Wemmbu's exact trims, copied by the impersonators.
- [The Search for Wemmbu](../episodes/season-2/081-the-search-for-wemmbu.md) (ep. 81, ParrotX2, 22 October 2025): Parrot's replica Invisible Knight armour.
