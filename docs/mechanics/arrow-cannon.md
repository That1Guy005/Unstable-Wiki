A TNT-powered launcher that fires arrows so fast they register as many rapid hits, enough to one-shot a fully geared player even with totems in both hands. Often (inaccurately) called a railgun. The Director kills Nufuli with one, WyLL uses one on Wemmbu, and Wemmbu packs that same cannon into a shulker box and turns it on WyLL's crew in Mafia City.

## How it works on the server

- Arrows are stacked in layers on a block and launched by a TNT "railgun", so the game fires them milliseconds apart and counts them as separate hits.
- Spoke's triple-layered version kills straight through totems, which is how he beats NULL's hunters (ep. 119).
- Defenders use them as area denial: Terminus's guns kill anyone who tries a Nether portal, and the Warriors build one for LAW against Cindercrest (ep. 160).
