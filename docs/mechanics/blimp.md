[Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md)'s redstone airships, which carry troops over enemy territory and drop them in. They win Fort Feather, are stopped at Merchant City by Parrot's arrow cannon, and five of them are raided and destroyed by Parrot when Cindercrest and the Kingdom of the Caves attack Steampunk City.

## Used in

- [I Created the World's Largest Minecraft Army](../episodes/season-2-plus/129-i-created-the-worlds-largest-minecraft-army.md) (ep. 129, ParrotX2, 4 May 2026): Cindercrest's airship heading for Spawn.
- [I Joined a 10,000 Player Minecraft Army](../episodes/season-2-plus/132-i-joined-a-10-000-player-minecraft-army.md) (ep. 132, FlameFrags, 15 May 2026): Cindercrest's airship, slow-falling drops into Fort Feather and Merchant City.
- [Unstable SMP: Civil War](../episodes/season-2-plus/133-unstable-smp-civil-war.md) (ep. 133, ParrotX2, 16 May 2026): Cindercrest's airship, beaten by Parrot's TNT machine at Merchant City.
- [Unstable SMP: Civil War FINALE](../episodes/season-2-plus/138-unstable-smp-civil-war-finale.md) (ep. 138, ParrotX2, 3 June 2026): Cindercrest's airships, rigged and destroyed by Parrot.
- [Escaping Terminus: The Deadliest Minecraft Prison](../episodes/season-2-plus/157-escaping-terminus-the-deadliest-minecraft-prison.md) (ep. 157, FlameFrags, 6 October 2026): [ShoeBilly_](../characters/shoebilly.md) appears: he knew Flame would end up here since their talk on the Blimp.
- [10,000 Player Minecraft War: Nether VS Overworld](../episodes/season-2-plus/161-10-000-player-minecraft-war-nether-vs-overworld.md) (ep. 161, ParrotX2, 2 November 2026): Parrot's transport, and his escape from TorrinTheTitan.
