A flying machine that keeps duplicating and dropping TNT, leaving lines of craters. It's the [Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md)'s weapon of choice for razing civilizations (the A1C design, by MythicalPingu): one drops TNT on Zam's castle, and repeated A1C raids hit the [Far Lands Civilization](../places/far-lands-civilization.md).

## Used in

- [It's Impossible to Catch this Player](../episodes/season-1/006-its-impossible-to-catch-this-player.md) (ep. 6, Wemmbu, 25 May 2024): The TNT plane that bombs Zam's castle.
- [Why I Destroyed the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/010-why-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 10, Wemmbu, 8 July 2024): Missile planes against Ferre's tower, and bombers that break in unloaded chunks.
- [It's Impossible to Find this Player](../episodes/season-1/012-its-impossible-to-find-this-player.md) (ep. 12, ParrotX2, 22 July 2024): Wemmbu's bombers over Rekrap's base.
- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): The Mafia's A1C bombers.
- [Unstable SMP: The First War](../episodes/season-1/024-unstable-smp-the-first-war.md) (ep. 24, ParrotX2, 7 December 2024): The A1C bombers the Farmers set out to destroy.
- [I Got Hunted by a Minecraft Redstone Genius](../episodes/season-2/031-i-got-hunted-by-a-minecraft-redstone-genius.md) (ep. 31, ParrotX2, 9 March 2025): MrCube's ground bomber, destroyed by BAT.
