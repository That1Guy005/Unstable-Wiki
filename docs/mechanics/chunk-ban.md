A rigged area that bans any player who steps into it. In Season 1, [PrinceZam](../characters/princezam.md)'s empire uses chunk bans as its ultimate weapon against Spoke's crew, which is why prisons had to be built around them. Wifies and then Spoke step into one at the end of the scavenger hunt, Parrot's team frees prisoners by blowing chunk bans up, and Proton hides "fox bans" on its walls. There's a brief delay before the ban takes effect, which Parrot's team exploits to lure guards in.

## Used in

### Season 1

- [How to Destroy the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-1/001-how-to-destroy-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 1, SpokeIsHere, 21 April 2024): Spoke rules them out for now, which foreshadows what comes next.

- [I Broke into Minecraft's Most Secure Prison](../episodes/season-1/007-i-broke-into-minecrafts-most-secure-prison.md) (ep. 7, ParrotX2, 9 June 2024): The heart of every prison, the bait trap that bans six guards, and Fantst's return to one.
- [I Created Minecraft's Greatest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/008-i-created-minecrafts-greatest-civilization.md) (ep. 8, Wemmbu, 15 June 2024): Parrot's "flycatcher" traps, which catch Jaden.
- [I Got Hunted by Minecraft's Deadliest Players](../episodes/season-1/009-i-got-hunted-by-minecrafts-deadliest-players.md) (ep. 9, ParrotX2, 30 June 2024): Traps at Clown's castle, and Parrot's flycatchers at Wemmbu's Civilization.
- [How I Escaped Minecraft's Largest Prison](../episodes/season-1/014-how-i-escaped-minecrafts-largest-prison.md) (ep. 14, Wemmbu, 17 August 2024): Lines the walls, and catches Pangi through Zam's portal.

- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): Part of the civilization's defences.
