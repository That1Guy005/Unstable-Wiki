The wings every top player relies on, scarce and fought over: Wemmbu taunts Spoke with a shulker box full of them in the first episode, and the Invisible Mafia makes collecting them a mission. The PvP tournament bans them, which takes away a big part of Wemmbu's mace game; in his rematch with Flame, elytra-macing is what finally breaks Flame's shield.

## How it's used

- Elytra-macing (diving from height to slam with a [Mace](mace.md)) is one of Wemmbu's two signature techniques with the orbital cannons; without both, he says he can't take on LAW (ep. 93).
- Counters include slow-falling arrows (Flowtives, ep. 154), cobwebs and fighting under roofs, and Flame advises Wemmbu to win without it (ep. 150).
- Rockets don't work in parts of the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md).
