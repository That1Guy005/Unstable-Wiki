A thrown ender pearl held in place (usually in a bubble column) that teleports its owner back the moment someone "pulls" it. It's the Unstable SMP's universal emergency exit, and also a leash. [Ashswag](../characters/ashswag.md) forced every Invisible Mafia member to set a stasis he could pull into the void, so Spoke's destruction of the Mafia's mass stasis chamber is what ends the Mafia. Stases also work across dimensions, which is why [Kenadian](../characters/kenadian.md) has to stage a rescue to keep Dean alive.
