A redstone module that lets a player activate a machine from anywhere by "reeling in" a rod linked to a pressure plate on a chunk border. It's used to fire orbital cannons, summon Flame's wolves, and pull stasis chambers without a second player.

## Used in

- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Void](../episodes/season-2/060-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-void.md) (ep. 60, Wemmbu, 7 August 2025): Part of Egg's setup to fire the test strike.
- [I Explored the Minecraft Farlands](../episodes/season-2/108-i-explored-the-minecraft-farlands.md) (ep. 108, Wemmbu, 31 January 2026): Egg's rod linked to a stasis back to the cannons.
- [Minecraft Pro VS 1000 Players](../episodes/season-2/111-minecraft-pro-vs-1000-players.md) (ep. 111, Wemmbu, 7 February 2026): Wemmbu's orbital rod, and Lettuce's rod tied to Jaden's life.
- [Unstable SMP: The LAW](../episodes/season-2/114-unstable-smp-the-law.md) (ep. 114, Wemmbu, 14 February 2026): Lettuce's rod linked to Jaden, which fails.
- [I Joined a 100,000 Player Minecraft Server](../episodes/season-2-plus/116-i-joined-a-100-000-player-minecraft-server.md) (ep. 116, Wemmbu, 21 February 2026): The stab-shot rod Wemmbu gives Egg.
- [10,000 Players Simulate COLD WAR in Minecraft](../episodes/season-2-plus/159-10-000-players-simulate-cold-war-in-minecraft.md) (ep. 159, SpokeIsHere, 23 October 2026): In a new Overworld base, the Commander has combined a [Pearl Cannon](pearl-cannon.md) with Wemmbu's [Orbital Strike Cannon](orbital-strike-cannon.md): the Orbital Automatic Pearl Launching Contraption, a fishing rod that summons NULL.
