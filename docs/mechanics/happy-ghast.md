Rideable ghasts that carry players and cargo through the sky. Leow0ok flies in on one to rescue Parrot, Wemmbu steals one in a strange dimension, Parrot and Theo's ghast Sky is killed (they blame Wemmbu), and in Season 2+ armies fly loot and even cannons around on them.
