The potion that defines half the server's politics. [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) forced its members to stay invisible at all times, so drinking milk to unmask became the sign of its fall. Spies, assassins like the Wraiths, and Wemmbu's "Invisible Knight" all rely on it, and Mapicc later finds a glitch that hides even armour.
