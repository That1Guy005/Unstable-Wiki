A heavy weapon whose smash attack hits harder the further the wielder falls. On the Unstable SMP maces are extremely rare, because the Invisible Mafia destroyed nearly every ominous spawner outside the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md), so owning one is a sign of real power. Almost every Mafia diamond-trim carried one. [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) becomes the server's great mace fighter (his maces include Gambit, a gift from Jaron, and later Crucible), and Spoke's mace is called Vengeance. "Breach swapping" (switching to a Breach-enchanted mace mid-combo to cut through armour) is a favourite of ClownPierce and Jaden_MAN.
