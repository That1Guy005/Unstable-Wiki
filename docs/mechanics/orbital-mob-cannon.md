An orbital launcher that can fire almost any entity to any position in its dimension. [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) steals its blueprint from the Citadel in [The Underworld](../places/the-underworld.md), [NULL](../factions/null.md) builds it with technology reverse-engineered from Wemmbu's cannons, tests it with turtles and creakings, and Spoke eventually fires a load of wardens at the [Nether Kingdom](../factions/nether-kingdom.md).

## Used in

- [Exploring Minecraft's Forgotten Dimension](../episodes/season-2-plus/152-exploring-minecrafts-forgotten-dimension.md) (ep. 152, SpokeIsHere, 7 September 2026): "Weapons Vol. 5": stolen from the Citadel, describing the one weapon stronger than orbitals (Orbital Mob Cannon).
- [10,000 Players Simulate COLD WAR in Minecraft](../episodes/season-2-plus/159-10-000-players-simulate-cold-war-in-minecraft.md) (ep. 159, SpokeIsHere, 23 October 2026): Creakings and two hundred wardens on call.
