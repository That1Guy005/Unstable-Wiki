An exploit-based redstone superweapon that drops huge volleys of TNT on any coordinates in its dimension. The Unstable Universe uses a modified version with no delay, unlimited range and aiming. It's [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md)'s signature weapon: he shows it off at his Wonder guardian interview, levels the [Zam Empire](../places/zam-empire.md) with one, and later commands a whole battery in the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md), whose fast "Stab Shot" mode lets him nuke a spot almost instantly. [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) uses one to destroy [The Wonders](../places/the-wonders.md), and NULL later reverse-engineers the technology.

## How it works on the server

- Duplicated TNT is stored in "lazy chunks" and fired at preset coordinates; Wemmbu's Far Lands cannons sit beside a machine that speeds up the local tick rate, so they load and fire almost instantly.
- Obsidian roofs block it, and firing near civilians has consequences: [Arachn1d](../characters/arachn1d.md) punishes Wemmbu for a stab shot that kills civilians.
- It's triggered remotely with a [Fishing Rod Trigger](fishing-rod-trigger.md).

## Variants

- [Orbital Mob Cannon](orbital-mob-cannon.md), [Orbital Wither Cannon](orbital-wither-cannon.md) and the [Orbital Automatic Pearl Launching Contraption](orbital-automatic-pearl-launching-contraption.md) all build on the same idea.
