An orbital cannon that blasts blue wither skulls, capable of destroying water, lava and obsidian, at a target. [JamatoP](../characters/jamatop.md) uses one to wipe out Spoke's army and as a last resort in the fight for the Totem of NULL, which destroys much of [Purgatory](../places/purgatory.md). Spoke later forces Jamato to build more.

## Used in

- [Unstable SMP: Purgatory](../episodes/season-2-plus/143-unstable-smp-purgatory.md) (ep. 143, SpokeIsHere, 23 June 2026): Jamato's blue wither-skull cannon, which kills Spoke's army and destroys Purgatory.
- [1000 Players Build END Civilization in Hardcore Minecraft](../episodes/season-2-plus/146-1000-players-build-end-civilization-in-hardcore-minecraft.md) (ep. 146, SpokeIsHere, 26 July 2026): Built by Jamato under threat, used on the bunker and at the spawn island.
- [How I Saved the World's Largest Minecraft Server](../episodes/season-2-plus/154-how-i-saved-the-worlds-largest-minecraft-server.md) (ep. 154, Wemmbu, 19 September 2026): NULL's weapon against the original players' city.
- [I Created Minecraft's Greatest Empire](../episodes/season-2-plus/155-i-created-minecrafts-greatest-empire.md) (ep. 155, SpokeIsHere, 20 September 2026): NULL's barrage on Wemmbu and the rainbow city.
