A TNT launcher that throws an ender pearl thousands of blocks, teleporting its thrower. [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md) brings prisoners into [Proton](../places/proton.md) by pearl, and [Kenadian](../characters/kenadian.md)'s pearl cannon is how Wemmbu gets out. It's also how Spoke and Mapicc reach the Mafia's stasis chambers, how [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md) exiles Wemmbu and Egg to [The Great Sea](../places/the-great-sea.md), and how a duel with Sharpness throws Wemmbu out to the [Far Lands](../places/far-lands.md).
