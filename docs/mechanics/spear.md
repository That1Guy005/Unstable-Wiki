The newest weapon, with long reach and velocity-based charge attacks, but useless at point-blank range. Early spears on the server were modified and overpowered until that ended. [ManePear](../characters/manepear.md) is the first spear master, and teaches [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) in "I Got Trained By a Minecraft Spear Master".
