TNT in a minecart, set off with a flame arrow or rails. It's used as a trap (Egg's "order 36"), a bomb, and an entire fighting style. [TheobaldTheBird](../characters/theobaldthebird.md) is its master, winning duels with it, and teaches it to [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) as the last technique Flame needs to become a combat master.
