The item that saves you from one death, and so the unit everyone counts fights in ("popping" a totem). The server's exploits include stacking them, Proton's prisoners get three each, and the enchanted Totem of NULL is the symbol of leadership of [NULL](../factions/null.md), won by Spoke from Jamato.

## How it's used

- Fights are counted in pops; Proton gave prisoners three each, and some arenas end at the first pop.
- Popping one on purpose can clear an effect: Wemmbu used fall damage to clear mining fatigue (ep. 93).
- Parrot's barrel "immortality machine" ([Exploits](exploits.md)) and Spoke's totem-piercing [Arrow Cannon](arrow-cannon.md) both work around totems.
- The Totem of NULL marks NULL's leadership. Mapicc swears to pop it and kill Spoke (ep. 152), and Spoke keeps it even when emptying his inventory to meet Ashswag (ep. 155).
