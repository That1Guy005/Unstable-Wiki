[FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md)' answer to the orbital strike cannon: a teleport machine, triggered by fishing rod, that summons an army of armoured wolves to his side. Unlike orbitals it ignores obsidian roofs and never hurts its owner, but it needs huge amounts of food and armadillo scutes. Flame built it specifically to counter Wemmbu.
