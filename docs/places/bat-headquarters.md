The base of the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md) in its several versions, including the "infinity base" suspended over the void and the later BAT Headquarters 2.0 in [The Amplified](the-amplified.md). Parrot hides here during the Director Arc, and Spoke secretly lives in its vents.

## Overview

- Home of the [Bounty Assassin Team](../factions/bounty-assassin-team.md), in two versions (1.0 and 2.0).

## History

- Parrot lives there in [The Director Arc](../arcs/the-director-arc.md), where MrCube6 rigs it with traps and TruOriginal wipes it out.
- Its old void trap opening later saves Wemmbu from WyLL; Wemmbu loots the abandoned base, and Flame finds ItzRealMe at the old base.
