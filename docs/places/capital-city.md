The server's biggest city. Under [Reddoons](../characters/reddoons.md) and [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) it was a corrupt mining city where players mined in servitude, and it later became the main refuge for new players. [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) smuggles players into it, gets put on trial there, and eventually hands it to Fymada as mayor.

## Overview

- Also spelled Capitol City. Under [Reddoons](../characters/reddoons.md) it was two places: an almost empty City, and the Mines beneath, a labour camp watched by the warden CoinMonke, where miners could leave only after reaching absurd diamond quotas. A roulette table killed one suspect a week.
- The city paid tribute to [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) in exchange for being left alone; Ash called it "basically his".

## History

- Parrot, Wifies and Dean are forced into the Mines; Parrot robs Reddoons' vaults to buy others' freedom, including LettuceK's, and Reddoons executes LuigiToan.
- After Reddoons, it becomes the capital of the Spawn Kingdom under King Parrot, with Fymada as mayor, until the [Capitol City Police Force](../factions/capitol-city-police-force.md) kills new players and frames Parrot.
- Cindercrest seizes it in [The Civil War](../events/the-civil-war.md); in ep. 161 its anti-portal defences fall to Clown, and Parrot kills the villagers to keep them from him.
