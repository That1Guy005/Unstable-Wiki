The empire [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) built around ManePear's old treehouse, whose currency was bottles of enchanting, until the Pirates robbed and captured them.

## Overview

- Wemmbu and Egg's civilization on the ruins of [ManePear's Tree](manepears-tree.md), built on a monopoly of XP bottles, which had become rare without villagers.

## History

- Wemmbu steals bottles, beats off an attack, and gets a barrel free from Pangi. Horace builds Egg a tower and Wemmbu kicks him out (no builders).
- Spongs, invited by Egg, turns out to be with Jaden's pirates, who make Wemmbu drop everything to save Egg, and then exile them both. It's abandoned and probably destroyed.

- [Unstable SMP: The Eggchan Empire](../episodes/season-2/030-unstable-smp-the-eggchan-empire.md) (ep. 30, Wemmbu, 27 February 2025): The Eggchan Empire is founded, with Wato, Arcn, Dione4k and Horace among its first members.
