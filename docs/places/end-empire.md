[SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md)'s empire in the End, carved out by [NULL](../factions/null.md) after it overthrew [MinuteTech](../characters/minutetech.md). It has terraformed islands, a five-floor prison, and End portals NULL uses to strike the Overworld.

## Overview

- [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md)'s empire in the End, built by NULL after defeating [MinuteTech](../characters/minutetech.md)'s guardians, starting in the End's southern quadrant. Rows of fully enchanted netherite NULL stand in formation, a lava-ringed obsidian platform guards the arrival, and a flying machine leads to the city. Spoke's five-floor prison is also in the End.

## History

- Ep. 146: Spoke conquers the End and founds the empire ([NULL's Conquest of the End](../events/nulls-conquest-of-the-end.md)).
- Ep. 154–155: Wemmbu and Egg are welcomed, recruited and threatened there; the wither cannons destroy their flying machine as they flee.
- Ep. 158: Parrot clears the prison's floors.
- Ep. 159: Clown's truce names NULL's End capital as off limits; at the end, the Nether Kingdom reaches the End.
