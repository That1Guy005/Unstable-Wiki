The city led by [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md), split into Builders, Farmers and Warriors districts, where [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) takes refuge from the Mafia, meets [TheobaldTheBird](../characters/theobaldthebird.md), and fights the First War before the city is destroyed.

## Overview

- One of the last resistance cells against the Mafia, led by [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md), with three districts: Builders, Farmers and Warriors. Newcomers were sorted by how they handled a wither with five items of their choice: fight it, hide from it or trap it.

## History

- Parrot arrives with LuigiToan's compass and joins the Warriors (testing as both warrior and farmer). A mission leader dies to a diamond-trim Mafia member; Theobald wins the mission-leader contest with TNT carts; Parrot wins a council seat with tridents in a flooded arena.
- Too remote for help, it's overrun by the Mafia in ep. 24, shortly before the Mafia's own fall ([Fall of the Invisible Mafia](../events/fall-of-the-invisible-mafia.md)). NULL later strips the ruins.

## Featured in

- [The Search for Minecraft's Far Lands](../episodes/season-1/021-the-search-for-minecrafts-far-lands.md) (ep. 21, ParrotX2, 4 November 2024): On the run after Mining City, [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md), [Wifies](../characters/wifies.md) and [Deanthebean9](../characters/deanthebean9.md) follow [LuigiToan](../characters/luigitoan.md)'s compass towards the legendary Far Lands Civilization to rally help against [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md).
- [Unstable SMP: The First War](../episodes/season-1/024-unstable-smp-the-first-war.md) (ep. 24, ParrotX2, 7 December 2024): Parrot and Wifies reconcile and Parrot has his spyglass back, but the Far Lands Civilization is destroyed.
- [Unstable SMP: Doomsday](../episodes/season-1/025-unstable-smp-doomsday.md) (ep. 25, ParrotX2, 21 January 2025): With the Far Lands Civilization gone, Parrot founds [District 13](../factions/district-13.md), loses [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md) to the Mafia and the resistance to despair, and brings in [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md), [Mapicc](../characters/mapicc.md) and [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md).
- [I Created Minecraft's Greatest Empire](../episodes/season-2-plus/155-i-created-minecrafts-greatest-empire.md) (ep. 155, SpokeIsHere, 20 September 2026): At the ruined Far Lands Civilization, destroyed in the Mafia era, NULL strips everything.
