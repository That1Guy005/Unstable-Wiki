The glitched terrain about twelve and a half million blocks from spawn, which grows more corrupted the further out you go. Rockets fail, ender chests come up empty, and impossible structures appear. [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) finds his orbital strike cannons here, and NULL later conquers it.

## Overview

- Corrupted terrain that replaces the world border about 12.5 million blocks out. It degrades further and further into fractals of dirt and oceans of void. Rockets and portals fail in parts of it, ender chests can come back empty, and it's full of echoes of past events.
- Places include Ashswag's old house, the [Far Lands Civilization](far-lands-civilization.md), a ruined copy of the Zam Empire, the Wavylands, CookieBunsquat's civilization, and the first-generation players' city at the far end.

## History

- Parrot reaches the Far Lands Civilization in Season 1; the Mafia destroys it.
- Wemmbu and Egg chase a stolen mace, are hunted by [The Avengers](../factions/the-avengers.md), and reach the city at the end, whose orbital cannons Wemmbu takes.
- Spoke, Mapicc and Jumper cross it to find NULL and JamatoP.
- Ep. 154–155: NULL conquers it, destroys Wemmbu's cannons and the first-generation city, and Jaden dies there.
