[FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md)' home in the badlands, griefed and rebuilt many times, and finally replaced by a megabase that [LAW](../factions/law.md) built as a gift.

## Overview

- A megabase LAW built Flame after a fan blew up his old one: four quadrants linked by bridges, with storage and training rooms, grey with dark red, over a river in a mesa about a hundred blocks from [LAW City](law-city.md). Flame, Lomedy, NotSkrilley, Spongs and Falconu live there.

## History

- LAW came to take it back for overspending; Flame worked 100 days as a LAW soldier to pay it off.
- Wemmbu partly wrecks it looking for Egg; LAW rebuilds it, adding Spongs' basement room. TheG0dofWar jumps Flame there.
- Ep. 160: Cindercrest damages it, Lomedy leaves a warning book, and ShoeBilly blows up part of it.
