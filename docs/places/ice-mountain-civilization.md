A small civilization inside the jagged ice peaks that Spoke once terraformed for the scavenger hunt, run by Arcn and [Loppezz](../characters/loppezz.md) and later taken over by [Fantst](../characters/fantst.md)'s team.

## Overview

- A small nation in jagged ice peaks that Spoke had built for Parrot's scavenger hunt, founded by [Loppezz](../characters/loppezz.md), Arcn and LettuceK after the [Skyblock Civilization](skyblock-civilization.md) fell.

## History

- They throw Wemmbu out for leaving them to the Mafia; Egg makes peace. Wemmbu secretly uses the city as bait for a diamond-trim, until Egg talks him into protecting it.
- Fantst takes it over and blows it up despite Wemmbu and Loppezz.

## Featured in

- [I Mastered Minecraft Combat](../episodes/season-1/023-i-mastered-minecraft-combat.md) (ep. 23, Wemmbu, 4 December 2024): Wemmbu finds the hidden Ice Mountain Civilization, gets [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) back from the Mafia, and pops a diamond-trim, but [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) takes Egg hostage for the mace, and Mane vanishes.
- [How I DESTROYED the Unstable SMP](../episodes/season-2/043-how-i-destroyed-the-unstable-smp.md) (ep. 43, Wemmbu, 28 May 2025): Loppezz's ice mountain (Ice Mountain Civilization) is destroyed.
