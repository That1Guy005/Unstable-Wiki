[LAW](../factions/law.md)'s home base and prison, about 100 blocks from [FlameFrags' Base](flamefrags-base.md), where Wemmbu, Lomedy and Jaden are all imprisoned during the Kings Arc.

## Overview

- [LAW](../factions/law.md)'s headquarters and prison, in a mesa-desert about a hundred blocks from [FlameFrags' Base](flamefrags-base.md).

## History

- LettuceK builds it while Flame is in the Nether, and uses it against Flame and Wemmbu; after [The 2 VS 1000 War](../events/the-2-vs-1000-war.md), Wemmbu is held in its prison ([The LAW Prison](../events/the-law-prison.md)).
- Spoke breaks Wemmbu out; after the Battle for the Great Sea, Wemmbu and Flame break their friends out, leaving it in ruins.
- Rebuilt under CircusMaximus, it builds Flame's base. Parrot blows it up in the civil war, killing everyone inside; SargeLAW rebuilds it.
- Ep. 160: Flame leads LAW from it against Cindercrest; ep. 161: Clown's army takes it.
