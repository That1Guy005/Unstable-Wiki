[Lomedy](../characters/lomedy.md)'s farm and base, where [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) first takes refuge and which is the battleground of the Great Farm War.

## Overview

- [Lomedy](../characters/lomedy.md)'s farm, also called the Great Farm.

## History

- Flame takes refuge there, beats bandits and V3NOM, and gives Lomedy a mace, which Jaden steals with pots.
- Lomedy and Flame win the Great Farm War against Spongs and Willsion.
- When Flame hides there from LAW, Deputy Ace seizes the farm and arrests Lomedy; it's probably abandoned since.
