[The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md)'s underground capital near the bottom of the world: four huge disks, one for each rank, with members earning their way up through upgrade tokens.

## Overview

- [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md)'s underground city, around y -50: four huge discs on pillars, one per rank. The untrimmed level held housing, factories (golden carrots, iron, XP, lava, emeralds, elytra) and a prison; copper had barracks and mission briefings; iron had wooden houses and a library; diamond had six castles; above them stood Ashswag's command castle.
- Promotion took three tokens from missions, traded to Ash for the next rank's chestplate.

## History

- Spoke, PlanetLord and Leow0ok infiltrate it, and Mapicc is held in its prison.
- After the Mafia falls, WyLL takes it over with the leftover resources; Wemmbu later infiltrates it disguised as one of WyLL's men.
