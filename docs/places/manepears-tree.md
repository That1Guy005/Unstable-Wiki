[ManePear](../characters/manepear.md)'s treehouse refuge, where he trains [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md), and which Wemmbu and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) later use as a base until [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md) and [FerreMC](../characters/ferremc.md) burn it down.

## Overview

- [ManePear](../characters/manepear.md)'s treehouse refuge, later Wemmbu and Egg's base, and the site of [Eggchan's Civilization](eggchans-civilization.md).

## History

- ClownPierce and FerreMC burn it down, which sets Wemmbu on his vow to kill Clown.

## Featured in

- [I Mastered Minecraft Combat](../episodes/season-1/023-i-mastered-minecraft-combat.md) (ep. 23, Wemmbu, 4 December 2024)
- [Unstable SMP: The Mafia](../episodes/season-1/026-unstable-smp-the-mafia.md) (ep. 26, Wemmbu, 21 January 2025): ManePear's treehouse is destroyed; two gold-trims die to order 36.
- [Unstable SMP: After The Mafia](../episodes/season-2/028-unstable-smp-after-the-mafia.md) (ep. 28, Wemmbu, 2 February 2025): Their first real base since ManePear's Tree goes up in a Pale Garden.
- [Unstable SMP: The Eggchan Empire](../episodes/season-2/030-unstable-smp-the-eggchan-empire.md) (ep. 30, Wemmbu, 27 February 2025): [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) start the [Eggchan Empire](eggchans-civilization.md) on the site of ManePear's treehouse.
