A large walled trading city crossed by a river, defended by the [Vanguard](../factions/vanguard.md). It's where Flame trades for netherite, Wemmbu is accused of theft, and a key battle of the civil war is fought.

## Overview

- One of the richest places on the server: a neutral trading city with an outer wall around houses, stalls, farms and a river, and a hexagonal inner city around a huge circus-like building. A giant Nether portal with an owl statue wearing Clown's mask stands on its east side. Its leader is DanchoGold.

## History

- Parrot forces it and its [Vanguard](../factions/vanguard.md) into his Royal Army by kidnapping DanchoGold; he owes it 100,000 diamonds.
- Cindercrest's air assault on it fails; later it's overrun with Wemmbu and Jaden's help during the civil war.
- Ep. 161: a lava blockade stops Parrot's blimp there.
