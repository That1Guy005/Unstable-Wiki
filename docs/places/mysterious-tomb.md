A cave tomb, entered by dropping into a pool of water, where [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) fights the Wraith for FieldsOfElysium's stolen mace.

## Overview

- A cave tomb in the [Wheat Kingdom](the-wheat-kingdom.md) where a retired hero left their mace on a giant sword, behind a hidden door and an arrow-and-warden trap on a bridge.

## History

- Following LordSirWessley's word-for-word directions, Wemmbu and Egg find it. Wemmbu loses a totem on the bridge, kills two wardens, and fights an invisible player (a Wraith) who snatches the mace, then yields it after Wemmbu's stab shots tear up the cave. The mace goes back to the Wheat Kingdom.

## Featured in

- [I Joined a 100,000 Player Minecraft Server](../episodes/season-2-plus/116-i-joined-a-100-000-player-minecraft-server.md) (ep. 116, Wemmbu, 21 February 2026): Defending [The Wheat Kingdom](the-wheat-kingdom.md), he agrees to recover its lost guardian's mace from a hidden tomb (Mysterious Tomb).
