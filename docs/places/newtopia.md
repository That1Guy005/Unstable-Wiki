A city of the Spawn Kingdom, built during [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md)'s reign as a refuge for builders and players fleeing war, and led by [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md). The Nether Kingdom's invasion is turned back here, and the three kings of Unstable unite here.

## Overview

- A city in the Spawn Kingdom built by [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md) as a refuge for builders and anyone fleeing war, designed to look like the Far Lands. Horace is its leader.

## History

- Built during Parrot's reign; Cindercrest controls it after the civil war; Horace upgrades it with Parrot's payment for rebuilding the Advanced Civilization.
- Ep. 161: the Overworld's refugees gather there. The leaders agree to hold for 22 hours; Clown's army breaches the first wall and fights to the second, until Reina arrives with the Advanced Civilization and the Nether retreats. The three kings speak at Horace's Citadel.

## Featured in

- [10,000 Player Minecraft War: Nether VS Overworld](../episodes/season-2-plus/161-10-000-player-minecraft-war-nether-vs-overworld.md) (ep. 161, ParrotX2, 2 November 2026): Newtopia holds; Lettuce, Saparata and Parrot unite the Overworld against Clown.
