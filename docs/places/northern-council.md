The seat of the ruling monarch, first built by [LAW](../factions/law.md) for [LettuceK](../characters/lettucek.md). The election candidates live here, the Purge happens here, and the final battle against LAW is fought here, before it becomes [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md)'s castle.

## Overview

- The seat of the Spawn Kingdom's monarch, used for speeches, elections and meetings. Built by LAW for LettuceK.

## History

- It hosts the election debates, held without armour under LAW guard (Spepticle is killed during one), and Lettuce's orders to hunt Parrot and Wemmbu.
- District 13's battle there against 2,500 lawmen and BAT was the largest in the server's history ([The War Against LAW](../events/the-war-against-law.md)); it was rebuilt under King Parrot.
- Ep. 160: LAW frees Lettuce's stasis there; ep. 161: Parrot sneaks in and spares Saparata.
