The prison of artificial biomes that [Wifies](../characters/wifies.md) built to hold, and "protect", [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md): a world without danger at the cost of Parrot's freedom, designed as an extension of Wifies' own mind.

## Overview

- [Wifies](../characters/wifies.md)' prison for Parrot: a safe mini-world of artificial biomes behind obsidian walls five blocks thick, up to build height, with mining fatigue and invisible guards (JumperWho, Leow0ok and Derapchu). Its real strength was psychological: Wifies knew Parrot too well.

## History

- Parrot makes no progress for weeks, but keeps the spyglass Wifies once gave him.
- In a secret chamber, Wifies makes them both stand on pressure plates over TNT, forever. Parrot leaves the spyglass on his plate and walks away; Wifies runs for it and the chamber explodes.
