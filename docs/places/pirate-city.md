A huge marine complex in the middle of [The Great Sea](the-great-sea.md), formerly the Rangers' Ranger Point, which [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md) turns into the capital of his Pirate Civilization.

## Overview

- Formerly Ranger Point, built by TheLobsterMan: a huge marine complex in the middle of [The Great Sea](the-great-sea.md), which [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md) turned into his pirate civilization.

## History

- The disguised Wemmbu and Jaden fail to take it; Parrot and Theobald are caught in its "Lobster Cage" and escape.
- Wemmbu kills TheLobsterMan in a duel, and Jaden takes over.
- LettuceK's orbital threat and constant attacks force it to be abandoned; Wemmbu destroys LAW's sea cannon in the Battle for the Great Sea (ep. 101).

## Featured in

- [I Joined a PIRATE CIVILIZATION in Minecraft](../episodes/season-2/097-i-joined-a-pirate-civilization-in-minecraft.md) (ep. 97, Wemmbu, 14 December 2025): [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md) offers [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) refuge in Pirate City if they help fight [LAW](../factions/law.md), which is building a super-powered orbital cannon at the edge of [The Great Sea](the-great-sea.md).
- [Unstable SMP: The Battle for The Great Sea](../episodes/season-2/101-unstable-smp-the-battle-for-the-great-sea.md) (ep. 101, Wemmbu, 28 December 2025): Wemmbu and Egg abandon Pirate City; Deputy Ace quietly lets them go.
- [2 Minecraft Pros VS 1000 Hunters](../episodes/season-2/106-2-minecraft-pros-vs-1000-hunters.md) (ep. 106, ParrotX2, 24 January 2026): After sixteen hours of travel, Pirate City is abandoned, but Wemmbu and Egg are there.
