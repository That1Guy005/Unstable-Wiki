The prison [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md) has built for his own entertainment, designed by [Kenadian](../characters/kenadian.md): three enormous rings of crying obsidian and chunk bans around huge simulated landscapes. [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md), [PrinceZam](../characters/princezam.md), [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md), [Wifies](../characters/wifies.md) and many missing players are locked inside, and both Wemmbu and Parrot escape it in their own POVs.

## Overview

- [ClownPierce](../characters/clownpierce.md)'s prison, designed by [Kenadian](../characters/kenadian.md), built for Clown's entertainment.
- Three huge circular cells of open terrain (with leaves, sand and similar resources removed), walled with crying obsidian and chunk bans from bedrock to build height. They're linked by tunnels with toggled chunk bans, solitary cells rigged with sculk to chunk-ban anyone doing more than moving, and a central guard room.
- About every hour, invisible guards armed with poison potions move prisoners to another cell; each cell holds two, and you meet the same person every six hours.

## History

- Wemmbu, Eggchan, PrinceZam and Pangi are dropped in; Wemmbu escapes by pearl cannon and the others follow.
- Parrot, Wifies, JumperWho, Derapchu, Kenadian and Rekrap2 are dropped in, and escape with a honey stasis chamber and outside help ([Imprisonment in Proton](../events/imprisonment-in-proton.md)). It's presumed abandoned.
