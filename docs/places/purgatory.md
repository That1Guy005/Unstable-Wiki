[NULL](../factions/null.md)'s enormous prison over a sea of lava, millions of blocks out in the Nether. It is run by [JamatoP](../characters/jamatop.md), holds thousands of captured players, and forces them into a hunger games, until [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) breaks it open and takes over NULL.

## Overview

- [NULL](../factions/null.md)'s vast prison and former headquarters, millions of blocks out over an endless lava sea in the Nether, linked to the Overworld's [Far Lands](far-lands.md). It held thousands of prisoners, more than any other prison, with [JamatoP](../characters/jamatop.md) as warden.

## History

- Built after Jamato took over NULL. Mapicc escapes on the way in; Spoke and JumperWho are captured in the Far Lands.
- Spoke's escape with DevMCChamp's rebels fails and everyone but Spoke is executed; another attempt via the black market and a Nether portal ends with Spoke in solitary and Jumper and Mapicc sentenced to death. He breaks out of solitary with a smuggled fire-resistance potion and a book from found2.
- Jamato's Hunger Games, the Commander's betrayal, and Spoke's victory end the [Purgatory Arc](../arcs/purgatory-arc.md). Wither cannons destroy the prison and kill many prisoners; the survivors are freed. Its loss leaves NULL short of resources for the move to the End.
