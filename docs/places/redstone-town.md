A town of redstone engineers, home to [4CVIT](../characters/4cvit.md), where players go for cannon parts and machines.

## Overview

- [4CVIT](../characters/4cvit.md)'s outpost for redstone fans, built around his Basic Redstone Shop, whose lowest level hides a vault behind a fake pressure-plate TNT trap. It may later have become Steampunk City.

## History

- Parrot and Derapchu recover BAT's stolen gear there, solving the pressure plates only to learn the TNT was fake.
- Wemmbu sneaks in through the farms to demand a pearl cannon to the Far Lands; 4C says he destroyed it and calls LAW.
- 4C builds King Parrot a cooked-chicken farm.
