A small civilization led by [Roshambogames](../characters/roshambogames.md), linked to the duped-items stockpiles and later targeted by the [Zam Empire](zam-empire.md), when [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) holds off [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) so its members can escape.

## Overview

- A small, unnamed settlement led by [Roshambogames](../characters/roshambogames.md), now abandoned.

## History

- Zam's guards try to take it; FlameFrags nearly kills Roshambo before Wemmbu steps in, duels Flame and covers the evacuation.

## Featured in

- [I Bankrupted Minecraft's Richest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/011-i-bankrupted-minecrafts-richest-civilization.md) (ep. 11, SpokeIsHere, 19 July 2024): The duped items [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) and [Mapicc](../characters/mapicc.md) destroyed are back, and Roshambo's Civilization is richer than ever.
- [Unstable SMP: The PrinceZam Empire War](../episodes/season-1/013-unstable-smp-the-princezam-empire-war.md) (ep. 13, Wemmbu, 29 July 2024): His first assault fails, so he recruits the defector [ManePear](../characters/manepear.md), defends Roshambo's Civilization against [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md), and starts kidnapping Zam's officers, which troubles [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md).
