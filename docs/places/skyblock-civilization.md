A tightly watched city in the sky founded by Yeah_Jaron, where [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) gets stuck working the farms, before a betrayal lets [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) conquer it.

## Overview

- A secretive sky city ruled by Yeah_Jaron that forced outsiders to become citizens or die, with resources impossible to get in normal skyblock. Members included Wato1876, Horace, Pangi, Loppezz, Arcn, LettuceK and the enforcer Straight.

## History

- Wemmbu follows players flying up, is forced into tasks, loses a sumo duel to Straight, and spies on Jaron.
- The Mafia finds them when they go down; at the raided End Civilization their invisibility runs out, and Wemmbu leaves them to the Mafia, which destroys the city. The survivors blame him.

## Featured in

- [I Discovered a Minecraft Sky Civilization](../episodes/season-1/020-i-discovered-a-minecraft-sky-civilization.md) (ep. 20, Wemmbu, 30 October 2024): The Skyblock Civilization falls to the Mafia after Straight's betrayal.
