King [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md)'s settlement for new players, more than 100,000 blocks from spawn, whose thousand settlers vanish within a week. It is razed as part of the build-up to the civil war.

## Overview

- Parrot's experimental haven for new players: a thousand settlers sent to see whether the territory was liveable.

## History

- Parrot returns after a week to find it empty except for a sign from Zoe444 to ReinaDrop.
- Cindercrest and [ShoeBilly_](../characters/shoebilly.md) had killed 990 settlers and taken 10 to grow wheat; ShoeBilly picked them off on food expeditions. Only Zoe, who escaped and starved, and Reina survived, and Reina joins Parrot.

## Featured in

- [Searching for a Civilization that Doesn't Exist](../episodes/season-2-plus/125-searching-for-a-civilization-that-doesnt-exist.md) (ep. 125, ParrotX2, 14 April 2026): Skymore's thousand settlers and fifteen guards are lost; Zoe is presumed dead.
