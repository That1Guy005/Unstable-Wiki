The server's central, semi-neutral hub, where Wemmbu, Spoke, Mapicc and Parrot all had early houses. Control of Spawn changes hands across several eras, and [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) later rebuilds it as the seat of his kingdom.

## Overview

- The server's central hub, where new players arrive. It has changed hands through six eras.

## History

- Early days: Wemmbu, Spoke, Mapicc and Parrot build houses there (Spoke's lava-cast house, Parrot's birdcage); the Zam Empire claims it with a village and statue, which Wemmbu and Egg blow up.
- End of Season 1: the Mafia turns it into a prison to press-gang new players; District 13 breaks in to follow its pearls.
- Season 2: a lava-cast, wither-ridden wasteland.
- Under King Parrot: a castle built by Horace to protect and feed new players.
- Under Cindercrest: a trap for new players during the famine.
- Ep. 161: ShoeBilly defends it while the villagers are bred, until Clown's army arrives.
