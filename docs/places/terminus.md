A maximum-security prison for overpowered players, built by Cloudwatch and funded by [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md). [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) is lured into it with the story of a hidden vault.

## Overview

- A maximum-security prison built by Cloudwatch and funded by [Cindercrest](../factions/cindercrest.md), meant to wear the strongest players down until they break or submit. It lies millions of blocks out and also holds Cindercrest's enemies.
- Layers: a starting obsidian box of rooms (TNT, an [Arrow Cannon](../mechanics/arrow-cannon.md), wardens, guardians and illagers, invisible guards), then a stone wasteland where TNT falls and a warden is dropped in every day, then a lava maze of obsidian stairs with prisoners caged above it, and finally "limbo", a quiet village at the very end. Elder guardians switch mining fatigue on near the obsidian.

## History

- FlameFrags and NotSkrilley are lured in by the fake Windgrove vault (ep. 153).
- Ep. 157: Flame, BannerBurger, Waylonblade and Spongs work their way through the layers; Flame agrees to join Cindercrest, Cloudwatch tries to kill him, and ten factions led by LAW raid the prison and free him.
- Ep. 160: Flame and Lettuce sneak back in as guards and rescue Skrilley.
