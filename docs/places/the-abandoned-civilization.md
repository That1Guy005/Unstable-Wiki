A huge sealed-off civilization deep underground, closed off from the world by a vote and full of wardens. [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) leaves [Jaden_MAN](../characters/jaden-man.md) trapped there, and [Rejoicin](../characters/rejoicin.md) dies there.

## Overview

- A huge underground ruin full of wardens, with elder guardians giving the whole place mining fatigue. Its books say its people voted to shut themselves away from the world; why they abandoned it is unknown.

## History

- Wemmbu, Egg and Loppezz, with its last guardian [Rejoicin](../characters/rejoicin.md), chase Jaden through its spires and armouries. Rejoicin dies to wardens, and they leave Jaden trapped there with Mining Fatigue III, until FlameFrags rescues him.
