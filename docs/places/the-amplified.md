A region of "amplified" terrain, home to BAT Headquarters 2.0 and [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md)'s castle and sky bases.

## Overview

- A region of amplified terrain, home to [BAT Headquarters](bat-headquarters.md) 2.0 and BAT's secret vault, Spoke's old castle, and once Squiddo's house and Quackenstein123's family.

## History

- Spoke burns Squiddo's house with his invisibility exploit; BAT destroys Quackenstein's family home.
- Parrot rescues a Treebo Village member from the Phantoms bandits here.
- During Parrot's reign, Cindercrest's Brigadier-General And3rson takes it over with bounty hunters Gunside and Hypan.
