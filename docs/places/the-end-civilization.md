The civilization [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md), [Wifies](../characters/wifies.md) and [Deanthebean9](../characters/deanthebean9.md) found on the outer End islands to escape being hunted. It is built up by [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md), then repeatedly attacked by [The Invisible Mafia](../factions/the-invisible-mafia.md) until it falls.

## Overview

- Founded in the End by [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md), [Wifies](../characters/wifies.md) and [Deanthebean9](../characters/deanthebean9.md), with Horace's island, castle and dome, to be undetectable by the Mafia. Without an Overworld bed, members can't leave the End.

## History

- Dean, an invisible Mafia follower, is unmasked and recruited; Parrot rescues him from the Mafia's stasis.
- Horace sinks the island, builds a castle, outposts and chunk-ban walls; bomber planes are fought off.
- Boosfer loses the election to Parrot and chunk-bans himself running away.
- A Mafia mole switches off the chunk bans and the Mafia takes the civilization.

## Featured in

- [I Built Minecraft's Safest Civilization](../episodes/season-1/016-i-built-minecrafts-safest-civilization.md) (ep. 16, ParrotX2, 9 September 2024): The End Civilization falls, and Parrot sets it off himself.
