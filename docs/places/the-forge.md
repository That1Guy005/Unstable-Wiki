A mysterious place in the [Far Lands](far-lands.md), said to hold great power and to be where "many weapons" are made. [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) spends the Farlands Arc searching for it.

## Overview

- A mysterious place, believed to hold great power, more than 12 million blocks out, reached through [The Underworld](the-underworld.md). Statues on the way suggest it was built by Lakerek and another player.

## History

- Mxsterwu first mentions it as a place to create "many weapons". Wemmbu mistakes an orbital cannon array for it, which Serpentbound333 denies.
- Ep. 154: Serpentbound gives Wemmbu the compass to the real Forge, where he'll find what's needed to defeat NULL and future threats. The route crosses the Far Lands and impossible custom biomes to a staircase and an Underworld portal, which Wemmbu and Egg go through.

## Featured in

- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Fartherlands](../episodes/season-2/054-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-fartherlands.md) (ep. 54, Wemmbu, 15 July 2025): Wemmbu and Egg stay in the Far Lands to search for the Forge.
- [I Explored to the END of the Minecraft Farlands](../episodes/season-2/058-i-explored-to-the-end-of-the-minecraft-farlands.md) (ep. 58, Wemmbu, 26 July 2025): Tired of losing fights, [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) push towards the very end of the [Far Lands](far-lands.md) in search of The Forge.
- [I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Void](../episodes/season-2/060-i-explored-beyond-the-minecraft-void.md) (ep. 60, Wemmbu, 7 August 2025): In the city in the void, [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) are welcomed by its leader Serpentbound333, while the residents (the builder GeeGeeGamer, the fighter Nitenly and the eager Firestar2008) seem determined to keep them busy instead of telling them where The Forge is.

- [10,000 Players Simulate COLD WAR in Minecraft](../episodes/season-2-plus/159-10-000-players-simulate-cold-war-in-minecraft.md) (ep. 159, SpokeIsHere, 23 October 2026): One NULL wonders if this is The Forge, which Spoke doesn't know of.
