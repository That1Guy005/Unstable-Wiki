An anomalous ocean about three million blocks from spawn that stretches for millions of blocks, full of islands and lost civilizations. It's where [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) is exiled, the Sunken Empire lies, and the Pirates fight [LAW](../factions/law.md) for control.

## Overview

- A huge anomalous ocean about three million blocks from Spawn, said to stretch for millions of blocks. Apart from isolated, resource-poor players, it was ruled by the [Pirates](../factions/pirates.md) from [Pirate City](pirate-city.md), until LAW tried to take it over.

## History

- Wemmbu and Egg are exiled to its Pirate Islands; Parrot and Theobald search for its sunken empire ([The Sunken Empire Treasure Hunt](../events/the-sunken-empire-treasure-hunt.md)).
- LAW builds an orbital cannon at its edge, which Wemmbu destroys; LAW gives up the sea after heavy losses (ep. 101).
- Ep. 160: LettuceK's loot stash there funds LAW's war.
