A frozen, fog-covered region millions of blocks from spawn, ruled by the Mist Civilization, which never lets anyone leave. Its warrior Mistrul hunts down anyone who tries, until [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md) breaks its hold.

## Overview

- A polar, clouded region millions of blocks from Spawn, with constant snow and dangerous terrain. The Mist Civilization, among the strongest on the server but isolated from it, stops explorers from leaving. It was led by Mistrul, an elytra-macer who beat Flame when Flame wasn't "in the zone", and then by his teacher Flowtives. Windgrove was its secret branch.

## History

- LAW hires Flame to assassinate Mistrul; he kills him, finds Lomedy with NotSkrilley's help, and massacres the Mist.
- Mistrul's apprentice Cloudwatch builds [Terminus](terminus.md) to take revenge on Flame.
