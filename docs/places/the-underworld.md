A forgotten dimension hundreds of blocks below the Nether's bedrock: grey, black, full of chasms, and impossible to build in. The [Soul Keepers](../factions/soul-keepers.md) patrol it. Wemmbu, Parrot, Spoke and Mapicc all end up trapped in it.

## Overview

- A hidden fourth dimension hundreds of blocks below the Nether's bedrock, where the void should be. Blocks can't be placed or broken, and coordinates are broken.
- Mostly dark soul soil and chasms: a castle with withers, a graveyard, broken bridges, and a room of twenty portals (two known to lead to a Far Lands forest and to Serpentbound's kingdom). It's home to the hostile Soul Keepers and to TheOathBinder.
- At one edge it fractures into void, with escape-room castles linked by glass bridges, getting darker towards the end; beyond them stands the Citadel, the scholars' library.

## History

- Boosfer's void trap drops Wemmbu and Boosfer in ([Wemmbu's Fake Death](../events/wemmbus-fake-death.md) era).
- Ep. 151: Parrot leads Clown's prisoners down through the void; ep. 156: TheOathBinder lets only Parrot reach the one-person exit ([Trapped in the Underworld](../events/trapped-in-the-underworld.md)).
- Ep. 152: Spoke reaches the Citadel and seals Mapicc in.
- Ep. 154: Wemmbu and Egg enter in search of [The Forge](the-forge.md).
