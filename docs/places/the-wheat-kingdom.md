A farming civilization led by FieldsOfElysium. [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) defends it from Yungyx's raid and recovers its stolen mace, and it is later destroyed.

## Overview

- A farming kingdom led by FieldsOfElysium, allied with Wemmbu and OrionCat. It's now destroyed.

## History

- Wemmbu stops Yungyx's raid, then wins back its retired protector's mace from the [Mysterious Tomb](mysterious-tomb.md), earning horses and free supplies.
- After the kingdom is robbed, FlameFrags and a Wraith take Eggchan from it; Wemmbu arrives later and attacks the Wraith.
