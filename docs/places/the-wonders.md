A set of enormous showpiece builds, among them a Hanging Gardens-style temple and the Frosted Palace ice castle, guarded by hired players. [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md) destroys them with orbital TNT cannons to hide the evidence of his exploits, despite [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md)'s attempt to save them.

## Overview

- Five giant builds by TheRealSquiddo's Master Builders, paid for by [SpokeIsHere](../characters/spokeishere.md): the Gardens of Babylon (a savanna temple), the Crone (a cloaked stone statue ringed by river), the Pyramid of Anubis, the Marshland Mansion (mud, copper and froglights in a swamp) and the Frosted Palace (an ice castle).
- Spoke's idea was to make them too beautiful for anyone to destroy, so he and [JamatoP](../characters/jamatop.md) could hide the first exploited features inside without the builders knowing.

## History

- Spoke hires JumperWho, Roshambogames, Fantst, Derapchu and Wemmbu as guards.
- Wemmbu discovers what's inside and turns on Spoke.
- Spoke destroys all five with orbital strikes, in order: the Gardens, the Crone, the Pyramid, the Mansion, and last the Frosted Palace, which [ParrotX2](../characters/parrotx2.md) tried to save ([Destruction of the Wonders](../events/destruction-of-the-wonders.md)).
