The colony [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) build for the [Zam Empire](zam-empire.md) on top of a secret vault. After [PrinceZam](../characters/princezam.md) betrays and blows it up, it becomes Wemmbu's independent base of operations for his revenge war, and is rebuilt with a bigger obsidian wall by [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md).

## Overview

- Built by [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) and [Eggchan](../characters/eggchan.md) as a colony for the Zam Empire, supposedly to guard Zam's vault, with builder [Horace Altman](../characters/horace-altman.md). It declared independence when Zam betrayed Wemmbu, and was his base for revenge.

## History

- FlameFrags, posing as a stranger, attacks Wemmbu there; Jaden_MAN's team raids it and loses to Wemmbu's traps.
- Clown chases the invisible Parrot through it and kills villagers for information.
- The Zam Empire destroys it; Wemmbu and Egg dig a hideout beneath the ruins.
