[PrinceZam](../characters/princezam.md)'s empire and the dominant power of early Season 1, defended by guards including [FlameFrags](../characters/flamefrags.md), [ManePear](../characters/manepear.md) and [LuigiToan](../characters/luigitoan.md). It allies with [Wemmbu](../characters/wemmbu.md) to hunt down Spoke, builds the prisons around the chunk bans, and is eventually destroyed after Zam betrays Wemmbu and the Empire War breaks out.

## Overview

- [PrinceZam](../characters/princezam.md)'s empire, ruled by paranoia: a great wall, chunk-ban prisons and a constant hunt for threats. Its guards included FlameFrags, ManePear, Pangi, Yungyx, WyLL, Loppezz and LuigiToan.

## History

- It allies with Wemmbu to kill Spoke, chunk-bans Spoke (in the same prison as Wifies), and hires ClownPierce to kill Parrot after he frees the prisoners.
- It sends Wemmbu to build a colony that's really a trap, sends FlameFrags to kill him, blows up his orbital cannon, and puts a giant statue of Zam at [Spawn](spawn.md).
- After ManePear defects, Wemmbu wages war on the empire and destroys it with an orbital strike. A ruined copy survives as an echo in the [Far Lands](far-lands.md), which NULL strips in ep. 155.
